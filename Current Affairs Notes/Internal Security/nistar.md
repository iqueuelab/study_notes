# Nistar: indigenous diving support vessel

## Delivery and capability
- **Hindustan Shipyard Limited, Visakhapatnam delivered Nistar to the Indian Navy on July 8, 2025**, announced by PIB July 9. The source wrongly calls July 9 commissioning; delivery and commissioning are separate events. [Navy delivery release](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2143335&lang=2&reg=48).
- First indigenously designed/built diving support vessel of this project: approximately **118 m, 10,000 tonnes, 75% indigenous content**, built to Indian Register of Shipping classification rules.
- **Saturation diving to 300 m; side diving to 75 m; ROV operations to 1,000 m**. These are different capabilities, not interchangeable diver depths. Acts as mothership for a **Deep Submergence Rescue Vehicle**, supporting submarine rescue, deep-sea diving, salvage and underwater repair.
- Sanskrit name conveys liberation/rescue/salvation. Capability supports maritime safety, shipbuilding self-reliance and sustained operations away from shore. PYQ distinction: **INS Astradharini is a torpedo launch/recovery vessel; Dhruv is an advanced light helicopter**.

## Commissioning: 18 July 2025
- The July 19 clipping reports **INS Nistar commissioned**, following delivery on July 8. Commissioning, delivery and earlier launch are distinct milestones; existing diving-depth/ROV/DSRV capabilities remain unchanged.
- Official commissioning release reports **over 80% indigenous content and 120 MSMEs**, compared with the PDF/earlier delivery summary's roughly 75%. Treat these as differently reported event-stage figures; the commissioning announcement is the later official account. [Navy commissioning release](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleaseIframePage.aspx?PRID=2145766&lang=2&reg=48).
