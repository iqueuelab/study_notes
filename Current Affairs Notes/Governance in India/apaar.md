# APAAR student identity and educational records

## Purpose and safeguards
- **Automated Permanent Academic Account Registry**: a 12-digit lifelong student identifier, linking educational records through DigiLocker and the Academic Bank of Credits.
- ABC stores academic credits for transfer, not money. Identity infrastructure does not by itself make credentials accurate or ensure credit acceptance by every institution.
- Participation and informed consent, including parental consent for minors, matter. A saturation target should not turn a voluntary identifier into a condition for access to education.
- Privacy chronology: Puttaswamy privacy judgment **2017**; Aadhaar judgment **2018**. DPDP Act was enacted in 2023, although its main provisions were not operative in March 2025.
- Risks: mismatched records, exclusion, unnecessary Aadhaar linkage, profiling and breaches. Encryption alone cannot prevent misuse.
- Safeguards: purpose limitation, minimum data, clear consent/alternatives, correction, access controls, independent audit and accessible grievance redress.
