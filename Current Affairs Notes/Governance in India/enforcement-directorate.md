# Enforcement Directorate: mandate, tenure and safeguards

## Institution and laws
- **Department of Revenue, Ministry of Finance; headquarters New Delhi**. Financial investigations involving money laundering and foreign-exchange violations; headed by Director of Enforcement.
- **1 May 1956:** Enforcement Unit under Department of Economic Affairs to enforce **FERA 1947**; renamed **1957**; transferred to Revenue **1960**. **FEMA 1999** replaced FERA 1973 from **1 June 2000**; **PMLA 2002** enforcement began **1 July 2005**. [ED history](https://enforcementdirectorate.gov.in/about-us/history-of-ed/).
| Law | Character and principal role |
|---|---|
| FEMA, 1999 | Mainly civil contraventions; external trade/payments and orderly foreign-exchange market development |
| PMLA, 2002 | Criminal money laundering linked to proceeds of scheduled crime; investigation and attachment subject to statutory process |
| Fugitive Economic Offenders Act, 2018 | Proceedings concerning qualifying fugitives and confiscation through the designated court |
- Attachment/freezing, adjudication and final confiscation are different stages; ED does not autonomously replace judicial findings. Hawala/terror-financing links require evidence; every forex contravention is not a money-laundering offence.

## Organisation and tenure correction
- Regional/zonal/sub-zonal offices; recruitment combines direct entry and deputation from revenue, police and other services. Source lists five regional centres—**Mumbai, Chennai, Chandigarh, Kolkata and Delhi**—but its **10 zonal/11 sub-zonal** count is undated and should not be memorised as a current organisational total.
- **2021 amendments:** ED Director's tenure provision is in **Central Vigilance Commission Act, 2003, section 25**; CBI Director's corresponding provision is in **Delhi Special Police Establishment Act, 1946**. The PDF incorrectly reverses the mapping and calls the CVC Act amendment a provision for the CVC chief.
- Statutory tenure at least **two years**; public-interest extensions, **up to one year at a time**, based on committee recommendation with reasons recorded, subject to **five-year total ceiling**. Extension is discretionary and conditional, not an automatic entitlement to three further years. [CVC statute](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/2067/1/A2003-45.pdf).

## Accountability and financial security
- Track illicit proceeds, support asset recovery and coordinate international assistance; **FIU-IND**, INTERPOL channels and FATF standard-setting have distinct institutional roles. FATF is not an ED overseas investigation branch.
- Concerns: selective/political use allegations, prolonged cases, liberty, bail and evidentiary safeguards. Distinguish allegations from established misconduct; “low conviction rate” needs a denominator and time period—convictions among completed trials differ from convictions divided by all initiated investigations.
- Balance effective financial-crime enforcement with reasoned actions, statutory notice/arrest safeguards, prompt judicial scrutiny, fair hearings and transparent outcome reporting; general judicial approval of powers does not remove case-specific safeguards.
