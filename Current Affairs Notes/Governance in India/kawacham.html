Governance in India

KaWaCHaM and last-mile disaster warnings

Editable notes

Kerala system

  • Kerala Warnings Crisis and Hazards Management System, KaWaCHaM, Malayalam shield; functional 1 April 2024, formally inaugurated 21 January 2025.
  • National Cyclone Risk Mitigation Project, NDMA/World Bank-supported early-warning infrastructure; NCRMP is a programme, not a separate international funding organisation. Kerala SDMA.
  • 126 sirens, strobe lights/cameras, public-building/tower/school sites; source eight loudspeakers, reach up to 1,200 m, lights red/orange/yellow. Reach depends on terrain/noise and must not be mistaken for guaranteed audibility everywhere.

Warning to action

  • Inputs from IMD, INCOIS, CWC, relevant agencies and validated situational reports; social-media information needs verification.
  • State Emergency Operations Centre, district/local/taluk coordination; multi-hazard alerts for coastal impacts, intense rain, strong wind, extreme heat.
  • Hazard assessment → authorised alert → siren/voice/light and other channels → evacuation/safe shelter instructions → coordinated response and monitoring.
  • Pre-recorded messages, camp/safety information, accessible languages/formats, regular tests and community drills. Source “world's fastest” is not a measured universal ranking; warning value depends on timely receipt, understanding and action.
