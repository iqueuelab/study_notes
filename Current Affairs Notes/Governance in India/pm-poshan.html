Governance in India

PM-POSHAN school meals

Editable notes

Scheme and beneficiaries

  • Pradhan Mantri Poshan Shakti Nirman (PM-POSHAN): rights-based centrally sponsored school-meal scheme, previously the Mid-Day Meal programme; Ministry of Education.
  • September 2021 approval covered 2021–22 to 2025–26, with combined Centre/State/foodgrain support about ₹1.31 lakh crore. The approval period is historical, not proof of indefinite continuation or that the whole amount is only the Centre's cash allocation.
  • One hot cooked meal on school days for eligible government/government-aided school children in classes I–VIII, with Balvatika inclusion; not every private school or every preschool child nationwide.

Quality, participation and oversight

  • School nutrition gardens, locally appropriate menus and eligible SHG/FPO participation can support diversity and local livelihoods.
  • Supplementary nutrition prioritises aspirational/high-anaemia areas under applicable funding arrangements; permission/support does not guarantee every school receives the same milk/egg menu.
  • Tithi Bhojan: voluntary community food contributions on special occasions; complements rather than replaces regular entitlement.
  • Direct payment to cook-cum-helper bank accounts concerns their honorarium, not conversion of every child's hot meal to cash. Digitisation alone does not eliminate all leakage.
  • Social audit mandatory in all districts; inspections, food safety, grievance mechanisms and community participation test implementation. The source's promise of a separately appointed nutrition expert in every school is not evidence that such posts are operational everywhere. Official scheme overview.
  • Nutrition/health tracking and expert support should complement safe kitchens, adequate ingredients, inclusive access and trained cooks. PM-POSHAN school meals ≠ POSHAN Abhiyaan/Anganwadi nutrition programme.
