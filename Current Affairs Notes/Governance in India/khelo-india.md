# Khelo India and youth sports

## Bihar Youth Games: April 2025 preview
- Source announces the **7th Khelo India Youth Games, 4–15 May 2025**, in **Patna, Rajgir, Gaya, Bhagalpur and Begusarai**. These were scheduled dates at the issue date; Delhi also hosted specialised events including shooting, gymnastics and track cycling.
- **2025 KIYG was under-18**, not the source's recycled school under-17/college under-21 table. [Sports Ministry confirmation](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2139650&lang=2&reg=3)
## Evolution and purpose
- **2018:** Khelo India School Games, New Delhi; **2019:** renamed Youth Games, Pune/Maharashtra; **2020:** Assam; the **2021 edition held in 2022** in Haryana; **2023:** Madhya Pradesh; **2024:** Tamil Nadu; **2025:** Bihar.
- Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports/SAI ecosystem: grassroots participation, sports infrastructure, talent identification, training and competition. Youth, university, winter and para competitions serve different groups; avoid collapsing their eligibility.
- Talent support follows selection and scheme conditions, not automatic grants to every participant. The source's **₹5 lakh annually for eight years** reflects an earlier talent-development formulation; amounts/components evolved and should not be treated as an unchanging 2025 entitlement.
- Khelo India mobile app (2019) supports fitness assessment and sports information; digital engagement complements coaching/facilities.
- Bihar hosting can broaden access and regional sporting infrastructure, tourism and employment. Durable gains require maintenance, qualified coaches, safe participation, gender/disability inclusion and athlete pathways beyond the tournament.
- Mains: distinguish **participation → talent identification → sustained development → elite outcomes**; hosting and medal counts alone do not demonstrate universal fitness or lasting local benefits.
