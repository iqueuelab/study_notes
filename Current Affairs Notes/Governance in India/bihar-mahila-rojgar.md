# Bihar Mukhyamantri Mahila Rojgar Yojana: women’s livelihood grants

## September 2025: self-employment grants
- Bihar's **Mukhyamantri Mahila Rojgar Yojana** offers an initial **₹10,000 grant** to one eligible woman per family for a chosen livelihood; the clipping anticipates **₹7,500 crore to 75 lakh women** by DBT. The national launch/transfer was scheduled for **26 September 2025**, not August 2024 as its revision box claims. Administrative announcement/approval and public launch differ; do not record the transfer as completed on this issue's physical date. [PMO launch announcement](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2171342&lang=2&reg=48).
- Subsequent support is **up to ₹2 lakh**, conditional on assessment/business progress, rather than an automatic ₹2-lakh entitlement for every recipient. SHG-linked community resource persons, training and village markets can connect finance to viable enterprises.
- Source eligibility description: **18–60 years**, non-income-tax-paying households, Jeevika/SHG routes, and specified unmarried/orphaned women; it reports **1.11 crore applications**. These are source-period administrative details, not a claim every SHG member automatically qualifies regardless of all rules. Rural/urban implementation channels and family definition require the applicable scheme guidelines.
- Benefits: women's control over income, micro-enterprises and local demand. Constraints: small initial capital, skills/market access, fair assessment, administrative capacity and fund use. Evaluate business survival and income alongside transfers; DBT improves traceability but does not itself guarantee zero leakage.
- Links: **DAY-NRLM/Jeevika, PMMY, Stand-Up India and PMEGP**; grants differ from loans. Pair livelihood finance with training, bookkeeping, market links and accountable follow-up.
