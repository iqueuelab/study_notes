# BARC India and television audience ratings

## Television audience measurement
- **Broadcast Audience Research Council (BARC) India**, established in **2010**, Mumbai: industry body representing broadcasters, advertisers and advertising agencies. It owns/manages a television audience-measurement system; it is distinct from the atomic-research institution with the same acronym.
- Television Rating Points indicate measured viewing/popularity over a defined period. Broadcasters and advertisers use audience estimates to value programmes and advertising; ratings are sample-based estimates, not a census of every viewer.
- **Embedded inaudible audio watermark → broadcast → household BAR-O-Meter → cleaned, weighted viewing data → population estimates.** Individual household members identify their viewing through assigned remote buttons; channel/time codes identify programmes. BARC's published methodology supports audio watermarking, **not the issue's claim that its standard method photographs the television screen**. [BARC technology](https://www.barcindia.co.in/technology), [measurement methodology](https://www.barcindia.co.in/measurement/television-audience-measurement-description-of-methodology.pdf).
- Sample representativeness, panel secrecy, tampering prevention and transparent methods matter. Weekly reporting is the usual cycle; the clipping reports an I&B direction to halt news-channel TRP reporting for four weeks amid sensational conflict coverage. Do not convert the source's “sole authorised body” wording into a permanent statutory monopoly.
