# AI governance: inclusion, safety and India’s global role

## July 2025 editorial proposals
- With India then preparing to host the **AI Impact Summit in February 2026**, the editorial proposes using DPI experience (Aadhaar/UPI/CoWIN), technical skills and Global South diplomacy to promote human-centred AI. These are source-era proposals, not agreed summit outcomes.
- **Accountability:** public, measurable pledges by governments/companies/universities, tracked for a year; examples include greener computing, wider training and local-language public services.
- **Inclusion:** proposed **AI for Billions Fund** for cloud credits, fellowships and local-language datasets; development-bank/Gulf support and an underserved-language model challenge are suggestions.
- **Safety:** proposed collaborative sharing of red-team tests, incident logs and model stress tests, plus open tools to assess bias/fairness.
- **Regulation:** proposed voluntary frontier-model code building on Seoul commitments, with timely safety disclosures (editorial suggests 90 days), compute transparency and an accident-reporting channel.
- **Cooperation:** connect existing institutions, broaden developing-country participation and avoid rival blocs fragmenting standards. Balance innovation with safeguards; simplistic US/EU/China labels do not capture every law or policy.
- MyGov consultation is presented as participatory preparation; it does not establish that no previous host consulted its public. The printed mains question is coaching practice, not a UPSC PYQ.
