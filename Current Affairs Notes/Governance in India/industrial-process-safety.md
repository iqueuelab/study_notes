# Industrial process safety and migrant-worker protection

## Sigachi blast and safety culture
- **30 June 2025, Sigachi Industries, Pashamylaram near Hyderabad, Telangana:** microcrystalline-cellulose manufacturing blast; the issue reports **36 deaths**, mainly migrant workers. This is the report's evolving toll, not a final casualty count.
- **MCC** is used as a pharmaceutical excipient and in food/cosmetics; relative inertness of the final material does not make high-temperature/pressure/dust-handling manufacturing safe. Equipment failure/temperature build-up were suspected, not a concluded investigation finding.
- **HAZOP=hazard and operability study**: systematic process-hazard examination. Maintain equipment/interlocks, temperature/pressure monitoring linked to shutdowns, dust/fire controls, trained operators, audits, emergency response/evacuation and management accountability.
- The source's Anakapalli comparison misdates the widely reported pharma blast: **August 2024**, not 2023. Other incidents are context, not proof identical causes.
- Source reports Telangana's **₹1-crore ex gratia** announcement. Compensation needs accessible claims, migrant-family support, medical care, social security and labour-law enforcement; relief does not substitute for prevention or lawful accountability.
