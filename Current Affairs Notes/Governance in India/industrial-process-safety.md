# Industrial process safety and migrant-worker protection

## Sigachi blast and safety culture
- **30 June 2025, Sigachi Industries, Pashamylaram near Hyderabad, Telangana:** microcrystalline-cellulose manufacturing blast; the issue reports **36 deaths**, mainly migrant workers. This is the report's evolving toll, not a final casualty count.
- **MCC** is used as a pharmaceutical excipient and in food/cosmetics; relative inertness of the final material does not make high-temperature/pressure/dust-handling manufacturing safe. Equipment failure/temperature build-up were suspected, not a concluded investigation finding.
- **HAZOP=hazard and operability study**: systematic process-hazard examination. Maintain equipment/interlocks, temperature/pressure monitoring linked to shutdowns, dust/fire controls, trained operators, audits, emergency response/evacuation and management accountability.
- The source's Anakapalli comparison misdates the widely reported pharma blast: **August 2024**, not 2023. Other incidents are context, not proof identical causes.
- Source reports Telangana's **₹1-crore ex gratia** announcement. Compensation needs accessible claims, migrant-family support, medical care, social security and labour-law enforcement; relief does not substitute for prevention or lawful accountability.

## Accountability and human cost
- August editorial emphasises repeated accidents as preventable governance failures: missing fire approvals, blocked exits, unreliable firefighting equipment, unsafe maintenance/hazardous work without **permit-to-work**, superficial audits and inadequate migrant/contract-worker training.
- Beyond process controls already noted, build transparent **near-miss/hazard reporting**, worker whistleblower protection, multilingual induction, independent inspections and meaningful management accountability. Safety must enter plant design, maintenance budgets, procurement and production targets.
- Worker death/disability can trigger household poverty, school dropout, mental distress and productivity loss; prompt medical care, compensation and portable social protection complement prevention.
- Source reports **~6,500 deaths in factories/construction/mines over fiveyears**, **130+chemical accidents/218 deaths/300+injuries in30 months after 2020**, with informal-sector undercount. Treat these as separately scoped source aggregates; no shared denominator or verified single official census is supplied.
- Source advocates corporate-manslaughter reform, stronger inspections and repeat-offender sanctions; “India has no executive criminal accountability” is too categorical—existing penal/factory-law liability exists. Assess investigation, proof, enforcement and coverage gaps; the editorial's proposed law is not an enacted national entitlement.
