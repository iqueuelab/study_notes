Governance in India

Former prime ministers: state funerals and memorials

Editable notes

December 2024 context

  • Manmohan Singh died 26 December 2024; seven-day mourning and a state funeral; cremated at Nigambodh Ghat on 28 December.
  • Source account distinguishes state-funeral guidelines from the absence of a specific rule mandating a memorial for every former PM. Past commemorative practice is not itself a constitutional entitlement.
  • MHA subsequently conveyed a commitment to allocate memorial space; retain this as the source's December 2024 account, not a claim about today's allocation status.

Memorial associations

  • Nehru - Shanti Van; Shastri - Vijay Ghat; Charan Singh - Kisan Ghat; Indira Gandhi - Shakti Sthal; Rajiv Gandhi - Vir Bhumi; P.V. Narasimha Rao - Ekta Sthal; Vajpayee - Sadaiv Atal at Smriti Sthal.
  • Source notes exceptions/local sites, including V.P. Singh and Morarji Desai's Ahmedabad samadhi. Avoid its inconsistent assertion that every memorial is in/near Delhi.
  • Upkeep depends on the site and responsible government/trust; source reports Vajpayee's memorial funded by Atal Smriti Nyas, Rs 10.51 crore.

Governance link

  • Clear, consistent criteria, transparent allocation and preservation of public commemorative spaces.
