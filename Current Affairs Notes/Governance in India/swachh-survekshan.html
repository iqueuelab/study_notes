Governance in India

Swachh Survekshan 2024–25 and urban sanitation

Editable notes

Awards presented 17 July 2025

  • MoHUA survey under Swachh Bharat Mission–Urban promotes sanitation, waste management and competitive improvement. 78 awards, including 23 Super Swachh League cities across all population categories; 23 is not the number in the million-plus category alone.
  • Separate league recognises sustained performance. Source eligibility: top-three position at least once in preceding three years plus top 20% of current population category. Assessment uses 10 parameters/54 indicators; five population bands: under 20,000; 20,000–50,000; 50,000–3 lakh; 3–10 lakh; above 10 lakh.
  • Swachh Shehar top three: above 10 lakh—Ahmedabad, Bhopal, Lucknow; 3–10 lakh—Mira-Bhayandar, Bilaspur (Chhattisgarh), Jamshedpur. The source wrongly places Bilaspur in Madhya Pradesh.
  • Source graphic also shows 50,000–3 lakh: Dewas, Karhad, Karnal; 20,000–50,000: Panaji, Aska, Kumhari; under 20,000: Bilha, Chikiti, Shahganj.
  • League examples: Indore, Surat, Navi Mumbai, Vijayawada; Noida, Chandigarh, Mysuru; New Delhi. Best Ganga town Prayagraj, best cantonment Secunderabad. Awards also recognise SafaiMitra safety and promising state/UT cities; rankings complement, not replace, sustained service delivery and worker safety. MoHUA announcement.

Additional award recall

  • 34 Promising Swachh Shehars recognised improvement; Visakhapatnam, Jabalpur and Gorakhpur received SafaiMitra safety recognition. Special Mahakumbh waste-management recognition went to Uttar Pradesh government, Prayagraj Mela administration and municipal corporation; source footfall 66 crore is event-reported attendance, not unique persons.
  • July 20's ordinal Indore 1st/Surat 2nd/Navi Mumbai 3rd/Vijayawada 4th is the award-list sequence of million-plus Super Swachh League cities, not an overall national ranking across all categories. Distinguish that league from Ahmedabad/Bhopal/Lucknow, ranked in the million-plus Swachh Shehar category. Official award categories.
