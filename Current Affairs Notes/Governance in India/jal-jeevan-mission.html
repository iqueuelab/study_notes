Jal Jeevan Mission: functional supply and accountability

Editable notes

December 2025: complaints and service quality

  • Parliamentary reply reported in this issue: 17,036 complaints across 32 States/UTs, including Uttar Pradesh 14,264 (~84%), Assam 1,236, Tripura 376. Complaints concern alleged financial irregularities and poor work; complaint counts are not all established corruption findings or a population-adjusted State performance ranking.
  • Source nationwide action counts: 621 departmental officials, 969 contractors, 153 third-party inspection agencies. UP: 14,212 reports submitted, 52 inquiries pending; actions involving 171 officials, 120 contractors, 143 TPIAs. The PDF calls their sum 434 ‘cases’; categories count persons/entities, so avoid assuming 434 distinct complaint cases without the reply's denominator.
  • JJM, launched 15 August 2019, Ministry of Jal Shakti's Department of Drinking Water and Sanitation; Centrally Sponsored Scheme for rural functional household tap connections, adequate quality and 55 litres per capita/day. Original universal-coverage goal 2024; Budget 2025–26 announced extension until 2028, already an earlier announcement rather than a fresh December launch. Official Budget account.
  • Community planning, VWSC/Pani Samiti with at least 50% women, source sustainability, water-quality field testing and greywater management link infrastructure to reliable service. Standard source funding pattern: 90:10 NE/Himalayan States, 50:50 other States, full central funding for UTs, subject to applicable component guidelines.
  • Monitor actual water availability/quality and operation-and-maintenance, not only tap-installation counts. Strengthen inspections, public grievance tracking, social audit, contractor accountability and local technical capacity; larger coverage can also generate more complaints, so raw totals alone cannot prove relative corruption.
