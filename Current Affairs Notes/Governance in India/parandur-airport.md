# Parandur airport: infrastructure and wetland trade-offs

## Proposal and concerns
- Proposed second Chennai airport at **Parandur, Kanchipuram**, site chosen 2022. Source₹29, 144 crore/ultimate 100 million passengers and 2028 phase targets are planning figures, not completed capacity.
- Reported 13affected villages include Ekanapuram; displacement, farm livelihoods and water-body/wetland networks require transparent assessment and fair rehabilitation.
- Parandur is not automatically a Ramsar-listed site. Wetland Rules 2017 definitions/exclusions, other water/land laws and project clearances must be examined separately.
- Basin hydrology, Kamban canal links, flood storage and cumulative effects matter; elevating a runway alone does not resolve downstream flood risk.
## Decision framework
- Assess demand/site alternatives, environmental impacts and public consultation; disclose clearances, acquisition terms and compensation/R&R.
- Pair connectivity/economic gains with ecological limits, meaningful grievance redress and monitored mitigation; projected jobs are not proof all displaced households gain.
