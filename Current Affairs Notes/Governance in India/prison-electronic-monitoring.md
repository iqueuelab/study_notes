# Prison overcrowding and electronic monitoring

## Dated prison picture
- **31 December 2022:** **5,73,220** inmates against capacity **4,36,266**; occupancy about **131.4%**, undertrials **4,34,302 (75.8%)** (the issue rounds this to 75.7%). Occupancy measures total inmates/capacity; undertrial does not mean convicted.
- Supreme Court publication **Prisons in India: Mapping Prison Manuals and Measures for Reformation and Decongestion**, released by President on **5 November 2024**. [President’s release](https://www.presidentofindia.gov.in/press_releases/president-india-releases-three-publications-supreme-court-india).
## Proposed alternative
- Source describes phased tagging of selected low/moderate-risk undertrials with good conduct, following risk assessment and feasibility/community-readiness evaluation. This is a reform recommendation, not a universal implemented bail scheme.
- Potential benefits: less custodial crowding/cost, family contact and rehabilitation while supervising compliance. US, UK, Canada, Australia and Malaysia offer differing bail/parole/home-confinement models; results depend on design.
## Legal safeguards
- Prisons are a **State List** subject. **Model Prisons and Correctional Services Act 2023**, shared with States/UTs **10 May 2023**, is a model for local enactment; it provides for tracking during temporary release/leave, not automatic national authority to tag all undertrials. [MHA reforms](https://www.mha.gov.in/en/commoncontent/prison-reforms-pr-section).
- **Frank Vitus v NCB, 8 July 2024:** a bail condition enabling constant tracking violates Article 21 privacy; Court deleted the Google Maps PIN condition. A dropped PIN is not itself a live-tracking technology. [Judgment](https://www.narcoticsindia.nic.in/Judgments/Frank_Vitus_vs_Narcotics_Control_Bureau_on_8_July_2024.pdf).
- Require lawful, necessary, proportionate conditions; judicial oversight, short retention/access controls, review and grievance mechanisms. Avoid widening supervision to people otherwise entitled to ordinary bail or shifting unaffordable device costs onto poor accused.
- Faster trials, legal aid, effective bail and periodic undertrial review remain central; technology cannot resolve every cause of incarceration.
