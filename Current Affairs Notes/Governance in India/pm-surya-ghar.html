PM Surya Ghar: residential rooftop solar and skills

Editable notes

Scheme and skills

  • Launched in February 2024 under MNRE, PM Surya Ghar targets rooftop solar for one crore residential households, with up to 300 units/month generated through suitable systems. Actual generation, consumption, export/net-metering and bills vary; it is not unconditional free grid supply to every household.
  • Cabinet outlay is ₹75,021 crore; residential central assistance is normally ₹30,000 for 1 kW, ₹60,000 for 2 kW and ₹78,000 for 3 kW or more, subject to applicable scheme conditions. The issue's 40% up to 3 kW/20% for 3–10 kW table is inaccurate for this scheme. PIB scheme account.
  • Capacity building covers grassroots installation, operation, maintenance and repair, aiming at over three lakh skilled people through fresh training and upskilling. Local technicians, quality installations, DISCOM coordination and reliable after-sales service are essential. The supplied 2025 PYQ has all three statements correct.
