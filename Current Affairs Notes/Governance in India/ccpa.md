# Central Consumer Protection Authority

## Statutory institution
- **CCPA**, under **Section 10, Consumer Protection Act 2019**, protects consumers as a class against rights violations, unfair trade practices and misleading advertising. Relevant provisions commenced **24 July 2020**; Act replaced the 1986 framework. [India Code commencement](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/indiacode/handle/123456789/21423?view_type=browse).
- Chief Commissioner and other Commissioners appointed by Central Government; the Act does not permanently prescribe exactly two Commissioners divided into goods/services. An investigation wing is headed by a Director-General.
## Powers and limits
| Provision | Function |
|---|---|
| Sections 18–19 | Investigate on complaint, government direction or own motion; refer matters to relevant regulators; file complaints before consumer commissions/intervene in proceedings |
| Section 20 | Recall unsafe goods, withdraw dangerous services, reimburse prices and stop unfair practices |
| Section 21 | Direct discontinuance/modification of misleading ads; impose specified penalties and prohibit endorsements |
- Manufacturer/endorser misleading-ad penalty: up to **₹10 lakh**, subsequent contravention **₹50 lakh**; endorsement prohibition up to **one year**, subsequent contravention **three years**, subject to statutory safeguards.
- CCPA enforcement is distinct from individual compensation adjudication by District/State/National consumer commissions. Licence suspension is not a blanket CCPA administrative power; the Act separately provides court-ordered consequences for specified offences. [Consumer Protection Act](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/15256/1/eng201935.pdf).
## Digital-market relevance
- E-commerce claims, deceptive advertising and systemic unsafe-product sales make proactive enforcement valuable.
- Improve investigation capacity, consumer awareness, regulator coordination and platform accountability; digital and cross-border transactions complicate evidence collection and enforcement.
