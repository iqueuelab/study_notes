# Panchayat Se Parliament and women’s representation

## Programme: January 2025
- **Panchayat Se Parliament 2.0**, inaugurated **6 January 2025** in the **Central Hall, Samvidhan Sadan**; **PRIDE, Lok Sabha Secretariat**, with **NCW and Ministry of Tribal Affairs**. [Parliament release](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2090660&lang=2&reg=48).
- Source participants: **502 elected Scheduled Tribe women representatives from 22 states/UTs**; commemorates **Birsa Munda's 150th birth anniversary**.
- Training on constitutional provisions, parliamentary procedure and governance; recognition of grassroots work in education/rural development.
- Workshops and visits: Parliament, Samvidhan Sadan, Pradhanmantri Sangrahalaya and Rashtrapati Bhawan; collective Preamble reading.
- Earlier **2024** edition reportedly involved **500 women sarpanches**.
## Mains value
- Reservation/elected presence must be backed by skills, access, autonomy, resources and freedom from proxy control.
- Peer learning and institutional exposure can build confidence; assess whether training improves actual participation, budgeting and public service delivery.
