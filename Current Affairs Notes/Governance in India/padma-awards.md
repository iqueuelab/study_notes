# Padma Awards: categories and selection

## Civil Investiture Ceremony-I: 28 April 2025
- President Droupadi Murmu presented **Padma Awards 2025**, not 2024 as the source heading states. First ceremony honoured **71** recipients: **4 Padma Vibhushan, 10 Padma Bhushan, 57 Padma Shri**. [President's announcement](https://www.presidentofindia.gov.in/press_releases/president-india-presents-padma-awards-2025-civil-investiture-ceremony-i), [official broadcaster](https://newsonair.gov.in/president-droupadi-murmu-presents-padma-awards-to-eminent-personalities-of-various-disciplines-fields/).
- Vibhushan recipients included **D. Nageshwar Reddy (medicine), L. Subramaniam (art), M.T. Vasudevan Nair (literature/education, posthumous), Osamu Suzuki (trade/industry, posthumous)**.

Sreejesh (sports), Vinod Dham (science/engineering), Sushil Kumar Modi (public affairs, posthumous), Pankaj Patel (trade/industry). Avoid the source table's mixed medical-field attribution.

[2025 gazette](https://www.padmaawards.gov.in/document/pdf/notifications/padmaawards/2025.pdf).
## Institutional recall
- Instituted **1954**; Vibhushan: exceptional/distinguished service; Bhushan: distinguished service of a high order; Shri: distinguished service. Civilian hierarchy: **Bharat Ratna → Padma Vibhushan → Padma Bhushan → Padma Shri**.
- Diverse fields include art, social work, public affairs, science/engineering, trade/industry, medicine, literature/education, sport and civil service. Foreigners may be honoured.
- Public nominations, including self-nominations, are scrutinised by the Padma Awards Committee constituted by the Prime Minister; recommendations receive PM/President approval. President normally confers awards at Rashtrapati Bhavan. Awards recognise service rather than confer hereditary titles.
