# PM SHRI: exemplar schools and NEP implementation

## Scheme design
- **Pradhan Mantri Schools for Rising India**, centrally sponsored scheme approved **7 September 2022**; strengthen **over 14,500 existing schools** as exemplars of **NEP 2020**, not create that many universities/new institutions.
- Scheme period **2022–23 to 2026–27**; States/UTs sign MoU and maintain achieved standards afterward. Eligible government/local-body schools, **KVs/JNVs** subject to selection criteria. [Education Ministry framework](https://pmshri.education.gov.in/about-us).
| Funding category | Centre : State/UT |
| --- | --- |
| Ordinary States / UTs with legislature | 60 : 40 |
| Northeastern / Himalayan States and J&K | 90 : 10 |
| UTs without legislature | 100% Centre |
- Holistic/inclusive learning, multilingual needs, experiential inquiry, communication/collaboration and competency-based assessment; labs, smart facilities and vocational exposure support pedagogy, not replace teachers.
- Green schools: water conservation, recycling and resource efficiency. **SQAF = School Quality Assessment Framework**, tracks quality/implementation.
- Evaluate attendance, inclusion, teacher capability, learning and maintenance; infrastructure selection/MoUs alone do not prove school transformation.
