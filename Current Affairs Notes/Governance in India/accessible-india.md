# Accessible India Campaign and barrier-free access

## Campaign and implementation
- **Sugamya Bharat Abhiyan / Accessible India Campaign**, launched **3 December 2015** by the Department of Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities, Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment; supports UNCRPD obligations.
- Three verticals: **built environment; transport; information/communication ecosystem**. Accessibility audits, retrofitting, ramps/lifts/tactile signage, accessible websites/documents and the Sugamya Bharat mobile app address physical/digital barriers.
- Campaign work continued through **Creation of Barrier-Free Environment under SIPDA**; the PDF's March 2024 completion reference is a programme milestone, not the end of the State's continuing accessibility duty.
- **6 August 2025 Rajya Sabha reply: ₹564.50 crore released for retrofitting 1,835 government buildings**. Funding/coverage does not establish every building's completed, independently verified accessibility. The source's older “700+” list and inset “1,748” describe different snapshots; do not combine them into one current achievement total. [Parliament reply](https://sansad.in/getFile/annex/268/AU2059_Ws1BZj.pdf?source=pqars).
- Practice: coverage of all three verticals is correct; the nodal ministry is Social Justice and Empowerment. The PDF explanation refers to statements 3/4 absent from its two-statement question.
