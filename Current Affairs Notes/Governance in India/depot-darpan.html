Governance in India

Depot Darpan and smart foodgrain storage

Editable notes

May 2025 initiative and status

  • DFPD, Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution: digital depot appraisal for foodgrain quality, safety, accountability and operational efficiency.
  • 1 May release scheduled formal inauguration for 20 May 2025; the source's “launched” wording is premature for the 5 May issue. Around 2,278 warehouses were envisaged for onboarding: FCI/CWC-owned and hired State/private depots; not proof all had completed adoption. AI/security tools were on a pilot basis. DFPD announcement.
  • Managers upload geo-tagged infrastructure/operation data → automated composite/star ratings and action points → 100% supervisory validation plus random third-party audits → improvement reviews. Mobile app supports supervisory monitoring/reports.
  • Assess safety, storage condition, environment, technology and statutory compliance, alongside stock turnover, grain losses, space use, staff costs and financial performance.
  • Sensors: temperature/humidity for storage conditions; CO₂ as an infestation-risk signal; phosphine for fumigation/worker safety; door sensors for access and ventilation; fire/smoke warning. AI bag counting, ANPR vehicle identification and facial recognition support stock/security checks.
  • Food security/PDS and SDG 2 links: reduce losses/pilferage and improve utilisation. These are intended gains, not quantified savings already achieved; data accuracy, maintenance, audit independence and worker/privacy safeguards matter.

18 November 2025: expanded digital food-distribution systems

PlatformNew function reported
ASHA — Anna Sahayata Holistic AI SolutionMultilingual AI calls seek beneficiary feedback on full ration, quality and FPS problems; translation, sentiment analysis, complaint categorisation and dashboards
Bhandaran 360, CWCERP integrates 41 modules for staff, finance, marketing, storage, contracts and projects; source describes implementation ahead of schedule
Smart EXIM Warehouse SystemAutomates CFS/ICD and general warehouse processes, linking cargo/vehicle handling and logistics
ANNA DARPAN, FCIMicroservices platform replacing Depot Online System; procurement, storage, movement, sales, quality, labour and contracts share data with FCI/DFPD
  • ASHA's 20 lakh beneficiaries/month is the announced scale/target. Official release gives both present-scale wording and a March 2026 nationwide rollout target, after pilots and expansion; do not infer every State/UT had complete coverage in November. Wadhwani Foundation partnership and IndiaAI/Bhashini infrastructure support multilingual delivery. Source ₹5 lakh cost claim lacks a clearly matched accounting period/scope.
  • Digital integration aligns with PM Gati Shakti logistics coordination, faster handling, public accountability and evidence-based service monitoring. Benefits are objectives requiring measured delivery, not proof that digitisation automatically eliminates diversion or data errors. DFPD launch release.
  • ANNA DARPAN is distinct from Depot Darpan, the existing depot-appraisal portal, and ASHA's feedback workflow should not be conflated with all functions of the earlier Anna Sahayata grievance platform. Preserve accessible human escalation, audit trails, role-based data access and alternatives for beneficiaries without working phones.
