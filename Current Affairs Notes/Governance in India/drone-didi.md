# Namo Drone Didi and women SHG enterprises

## Scheme: November 2024 guidelines

- **Central Sector**, Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare, women SHGs under **DAY-NRLM**; agricultural drone **rental services**, not free drone ownership for every farmer.
- **14,500 selected SHGs, FY2024–25 to 2025–26**; distinguish this phase from broader initial 15,000 target including earlier provision.
- Grant **80% of drone/accessories cost, maximum ₹8 lakh**; remaining finance through eligible lending, **AIF 3% interest subvention**. Maximum cap means SHG burden can exceed 20% for a higher-cost package. [Official guidelines](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleaseIframePage.aspx?PRID=2070029&lang=2&reg=3).
- Package: drone/spray assemblies, batteries, chargers, cameras/tools and supporting parts; source **up to 20 acres/day**, dependent on crop/weather/refills.
- Source pilot training **15 days**, fertiliser/pesticide applications; maintenance skill and ongoing support also matter. **Lead Fertiliser Companies**, State departments/manufacturers/SHG federations, Central Empowered Committee; MIS/Drone Portal for monitoring/funds/utilisation.

## Benefits → business viability → safeguards

- Women-led service income, rural technical skills, timely input application, labour/time savings and access to precision tools; yield gains are not guaranteed.
- Risks: repayment/utilisation, maintenance/spares, insufficient training, bureaucratic delay, chemical drift/pollinator/human exposure, notably sensitive Nilgiri habitats.
- Demand/business planning, qualified pilots, safe approved applications, no-spray buffers/weather checks, transparent monitoring and after-sales service; technology access needs environmental and livelihood safeguards.
