Governance in India

Senior citizens: policy reform and assistive support

Editable notes

4–5 June 2025: policy discussion

  • Fourth National Council for Senior Citizens meeting, chaired by Social Justice Minister Virendra Kumar, discussed a new policy reflecting ageing, digital inclusion, NGO/senior associations’ involvement, elder-abuse redress, care-home standards and intergenerational/community bonds. It is policy preparation, not a newly implemented national policy. Ministry meeting.
  • Source shares 8.23% (2011), projected 12.16% (2026), about 20% (2047) require age definition/projection source; 2047 is a scenario estimate, not a Census finding. Plan health/care, income security, accessible services and representation without assuming all older people have identical needs.
  • Council reviewed RVY, Integrated Programme for Senior Citizens (IPSrC), Senior Citizen Portal, quality/post-distribution support and coverage. Source “over five lakh beneficiaries” is a dated reported reach; do not also treat the same count as an unfulfilled target.

Rashtriya Vayoshri Yojana

  • Launched 1 April 2017, central-sector assistive-device scheme, Social Justice Ministry; ALIMCO implementation; source funding through Senior Citizens’ Welfare Fund.
  • Eligible 60+ people with qualifying age-related impairments: BPL or monthly income not exceeding ₹15,000, correcting the source’s BPL-only summary. Ministry eligibility.
  • Assessment-based free aids include walking sticks, elbow crutches/walkers, hearing aids, wheelchairs, dentures and spectacles. Devices improve functional independence; they do not replace clinical assessment, maintenance and accessible community infrastructure.
