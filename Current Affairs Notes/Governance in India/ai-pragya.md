# AI Pragya: digital skills in Uttar Pradesh

## 9 May 2025: programme announcement
- **AI Pragya**, source expansion: *Artificial Intelligence Programme for Resourcefulness, Awareness, Growth and Youth Advancement*, targets **10 lakh citizens/youth** trained in AI, ML, cybersecurity, data analytics and emerging technologies. It is a training target, not a completed beneficiary total.
- Source identifies **Centre for e-Governance, UP IT & Electronics Department** as nodal agency; collaboration across education, health, agriculture and rural development. Partners listed: **Microsoft, Intel, HCL, Amazon, Google and Wadhwani Foundation**; each partnership's exact obligations require programme documents. [UP IT department presentation](https://uplc.up.gov.in/policy/Department-of-IT-and-Electronics-2025.pdf).
- Goals: digital literacy, employability, rural–urban inclusion and startup capability, including Noida's ecosystem. Source's **14,000+ DPIIT startups/US$8.6 billion since 2014** are dated reported counts/funding estimates without verified cutoff, not programme achievements.
## Distinguish funding and programmes
- UP-AGREES IBRD support should not be described as a verified joint loan earmarked equally for AI Pragya. National **IndiaAI Mission approved 7 March 2024: ₹10,371.92 crore**, over five years; source's **₹2,000 crore** is not its total approved outlay. [Cabinet decision](https://www.pib.gov.in/Pressreleaseshare.aspx?PRID=2012357&lang=2&reg=48).
- The issue lists **PMKVY 4.0** AI training, Digital India future-skills support, **₹500 crore AI education CoE** and State examples **Telangana T-AIM/Tamil Nadu AI for Youth**. These are separate programmes/allocations; unverified **19,000 trained/₹1,000 crore skilling** must not be conflated with AI Pragya reach.
- Evaluate access/connectivity, curriculum quality, trainers, cybersecurity/ethical literacy, certification and actual placements; training announcements alone do not prove employment or bridging the digital divide.
