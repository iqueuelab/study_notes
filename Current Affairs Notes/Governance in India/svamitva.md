# SVAMITVA: rural property cards and drone surveys

## Scheme identity
- **Survey of Villages and Mapping with Improvised Technology in Village Areas (SVAMITVA)**; **Ministry of Panchayati Raj**, **Central Sector scheme**, launched **24 April 2020**, National Panchayati Raj Day; nationwide phase followed in **2021**.
- Maps **inhabited village abadi areas**, using **drones**, Survey of India surveying support, State revenue/Panchayati Raj departments and local participation. **Not a blanket cadastral survey of every agricultural field**.
- **Property cards / Record of Rights** document residential-property boundaries/rights through State legal processes. A drone image alone neither settles disputed title nor grants land to landless households.
- **18 January 2025**: about **65 lakh cards**, **50,000+ villages**, **10 States + 2 UTs**. [Official distribution brief](https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2025/jan/doc2025118486901.pdf).
## How it helps
- Demarcation + local verification/objections → property records → fewer disputes and clearer transactions; potential bank collateral, household investment and revenue/planning for Gram Panchayats.
- GIS-based village development planning supports roads, water, drainage and service allocation; this is **rural**, not principally urban planning.
- Credit access remains subject to legal enforceability and lending assessment; property cards do not guarantee a loan.
## Implementation safeguards
- Pilot in selected States, then phased expansion; source 2025 full-coverage ambition is a target, not proof all villages surveyed/cards issued.
- Accurate survey/georeferencing, resolve old-record conflicts, public notice and grievance access; ensure women/co-owners and marginalised residents are correctly recorded.
- Coordinate Survey of India, State departments and Gram Panchayats; protect personal/property data, train staff and maintain records after transactions.
- Mains: technology enables tenure security only when supported by **law, verification, inclusion and ongoing record updates**.
