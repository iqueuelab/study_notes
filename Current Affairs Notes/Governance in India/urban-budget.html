Urban Challenge Fund

  • Announced corpus ₹1 lakh crore, FY2025–26 allocation ₹10,000 crore; bankable growth-hub, redevelopment, water/sanitation projects.
  • Finance up to 25% of project cost; at least 50% from bonds, bank loans and PPPs, not necessarily 50% private equity alone. Budget announcement.

Priorities and risks

  • Metro versus buses/last-mile access, affordable housing, sanitation, climate resilience and employment need integrated assessment; capital intensity is not in itself proof of poor public value.
  • 74th Amendment concerns urban self-government; 73rd rural panchayats. Municipal taxes, predictable transfers, State Finance Commissions, staffing and transparent borrowing determine usable autonomy.
  • PPP/bond finance needs repayment capacity, risk allocation and disclosure; otherwise projects may favour profitable enclaves while excluding smaller cities and poorer residents.
