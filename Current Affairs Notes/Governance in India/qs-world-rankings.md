# QS university rankings and higher-education quality

## QS 2026 edition released in 2025
- **54 Indian institutions**, versus 11 in 2015; **IIT Delhi 123** leads, with IIT Bombay, IIT Madras, IIT Kharagpur and IISc among prominent entries. **Ashoka and Shiv Nadar** are among eight first-time Indian entrants. Ranking edition year is not release year. [QS 2026](https://www.topuniversities.com/world-university-rankings/2026).

- Source combines international-faculty/student weights into 10%; separate indicators explain the nine weighted dimensions. Ratios reflect the selected population/institution; source **national 19:1/Ashoka 11.5:1/desired 10–15:1** are contextual, not a universal legal standard.
- Improvement priorities: research quality and integrity, faculty hiring/time, international collaboration/student access, industry-linked employment support and sustainable campuses. Reputation/citation metrics are partial proxies; rank changes do not prove equal improvement in every institution or guarantee graduate jobs.
