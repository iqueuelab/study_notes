# Sanchar Saathi and National Broadband Mission 2.0

## Citizen telecom protection
- **17 January 2025**, Department of Telecommunications: **Sanchar Saathi mobile app**; report suspected fraudulent communications through **Chakshu**, check connections in one's name, request blocking/tracing of lost/stolen mobiles, verify handset authenticity using **IMEI**.
- IMEI identifies a handset; blocking/tracing assistance does not guarantee recovery. Suspected-communication reporting differs from complaint/remedy for money already lost.
## NBM 2.0 targets: 2030
- Optical fibre connectivity to **2.70 lakh villages**; **95% uptime**; broadband access for **90% anchor institutions**, including schools, health centres, Anganwadis and Panchayat offices.
- Source Right of Way clearance goal **30 days**, from about **60 days**, alongside **Telecommunications Act 2023 / RoW Rules 2024**; approval-time targets are not achieved performance everywhere.
- **Government/PSU fibre mapping by 2026**, **PM Gati Shakti** coordination; **Call Before u Dig (CBuD)** reduces excavation damage through utility–excavator coordination.
- Infrastructure + affordable access + reliability + digital literacy/security determine meaningful inclusion. [Official launch](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2093732&lang=2&reg=3).
