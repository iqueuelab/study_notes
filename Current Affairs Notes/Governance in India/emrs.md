# Eklavya Model Residential Schools

## Recall
- Began 1997–98 for quality residential education for Scheduled Tribe children; historical grants under **Article 275(1)**, later central scheme framework.
- Coeducational Classes VI–XII, usually 480 students/equal boy–girl seats, free education/CBSE curriculum, local culture, sport and skills. Non-ST admission up to 10% is subject to scheme rules.
- **NESTS**, autonomous society under Tribal Affairs Ministry, coordinates EMRS; State societies support operation.
-2018–19 expansion criterion: blocks with over 50%ST population and at least 20, 000 ST residents. Original 2022 target is not proof universal completion.
-15-acre site requirement can be difficult in hills/Northeast/conflict areas; dispersed tribal populations may miss density thresholds. Teacher vacancies, contractual staffing and variable quality need attention; source July 2022 staff figures are historical.

## 2025 entrance examinations
- Tribal Affairs Ministry reported **596 EMRS students across 12 States** qualifying in engineering/medical entrance examinations: **218 JEE Mains, 34 JEE Advanced and 344 NEET**; qualification does not itself establish admission. The Ministry planned scholarship and application support.
- Correction: **NESTS was established in April 2019**, distinct from the 2018–19 policy expansion. Its 2022 teacher-shortage example is historical, not proof no national recruitment rules existed in July 2025.
