# BIS certification and consumer safety

## Standards and conformity
- National standards body under Department of Consumer Affairs; headquarters New Delhi. BIS succeeded ISI in **1987**, under the earlier 1986 Act; current **BIS Act 2016 commenced in 2017**.
- Formulates Indian Standards, runs conformity certification/testing and supports consumer awareness. Certification is generally voluntary unless an applicable **Quality Control Order** makes compliance compulsory. [BIS](https://www.bis.gov.in/product-certification/product-certification-overivews/?lang=en).
- ISI mark concerns product conformity; hallmarking concerns specified precious-metal purity. Neither means every conceivable product hazard is ruled out.
- Ecomark addresses environmental criteria; **2024 rules provide CPCB implementation in partnership with BIS**, not a wholly unchanged older arrangement. [Environment Ministry](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2061878&lang=2&reg=3).
- E-commerce enforcement targets products violating applicable compulsory standards/marking, not every uncertified product regardless of legal category.
- Use traceable licences, marketplace checks, credible testing and consumer complaints; simplify MSME compliance without weakening safety. Council-size claims require the applicable rules rather than an unexplained fixed 25-member total.
