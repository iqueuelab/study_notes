# Healthcare financing and household costs

## Correct denominator

- **NHA2021–22**, released September 2024: out-of-pocket spending **39.4% of total health expenditure**, ~1.51%GDP; government health spending~1.84%GDP. Source 47%/2.3% uses older/mismatched figures. [Health Ministry](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2058791&lang=2&reg=48).
- Union health-budget allocation is not total Centre+State public health spending. Budget 2023–24 figures are historical, not March 2025 current spending.
- Reduce **household financial burden**, while increasing efficient public provision; cutting all health spending would undermine access.

## Reforms

- Prevention, primary care, affordable drugs/diagnostics, referral systems and usable insurance reduce catastrophic costs; coverage enrolment is not guaranteed effective access.
- National Health Policy public-spending target 2.5%GDP; source proposed cost commission is a recommendation, not an established regulator.
- Clinical Establishments **Act 2010 /Central Rules 2012**, not source's 2010 Rules; applicable regulation and fair rates need enforcement/State-context checks.
- HAQ rank 145/195 and poverty/medical-inflation estimates are dated study results; distinguish medical-price indices and forecasts from general inflation.
