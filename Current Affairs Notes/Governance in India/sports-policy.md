# Sports policy, governance and physical literacy

## National Sports Policy 2025
- Cabinet approved **NSP 2025 on 1 July**, superseding 2001 policy. Five pillars: **global excellence; economic development; social development; mass movement; education integration**. Whole-government coordination, State model policies, sport science/technology, governance, PPP/CSR and measurable monitoring support implementation; **2036 Olympic hosting is an aspiration**, not an award already secured. [Cabinet](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2141141&lang=2&reg=48).

- State-subject status, fragmented funding, weak coordination, limited infrastructure and undervaluing sport careers constrain mass participation. Promote school physical literacy, qualified coaches, rural access, women/disability inclusion, independent fair anti-doping procedures, athlete welfare and dual careers. WADA positive-test statistics are not automatically finally adjudicated doping violations.
- Sporting recall: men's hockey success 1928–1980; Milkha Singh, Gurbachan Singh, Kamaljeet Sandhu; Rathore Olympic silver 2004, Bindra gold 2008,**Vijender bronze 2008** (not 2012), Mary Kom bronze 2012. Later medals cannot be causally attributed solely to one scheme.
- **Tokyo 2020 PYQ 2021: (b)2 only.** Tokyo Games slogan **“United by Emotion”**; “A New World” belonged to Rio 2016. IOC's 2021 addition of “Together” to the general Olympic motto is a separate fact. Tokyo included sport climbing, surfing, skateboarding, karate and returning baseball/softball. [Tokyo organising account](https://www.paralympic.org/news/united-emotion-be-tokyo-2020-games-motto).
