# SANKALP: skilling institutions and implementation

## Programme and implementation gap
- **Skill Acquisition and Knowledge Awareness for Livelihood Promotion (SANKALP)**, Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship: Cabinet approval **October 2017**, launch **January 2018**; strengthens institutions, short-term skills, industry linkages and inclusion of marginalised groups.
- Approved **₹4,455 crore** design: World Bank assistance **₹3,300 crore**, State leverage **₹660 crore**, industry leverage **₹495 crore**. Original March 2023 completion was extended to March 2024; these are programme-era dates, not a fresh February 2026 launch.
- **CAG Report 8 of 2024:** ministry disbursement **₹850.71 crore** against **₹1,924.08 crore budget provision**, about **44%**, during 2017–18–2023–24 **up to December 2023**. The newspaper's October cutoff differs from the audit table; use the audit's explicit period. This is not 44% of the full ₹4,455 crore design outlay. [CAG report](https://saiindia.gov.in/webroot/uploads/download_audit_report/2024/Report-No.-8-of-2024_Civil_English_PDF-A-066b48ba3059fd9.48981092.pdf).
- World Bank disbursement of **₹1,606.15 crore (86% of the first agreed US$250 million tranche)** and ministry spending are different flows. Weak preparedness, guideline compliance and monitoring delayed progress; track financial execution alongside training quality, placements, inclusion and institutional capacity.
