# Indian Statistical Institute: autonomy and the 1959 Act

## December 2025: proposed institutional reform
- The issue reports a Kolkata protest by **over 1,500 academics/researchers** over the proposed replacement of the **Indian Statistical Institute Act, 1959**. Academic freedom, representative governance and government control are the policy issues; protest concerns are not enacted legal consequences.
- **ISI**, founded in **1931 by P. C. Mahalanobis in Calcutta**, is a statistics, mathematics, quantitative economics and computing research/teaching institution under **MoSPI**. Its work supports sample surveys, statistical capacity and evidence-based economic planning; Mahalanobis distance is associated with its founder.
- **1959 Act:** section **3** declares national importance; **4** authorises examinations/degrees/diplomas in specified statistical and allied fields; **5–6** cover government finance and audit; **7** requires prior approval for specified institutional changes; **8–10** provide review committees and access; **11–12** provide directions and conditional assumption of control. Accountability powers coexist with institutional autonomy. [Act text](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/1425/1/A1959-57.pdf).
- **Correction:** the **28 November 2025 revised draft Bill** expressly retains **Institution of National Importance** status and proposes incorporation as a **statutory body corporate**. It is inaccurate to state that replacement necessarily abolishes that status or leaves ISI without a statute. Debate the proposed governance/appointment structure and safeguards on their terms; a draft is not an operative repeal. [MoSPI revised draft](https://www.mospi.gov.in/uploads/announcements/announcements_1764341509114_de871836-274e-4fe5-bce5-ae97b81214dd_Revised_draft_ISI_Bill_28.11.2025.pdf).
