# National Disaster Response Force and specialised search capability

## Institutional role
- **NDRF force**, constituted under **Disaster Management Act 2005, section 44**, raised 2006 with 8 battalions; source-era strength 16. Under **Ministry of Home Affairs**, headed by a Director General, with personnel drawn from BSF/CRPF/CISF/ITBP/SSB/Assam Rifles. [NDRF](https://www.ndrf.gov.in/en/about-us).
- Specialised multi-skilled response to natural/man-made disasters; pre-positioning reduces response time. International deployments include Japan 2011 and Nepal 2015. Do not confuse the force with the similarly abbreviated disaster-response **fund**.
## July 2025 cadaver-dog training
- Source reports first batch of about **six Belgian Malinois/Labrador dogs** trained at **Arakkonam (Tamil Nadu) and Ghaziabad (UP)** to locate human remains using specialised decompositional scent training.
- Cadaver detection complements live-victim search; finding remains supports identification and families' closure. It does not replace rapid rescue during the crucial early response period.
