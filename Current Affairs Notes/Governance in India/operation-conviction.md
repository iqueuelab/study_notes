# Operation Conviction and prosecution outcomes

## Uttar Pradesh campaign
- Launched **1 July 2023** to prioritise serious-crime prosecution, including murder, rape, organised crime and crimes against children; the July 2025 report claims **97,158 people convicted** by mid-June 2025.
- Source dataset: **114,029 identified cases;74,388 disposed;69,422 conviction cases**. People and cases are different denominators; **69,422/74,388≈93.3%** is the reported selected-case conviction ratio, not a statewide conviction rate for all registered crimes or causal proof of campaign effectiveness.
| Sentence category | Reported people |
|---|---:|
| Death |68|
| Life |8,172|
| Over 20 years |1,453|
| Up to 20 years |87,465|
- POCSO cases: **17 death/619 life sentences**. Organised-crime figures: **395 people convicted in 272 major cases;29 of 69 identified mafia figures convicted**. Daily averages **143 disposals/187 people convicted** are source working-day averages.
- Assess investigation quality, timely evidence/witness support, prosecution coordination and victim support alongside fair trial, legal aid and appeal outcomes. High conviction counts alone cannot establish reduced crime or justify coercive investigation.
