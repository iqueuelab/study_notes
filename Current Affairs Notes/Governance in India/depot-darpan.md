# Depot Darpan and smart foodgrain storage

## May 2025 initiative and status
- **DFPD, Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution**: digital depot appraisal for foodgrain quality, safety, accountability and operational efficiency.
- **1 May release scheduled formal inauguration for 20 May 2025**; the source's “launched” wording is premature for the 5 May issue. Around **2,278 warehouses** were envisaged for onboarding: FCI/CWC-owned and hired State/private depots; not proof all had completed adoption. AI/security tools were on a **pilot basis**. [DFPD announcement](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2125724&lang=2&reg=48).
- Managers upload **geo-tagged** infrastructure/operation data → automated composite/star ratings and action points → **100% supervisory validation** plus random third-party audits → improvement reviews. Mobile app supports supervisory monitoring/reports.
- Assess **safety, storage condition, environment, technology and statutory compliance**, alongside stock turnover, grain losses, space use, staff costs and financial performance.
- Sensors: **temperature/humidity** for storage conditions; **CO₂** as an infestation-risk signal; **phosphine** for fumigation/worker safety; **door sensors** for access and ventilation; **fire/smoke** warning. AI bag counting, **ANPR** vehicle identification and facial recognition support stock/security checks.
- Food security/PDS and **SDG 2** links: reduce losses/pilferage and improve utilisation. These are intended gains, not quantified savings already achieved; data accuracy, maintenance, audit independence and worker/privacy safeguards matter.
