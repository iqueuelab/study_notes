# e-RaktKosh and rare blood donor coordination

## Blood safety and rare donors
- **e-RaktKosh**, launched **7 April 2016**, is a centralised blood-centre management platform: donor records/biometric identification, blood grouping, transfusion-transmitted infection screening, antibody screening, stock management and biomedical-waste records. Works within Drugs and Cosmetics law and National Blood Policy standards. [Official functions](https://eraktkosh.mohfw.gov.in/BLDAHIMS/bloodbank/about.cnt).
- June reporting describes planned integration of the **Rare Donor Registry of India** with e-RaktKosh: a national search/coordination system helps locate compatible blood in emergencies. **Bombay phenotype and Rh-null** illustrate rare blood groups; a rare phenotype is not itself a blood disorder.
- Revision: red-cell disorders include anaemias; leucocyte/haematological malignancies include leukaemia, lymphoma and myeloma; haemophilia/von Willebrand disease concern clotting; thrombocytopenia means low platelets. Thalassaemia/sickle-cell disease are inherited red-cell conditions, whose frequency varies substantially by population—do not label them universally rare.
- Real-time availability, confirmatory compatibility testing, donor consent/privacy and reliable transport all matter; a donor match in a database does not by itself complete safe transfusion.
