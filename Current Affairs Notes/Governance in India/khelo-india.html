Governance in India

Khelo India and youth sports

Editable notes

Bihar Youth Games: April 2025 preview

  • Source announces the 7th Khelo India Youth Games, 4–15 May 2025, in Patna, Rajgir, Gaya, Bhagalpur and Begusarai. These were scheduled dates at the issue date; Delhi also hosted specialised events including shooting, gymnastics and track cycling.
  • 2025 KIYG was under-18, not the source's recycled school under-17/college under-21 table. Sports Ministry confirmation

Evolution and purpose

  • 2018: Khelo India School Games, New Delhi; 2019: renamed Youth Games, Pune/Maharashtra; 2020: Assam; the 2021 edition held in 2022 in Haryana; 2023: Madhya Pradesh; 2024: Tamil Nadu; 2025: Bihar.
  • Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports/SAI ecosystem: grassroots participation, sports infrastructure, talent identification, training and competition. Youth, university, winter and para competitions serve different groups; avoid collapsing their eligibility.
  • Talent support follows selection and scheme conditions, not automatic grants to every participant. The source's ₹5 lakh annually for eight years reflects an earlier talent-development formulation; amounts/components evolved and should not be treated as an unchanging 2025 entitlement.
  • Khelo India mobile app (2019) supports fitness assessment and sports information; digital engagement complements coaching/facilities.
  • Bihar hosting can broaden access and regional sporting infrastructure, tourism and employment. Durable gains require maintenance, qualified coaches, safe participation, gender/disability inclusion and athlete pathways beyond the tournament.
  • Mains: distinguish participation → talent identification → sustained development → elite outcomes; hosting and medal counts alone do not demonstrate universal fitness or lasting local benefits.

Bihar Youth Games: 4 May 2025 opening

  • The 7th Khelo India Youth Games inaugurated on 4 May 2025, with competition scheduled through 15 May; source's “will be held” preview can now be updated to the opening milestone. The existing notes retain Bihar host cities, Delhi specialised venues, evolution and corrected under-18 eligibility. Official inaugural account.
  • An opening ceremony is not proof every planned competition/infrastructure benefit has been completed. Existing talent-support qualifications continue to apply; the PDF's under-17/under-21 and universal ₹5-lakh-for-eight-years table should not replace them.

21–23 August 2025: Dal Lake water-sports festival

  • First Khelo India Water Sports Festival, Srinagar's Dal Lake, organised by SAI and the J&K Sports Council; rowing, canoeing and kayaking medal events, with water skiing, dragon boat and shikara demonstrations. This broadens the calendar beyond winter, youth, university, para and the May 2025 Diu Beach Games.
  • The embedded opening report counts 409 athletes, 36 States/UTs and 24 gold medals, including 202 women, and team sizes MP 62, Haryana 37, Odisha 34 and Kerala 33. These are the opening report's event figures, not national participation statistics. Official festival programme.
  • Develop facilities, coaches, sports science and sustained competition pathways; hosting can support lake livelihoods/tourism but requires pollution and carrying-capacity safeguards. Existing notes retain the corrected Youth Games under-18 eligibility and evolving talent-support components; the recycled under-17/under-21 and ₹5 lakh for eight years formula is not a universal new 2025 entitlement.
  • Practice answer 1, 2 and 3 only (B): Khelo India includes indigenous and non-Olympic sports; Olympic-only restriction is false.
