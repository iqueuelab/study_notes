EU age verification and children’s online safety

Editable notes

July 2025 blueprint

  • European Commission released an open-source age-verification blueprint on 14 July 2025, supporting child protection under the Digital Services Act, Article 28. Pilot states: Denmark, France, Greece, Italy and Spain.
  • Issuer checks age using identity evidence; website receives only over-18 proof, not name/date of birth. Separate issuance/presentation and single-use proofs aim to prevent cross-service tracking.
  • Built for compatibility with forthcoming European Digital Identity Wallets (then expected by end 2026). Zero-knowledge-proof integration was ongoing, not an already completed feature of the initial July version. Commission's dated release.
  • Debate: child safety versus data minimisation/security, usability, tracking and displacement towards unregulated sites. Industry's proposed device/OS-level checks are an alternative policy position, not an adopted EU rule. Source French under-15 social-media restriction is a political proposal, not proof of a uniform EU ban.
  • Distinguish age verification (threshold proof) from age estimation, and assess effective privacy guarantees across issuer, device and platform.
