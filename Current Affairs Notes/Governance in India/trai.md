# TRAI, telecom regulation and TDSAT

## Institution

- **TRAI Act 1997**, established **20 February 1997**, New Delhi; statutory regulator for specified telecommunications/broadcasting matters.
- **Chair +not more than two whole-time +not more than two part-time members**, appointed by the Central Government. Do not learn source's different fixed membership as the Act's rule. [Official annual report](https://www.trai.gov.in/sites/default/files/2024-10/TRAI-English-Annual-Report-10032014.pdf).

## Division of responsibility

| Institution | Main distinction |
| --- | --- |
| TRAI | Tariff/quality/interconnection regulation and recommendations; competition/consumer interests |
| DoT /Government | Licensing and spectrum administration under the legal framework; TRAI does not itself allocate every spectrum licence |
| TDSAT | Telecom dispute adjudication/appellate tribunal, separated by **2000 amendment** |

- Regulation balances affordable access, quality, innovation, fair competition and viable investment. Recommendations are not identical to binding government licensing decisions.
- Individual subscriber complaints ordinarily use service-provider grievance mechanisms; TRAI is not a general consumer court adjudicating every complaint.
- Digital divide, unsolicited communication, service quality and emerging technologies require transparent consultation, evidence and coordination with other regulators.
