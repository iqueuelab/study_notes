Governance in India

Unapproved drug combinations and pharmaceutical-export ethics

Editable notes

Action and correction

  • Tapentadol: opioid analgesic; carisoprodol: centrally acting muscle relaxant. Individually approved products do not make their fixed-dose combination approved.
  • February 2025 action followed reports of Aveo exports to West Africa: stop activities, withdraw export NOCs and act on unauthorised manufacturing permissions. Joint audit21–22 February 2025, not PDF's2024. Health Ministry23 February release.
  • Official source reports ~1.3 crore tablets/capsules /26 API batches and wider inspections 905 units /694 actions; separate seizure from concluded criminal guilt and inspection from permanent closure.
  • Addiction/misuse and combined CNS effects are public-health concerns; whether a drug is listed under NDPS is different from whether it is regulated under drug law.

Institutions and ethics

  • CDSCO, national regulator under MoHFW, New Delhi; headed by DCGI. New drugs/trials/imports/standards and specified categories involve central powers; State regulators have important manufacturing/sale enforcement roles.
  • Drugs and Cosmetics Act1940 /Rules /New Drugs and Clinical Trials Rules2019; devices use their relevant framework. No single officer personally issues every drug licence in India.
  • Safe exports require approvals/quality, traceability, importing-country coordination and ethical marketing. Weaker enforcement abroad does not justify exporting unsafe/unapproved combinations.
  • Risk-based joint inspections, authentic supply-chain data, recalls/enforcement with due process and international alerts protect patients and India's pharmaceutical credibility.
  • Proposal to add substances to NDPS is not proof notification occurred; do not confuse action against this combination with a blanket ban on every lawful standalone product.
