Selection/featuresSECC 2011 deprivation, Gram Sabha verification, geo-tagged monitoringFemale/joint ownership emphasis; statutory towns/planning areas
  • Rural priority groups include SC/STs, freed bonded labourers, vulnerable poor households, eligible defence/paramilitary families, persons with disabilities and minorities, subject to scheme criteria.
  • PMAY-G cost sharing: generally 60:40 Centre:State, 90:10 NE/Himalayan arrangements, and central funding for relevant UT arrangements. Avoid treating the source's simplified J&K/UT table as a timeless universal funding rule.
  • Original PMAY-U four verticals: in-situ slum redevelopment using land as resource; credit-linked interest subsidy; affordable housing in partnership; beneficiary-led construction/enhancement. Original AHP/BLC central support was ₹1.5 lakh per eligible EWS house. Source CLSS examples: 6.5% subsidy up to ₹6 lakh EWS/LIG loan, 4% on ₹9 lakh MIG-I, 3% on ₹12 lakh MIG-II; historical windows/eligibility apply.

June 18 development: PMAY-U 2.0

  • CSMC approved 2,34,864 homes across nine States, taking total PMAY-U 2.0 sanctions to about 7.10 lakh. States: Assam, Bihar, Chhattisgarh, Gujarat, Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra, Odisha, Rajasthan, Uttar Pradesh. MoHUA, 18 June 2025.
  • This clipping concerns Urban 2.0, even though placed beside rural background in the PDF. “Housing for All by 2022” is an original target, not a claim that the programme ended or achieved universality in 2022. Source March 2023 rural figures (2.85 crore sanctioned/2.22 crore complete) and undated original-urban dashboard figures (118.64/83.67 lakh) are older snapshots, not June 2025 progress.
