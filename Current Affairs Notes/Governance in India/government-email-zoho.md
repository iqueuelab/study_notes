# Government email modernisation and Zoho

## October 2025: reported migration and digital sovereignty
- The issue reports roughly twelve lakh official government email accounts, including PMO accounts, migrated to a Zoho-developed platform over the preceding year. Existing gov.in/nic.in domains continue; change of software/backend does not require a change of official address.
- NIC, under MeitY, is the government's informatics/service organisation; Zoho supplies Indian-developed email/productivity technology. Distinguish public-service responsibility, software supplier, hosting infrastructure and data ownership. A later parliamentary clarification confirms NIC selected Zoho as Master System Integrator and NIC remains the mandated official email provider; use this to qualify the October “NIC replaced” framing, not to import the later account total. [Parliament clarification of the arrangement](https://sansad.in/getFile/loksabhaquestions/annex/186/AU1805_WXRrbO.pdf?source=pqals).
- Integration with documents, spreadsheets and presentations can improve collaboration; domestic capability and jurisdiction support digital-sovereignty goals. Domestic ownership or an audit does not by itself guarantee security, lower costs or immunity from breaches. The source's blanket suggestion that open-source software is inherently insecure is unsupported.
- Assess encryption/access control, auditability, uptime, disaster recovery, training and interoperability/exit arrangements. Vendor concentration and migration disruption remain concerns even with an Indian supplier. The issue's claimed NIC/CERT-In/external audits are source-reported, not independent proof every security risk was eliminated.
