# Disaster-resilient telecom infrastructure

## CDRI–DoT framework
- **DRRAF report launched 12 February 2025**: disaster risk/resilience assessment for telecom infrastructure; national and subnational planning. [DoT announcement](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2102281&lang=2&reg=48).
- Cyclones, floods, earthquakes, power failure and damaged coastal cable landings can cut communications precisely when warnings/rescue need them.
| Weak link | Resilience measure |
| --- | --- |
| Towers /overhead lines | Risk-appropriate structural design, siting, protected cables |
| Power /fuel supply | Elevate equipment, diverse backup, adequate storage/emergency access |
| Cable landing /backhaul | Protect sites, diversify routes and plan specialist repairs |
| Fragmented response | Shared damage data, drills, operator–government coordination |
- **Dig-once:** coordinate underground utilities during excavation; buried cable still faces flood/digging risk. Subsea fibre offers high-capacity intercontinental links, not automatically lower latency than every land route.
- **Parametric insurance** pays on defined hazard triggers; fast payment but **basis risk** means payment may differ from actual loss.
- Humanitarian communications, redundancy, maintenance and trained teams matter alongside new infrastructure. Measure restored service/time and vulnerable-community access.
