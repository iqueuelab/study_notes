# Dharti Aaba Janjatiya Gram Utkarsh Abhiyan

## Scheme and June outreach
- **DAJGUA**, Ministry of Tribal Affairs, launched **2 October 2024, Hazaribagh, Jharkhand**, not2023; honours **Birsa Munda, Dharti Aaba**. **25 interventions,17 ministries**, 63,000+ tribal villages, **₹79,156 crore** approved outlay: infrastructure, health, education and livelihoods through convergence. [Launch release](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleaseIframePage.aspx?PRID=2061196&lang=2&reg=48).
- Issue reports **22,000 Jan Seva camps in nine days, over53lakh people reached**: Aadhaar1.38lakh, Ayushman cards1.68lakh, PM-Kisan46,000, Ujjwala22,000, Jan Dhan32,000. Enrolments do not establish unique beneficiaries across schemes or actual sustained use.
- **Saturation:** every eligible person/household receives entitled schemes. Doorstep documentation, banking, health and livelihood access address remoteness; monitor exclusions and actual service outcomes.
- Adjacent **2014 watershed PYQ: None(d)**, not3only: DPAP/DDP—Rural Development; NWDPRA—Agriculture. Already captured June16. [Contemporary PIB](https://www.pib.gov.in/newsite/PrintRelease.aspx?relid=78596).
