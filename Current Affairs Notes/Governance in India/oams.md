# Government assurances and OAMS

## Parliament to implementation
- **Online Assurances Monitoring System**, Ministry of Parliamentary Affairs: records and tracks ministerial assurances made during questions/debates.
- An assurance is an undertaking to provide information or take specified action; not every political promise becomes a parliamentary assurance automatically.
- Normally expected within three months; extensions and requests for dropping assurances undergo the appropriate process. Delay is not proof every assurance automatically lapses.
- Ministry/department follows up → implementation report → parliamentary Committee on Government Assurances scrutiny.
- Digital tracking supports visibility and coordination but cannot ensure fulfilment without accurate updates, responsible officers and committee follow-up. AI-based prioritisation is a recommendation, not the source of legal accountability.
