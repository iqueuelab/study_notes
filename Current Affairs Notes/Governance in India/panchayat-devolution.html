Governance in India

Panchayat devolution, finance and democratic participation

Editable notes

Constitutional design

  • 73rd Amendment Act, 1992, effective 24 April 1993: Part IX; elected rural local government, Gram Sabha, reservations, regular elections and State Finance Commissions.
  • Village →intermediate →district tiers; intermediate tier may be omitted in States with population ≤20 lakh. Article 243G /Eleventh Schedule's 29 subjects: State legislatures may devolve authority; listing does not automatically transfer every function/staff/fund.
  • At least one-third of seats/chairperson offices reserved for women constitutionally; many States provide 50% by their laws. Source treats 50% as the original nationwide constitutional rule: incorrect.

Devolution Index 2024, released 13 February 2025

  • Ministry of Panchayati Raj/IIPA assessment; six dimensions: framework, functions, finances, functionaries, capacity building, accountability.
  • Report scores: Karnataka 72.23, Kerala 70.59, Tamil Nadu 68.38. Scores measure the stated index, not percentages of every possible power transferred. Official report.
  • Helps citizens compare decentralisation and governments identify weak dimensions; legal delegation must be matched by practical staff/funding authority.

Distress and reform

  • Incomplete 3Fs—functions, funds, functionaries, weak own revenues, delayed SFC action, tied schemes and political interference can make Panchayats delivery agents rather than autonomous governments.
  • 15th FC FY2021–26 rural-local grants: 40% untied /60% tied, chiefly water/sanitation; 2020–21 interim 50/50. PDF's “60% untied” is wrong. MoPR Finance Commission reports.
  • DBT/JAM can change payment channels but do not eliminate Gram Sabha participation, local verification or grievance responsibilities. PM-KISAN source ₹6,000 annually is a scheme entitlement, not a Panchayat grant.
  • Strengthen meaningful Gram Sabha/GPDP planning, devolved staff, predictable transfers, local tax capacity, social audit, transparent digital records and elected-member training.
  • Reported 14 lakh women representatives is dated; source's 20%-State full-devolution and 40%-urban-population figures lack clear verified denominators. Avoid using them as census facts.
  • Mains: fiscal flexibility +accountability +community voice; preserve welfare delivery while empowering local institutions and addressing rural–urban transition.

Practice-question corrections

  • SFCs, the normal three-tier structure and at least one-third women's reservation all arise from the constitutional Panchayat framework; the PDF's key incorrectly treats one-third reservation as false. Remember the small-State intermediate-tier exception.
  • Untied grants support local flexibility; tied grants specify purposes. Source's “60% untied” and its explanation reversing the statement numbers are unreliable; use the corrected 15th FC split above.
  • These questions are supplied coaching practice, not dated official PYQs.
