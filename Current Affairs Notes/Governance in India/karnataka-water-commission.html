Governance in India

Karnataka State Water Commission proposal

Editable notes

November 2025 proposal: permanent technical water institution

  • Karnataka proposed a permanent State Water Commission, modelled on the Central Water Commission, with legislation planned. Its statutory status, final composition and binding powers depend on an enacted law; the report does not establish that it already exists or can adjudicate interstate disputes.
  • Issue describes 10–15 members spanning irrigation/finance officials, water/environment experts and farmers. Proposed tasks: assess water availability/demand; advise on Cauvery, Krishna and Mahadayi disputes; coordinate data and legal preparation; improve cropping choices, irrigation efficiency and flood/drought resilience; consider 50-year Bengaluru water needs, including Cauvery supplies.
  • Article 246 and Seventh Schedule: State List Entry 17 covers water subject to Union List Entry 56, which permits parliamentary regulation/development of interstate rivers and river valleys in the public interest. A State advisory body cannot override tribunal/court orders or redistribute another State's entitlement.
  • CWC is a Union technical organisation under the Ministry of Jal Shakti; technical assessment differs from binding dispute adjudication. Revision chain: basin-level data → demand/crop planning → efficient canals/micro-irrigation and groundwater management → drought/flood preparedness → transparent allocation and accountability. National Water Policy 2012, Jal Shakti Abhiyan and Atal Bhujal Yojana provide related context; the source's vaguely labelled “Aatmanirbhar Krishi” should not be treated as a verified separate national scheme.
