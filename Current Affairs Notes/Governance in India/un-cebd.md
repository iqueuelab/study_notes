# UN committee on big data for official statistics

## Institution and purpose
- India joined **UN Committee of Experts on Big Data and Data Science for Official Statistics (UN-CEBD)** in **January 2025**.
- Established **2014**, initially chaired by **Australia**; supports official-statistics innovation, including **2030 SDG monitoring**. Reports to the **UN Statistical Commission**; not the UN Chief Executives Board.
| Structure | Function |
| --- | --- |
| Advisory Board | Strategic management; source describes roughly four meetings/year |
| Bureau | Continuing operational coordination |
| Task teams | Methods, applications and capacity building |
- Priorities: data sources/technology, training and experience-sharing, international/cross-border cooperation, advocacy and public trust. [UN Statistics mandate](https://unstats.un.org/bigdata/about/index.cshtml).
- Big data can improve timeliness/detail, but coverage bias, privacy, security, reproducibility and quality need safeguards; it does not automatically replace representative surveys/censuses.
