# Registrar General of India and civil registration

## Birth certificates and population statistics
- RGI asked States to issue newborns' birth certificates to mothers **before hospital discharge**, especially in public hospitals: registration is essential for identity, services and reliable population statistics.
- RGI's office was set up in **1949 under MHA**; Registrar General and ex-officio Census Commissioner oversees Census and civil-registration coordination. Census is periodic; **Civil Registration System records births/deaths continuously**.
- **Registration of Births and Deaths Act, 1969, section 12:** certificate/extract after registration. The **2023 amendment**, effective **1 October 2023**, strengthens national/state electronic databases; avoid implying every local registrar's workflow is identical or every civil-registration event is a Census enumeration.
- Linguistic surveys document India's language diversity; Grierson's original Linguistic Survey culminated in **1928**. Avoid assigning the RGI a fixed Joint Secretary rank or claiming every linguistic survey is inseparable from a decennial Census.
- **AG/SG PYQ 2022:** neither statement correct. Attorney General has parliamentary speaking/participation rights without voting under Article 88; Solicitor General lacks the same constitutional right. AG holds office at President's pleasure under Article 76(4); resignation upon a government change is not constitutionally mandated.
