# Gandhi Peace Prize and Indira Gandhi Prize

## Two distinct awards
- **Indira Gandhi Prize for Peace, Disarmament and Development**: instituted **1986**, administered by **Indira Gandhi Memorial Trust**; recognises peace, development, disarmament and beneficial use of science. **2015 PYQ on 2014 recipient: ISRO (c)**, for peaceful space applications. The source's past-awardee names/years are not a dependable chronology; for example its “Mandela 1987” is incorrect. [Trust’s awardee record](https://indiragandhi.in/en/awardees).
- **Gandhi Peace Prize**: Government of India/Ministry of Culture, instituted **1995**; social, economic and political transformation through non-violence/Gandhian methods. Award includes **₹1 crore, citation and plaque**; distinguish this from the source's outdated Indira-prize cash figure.
- **Five-member Gandhi jury**: **Prime Minister (chair), Chief Justice of India, Lok Sabha Leader of Opposition** (or leader of its single largest opposition party if no recognised LoP), **two eminent persons**. Source's “sometimes one” understates the prescribed composition. **President is not a jury member**; **2025 PYQ: (c), II, III and IV**. [Parliamentary answer/code](https://sansad.in/getFile/loksabhaquestions/annex/175/AU2155.pdf?source=pqals).
- Correct Gandhi award years: **Qaboos bin Said Al Said—2019**, **Sheikh Mujibur Rahman—2020**, **Gita Press—2021**; the PDF incorrectly labels Qaboos as 2022. Prize year and announcement/presentation year can differ. [Culture Ministry list](https://culture.gov.in/awards-category/1634).
