# SWAYAM and SWAYAM Plus: online learning and employability

## Access and academic value
- **SWAYAM: Study Webs of Active–Learning for Young Aspiring Minds**, Ministry of Education's national MOOC platform, formally launched in 2017; the issue reports free AI courses. Covers Class 9 through postgraduate learning; courses are free, while an optional certificate generally requires a fee and proctored examination. University credit transfer follows applicable UGC/AICTE rules rather than automatic acceptance of every course.
- Four learning quadrants: **video lectures → downloadable reading → self-assessment → discussion forum**. Access, equity and quality are aims; devices, connectivity, accessibility, language and completion support determine effective inclusion. [SWAYAM](https://swayam.gov.in/about).
- Coordinator anchors: **NPTEL** engineering; **UGC** non-technical postgraduate; **CEC** undergraduate; **NCERT/NIOS** school; **IGNOU** out-of-school learners; **IIM Bangalore** management; **NITTTR** teacher training; **AICTE** self-paced/international; source also lists Institutes of National Importance for non-technical courses. Institutional remit matters more than memorising an undated coordinator count.
- **SWAYAM Plus**, managed by IIT Madras, links industry and academia for employability: manufacturing, energy, IT/ITES, management, healthcare, hospitality/tourism and Indian knowledge systems. Source describes multilingual content, AI guidance, credit recognition and employment pathways; completing a course is not a job guarantee.
- **2016 PYQ: education access, option D**; the 2016 question predates the 2017 formal launch, so these dates are not contradictory.
