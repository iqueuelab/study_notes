# Apna Ghar: highway rest facilities for truck drivers

## Design and progress
- Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas initiative; **public-sector Oil Marketing Companies** provide safe, hygienic rest at highway retail fuel outlets.
- **1 July 2025: 368 facilities/4,611 beds**. Dormitories typically **10–30 beds**, dhabas/restaurants, self-cooking spaces, toilets, bathing areas (**houdas**) and purified drinking water; app supports bookings and user feedback.
- Rest/access improves truck-driver welfare and can reduce fatigue-related road risk. An OMC delivery model does not establish every facility is a separately procured PPP. [31 July 2025 Parliament-reply release](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleaseIframePage.aspx?PRID=2150823&lang=2&reg=48).
