# Prime Minister Internship Scheme

## March 2025 pilot design
- Ministry of Corporate Affairs; launched in 2024 following the Budget announcement. Overall ambition: **one crore internships in top 500 companies over five years**, not one crore already trained.
- Pilot eligibility: age **21–24**, not in full-time education or full-time employment; other qualification/exclusion criteria apply. It is not a programme automatically open to every current full-time student.
- **12-month internship**, ₹5,000 monthly assistance and ₹6,000 one-time grant; at least six months of relevant professional experience. [MCA pilot account](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2104914&lang=2&reg=48).
- Mobile app improves access/application management. Internship selection is not a guaranteed permanent job; overall top-500 ambition differs from actual participating-company counts in a pilot round.
- Evaluate mentorship, meaningful tasks, safe workplaces, travel/living costs, equitable selection, completion and subsequent employment. Proposed incentives are not automatically enacted tax benefits.
