# Prime Minister Internship Scheme

## March 2025 pilot design
- Ministry of Corporate Affairs; launched in 2024 following the Budget announcement. Overall ambition: **one crore internships in top 500 companies over five years**, not one crore already trained.
- Pilot eligibility: age **21–24**, not in full-time education or full-time employment; other qualification/exclusion criteria apply. It is not a programme automatically open to every current full-time student.
- **12-month internship**, ₹5,000 monthly assistance and ₹6,000 one-time grant; at least six months of relevant professional experience. [MCA pilot account](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2104914&lang=2&reg=48).
- Mobile app improves access/application management. Internship selection is not a guaranteed permanent job; overall top-500 ambition differs from actual participating-company counts in a pilot round.
- Evaluate mentorship, meaningful tasks, safe workplaces, travel/living costs, equitable selection, completion and subsequent employment. Proposed incentives are not automatically enacted tax benefits.

## July 2025 pilot uptake
- Image clipping reports **over 50,000 accepted of 1.53 lakh offers** across two rounds; **over 8,700 joined** in the first round. Around 6% is a rounded joined/all-offers comparison, **not acceptance rate**; second-round acceptance/joining was ongoing. First-round offers over 82,000 to over 60,000 candidates; about 28,000 acceptances. Multiple offers per candidate mean offers and persons are not interchangeable.
- Monthly assistance split **₹4,500 government viaDBT + ₹500 companyCSR**; ₹6,000 joining support and government-paid **PMJJBY/PMSBY** insurance.
- Pilot exclusions include specified elite-institute graduates/professional or higher qualifications, prior/current specified government training, and regular government employees in defined family. Official rule excludes when **any family member's FY 2023–24 income exceeds ₹8 lakh**; source **≥₹8 lakh family income** is imprecise. Family means self, parents, spouse. [Pilot rules](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2061909&lang=2&reg=3).
- Quality mentorship, meaningful work, digital access, living/travel costs and post-internship transition are key to uptake; an internship is not guaranteed employment or automatic eligibility for every MSME as host.
