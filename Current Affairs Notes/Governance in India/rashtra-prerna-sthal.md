# Rashtra Prerna Sthal: memorialisation and governance

## 25 December 2025: Lucknow memorial inaugurated
- PM inaugurated **Rashtra Prerna Sthal**, **Hardoi Road, Lucknow**, on **Atal Bihari Vajpayee's 101 st birth anniversary**, observed as **Good Governance Day(25 December)**. Source describes **65-acre**, lotus-shaped complex with bronze statues and a museum honouring **Vajpayee, Deendayal Upadhyaya and Syama Prasad Mookerjee**.
- Associations: Vajpayee—former PM, coalition politics/governance; Upadhyaya—**Integral Humanism and Antyodaya**, uplift of the last person; Mookerjee—**Bharatiya Jana Sangh founder**, national integration. The ceremonial speeches' ideological assessments are speakers' positions, not neutral proof of political achievement.
- PM said **over 30 acres of accumulated garbage** had been cleared over **three years**, a reported land-redevelopment example. This does not itself establish an independent waste-remediation/environmental audit. [PMO account](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2208531&lang=1&reg=3).
- Mains link: memorials shape public memory and civic education; assess inclusion, historical accuracy, accessibility, public expenditure and maintenance alongside symbolism. Governance principles in the page's visual include **participation, accountability, transparency, rule of law, responsiveness, equity/inclusion and effectiveness**.
