# BIND and public broadcasting infrastructure

## Public broadcasting
- **Broadcasting Infrastructure and Network Development (BIND)** is a Central Sector scheme supporting **Prasar Bharati: Akashvani/AIR and Doordarshan**. Embedded Cabinet graphic gives **₹2,539.61 crore** outlay.
- Modernises broadcasting equipment/studios, content production, civil infrastructure and DTH channel capacity; expands reliable information in remote, tribal, border and Left Wing Extremism areas. Domestic/regional programming and DD India's international reach support cultural access and public communication.
- Source targets AIR FM reach **59% →66% geographic area** and **68% →80% population**: these are separate denominators and intended coverage, not already achieved results. It reports an **Akashvani Kendra at Ujjain, Madhya Pradesh** under the scheme.
- Digital/HD/satellite upgrades, accessibility and credible regional content matter alongside transmitter coverage; equipment installation can support indirect employment.
