# Adi Vaani: AI translation and tribal language inclusion

## Beta platform launched 1 September 2025
- **Ministry of Tribal Affairs**, with **IIT Delhi, IIIT Hyderabad, IIIT Naya Raipur, BITS Pilani and Tribal Research Institutes**, launched **Adi Vaani**, an AI-based tribal-language translation/transcription/learning platform. Web portal available; release says mobile beta would shortly appear on app stores, so website launch is not proof every mobile store already carried it. [Official release](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2162846).
- First-phase languages: **Santali, Mundari, Bhili, Gondi, Kui, Garo**, enabling communication with Hindi/English. Broad regional associations: Santali/Mundari in eastern India; Bhili in western/central India; Gondi in central India; Kui in Odisha; Garo in Meghalaya/Assam. Communities cross State borders; **Ol Chiki** is associated with Santali, not a universal script for all tribal languages.
- Uses: access to welfare, education, health, administration, legal information and entrepreneurship; preserve oral/language resources and enable tribal participation. Links **NEP 2020 mother-tongue learning**, digital inclusion and the **International Decade of Indigenous Languages, 2022–2032**.
- Translation quality, dialect variation, community consent/data governance, linguist participation and device/connectivity gaps shape usefulness. Human checking is necessary for consequential health/legal translations; AI output alone does not guarantee accurate or culturally appropriate meaning.
