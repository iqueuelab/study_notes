# DAY-NRLM: self-help groups and rural livelihoods

## Mechanism and PYQ
- **Deendayal Antyodaya Yojana–National Rural Livelihoods Mission** organises rural poor households, especially women, into SHGs and supports skills, financial inclusion/bank linkage, micro-enterprises, entrepreneurship and market/service access.
- **2012 Prelims: (b), statement 2 only**. Its core approach is strengthening SHGs and skill development; the listed options about directly setting up large numbers of factories or universally giving seeds/fertiliser/pumps free do not describe the Mission.
- Source's comparison is also overbroad: **PM-KISAN is income support and PMFBY is crop insurance**, neither a universal free-input supply scheme.
