# AIIMS faculty shortages and regional healthcare capacity

## February 2025 report
- Faculty vacancies reported: **New Delhi 34%, Rishikesh 39%, Raipur 38%, Mangalagiri 41%**. These are dated source percentages, not current vacancy counts.
- **PMSSY** aims to correct regional tertiary-care imbalances and strengthen medical education; **22 new AIIMS approved** is distinct from 22 fully staffed/operational institutions.
## Cause → consequence → response
| Cause | Consequence | Response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Slow recruitment / rapid expansion | Vacant posts, weak mentoring | Planned recruitment tied to sanctioned posts and opening schedules |
| Remote posting / research and housing gaps | Uneven specialist distribution | Research support, housing, incentives and career progression |
| Heavy clinical load | Burnout, less teaching/research | Adequate teams, workload safeguards and protected academic time |
- Patient queues, delayed specialist treatment, weaker clinical trials and rural–urban inequality undermine the promise of new buildings.
- **CRE and NORCET** streamline specified non-faculty/nursing recruitment; **do not claim they centrally recruit all faculty**. Faculty selection uses separate institution-specific processes. [AIIMS recruitment archive](https://aiimsexams.ac.in/info/archive_notice2025.html).
- Track filled specialty posts, retention, teaching quality and patient outcomes; PPP/foreign collaboration proposals need academic standards and accountability.
