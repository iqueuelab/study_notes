# Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana

## Clean cooking access

- **PMUY launched in May 2016**, Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas: deposit-free LPG connections in adult women's names for eligible poor households without an existing household LPG connection.
- Initial target: **5 crore connections**, subsequently expanded to **8 crore**. Inclusion extended beyond the original deprivation-based coverage to SC/ST households, PMAY-Gramin and Antyodaya beneficiaries, forest dwellers, most backward classes, tea/ex-tea garden communities and island residents.
- Connection support covers cylinder security deposit, regulator, hose and installation. The initial assistance for a 14.2-kg connection was **₹1,600**; do not treat this historical amount as an unchanging entitlement.
- **Ujjwala 2.0, August 2021:** additional connections, free first refill and stove; migrants can use prescribed self-declarations for address and family composition instead of conventional address/ration-card documents. [PIB Ujjwala 2.0 overview](https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2022/mar/doc202232832001.pdf)

## Access must become sustained use

**Connection → affordable refill → reliable delivery → regular clean cooking → lower smoke exposure.** A connection count measures access, not exclusive LPG use or verified health gains.

- Benefits: reduced biomass collection, women's drudgery and household smoke exposure; time for education and income generation.
- Barriers: refill cost and irregular income, delivery distance, continued biomass use alongside LPG, inadequate awareness and safety support.
- Priorities: targeted refill assistance through DBT, dependable last-mile distribution, smaller-cylinder options, LPG Panchayats, refill-based monitoring and locally suitable clean alternatives such as biogas.
- **Exam trap:** a free first refill does not mean unlimited free refills. Subsidies are separate, periodically revised policy benefits. [Official eligibility and benefits](https://www.pmuy.gov.in/faq.html)
