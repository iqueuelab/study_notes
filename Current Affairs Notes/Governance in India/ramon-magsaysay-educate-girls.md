# Ramon Magsaysay Award and Educate Girls

## 2025 award announcement
- **Foundation to Educate Girls Globally (Educate Girls), India**, named a **2025 Ramon Magsaysay Awardee**, alongside **Shaahina Ali, Maldives** (environmental work), and **Flaviano Antonio L. Villanueva, Philippines** (humanitarian/human-rights work). [RMAF award list](https://rmaward.asia/yearawarded/2025/).
- Founded **2007 by Safeena Husain**, Educate Girls mobilises communities to identify, enrol and retain out-of-school girls, particularly rural/remote communities, with **Team Balika volunteers and Preraks**. Source highlights Rajasthan, Madhya Pradesh and Uttar Pradesh. The organisation describes itself as the **first Indian organisation** to receive the award; this is distinct from the many earlier individual Indian awardees.
- Award established **1957** in memory of Philippine President **Ramon Magsaysay**; first awards **1958**. Administered by the **Ramon Magsaysay Award Foundation**, recognising service and transformative leadership in Asia, popularly “Asia's Nobel Prize”. The label does not make it a Nobel Foundation award.
- Earlier Indian examples: **Vinoba Bhave 1958, Jayaprakash Narayan 1965, Aruna Roy 2000, Arvind Kejriwal 2006, P. Sainath 2007, Ravish Kumar 2019**. Old category labels changed over time; emergent leadership was not one of the original 1958 categories.
- Source's blanket **31 August presentation** claim is incorrect for 2025: announcement and ceremony are different events; the **2025 ceremony was scheduled for 7 November in Manila**. [Educate Girls ceremony record](https://www.educategirls.ngo/blog/events/ramon-magsaysay-ceremony/). At this issue date the ceremony remained prospective.
- Mains link: community engagement + public schools + gender-norm change → enrolment/retention/agency; distinguish admission counts from learning, completion and durable empowerment. Undated award practice: **(c), 1 and 3 only**; not a PYQ.
