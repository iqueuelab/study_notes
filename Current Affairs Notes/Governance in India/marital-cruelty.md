# Marital cruelty law and safeguards against violence

## Statutory offence
- **IPC498A inserted1983**; under the BNS, **section85** punishes cruelty by husband/his relative with **up to three years and fine**; **section86** defines cruelty. It covers wilful conduct likely to drive suicide or cause grave injury/danger to life, limb or mental/physical health, and harassment linked to unlawful property/valuable-security demands. [BNS official text](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/indiacode/bitstream/123456789/20062/1/a202345.pdf).
## July 2025 decision and editorial debate
- **Shivangi Bansal v Sahib Bansal,22 July2025**, not the PDF's July2024. Source discusses endorsement of Allahabad HC Family Welfare Committee guidelines and a two-month cooling period before coercive action in covered matrimonial complaints. This is an account of the July2025 ruling, not an instruction to delay emergency protection or a substitute for reading its scope. [SC judgment](https://api.sci.gov.in/supremecourt/2023/35609/35609_2023_11_1502_62486_Judgement_22-Jul-2025.pdf).
- Editorial concern: delayed intervention can expose complainants to violence/intimidation or coerced compromise, while treating criminal complaints as only family disputes. Balance prompt protection/investigation with reasoned arrest safeguards and due process; **Arnesh Kumar(2014)** opposed automatic arrest, not investigation itself.
- Source recalls **Sushil Kumar Sharma(2005)** and **Preeti Gupta(2010)** on misuse concerns; individual false allegations do not establish nationwide misuse rates. Conversely, low conviction cannot by itself prove false cases: evidence, investigation, compromise and reporting barriers matter.
- Source cites **NCRB2022:1,34,506 cases/~18% conviction**, plus NFHS-5 under-reporting concerns; keep reporting and conviction denominators separate. Practice **A (1 only)**: punishment is three, not five years. Gender-sensitive legal aid, shelters and safety assessment should accompany procedural safeguards.
