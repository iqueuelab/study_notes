# Neeraj Chopra and the 90-metre javelin milestone

## Doha result: 16 May 2025
- **Doha Diamond League 2025**, correcting repeated PDF **2024** labels: **Neeraj Chopra 90.23 m**, Indian record and first Indian beyond 90 m, **second** to Germany's **Julian Weber 91.06 m** in the final round. A personal/national milestone does not necessarily win that competition. [World Athletics result](https://worldathletics.org/competitions/diamond-league/news/weber-chopra-doha-diamond-league-2025).
- Source describes Chopra as **third Asian /25th male athlete** to join the 90-metre club at this milestone; these are dated achievement counts, not a permanent ranking. **Kishore Jena eighth, 78.60 m** in the source result; Paris 2024 “preparation” is stale in a May 2025 issue.
- **Wanda Diamond League:** annual elite track-and-field meeting series, qualification points leading to a final; meeting placing differs from Olympic/world-championship medals.
- Chopra: **Tokyo Olympic gold (Games held 2021)**, first Indian Olympic athletics gold; **World Championships silver 2022 /gold 2023**; coach **Jan Železný**, world-record holder, whose record had not been broken by 90.23 m.
- Training, biomechanics, coaching and athlete-support pathways matter; the source's weather/wind explanation is not proof a single factor caused the improvement.
