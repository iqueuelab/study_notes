# Poshan Tracker and Anganwadi nutrition monitoring

## Digital service delivery
- **Women and Child Development Ministry**, with **National e-Governance Division (NeGD)**; rolled out **1 March 2021** as a mobile governance tool for Anganwadi nutrition services.
- Records beneficiary attendance, supplementary nutrition, growth/health parameters and service delivery; dashboards aid tracking of children, pregnant women and lactating mothers, resources and gaps. Official platform access is role-based; a tracking entry is not itself proof a nutritional outcome improved.
- July source reports **technical glitches and weak internet** impeding frontline workers' real-time updates. Priorities: reliable devices/connectivity, usable workflows, training, data quality and privacy, with service continuity during failures.
- **POSHAN Abhiyaan**, launched **8 March 2018**, combines departmental convergence, technology and community participation. Objectives: reduce stunting/underweight among children 0–6, anaemia among children 6–59 months and women 15–49, and low birth weight; adolescent girls, pregnant/lactating women and young children are key groups. [MoWCD account](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=1776877&lang=2&reg=48); [MoWCD rollout bulletin](https://www.nipccd.nic.in/uploads/pdf/e-BulletinPoshanSep2022pdf-026613228c26ed82aaf5d7739ee96c3a.pdf).
