# Bodhan AI and Bharat EduAI Stack

## 12 February 2026: education AI initiative
- **Dharmendra Pradhan** launched the **Bodhan AI Centre of Excellence at IIT Madras**, established by the Ministry of Education, at **Bharat Bodhan AI Conclave, Bharat Mandapam**. The event began development of an **open/interoperable Bharat EduAI Stack**; it did not establish that a complete nationwide stack was already deployed. [Education Ministry](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2227059&lang=1&reg=6).
- Scope: school education, higher education, skilling/workforce readiness and AI research/deep tech. Proposed functions include adaptive/multilingual learning, feedback, teacher planning/assessment and system analytics, building on India's digital-public-infrastructure approach.
- Policy links: **NEP 2020**, digital learning and responsible AI. Evaluate learning outcomes, teacher agency, accessibility, local languages, privacy, bias and interoperability; affordable scale and reduced dropout are intended benefits, not already demonstrated impacts of this launch.
