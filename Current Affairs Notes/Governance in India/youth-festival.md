# National Youth Festival and Young Leaders Dialogue

## Dates and programme

- **12 January**, Swami Vivekananda's birth anniversary, **National Youth Day**; source notes Youth Week observance from **1985**. Do not confuse Youth Week with the festival's specific dates each year.
- National Youth Festival: youth integration, cultural competitions/non-competitive events, conventions/**Suvichar**, exhibitions and adventure; linked to **National Programme for Youth and Adolescent Development**, national-integration component.
- **Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports**, host State/UT partnership in the conventional format; not invariably one fixed venue or unchanging 12–16 January schedule.

## 2025 reimagined event

- **Viksit Bharat Young Leaders Dialogue, 10–12 January 2025**, **Bharat Mandapam, New Delhi**; PM interaction **12 January**.
- **3,000 selected participants**, not the source's generic **7,500** festival-delegate figure: **1,500 Viksit Bharat Track + 1,000 Traditional Track + 500 Pathbreakers**.
- Ten development themes; innovation in science/technology is one component, not the event's only overarching theme. Selection: quiz → essay → State Vision Deck; cultural programmes, mentoring and youth-policy dialogue. [Youth Affairs release](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2091554&lang=2&reg=48).
- Source practice answer that treats a fixed annual 12–16 January schedule and a sole science/technology theme as correct is unreliable.
- Mains: civic participation, skills, inclusion and youth-led solutions; evaluate representation and follow-through beyond a one-off event.
