Governance in India

SC/ST and other disadvantaged-group scholarships

Editable notes

August 2025: proposed eligibility expansion

  • Ministries were considering income-ceiling revisions for 2026–27 to 2030–31; reported ST proposal ₹2.5 lakh→₹4.5 lakh and upward review for SC/OBC/DNT schemes are proposals, not operative revised entitlements.
  • Tribal Affairs administers ST scholarships; Social Justice and Empowerment covers relevant SC/OBC/EBC/DNT schemes. Pre-matric generally targets school access; post-matric supports study after ClassX. Top-class schemes have separate rules.
  • Important exception to source's blanket₹2.5-lakh claim: SC pre-matric ComponentI covers ClassesIX–X with₹2.5-lakh ceiling; ComponentII covers ClassesI–X children of persons in specified unclean/hazardous occupations irrespective of caste/religion, with no family-income ceiling. Official scheme.
  • Source CSS comparison 60:40 general/90:10 northeastern States must be checked against the particular scheme's guidelines; it is not one funding rule for every scholarship/UT.
  • Source 2025–26 budget comparisons: social-justice scholarships 66.7%of₹13,611 crore; tribal scholarships 18.6%of₹14,925.81 crore. These are ministry allocation shares, not all national education spending.
  • Source beneficiary trends: SC pre-/post-matric −30.63%/−4.22%(2020–21 to 2024–25); OBC/EBC/DNT pre-matric 58.62 lakh(2021–22)→20.25 lakh(2023–24), post-matric 43.34→38.42 lakh; ST reported falls 4.63/3.52 lakh. Periods/scheme redesign/data completeness differ; low ceilings are a proposed explanation, not demonstrated sole causation.
  • Parliamentary recommendations: higher ceilings, OBC pre-matric from ClassV, broader top-class coverage. Index ceilings to costs, simplify applications, ensure timely payments and measure retention/outcomes. Source practice is unyearred and its universal income/funding statements are overbroad; do not memorise the printed key without the exceptions.
