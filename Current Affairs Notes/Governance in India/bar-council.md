# Bar Council of India and professional ethics

## Statutory professional regulation
- BCI is established under **section 4, Advocates Act 1961**, not a constitutional body. It regulates professional conduct/legal-education standards and represents the Bar.
- Members elected from State Bar Councils plus Attorney General and Solicitor General ex officio. State Council terms and a BCI representative's term are not automatically identical; the Act specifies category-dependent tenure.
- Recognises law qualifications/institutions for professional purposes; conducts AIBE for the relevant certificate-of-practice framework. State Bar enrolment is a separate step.
- Disciplinary functions involve State and national mechanisms; BCI is not simply a department whose every disciplinary decision is directed by the Law Ministry. [Act](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/1631/1/A1961_25.pdf).
- Rule 36 restricts solicitation/advertising, including misleading online promotion. Limited prescribed factual website information is permitted; not every informative legal post is automatically advertising.
- Professional independence, truthful communication, client confidentiality and access to justice guide digital practice. Rules permitting limited foreign-law practice are not blanket permission for foreign firms to practise Indian litigation.
