# PARAKH and school learning assessment

## Institution and portal
- **Performance Assessment, Review and Analysis of Knowledge for Holistic Development**, established within **NCERT in 2023** under **NEP 2020**. It develops competency-based assessment norms, large-scale surveys, board equivalence and holistic progress cards for foundational, preparatory, middle and secondary stages; it is not an independent statutory school regulator.
- The dissemination portal opens national/State learning data for officials, educators and researchers to identify gaps and target interventions. **PARAKH Rashtriya Sarvekshan, 4 December 2024:** **21,15,022 pupils in Grades 3, 6 and 9**, **74,229 schools**, **781 districts**; questionnaires from **2,70,424 teachers/school leaders**. Do not replace Grade 6 with Grade 5.
- Article highlights Punjab, Himachal Pradesh, Kerala, Chandigarh and Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu, with rankings varying by grade/subject. Kendriya Vidyalayas were weak in Grade 3 mathematics but strong across Grade 9 subjects; this is not one universal school-type ranking.
- Illustrative competency results: Grade 3 **55%** could arrange numbers up to 99; Grade 6 **54%** could represent numbers using Indian place value and **38%** solve specified whole-number problems; Grade 9 **54%** could identify main textual points, **31%** understand specified number sets and **45%** explain the Constitution's emergence/ideals. These are item/competency performance percentages, not board-examination pass rates.
