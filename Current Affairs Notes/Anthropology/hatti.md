# Hattee community and Boda Tyohar

## Community and geography
- **Boda Tyohar**, major annual festival of the **Hattee/Hatti**, **Trans-Giri, Sirmour district, Himachal Pradesh**.
- Name associated with selling agricultural/wool produce at a **haat**. Related communities across **Jaunsar-Bawar, Uttarakhand**; **Giri and Tons**, Yamuna-system rivers; Tons forms sections of the Himachal–Uttarakhand boundary.
- Traditional community council **khumbli**; ceremonial white headgear; agriculture and cash crops. Do not relocate the community to Kinnaur/Lahaul-Spiti because a generic Himachal economy paragraph mentions those districts.
- Source **2011 population about 2.5 lakh**, later roughly **3 lakh** estimate without a specified census year: preserve dates/estimate status.
## Scheduled Tribe status
- **Constitution (Scheduled Tribes) Order (Second Amendment) Act, 2023**, inserted **“Hattee of Trans Giri area of Sirmour district”** in Himachal's list; geographic scope matters. It is an ordinary Act amending the ST Order, not a numbered constitutional amendment. [Gazette Act](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/repealedfileopen?rfilename=A2023-14.pdf).
- Source contrasts **Jaunsar-Bawar recognition in 1967**; don't assume every group using a similar name everywhere automatically has identical legal status.
