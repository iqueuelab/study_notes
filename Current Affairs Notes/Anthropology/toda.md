# Toda: society, livelihood and embroidery

## Nilgiri community
- **Nilgiri Hills, Tamil Nadu**; hamlet **mund**. Source describes a language without a traditional script; oral language does not mean absence of linguistic complexity.
- **Patrilineal descent**; source's two endogamous divisions **Tharthazoll and Theveioll**. Clan names given: **Paiki, Pekkan, Kuttan, Kenna and Todi**; transliterations vary.
- Traditional council **Noim**, headman **Monegar**, social regulation/dispute settlement; do not interpret traditional authority as unlimited legal power overriding Indian law.
- Buffalo pastoralism and embroidery; sacred buffalo/dairy associations make livelihood and ritual closely connected.
- **Red-and-black embroidery on white cloth**, GI-protected **Toda Embroidery, Tamil Nadu**. [GI register](https://search.ipindia.gov.in/GIRPublicSearch/Application/Details/135).
- **Modhweth**, New Year/buffalo festival; source places observance on last Sunday of December or first Sunday of January.
- Anthropology recall: **descent ≠ residence ≠ marriage rule**; patriliny alone does not establish every household's residence practice. Preserve culture with community participation, livelihood security and language transmission.
