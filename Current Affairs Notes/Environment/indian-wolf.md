# Indian grey wolf and Bankapur sanctuary

## Habitat and news

- January report: **eight pups at Bankapur Wolf Sanctuary, Koppal, Karnataka**, State's first dedicated wolf sanctuary.
- Source calls it India's second after **Mahuadanr, established 1976**, now **Jharkhand**, not present-day Bihar.
- Traditional subspecies name **Canis lupus pallipes**; Indian wolf lineages/taxonomic assessments need care. Smaller packs, relatively less vocal, thin coat suited to warm **grassland, scrub and semi-arid pastoral landscapes**; often nocturnal/crepuscular.
- Source's broad Israel–subcontinent distribution is a traditional subspecies range, not proof all populations are one uniform Indian genetic lineage.

## Conservation traps

- Source **IUCN Least Concern** refers to the broad **grey-wolf assessment**, not proof Indian populations are secure or a current independent Indian-wolf assessment.
- **Wildlife (Protection) Act Schedule I**; **CITES Appendix I** includes relevant Indian wolf population listing, not every grey-wolf population worldwide.
- Habitat loss/fragmentation, prey depletion and persecution; protect open habitats as well as forest reserves. Bankapur scrub, hills/caves also support blackbuck, foxes, hares, porcupines, leopards and peafowl.
