# Caracal and semi-arid habitat conservation

## Species anchors
- **Caracal caracal**, Felidae, Carnivora; medium-sized tawny cat with long black ear tufts, powerful limbs and a solitary, elusive habit.
- Broad distribution across Africa and parts of West/Central/South Asia. India's surviving records are especially associated with Rajasthan and Gujarat; older range does not prove healthy populations in every historic State.
- Uses dry forest, scrub, semi-arid grasslands; hunts small mammals and birds. Often nocturnal, but activity varies.
- **Globally Least Concern; Schedule I in India; CITES Appendix I for Asian populations**, Appendix II elsewhere. Global status can coexist with severe local rarity.
- First photographic record **in Mukundra Hills Tiger Reserve** is a local documentation milestone, not the first proof that caracals occur anywhere in Rajasthan.
- Species-recovery prioritisation, surveys, habitat connectivity, conflict reduction and anti-poaching are important. Grasslands are functioning ecosystems, not ecologically empty “wastelands”.
