# Lyriothemis: Kerala dragonfly discoveries

## February 2026: two distinct Kerala discoveries
| Species | Identification and habitat |
|---|---|
| **Lyriothemis keralensis**, “Slender Bombardier” | Described **7 February 2026** from Kerala's coastal plains adjoining Western Ghats; previously misidentified as **L. acigastra**. Leaner abdomen and genital/caudal structures distinguish it; seasonal monsoon activity, shaded canals/pools amid rubber/pineapple plantations and other modified landscapes |
| **Lyriothemis abrahami** | Separate **phytotelma-breeding** species: larvae develop in small water-filled **tree holes**. Earlier confused with **L. flava (=L. tricolor)**; named for odonatologist Abraham Samuel. Kerala Western Ghats records include Thenmala/Ponmudi/Peppara/Aralam; forest microhabitats matter |
- Both are **Odonata, Libellulidae**; do not transfer plantation-pool breeding to the tree-hole species or merge their earlier misidentifications. Sexual dimorphism makes linked larvae/adult specimens and structural examination important for taxonomy.
- A newly described species is not automatically assigned a threatened IUCN category. Conserve both forest tree-hole microhabitats and suitable shaded wet features in agricultural mosaics. [Keralensis original description](https://www.worlddragonfly.org/article/tijo20-v029-a3-chandran), [Abrahami original description](https://entomon.in/index.php/Entomon/article/view/1470).
