# Similipal and tiger conservation

## Similipal

- **Mayurbhanj, Odisha**; associated elephant-reserve landscape includes Hadgarh and Kuldiha sanctuaries. Named for red silk-cotton trees.
- Forests: moist mixed deciduous, semi-evergreen and dry deciduous with grasslands; rivers **Khairi and Deo**; elephants, carnivores and mugger crocodiles.
- UNESCO World Network of Biosphere Reserves since **2009**; notable wild **pseudo-melanistic tigers**. Avoid the source's unsupported 'Asia's second-largest biosphere' ranking and any confusion between biosphere reserve, national park and tiger-reserve boundaries.

## Tiger basics

- National animal; **keystone/predator** and ecosystem-health indicator; IUCN **Endangered**, CITES **Appendix I**.
- **Project Tiger: 1973**; **NTCA** statutory authority; **M-STr IPES** supports monitoring/patrolling.
- 2022 All India Tiger Estimation: estimated **3, 682**, range **3, 167-3, 925**. The source uses 3, 167 without explaining that it is the lower bound. [Official estimate](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2076921&lang=1&reg=3).
- Source highlights MP, Karnataka, Uttarakhand; Nagarjunasagar-Srisailam landscape in AP/Telangana; Sundarbans mangrove tigers; Corbett reserve. Rankings require a stated metric/year.
- International cooperation: **Global Tiger Forum**; **TX2** goal sought to double wild tiger numbers by 2022.

## January 2025 recovery study

- **“Tiger recovery amid people and poverty”**: tiger **occupancy expanded ~30%** to **~138, 200 km²**; this is not a 30% population rise. Protected breeding areas and connected multiple-use landscapes support coexistence; source cites ~66 million people in occupied landscapes. [NTCA account](https://ntca.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/Stripes_Vol14_1_Jul25_compressed.pdf).

- Source labels **2, 226** and **3, 167** as 2024: incorrect. **2, 226 = 2014**; **3, 167 = 2022 lower estimate**, not final central estimate. [Official census comparison](https://www.pib.gov.in/Pressreleaseshare.aspx?PRID=2076921&lang=2&reg=48).
- **Project Tiger 1973**, initially nine reserves; NTCA statutory foundation **2006 amendment**, not 2005. Source 53-reserve count is dated and should not be memorised as current.
- *Panthera tigris*: IUCN **Endangered**, **CITES Appendix I**; commercial international trade tightly restricted, not every exceptional transaction absolutely banned. Wildlife hunting prohibition has statutory exceptions.
- Recall: **Corbett—Uttarakhand; Bandipur—Karnataka/Nilgiri landscape; Kanha—MP; Sundarbans—West Bengal and Bangladesh mangroves; Nagarjunasagar–Srisailam—AP/Telangana landscape**.
- **M-STr IPES** supports patrol/ecological monitoring; conflict prevention, corridors, prey recovery, livelihood support and locally applicable compensation matter. Source “TOES” scheme name and uniform ₹5 lakh compensation are unverified.
- **Global Tiger Initiative 2008**, **TX2** doubling ambition adopted 2010 for 2022; distinguish targets and census methods. WWF **73% decline** refers to average monitored vertebrate-population size **1970–2020**, not 73% of species becoming extinct.

## NTCA institutional recall

- Statutory **2006 wildlife-law amendment**, **Mo EFCC**; Union Environment Minister chair, Minister of State vice-chair, **three MPs**, Ministry Secretary and other specified members.
- Project Tiger oversight, Centre–State accountability/Mo Us, parliamentary oversight and livelihood interests around reserves; NTCA is distinct from NBWL and State field administrations.

## Trail Guard AI in Similipal, February 2025

- Compact cameras perform **edge inference**, classify humans/animals/vehicles and send alerts using **cellular communication**; source reports **30–40 seconds**, low-power operation and **six months–one year** battery life.
- Field-response chain: image classification →control room →patrol communication via VHF/other channels →investigation. Satellite transmission is not the source's described normal link.
- Source reports **96 arrests /86 guns in ten months**, units around **₹50, 000–53, 000**, deployment in **five States /over 14 sites**; dated operational claims. Predicted **80% poaching reduction** is a forecast, not a measured causal result.
- Reduce false positives, protect sensitive location data and distinguish poaching from lawful livelihood activity. Community consultation, access arrangements and forest-rights safeguards build trust.

## Similipal recall

- **Barehipani /Joranda waterfalls**; Budhabalanga, Khairi and Salandi rivers; mixed deciduous/semi-evergreen habitats, elephants and pseudo-melanistic tigers.
- Communities include **Santhal, Kol, Bhumij, Mankidia**; Mankidia are a **PVTG**. Traditional cultural practice does not automatically exempt prohibited wildlife hunting.
- Biosphere designation **2009**; boundaries/status of biosphere, sanctuary, tiger reserve and national park differ. Source's **“National Park 1979”** is not a reliable gazette date; do not memorise it.
- Practice: edge inference true; “continuous high power /satellite transmission” false. **Dudhsagar—Goa /Athirappilly—Kerala** are outside Similipal. Practice questions have no official exam year.

- **Later status clarification: 24 April 2025**, Odisha formally notified **845.70 km² Similipal National Park**; distinguish this from the much larger tiger/biosphere landscape. [Odisha notification announcement](https://inpr.odisha.gov.in/sites/default/files/2025-04/News-Forest_24042025EEEE.pdf).

## Coexistence and monitoring

- Land sparing protects breeding cores; land sharing/corridors support movement/genetic exchange. Livelihood support, conflict response and community participation accompany habitat protection.
- Poaching, infrastructure, mining, prey depletion and climate pressures differ locally; economic prosperity/conflict associations are not single-cause explanations.
- **M-STr IPES:Monitoring Systemfor Tigers–Intensive Protectionand Ecological Status**; field records/patrol monitoring, not just tracking every tiger with GPS.
- GTI2008/Global Tiger Forum/TX2 doubling goal are different initiatives; India did not alone create/lead every global mechanism.
- WWF's 73%Living Planet Index decline concerns average monitored vertebrate population abundance, not 73%of every species or all individual wild animals disappearing.
