# Kole wetlands and pygmy grasshopper diversity

## February 2026: new State records
- Kole survey reported **12 pygmy grasshopper species**, including **four newly recorded in Kerala**, not four species newly discovered to science: *Thoradonta spiculoba, Tetrix arcunotus, Hedotettix lineifera* and *Hedotettix attenuatus*. **Thoradonta and Tetrix** were reported as new genus records for the State.
- **Kole wetlands, Thrissur–Malappuram, Kerala**, form part of **Vembanad-Kol Ramsar site (2002)**. Seasonal inundation, bunds/drainage and paddy cultivation link floodplain hydrology and livelihoods; “Kole” is associated with a bumper crop. [Ramsar information sheet](https://rsis.ramsar.org/RISapp/files/RISrep/IN1214RIS.pdf).
- Pygmy grasshoppers: **Orthoptera, family Tetrigidae**, small ground-dwellers often in moist/muddy margins; elongated pronotum and reduced forewings are useful cues. Wing forms vary: avoid claiming every species lacks all wings or sensory capacity.
- Insect diversity broadens wetland conservation beyond birds. Microhabitat sensitivity can inform bioindicator research, but the occurrence of one species alone is not proof a whole wetland is unpolluted. Cicada/froghopper/pond-skater 2024 PYQ already exists in the bank.
