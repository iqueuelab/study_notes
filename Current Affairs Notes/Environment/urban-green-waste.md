# Urban horticultural waste and composting

## February 2026: Delhi processing initiative
- The issue reports Delhi's first dedicated green-waste processing plant, developed by **PWD**, for roadside horticultural residues. “First” is the report's category-specific claim, not proof no composting facility previously operated anywhere in Delhi.
- **Leaves, grass, prunings and branches → segregation/collection → shredding and controlled decomposition → compost for landscaping/parks.** Woody fractions need suitable processing; contaminated mixed municipal waste should not enter clean compost indiscriminately.
- Benefits: less open burning/particulate pollution, roadside dumping and landfill load; recovered nutrients/organic matter can support soils. Outcomes require collection coverage, appropriate aeration/moisture, safe compost quality and actual end use rather than inauguration alone.
- Circular economy links waste management with PWD/horticulture maintenance, local reporting, resident institutions and urban greenery.
