Environment

Forest diversion, linear projects and compensatory land

Editable notes

Supreme Court interim direction

  • 3 February 2025, Ashok Kumar Sharma v Union of India, challenges to 2023 forest-law amendment: until further orders, no Union/State steps reducing forest land unless compensatory land is provided for afforestation. Original order.
  • Applies to reduction, including relevant linear projects; not a categorical ban on all roads/railways/pipelines, nor final invalidation of the whole amendment.
  • T.N. Godavarman 1996 dictionary meaning of forest, ownership/classification irrespective, and recorded forests remain central. Source's “February 2023” interim reference appears confused with 19 February 2024 directions pending consolidated records.
  • Section 1A, 2023 amendment: statutory coverage of notified and government-recorded forest categories; exceptions and provisos require reading the Act. Rule 16, 2023 Rules consolidated State/UT records; source 1.97 lakh km² estimate is not itself a court-certified universal inventory.

Conservation versus infrastructure

  • Prior statutory approval, applicable environmental safeguards, forest/community rights and compensatory measures are distinct requirements; planting does not automatically authorise diversion.
  • New plantation area does not instantly replace old-growth biodiversity, carbon stocks or livelihoods.
  • Prefer avoidance/alternative alignments, minimise fragmentation, provide wildlife crossings, verify suitable compensatory land, survival/maintenance and transparent compliance monitoring.
  • Articles 48A / 51A(g) environmental duties and Article 21 jurisprudence; balance public infrastructure need with ecological and rights safeguards.
