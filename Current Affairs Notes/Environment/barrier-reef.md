# Great Barrier Reef and coral bleaching

## Map and conservation
- World's largest **coral reef system**, **Coral Sea, northeast Australia/Queensland**, Pacific; roughly **2,300 km**, **~3,000 reefs**. Marine Park **344,400 sq km**; source rounded **350,000** and World Heritage area measurements use differing boundaries. **Not every square kilometre is living coral**. [Reef Authority facts](https://www.gbrmpa.gov.au/learn/fascinating-facts-about-great-barrier-reef).
- **UNESCO World Heritage, 1981**, managed largely by **Great Barrier Reef Marine Park Authority/Reef Authority**. Source around **400 coral types, 1,500 fishes, 4,000 molluscs**; dugongs and green turtles are notable fauna. Source **10% global coral ecosystems** needs denominator/date.
- Platform/patch reefs, elongated reefs/walls and **fringing reefs**; classic evolution **fringing → barrier → atoll** with subsidence and continued growth, not a universal history for every reef.
## Coral recall
- **Coral polyps are animals**, commonly colonial; reef-building **hard corals deposit calcium carbonate**, unlike soft corals' usual structure. Many tropical corals associate with **zooxanthellae**.
- Heat/other stress → loss of symbiotic algae/pigment → **bleaching**. Bleached coral is **not necessarily dead**, but prolonged stress causes starvation/disease and mortality.
- Threats: warming, acidification, sediment/pollution, salinity changes, destructive fishing/coastal development and tourism pressure; protect water quality and habitats alongside emissions reduction.
- Benefits: biodiversity, coast protection, fisheries/jobs/tourism and research. Global reef biodiversity claims concern all coral reefs, not GBR alone.
- Indian examples: **Gulf of Kachchh, Gulf of Mannar, Andaman–Nicobar, Lakshadweep**; source also mentions Malvan. Conservation examples: **International Coral Reef Initiative**, **Global Coral Reef R&D Accelerator Platform**, India's **National Coastal Mission**; cryopreservation/larval restoration and mineral-accretion **Biorock** are tools, not substitutes for controlling stress.
