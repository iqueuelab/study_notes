# Indonesia peatland fires and transboundary haze

## Image-only place in news
- Caption depicts firefighters on peatland in **Riau province, Indonesia,20 July 2025**.
- Peat fires can smoulder slowly/deeply and be difficult to extinguish; smoke/haze can travel towards **Malaysia and Singapore**, creating transboundary air-quality concerns. The caption describes a hazard pathway, not a measured air-quality episode for every neighbouring country.
