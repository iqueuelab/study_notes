Environment

Mangrove ecosystems and restoration

Editable notes

Ecology and distribution

  • Salt-tolerant intertidal forests of tropical/subtropical coasts, estuaries and deltas. Pneumatophores, stilt roots, salt filtering/excretion and vivipary vary among species; not every mangrove has every adaptation.
  • Indian examples: Sundarbans, Gujarat's coast/Kutch, Bhitarkanika, Pichavaram and Andaman–Nicobar. West Bengal has the largest State extent.
  • ISFR 2021 reported about 4,992 sq km, roughly 0.15% of India's geographical area; 2023's 4,991.68 sq km is essentially the same rounded scale, not a large national doubling.
  • Functions: fish nurseries, shoreline stabilisation, carbon storage in biomass/soil and wave-energy reduction. They cannot guarantee complete cyclone protection.

Restoration and interpreting studies

  • Restore tidal hydrology and suitable native communities, not merely plant saplings anywhere. Control invasive vegetation while avoiding damage to existing habitat.
  • Tamil Nadu study estimates of 4,500 ha in 2021 and 9,039 ha in 2024 use their own survey/mapping scope; do not equate them automatically with an official FSI series.
  • Carbon stock is not annual sequestration, and tonnes of carbon differ from tonnes of CO₂. Compare methods, pools, area and dates.
  • CRZ regulation and community stewardship support protection. Mangrove Alliance for Climate is a voluntary initiative, not a binding treaty.
  • Carbon credits need credible baselines, additionality, permanence and safeguards for local rights.

PYQ recall

  • The combination of mangrove, evergreen and deciduous forests points to Andaman and Nicobar Islands; distinguish biome combinations from a single species' range.
