# Indian wild ass and protected-species conservation

## Khur: identification and habitat
- **Equus hemionus khur**, Equidae; Indian subspecies of the Asiatic wild ass, called **khur/ghudkhur**. Pale reddish-brown body, pale underside/rump and dark dorsal stripe; not the domestic donkey or kiang.
- Surviving wild range centred on **Little Rann of Kachchh, Gujarat**, with adjoining Greater Rann/Banni and village/agricultural landscapes; source “exclusively inside Rann” is narrower than its own expansion account. Open saline desert, grassland and scrub; grazes grasses/saline vegetation and browses shrubs; freshwater access shapes movement.
- **Wild Ass Sanctuary, 1973**, roughly **5,000 km²**. Source **6,000+** population is undated; the infographic **4,800** reflects an older snapshot rather than the same survey.
- **Correction: Indian khur is CITES Appendix I**, not II. Species-wide **E. hemionus Near Threatened** assessment and a subspecies' status require separate taxon/date checks; source's Mongolian “Endangered” comparison cannot substitute for the applicable assessment. Indian protection **Schedule I**. [CMS/CITES species distinctions](https://cami.cms.int/species/equus-hemionus).
## Threats and protection
- Salt-pan/agricultural/infrastructure expansion fragments habitat; drought and water scarcity, livestock competition, domestic-equid disease and isolation create risks. **Surra—Trypanosoma evansi**; monitor veterinary spillover and avoid depicting all traditional pastoralists as illegal encroachers.
- Protect movement/water/forage, manage grazing and salt-pan pressures, monitor populations/disease and use evidence-based buffer/ESZ measures; a proposed buffer is not a notified operative zone.
- **2017 PYQ: (d), gharial, Indian wild ass and wild buffalo** protected against hunting except statutory permissions. Section 11 includes tightly conditioned orders for dangerous/irrecoverably injured animals; lawful good-faith defence and Section 12 special-purpose permits are separate provisions. **Schedule I does not mean no statutory exception whatsoever.**
- **2012 endangered-animal grouping: (a)** in the question's historical context; do not assign all four species today's same Red List category.
