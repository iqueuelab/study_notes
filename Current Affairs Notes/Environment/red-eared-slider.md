# Red-eared slider turtle and pet-release invasions

## Identity and invasion
- **Trachemys scripta elegans**, a semi-aquatic slider native to the southern United States/northeastern Mexico; distinctive red patch behind each eye, dark green/brown shell and yellow markings. Omnivorous; feeds in water and basks on logs/rocks; long-lived (source range **20–50 years**).
- Mainly freshwater habitats; can tolerate some brackish conditions. Pet releases establish alien populations competing with native turtles for food, basking sites and habitat; India's occurrence does not make it native.
- Source **Least Concern** refers to conservation assessment of the slider taxon; abundance in its native range is compatible with invasive harm elsewhere. Listed in the widely used **100 worst invasive alien species** compilation, not a current numerical rank.
- Practice trap: **statement 2 only = option B**. PDF prints “A. 2 only” despite its option A being “1 only”; statements about Indian nativity and Critically Endangered status are false. [USDA invasive-species profile](https://www.invasivespeciesinfo.gov/aquatic/fish-and-other-vertebrates/red-eared-slider).
