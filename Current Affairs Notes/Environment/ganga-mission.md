# National Mission for Clean Ganga (NMCG)

## Institutional map

- NMCG registered as a society in **2011** under the **Societies Registration Act, 1860**; functions under **Ministry of Jal Shakti**.
- Earlier implementation arm of **National Ganga River Basin Authority (NGRBA)**, constituted under Environment (Protection) Act, 1986; replaced by **National Ganga Council in 2016**, chaired by the **Prime Minister**.
- Source describes Governing Council and Executive Committee, headed by NMCG Director General; objectives are pollution abatement, conservation and rejuvenation through a **river-basin approach**.

## January news and mains use

- Source highlights Chandauli sewage-treatment initiative. Sewage collection plus reliable plant operation, discharge monitoring and ecosystem-flow management matter more than capacity announcements alone.
- Link sanitation, river ecology, municipal capacity and basin-wide coordination.
