# Black plastic, recycled e-waste and food-contact safety

## Risk pathway

- Poorly controlled recycling of electronic plastics can transfer flame-retardant chemicals into other products. **Colour alone does not establish contamination** in every black utensil.
- Bromine detection is a screening clue, not identification of every chemical or proof of illness. Migration depends on material, temperature, use and dose.
- Widely circulated exposure claims from a 2024 study were subject to a calculation correction; do not memorise the original headline as an established daily toxic dose.
- Carbon-black pigments can impede conventional near-infrared sorting. Segregate e-waste, enforce traceability and food-contact standards, and improve sorting/authorised recycling.
