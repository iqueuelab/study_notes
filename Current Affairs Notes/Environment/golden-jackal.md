# Golden jackal and human-modified habitats

## Identity and ecology
- **Canis aureus**, canid; omnivorous opportunistic forager/scavenger, widespread across India and Eurasian habitats.
- Often more nocturnal near people; flexible behaviour elsewhere. Forms breeding pairs, shelters in dens/crevices and occupies riverine, grassland, wetland/mangrove and other suitable habitats.
- Source January 2025 context: adaptation to human-modified settings; coexistence requires waste control and habitat protection, not feeding wildlife.
- Habitat encroachment and poor management threaten local populations; scavenging and food-web roles support ecosystem functioning.
## Conservation and taxonomy traps
- Source status: **IUCN Least Concern; CITES Appendix III (India); Wildlife (Protection) Act Schedule I under the amended schedules**. IUCN risk category, trade listing and Indian legal protection measure different things. [India Code](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/1726/1/a1972-53.pdf).
- The PDF's North/East Africa distribution reflects older lumping: African golden-wolf populations were distinguished from **Eurasian golden jackal C. aureus**. [Genomic study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26234211/).
