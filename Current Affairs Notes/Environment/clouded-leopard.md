# Clouded leopards and habitat connectivity

## Species and map

| Species | Range recall |
| --- | --- |
| **Neofelis nebulosa**, mainland clouded leopard | Himalayan foothills/Northeast India, mainland Southeast Asia and southern China |
| **Neofelis diardi**, Sunda clouded leopard | **Borneo and Sumatra** |

- Source both **Vulnerable**; mainland clouded leopard **Meghalaya State animal**. Indian source locations: Sikkim/northern West Bengal and northeastern States.
- Rainforest, secondary woodland and hill habitats; Borneo mangrove examples concern the **Sunda species**, not proof mainland Indian species occurs on Borneo.

## Adaptations and conservation

- Cloud-patterned coat, pale yellow to brown ground colour; source approximate **60–110 cm**, **11–20 kg**, **12–15-year lifespan** (conditions vary).
- Long balancing tail, short legs/broad paws, exceptional climbing and headfirst descent; generally solitary. **Canine teeth longest relative to skull/body size**, not necessarily longest absolute canines of every living feline. [Smithsonian](https://www.nationalzoo.si.edu/animals/clouded-leopard).
- Taxonomically **Felidae, Pantherinae**; “neither big nor small cat” is informal language, not a separate family. Smithsonian accounts themselves differ on purring; avoid using ability to roar/purr as the defining taxonomic test.
- Study focus: habitat suitability, fragmentation and connectivity across historic/current range. Protect corridors, forest quality and prey; distinguish modelled suitable habitat from verified occupancy.
