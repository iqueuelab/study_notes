# Donkey-skin trade, welfare and rural livelihoods

## African policy and livelihood risks
- **Ejiao** is gelatin made from donkey hides, used in Chinese traditional medicine/wellness products. Claimed fertility, blood-replenishment and anti-ageing effects are not established clinical benefits in this source.
- **Dar es Salaam Declaration, 1–2 December 2022**, proposed a **15-year moratorium on donkey slaughter for skins**; AU adopted a preservation/moratorium resolution in **2024**. **26 May 2025 AU-IBAR** urged member-state implementation and discussed a **draft** Pan-African preservation/welfare strategy. A continental decision is not proof uniform national enforcement or that 33 million donkeys have already been saved. [AU-IBAR implementation account](https://www.au-ibar.org/news/latest-news/au-ibar-urges-immediate-action-donkey-welfare-across-africa-woah-general-session).
- Theft, poorly regulated slaughter, animal cruelty and slow reproduction threaten working-animal populations. Loss of transport/ploughing/water-carrying capacity undermines farmer income, increases women's/children's labour and can disrupt schooling; unsafe slaughter creates occupational/zoonotic risks.
- Source market estimates **160% ejiao growth, 2016–21; ~6 million hides/year; 6.8 million slaughtered/year by 2027** combine growth estimates and a forecast. China **11 million donkeys in 1992 → under 2 million** and Botswana **493,000 in 2003 →125,000 in 2021** need original census verification. Do not equate demand with verified annual kills or infer all population decline caused by trade.
- SDG links include poverty/food security, health, gender, water access, decent work and sustainable ecosystems; “nine SDGs undermined” is advocacy framing, not a measured index.
## Indian equids
- **Kachchhi—Kutch, Gujarat; Halari—Saurashtra, Gujarat; Spiti—Himachal Pradesh** are donkey-breed anchors. Do not confuse **Kachchhi-Sindhi horse** with a formally registered Sindhi donkey breed. Source carrying/draught figures, especially **1,000–1,500 kg**, are not safe loading recommendations. [ICAR-NBAGR breed register](https://nbagr.res.in/horse-pony-donkey).
- Source reports **~61% domestic donkey decline, 2012–19**; monitor trafficking, abandonment, veterinary care and breed conservation. Welfare and livelihood support require coordinated enforcement, traceability and owner participation.
