# Ecology as the foundation of economic prosperity

## “Ecology is the permanent economy”
- Idea associated with **Sunderlal Bahuguna**: livelihoods, water, soil fertility, biodiversity and durable prosperity depend on functioning ecosystems. GDP can rise while natural capital and wellbeing decline; ecological health belongs in development decisions.
- Science supplies evidence and modelling; ethical stewardship adds intergenerational responsibility, respect for limits and attention to communities bearing environmental costs.
- Restore forests/wetlands and protect biodiversity, but **nature-based solutions cannot compensate indefinitely for continuing destructive extraction or fossil emissions**. Prevention, sustainable production/consumption and enforceable policy accompany restoration.
- The PDF's “climate change is natural” is misleading for today's warming: human greenhouse-gas emissions have unequivocally caused modern global warming. [IPCC synthesis](https://www.ipcc.ch/report/ar6/syr/summary-for-policymakers/).
- Personal emotional/cultural connection with nature can motivate conservation and mindful lifestyles; collective institutions, incentives and structural change remain necessary. Avoid romantic universal claims that early humans never overused ecosystems or that only humans anticipate future needs.
- Mains: ecological dependence → depletion/externalities → stewardship and fair development → measurable ecosystem/livelihood outcomes. Treat ecology as productive infrastructure, not an automatic prohibition of all development.
