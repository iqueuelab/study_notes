# Red Sanders: ecology, trade and benefit-sharing conservation

## Access-and-benefit-sharing conservation funding
- **29 September 2025:** NBA sanctioned **₹82 lakh** to Andhra Pradesh Biodiversity Board to raise **one lakh Red Sanders saplings** for farmers/Trees Outside Forests. Funding comes from **benefit-sharing receipts from resource users**, additional to sale proceeds, connecting use with local stewardship. Earlier NBA releases to AP Forest Department totalled **over ₹31.55 crore** at that announcement. [Official announcement](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2172620&lang=2&reg=48).
- **Pterocarpus santalinus, Fabaceae**, endemic to southern Eastern Ghats, particularly Andhra Pradesh; dry deciduous/rocky red-soil habitats. Deep-red timber valued for musical instruments, furniture and carving; traditional medicinal use does not establish universal clinical efficacy. Different from **Santalum album** sandalwood.
- Commercial demand, illegal cutting/smuggling and habitat loss threaten its narrow natural range. **CITES Appendix II** regulates international trade rather than banning every legal transaction. Source **Endangered** is its reported IUCN assessment; assessments must be dated rather than mixed with older Near Threatened labels. **2016 PYQ: statement1 only (a)**—South Indian tree, not characteristic tropical rainforest tree.
- Source's generic **Wildlife Act Schedule IV** label should be read in the amended **CITES-specimen/trade framework**, not as proof it is an animal in the old four-schedule classification. Forest/export permissions and CITES documentation remain distinct controls.
## NBA institutional correction
- NBA established **2003 under Biological Diversity Act 2002**, Chennai; works on conservation, sustainable use, access regulation and fair benefit sharing. **NBA/SBB/BMC** have different statutory roles; local BMCs prepare People's Biodiversity Registers, not merely subordinate offices in one hierarchy.
- PDF's **chairperson +10 ex officio +5 experts** is the earlier composition. **2023 amendment** provides **16 ex officio members**, **four rotating SBB representatives**, experts and Member-Secretary; do not memorise the old count as current for October 2025. [Amending Act, section10](https://egazette.gov.in/WritereadData/2023/247815.pdf).
