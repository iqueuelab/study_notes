# Sea otters and trophic cascades

## Identification and ecological role
- **Enhydra lutris**, coastal North Pacific marine mammal, renowned for dense fur and tool use to open shellfish.
- **Keystone predator**: controls sea urchins and helps protect kelp; diet also includes crabs and other invertebrates. Source reports food intake up to 25% of body mass daily.
- California's **Elkhorn Slough** example: otter predation may help control invasive **European green crabs**, which damage native coastal habitats.
## Conservation
- Source lists IUCN **Endangered**; threats include oil spills, pollution, disease, prey/habitat change and climate impacts. Protect habitats, reduce pollution and monitor populations.
- Avoid the source's absolute 'smallest marine mammal' superlative; distinguish sea otters from freshwater/river otters.
## Recall chain
Sea otter predation → fewer grazing sea urchins → healthier kelp habitat → more biodiversity.
