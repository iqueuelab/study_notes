# Bar-headed goose and Himalayan migration

## Identification and adaptation
- **Anser indicus; IUCN Least Concern**. Pale grey/white body, two dark bars on the head and orange-yellow bill/legs; sexes look similar, with males generally larger.
- Breeds around high-altitude lakes in Central Asia and winters in South Asia, including India. A March 2025 Arunachal sighting is an occurrence record, not proof of a newly established breeding range.
- Himalayan migration is aided by efficient oxygen uptake, circulation and flight physiology. Very high maximum flight reports do not describe every bird's routine route.
- Threats include wetland degradation, hunting and disturbance; secure breeding and wintering habitats along the migratory network.
