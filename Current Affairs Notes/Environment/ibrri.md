# Indo-Burma Ramsar Regional Initiative

## COP15: Strategic Plan 2025–2030
- **IBRRI** brings together **Cambodia, Lao PDR, Myanmar, Thailand and Viet Nam**; India/Bangladesh are not its five participating countries. New **2025–2030 plan** launched at Ramsar COP15, **Victoria Falls, Zimbabwe**, in July 2025.
- Aims to reverse wetland loss through restoration, better management, policy/investment integration, awareness and regional cooperation. Wetlands support water purification, flood regulation, biodiversity, livelihoods and carbon storage.
- Government focal points form the steering mechanism; **IUCN Asia Regional Office, Bangkok** hosts secretariat; stakeholder participation brings experts, NGOs and communities into advice. IUCN's **BRIDGE** work supports shared-water cooperation. The source's Indo-Burma biogeographic map covers more territory than the five-country membership.
