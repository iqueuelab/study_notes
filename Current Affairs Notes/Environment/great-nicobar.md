# Great Nicobar: development, ecology and tribal rights

## Project and map recall
- **Great Nicobar**, southern Nicobar Islands, Andaman and Nicobar UT; strategic proximity to Indian Ocean sea lanes and Southeast Asia.
- Core integrated project: **container transshipment terminal at Galathea Bay, international airport, township and power plant**. [Environmental-clearance project record](https://environmentclearance.nic.in/auth/FORM_A_PDF.aspx?cat_id=IA%2FAN%2FNCP%2F201159%2F2021).
- The January 2025 PDF reports a **₹72,000-crore** overall proposal and an additional **100-acre seafront** request for cruise/tourism infrastructure and possible ship breaking. Do not replace the main container terminal with a cruise terminal or assume every additional proposal is approved.
## Ecological and social stakes
- Rainforests, mangroves, reefs and seagrass provide habitat/coastal protection; construction, dredging, pollution and noise may disrupt these systems.
- **Leatherback turtle nesting; Nicobar megapode; dugong/seagrass habitat** are key examples. Source also lists wild pig, treeshrew, crested serpent eagle and Nicobar paradise flycatcher.
- Embedded graphic reports **over 8.5 lakh trees / 130 km²**, and **648 flora / 330 fauna species** for the cited forest context. These are source-reported proposal/biodiversity figures, not independently harmonised counts.
- Graphic contrasts a ZSI report of **117 scleractinian coral species** with an EIA claim about the Galathea Bay site; retain as a **disputed site/assessment claim**, not a resolved finding.
- **Shompen** hunter-gatherer foraging grounds and **Nicobarese** livelihoods/land rights; migration can pressure water, housing, health services and cultural continuity.
## Development and security
- Potential benefits: transshipment, trade connectivity, jobs and strategic maritime presence; actual gains depend on viability, skills and equitable benefit sharing.
- Dual-use infrastructure needs access control, surveillance, resilience and coordination of civilian/military activities; hypothetical risks do not establish that sabotage or interference has occurred.
- Tourism and ship recycling, if pursued, need carrying-capacity assessment and robust waste/hazardous-material controls.
## Transparency and decision framework
- Source criticises security-based RTI restrictions and public participation; treat claims about particular denials as its reported dispute, not proof that no clearance or consultation exists.
- **Alternatives → cumulative EIA → rights/community consultation → security-sensitive disclosure rules → enforceable safeguards → independent monitoring**.
- Balance strategic value with irreversible habitat loss, tribal welfare and disaster vulnerability; scrutinise both costs and claimed mitigation effectiveness.

## July2025 seismic-risk debate
- Embedded reporting contrasts the project's EIA treatment of a2004-scale earthquake with an **IITKanpur assessment stressing uncertainty in predicting extreme seismic events**. Low estimated recurrence/probability is not zero risk; cumulative earthquake,tsunami,coastal-subsidence and evacuation/resilience analysis matters for port,airport,township and power infrastructure.
- Source quotes **~15ft2004subsidence**, a locality-dependent estimate, not uniform sinking of the entire island. Safety concerns do not themselves constitute a final engineering verdict that no infrastructure can be built.
- **Campbell Bay and Galathea national parks**, GreatNicobarBiosphereReserve; **TenDegreeChannel separates the Andaman and Nicobar groups**, not a narrow channel immediately north of GreatNicobar alone. **IndiraPoint** is India's southernmost land point.
- Source project footprint **16,610ha**, tribal reserve **751km²/84km²proposeddenotification**, and power unit **450MVA** are source descriptions requiring document-specific scope; MVA(apparent power) is not interchangeable with MW(real power). Shompen PVTG resource access and Nicobarese2004relocation experience require meaningful consultation/rights assessment.
- November2022TribalCouncil withdrawal ofNOC and April2023NGThigh-powered review are reported legal/administrative history; a committee review is not unconditional validation of all claims. Do not infer commercial-port Navy control solely from strategic rationale; official operating arrangements require separate evidence.
