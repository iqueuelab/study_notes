# Chinar trees and digital heritage conservation

## Tree identity

- **Platanus orientalis, Oriental plane**, family **Platanaceae**; Kashmir **Booun/Boueen**, J&K heritage/state tree.
- **Maple-like leaves do not make it an Acer maple**; poplars are also a different group. Source's “only Platanaceae species in India” needs cultivated/native scope rather than an exhaustive botanical claim.
- Large spreading **deciduous** tree, around **30 m** height, old trunks **10–15 m girth** in source; green summer foliage → red/amber/yellow autumn. Growth **30–50 years to mature height / ~150 full size** is approximate/site-dependent.
- Cultural links to landscape, literature, **papier-mâché/embroidery/wood carving**; source Bhavani-associated local-name tradition and Persian **“che na(r) ast”** etymology are traditions, not verified sole origins.
- Timber/lacewood for furniture; source traditional leaf/bark use and dye-making from twigs/roots are **reported uses, not clinical efficacy claims**.

## Tree Aadhaar

- **J&K Forest Research Institute/Forest Department** tree census, coordinates and **QR-linked unique ID**, recording **25 parameters** such as location, estimated age, girth/height and health.
- January clipping: **28,560 surveyed/geotagged**, estimated regional total **32,000–33,000**, Kashmir and Chenab region; interim census estimates, not all surveyed on a single day.
- Monitor health/removal risks, legal protection and changing condition; permissions remain relevant even on private land.
- Source claims some trees **over 1,000 years**: **geotagging records an age estimate; it does not itself date wood or prove that age**. Credible age evidence and repeat inspections matter.
