# WMO and the 2024 temperature record

## Institution

- **World Meteorological Organization**, **UN specialised agency**, founded **1950**; predecessor **International Meteorological Organization, 1873**. Headquarters **Geneva, Switzerland**.
- International cooperation in meteorology, hydrology, climatology and related geophysics; atmosphere–ocean interactions, climate and water resources.

| Body | Role |
| --- | --- |
| World Meteorological Congress | Supreme policy body, normally four-year cycle |
| Executive Council | **36 members**, annual sessions, implements Congress decisions |
| Secretariat | Administration; Secretary-General appointed for **four-year term** |

- Source **193 members = 187 States + 6 Territories**, January 2025 snapshot; members are not all sovereign states.

## 2024 record: interpret carefully

- January 2025 WMO synthesis of **six international datasets**: **2024 warmest year on record**, global mean near-surface temperature **about 1.55°C ± 0.13°C above 1850–1900**.
- A **single calendar year above 1.5°C** does not itself establish that the Paris Agreement's long-term warming threshold has been permanently crossed. Dataset differences and natural variability affect yearly estimates.
- Distinguish **weather event**, **annual anomaly**, and **long-term climate warming**; base period must accompany a temperature anomaly. [WMO announcement](https://wmo.int/news/media-centre/wmo-confirms-2024-warmest-year-record-about-155degc-above-pre-industrial-level).
- Mains: emissions mitigation, adaptation/early warning and climate-resilient water, food, health and infrastructure policy.
