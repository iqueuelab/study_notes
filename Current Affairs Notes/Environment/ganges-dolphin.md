# Ganges river dolphin

## Species
- **Platanista gangetica**, Susu; National Aquatic Animal 2009, IUCN Endangered, CITESAppendixI and Wildlife Protection ActScheduleI.
- Ganges and Indus dolphins are now recognised as distinct species; older subspecies nomenclature persists in older sources.
- Reduced vision; echolocation supports navigation/prey detection in turbid rivers. Confluences/deeper channels are important habitats.
## Population and protection
- First national estimation, surveys 2021–23/reportMarch 2025: **6, 327 river dolphins overall**, distinguished from 6, 324Ganges and 3Indus dolphins; do not label the entire combined count Ganges-only.
- Project Dolphin 2020; Vikramshila sanctuary, Bihar. First satellite tag inAssam 18December 2024. [Official tagging account](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressNoteDetails.aspx?ModuleId=3&NoteId=153834&lang=2&reg=3).
- Pollution, dams/flow fragmentation, entanglement and poaching threaten survival; protect ecological flows, reduce bycatch and monitor connected river habitat.
