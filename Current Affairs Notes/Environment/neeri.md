# CSIR-NEERI and environmental engineering

## Institution and April 2025 context
- **CSIR–National Environmental Engineering Research Institute**, headquarters **Nagpur, Maharashtra**; a constituent CSIR laboratory under the Ministry of Science and Technology, rather than a pollution-regulating statutory authority.
- Established **1958** as **Central Public Health Engineering Research Institute (CPHERI)**; renamed NEERI in **1974**. Original focus: water supply, sewage disposal, communicable diseases, industrial pollution and occupational health. [Institutional history](https://ddtalternative.neeri.res.in/about.php).
- The April 23 source reports NEERI being asked to study industrial impacts on the **Taj Mahal**: scientific assessment supports environmental adjudication; it is not itself a clearance or judgment.
- Zonal laboratories in **Chennai, Delhi, Hyderabad, Kolkata and Mumbai** support regional research and field work.
## Functions and governance relevance
- Environmental science/engineering R&D; air, water and soil pollution assessment; laboratory/field testing; waste management; climate studies; public/occupational health; cleaner technologies and fuels.
- Supplies evidence, technical advice and environmental-standard inputs to governments; supports EIA studies and judicial fact-finding. Regulatory clearance decisions remain with competent authorities.
- Mains link: credible monitoring, independent scientific assessment and practical pollution-control technology connect environmental law to implementation.
