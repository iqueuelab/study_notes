# Cities Coalition for Circularity

## Initiative
- **C3 announced 3March 2025, Jaipur**, during 12th Regional 3R/Circular Economy Forum. [MoHUA](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2107712&lang=2&reg=48).
- City cooperation, shared knowledge, digital coordination, private participation and resource-efficient urban systems; proposed collaborative mechanism, not a binding international treaty.
- **Reduce→Reuse→Recycle**; also design for durability/repair and recover suitable materials through safe systems.
- CITIIS2.0's reported₹1, 800 crore/18 cities/14States is a separate programme context, not proof the entire amount finances C3.
- Track material recovery, lifecycle impacts and waste-worker inclusion; a digital platform alone does not make a city circular.
