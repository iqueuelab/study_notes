# International Big Cat Alliance

## Conservation platform
- India-led **IBCA**, launched **9 April 2023** on Project Tiger's 50th anniversary after the PM's 2019 call for cooperation. Seven cats: **tiger, lion, leopard, snow leopard, cheetah, jaguar, puma**; India hosts the first five, including reintroduced cheetahs.
- Cooperation covers habitat protection, anti-poaching/illegal trade, research, technical expertise, conservation finance and climate resilience. Both range and interested non-range countries can participate; finding all seven species is not a membership condition.
- Cabinet approved headquarters in India and **₹150 crore support, FY 2023–24 to FY 2027–28**; governance includes **Assembly of Members, Standing Committee, Secretariat**. [Cabinet decision](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleaseIframePage.aspx?PRID=2010124&lang=2&reg=48).
## July 2025 membership snapshot
- Issue distinguishes **24 countries agreeing to join** from **12 members**: India, Armenia, Bhutan, Cambodia, Ethiopia, Eswatini, Guinea, Liberia, Nicaragua, Rwanda, Somalia, Suriname. Agreement/intention, ratification and full membership are different stages; the practice question's 'as of 2024' dates this July snapshot incorrectly.
- Landscape conservation protects prey, habitat and ecosystem functions, not only individual flagship cats. Link **Project Tiger 1973**, Gir lion conservation and **2022 Kuno cheetah reintroduction**; the source's 96-country reach is not a permanent membership total.
