# Pelicans and painted storks

## Identification
| Bird | Recall |
| --- | --- |
| Great white pelican, Pelecanus onocrotalus |Pelecanidae; large pouch; IUCN Least Concern|
| Dalmatian pelican, P.crispus |Pelecanidae; source Near Threatened|
| Spot-billed pelican, P.philippensis |Pelecanidae; source Near Threatened; southern breeding colonies|
| Painted stork, Mycteria leucocephala |Ciconiidae; yellow-orange bill, pink tertials; tactile feeding in shallows|
- Pelicans scoop prey with expandable pouches; some fish cooperatively. Storks probe/sweep the bill; no pelican-like pouch.
- **Kokkare Bellur, Karnataka; Vedanthangal, Tamil Nadu; Ranganathittu, Karnataka; Keoladeo, Rajasthan**: important breeding/wetland examples; each bird is not equally present at every site.
## Conservation
- Maintain wetland water regimes, food availability and nesting trees; reduce pollution, disturbance and persecution; community stewardship matters.
- Source blanket “all pelicans Near Threatened” contradicts its great-white entry. Conservation assessment and legal schedule must be species/date-specific; old Schedule IV labels cannot be copied after 2022 schedule restructuring.
- Ramsar designation recognises a wetland, not automatic protection of every habitat occupied by these birds.
## Status correction
- **Painted stork: Least Concern since 2023**, replacing Near Threatened in the older notes/source. [Bird Life assessment update](https://www.birdlife.org/news/2023/12/12/the-2023-red-list-update-reveals-hope-for-birds-in-crisis/).
