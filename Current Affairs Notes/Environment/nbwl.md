# National Board for Wildlife and statutory safeguards

## Legal identity

- **Section 5A, Wildlife (Protection) Act 1972**, introduced by **Wild Life (Protection) Amendment Act 2002**, **Act 16 of 2003**. Source **2022 creation** is wrong; predecessor **Indian Board for Wildlife, 1952**.
- **Prime Minister chair; Environment Minister vice-chair**, senior government representatives, institutions, NGOs, nominated conservationists/ecologists and rotating State/UT representatives. Source **47 members** is a reported composition count, not the safest substitute for statutory categories.
- Member-secretary: **Additional Director General of Forests (Wildlife)/Director of Wildlife Preservation**.
- **Section 5B Standing Committee**: vice-chairperson, member-secretary **plus no more than ten nominated Board members**; not “ten total” and not an independent external regulator. [Amendment text](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/6199/1/wild_act_2002.pdf).

## Roles and limits

- Advises on policy, conservation and protected-area assessment; Standing Committee exercises delegated functions/project scrutiny.
- Statutory safeguards cover protected-area habitat diversion/destruction, relevant tourism development and boundary changes; tiger-reserve denotification requires **NTCA recommendation + NBWL approval**, with applicable State/central procedures.
- NBWL recommendation **does not replace every forest/environment clearance** or permit all activity automatically. Applicable protection differs by sanctuary, national park, tiger reserve and eco-sensitive area.
- January report: refusal of **soapstone mining proposal near Kedarnath Wildlife Sanctuary, Uttarakhand**; compare mineral extraction, habitat/hydrology and site-specific legal constraints.
