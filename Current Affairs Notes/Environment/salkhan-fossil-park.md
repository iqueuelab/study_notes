# Salkhan Fossil Park and stromatolites

## Fossil geoheritage
- **Salkhan/Sonbhadra Fossil Park, Uttar Pradesh:** added to UNESCO's **Tentative List on 4 June 2025**, not inscribed as a World Heritage Site. In the Vindhyan region, within Kaimoor Wildlife Sanctuary's Gurma range.
- Approximately **1.4-billion-year-old Mesoproterozoic stromatolites**, layered structures formed through microbial communities including cyanobacteria. Domal/columnar forms reveal water-depth, sedimentation and wave conditions; “Evolution of life” is an IUCN geoheritage category. [UNESCO nomination](https://whc.unesco.org/en/tentativelists/6842).
- Cyanobacterial oxygen production transformed Earth's atmosphere; the **Great Oxidation Event about 2.4 billion years ago** substantially predates these fossils. Oxygen did not become the predominant atmospheric gas; nitrogen dominates today. Do not confuse ancient fossil stromatolites with modern living microbial structures at Shark Bay.
- **2024 PYQ recall:** Santiniketan and Sacred Ensembles of the Hoysalas entered the World Heritage List in **2023**; Rani-ki-Vav **2014**, Mahabodhi complex **2002**. Thus **only two**.
- Among the intangible-heritage options: Chhau **2010**, Kumbh Mela **2017**, Durga Puja in Kolkata **2021**, **Garba 2023**—latest among those choices.
