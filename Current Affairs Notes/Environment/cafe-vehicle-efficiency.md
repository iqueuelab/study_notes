# CAFE vehicle-efficiency norms and clean mobility

## February 2026: CAFE-III proposal under review
- **Corporate Average Fuel Efficiency (CAFE)** regulates a manufacturer's fleet-average fuel consumption/CO₂, rather than imposing one identical emissions limit on each car. BEE under the Power Ministry anchors the energy-efficiency framework; proposed third-cycle norms cover **FY2027–28 to FY2031–32**. [BEE consultation draft](https://beeindia.gov.in/sites/default/files/CAFE_2027.pdf).
- The clipping reports a PMO review involving Power/BEE/Heavy Industries, with **no final decision**. A subsequently circulated industry proposal reportedly removed the earlier **3g CO₂/km derogation** for cars **up to909 kg/engine≤1,200 cc**; this privately circulated revision should not be treated as a notified binding rule.
- Reported mass–emissions slope changes: **0.002** in the earlier proposal versus **0.00153 in year 1, falling annually to0.00128 in year 5**. These are slope coefficients, not standalone g/km fleet targets or an annual percentage reduction. The distribution of compliance effort between small and heavier vehicles drives industry disagreement.
- Cleaner fleets can lower oil use and CO₂ while encouraging EV/efficient-vehicle innovation. Design must assess actual fleet mix, testing cycles, compliance credits and consumer affordability. CAFE's fuel/CO₂ metric differs from Bharat Stage pollutant limits.
- Minister cited the **₹10,900-crore PM E-DRIVE** programme and **over 28 lakh EV sales supported as of February**. This is his dated reported progress figure, not all Indian cumulative EV sales or evidence every passenger car receives a subsidy.
