# Smooth-coated otter and wetland conservation

## Source report and identification
- Source reports a **pair transferred from Surat to National Zoological Park, Delhi**, an exchange after a long gap; exchange supports captive conservation/awareness, not proof wild populations recovered.
- **Lutrogale perspicillata**, traditionally the only living Lutrogale species; large, social semi-aquatic otter with shorter, smoother brown fur and lighter underside. Dense underfur insulates; guard hairs repel water. Strong swimmers, can move over land; cooperative fish hunting may use a V-like formation.
- Lowland rivers/lakes, freshwater wetlands, mangroves, peat swamps and rice-growing landscapes across South/Southeast Asia; an isolated **Iraqi** population extends beyond that core range. Habitat loss, pollution and illegal fur/pet trade threaten it. [IUCN SSC species profile](https://www.otterspecialistgroup.org/otter-species/lutrogale-perspicillata/).
## Protection and practice correction
- **IUCN Vulnerable**. The PDF's **Schedule II** label is outdated: the amended Wild Life (Protection) Act, consolidated **5 June 2025**, lists **Smooth-coated Otter, item 105 of Schedule I**. [Primary statute](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/1726/1/aA1972-53.pdf).
- Practice statements on its Southeast Asian size and Iraqi population are both intended correct: **option (c), both 1 and 2**. Printed “(a) 1 and 2” is an option-letter error; (a) actually says “1 only”.
