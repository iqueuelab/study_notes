# Mission Amrit Sarovar and pond restoration

## Mission and implementation
- Launched on **24 April 2022**, with the initial objective of developing/rejuvenating at least 75 ponds per district; Ministry of Rural Development coordinates convergence across programmes and ministries.
- MGNREGA, community participation and local institutions can support construction and maintenance. Original 2023 milestones and later 2025 proposals refer to different phases.
- Railways' proposed use of desilted material for embankments links water restoration to infrastructure; suitability and contamination require testing.
- Potential benefits: storage, recharge, livelihoods and biodiversity. Recharge depends on local geology and hydrology, not merely excavation.
- Choose appropriate sites, prevent encroachment, preserve drainage, monitor water quality and finance long-term maintenance; avoid treating completion counts as proof of ecological recovery.
