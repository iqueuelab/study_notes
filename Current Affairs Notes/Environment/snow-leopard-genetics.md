# Snow leopard: genetic diversity and conservation

## October 2025: low diversity from a persistently small population
- **Panthera uncia**, an alpine/subalpine Asian predator, inhabits twelve range countries: Afghanistan, Bhutan, China, India, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, Mongolia, Nepal, Pakistan, Russia, Tajikistan and Uzbekistan. Blue sheep/ibex and smaller mammals form prey; livestock predation can trigger retaliatory killing.
- Stanford-led PNAS study was published **7 October 2025**, not 2024. It added 37 newly sequenced genomes to four existing ones (41 total). Long-term small population explains exceptionally low diversity among compared big cats; this is different from asserting a single recent population crash. Historic purging can reduce strongly harmful variants without restoring diversity or ensuring resilience. [Stanford research account](https://news.stanford.edu/stories/2025/10/snow-leopards-genetic-diversity-climate-change-research).
- Heterozygosity measures genetic variation; homozygous genetic load concerns harmful variants inherited in duplicate. Low diversity may constrain adaptation to rapid climate/habitat changes even without unusually high present inbreeding. Avoid calling the population genetically safe or every harmful gene eliminated.
- India's SPAI estimate **718**, released January 2024 from the 2019–2023 assessment: Ladakh 477, Uttarakhand 124, Himachal Pradesh 51, Arunachal Pradesh 36, Sikkim 21, J&K 9. These are assessment estimates rather than a complete October 2025 census. [MoEFCC release](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleaseIframePage.aspx?PRID=2000545&lang=2&reg=3).
- Source-era IUCN Vulnerable; CITES/CMS Appendix I. Project Snow Leopard (2009), GSLEP/Bishkek Declaration (2013), landscape/corridor protection, camera traps/genetics, prey recovery and community livestock safeguards complement enforcement. Warming, fragmentation, prey loss and poaching remain threats. Yak/wolf should not both be labelled routine prey; wolf is another predator. The source's fixed zoo stock counts are undated, not current population estimates.
- Punjab PCS 2020 panel is a State-exam question, excluded from CSE banks; its conservation-centre proposal is historical, not proof construction is complete. “20 by 2020” was GSLEP's landscape target, not a current claim of twenty completed landscapes.
