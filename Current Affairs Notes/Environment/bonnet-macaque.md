# Bonnet macaque and conflict management

## Species and Kerala proposal
- **Bonnet macaque (Macaca radiata):** southern Indian primate with a cap-like crown of radiating hair; occupies forests including Western Ghats and human-dominated farms, settlements and temple/tourist sites.
- Diurnal, arboreal and terrestrial; social multi-male/multi-female troops, quadrupedal movement. Omnivorous: fruit/leaves, insects, small vertebrates/eggs and human food. Females mature around 3–4 years; gestation about six months, nursing roughly 6–7 months with longer maternal association.
- Kerala proposed seeking Union permission for sterilisation following crop/property damage and conflict. It was a **proposal requiring wildlife-law permissions**, not an implemented blanket programme; anaesthesia, veterinary risks/welfare, monitoring and prevention of feeding/open waste matter. Do not call surgery inherently painless or risk-free.
- Source conservation status: **IUCN Vulnerable; Schedule I, Wild Life (Protection) Act**. Local conflict does not establish a growing population across the species' range.
- **PYQ 2023 revision:** lion-tailed macaque is diurnal; Malabar civet is nocturnal; sambar often crepuscular/nocturnal. Answer **only two** active mainly after sunset.
