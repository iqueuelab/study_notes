# Steel slag: roads, circular economy and material safeguards

## Resource and applications
- **Steel slag** is a by-product of steelmaking as fluxes bind impurities; important routes **basic oxygen furnace (BOF)** and **electric arc furnace (EAF)**. BOF refines molten iron using oxygen; saying it simply smelts ore omits the preceding ironmaking stage.
- Composition varies: calcium/magnesium silicates and oxides of iron, manganese and aluminium; cooling, processing, ageing and particle grading determine suitability. Potential uses: **road base/sub-base and aggregate, cement/raw-material applications, acidic-soil improvement, and studied water-remediation uses**.
- Alkalinity can neutralise acidity and aid metal precipitation/adsorption; it is not proof every untreated slag is safe in water/soil. Check free-lime/magnesia expansion, leaching, grading and relevant standards. **Blast-furnace slag and steelmaking slag are different**: Portland slag cement commonly uses granulated blast-furnace slag; do not assume all steel slag is directly interchangeable with it.
## January 2026: hill roads and ECOFIX
- Minister promoted wider hill-state adoption of processed slag technology; **20 January TDB–Ramuka Global Eco Work agreement** supports **CSIR-CRRI ECOFIX**, ready-to-use pothole repair mix designed for wet/waterlogged conditions. Outreach/workshops were proposed for J&K and other hill States/UTs.
- Planned processing facility **nearly 2 lakh tonnes/year**, commercial production **expected end-2027**; distinguish plan from an already operating plant. Potential gains: less quarrying/disposal, quicker repairs, durability and lifecycle savings, subject to engineering/environmental performance. [Technology Development Board](https://tdb.gov.in/dr-jitendra-singh-pushes-steel-slag-roads-hilly-terrains).
- **2020 PYQ: road base, soil improvement and cement—1, 2 and 3 (d)**. The question asks potential material uses, not unrestricted suitability of every slag batch.
