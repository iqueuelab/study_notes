# Agasthyamala Biosphere Reserve

## Location and recognition
- Established **2001** in the southern Western Ghats, spanning **Kerala and Tamil Nadu**; approximately **3,500 km²**.
- Joined UNESCO's **World Network of Biosphere Reserves in 2016**, under the Man and the Biosphere programme. This differs from the **2012 Western Ghats World Heritage inscription**; biosphere-reserve recognition does not mean the entire reserve is one World Heritage property.
- Includes **Shendurney, Peppara and Neyyar wildlife sanctuaries**, and **Kalakkad Mundanthurai Tiger Reserve**. Peaks reach about **1,868 m**.

## Biodiversity and people
- Tropical wet evergreen/montane forests and high-altitude grasslands support elephants, tigers, lion-tailed macaques and diverse amphibians, reptiles and birds.
- UNESCO records **2,254 higher-plant species**, including about **405 endemics**; the reserve also conserves crop genetic resources such as cardamom, pepper, jamun, nutmeg and plantain.
- **Chenkurinji (*Gluta travancorica*)**, family Anacardiaceae, is native to southwest India. Do not equate its native range with only this reserve or treat traditional medicinal uses as established clinical treatments.
- **Kani/Kanikaran communities** contribute ecological and ethnobotanical knowledge. Conservation should respect community rights, participation and equitable benefit sharing; avoid unsupported claims that they are the oldest surviving tribe.

| Zone | Main role |
|---|---|
| Core | Biodiversity conservation and compatible research |
| Buffer | Activities compatible with conservation, education and monitoring |
| Transition | Settlements and sustainable livelihood/development cooperation |

**Protect ecosystems + involve local communities + sustain livelihoods** is the biosphere-reserve model; its zones do not replace applicable national protected-area laws.
