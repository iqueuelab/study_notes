# Membrane bioreactors and wastewater treatment

## Mechanism and use
- **Biological wastewater treatment + microfiltration/ultrafiltration** combine in membrane bioreactors (MBRs), removing organic load and separating solids/microorganisms.
- Used for municipal sewage and industrial effluents; treated water can support reuse after meeting purpose-specific standards. Membrane treatment alone does not guarantee potable safety or removal of every dissolved contaminant.
- 2024 PYQ: **wastewater treatment technologies (d)**.
