Environment

Jacaranda tree

Editable notes

Identify

  • Jacaranda mimosifolia, family Bignoniaceae, order Lamiales: subtropical ornamental tree, violet-blue trumpet flowers, bipinnate fern-like leaves and woody flattened seed capsules.
  • Kew records the native distribution as Bolivia, northwestern Argentina and Paraguay; do not list Brazil as this species' confirmed native range merely because the genus occurs widely in South America. Kew POWO.
  • Widely planted beyond its native range, including India, Australia and South Africa. Introduced ornamental presence is different from native/endemic status.

Urban ecology

  • Shade, amenity and flowering habitat can benefit cities; site choice should consider water, soil drainage, branch safety, litter and local biodiversity.
  • Naturalisation/invasiveness is location-dependent; assess competition with native vegetation rather than treating it as invasive everywhere.
  • Traditional medicinal uses are not proof of clinically effective or safe treatment. Timber/ornamental use and cultural associations do not define its ecological origin.
