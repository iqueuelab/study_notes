# Bannerghatta: elephant exchange with Japan

## July 2025 zoo exchange
- **Bannerghatta Biological Park, Bengaluru**, planned its first international animal transfer: four **Asian elephants**, one male/three females, to **Himeji Central Park–Safari Park, Japan**. Source names **Suresh, Gowri, Shruthi and Tulsi**.
- Multi-species exchange envisages BBP receiving **four each cheetahs, jaguars and pumas, three chimpanzees and eight black-capped capuchins**; planned receipts are not proof all animals had arrived.
- Transport via Bengaluru–Kansai/Osaka, medical clearance, trained crates/handling and accompanying veterinarians/caretakers; source says Indian staff would remain two weeks and Japanese keepers trained at BBP in May. Travel-time figures vary by flight/ground segment, so avoid treating “20 hours” as a reconciled end-to-end duration.
- Conservation education/research and ex-situ management require welfare, veterinary quarantine, lawful exchange authorisation and post-transfer monitoring. A zoo collection exchange is distinct from wild population reintroduction.
