Nilgiri tahr: montane grassland conservation

Editable notes

Identity and distribution

FeatureRecall
SpeciesNilgiritragus hylocrius, also called Nilgiri ibex
StatusEndangered, IUCN; Schedule I, Wildlife (Protection) Act, 1972
State animalTamil Nadu
RangeEndemic to the southern Western Ghats, in Tamil Nadu and Kerala
HabitatOpen montane grasslands, cliffs and the shola–grassland landscape; not a rainforest-canopy species
Male identificationMature males develop a pale grey back patch: saddlebacks

The Forest Department's Project Nilgiri Tahr material confirms its endangered status, Schedule-I protection and restricted shola–grassland range. Schedule-I protection is stringent but should not be memorised as an exception-free legal ban. Tamil Nadu Forest Department project booklet.

April 2025 development and conservation

  • The issue's clipping announces a joint Tamil Nadu–Kerala census from 24 April. This is a survey announcement; no census result is supplied.
  • Threats: grassland loss and degradation, livestock competition, invasive plants, poaching, climate change and isolated small populations vulnerable to local extinction.
  • Habitat protection → invasive-plant control and grassland restoration → connected populations → coordinated monitoring across the two States.
  • Key distinction: native montane grasslands are valuable habitat; indiscriminate tree planting can harm a grassland specialist. Wildlife Institute of India assessment.
