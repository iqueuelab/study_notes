# Vela carli: gynandromorphy in a freshwater crab

## March 2026: Silent Valley discovery
- Researchers reported three **Vela carli** freshwater crabs with male and female external reproductive traits in tree holes in **Silent Valley National Park, Kerala**. The Crustaceana study describes the first reported gynandromorphy in **Gecarcinucidae**; a first report in this family is not the first such organism anywhere.
- **Gynandromorphy** means a mosaic of male/female characteristics. Developmental/genetic mechanisms vary; the observed anatomy alone does not establish a specific chromosomal mechanism or functioning simultaneous hermaphroditism.
- Vela carli is a Western Ghats freshwater crab associated with forest/tree-hole habitats. Silent Valley lies in **Palakkad**, within the **Nilgiri Biosphere Reserve**; tropical evergreen forest and **Kunthipuzha River** are key map associations.
- Significance: sex differentiation, developmental biology and the conservation value of poorly studied forest microhabitats. [ZSI research bibliography](https://zsi.gov.in/uploads/documents/importantLinks/english/ZSI_ENews_Mar_2026_Vol_86.pdf).
