# Ecocentric conservation and endangered grassland birds

## November 2025: Supreme Court conservation hearing
- During the **M. K. Ranjitsinh** bird-conservation proceedings, **Justice P. S. Narasimha** questioned whether human-centred imported environmental concepts alone could prevent extinction of the **Great Indian Bustard and Lesser Florican**. These are hearing observations, not a judgment abolishing intergenerational equity or holding all Western principles invalid.
- **Anthropocentrism** values nature principally for human benefits; **ecocentrism** recognises intrinsic ecological/species value. **Intergenerational equity** concerns fair resource opportunities across generations; it can coexist with species protection rather than logically prohibiting it. “Western versus Indian” is the source's framing, not a universal philosophical classification.
- **Article 51A(g)** combines environmental protection/improvement with compassion for living creatures. The hearing recalled the **2012 red-sanders conservation precedent**, which favoured an ecocentric approach; protect species beyond merely their instrumental worth to humans.
- **GIB, Ardeotis nigriceps:** Critically Endangered, principally Rajasthan/Gujarat; power-line collisions, grassland loss and disturbance are major concerns. Hearing figures **~70 captive/~150 wild** are dated representations, not a fresh independently verified census.
- **Lesser Florican, Sypheotides indicus:** grassland bird of the Indian subcontinent; breeding displays in Rajasthan/Gujarat/MP landscapes. Correct the text's “Endangered”: **Critically Endangered since 2021**. Its hearing reference to **~70** must not be memorised as a verified complete global wild population. [BirdLife status/history](https://www.birdlife.org/news/2023/01/17/one-to-watch-lesser-florican/).
- Conservation needs secure grasslands, safe infrastructure siting/mitigation, field surveys, farmer/community incentives and breeding expertise; captivity alone cannot replace viable habitat. Evaluate energy development and species survival through science, precaution and constitutional duties.
