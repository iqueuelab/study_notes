June 2025 discovery

  • Spartaeus karigiri, family Salticidae, subfamily Spartaeinae: reported from Devarayanadurga, Karnataka, and Viluppuram, Tamil Nadu, named for Karigiri/Elephant Hill. The species is catalogued in 2025. World Spider Catalog.
  • First Indian records reported for genera Spartaeus and Sonoita; do not extend this automatically to the whole subfamily. Sharp vision and predation involving invasion of other spiders' webs/aggressive mimicry differ from building orb webs for prey capture.
  • Sonoita cf. lightfooti from Karnataka raises unresolved range-extension versus introduction questions; cf. signals provisional comparison, not certain species identification or proof of invasion.
  • Source reports Marpissa gangasagarensis (2005) synonymised with Phaeacius fimbriatus (1900): taxonomic revision avoids counting a known species twice.
  • Reject the practice explanation that both discovery sites lie in the Western Ghats; Viluppuram is in eastern Tamil Nadu. Spider versus insect: 8 legs/two main body regions/no antennae versus 6 legs/three regions/one antennal pair.
