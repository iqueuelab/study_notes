# World Soil Day: healthy soils for healthy cities

## 5 December 2025: urban and peri-urban soils
- **World Soil Day: 5 December**; **2025 theme: “Healthy soils for healthy cities”**. FAO facilitates the observance and highlights damage from rapid urbanisation and soil sealing. [FAO 2025 event](https://www.fao.org/webcast/detail/world-soil-day-2025--healthy-soils-for-healthy-cities/en).
- **Living soil → vegetation/carbon storage + rainfall infiltration/filtering + soil biodiversity → cooler, more flood-resilient cities**. Soil stores water and supports groundwater recharge where geology and management permit; it does not prevent every flood or neutralise every contaminant.
- Urban/peri-urban cultivation, community gardens and rooftop/backyard growing support local food, recreation and shorter supply chains. Earthworms, microbes and decomposers underpin soil processes; green spaces also offer physical activity, social contact and well-being benefits.
- Threats: **concrete/asphalt sealing**, construction compaction, organic-matter loss and industrial/heavy-metal/chemical contamination. Sealing reduces infiltration and biological functions; avoid the literal claim every covered soil instantly “dies”. Source figures **56% urban population** and **one-third degraded soils** are contextual estimates, not December censuses.
- Responses: soil testing and remediation before food growing, compost/organic amendments, mulch/native plants, reduced unnecessary agrochemicals, rain gardens, parks/tree belts/green medians and prevention of further sealing. Integrate land use, water and waste management with community stewardship and soil literacy.
- Practice MCQ: temperature regulation, water absorption, biodiversity support and carbon sequestration are all relevant—**(d), 1–4**. The urban-resilience Mains prompt is undated practice; the **2025 theme is not a PYQ exam year**.
