# Coalition for Disaster Resilient Infrastructure

## Institution and mission
- India-led **CDRI**, launched at **UN Climate Action Summit, 2019**; headquarters **New Delhi**. Partnership of governments, international organisations, development banks, academia/private sector.
- Resilience across **planning →design →construction →operation →recovery**; maintain critical water, transport, power, communication and health services during disasters/climate extremes.
- Source **40 countries /seven organisations** is a dated membership count, not a fixed legal composition or current total.
## Initiatives and frameworks
- **IRIS, 2021**: Infrastructure for Resilient Island States, especially **SIDS**; distinct from IIT Madras's IRIS processor. [IRIS vision](https://iris.cdri.world/upload/pages/IRIS_Vision.pdf).
- **Global Infrastructure Resilience Report 2023**, **DRI Connect**, international resilience conferences; **IRAF** supports infrastructure-resilience action with partner institutions including UNDP/UNDRR.
- Connect to **Sendai Framework 2015–30**, SDGs and Paris climate goals; India's **Disaster Management Act 2005 /National Policy 2009** are domestic frameworks.
- CDRI is not a single global emergency-relief force or replacement for national agencies.
## Mains application
- Risk assessment/standards, retrofit, resilient finance, local capacity and nature-based solutions reduce future service disruption and recovery costs.
- Constraints: affordability, data, maintenance, institutional coordination and unequal small-island capacity. Measure service continuity and avoided losses, not only new construction.
