# Blue Flag beaches

## Certification

- Voluntary international eco-label run by **Foundation for Environmental Education (FEE), Denmark**; beaches, marinas and sustainable tourism boats.
- Origin **France, 1985**; expanded beyond Europe **2001**. Four pillars: **water quality; environmental management; environmental education/information; safety/services**.
- January 2025 report: **Kappad, Kozhikode + Chal, Kannur, Kerala**. Kappad had earlier certification; this is not proof its first-ever recognition occurred in 2025. Award depends on compliance/renewal, not permanent protected-area status. [Kerala Tourism](https://www.keralatourism.org/newsletter/news/2025/blue-flag-certification-for-keralas-kappad-and-chal-beaches/2284).
