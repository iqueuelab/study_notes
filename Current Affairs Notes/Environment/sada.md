# Sada lateritic plateaus and seasonal ecology

## Konkan landscape
- **Sada**: local term for lateritic rock plateaus in **Konkan**, also called **pathar**; weathered ferruginous surfaces, seasonal herbaceous vegetation and pools. Compare **Kaas plateau, Satara**, without equating every Sada with Kaas.
- Monsoon biodiversity and dry-season bare appearance: **seasonal habitat ≠ wasteland**. Fractures/pools support infiltration, runoff regulation and groundwater recharge.
- Source regional survey records **459 plant species, 105 Konkan endemics; 31 reptiles, 13 amphibians, 169 birds, 41 mammals**. Not a census of every individual plateau.
- Rock engravings/geoglyphs testify to prehistoric activity; source **around 10,000 years BP** is approximate and not a securely established date for every engraving.
## Threats and protection
- Orchards/cultivation, construction and land-cover conversion; missing habitat recognition can hide losses.
- Map seasonal ecology, protect drainage/microhabitats and heritage, regulate conversion and involve local communities. Small barren-looking areas can carry high endemism.
