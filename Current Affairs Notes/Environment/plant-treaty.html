Plant Treaty: genetic resources and benefit sharing

Editable notes

Treaty structure

  • International Treaty on Plant Genetic Resources for Food and Agriculture, under FAO: adopted 2001, in force 2004; conservation,sustainable use and fair/equitable benefit sharing for food security. India is a contracting party, not merely an interested observer.
  • Article9:Farmers' Rights;Article10:Multilateral System;Article11:coverage;Article12:facilitated access/SMTA;Article13:benefit sharing. The PDF inaccurately assigns all SMTA rules toArticle11. AnnexI:35food crops/crop complexes and29forages under the established list. Treaty text, AnnexI.
  • Current MLS coverage principally concerns AnnexI material under contracting parties' management/control and in the public domain; access is for research,breeding,training for food/agriculture. Recognises sovereignty and national implementation of farmers' rights; it is not a blanket commercial transfer of ownership over every farmer's seed.

July2025 negotiations and concerns

  • Fourteenth working-group meeting,7–11July2025,Lima,Peru, discussed an enhanced MLS/package of measures for later GoverningBody consideration. Scope expansion,SMTA/payment arrangements and benefit sharing were being negotiated, not already adopted as Indian operative law. FAO opening account.
  • Source concerns from Bharath Beej Swaraj Manch,Rashtriya Kisan Mahasangh,Kerala representatives and scientists: wider germplasm access, inequitable commercial capture, farmers' knowledge/rights and inadequate consultation. These are stakeholder objections, not a final finding the proposal violates the treaty or abolishes all Indian seed law.
  • ICAR-NBPGR/Sunil Archak represented technical negotiating interests; ICAR said India would protect its interests. Evaluate exact proposed scope,access conditions,farmer participation,traceability and enforceable benefit sharing.
  • Biodiversity PYQ explanation remains useful: Plant Treaty,UNCCD and World Heritage Convention all have a bearing on biodiversity. Printed2020year is doubtful and held for review rather than copied into a bank.
