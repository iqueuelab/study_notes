# Darvaza gas crater and methane emissions

## Karakum map and June 2025 report
- **Darvaza/Derweze gas crater, Karakum Desert, Turkmenistan**, “Gateway/Gates to Hell”: burning natural gas from a collapsed drilling site; **not a volcanic crater and not Kazakhstan**.
- Widely repeated story: Soviet drilling collapse and deliberate ignition **1971** to dispose of escaping gas. Origin/ignition chronology is incompletely documented and competing accounts exist; label **1971 as the conventional account**, not a securely established scientific date.
- **June 2025:** authorities reported the fire reduced **threefold**, associated with surrounding wells capturing/diverting gas; reduced visible flames do not by themselves establish a quantified reduction in methane leakage. Exact measurement basis/timeframe was not supplied. Reported control is not complete extinguishment.
- The PDF's **“longest continuously burning man-made fire”** is unsupported; long-burning coal fires have different histories. Do not equate a burning gas crater with the whole country's fossil-fuel leakage inventory.

## Climate distinctions
- **CH₄** is a potent, relatively short-lived greenhouse gas. Source **84× CO₂ over 20 years** is a specified assessment/time-horizon convention, not one universal potency at every horizon; 100-year factors differ.
- **Combustion converts methane mainly to CO₂/water**, reducing methane released if efficient; unburned leakage/venting still warms strongly. A smaller flame may reflect less inflow, but actual emissions need measurements.
- Turkmenistan has very high **oil/gas methane intensity** and major super-emitter leaks; it is not automatically the world's largest **absolute** methane emitter across all sectors. Source's two-field comparison with UK annual emissions is an attributed CO₂-equivalent calculation requiring year/horizon and is not an IEA ranking of all national emissions. [IEA 2025 tracker](https://www.iea.org/reports/global-methane-tracker-2025).
- Source ranks Turkmenistan **fourth in gas reserves**; ranking depends on reference year/reserve definition. Gas-resource abundance is distinct from current output or methane intensity.
- Priorities: leak detection/repair, capture and safe use of gas, measured verification and ending routine venting/flaring. Source practice answer **(b)** reflects its conventional history, but the ignition date and “largest emitter” explanation require these qualifications.
