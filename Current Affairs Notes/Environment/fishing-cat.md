# Fishing cat: wetland conservation and Kaziranga

## February 2026: Kaziranga assessment
- First scientific assessment reported **Kaziranga National Park and Tiger Reserve, Assam**, as an important fishing-cat stronghold. A local stronghold finding does not establish a global population count or a recovery trend.
- **Fishing cat, *Prionailurus viverrinus***: medium-sized South/Southeast Asian felid associated with wetlands, marshes, mangroves and floodplains; strong swimmer, fish/amphibian/crustacean and small-animal predator. Partially webbed feet and dense coat support aquatic habits; males are larger.
- **IUCN Vulnerable**, **WPA Schedule I**, **CITES Appendix II**. Wetland conversion, pollution, prey loss and persecution threaten it. [IUCN assessment](https://doi.org/10.2305/IUCN.UK.2016-2.RLTS.T18150A50662615.en).
- Conserve connected marsh/riparian habitat and resolve conflict around fisheries. The source's Western Ghats and country-by-country distribution list should not substitute for verified locality records; protected-area value extends beyond rhino/tiger flagship species.
