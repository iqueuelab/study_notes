# Kanger Valley National Park and tentative heritage status

## Location and landscape
- Bastar district near Jagdalpur, Chhattisgarh; national park designated in **1982**, approximately **200 sq km**, named for the Kanger River.
- Moist deciduous Sal/teak forests, bamboo, semi-evergreen patches, gorges and karst caves: **Kotumsar, Kailash and Dandak**; subterranean habitats have distinctive fauna.
- **Tirathgarh Falls is on Munga Bahar**, which joins the Kanger, not directly on the main Kanger River. Hill myna, mammals and cave species add conservation value.
- UNESCO **Tentative List**, submitted 11 February 2025, natural category: a candidate inventory, **not World Heritage inscription**. [UNESCO entry](https://whc.unesco.org/en/tentativelists/6801/).
- A national park containing tigers is not automatically a notified tiger reserve or Project Tiger critical tiger habitat.
- Protect forest/cave ecology with controlled visitation, anti-poaching, pollution prevention and community participation; Bastar's tribal knowledge and cultural associations matter.
## Other tentative-list additions in the source illustration
- Mudumal megalithic menhirs, Telangana; Ashokan edict sites across States; Chausath Yogini temples; Gupta temples; and palace-fortresses of the Bundelas.
- Keep their nomination-stage status separate from inscription; “Ashokan” sites are not collectively assigned a new archaeological date by listing.
