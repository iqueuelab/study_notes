# Asian giant tortoise: community conservation in Nagaland

## Reintroduction in Nagaland
- Ten captive-bred **Asian giant tortoises, Manouria emys**, released into a **soft-release enclosure at Zeliang Community Reserve, Peren, Nagaland**, near **Ntangki National Park**. Nagaland Forest Department, India Turtle Conservation Programme and communities participated; bred at **Nagaland Zoological Park, Chümoukedima**.
- Soft release acclimatises animals and encourages site fidelity before dispersal; release counts are not evidence of a self-sustaining recovered wild population.
## Species revision
- **Largest mainland Asian tortoise**, terrestrial, forest-dwelling; subspecies **M. e. emys and M. e. phayrei**. Northeastern India through Bangladesh/Myanmar to Southeast Asia, including Thailand, Malaysia and Indonesia.
- **Critically Endangered; CITES Appendix II**. Builds above-ground leaf-litter nest mounds, unusual among tortoises; seed dispersal supports forest regeneration. Late maturity/slow life history make recovery difficult.
- Deforestation, habitat change, bushmeat hunting and illegal pet trade threaten it.
