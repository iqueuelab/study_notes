# Uniyala keralensis and Western Ghats endemism

## Discovery and conservation
- *Uniyala keralensis*: Asteraceae shrub with light-purple flowers, roughly 1–3 m tall; reported from Agasthyamalai slopes in south-western India, around 700–1,400 m elevation.
- Collected in 1998 by E. S. Santhosh Kumar and S. M. Shareef; initially mistaken for a related taxon. The genus honours botanist B. P. Uniyal.
- Larger leaves, longer petioles and fewer lateral veins distinguish it from compared relatives. Source field estimates describe about 5,000 individuals in four populations; these are survey results, not a permanent population total.
- Authors' suggested Data Deficient status is **not automatically a formal IUCN listing**; Data Deficient does not mean safe.
- Agasthyamalai Biosphere Reserve spans Kerala and Tamil Nadu and joined UNESCO's biosphere network in 2016. Biosphere recognition differs from the Western Ghats World Heritage serial property.
