# Dhanauri wetland and Sarus crane conservation

## Location and notification

- **Dhanauri, near Dankaur/Greater Noida**, **Gautam Buddha Nagar, Uttar Pradesh**, near Jewar airport; wider **Yamuna floodplain/basin**, source about **15 km from Yamuna**, with Okhla/Surajpur regional wetland connections.
- **Yamuna is within the larger Ganga drainage system**. Practice key's absolute “not Ganga Basin” is overdrawn: distinguish the immediate **Yamuna sub-basin** from the overarching Ganga basin.
- January **NGT** direction for State's wetland-notification status in **four weeks**; direction/proposal **does not itself notify the wetland or designate a Ramsar site**. **Important Bird Area ≠ Ramsar designation**.
- Embedded historical graphic: identification **2014**, Centre sought Ramsar proposal **2019**; State forest proposal **January 2022**, consent issue **February**, **YEIDA NOC for 112 ha, June 2022**. Those steps precede the January 2025 status inquiry; retain chronology without assuming final approval.

## Birds and ecology

- Marsh habitat important for **Sarus crane, Antigone antigone**, **world's tallest flying bird**, source IUCN **Vulnerable**.
- Three recognised living regional subspecies: **Indian, Eastern, Australian**; Indian is generally largest/heaviest. IUCN threatened category differs from the legal status of a particular wetland.
- Source birds: **common teal, mallard, northern pintail, greylag/bar-headed geese; woolly-necked, black-necked and painted storks; Eurasian marsh harrier**. Great Indian Bustard is principally dry grassland/semi-arid habitat, not this marsh's flagship.
- Protect habitat, drainage, water quality and feeding/breeding areas; land-use/airport development requires transparent assessment and bird/habitat safeguards.
