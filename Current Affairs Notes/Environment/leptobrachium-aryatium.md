# Leptobrachium aryatium: new Northeast frog

## Identification
- New **Megophryidae** frog described in **Zootaxa, 14 April 2025**, from **Garbhanga Reserve Forest, Assam**, with reported range into adjoining **Ri-Bhoi, Meghalaya**. Named for **Arya Vidyapeeth College, Guwahati**; earlier material had been identified as *L. smithi*.
- Distinct **orange upper iris/black remainder**, net-like dark throat markings and diagnostic calls; broad-headed stocky frog. Adult snout–vent lengths approximately **49–55 mm males**, **53–62 mm females**.
- Integrative taxonomy combined morphology → **mitochondrial 16S rRNA** phylogeny → call analysis, distinguishing it from related *L. sylheticum* and *L. smithi*.
## Conservation recall
- Garbhanga lies near **Deepor Beel**, Assam's Ramsar wetland, in an elephant-corridor landscape. Habitat connectivity protects amphibians as well as large mammals.
- Amphibians' sensitivity to water quality, habitat disturbance and climate makes them useful bioindicators; a new description is not itself an IUCN threat-category assessment.
- Protect forest/stream microhabitats, monitor populations and control fragmentation/pollution. Source's sanctuary proposal should be distinguished from a formally gazetted designation.
