# Sarus crane conservation

## Identification and habitat
- **Antigone antigone**; older synonym *Grus antigone*. Tallest flying bird, roughly 1.5 m tall, with grey plumage and a conspicuous red head/upper neck.
- Found in South/South-East Asia and Australia; Indian strongholds include the Gangetic plains and eastern Rajasthan.
- Wetlands, marshes, flooded fields and agricultural landscapes support feeding and nesting. Generally resident with local movements; nesting pairs and seasonal social behaviour vary.
- **IUCN Vulnerable; CITES Appendix II**. Older statutory schedules in source material should not be treated as current after India's wildlife-law amendments.
- Wetland loss, drainage, disturbance and agricultural intensification threaten breeding. Protect nesting habitat and work with farming communities; very long lifespan claims about cranes generally should not be assigned to this species without evidence.
