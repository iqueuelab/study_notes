Environment

Goby fishes and Coringa mangroves

Editable notes

Distribution records

  • ZSI records Hemigobius hoevenii and Mugilogobius tigrinus in Coringa Wildlife Sanctuary, Andhra Pradesh, Godavari-estuary mangroves.
  • Previously unrecorded in Andhra Pradesh; M. tigrinus reported first on India's east coast. New locality records, not newly named species.
  • Source inventory: 135 coastal Indian goby species; 95 in Indian mangroves; 53 at Coringa—study-specific counts.

Ecology

  • Usually small bottom-dwelling fishes; many marine/estuarine species, with freshwater forms too. Commonly fused pelvic fins form a weak suction disc; morphology/diet varies across species.
  • Abundant in tropical, also temperate coasts/reefs/mangroves. Food-web roles, benthic ecology and potential environmental indicators; some island freshwater systems depend strongly on gobies.
  • Protect estuarine water quality and mangrove habitat rather than equating a regional discovery with an automatic threatened-species category.
  • Weekly practice Q23 prints an inconsistent code explanation; both stated locality records are correct in its account, not a new taxonomic discovery.
