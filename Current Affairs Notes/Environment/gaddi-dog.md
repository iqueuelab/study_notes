# Gaddi dog and indigenous breed conservation

## Himalayan guardian
- **January 2025**: **ICAR–National Bureau of Animal Genetic Resources**, Karnal, recognised Gaddi as a registered indigenous dog breed; source calls it the **fourth** after **Rajapalayam, Chippiparai (Tamil Nadu)** and **Mudhol Hound (Karnataka)**. This is a dated registration count.
- Named after **Gaddi shepherds, Himachal Pradesh**; guards sheep/goats against predators. Nicknames **Indian Panther Hound / Indian Leopard Hound** reflect guarding ability, not a taxonomic relationship with cats.
- Mainly Himalayan; predominantly **black**, occasional white feet/tail/trunk, strong arched neck and muscular, moderately long body.
- Registration documents breed characteristics and supports conservation, suitable breeding and avoidance of genetic erosion; no automatic wildlife protection schedule follows from breed registration.
