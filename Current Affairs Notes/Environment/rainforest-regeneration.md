# Rainforest nutrient cycles and regeneration

## 2011 PYQ: regeneration after clearance
- Answer **(a), rainforest soil nutrient deficiency**: heavy rainfall/leaching and rapid cycling concentrate much nutrient stock in living biomass/litter. Removing forest exports that stock and changes moisture/microclimate, hindering recovery.
- Deciduous forests often have less intense leaching and seasonal litter inputs; site soils and disturbance severity vary. High rainforest biodiversity/productivity does not imply uniformly fertile mineral soil; nor are every rainforest tree slow-growing or every propagule unviable.
- Restoration needs habitat/soil protection, native vegetation and connectivity rather than treating exotic invasion as the question's universal primary mechanism.
