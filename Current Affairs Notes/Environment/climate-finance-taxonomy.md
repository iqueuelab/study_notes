# Climate finance taxonomy and credible green investment

## Draft framework: 2025
- **Department of Economic Affairs, Finance Ministry** released a draft in May 2025, following Budget 2024–25; comments invited until **25 June 2025**. It classifies climate-aligned activities to guide capital, reduce greenwashing and support **net zero 2070**, adaptation and reliable/affordable energy. A classification framework is not itself a fund. [DEA communiqué](https://www.dea.gov.in/files/announcements_documents/DraftFrameworkIndiasClimateFinanceTaxonomyPublication6thMay2025.pdf).
- **Mitigation:** reduce/avoid emissions; **adaptation:** improve resilience; **transition:** improve hard-to-abate sectors without assuming immediate zero emissions. Source outlines climate-supportive **Tier 1/Tier 2** and transition activities; detailed sectoral annexures/thresholds require separate development.
- Focus includes **power, transport, buildings, iron/steel/cement, agriculture, water and food security**. The PDF's claim that energy/transport/cement are ignored contradicts its own sector list; treat it as criticism of prioritisation/stringency, not literal absence.
- Evaluation: scientific thresholds, measurable outcomes, disclosure/verification, clear governance and periodic updates; include states, local bodies, workers, tribal/farming communities and traditional ecological knowledge. Protect food security, labour rights and equity; avoid locking in high-emission assets under a loose transition label.
- **EU taxonomy:** substantial contribution, enabling and transitional activities with safeguards; copying labels cannot establish India-appropriate criteria. Claims that India's draft simply copies the EU or entirely lacks safeguards are editorial criticism, not independently established facts.
## Related EU revision
- **European Stability Mechanism:** euro-area financial assistance, not a refugee/trade-dispute body.
- **BTIA:** India–EU trade/investment negotiations begun in 2007.
- **Nature Restoration Law 2024:** EU ecosystem-restoration framework; measures aim to cover at least **20% of EU land and sea areas by 2030**, with longer-term restoration objectives. The 2025 PYQ identifies the EU.
