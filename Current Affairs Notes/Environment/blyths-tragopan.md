# Blyth’s tragopan: northeastern Himalayan pheasant

## Species comparison: a distinct tragopan
- The separate infographic describes **Blyth’s tragopan (Tragopan blythii)**, also called **grey-bellied tragopan**, **Nagaland's State bird** and **Vulnerable**. This is distinct from **western tragopan (T. melanocephalus)**, Himachal Pradesh's State bird.
- Its northeastern Indian/eastern Himalayan distribution extends beyond India; the infographic mentions Bhutan, Myanmar, southeastern Tibet and China. Do not carry that distribution into western-tragopan notes. Habitat protection and species-specific identification matter; all tragopans are not one species or population.
