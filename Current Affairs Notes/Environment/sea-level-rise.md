# Sea-level rise and coastal adaptation

## Causes and consequences
- Ocean warming causes **thermal expansion**; melting **land ice** adds ocean water. Ocean circulation and land subsidence/uplift affect regional relative sea level.
- Impacts: inundation, erosion, saltwater intrusion, freshwater/crop damage, displacement, waterborne disease and fishing/tourism losses.
- Indian examples: Mumbai, Chennai, Kolkata, Sundarbans mangroves and Odisha's olive-ridley nesting beaches.
## Adaptation
- Risk-sensitive coastal zoning/CRZ enforcement; early warnings and evacuation; climate-resilient infrastructure; mangrove restoration; context-appropriate protection and managed retreat; saline-tolerant agriculture.
## Climate target distinction
- India's 2022 updated NDC aimed for about **50% of cumulative installed electric-power capacity from non-fossil sources by 2030**. This is not 50% of all energy use or necessarily of electricity generation. [Official NDC explanation](https://www.pib.gov.in/Pressreleaseshare.aspx?PRID=1913467&lang=2&reg=48).
## Quantitative caution
- Source estimates (~20 cm rise since 1880, rate increasing from ~2 to ~4 mm/year, 1.2 m by 2100, 80% Sundarbans loss) lack consistent periods/scenarios. Do not treat them as universal forecasts; retain in fact-review pending underlying evidence.
- The source's 30% EV-share goal also needs its policy scope verified.
