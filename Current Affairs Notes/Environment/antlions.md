# Antlions: predation and new Indophanes species

## Newly described Indophanes species
- **Indophanes keralaensis** and **I. sahyadriensis**, antlions described from India in **Zootaxa, 23 September 2025**; bring recorded Indian Indophanes species to **five**. Issue clipping names forest collections in Kerala and highlights Western Ghats diversity. [Original research abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41118960/).
- Insects, **Myrmeleontidae, Neuroptera**. Predatory larvae have sickle-like jaws; many trap ants/small insects in conical sandy pits, while others ambush under debris. Pit-building is not universal to every antlion species.
- **Egg → larva → pupa in silk/sand cocoon → winged adult**; source broad larval duration **1–2 years**, pupation **~one month**, adults **weeks** varies with species/environment. “Doodlebugs” refers to larval tracks; adults often crepuscular/nocturnal, weak-flying and superficially damselfly-like, but belong to a different insect order.
- Dry sandy microhabitats suit pit builders; the family occurs widely, including temperate Europe. They help regulate small-insect populations; describing all as completely harmless or universally agricultural pest controllers is broader than the evidence.
