# IUCN Green List and effective conservation

## What is certified?
- Voluntary global standard for **protected and conserved areas**, administered by IUCN; not a UNEP programme or mandatory status for every park.
- Four pillars: **good governance; sound design/planning; effective management; successful conservation outcomes**. It assesses how well an area works, not merely whether it has legal designation.

- IUCN **Red List** assesses species extinction risk; Green List assesses area conservation performance. Neither is UNESCO inscription.
- Aichi Target 11's 17% terrestrial/10% marine goals were for 2020. The current global framework's Target 3 seeks effective conservation of 30% of land/inland waters and marine/coastal areas by 2030.
- Community rights, equitable management and measurable ecological outcomes matter as much as coverage statistics.
