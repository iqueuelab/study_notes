# Black-collared yellow catfish conservation

## Verified identity and news

- **Horabagrus nigricollaris**, endemic to the **Chalakudy River, Kerala**; endangered freshwater catfish with ornamental value.
- **ICAR-NBFGR's Centre for Peninsular Aquatic Genetic Resources, Kochi** reported a captive-breeding protocol on **3 January 2025**; programme began **2020**, with first-generation breeding, restocking and farmer collaboration. [ICAR](https://www.icar.gov.in/en/breakthrough-captive-breeding-endangered-black-collared-yellow-catfish-horabagrus-nigricollaris).
- The supplied PDF confuses this fish with a coexisting species and says it 'coexists with H. nigricollaris'; **H. nigricollaris is the target species itself**. Do not attribute its shorter-barbel/green-mottled description without resolving the species identity.

## Conservation and institution

- Threats: habitat loss, overfishing and a very restricted distribution; **ex-situ breeding complements in-situ river habitat protection**.
- Genetic diversity, disease control and appropriate restocking are crucial; releasing fish is not proof of a self-sustaining recovered population.
- **NBFGR**: ICAR bureau established **1983 at Allahabad/Prayagraj**; current headquarters **Lucknow**. Works on assessment/conservation of fish genetic resources and sustainable use.
