# India’s carbon emissions: sectors and comparison

## 2025 PYQ revision
- India's per-capita CO₂ emissions are **well above 0.5 tonnes**, though below many richer economies. Per-capita values need a year and accounting boundary; the source's ~1.9 tonnes is an illustrative older estimate.
- In the question's **fuel-combustion/Asia-Pacific** comparison India follows China; electricity and heat producers are the largest source category. Distinguish total emissions, per-capita emissions and sector-specific measures.
- Printed **2025 Prelims key: (c), II and III only**; statement I is false. Historical question context is not a claim that every contemporary emissions dataset uses identical boundaries.
