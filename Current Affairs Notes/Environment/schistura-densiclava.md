# Schistura densiclava and Meghalaya cave biodiversity

## May 2025 species description
- **Schistura densiclava**, freshwater stone loach, **Nemacheilidae**, described from **Krem Mawjymbuin, East Khasi Hills, Meghalaya**, in *Journal of Fish Biology*. Team included **D.K.B. Mukhim and Kangkan Sarma**; newly described does not mean newly evolved. [Primary description](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/jfb.70092).
- **Troglophile:** cave-associated organism capable of living/reproducing in surface (**epigean**) environments; distinguish an obligate cave specialist/troglobite.
- Clipping reports collection about **60 metres inside** in a cold, flowing stream, approximately **18°C**, with low oxygen. Retains eyesight/pigment; pale yellow-green body, **14–20** grey-to-black bars and a thick dark dorsal-fin-base stripe reflected in its name.
- Reported sexual dimorphism: slimmer males, puffier cheeks/irregular markings; sturdier females with more consistent markings. Genetic/morphological evidence supports species distinction.
- Known distribution is limited to the cave system in this report; ability to survive above ground does not prove a broad wild surface distribution. Protect cave hydrology/water quality and carefully assess limestone extraction, disturbance and tourism. Endemism increases vulnerability without automatically assigning an IUCN category.
