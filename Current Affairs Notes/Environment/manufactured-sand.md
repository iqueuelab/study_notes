# Manufactured sand

## Meaning and policy context
- **M-sand**: sand-size aggregate produced by crushing suitable rock, including granite/basalt, often using a **Vertical Shaft Impact (VSI)** crusher.
- Source context: Rajasthan M-Sand Policy 2024 seeks to reduce dependence on river sand and encourage sustainable construction.
## Benefits and limits
- Reduces riverbed disturbance, erosion, groundwater impacts and habitat damage from excessive extraction.
- Controlled processing can improve grading and consistent supply; suitable mine overburden may provide feedstock after quality testing.
- Cost and construction performance depend on rock quality, processing and transport. **Do not memorise the source's blanket claim that M-sand needs no washing:** fines removal and treatment may be necessary.
## Way forward
- Enforce grading/quality standards; invest in local processing and logistics; offer appropriate incentives and research support; demonstrate performance to builders; support alternative livelihoods for affected mining workers.
