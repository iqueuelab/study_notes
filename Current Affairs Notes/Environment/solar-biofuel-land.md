# Solar panels, biofuels and land-use efficiency

## January 2026: comparing useful energy per hectare
- Crop biofuels require **sunlight → biomass → processing → liquid fuel → engine**, with losses at each step. **Solar PV → electricity → electric motor** generally delivers much more transport service per hectare. Cropland also has food, habitat and carbon-restoration opportunity costs; biofuel lifecycle accounting must include fertilisers, processing and direct/indirect land-use change.
- Source uses **about 32 million hectares** of global biofuel cropland and estimates **32,000 TWh/year** of potential solar generation, compared with **roughly 7,000 TWh/year** for electrified global cars/trucks. These are a global land-allocation thought experiment, not already built capacity or proof every field has grid access. Storage, transmission, siting and vehicle electrification remain necessary.
- **Numerical correction:** original researchers give **1,400 TWh of 2024 biofuel energy**, implying roughly **23×**, rather than the supplied summary's **3,400 TWh /9–10×**. Their technical assumptions govern the comparison; electricity and liquid-fuel energy should not be mistaken for equal vehicle efficiency. [Original analysis](https://ourworldindata.org/biofuel-land-solar-electric-vehicles), [methods](https://docs.owid.io/projects/etl/analyses/biofuels_land_use/).
- Typical source comparison **15–25% PV conversion versus <1% crop-to-biomass capture** illustrates different conversion chains, not universal values for every crop/panel or a farm's entire land footprint. Solar has manufacturing/land impacts despite low operational emissions; more PV does not itself create forest restoration.
- India: prioritise land-efficient electrification, protect food security and assess ethanol feedstocks. Waste/residue-based fuels differ from dedicated energy crops; sustainable biofuels may complement aviation/shipping and other difficult-to-electrify uses, subject to lifecycle evidence rather than a blanket ban or guaranteed carbon neutrality.
