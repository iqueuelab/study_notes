# Animal classification and behaviour: PYQ recall

## Comparative recall
- **Orangutans** use sticks to extract insects/food; otters use stones for shells; sloth bears principally use snout/lips to feed on termites. Indian tree squirrels build dreys, cache food and have omnivorous diets; species/ecology matter.
- **Cicada, froghopper, pond skater:** insects, all Hemiptera. Cicadas have subterranean nymphs; froghopper nymphs form protective spittle; pond skaters exploit surface tension.
- **Ceylon frogmouth, coppersmith barbet, grey-chinned minivet, white-throated redstart:** birds. Frogmouth is nocturnal (Western Ghats/Sri Lanka); minivet/redstart recall Himalayan/Northeast habitats.
- **Mite, scorpion, spider:** Arachnida; crab is a crustacean. All are arthropods, so phylum alone does not distinguish the odd one out.
- **Peacock/Gooty tarantula, Poecilotheria metallica:** carnivorous arboreal spider with restricted Indian forest habitat; not an omnivorous crustacean. The historical Gooty collection locality should not be equated automatically with its entire extant habitat.
- Supplied answers: 2023 tool use **orangutan**, squirrel **only two**; 2024 insects **(c)**; 2020 birds **(a)**; 2014 odd-class **crab**; 2025 tarantula **2 and 3**.

## Squirrel PYQ correction
- The repeated **2023 squirrel question** prints “only one” and denies food caching; this conflicts with the existing bank's **only two (b)**. Retain **statements 2 and 3**: food caching and omnivory; Indian tree squirrels generally build nests above ground rather than burrows. Species behaviour varies, so avoid extrapolating a palm-squirrel anecdote to all Indian squirrels.
