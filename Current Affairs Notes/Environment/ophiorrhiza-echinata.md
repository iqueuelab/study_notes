# Ophiorrhiza echinata: a Western Ghats Rubiaceae herb

## Western Ghats discovery
- **Ophiorrhiza echinata**, herb in **Rubiaceae (coffee family)**, described in Nordic Journal of Botany, first published **26 September 2025**. It is not a newly discovered **Coffea beverage crop** simply because it belongs to the coffee family.
- Type locality **Devikulam, Idukki, Kerala**, southern Western Ghats; **evergreen forest–grassland ecotone at 1,630 m**. Known area of occupancy **<4 km²**, observed populations at most **35 plants**. Flowering/fruiting **August–December**.
- Original paper assigns **Data Deficient**, correcting the coaching's speculative “Critically Endangered if assessed”. An author-proposed IUCN-framework category is distinct from a completed global Red List listing. Narrow known distribution calls for surveys/habitat protection, not invention of a formal threat listing.
- Closest morphological allies in the paper **O. caudata/O. munnarensis**; echinate young stems and floral/seed differences distinguish it. The source's O. mungos medicinal analogy does not establish anticancer efficacy, safe use or a treatment from the new species. [Original botanical description](https://nsojournals.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1002/njb.04979).
