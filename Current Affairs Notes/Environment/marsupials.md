# Marsupials: development and distribution

## Identification and PYQ
- Mammals whose young are born relatively undeveloped and continue developing attached to teats, often within a pouch. Examples: **kangaroos, koalas, wombats, Tasmanian devils** in Australasia; **opossums** in the Americas.
- Occupy forests, deserts, wetlands and savannas, and coexist with predators; not confined to predator-free montane grasslands. India has **no native marsupials**.
- **2023 Prelims: (c)** — statement I is correct, statement II incorrect. Pouches are common but not universal across all marsupials.

## Development, energy use and source corrections
- Marsupials are a distinct living mammal lineage, **not an intermediate evolutionary rung** between monotremes and “advanced” placentals. Very short gestation and prolonged lactation support relatively undeveloped young; pouches are common, not universal. Some develop a short-lived yolk-sac placenta, so **“no placenta” is too absolute**. [Natural History Museum](https://www.nhm.ac.uk/discover/news/2023/may/marsupials-might-be-the-more-evolved-mammals.html).
- Epipubic bones occur in both sexes; embryonic diapause is an adaptation in certain species, not all marsupials. Wombats' backward-facing pouches suit burrowing. Habitat loss, introduced predators, road deaths, heat/drought and **Tasmanian devil facial tumour disease** illustrate different conservation pressures.
- Kangaroo tendons store and return elastic energy during hopping; posture and gait influence efficiency. The issue's claim of absolutely constant energy per hop at all speeds is an oversimplification, and its **eLife 2024** study attribution is unresolved. Elasticity is relevant to locomotion/robotics, not evidence that faster movement is energetically free.
- Existing **2023 PYQ (c)** and habitat/distribution notes already cover the two statements. Avoid the source's fixed ~330 species as a present census or “only one species in North America” without clarifying northern North America versus the entire continent including Central America. The **Virginia opossum** extends into temperate North America; American marsupial diversity is wider.
