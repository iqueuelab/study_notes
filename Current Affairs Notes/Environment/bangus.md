# Bangus Valley and community ecotourism

## Map and proposal
- **Kupwara, north Kashmir, near Lo C**; two meadow bowls amid mountains/forest, reported~10, 000 feet/~100 km from Srinagar.
- March 2025 plan emphasised modest shelters, toilets, signage, waste systems and registered homestays, rather than large hotels; source 19 registered stays is a snapshot.
- Diversifies pressure away from Pahalgam/Gulmarg/Sonamarg; potential benefits to shepherds, nomadic communities and local artisans.
- Carrying capacity, waste/water, habitat disturbance, local consent and border security need coordination; PARIVESH is a clearance portal, not automatic permission.
- The source's National Tourism Policy 2022 reference concerns a draft; do not treat every proposed national policy as enacted.
