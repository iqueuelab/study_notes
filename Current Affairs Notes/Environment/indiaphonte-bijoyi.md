# Indiaphonte bijoyi: Kavaratti meiofauna and taxonomy

## Discovery and taxonomy
- **Indiaphonte bijoyi**, new genus and species of microscopic crustacean from **Kavaratti lagoon, Lakshadweep, southeastern Arabian Sea**; described by **Neelima Vasu and Samuel Gómez**, published **6 January 2026 in Zootaxa**. Classification: **Arthropoda → Copepoda → Harpacticoida → Laophontidae**. [Original description](https://www.mapress.com/zt/article/view/zootaxa.5741.2.3).
- Distinctive combinations of body/appendage morphology justify the new genus; the paper compares leg segmentation, setae and sexual dimorphism with related genera. It is **not the first copepod ever recorded from India**.
- Source describes a semi-cylindrical body, **518–772 μm**, females slightly larger; genus honours India and species honours marine scientist **S. Bijoy Nandan**. Work links CUSAT with Mexican taxonomic expertise.
- **Meiofauna** are small sediment-associated animals such as copepods and nematodes; size definitions depend on sieve conventions rather than one universal 1-mm boundary. They support nutrient cycling, sediment processes and food webs. These general ecological roles do not by themselves prove this newly described species has been experimentally validated as a pollution indicator.
- A lagoon is shallow water partly separated from a larger sea by a reef, sand barrier or similar feature; atoll and coastal lagoons differ. Conserving lagoon sediments protects overlooked biodiversity alongside corals and larger animals.
