Additions to biodiversity records

  • Reports released in Kolkata: 683 faunal additions = 459 species new to science + 224 new Indian records; 433 floral taxa = 410 species + 23 infraspecific taxa. Combined 1,116 additions is not “1,116 species newly discovered globally”. New record means previously known elsewhere, recorded in India for the first time.

  • Zoological Survey's report gives 22 new genera, correcting source's “2”; it also notes 37 reptile and five amphibian species. ZSI report.
  • Named examples: Dravidoseps gouensis (lizard; Dravidoseps is a new genus) and Anguiculus dicaprioi, a colubrid snake.
  • Floral additions: Kerala 58, Maharashtra 45, Uttarakhand 40; Western Ghats and northeast contributed about 35%. Categories: angiosperms 154, pteridophytes 4, bryophytes 15, lichens 63, fungi 156, algae 32, microbes 9 (total 433).
  • Orchids: Bulbophyllum gopalianum, Coelogyne tripurensis, Gastrodia indica, G. sikkimensis. The report's cumulative botanical documentation count is 56,177 across its broad organism categories, which include fungi/algae etc. India is among 17 megadiverse countries; discovery counts measure documentation effort as well as biodiversity.
