# Bhadra Tiger Reserve and soft release of elephants

## Map recall
- Western Ghats, **Karnataka**, Chikkamagaluru–Shivamogga landscape; Bhadra became a tiger reserve in **1998**.
- Bhadra River joins Tunga to form **Tungabhadra → Krishna**. Dry/moist deciduous, semi-evergreen and upland shola habitats support tigers, elephants and diverse birds.
- Other Karnataka tiger reserves: Bandipur, Nagarahole, Kali and Biligiri Ranganatha Temple.
## Human–elephant conflict
- **Soft release**: acclimatisation before freer movement, with monitoring, suitable habitat and veterinary care. Capturing and releasing a conflict elephant does not guarantee it will remain away from settlements.
- Assess carrying capacity, corridors, food/water, behaviour and risks to resident animals/nearby communities; combine early warnings and habitat protection with fair compensation.
- Tiger reserve, wildlife sanctuary and elephant reserve are distinct designations; do not infer one automatically from another.
