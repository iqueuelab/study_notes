# Geologic carbon storage: prudent capacity limits

## September 2025 Nature study
- **Capture → transport → inject CO₂ into suitable deep geological formations → monitor containment.** A Nature study published **3 September 2025**, not 2024 as printed, estimates prudent global potential near **1,460 Gt CO₂** after safety/environmental/technical exclusions. [Study](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-09423-y).
- Mapping sedimentary basins and excluding unsuitable seismic, polar/ecologically sensitive or technically inaccessible areas reduces earlier broad potential estimates. Geological pore volume is not equivalent to safe, accessible, affordable storage.
- Capacity is geographically uneven: the issue highlights Russia, US and Saudi Arabia versus limited options in India/many European countries. This raises access, cross-border transport, liability and allocation questions.
- A capacity exhaustion date around **2200** is a scenario, not a measured countdown. The study's roughly **0.7°C** reversal ceiling assumes use of the full prudent potential for durable removal and a specified climate response; residual emissions or CCS sustaining fossil use reduce reversal potential. These are model-dependent limits, not assured cooling.
- Storage complements rapid emissions cuts; prioritising hard-to-abate residual emissions versus removal requires long-term monitoring, permanence and intergenerational allocation choices.
