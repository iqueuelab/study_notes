# Flamingo Festival and wetland ecotourism

## January 2025 event and locations

- Source scheduled **18–20 January 2025**, **Sullurpeta, Tirupati district, Andhra Pradesh**, returning after five years.
- Birdwatching/eco-tourism at **Nelapattu**, **B.V. Palem**, **Atakanithippa**, **Sri City** and nearby wetland sites; **Pulicat** landscape links Andhra Pradesh and Tamil Nadu. The source's 'over 200 bird species' is broad event-region richness, not a promise every visitor sees 200 species.
- Cultural programmes, arts/crafts, biodiversity awareness and community participation; source says eligible local infrastructure work supported through **MGNREGA**, not that the entire festival is an MGNREGA scheme.

## Mains balance

- Benefits: habitat awareness, local businesses/livelihoods and cultural exchange.
- Risks: disturbance to birds, waste, overbuilding and exclusion of marginalised communities.
- Response: carrying-capacity limits, habitat buffers, waste control, ecologically suitable access and transparent local benefit sharing; conservation outcomes matter alongside tourist numbers.
