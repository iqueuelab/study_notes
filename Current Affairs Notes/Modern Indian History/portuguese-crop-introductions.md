# Portuguese crop introductions in India

## 2025 PYQ correction
- Source's answer **“only two”** is wrong: **papaya, pineapple and guava** are American-origin crops associated with Portuguese introduction to India in the question's sixteenth/seventeenth-century context; **all three (c)**.
- NHB records pineapple introduction **1548**; do not assign this exact year to papaya/guava without evidence. Source's assertion that guava reached India through Spaniards is unsupported; an ICAR-hosted horticulture paper attributes its Indian introduction to the Portuguese. [NHB pineapple](https://www.nhb.gov.in/report_files/pineapple/pineapple.htm), [ICAR guava paper](https://epubs.icar.org.in/index.php/IJAgS/article/download/153958/60535).
- Crop transfers illustrate the Columbian Exchange and maritime trade; native origin, introduction route and later cultivation are separate facts.
