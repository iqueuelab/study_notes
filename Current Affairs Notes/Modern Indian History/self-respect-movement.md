# Self-Respect Movement: centenary and social reform

## 1925–2025: centenary
- **E. V. Ramasamy (Periyar)** founded the Self-Respect Movement in **1925**; **Kudi Arasu**, his Tamil weekly, disseminated its critique of caste hierarchy, priestly dominance, superstition and gender inequality. His participation in **Vaikom Satyagraha** forms part of the anti-untouchability background.
- Equal dignity, rational inquiry and social justice informed opposition to untouchability and the Devadasi system, support for widow remarriage and women's participation. Source highlights **Annai Meenambal and Veerammal** as women leaders; recognise women as organisers, not merely beneficiaries.
- **Self-Respect marriages** rejected priest-centred ritual and emphasised equal partnership; later legal recognition is distinct from the movement's founding. **1929 Chengalpattu conference**, associated with **W. P. A. Soundara Pandian**, spread its programme.
- Source pamphlets **Namathu Kurikkol** and **Tiravitak Kalaka Lateiyam** articulate objectives; the movement helped shape later Dravidian politics and social-welfare debates in Tamil Nadu. [Movement's archival account](https://www.periyardigitallibrary.in/assets/img/annual-numbers/pdf-3.pdf).
- Revision: founder **Periyar**, not Ambedkar, Bhaskarrao Jadhav or Dinkarrao Javalkar. The pictured question has no supplied year, so is excluded from dated PYQ banks pending review.
