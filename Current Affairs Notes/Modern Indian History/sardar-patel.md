# Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel and National Unity Day

## National Unity Day: 150th birth anniversary, 2025
- **Rashtriya Ekta Diwas, 31 October**, commemorates **Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel (1875–1950)**, India's first Deputy Prime Minister and Home Minister. The 2025 issue anticipates a parade/cultural programme at **Ekta Nagar (Kevadia), Narmada district, Gujarat**; these are announced arrangements, not completed events as of this issue.
- **24 October MHA announcement:** CAPFs/State police, women personnel with a **female officer leading the Guard of Honour**, **five CRPF Shaurya Chakra awardees**, **16 BSF gallantry-medal recipients**, State/UT tableaux and **900 classical-dance artists**. **Rampur and Mudhol hounds** showcase indigenous BSF breeds. Avoid turning a female-led Guard into a claim that every member is a woman. [MHA programme](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2182158&lang=2&reg=48).
- Public participation, diversity and cooperative federalism frame the commemoration. The issue's **~150 deployed dogs** is an operational snapshot, not an enduring force total. **31 October** is also Indira Gandhi's death anniversary.
## Patel: freedom struggle and integration
- Born **31 October 1875, Nadiad, Gujarat**, from a Lewa Patidar farming background; studied law in England, returned in **1913**, and practised in Ahmedabad. Gandhi's satyagraha influenced his mobilisation.
- **Kheda (1918):** crop failure and revenue demands; peasant organisation seeking relief. **Bardoli (1928):** disciplined non-payment campaign and the title **Sardar**. The imposed revenue increase commonly cited in official recall is **22%**, not the issue's undifferentiated 30%. [Government historical account](https://cbcindia.gov.in/wp-content/themes/boc/mann_ki_baat/e_october_2025/files/basic-html/page44.html).
- Participated in Non-Cooperation, Civil Disobedience/**1930 Salt Satyagraha**, and **Quit India (1942)**, with repeated imprisonment. Presided over **Karachi Congress (1931)**, associated with Fundamental Rights and National Economic Programme resolutions and the Gandhi–Irwin Pact debate.
- **1947–49:** Patel and **V. P. Menon** combined negotiation, accession and administrative integration of princely states; source/MHA count **562**. **Junagadh:** popular opposition and later plebiscite; **Hyderabad: Operation Polo, 1948**; **Jammu and Kashmir: accession in 1947**, followed by distinct constitutional arrangements. Accession, merger and later Article 370 should not be collapsed into one act. Lapse of British paramountcy created an integration problem; it should not be taught as a durable, uncontested right of every State to remain independent.
- Defended a professional, impartial **All India Services** framework; **21 April Civil Services Day** commemorates his **1947 address** to civil servants. The “steel frame” metaphor predates Patel, although associated with his defence of civil services. Chaired the Constituent Assembly's **Advisory Committee on Fundamental Rights, Minorities and Tribal and Excluded Areas**.
## Statue and significance
- **Statue of Unity: 182 m**, **Ram V. Sutar**, inaugurated **31 October 2018**, near the Narmada at Ekta Nagar. Leadership links: national integration, administrative continuity, constitutional loyalty and balancing firmness with negotiation; heroic epithets do not replace analysis of individual accessions.
