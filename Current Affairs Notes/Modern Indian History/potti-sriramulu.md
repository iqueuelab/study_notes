# Potti Sriramulu and linguistic reorganisation

## Potti Sriramulu and linguistic federalism
- **Potti Sriramulu (1901–1952)** was a Gandhian freedom fighter and social reformer associated with the movement for a Telugu-speaking State. His **58-day fast ended in death on 15 December 1952**, triggering widespread protests.
- **Andhra State formed on 1 October 1953**, separating Telugu-speaking areas from Madras State, with **Kurnool** as capital. It is conventionally described as independent India's first State formed on linguistic grounds; this does not deny pre-independence linguistic provincial reorganisation.
- In **1956**, Andhra State and the Telangana region of Hyderabad State combined as Andhra Pradesh during States reorganisation. Distinguish the 1953 Andhra State from 1956 Andhra Pradesh and the later 2014 bifurcation.
- The March issue reports a **58-foot bronze “Statue of Sacrifice”** unveiled by Andhra Pradesh CM N. Chandrababu Naidu at **Sakhamuru Park, Amaravati**, for Sriramulu's **125th birth anniversary**. The height symbolises his fast; the memorial links linguistic identity, public mobilisation and federal accommodation.
- [Lok Sabha biographical account](https://eparlib.sansad.in/bitstream/123456789/56236/1/Tanguturi_Prakasam_AK_English.pdf) confirms the fast, 1 October 1953 formation and Kurnool capital.
