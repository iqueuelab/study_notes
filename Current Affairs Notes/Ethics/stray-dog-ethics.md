# Stray dogs: public safety and humane management

## January 2025 controversy
- Morocco to co-host **2030 FIFA World Cup with Spain/Portugal**; source discusses animal-welfare campaign allegations of culling **three million dogs**, **300,000 annually**, using poisoning/shooting.
- These are **reported allegations/estimates**, not independently verified official policy or completed numbers. Do not infer government intent from the article's speculation. Campaign evidence requires attribution: [OIPA January appeal](https://www.oipa.org/international/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/2030-FIFA-World-Cup_OIPA-Appeal-January-2025.pdf).
## Ethical balance
- **Public health, rabies prevention and bite safety** alongside compassion, proportionality, ecological/public responsibility and accountable spending; urban appearance alone is a weak justification for cruelty.
- Humane population management: **sterilisation + vaccination**, responsible ownership, waste/food-source management, adoption where suitable and monitoring.
- India's **Animal Birth Control** framework is a comparison, not Moroccan law. Standards of **WOAH (formerly OIE)** do not automatically establish a particular international legal offence or give animals identical human constitutional rights.
- For a case study: verify allegations → assess risk → consult veterinary/public-health expertise → adopt lawful humane measures → publish outcomes and audit accountability.
## May 2025: Indian governance framework
- **PCA Act 1960** animal-care/cruelty duties; **ABC Rules 2023**, notified **10 March 2023**, superseded the **2001 rules**. Local bodies oversee sterilisation and immunisation, through qualified facilities/recognised organisations. [Official notification account](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleaseIframePage.aspx?PRID=1917510&lang=2&reg=48).
- The 2023 framework covers humane capture, surgery, recovery and return; designated feeding arrangements away from play areas/entry-exit points; complaint/help mechanisms. Euthanasia is restricted to qualifying incurably ill/mortally wounded animals with veterinary determination, not blanket permission to kill healthy dogs. [AWBI rules](https://awbi.gov.in/Document/rules).
- Safety concerns: bites/rabies, road accidents, resident–feeder conflicts and wildlife predation near forests. Open waste, abandonment and inadequate management can sustain populations.
## Numbers and rabies graphic: corrections
- The PDF's **35 million strays/over 10 million pets** are unverified estimates, not a stated census. **2019 Livestock Census: about 1.53 crore stray dogs**, confirmed in a later official advisory. [DAHD/AWBI advisory](https://www.awbi.gov.in/uploads/regulations/175439942841Joint%20Advisory%20Secretaries%2016.07.2025.pdf).
- **4,146 “dog-bite deaths in 2019”** lacks a reliable source/category; bites, rabies deaths and estimated versus reported deaths differ. A **90% sterilisation** assertion is not a universally established rapid-control threshold.
- Embedded graphic distinguishes vaccination from sterilisation: rabies virus affects the nervous system and is almost invariably fatal after symptoms. Dog vaccination prevents transmission; timely wound washing and medical post-exposure care are critical. The graphic's **70% dog vaccination** benchmark is not a 70% sterilisation benchmark. [WHO rabies](https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/rabies).
## Evaluate outcomes and lawful reforms
- Establish baseline dog counts, geographic coverage, vaccination/sterilisation tracking, adequate veterinary funding and accountable procurement; measure population trends, bites and welfare, rather than procedures alone.
- Waste management, responsible ownership, licensing/anti-abandonment enforcement, adoption and suitable shelters complement population management; involve RWAs/NGOs without stigmatising all feeders.
- National targets, mandatory non-breeding-pet sterilisation and broader feeding bans are **editorial proposals**, not universal new legal duties enacted by this issue. Ooty, Chennai, Goa and locations in Delhi/Uttarakhand are cited as models, not independently proven equal successes.
