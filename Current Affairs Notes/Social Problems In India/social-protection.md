# Social protection, informal workers and the ILO

## Coverage and dimensions
- Source **24%→49%** rounds the reported **24.4%2021 →48.8%2024** coverage under **at least one social-protection benefit**; not proof everyone covered has comprehensive protection. [Official clarification](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2115391&lang=2&reg=3).
- **Social insurance**: contributory risk protection; **social assistance**: tax-financed support; labour-market programmes support income/work. Adequacy, timely payment and actual access matter with enrolment.
- **e-Shram2021**, worker database; registration is not automatic pension/insurance entitlement. DBT can improve delivery but is **not inherently leakage-free** or free from exclusion.
- **PM-KISAN₹6,000/year**, eligible **PM-JAY** hospital cover, **APY** contributory pension and **MGNREGA100 days per rural household demanding unskilled work**—not100days guaranteed to every individual regardless of demand.
- February2025 Labour Codes enacted but not all implemented; recognition of gig/platform workers did **not automatically make every gig worker an insured ESIC beneficiary**. Scheme announcement, registration and benefit receipt differ.
## ILO recall
- **1919, Treaty of Versailles; Geneva; UN specialised agency1946**. India member since **28June1919**, correcting PDF's1920. [ILO country profile](https://normlex.ilo.org/dyn/nrmlx_en/f?p=NORMLEXPUB%3A11110%3A0%3A%3ANO%3A%3AP11110_COUNTRY_ID%3A102691).
- **Tripartite: governments, employers, workers**; International Labour Conference sets standards; Governing Body executive oversight.
- Reports: **World Employment and Social Outlook, Global Wage Report, World Social Protection Report2024–26, India Wage Report2018**.
- India ratified **C100 equal remuneration, C138 minimum age, C182 worst child labour**; source-era **C87/C98** not ratified. Ratification and domestic implementation are distinct; total convention count changes with time.
- Source187members/DG **GilbertF.Houngbo** are dated institutional facts, not permanent constitutional rules. Cross-country49/70/90/100coverage table lacks comparable definitions.
## Reform
- Informal earnings, digital barriers, gender gaps and portability complicate coverage; reliable funding, outreach, grievance redress and linked schemes are needed.
- Measure effective benefits, adequacy/equity and shock resilience; protect workers through formalisation without assuming informal work means no employment.
