November 2025: interpreting the 2025 ranking

  • World Happiness Report 2025: India 118/147, score 4.389; Pakistan 109; United States 24. Finland first for eighth consecutive year, followed by Denmark and Iceland; Sweden is fourth. Produced by Oxford's Wellbeing Research Centre with partners including Gallup and the UN Sustainable Development Solutions Network. 2025 report.
  • Ranking uses respondents' Cantril Ladder life evaluation, 0–10, averaged over 2022–24 for this edition. Six variables—log GDP per capita, social support, healthy life expectancy, freedom, generosity, perceived corruption—help explain differences; they are not six weighted ingredients used to construct the ranking. Report's methodology.
  • GDP size differs from per-capita income and subjective wellbeing. Expectations, inequality, public-service experience, community support, trust and mental health can matter; the editorial's claim that India ranks low simply because democracy encourages complaints is a hypothesis, not demonstrated causal evidence.
  • Cross-cultural response differences and uncertainty merit scrutiny; critiques of Freedom House/V-Dem in a 2022 EAC-PM paper do not automatically establish defects in Gallup's separate household-survey methodology. “WEIRD” means Western, Educated, Industrialised, Rich, Democratic; the assertion that the report ignores family support is overstated because social support is explicitly studied.
  • Source says 19% of young adults globally report no one to rely on, up 39% since 2006; this is a global observation, not an India-specific prevalence estimate. The PDF's “best 2022/worst 2012” Indian historical-rank explanation is unreliable and omitted.
  • Editorial priorities: shared public spaces/community networks, intergenerational support, accessible and transparent services, and stronger mental-health care including Tele-MANAS. Evaluate these for wellbeing rather than rank alone; the cited US$1→US$4 mental-health return is a study-based economic estimate, not a guaranteed return for every intervention.
