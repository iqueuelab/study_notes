Social Problems In India

Global Gender Gap Report 2025 and women’s empowerment

Editable notes

2025 report: read rank, score and ratios separately

  • WEF index: economic participation/opportunity, education, health/survival, political empowerment. India 131/148, score 64.4%; source's 64.1% is stale. Global 68.8%, estimated 123 years to parity at observed pace, not a guaranteed deadline. Iceland, Finland, Norway, UK, New Zealand lead; Bangladesh 24th.
  • India: economic parity 40.7% (+0.9 percentage points); estimated earned-income parity 28.6→29.9%; labour-force-participation parity ratio 45.9%, not the proportion of Indian women in work. Education parity 97.1%. Parliament women 14.7→13.8%; these percentages are not the whole political subindex score. Health parity can improve even as life expectancy falls for both sexes. WEF report.
  • Rankings depend on other countries and coverage; the report's retrospective comparable-rank change need not equal a simple subtraction from the prior published 129th rank. Closing a relative gap does not prove adequate absolute opportunity or establish causation by a particular scheme.

Policy links from the June issue

  • BBBP: girl-child survival, protection and education; KGBV: residential schooling for disadvantaged girls; rural community empowerment/skills initiatives such as historical Mahila Shakti Kendra; Sukanya Samriddhi girl-child savings. Earlier Rashtriya Mahila Kosh microcredit and Mahila-e-Haat marketing exemplify programme approaches, not proof all historical schemes continued unchanged in 2025.
  • Stand-Up India: loans historically ₹10 lakh–₹1 crore for eligible SC/ST and women entrepreneurs; skills, childcare, safe transport, formal work and SHGs help translate education into employment. Audit observations about programme spending require a specific report/period, not generalisation.
  • 73rd/74th Amendments: at least one-third women's representation in rural/urban local bodies; States may provide more. 106th Amendment Act 2023 provides one-third reservation in Lok Sabha/State Assemblies (and Delhi Assembly), tied to its census/delimitation implementation trigger; it was already enacted, not a pending Bill, but not yet operating in the 2025 seat distribution.
  • Address proxy representation, capacity, care burdens, wage gaps and data transparency. 2017 PYQ: Global Gender Gap Index—WEF (a); 2019: Global Competitiveness Report—WEF (c).
