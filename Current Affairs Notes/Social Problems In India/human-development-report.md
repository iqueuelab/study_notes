# Human Development Report 2025: India and AI

## Index and 2025 report
- **UNDP Human Development Report 2025**, released **6May 2025**: *A matter of choice: People and possibilities in the age of AI*. Human development focuses on capabilities/choices beyond GDP.
- **HDI** combines health (**life expectancy at birth**), education (**expected and mean years of schooling**) and living standards (**GNI per capita, PPP**). Mean years measure attainment, not directly learning quality. HDI is the geometric mean of normalised dimension indices.
- Latest table refers to **2023 data**, not outcomes measured in 2025. **India 130/193, 0.685** vs recalculated **2022 rank 133/value 0.676**; medium category, high threshold **0.700**. India improved by over 53% since 1990. **Iceland 0.972** leads, followed by Norway/Switzerland; **South Sudan 0.388**, rank 193, last. [UNDP HDR](https://www.undp.org/india/publications/human-development-report-2025).

## Inclusion and AI
- Inequality lowers India's HDI by **30.7%**; income/gender gaps and unequal schooling opportunities persist. **13.5 crore** people exited multidimensional poverty between **2015–16 and2019–21**; this differs from the 2022–23HDI change.
- Source cites female LFPR **41.7% in 2023–24**, a PLFS measure with defined age/status coverage; it does not measure job quality or wages. One-third women's legislative reservation was enacted but **not operational inMay 2025**. MGNREGA, RTE and NRHM illustrate welfare contributions without attributing every index movement to a single scheme.
- AI: India is the only low-/middle-income country among the Global AI Index top 10; high **self-reported** AI-skill penetration is a platform-based measure, not proof universal proficiency. Source reports about **20%** domestic AI-researcher retention in 2023 vs near-zero 2019; read with dataset limits.
- Opportunities: farm advice/precision farming, diagnostics/telemedicine, personalised education and public-service delivery. Risks: unequal access, displaced/poor-quality jobs, biased algorithms and privacy. Build skills, inclusive digital infrastructure, human oversight and accountable data governance. Global trade tensions, debt and jobless industrialisation compound stalled human-development progress; AI outcomes reflect public choices, not technological inevitability.
