# Construction workers and portable welfare

## Coverage and access barriers
- BOCW Act 1996 and its cess framework support State welfare boards; construction cess is generally **1% of construction cost** under the notified rate.
- Registration eligibility includes age 18–60 and at least **90 days of construction work in the preceding 12 months**, not necessarily continuous work for one employer.
- Migrant mobility, fragmented registration, proof requirements, informal contracts and low awareness impede benefits. Heat, pollution-related stoppages, unsafe work and unpaid wages compound vulnerability.
- Source figures on accumulated/unspent cess are dated estimates; funds collected do not automatically translate into benefits delivered.
- **e-Shram has existed since 2021** as a national unorganised-worker database. Registration alone does not make every State BOCW benefit automatically portable.
- Link records with safeguards, simplify lawful proof, offer worksite registration/help desks, improve interstate coordination and grievance redress, and publish outcome-based spending information.
