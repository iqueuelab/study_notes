# Primary health care: access, trust and financial protection

## Comprehensive care
- Primary care combines **promotion → prevention → treatment → rehabilitation → palliation**, including NCDs, mental health, elderly care, communicable disease and environmental/social determinants.
- **Ayushman Bharat (2018)** originally has two complementary components: Health and Wellness Centres (now **Ayushman Arogya Mandirs, AAMs**) for comprehensive primary care, and **PM-JAY** for eligible secondary/tertiary hospitalisation with **₹5 lakh/family/year** cover.
- **PM-ABHIM (2021)** is a separate infrastructure mission supporting laboratories, surveillance and critical-care preparedness; **ABDM** supports digital health infrastructure.
- **1,75,418 AAMs operational as of 10 December 2024**. Out-of-pocket spending share of total health expenditure fell **48.8% (2017–18) → 39.4% (2021–22)**; a falling share does not establish a fall in absolute per-capita spending or exclusive causation by one scheme. [Health Ministry](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2084188&lang=2&reg=48).
## Quality, affordability and confidence
- Catastrophic health expenditure depends on the indicator: a **40% capacity-to-pay** threshold is one method; SDG monitoring also uses **10%/25% of household income or expenditure**. Distinguish measurement from insurance entitlement.
- **IPHS (introduced 2007)** specifies facility/service norms; **NQAS** evaluates eight domains: service provision, patient rights, inputs, support services, clinical care, infection control, quality management and outcomes. [NHSRC standards](https://nhsrcindia.org/national-quality-assurance-standards).
- Public trust improves when care is timely, clean, respectful and dependable. Staffing/absenteeism, uneven quality, weak referrals, digital gaps and urban migrant access can undermine use.
- Ensure free/affordable essential medicines and diagnostics, continuity of NCD care, NUHM urban primary services, community oversight and transparent patient-experience audits. Digital records and telemedicine require functioning referral systems and privacy protections.
