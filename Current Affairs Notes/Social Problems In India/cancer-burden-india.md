# Cancer burden in India: estimates and interpretation

## Keep datasets distinct
- NCRP research estimated **14,61,427 new cases in 2022**, about **one in nine** people developing cancer in their lifetime, and projected **15,69,793 cases in 2025**. The projection is not an observed 2025 count. [NCRP research](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/10231735/).
- **IARC GLOBOCAN 2022** independently estimated **14,13,316 new cases** and **9,16,827 deaths**. Different methodologies mean these estimates should not be spliced into one table. [IARC India factsheet](https://gco.iarc.who.int/media/globocan/factsheets/populations/356-india-fact-sheet.pdf).

| Cervix uteri | 1,27,526 | 9.0% |
| Lung | 81,748 | 5.8% |

- The PDF's breast **2.8 lakh/19.7%**, lung **1.2 lakh/8.6%**, cervical **1.05 lakh/7.5%**, oral **1.1 lakh/7.8%**, colorectal **0.88 lakh/6.2%**, and site-specific death table are not a consistent reproduction of this dataset. Its **8.1 lakh deaths/8.3% of all deaths** and sex-specific annual mortality-growth percentages lack a stated comparable source/period; do not memorise them as verified 2022 statistics.
## Distribution and policy significance
- GLOBOCAN leading incidence sites: **men — lip/oral cavity, lung, oesophagus; women — breast, cervix, ovary**. Tobacco/alcohol contribute to several cancers, but not every cancer can be attributed to them.
- Northeast registries show a substantial burden, with regional patterns such as stomach/oesophageal cancers in Mizoram; rates, absolute counts and registry coverage must be distinguished. The PDF's blanket State rankings for breast/oral cancer need a defined metric and period.
- Ageing and risk-factor exposure influence future burden. Strengthen tobacco control, HPV prevention, screening, timely referral, affordable treatment, cancer registries and caregiver support; incidence, mortality and survival measure different things.
