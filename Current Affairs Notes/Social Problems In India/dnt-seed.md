# Denotified and nomadic communities: SEED and inclusion

## Categories and exclusion
| Category | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **DNT** | Communities denotified after colonial criminal-tribe labelling |
| **NT** | Nomadic livelihoods/mobility |
| **SNT** | Semi-nomadic, periodically mobile |
- **Criminal Tribes Act, 1871** imposed collective stigma; denotification/repeal **1952**. Mobility and denotification are different criteria; categories overlap.
- Communities may be classified **SC, ST, OBC or none** depending on State/list; examples **Kanjar, Nat, Pardhi, Sapera**. Neither all STs nor all nomads are automatically DNTs.
- Identity/address documentation and certificates, insecure housing/livelihoods, interrupted schooling, healthcare access, stigma and limited representation; source's claim that none ever owned land/property is overbroad.
## Commissions: timeline recall
- **1947 Criminal Tribes Inquiry**; **1949 Ananthasayanam Ayyangar committee**; **1953 Kalelkar Commission**, first Backward Classes Commission.
- **Mandal** appointed **1979**, report **1980**; **NCRWC 2002** examined stigma; **Renke** constituted **2005**, report **2008**; **Idate** decision **2014**, report titled **2017**, submitted **8 January 2018**.
- Source lists **1,526 communities, 269 unclassified**; a 2022 official summary lists **1,262**. Different catalogues/counts should not be treated as one consistent current census. Population claims **25 crore**, **10%**, **10–12 crore**, and **150 DNT/500 NT groups** also differ in scope/period and are estimates, not settled present census totals.
## SEED and welfare
- **Scheme for Economic Empowerment of DNTs**, launched **February 2022**, Social Justice and Empowerment Ministry; planned **₹200 crore over five years from 2021–22**.
| Component | Delivery link |
| --- | --- |
| Competitive/professional entrance coaching | Education/employment access |
| Health insurance | **PM-JAY** |
| Livelihoods | **NRLM / State Rural Livelihood Missions** |
| Housing | **PMAY** |
- **DWBDNC**, registered society, established **21 February 2019**, rather than a permanent statutory commission. [Board record](https://dwbdnc.dosje.gov.in/index.php/content/about-us).
- **Dr Ambedkar pre/post-matric scholarships** and **Nanaji Deshmukh hostel scheme**, **2014–15**, source focus on DNT students outside SC/ST/OBC coverage. Eligibility/overlapping benefits require scheme-specific checks.
- Idate recommendations: permanent commission; State welfare departments; detailed survey; classification of unclassified groups; legal safeguards, including proposed additional SC/ST atrocities-law schedule. **Recommendations are not enacted protections by themselves**. [Official SEED background](https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2022/feb/doc202222519501.pdf).
## Mains response and PYQs
- Documentation camps/portable entitlements → accurate identification → accessible education, healthcare, housing and livelihoods → anti-stigma enforcement → participation and outcomes audit. Avoid treating mobility as criminal propensity.
- **Prelims 2014, Changpa**: primarily **Ladakh**, rear **pashmina goats**, **ST**; statements **2 and 3**, answer **(b)**. This comparison does not classify all Changpa as DNTs.
- **Mains 2017** asks for two major post-Independence legal initiatives addressing ST discrimination (**250 words, 15 marks**). Full questions are in the common subject PYQ files; welfare schemes and enforceable anti-discrimination laws must be distinguished.
