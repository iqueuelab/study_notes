# Xenophobia and racism

## Distinguish basis of exclusion
| Concept | Basis and reach |
|---|---|
| Xenophobia | Fear/hostility toward foreigners or people perceived as outsiders; may target someone of the same racial background |
| Racism | Racial/ethnic hierarchy, prejudice or discrimination; can affect fellow citizens within one country |
- Greek **xenos (stranger) + phobos (fear)**. Both can overlap in anti-immigrant violence and exclusionary nationalism; racism need not require foreign nationality, and xenophobia need not require racial difference.
- Source reports attacks in China/Japan as context; avoid attributing such conduct to whole national populations. Equal protection, effective investigation, inclusive public communication and institutional anti-discrimination practices address individual and systemic dimensions.
