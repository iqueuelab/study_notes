# Adoption: child-centred safeguards and CARA

## Streamline procedures while protecting children
- July discussion cites **13 prospective parents per child legally free for adoption**; this is a source snapshot of registered demand/eligible supply, not all children in shelters or all Indian couples. Many applicants seek young healthy infants while eligible children may be older or have special needs.
- Children in institutions are **not automatically orphans or legally adoptable**: tracing family, restoration possibilities and Child Welfare Committee due process matter. Simplifying lawful identification/coordination differs from removing anti-trafficking safeguards.
- Digitised district records, trained CWCs/agencies and timely eligibility decisions; effective home studies, parent preparation, older/special-needs support, counselling and post-adoption follow-up improve safe placements. Principle: find a suitable family for the child, not a child to satisfy demand.
## Central Adoption Resource Authority
- Established as an autonomous body in **1990**; statutory under **JJ(Care and Protection of Children)Act2015 section68**, **Ministry of Women and Child Development**. Nodal authority for in-/inter-country adoption of orphaned, abandoned and surrendered children through recognised agencies.
- India ratified **1993 Hague Intercountry Adoption Convention in2003**; CARA is Central Authority, with international NOC/conformity processes, including **Article23 certificates**.
- **CARINGS:** Child Adoption Resource Information and Guidance System; supports registration, child/parent matching workflow and monitoring with SARA,DCPU,SAA,CWC and authorised foreign agencies. Confidentiality/data accuracy and verified legal status are essential. [CARA Bench Book](https://cara.wcd.gov.in/PDF/Bench%20Book%20For%20Adoptions.pdf).
- Legal routes: secular **JJAct** adoption and **Hindu Adoptions and Maintenance Act1956** for those covered. **Guardians and Wards Act1890 creates guardianship, not full adoption**, correcting the source's adoption-route shorthand; JJ adoption is available irrespective of religion.
- CARA training, counselling, public awareness, agency recognition/oversight and international coordination support ethical placement; child safety and speed are complementary when administrative bottlenecks are removed.
