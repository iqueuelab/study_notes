# Fentanyl: opioid harms and precursor trafficking

## September 2025 trigger and public-health context
- Embedded news reports US visa revocations/denial of reapplications for Indian business executives and relatives over **alleged fentanyl-precursor trafficking**. Allegations/visa measures do not establish criminal conviction; legitimate chemical trade and diversion into illicit manufacture must be distinguished.
- **Fentanyl is a synthetic opioid** used under medical supervision for severe pain and anaesthesia; potency is commonly described as roughly **50–100 times morphine**, varying by comparison and administration. [CDC fentanyl facts](https://www.cdc.gov/stop-overdose/caring/fentanyl-facts.html). It is not a stimulant. Dependence, hidden adulteration of illicit drugs/counterfeit pills and respiratory depression drive overdose risk.
- Source overdose signs: marked drowsiness, pinpoint pupils, clammy skin, cyanosis, coma and respiratory failure; these demand emergency medical response. Potency makes uncontrolled dosing especially dangerous.
- Source cites **about 107,000 US drug-overdose deaths in 2021**, about three-quarters involving opioids: historical, not 2025 mortality. Death categories can overlap; illicit synthetic-opioid harms rose strongly from 2013.
- Chemical precursors, Mexican cartels, US demand and cross-border financial networks create a transnational problem. Do not equate Canada's border contribution with Mexico's or infer India is the largest supplier. Source **10%/25% tariffs** are earlier 2025 policy snapshots, not a verified September tariff schedule.
- Policy response combines evidence-based prevention/treatment, lawful medical access, precursor tracing, financial investigation and international cooperation through UNODC/INCB/CND. Trade pressure alone does not resolve addiction or healthcare gaps.
- Page 3 repeats the **BPA 2021 PYQ: polycarbonate (b)**, already banked; BPA's epoxy-resin use/endocrine concerns are covered in plasticisers notes. Fentanyl practice statements are undated and excluded from PYQ banks.
