# Sustainable Development Report and SDG progress

## 2025 SDG Index
- **Sustainable Development Report 2025**, **SDSN**, reports **India 99th of 167, score 67.0/100**; Finland leads, then Sweden and Denmark. This independent index is distinct from the official UN statistical SDG report. [2025 report](https://s3.amazonaws.com/sustainabledevelopment.report/2025/sustainable-development-report-2025.pdf).
- Earlier edition ranks cited: **2021:120; 2022:121; 2023:112; 2024:109; 2025:99**. Method/data changes limit comparisons; source's 'improved every year' is contradicted by 2022.
- Source comparators: US44/75.2, China49/74.4, Bhutan74/70.5, Nepal85/68.6, Bangladesh114/63.9, Pakistan140/57; Maldives53 and Sri Lanka93. Aggregate rank does not prove every goal/group improved.
- **17 SDGs adopted 2015, target 2030**; score nearer100 means closer aggregate achievement; **lower rank number is better**. Health, education, water, gender, climate and poverty need goal-level monitoring; high-ranked states also face biodiversity/climate and consumption spillovers.
- Conflict, fiscal space, inequality and institutions constrain progress. Source's **17% of targets on track** is also a UN 2024/early-2025 assessment, not 17% of the 17 goals. [UNDP March2025](https://www.undp.org/blog/bringing-everyone-table).
- Finance services, clean energy and waste management while targeting exclusion; do not optimise only rank.
- **2018 PYQ:** Climate Neutral Now—UNFCCC Secretariat (c), measure/reduce/offset. **2016 PYQ:** 2030 correct; Club of Rome 1972 origin claim false—2 only (b).
