# Rajasthan Cash Plus: maternal nutrition and behavioural support

## Model and implementation
- Combines **cash/DBT + Social and Behaviour Change Communication (SBCC)** through counselling, home visits, groups and digital/family outreach. Cash eases financial constraints; knowledge and services help translate it into nutrition/care.
- Source: pilot from **late2020** in **Udaipur,Banswara,Dungarpur,Pratapgarh,Baran**; statewide expansion **2022**; second-pregnancy support, annual programme outlay roughly **₹210crore**, target **3.5lakh women/year**. Distinguish scheme coverage from the evaluation sample.
- **2021–24 evaluation as reported:** early breastfeeding increased **49%** and reached about **90%**; counselling openness **+44%**, dietary diversity **+49%**, use of cash for nutrition **+54%**; **80%** reported improved nutrition access. These are reported changes, not automatically percentage-point gains or causal proof absent study design.
- Reported **3.3million** beneficiaries lacks clear cumulative/unit reconciliation with annual targeting; hold that total for verification. Incomplete data, remote access, poverty, literacy and monitoring gaps constrain scale; train frontline workers and track health/nutrition and respectful access.
