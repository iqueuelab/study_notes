# Honour crimes: caste, patriarchy and adult choice

## Social mechanism and constitutional harm
- “Honour” killings are murders justified by claimed family/community reputation, often targeting women/couples over inter-caste or inter-faith relationships, partner choice, sexuality or perceived sexual conduct. Caste endogamy, patriarchy, control over women's autonomy and informal community sanctions reinforce each other.
- Caste/khap assemblies have no authority to order violence or override adult consent. Harm includes loss of life/liberty, trauma, exclusion, restricted education/work and weakened confidence in policing; **Articles 14, 19 and 21** supply the equality, freedom and dignity frame.
- Source attributes **25 cases in each of 2019 and 2020, 33 in 2021** to NCRB. Treat these as recorded motive-based cases, not prevalence estimates; underreporting and misclassification limit inference.
## Legal framework: correct the source's numbering
- PDF recites **IPC 299–304/307–308/34–35**, which are legacy provisions; they do not become BNS provisions simply by adding “now BNS”. For post-commencement conduct use the applicable **BNS** provisions; murder punishment is **section 103**. Section **103(2)** addresses murder by **five or more acting in concert** on specified identity grounds, with death/life imprisonment and fine; every honour crime does not automatically meet those elements. [December 2024 official explanation](https://www.pib.gov.in/Pressreleaseshare.aspx?PRID=2080661&lang=2&reg=48).
- Source's **2011 draft unlawful-assembly/matrimonial-interference proposal** and **Law Commission 242nd Report (2012)** are reform history, not an enacted national standalone honour-killing statute. Proposed new disqualification or shorter marriage-notice period is a recommendation, not existing law.
## Courts → implementation
- **Lata Singh (2006)**: protect inter-caste couples; **Krishna Master (2010)** and **Arumugam Servai (2011)** condemn caste-based violence/intimidation. **Shakti Vahini (27 March 2018)** protects consenting adults' choice; directions include preventive action, district special cells/helplines, protection/safe accommodation and prompt investigation/trial. [Supreme Court judgment](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s3ec0490f1f4972d133619a60c30f3559e/documents/aor_notice_circular/46.pdf).
- Combine enforceable protection, accountable policing, witness/survivor support, timely designated trials, legal aid and education challenging caste/patriarchal control. Any marriage-procedure reform must protect safety and adult choice. Source daily mains **250 words/15 marks** is useful practice, without a supplied CSE year.
