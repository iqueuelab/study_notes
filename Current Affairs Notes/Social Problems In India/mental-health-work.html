Social Problems In India

Mental health at work: inclusion of blue-collar workers

Editable notes

Problem and evidence

  • Low pay, insecure work, excessive hours, low control, unsafe conditions and harassment create psychosocial risks; informal/blue-collar workers often lack accessible confidential care.
  • Source 10.6% adult prevalence / 70–92% treatment gap, drawn from earlier national survey/Economic Survey discussion, is not a new February 2025 census of workers; condition-specific gaps vary.
  • Corporate examples Infosys HALE, Wipro Mitra, TCS employee assistance illustrate unequal support, not universal coverage of all white-collar workers.

Labour-law debate

  • OSHWC Code 2020 employer health/safety duties and Code on Social Security 2020 employment injury/occupational disease framework need effective psychosocial-risk implementation. Source's categorical “all mental illness excluded” oversimplifies statutory language, causation and coverage.
  • Work-related injury claims may involve causation evidence and occupational-disease schedules; a contributory-stress court example is not automatic eligibility for every condition.
  • Source March 31, 2025 harmonisation deadline is a reported aim, not proof all four codes had commenced nationwide on that date.

Inclusive policy framework

  • Prevent risks through reasonable workload/hours, predictable employment, anti-harassment measures and worker participation; counselling cannot compensate for persistently harmful conditions.
  • Train supervisors, offer confidential affordable care and referral/return-to-work support; link Tele-MANAS, primary care and rehabilitation.
  • Employer–worker–mental-health specialist cooperation; evaluate explicit occupational-disease protection, enforcement, accessible reporting and non-discrimination. These reform proposals are not automatically enacted duties.
  • WHO workplace guideline supports organisational measures, manager/worker training, interventions and employment/return-to-work support; SDGs 3 and 8.
  • Source 15-mark question is coaching practice, no verified PYQ year; use mains structure risk/exclusion → legal gaps → prevention/access/accountability → equitable decent work.
