# Banjaras: trading heritage and community status

## Maharashtra ST-status demand
- September clipping reports Banjara mobilisation for Scheduled Tribe recognition in Maharashtra; a demand/rally is not a completed legal change. Community classification is State-specific; Maharashtra's Vimukta Jati/Nomadic Tribe categories are distinct from ST status elsewhere.
- Historically mobile **traders and transporters**, carrying grain/salt and supplies in **tandas**, led by a **Nayaka**. Alauddin Khalji's market supplies and Mughal army logistics illustrate their role; Jahangir's account mentions grain carried on bullocks.
- **Gor Boli/Lambadi** is associated with Indo-Aryan speech traditions; **Sant Sevalal Maharaj**, Teej and diverse regional traditions are cultural recall points. The source's single Afghanistan-origin story, universal “no script” and Chari dance assignment should not erase diverse origins/writing practices or be treated as exclusive identifiers.
- **2016 PYQ answer: (d) traders**. Banjara identity today spans varied livelihoods; a medieval occupational answer does not define every present community member.
