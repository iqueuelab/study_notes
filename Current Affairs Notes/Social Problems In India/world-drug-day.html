Editable notes

World Drug Day 2025

  • Observed 26 June, established by UNGA in December 1987; 2025 theme: “Break the cycle. #StopOrganizedCrime”. DoSJE held an observance at Dr Ambedkar International Centre, New Delhi.
  • UNODC publishes the World Drug Report. Its 2025 edition estimates 316 million people used drugs in 2023, excluding alcohol/tobacco; only 1 in 12 people with drug-use disorders received treatment. These are 2023 estimates published in 2025, correcting the issue's 1-in-8 figure. UNODC release.
  • Synthetic opioids and stimulants require separate terminology: fentanyl is an opioid; methamphetamine is a stimulant. Prevention, evidence-based treatment, social support, financial investigation and international cooperation address both health harms and organised crime.
  • Fentanyl practice-question revision: synthetic opioid analgesic, much more potent than morphine; the claim that India is the largest supplier to the US is unsupported. The question has no explicit exam/year, so is excluded from PYQ banks.
  • BPA PYQ 2021: answer polycarbonate; BPA also occurs in epoxy-resin manufacture and has endocrine-disrupting potential. Do not generalise that every plastic contains BPA.
