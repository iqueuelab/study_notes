# Women’s freedom in public spaces

## Inclusion beyond necessary travel

- Mobility supports work, health, politics and leisure; harassment, fear, patriarchal restrictions and victim blaming reduce autonomy.
- **Why Loiter?2011**, Phadke/Ranade/Khan: women need freedom to occupy public spaces without proving a compulsory purpose.
- Source NFHS4 market 54%/health 50%/outsidecommunity 48%unaccompanied mobility are dated survey measures, not universal travel rules.
- Source Women, Peaceand Security Index 2023 rank 128/177 is a dated composite; it is not solely a street-crime ranking.

## Action

- Lighting, accessible toilets, benches, parks, reliable transport and safe last-mile routes; women participate in design/audits.
- Enforce harassment laws, provide responsive complaint systems and accountability; CCTV/segregated transport alone cannot secure equality.
- Nirbhaya Fund/Safe City initiatives need utilisation/results assessment; surveillance must respect privacy.
- Source PLFS2023–24 female LFPR35.6%does not match the usual-status 15+annual 44.5%; cite age/status/year together. Employment, mobility and unpaid-care barriers interact.
