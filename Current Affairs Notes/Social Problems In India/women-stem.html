Social Problems In India

Women in STEM: recognition, retention and leadership

Editable notes

Concepts

  • Matilda effect: women's scientific contributions overlooked/credited to men.
  • Leaky pipeline: attrition across career stages; chutes/ladders: structural barriers and supports that shape progression/re-entry.
  • Biased hiring/promotion/grants, harassment, unequal care, poor mentoring and conference exclusion restrict advancement; do not attribute structural inequality merely to low confidence.
  • Source 17%women faculty across 98 institutions is a study sample, not every Indian STEMinstitution.

Supports

  • Transparent recruitment/funding, pay equity, harassment redress, childcare, parental flexibility and fair career-break/re-entry criteria; mentoring and leadership representation.
  • DSTGATI2020, WISE-KIRAN/Women Scientists programmes, Bio CARe support institutional change/research re-entry; ICMR initiatives have their own eligibility.
  • Bias Watch India tracks inequities; measure retention, decision-making and credit, not only admission counts. Diversity improves opportunities without guaranteeing every team identical outcomes.
