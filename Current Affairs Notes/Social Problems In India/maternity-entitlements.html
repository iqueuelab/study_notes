Social Problems In India

Maternity benefits and delivery gaps

Editable notes

Law and schemes

  • NFSA2013 section 4: maternity benefit of not less than₹6, 000, subject to specified exclusions; statutory entitlement and actual scheme design must be distinguished.
  • PMMVY: first living child₹5, 000 in two instalments; under PMMVY2.0, second child if a girl₹6, 000 in one instalment. Eligible JSY payments can supplement support;₹6, 000 is not automatically paid to every first-child beneficiary. WCD.
  • Maternity Benefit Act's 26-week provision applies where the woman has fewer than two surviving children; otherwise 12 weeks. It does not describe every worker's access.

Implementation

  • Source expenditure₹2, 700 crore 2018–19→₹870 crore 2023–24 and coverage 36%→9% are dated reported estimates, not universal measures of all maternity support.
  • Tamil Nadu₹18, 000/Odisha₹10, 000 are source-era scheme amounts with eligibility conditions; compare coverage using matching denominators.
  • Aadhaar/bank linkage, documentation and software failures can exclude eligible women. Simplify access, publish payments/denials, provide grievance redress and assess inflation-adjusted adequacy.

21 March 2026: adoption and shared parental care

  • Hamsaanandini Nanduri v Union of India, 17 March 2026 struck down the adopted child's three-month age cutoff in section 60(4), Code on Social Security, 2020. Adoptive mothers receive 12 weeks from handover irrespective of the child's age; commissioning mothers remain covered. This is not an award of 26 weeks to every adoptive mother.
  • The judgment ties the exclusion to Articles 14 and 21, reproductive autonomy, dignity and children's care/integration. Adoption procedures often exceed the very age limit that previously denied benefits. It urged the Union to recognise paternity leave as social security; that observation does not itself enact universal private-sector paternity leave. Judgment text.
  • Existing Central government rules provide 15 days paternity leave subject to eligibility; all Indian workers do not automatically receive this entitlement. Wider parental leave could redistribute care, support bonding and women's employment; uptake also needs workplace protection and changes in gender norms.
  • Time Use Survey 2024: among persons age 6+, unpaid caregiving participation 34% females/17.9% males; participating females average 137 minutes/day, males 75. The source's 41% females/21.4% males concerns ages 15–59 (participants' care time about 140/74 minutes), not the age-6+ headline. MoSPI factsheet, age-15–59 release.
  • The accompanying 250-word parental-leave prompt is authored practice, not a year-attributed CSE Mains PYQ.
