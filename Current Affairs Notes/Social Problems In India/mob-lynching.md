# Mob lynching: law and prevention

## Legal recall
- **BNS103(2):** murder by a group of five or more acting in concert on specified identity-related grounds attracts death or life imprisonment and fine; source's blanket “no provision” claim is outdated forMarch 2025.
- **Tehseen S.Poonawalla 2018**, not 2017: Supreme Court preventive, remedial and punitive directions, including nodal officers, prompt response/investigation, victim relief and accountability.
- Articles 14/15/21 support equality and protection of life; rumours, hate mobilisation and impunity do not justify violence.
- State anti-lynching Bills require assent/commencement checks; passage alone does not make every proposal operative law.
## Prevention
- Local intelligence, prompt action against incitement, accurate public communication, impartial policing and timely prosecution; victim/witness protection and compensation.
- Separate evidence of individual/group acts from stereotypes about whole religious communities. Accountability must cover official failure as well as perpetrators.
