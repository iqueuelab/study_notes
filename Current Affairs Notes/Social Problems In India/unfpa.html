UNFPA and reproductive agency

Editable notes

Institution and reproductive rights

  • UNFPA: UN sexual/reproductive-health agency, New York headquarters; trust fund established 1967, operations began 1969, renamed United Nations Population Fund in 1987 while retaining its acronym. Source's 1968 establishment date is incorrect. UNFPA institutional history.
  • Supports voluntary family planning, safe motherhood, reproductive health, gender equality and population research/census capacity, working with governments and country priorities. Its role is wider than demographic control; distinguish national primary statistical collection from UNFPA's analytical/technical support.
  • Three transformative goals for 2030: zero preventable maternal deaths, zero unmet family-planning need, zero gender-based violence/harmful practices.

2025 population estimates

  • Embedded June clipping: India's population 1,463.9 million (146.39 crore) and TFR 1.9 births per woman. These are UN estimates, not a newly completed census; compare with SRS 2021 TFR 2.0 using explicit source/reference year. UNFPA India 2025 data.
  • Below-replacement fertility does not immediately stop population growth: age structure and population momentum matter. The clipping's peak near 170 crore in roughly four decades is a projection, not an observed outcome.
  • 2024 PYQ: TFR is the average number of live births a woman would have by the end of childbearing under current age-specific fertility rates → (d); distinguish crude birth rate per 1,000 population.
