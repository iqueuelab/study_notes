# Sickle cell disease and disability justice

## September 2025: certification and substantive inclusion
- Editorial critiques **March 2024 disability-assessment guidelines** for SCD, SCD with beta-thalassaemia and SCD with Hb D. Recurrent painful crises, anaemia, fatigue, organ injury and interrupted school/work may be serious even when impairment fluctuates or is not visible; rural/tribal access and stigma compound disadvantage.
- **RPwD Act 2016** recognises SCD among specified blood disorders; it was not first recognised only in 2024. Benchmark disability ordinarily requires **at least 40%**, certified under the Act; recognition, assessment percentage and a particular reservation entitlement are separate.
- Crucial correction: **section 34 provides at least 4% government-employment reservation in its listed categories**, which do not include blood disorders as a separate category; **section 32 provides at least 5% seats in government/government-aided higher education for persons with benchmark disabilities**. The source's combined “4% jobs/education” assertion is wrong. [Act](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/15307/1/disabilitya2016-49.pdf).
- Reform arguments: accessible local certification, consistent assessment reflecting episodic effects, reasonable accommodation, social support and consideration of employment inclusion. These are proposals; the article does not itself amend reservation law.
