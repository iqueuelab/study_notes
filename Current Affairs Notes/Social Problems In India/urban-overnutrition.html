Social Problems In India

Urban overnutrition and prevention of metabolic disease

Editable notes

Study evidence and indicator corrections

  • Scientific Reports,24 March 2025: cross-sectional sample of 345 Hyderabad IT employees, recruited voluntarily, assessed during July 2023–July 2024; 84.06% liver fat/MAFLD,70.72% obese under the study's Asian BMI criteria. Associations with sedentary work, diet/sleep and metabolic risk support workplace prevention, but not a representative estimate for all IT workers or India, nor proof one factor caused disease. MAFLD is the paper's terminology; common MASLD note explains the current nomenclature. Original study.
  • Undernutrition and overnutrition can coexist in any income/location group; source's rural poor/urban affluent contrast is illustrative, not exhaustive. Food environments, activity, sleep, stress and access matter alongside individual behaviour.
  • Source NFHS figures ~7% younger men→~32% men 40–49 concern overweight or obesity, not necessarily obesity alone. 46.1% urban men/43.1% urban women are cited for high waist-to-hip ratio, not BMI-defined urban obesity; do not conflate measures. Wealthiest~37% vs poorest~10% is source-reported overweight/obesity gradient requiring age/sex cohort.
  • Source Tamil Nadu STEPS 2023–24: 94.2% low fruit/vegetable intake,31.6% overweight,14.2% obese, and 16% hypertensive patients on care controlled needs precise denominator. These State/cohort indicators are not national rates. WHO 74% global deaths in 2019 from NCDs is dated; premature mortality threatens SDG 3 and working-age productivity.

Integrated prevention

  • Workplace routines: activity breaks, healthy food choices, sufficient rest, appropriate screening/referral; urban design: walkability, parks and active commuting. Improve labelling/marketing safeguards, nutrition education, and surveillance without stigma.
  • Tamil Nadu Makkalai Thedi Maruthuvam doorstep chronic-disease services; clipping reports 3.79 lakh workers screened 2024. Health walk/Eat Right activities are associated initiatives, not proof all screened patients controlled disease.
  • FSSAI Eat Right India/Aaj Se Thoda Kam promote safer healthier intake. Front-of-pack health star rating is a proposed/debated regulatory approach; do not present a nationwide notified mandatory scheme as implemented. Existing labelling/advertising rules mean “unrestricted marketing” is too absolute.
  • Saudi comparator includes source-reported 100% energy drink/50% sweetened drink excise, calorie disclosure, sodium limits and trans-fat measures. Assess fiscal/regulatory mix and implementation, not automatic transfer of all Saudi outcomes. Saudi tax framework.
  • Nutrition tax, mandatory workplace checks, urban surveillance and stronger HFSS labelling are editorial recommendations. Source's 450 million overweight/obese adults in 2050 is a projection, not current count; 244% rise/121% future childhood growth need matched periods/cohorts. Claimed WHO/PHFI quotation attributions are unverified; do not use as authenticated quotes.
