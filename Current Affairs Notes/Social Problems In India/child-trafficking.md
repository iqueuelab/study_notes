# Child trafficking: victim testimony, prevention and rehabilitation

## December 2025: victim-centred adjudication
- **K. P. Kirankumar @ Kiran v State by Peenya Police, 19 December 2025, 2025 INSC 1473**: trafficked minor is an **injured witness, not an accomplice**. Credible, convincing testimony may sustain conviction; minor inconsistencies should be assessed with sensitivity to trauma, vulnerability and the layered exploitation network. This does not require uncritical acceptance of every statement. [Supreme Court judgment](https://api.sci.gov.in/supremecourt/2025/24596/24596_2025_1_1501_67131_Judgement_19-Dec-2025.pdf).
- Age determination and evidence rules, fair process and protection against secondary victimisation matter. Recruitment → transport/transfer → harbouring/receipt → exploitation may involve different actors; victims need not personally know every link. Poverty, caste exclusion, migration, isolation and deceptive job/education promises increase vulnerability.
- **Article 23** prohibits trafficking/begar/forced labour; **Article 21** protects dignity/bodily integrity. **IPC 370** was succeeded by **BNS section 143**; exploitation of trafficked persons is section 144. Do not invent explicit BNS categories solely from the PDF's generic “online/forced marriage” list. Palermo's child definition requires an act for exploitative purpose without proving coercive/deceptive means; “exploitation alone” is an oversimplification.
- Complementary frameworks: **ITPA 1956, POCSO 2012, JJ Act 2015, Bonded Labour System (Abolition) Act 1976**, child-marriage and organ-transplant laws. **AHTUs/MHA coordination, Mission Vatsalya child protection**, shelter, legal aid, education, counselling and livelihood support reduce re-trafficking. Mission Vatsalya is distinct from **Mission Shakti**; the source's portal-merger assertion is unverified.
- Source **NCRB 2023: 177,335 crimes against children, +9.2%**, high absolute counts in MP/Maharashtra/UP and rates Assam **84.2**, Delhi **140.3 per lakh children** are reported category-specific figures, not trafficking-only incidence. Its **2,183 trafficking cases /~2,878 child victims (2022)** have different units/years; **45% masked as kidnapping**, **68% post-COVID Delhi rise**, rescue/intervention totals and **270 AHTU districts** lack clear contemporary denominators and are held as source claims, not established national estimates.
- **US TIP Report 2024 Tier 2** means significant efforts with incomplete minimum-standard compliance, not a ranking among the world's worst offenders. SDGs **5.2, 8.7, 16.2**, UNTOC/Palermo, regional repatriation cooperation and NGOs (Bachpan Bachao Andolan, Prajwala, Vimukthi) support prevention, victim services and prosecution; balance data-sharing with survivor confidentiality.
