# Manual scavenging and sanitation-worker dignity

## Definition and safeguards
- Manual scavenging concerns manual handling of **undecomposed human excreta** from insanitary latrines, associated drains/pits and railway tracks under the statutory definition. **Hazardous cleaning of sewers/septic tanks** is separately prohibited; not every mechanised/protected sanitation task is legally manual scavenging.
- **1993 Act** prohibited employment of manual scavengers and construction of dry latrines; **Prohibition of Employment as Manual Scavengers and their Rehabilitation Act, 2013** widened the framework, prohibited insanitary latrines/hazardous cleaning and required identification and rehabilitation. The source's separate “Building and Maintenance of Insanitary Latrines Act, 2013” is not a distinct enacted law. The cited **2020 amendment initiative was a proposal**, not evidence of an operative amendment. [India Code](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/2119/1/201325.pdf).
- Constitution: **Article 17** abolishes untouchability; **21** protects life/dignity; **23** prohibits forced labour; **46** directs protection of weaker sections, especially SCs/STs, from injustice/exploitation. The SC/ST (Prevention of Atrocities) Act adds protection against caste-based coercion.
- **Safai Karamchari Andolan (2014)** directed ₹10 lakh compensation for sewer deaths; **Balram Singh (20 October 2023)** raised death compensation to **₹30 lakh**, with ₹20 lakh for permanent disability and at least ₹10 lakh for other disability. The issue's ₹10 lakh figure is historical, not the latest death-compensation standard. [Supreme Court judgment](https://api.sci.gov.in/supremecourt/2020/4072/4072_2020_8_1502_47917_Judgement_20-Oct-2023.pdf).
## April 2025 discussion
- Source cites a government statement of no manual-scavenging deaths during **2019–22** alongside an Andhra Pradesh High Court demand for sewer-death information since 1993. **Zero recorded deaths in one classification does not mean zero sewer/septic-tank fatalities**, eradication or complete reporting.
- Persistence: hereditary caste expectations/stigma; poverty; unsafe contractor outsourcing and weak municipal accountability; costly/uneven mechanisation; incomplete surveys; delayed rehabilitation and few alternative livelihoods.
- Consequences: indignity, exclusion and intergenerational deprivation; infections, respiratory/skin disease, injury and fatal exposure; lost incomes/productivity and health costs.
## Action chain
- **NAMASTE**, Safaimitra Suraksha Challenge and Swachhta Abhiyan app support safer mechanised sanitation/identification; **Rashtriya Garima Abhiyan** is a civil-society dignity campaign.
- Eliminate unsafe entry through affordable machinery and maintenance; enforce duties on local bodies and contractors; independent audits/accurate fatality registers and prompt compensation; health protection and emergency response; rehabilitation combining skills, housing, education and financial inclusion; anti-caste awareness and worker participation. Local governments need funds and capacity alongside accountability.
- Mains focus: connect dignity/caste discrimination with implementation, public procurement, occupational safety and rehabilitation; machines alone do not remove social exclusion.

## PYQ recall
- **2016 Prelims:** Rashtriya Garima Abhiyaan aims to eradicate manual scavenging and rehabilitate manual scavengers (**option c**); dignity, community mobilisation and alternative livelihoods are central.

## NAMASTE: waste-picker distinction
- NAMASTE expanded to **waste pickers in June 2024**; the **Waste Picker Enumeration App launched 5 June 2025**. Its **over 80,000 enumerated/validated as of 29 May 2025** were **sewer/septic-tank workers**, not 80,000 waste pickers. Support includes safety equipment/training, health coverage and livelihood finance. [Ministry release](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleaseIframePage.aspx?PRID=2134303&lang=2&reg=48).
- Distinguish the scheme's original sanitation remit, later waste-picker expansion and app launch; the PDF's generic “launched in 2024” mixes these stages.

## 4 February 2026: waste-picker enumeration and caste-linked work
- Parliament data reported **about 1.52 lakh waste pickers profiled/validated across urban areas of 35 States/UTs**, an **ongoing enumeration**, not a census of every Indian waste picker. The issue reports **SC 60.3%, OBC 13.7%, ST 10.5% (84.5% combined)**; **10.7% General**, with a residual category. Women constituted **48.7%**, men **51.3%**, and transgender persons **0.007%** (rounding applies).
- Delhi (**4,289 of over 6,500**) and Goa (**729 of 1,286**) had General-category majorities in this enumerated group; a national concentration does not eliminate regional variation or prove every person's occupation is inherited.
- Waste picking recovers reusable/recyclable solid materials for sale. **Waste pickers, manual scavengers and sewer/septic-tank workers are distinct categories**; do not transfer their demographic shares or mortality denominators to each other. The report's **859 deaths since 2014** concerns hazardous sewer/septic-tank cleaning, not waste-picker fatalities.
- NAMASTE supports formal recognition, protective equipment/training, health coverage, safer work and livelihood finance. ULB integration, worker collectives, contractor accountability and alternative skills should accompany mechanisation and caste-discrimination safeguards. Its waste-picker expansion/app chronology is already covered. [Ministry background on the waste-picker component](https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2025/sep/doc2025925646901.pdf).
- Repeated legal material retains the statutory distinction between manual scavenging and hazardous cleaning. The **2013 Act** is the operative law; the source's separate ‘Building and Maintenance of Insanitary Latrines Act, 2013’ is not a distinct Act, and the **2020 initiative is not an enacted amendment**. Existing notes retain the **2023 ₹30-lakh sewer-death compensation direction**, correcting the repeated historical ₹10-lakh-only shortcut.
- **2016 Garima Abhiyaan PYQ: c**, already in the subject bank; retain its eradication-and-rehabilitation explanation without duplicating the question.
