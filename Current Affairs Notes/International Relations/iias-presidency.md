# IIAS presidency and administrative-science cooperation

## June 2025 election
- **V. (Voruganti) Srinivas**, DARPG Secretary, elected **IIAS President for 2025–28**; India nominated him in November 2024. Final vote on **3 June 2025** against Austria after candidates from South Africa/Bahrain were also considered. Election and assumption of the term are distinct milestones. [IIAS announcement](https://iias-iisa.org/news/shri-srinivas-voruganti-india-elected-president-of-the-iias-20252028/).
- **International Institute of Administrative Sciences** is a global federation for public-administration research/collaboration; not a formal UN body, though it cooperates with UN initiatives. Source membership **31 countries, 20 national sections, 15 academic centres** is an election-era snapshot, not fixed constitutional composition.
- India member since **1998**, represented through **DARPG**. Source’s assertion that the election occurred at **Bharat Mandapam** needs event-specific corroboration; presidency election should not be confused with a conference hosted there.
- Potential: comparative governance research, public-grievance systems, digital administration and professional networks. Election does not give India binding authority to prescribe other countries’ public policy or prove every domestic reform already succeeded.
