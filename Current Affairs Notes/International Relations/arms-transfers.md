# SIPRI arms transfers2020–24

## Report and metric
- SIPRI, founded 1966, Stockholm: independent institute researching conflict, armaments, disarmament; **not a UNagency**.
- March 2025 report covers **2020–24 versus 2015–19**; trend-indicator value(TIV) measures major-arms transfer volume, not market price or every defence import. [SIPRI](https://www.sipri.org/publications/2025/sipri-fact-sheets/trends-international-arms-transfers-2024).
- India second-largest importer after Ukraine, 8.3%global share; imports down 9.3%. Russia 36%of Indiaimports versus 55%priorperiod/72%2010–14; India took 28%of French exports.
- US43%global exports; France second 9.6%; Russia exports down 64%. Pakistan imports up 61%, China 81%ofits imports. Period-specific figures do not describe every later year.
