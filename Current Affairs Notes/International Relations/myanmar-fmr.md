# India–Myanmar border and free movement arrangements

## Geography and original arrangement
- **1,643 km** border: **Arunachal Pradesh, Nagaland, Manipur, Mizoram**; Myanmar's adjoining administrative areas include **Kachin, Sagaing, Chin**. **Shan does not directly border India**.
- Traditional cross-border ethnic/family networks predate modern borders. Original FMR permitted eligible residents within **16 km**, with specified border-pass/stay conditions; not unrestricted entry for every person.
## Changing policy: distinguish dates
- **8 February 2024:** MHA announced decision to scrap FMR and recommended immediate suspension pending the process. [Official announcement](https://www.pib.gov.in/Pressreleaseshare.aspx?PRID=2003884&lang=2&reg=48).
- A **December 2024 revised border-pass arrangement** was reported with **10 km eligibility /seven-day visits**; therefore the PDF's unqualified 16 km description is historical. [Contemporaneous district-implementation report](https://www.newindianexpress.com/amp/story/nation/2025/Jan/05/mizoram-myanmar-border-movement-regulated-following-union-govts-directives-official).
- Suspension announcements, fencing plans and locally implemented permits are distinct stages; source 50 km/six-month descriptions should not override the specified rules.
## Security–community balance
- Concerns: insurgent movements, narcotics/arms trafficking, identity verification and spillover from Myanmar conflict. Refugees, ordinary ethnic visitors and criminals are not interchangeable categories.
- Restrictions affect kinship, medical access, local trade and livelihoods; consultation with border States and communities is essential.
- Improve lawful monitored crossings, verifiable documentation, intelligence cooperation, humanitarian safeguards and livelihood alternatives; fencing alone cannot address every security driver.
- **Act East /Kaladan /India–Myanmar–Thailand highway** connect regional strategy to border management; a proposed corridor is not automatically a functioning infrastructure network.
## Border management update
- The approximately 1,643 km India–Myanmar border touches Arunachal Pradesh, Nagaland, Manipur and Mizoram—not Tripura.
- Fencing and road announcements are plans; expenditure, sanctioned length and completed infrastructure are different measures.
- Earlier FMR arrangements allowed specified movement within 16 km; late-2024 revised pass arrangements used 10 km. Withdrawal announcements and implementation differed across locations; do not describe an unconditional permanent visa-free entitlement.
- Terrain, forests, drainage and cross-border Naga/Kuki-Zo ties complicate fencing. Combine lawful controls with community consultation, livelihood access and humanitarian safeguards; displacement does not itself establish criminality.

## 20 March 2026: fencing and alleged cross-border training
- The clipping reports arrest of **six Ukrainians and one US national**, alleged to have crossed via Mizoram to train Myanmar armed groups in weapons/drone operations. Arrest and allegations are not a judicial finding of guilt.
- Source cites a Home Affairs parliamentary-committee return: **1,643 km fencing approved; 390.39 km sanctioned; 43.75 km completed; 346.64 km under construction**. Sanctioned length equals the last two figures; nationwide approval does not mean completed fencing.
- Existing notes cover the four bordering States, original **16 km** FMR, **February 2024 withdrawal announcement** and reported later **10 km/seven-day pass** arrangements. The source alternates 10 and 16 km; it should not be read as unconditional visa-free travel. Biometric gates, verified crossings and counter-drone surveillance must balance security with kinship, livelihoods and humanitarian needs.
- Do not memorise its practice claim that today's entire boundary was demarcated in **1826**: the Treaty of Yandabo is historical background, while the modern bilateral boundary agreement was signed **10 March 1967**, with subsequent joint demarcation. [Parliamentary statement](https://rsdebate.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/517056/1/PQ_60_13061967_U799_p3602_p360.pdf).
