# India–Myanmar border and free movement arrangements

## Geography and original arrangement
- **1,643 km** border: **Arunachal Pradesh, Nagaland, Manipur, Mizoram**; Myanmar's adjoining administrative areas include **Kachin, Sagaing, Chin**. **Shan does not directly border India**.
- Traditional cross-border ethnic/family networks predate modern borders. Original FMR permitted eligible residents within **16 km**, with specified border-pass/stay conditions; not unrestricted entry for every person.
## Changing policy: distinguish dates
- **8 February 2024:** MHA announced decision to scrap FMR and recommended immediate suspension pending the process. [Official announcement](https://www.pib.gov.in/Pressreleaseshare.aspx?PRID=2003884&lang=2&reg=48).
- A **December 2024 revised border-pass arrangement** was reported with **10 km eligibility /seven-day visits**; therefore the PDF's unqualified 16 km description is historical. [Contemporaneous district-implementation report](https://www.newindianexpress.com/amp/story/nation/2025/Jan/05/mizoram-myanmar-border-movement-regulated-following-union-govts-directives-official).
- Suspension announcements, fencing plans and locally implemented permits are distinct stages; source 50 km/six-month descriptions should not override the specified rules.
## Security–community balance
- Concerns: insurgent movements, narcotics/arms trafficking, identity verification and spillover from Myanmar conflict. Refugees, ordinary ethnic visitors and criminals are not interchangeable categories.
- Restrictions affect kinship, medical access, local trade and livelihoods; consultation with border States and communities is essential.
- Improve lawful monitored crossings, verifiable documentation, intelligence cooperation, humanitarian safeguards and livelihood alternatives; fencing alone cannot address every security driver.
- **Act East /Kaladan /India–Myanmar–Thailand highway** connect regional strategy to border management; a proposed corridor is not automatically a functioning infrastructure network.
