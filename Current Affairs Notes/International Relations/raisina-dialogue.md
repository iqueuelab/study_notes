# Raisina Dialogue: geopolitics and geoeconomics

## 2026 conference
- **11th Raisina Dialogue, 5–7 March 2026, New Delhi**: geopolitics/geoeconomics forum organised by **Observer Research Foundation with the Ministry of External Affairs**, held annually since **2016**. It brings government, business, academia, media and civil-society participants together. **PM Narendra Modi** inaugurated it; Finnish President **Alexander Stubb** was chief guest/keynote speaker. [Organiser](https://raisinadialogue.org/raisina-2026/), [MEA visit announcement](https://www.mea.gov.in/press-releases.htm?dtl/40841/Visit_of_President_of_the_Republic_of_Finland_to_India_March_04__07_2026).
- Theme: **Saṁskāra — Assertion, Accommodation, Advancement**. The issue reports about **2,700 participants from 110 countries**; distinguish the participant estimate from membership in a standing international organisation.
- Six pillars: **Contested Frontiers** (power/polarity/periphery); **Repairing the Commons** (groups/guardians/avenues); **White Whale** (2030 development agenda); **The Eleventh Hour** (climate/conflict/delay); **Tomorrowland** (technology); **Trade in the Times of Tariff** (recovery/resilience/reinvention). The Munich Security Conference and Shangri-La Dialogue are comparable policy forums, rather than governing bodies of Raisina.
