# Tiger Triumph and tri-service disaster response

## April 2025 plan
- Fourth India–US **tri-service HADR** exercise, planned **1–13 April 2025**; harbour phase **Visakhapatnam, 1–7 April**, sea phase **8–13 April**.
- Shared procedures, logistics/medical response and a **Combined Coordination Centre** improve joint-task-force interoperability. At publication it was starting, not completed.
- Distinguish **Yudh Abhyas (Army), Cope India (Air Force), Malabar (naval/multilateral)** from tri-service Tiger Triumph.
- Cooperation rests alongside LEMOA **2016**, COMCASA **2018**, BECA **2020**; access/logistics agreements do not create an automatic treaty alliance or identical policies.
- HADR readiness needs timely communications, host consent, rapid supplies, medical support and local coordination—not military deployment alone.
