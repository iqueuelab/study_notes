# India, Cyprus and Croatia: June 2025 diplomacy

## June 2025 diplomatic itinerary
- Cyprus visit preceded the Canada G7 outreach; Croatia visit was forthcoming in the June16 issue. Cyprus supports India's permanent UNSC-seat aspirations and counter-terrorism concerns; engage it through bilateral investment/trade and EU ties rather than reducing relations to rivalry with Türkiye.
- Cyprus President then **Nikos Christodoulides**; scheduled Council of EU presidency in **first half of 2026**. Source recalls Indira Gandhi's 1982 and Vajpayee's 2002 visits.
- **Croatia:** Adriatic/Balkan state, capital **Zagreb**; borders **Slovenia, Hungary, Serbia, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Montenegro** (last omitted in source). **Dinaric Alps**, karst caves/sinkholes, highest peak **Dinara**; **Sava through Zagreb**, Drava joins Danube.
- Cultural links: Indology at University of Zagreb, Yoga/ISKCON; former Yugoslavia's non-aligned history supplies context but modern Croatia is an EU/NATO member.
- **2023 India–Croatia defence-cooperation MoU** supports further collaboration; do not rename it a completed combined defence-and-technology treaty. Ports/Adriatic access could support Europe connectivity; Croatia's role in **IMEC** was prospective, not a confirmed original corridor endpoint. [MEA May2025 background](https://www.mea.gov.in/Portal/ForeignRelation/India-Croatia-May-2025.pdf).
## Sea-border PYQ recall
- Adriatic—Albania; Caspian—Kazakhstan; Mediterranean—Morocco are correct. **Croatia is not on the Black Sea; Syria is not on the Red Sea**; 2019 answer **1,3,4 (b)**.
- Adriatic littorals: Italy, Slovenia, Croatia, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Montenegro, Albania. Black Sea: Türkiye, Bulgaria, Romania, Ukraine, Russia, Georgia. Caspian: Russia, Kazakhstan, Turkmenistan, Iran, Azerbaijan. Red Sea also has **Djibouti**, omitted in the source's short list.
