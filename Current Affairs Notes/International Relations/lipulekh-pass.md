# Lipulekh Pass: border trade, pilgrimage and territorial dispute

## Map recall and significance
- High Himalayan pass in **Kumaon, Uttarakhand**, connecting India with Tibet near the India–Nepal–China border region. Route for **Kailash Mansarovar Yatra** and historic trans-Himalayan trade; source elevation **~5,334 m** varies across references and should be treated as approximate.
| Pass | Indian State | Recall |
|---|---|---|
| Lipulekh | Uttarakhand | Tibet trade and Kailash pilgrimage |
| Shipki La | Himachal Pradesh | Tibet trade route |
| Nathu La | Sikkim | Reopened for trade in July 2006 after the 1962 closure |
## August 2025 dispute
- Source reports India–China arrangements to **resume border trade through Lipulekh** and Nepal's objection; an agreement/announcement is distinct from proof trade had fully restarted on this issue date. Pilgrimage resumption and commercial trade are also different activities.
- **Nepal's 2020 constitutional map** claims **Limpiyadhura, Kalapani and Lipulekh**, citing its interpretation of the **1816 Sugauli Treaty** and Kali River source. **India rejects those claims**, asserting the area is in Uttarakhand. Attribute the competing positions rather than label the boundary dispute settled by a bilateral India–China trade arrangement.
- Source dates **1992 Lipulekh, 1994 Shipki La and 2006 Nathu La** concern modern trade arrangements; 1992 is not proof Lipulekh had no earlier trade history. **2007 PYQ: Nathu La (c)**. Lipulekh practice: both statements true if 'trijunction' is read as the broader nearby region, not a precisely agreed tripoint.
