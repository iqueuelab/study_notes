# Eurasian Economic Union and India FTA negotiations

## Union and institutions
- **Treaty signed 29 May 2014; effective 1 January 2015**. Members: **Russia, Belarus, Kazakhstan, Armenia and Kyrgyzstan**; economic integration aims at freer movement of goods, services, capital and labour. [EEC commencement record](https://eec.eaeunion.org/en/news/01-01-2015-1/).
- **Supreme Eurasian Economic Council:** heads of State; **Eurasian Economic Commission:** permanent regulatory institution in Moscow; **EAEU Court:** Minsk. It has no common currency; do not assume EU institutions and enforcement powers apply unchanged.
## September 2025 negotiation plans
- Embedded clipping reports the first India–EAEU FTA round expected in **early November 2025** following the **15 September** Goyal–Slepnev meeting. This is a proposed negotiating schedule, not a signed FTA or implemented tariff cut.
- Terms of reference signed **20 August 2025** provide an **18-month work plan**; subsequent Commerce Ministry reporting confirms this milestone. [Official negotiation background](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2190478&lang=1&reg=3).
- India seeks export diversification, balanced trade and resilient supply chains; energy trade and INSTC connectivity are relevant. Assess logistics, sanctions exposure, rules of origin and sector-specific market access rather than assuming a treaty guarantees trade gains.
