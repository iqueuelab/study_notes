# G7: membership, institutions and summit diplomacy

## Composition and operation
- **US, Canada, UK, France, Germany, Italy, Japan**; EU participates alongside them. Informal forum: no founding treaty or permanent secretariat; annual rotating presidency, consensus-based political coordination, and no supranational lawmaking power.
- First **G6 summit in France, 1975**, amid the oil/financial crises; Canada joined **1976**. Russia's progressive inclusion produced the G8, whose first summit under that name was **Birmingham 1998**; Russia's participation was suspended **2014** after Crimea's annexation.
- The EU first participated in 1977 and has participated in all working sessions since **Ottawa 1981**. It is a regular participant, not merely an occasional guest like invited outreach countries.
- Agenda: economic stability, trade, security, health, climate and development. Economic influence is substantial but source percentages for global wealth/GDP lack a common reference year and should not be memorised as timeless shares.
- The printed “UPSC 2023” G7 exercise has a **disputed attribution and faulty key**: its 1998 G8 statement should not be dismissed just because Russia joined political discussions in 1997. Keep for review rather than label it a verified UPSC PYQ. [Canadian G7 material](https://g7.canada.ca/assets/ea689367/Attachments/student-teacher/2025-mock-g7-summit-teacher-workbook.pdf).
- **2020 G20 PYQ: Argentina, Mexico, South Africa, Türkiye (a)**. Malaysia, New Zealand, Iran, Vietnam and Singapore were not G20 country members in that question.
