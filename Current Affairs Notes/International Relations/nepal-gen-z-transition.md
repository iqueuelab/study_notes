# Nepal: youth protests and an interim government

## September 2025 political transition
- Former Chief Justice **Sushila Karki**, aged 73, became **Nepal's first woman Prime Minister**, heading an interim government after youth-led anti-corruption protests and K. P. Sharma Oli's resignation. She had also been its first woman Chief Justice. India welcomed the interim government on **12 September 2025**.
- President **Ram Chandra Poudel** dissolved the House of Representatives, with elections announced for **5 March 2026**; this was a future schedule at issue date. The source's general word Parliament should not imply abolition of Nepal's permanent upper chamber.
- Source reports at least **19 protesters killed on 8 September**, followed by arson and attacks on public buildings. This is an event-specific early figure, not the final death toll for the entire unrest.
- Immediate tasks: restore order, form an administration, investigate unlawful killings/arson, protect rights and conduct credible elections. Army mediation and emergency stabilisation raise civil–military and constitutional-accountability questions; predictions that courts would necessarily uphold the transition are speculation.
- India–Nepal relevance: democratic stability, open-border movement, development cooperation and citizen safety; youth frustration with corruption illustrates the need for accountable institutions beyond a change of leader.
