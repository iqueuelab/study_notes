Thailand–Cambodia border temples: Ta Muen Thom and Preah Vihear

Editable notes

July 2025 border conflict and Ta Muen group

  • Source describes artillery, drones and Thai airstrikes amid Thailand–Cambodia border clashes. Dangrek/Dongrak Mountains separate northern Cambodia from northeastern Thailand; colonial demarcation, contested boundaries and nationalism complicate shared Khmer heritage.
  • Ta Muen Thom, a Shiva temple of Angkorian/Khmer tradition, lies at the contested border by Surin, Thailand, on the ancient Angkor–Phimai highway. South-facing sandstone sanctuary, natural-rock linga and drainage channel; surrounding laterite structures and pool.
  • Distinguish adjacent structures: Ta Muen, dharmasala/traveller shelter; Ta Muen Tot, hospital shrine associated with Khmer royal public-health infrastructure. Do not assign all three the same builder/date: source inconsistently attributes the main complex to the 11 th/12 th centuries and later Jayavarman VII additions.
  • Ta Muen Thom is not a UNESCO World Heritage property and has no equivalent ICJ title judgment. Its territorial dispute must not be resolved merely by labelling it an undisputed temple in one country.

Preah Vihear and the ICJ distinction

  • Preah Vihear, Shiva temple on a Dangrek cliff in northern Cambodia, developed chiefly in the 11 th–12 th centuries, associated with Suryavarman I/II; successive sanctuaries, stairways and gopuras along an ~800 m ascending axis. UNESCO inscription 2008.
  • 1904/1907 Franco-Siamese demarcation forms the colonial background; Thai occupation after French withdrawal in 1954 preceded ICJ 1962 judgment establishing Cambodian sovereignty over the temple. Clashes recurred in 2011.
  • 2013 interpretation: Cambodian sovereignty over the Preah Vihear promontory and Thai withdrawal from it; not an award of the entire contested 4.6 km² surrounding region. The Court did not settle Ta Muen Thom or the full border. ICJ case 151.
  • Shared heritage can support cultural cooperation, but legal scope, bilateral border demarcation, civilian protection and de-escalation matter alongside national symbolism. Source comparison MCQ is practice, not a CSE PYQ.
