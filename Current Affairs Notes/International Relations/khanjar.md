# Exercise Khanjar and India–Kyrgyzstan cooperation

## March 2025 edition
- The 12th India–Kyrgyzstan joint special-forces exercise was scheduled for 10–23 March 2025 in Kyrgyzstan.
- Began in 2011 at Nahan; hosting alternates. Indian Para Special Forces and Kyrgyz Scorpion Brigade train in counter-terrorism, mountain operations and interoperability.
- Defence engagement builds skills and bilateral trust; an exercise is not a mutual-defence treaty.
- Nowruz is a regional spring festival celebrated across several societies, not exclusively a Kyrgyz festival.

## KHANJAR-XIII: February 2026
- **13th India–Kyrgyzstan joint Special Forces exercise**, **4–17 February 2026**, **Missamari/Misamari, Assam**; annual alternating hosts, previous edition in Kyrgyzstan in March 2025.
- Both contingents have **20 personnel**. India's **Parachute Regiment (Special Forces)** trains with Kyrgyzstan's **ILBRIS Special Forces Brigade**, the Defence Ministry's 2026 designation rather than automatically carrying forward the older Scorpion label.
- Counter-terrorism/Special Forces operations in **urban and mountainous terrain**; sniping, complex building intervention and mountain craft. Builds interoperability and bilateral defence ties; a UN-scenario training reference is not a current joint UN operational deployment. [Defence Ministry, 4 February](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2223244).
