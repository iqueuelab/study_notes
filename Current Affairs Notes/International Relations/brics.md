# BRICS: members, partners and cooperation

## January 2025 expansion
- **Nigeria: ninth partner country**, January announcement; partner status is **not full membership**. Other eight: **Belarus, Bolivia, Cuba, Kazakhstan, Malaysia, Thailand, Uganda, Uzbekistan**. [Brazilian presidency](https://brics.br/en/news/nine-nations-announced-as-brics-partner-countries).
- Original **BRIC**: Brazil, Russia, India, China; first leaders' summit **2009**. South Africa invited **2010**, joined summit **2011** → BRICS.
- **Egypt, Ethiopia, Iran, UAE** entered in 2024; **Indonesia** full-member announcement January 2025. Saudi Arabia's invitation/accession was reported inconsistently; source's unquestioned **11-member count** should not erase that distinction. **Argentina**, shown in its graphic, declined the invitation.
- Three pillars: **political/security; economic/financial; cultural/people-to-people**. Informal intergovernmental grouping, not a military alliance.
- Source **~40% population / 37.3% GDP** needs membership/date and **PPP versus nominal** labels; do not mix them as exact universal shares.
- India hosted **4th summit 2012, 8th 2016, 13th 2021**.
## Mains frame
- Global South voice, institutional reform, development finance and trade/payment cooperation; diverse interests and bilateral tensions constrain consensus.
- January newspaper reports **100% tariff threat** if BRICS undermines dollar dominance; a threat is not an imposed blanket tariff or proof a common BRICS currency exists.

## Summit PYQs
- **2014 historical context:** first BRIC summit **Yekaterinburg, Russia, 16 June 2009**, not Rio de Janeiro; South Africa was the last addition among the then five—**2 only (b)**. Later expansion does not change that historical answer.
- **16th summit: Kazan, Russia, 22–24 October 2024**; theme **Strengthening Multilateralism for Just Global Development and Security**, not Multiculturalism. **Indonesia full membership announced January 2025**; 2025 PYQ **I and II (a)**.

## July summit agenda
- Brazil was to host the **17th BRICS summit in Rio de Janeiro, 6–7 July 2025**; India takes the chair in **2026**. Agenda includes global-governance reform, peace/security, climate, health, AI and multilateral cooperation.
- The source's “five-member group with Argentina as a new invitee” is stale/misleading. **Argentina declined the invitation**; BRICS membership and partner status must be distinguished. This tour's Argentina stop does not make Argentina a BRICS member.

## Rio summit and partner update
- **17th BRICS summit,Rio de Janeiro,6–7July2025**: theme **Strengthening Global South Cooperation for a More Inclusive and Sustainable Governance**. Leaders sought fairer global-governance representation, condemned the Pahalgam attack/cross-border terrorism and supported the comprehensive terrorism convention; criticised attacks on Iran's nuclear facilities and called for diplomacy.
- Declaration welcomed **Indonesia** as member and ten partners: **Belarus,Bolivia,Kazakhstan,Cuba,Nigeria,Malaysia,Thailand,Vietnam,Uganda,Uzbekistan**. Vietnam is the addition missing from the January nine-partner list. Partner status differs from full membership. [Rio declaration](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2142786&lang=2&reg=48).
- Source table says11full members but actually prints ten names; Saudi accession/reporting remains a qualification in common notes, not resolved by this inconsistent table. Source49.5%population/~40%GDP/~26%trade requires membership,year and PPP/nominal definitions.
## Historical supplement: India's2021 chair
- **BRIC coined2001**, foreign-ministers' meeting **2006,UNGA NewYork**; earlier summit/expansion dates remain in common notes.
- India's2021 theme: **BRICS@15: Intra BRICS Cooperation for Continuity, Consolidation and Consensus**. Priorities: multilateral reform,counter-terrorism,digital tools forSDGs,people-to-people exchange; leaders' summit **9September2021**, foreign ministers **1June**, security advisers **24August**.
- Source highlights Digital Health Summit, first Water Ministers' meeting, Counter-Terrorism Action Plan, green-tourism cooperation, Agricultural Research Platform and remote-sensing satellite constellation agreement. These are2021cooperation milestones, not newly launched2025projects or a military alliance.

## Fortaleza recall
- **2014 Fortaleza Declaration, sixth BRICS summit**, associated with the New Development Bank and Contingent Reserve Arrangement. Both had **US$100 billion** headline figures, but distinguish NDB's initial authorised capital from its **US$50 billion subscribed capital**, and CRA's separate **US$100 billion commitment pool**. These are not cash contributions of identical size to both institutions. [Founding agreements](https://www.gov.br/mre/pt-br/canais_atendimento/imprensa/notas-a-imprensa/atos-assinados-por-ocasiao-da-vi-cupula-do-brics-fortaleza-15-de-julho-de-2014).
- 2015 PYQ answer: **BRICS (b)**. Development infrastructure lending differs from short-term balance-of-payments liquidity support.
