# Startup20 and G20 entrepreneurship cooperation

## Institution and 2025 agenda
- **Startup 20**, official G 20 engagement group initiated under **India's 2023 presidency**, gives startups/ecosystem actors a policy voice; followed by **Brazil 2024, South Africa 2025**. Recommendations work through consensus and feed G 20 discussions, not binding supranational startup law.
- Source describes the third edition's launch in**Johannesburg**, with five task forces: **Foundation and Alliances; Finance and Investment; Inclusion and Sustainability; Trade and Market Access; Township and Rural Entrepreneurship**. Private-sector/entrepreneur participation seeks practical market access, capital, collaboration and inclusion.
- G 20 began**1999** as Finance Ministers/Central Bank Governors' forum;19 countries plus**EU and African Union(AU joined 2023)**. B 20 business, C 20 civil society, L 20 labour, S 20 science, W 20 women, T 20 think tanks, Y 20 youth are distinct engagement groups.
- PYQ recall: Argentina, Mexico, South Africa, Turkey all G 20 members(2020); finance-minister origins and India's DPI priority both correct(2023). **Common Framework 2020**, endorsed with Paris Club, coordinates debt treatment for eligible low-income countries; debt distress support is not universal debt cancellation.
- South Africa's **22 On Sloane** served as its 2025 Secretariat; the source's generic reference to an Indian Sherpa/secretariat should not imply permanent Indian control. [Startup20 South Africa](https://su20.org/).
