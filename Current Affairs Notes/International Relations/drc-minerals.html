DR Congo: critical minerals and the resource curse

Editable notes

Map and minerals

  • Democratic Republic of Congo (DRC), capital Kinshasa, Central Africa; distinguish Republic of Congo, Brazzaville. Neighbours: Angola, Zambia, Tanzania, Burundi, Rwanda, Uganda, South Sudan, Central African Republic, Republic of Congo; short Atlantic outlet.
MineralMain use / issue
CobaltMany EV/lithium-ion cathodes; aerospace alloys; not every battery chemistry uses cobalt
CopperWiring, grids, motors
Coltan, columbite–tantaliteTantalum/niobium ore; tantalum capacitors
Gold / diamondsJewellery, investment / industrial uses; illicit trade risks
Tin / tungstenSolder / hard alloys and specialist industries
  • Source roughly 70% of world cobalt output; 2023 PYQ says about three-fourths. Production shares are dated, not fixed reserves or a prediction.

Conflict and supply chains

  • January 2025 eastern DRC conflict around Goma, M23 advances; other armed actors include ADF and Mai-Mai. Distinguish eastern conflict-mineral networks from all copper/cobalt mines across DRC.
  • External ownership, smuggling, weak revenue governance and armed-group finance can create a resource curse: wealth fails to deliver welfare. Child labour/unsafe artisanal mining, displacement, forest loss and water/soil pollution require safeguards.
  • China-linked firms have major mining/processing interests; US/EU seek diversified clean-tech supply. Responsible Indian partnerships need due diligence and community benefit.
  • Dodd–Frank section 1502 concerns tin, tantalum, tungsten, gold (3TG) and covered issuers' disclosure/due diligence; not a generic ban on all DRC minerals or automatic inclusion of cobalt.
  • Transparency EITI, traceability, accountable royalties, responsible sourcing, regional/UN cooperation, value addition and economic diversification.

PYQ recall

  • 2023 cobalt: DRC (c); Congo Basin country: Cameroon (a). The latter question's other options are Nigeria/South Sudan/Uganda; answer should not imply Uganda is one of the six commonly listed Congo Basin forest countries.
  • 2022 news-pairs: Guinea, Lebanon and Tunisia correctly matched; Chad–Chinese permanent base wrong → only three pairs (c).
