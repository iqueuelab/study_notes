# IBSA: South–South cooperation and UN Security Council reform

## November 2025: Johannesburg leaders’ meeting
- **IBSA = India, Brazil, South Africa**: three democracies linking Asia, South America and Africa. The leaders’ meeting called for more representative global institutions and united action against terrorism without double standards.
- **UNSC distinction:** Africa and Latin America have no **permanent seats**; they do have elected non-permanent representation. The PDF’s unqualified “no representation” is misleading. The P5 are China, France, Russia, UK and US; veto disputes illustrate the reform challenge.
- Modi proposed institutionalising **NSA meetings** (first held in **2021**), an **IBSA Digital Innovation Alliance** sharing UPI, CoWIN, cybersecurity and women-led technology, and a separate **climate-resilient agriculture fund**. These were proposals, not proof that new institutions/funding were operational. Safe, human-centred AI cooperation could feature at India’s **2026 AI Impact Summit**.
- Existing **IBSA Fund**: PM reported nearly **50 projects in 40 countries**, spanning education, health, women’s empowerment and solar energy. Other cooperation: millets, natural farming, disaster resilience, green energy and traditional medicine. [PM’s statement](https://www.pmindia.gov.in/en/news_updates/pms-statement-at-the-ibsa-leaders-meeting/).
- Correct the presidency sequence: **Indonesia 2022 → India 2023 → Brazil 2024 → South Africa 2025**. These are four Global South presidencies; the three IBSA countries held the last three. Indonesia is not an IBSA member. Reform arguments concern legitimacy, effectiveness and equitable voice; a declaration alone cannot change the UN Charter.
