# India–Seychelles maritime cooperation

## First Training Squadron visit, September 2025
- **INS Tir, INS Shardul and ICGS Sarathi** called at **Port Victoria, Seychelles**, during long-range training in the southwest Indian Ocean. Source describes this as the third Indian Navy port call there in 2025; interactions involved Seychelles Defence Forces, leadership and the Indian mission.
- Training cadets, professional exchanges, surveillance capacity and anti-piracy/anti-trafficking cooperation support maritime security and SAGAR. Seychelles lies in the western Indian Ocean; preserve the distinction between Navy and Coast Guard vessels in the squadron.
- Existing ties include gifted patrol vessels/surveillance aircraft. **Lamitye is an army/joint military exercise**, so the source practice question incorrectly calls it a bilateral naval exercise; its supplied answer accepting both statements should not be memorised. [Indian Army release](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2015283&lang=2&reg=48).
