# India–Malaysia relations and geography

## 7–8 February 2026: Prime Minister's Malaysia visit
- Visit advanced the **Comprehensive Strategic Partnership**, upgraded in **2024**. Eleven exchanged documents/outcomes span audiovisual co-production, disaster management, anti-corruption, UN peacekeeping, semiconductors, IBCA, social security, vocational training, national-security cooperation, health and the CEO Forum report. They are not eleven identical treaties already fully implemented. [Official outcomes](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2225060).
- Announcements: **Indian Consulate General in Malaysia**, **Thiruvalluvar Centre at Universiti Malaya**, scholarships, **NIPL–PayNet cross-border payment agreement**, and Ayurveda academic cooperation. Official outcomes do not name the consulate's city; avoid inventing one.
- Source emphasises **rupee–ringgit settlement**, semiconductors/AI/digital cooperation, defence and counter-terrorism, maritime security, ASEAN centrality and Malaysian support for India's permanent UNSC membership. Local-currency facilities are an option, not proof all bilateral trade has shifted out of dollars. **Harimau Shakti—Army; Udara Shakti—Air Force**.
## Malaysia: map essentials
- **Peninsular Malaysia + Sabah/Sarawak on Borneo**, separated by the **South China Sea**; capital **Kuala Lumpur**, federal administrative centre **Putrajaya**. Land neighbours **Thailand, Indonesia and Brunei**; causeway/bridge links to Singapore.
- **Strait of Malacca** west of peninsula; **Sulu and Celebes seas** near eastern Sabah. **Mount Kinabalu—Sabah; Rajang—Sarawak and longest river; Pahang/Perak—peninsula**. Equatorial rainforest/megadiversity; palm oil, rubber, electronics and shipping are important.
- **2011 navigation PYQ:** proposed **Kra Isthmus canal** could shorten India–East Asia voyages; deepening Malacca changes navigability rather than the route's geographic length. The proposed canal must not be described as already constructed.
