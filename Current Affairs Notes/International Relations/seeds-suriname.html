SEEDS and India–Suriname development cooperation

Editable notes

Development cooperation

  • SEEDS: Supply of Equipment for Efficient Development of SMEs, MEA initiative donating machinery/tools to partner developing countries; promotes productive capacity, farmers' income, local value addition and South–South cooperation.
  • June 2025 example: final consignment of passion-fruit-processing machinery to Suriname, source value US$1 million. Capacity building/maintenance and market access determine lasting benefits.

Suriname map recall

  • Paramaribo capital; Dutch official language; Surinamese dollar; northern South America with Atlantic Ocean north, Guyana west, Brazil south, French Guiana east (French Guiana is a French territory).
  • Suriname, Maroni/Marowijne and Courantyne rivers; Bakhuys and Van Asch Van Wijck ranges; Juliana Top in Wilhelmina Mountains is highest point.
  • Indian-origin population about 27% in the source, with roots in nineteenth-century indentured migration; cultural/linguistic ties and Yoga strengthen relations.
  • Programme/ministry PYQ revision: DPAP and Desert Development Programme belonged to Rural Development; National Watershed Development Project for Rainfed Areas belonged to Agriculture. Thus all three supplied pairs are incorrectly matched (2014 answer d, None), correcting the source's b. Contemporary PIB account.
