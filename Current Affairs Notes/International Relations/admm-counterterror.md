# ADMM-Plus counter-terrorism cooperation

## India–Malaysia work plan
- Co-chairmanship of the **ADMM-Plus Experts' Working Group on Counter-Terrorism, 2024–27**; proposed table-top exercise in Malaysia in 2026 and field exercise in India in 2027.
- These were planned events in March 2025, not completed exercises. Table-top discussion tests decisions; field training tests practical coordination.
- ADMM-Plus brings ASEAN and eight partners: **Australia, China, India, Japan, New Zealand, Republic of Korea, Russia and US**.
- Expert groups address areas including counter-terrorism, maritime security, peacekeeping and disaster response; ADMM-Plus is not a military alliance.
- EU–India work on preventing violent extremism complements ASEAN cooperation. Intelligence coordination, online radicalisation prevention and capacity building require due process and protection of legitimate expression.
