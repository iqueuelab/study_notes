# International Energy Agency and India membership

## Institution and India's application
- **IEA established 1974 after the 1973 oil shock; Paris; autonomous agency within the OECD framework.** India joined its **Association in 2017**, formally requested full membership **October 2023**, and membership talks were authorised **February 2024**. The February 2026 account reports progress, not completed accession. [IEA talks announcement](https://www.iea.org/news/iea-to-start-membership-talks-with-india-in-major-step-forward-for-global-energy-governance).
- Existing membership criteria require **OECD membership**, reserves equivalent to **90 days of the previous year's net oil imports**, demand-restraint capacity and national emergency-response arrangements. Commercial stocks accessible to government may count; this is not necessarily 90 days of government-owned underground reserves. [IEA criteria](https://www.iea.org/about/membership).
- India is an OECD Key Partner rather than member, creating a legal/institutional barrier requiring resolution; do not assert membership follows automatically from political support or invent a completed charter amendment.
- Original oil-security/collective-response mandate now includes efficiency, clean-energy transition and critical-mineral security. Membership could improve India's formal influence and crisis coordination; Association already supports data/policy collaboration. Source membership counts and 80% energy share are dated family-wide measures, not India's share or a claim all consuming countries have votes.
- **IEA and IAEA are different bodies**: IAEA safeguards concern peaceful nuclear-material use. The 2020 question is already in the common bank; do not infer that every domestic-fuel civilian reactor is categorically outside safeguards.
