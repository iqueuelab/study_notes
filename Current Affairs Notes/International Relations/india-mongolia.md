# India–Mongolia relations and military exercises

## Nomadic Elephant 17: underway inJune 2025
- India–Mongolia **annual bilateral Army exercise**, alternating hosts; **17 th edition 31 May–13 June 2025,Ulaanbaatar SpecialForcesTrainingCentre**; **16 th at Umroi, Meghalaya, July 2024**. Source's “scheduled” misses the accompanying clipping showing it already underway. The claimed 2006 origin is source background requiring historical verification.
- **45 Indian personnel**, primarily **Arunachal Scouts**, with Mongolian **150 Special Forces unit** (a unit designation, not necessarily 150 participants); joint semi-conventional operations in semi-urban/mountain terrain, interoperability, operational tactics, cultural understanding. [Defence Ministry 29 May](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleaseIframePage.aspx?PRID=2132473&lang=2&reg=48).
- **UN Chapter VII** concerns Security Council action for threats/breaches of peace/aggression; exercise under a UN-mandate scenario is not itself an authorised UN deployment. **Khaan Quest** is a separate multinational peacekeeping exercise hosted in Mongolia; do not equate bilateral training with all UN peacekeeping law.
## Mongolia map and economic links
- **Landlocked between Russia (north) and China (south/east/west)**; capital **Ulaanbaatar**, plateau/high-altitude continental setting. **Altai, Khangai, Khentii** mountain systems; **Orkhon River / UNESCO Orkhon Valley cultural landscape**; **Lake Khuvsgul** major freshwater lake. “Younger sister of Baikal” is a nickname, not hydrological classification.
- Coal, copper, gold, fluorspar are major resource sectors; lithium prospects do not by themselves establish Mongolia as a leading commercial lithium producer.
- Source reports **trial coking-coal sourcing by India** to diversify steelmaking inputs beyond suppliers such as Australia. Plans/trials are not proof of major established deliveries or eliminated dependence; landlocked transport via neighbours affects cost/feasibility. Coking coal makes coke for conventional blast-furnace steelmaking, not every steel-production route.
