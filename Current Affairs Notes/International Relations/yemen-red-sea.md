# Yemen, Houthis and Red Sea shipping

## Conflict and geography
- Houthis/Ansar Allah emerged from northern Yemen's Zaidi Shia context; took Sana'a in 2014. Saudi-led intervention began in 2015. Yemen is not under one uncontested national authority.
- **Bab el-Mandeb links the Red Sea and Gulf of Aden**, between Yemen and the Djibouti/Eritrea side of the African coast.
- US strikes on 15 March 2025 and Houthi retaliation were reported; early casualty figures and claims of hits require attribution and later verification.
- Iranian support does not establish identical control over every aligned group. Hamas is Sunni; the loosely described “Axis of Resistance” is not uniformly Shia.
- Shipping reroutes via the Cape of Good Hope raise journey time, insurance and freight costs, affecting Indian trade and energy supplies.
- Self-defence claims under UN Charter Article 51 require analysis of necessity and proportionality; invoking the Article is not a final ruling that every strike is lawful. Civilian protection remains essential.
