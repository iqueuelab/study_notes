# INS Sarvekshak and hydrographic diplomacy

## Mauritius survey: January 2025

- **INS Sarvekshak**, Indian Navy **Darshak-class hydrographic survey vessel**, Southern Naval Command, **Kochi**; completed Mauritius survey covering **over 25,000 square nautical miles**. Survey fair sheet and charts handed over; maritime navigation, port planning and resource governance benefits.
- **SAGAR = Security and Growth for All in the Region**; cooperation builds partner capacity and maritime trust. Source five-year regional record **89,000 sq km / 96 charts** uses a different unit from the Mauritius survey. [Navy release](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2095401&lang=2&reg=48).
- Multi-beam echo sounder, side-scan sonar and digital processing; integral **Chetak helicopter**. Source complement **15 officers/175 sailors** is a vessel description, not a universal survey staffing norm.
- Other cooperation: **Sri Lanka, Seychelles, Tanzania, Kenya** and earlier Mauritius missions.

## Hydrography versus oceanography

- Measures/describes **physical water-body features**, depth/seabed, tides/currents and navigational shoreline aids; forecasts relevant changes and supports safe navigation.
- Ships/boats use sonar/echo sounding; aircraft **laser bathymetry** and satellites contribute where suitable.
- Oceanography studies wider marine processes; hydrography's charting/navigational purpose is a distinction, not exclusion of overlapping science.
