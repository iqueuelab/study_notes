# India–Israel relations and West Asian balancing

## February 2026: Israel visit and diplomatic balancing
- The issue mixes pre-visit commentary with a clipping on Modi's **25 February Knesset address**; his State visit was scheduled **25–26 February 2026**, and he became the first Indian PM to address the Knesset. Preserve the distinction between anticipatory editorial risks and events already reported. [Knesset record](https://m.knesset.gov.il/EN/News/PressReleases/Pages/press25226a.aspx).
- Formal diplomatic relations date to **1992**; cooperation includes defence/security, agriculture, water and innovation. **2017** was the first visit by an Indian PM. Ties with Israel coexist with India's support for Palestinian statehood/two-state settlement and humanitarian concerns.
- Netanyahu's proposed **“hexagon” of partnerships**, naming India, Greece, Cyprus and other regional partners, is a political proposal; it is not evidence India acceded to a formal anti-Iran/anti-Sunni military alliance. Claims about targeted States are the commentary's strategic interpretation.
- India must consider Gulf energy/trade/diaspora interests, Iran/Chabahar connectivity and IMEC amid Gaza conflict and Iran–Israel/US tensions. Iran's historic oil role should not be described as continuing large Indian crude imports after sanctions; Qatar's LNG importance differs from oil suppliers.
- Strategic autonomy entails issue-based cooperation and consistent diplomacy on international law, humanitarian access and regional stability. IMEC is a proposed connectivity framework; insecurity can delay implementation without proving it has been cancelled.

## February 2026: geographical revision
- Israel lies on the **eastern Mediterranean,Levant/WestAsia**; neighbours include **Lebanon,Syria,Jordan,Egypt**, with **WestBank/Gaza Palestinian territories** requiring their distinct political status. Mediterranean coast, northern hills and southern **Negev Desert** contrast with the **Jordan Rift/Dead Sea depression**.
- **Dead Sea** is a hypersaline lake at Earth's lowest exposed land elevation, roughly **430m below sea level** with a changing level. **Sea of Galilee/Lake Tiberias** is a freshwater lake; it is not a sea. Water scarcity explains desalination, reuse and micro-irrigation cooperation.
- **Knesset** is Israel's parliament. Israel declares Jerusalem its capital and houses major institutions there; the city's status is internationally disputed, so the source's unqualified capital label omits a central diplomatic qualification. Naming **Samaria/Judea** as ordinary Israeli regions must not erase the West Bank's occupied-territory status.
- **Dead Sea Transform** separates the **Arabian Plate and Sinai/African-side block**; simplify cautiously rather than describing all regional tectonics as one undivided African Plate boundary. India's defence, agricultural centres/precision irrigation and innovation cooperation connect physical constraints with bilateral priorities; visit context is already covered under25–26 February. [UN on Jerusalem status](https://press.un.org/en/2023/sgsm21684.doc.htm), [USGS tectonic description](https://pubs.usgs.gov/of/1990/0667/report.pdf).

## 28 February–2 March 2026: joint strikes and regional escalation
- Israel and the US began coordinated attacks on Iran on **28 February**; the US operation was named **Epic Fury**. Iran retaliated against Israel and US-linked facilities in Gulf countries, widening risks to regional airspace and shipping. The US description of an imminent threat and Israel's pre-emptive justification are belligerents' claims, not independent legal findings. [CENTCOM initial fact sheet](https://www.centcom.mil/Portals/6/Documents/Publications/260301-Fact%20Sheet.pdf).
- The strikes occurred amid Oman-mediated US–Iran nuclear diplomacy. The compilation's assertion that a conclusively failed Geneva process alone triggered war oversimplifies the sequence; enrichment, verification, sanctions and regional security remained contested.
- **India's 28 February statement** urged restraint, civilian safety, dialogue and respect for sovereignty/territorial integrity. Missions issued vigilance/contact/local-security advisories. Airspace closures and suspended flights complicate assistance; source-period estimates of **~10,000 Indians in Iran, over 41,000 in Israel and 8–9 million across the Gulf** illustrate exposure, not an evacuation count. [MEA statement, reproduced by Indian Embassy Cairo](https://www.eoicairo.gov.in/section/press-releases/statement-on-the-evolving-situation-in-west-asia/).
- India's balancing involves Israel/US partnerships, Gulf energy/trade/diaspora interests and Iran/Chabahar connectivity. Historic Iranian oil ties do not establish resumed large Indian crude imports. The source's blanket **60% of energy imports** lacks a clear fuel/period denominator; distinguish crude, LPG and LNG exposure. Prior evacuation experience is background, not proof a new evacuation was completed.

## 10 March 2026: strategic assessment
- Editorial views the war as a contest over regional order as well as military/nuclear capability. Iran's ties to **Hezbollah (Lebanon), Houthis (Yemen) and Iraqi Shia militias** illustrate indirect influence; their autonomy, territorial control and capabilities vary, so they are not one uniform army.
- It argues that Israel's military priorities and Washington's concerns over prolonged engagement/other theatres may diverge. These are analytical judgments, **not proof of agreed US or Israeli war aims**. A drawn-out conflict could divert US attention from the Indo-Pacific and open space for China, Russia or regional powers; these are scenarios rather than established outcomes.
- India's existing energy/diaspora/diplomatic exposure now also requires assessing **remittance continuity, commercial shipping/insurance and the opportunity cost of a prolonged war**. Maintain engagement with Iran, Israel, the US and Gulf States; evacuation readiness, de-escalation and diversified supplies are practical responses.
- The issue's reference to **Ali Khamenei as a continuing wartime leader is stale**: Iran's **1 March letter to the UN** had already reported his killing. Do not carry that wording into a March10 leadership fact box. [Iran's letter at the UN](https://digitallibrary.un.org/record/4104578/files/S_2026_109-EN.pdf). The October2023 Hamas attack is regional background; the current joint strikes began **28 February 2026**, as recorded in the earlier notes.
