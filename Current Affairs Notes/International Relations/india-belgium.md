# India–Belgium defence manufacturing

## Collaboration
- John Cockerill Defence/Electro Pneumatics & Hydraulics joint venture, reported 60:40, to produce turrets in India; technology transfer/local production differ from fully indigenous intellectual property.
- DRDO–L&T **Zorawar light tank** development responds to high-altitude mobility needs following 2020 Ladakh tensions; trial stage is not fleet induction.
- Source 700 tank requirement and anticipated 2025 Mo U are reported plans, not signed orders or completed deliveries.
- Combine specialised foreign technology with Indian manufacturing/electronics, testing, spares and lifecycle support; local assembly alone does not remove every import dependency.
