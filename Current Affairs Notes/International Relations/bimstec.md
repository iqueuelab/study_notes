# BIMSTEC and Bay of Bengal cooperation

## Members and evolution
- **Bay of Bengal Initiative for Multi-Sectoral Technical and Economic Cooperation**; established by the **Bangkok Declaration, 6 June 1997**.
- **Seven members:** Bangladesh, Bhutan, India, Nepal, Sri Lanka; Myanmar and Thailand. Nepal/Bhutan are landlocked members—Bay of Bengal cooperation does not require every member to have a coast.
- BIST-EC began with Bangladesh, India, Sri Lanka and Thailand; Myanmar joined in 1997. Bhutan and Nepal joined in 2004, producing the present grouping/name.
- Bridges South/Southeast Asia through trade, technology, energy, transport, security and development cooperation. Overlap with SAARC/ASEAN is different from formally replacing either.
## April 2025 summit and India
- India's PM visited Thailand **3–4 April 2025**; the **sixth summit took place 4 April in Bangkok**, not throughout both days. Charter entered into force **20 May 2024**. [BIMSTEC summit declaration](https://bimstec.org/images/content_page_pdf/1743841366_6th%20BIMSTEC%20Summit%20Declaration.pdf).
- Supports India's **Neighbourhood First** and **Act East** policies: connectivity, maritime cooperation, resilient supply chains and regional public goods.
- **MAHASAGAR:** Mutual and Holistic Advancement for Security and Growth Across Regions; articulated during the **March 2025 Mauritius visit**, not created for the first time at the Thailand summit. [MEA briefing](https://www.mea.gov.in/outoging-visit-detail.htm?39163).
- BIMSTEC FTA negotiations/framework are not evidence of a fully operational free-trade area. Implementation, political instability, infrastructure gaps and non-tariff barriers matter alongside declarations.

## 2025 PYQ
- Seven members as of January 2025; **Bangkok Declaration 1997**, not Dhaka 1999; founders Bangladesh, India, Sri Lanka, Thailand; Nepal joined 2004.
- **Tourism led by Nepal**, not India; therefore **I only (d)**. This sector-leadership fact complements common membership notes.
