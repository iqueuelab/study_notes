# Pravasi Bharatiya Divas and the Indian diaspora

## Date, origin and institutions
- **9 January** marks Gandhi's return from South Africa **9 January 1915**. First convention: **2003, New Delhi**, under Atal Bihari Vajpayee; recommendations of **L. M. Singhvi committee**, report January 2002.
- Day observed annually; major conventions **biennial since 2015**. **MEA**, host state and partner organisations; CII participation does not make it the permanent sole organiser.
- **18th convention: 8–10 January 2025, Bhubaneswar, Odisha**; PM inaugurated 9 January. Theme: **Diaspora's contribution to a Viksit Bharat**.
- Chief guest **Christine Carla Kangaloo**, President of Trinidad and Tobago, addressed virtually. [MEA overview](https://www.mea.gov.in/pravasi-bharatiya-divas.htm).
## Samman and dated examples
- **Pravasi Bharatiya Samman Award**, highest honour for overseas Indians; President confers it on eligible NRIs/PIOs and their organisations for relevant contributions.
- Source January 2025: **27 awardees across 24 countries**. Examples: **Lekh Raj Juneja, Japan—science/technology; Robert Masih Nahar, Spain—community service**, Indian-origin Spanish senator since 2017 and cricket promotion; **Syed Anwar Khursheed, Saudi Arabia—medical service**, royal-protocol physician. [MEA award overview](https://www.mea.gov.in/pravasi-bharatiya-samman.htm).
- Source diaspora snapshot **over 3.5 crore**; USA **54 lakh**, UAE **35 lakh**, Canada **28 lakh**, Saudi Arabia **24 lakh**. Reference dates unspecified; not current population counts.
- Source remittance estimate **$125 billion in 2023**; distinguish remittances from foreign investment and diaspora headcount.
## Diaspora → contribution → policy
- Remittances, trade/investment, innovation, skills, philanthropy, cultural networks and public diplomacy; **Howdy Modi, Texas, 2019**, with Donald Trump, illustrates outreach.
- PM's January 2025 themes: developed India **2047**, humanity-first approach, Global South, African Union's G20 inclusion, Ashoka/Dhauli and Buddhist peace heritage.
- Preserve **Girmitiya/indentured-labour histories** through research and databases; historic Mandvi–Muscat commercial links described as about **250 years old**. Cultural memory complements present-day economic ties.
- Policy balance: consular protection, worker welfare, diaspora participation and host-country sovereignty; overseas Indian identity does not automatically confer Indian citizenship or voting rights.
