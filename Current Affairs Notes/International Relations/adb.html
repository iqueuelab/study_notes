Asian Development Bank: financing and safeguards

Editable notes

Institution

  • Established 1966, headquarters Mandaluyong/Metro Manila, Philippines; regional development bank supporting inclusive, resilient, sustainable development with loans, grants, equity and technical assistance. India collaboration: infrastructure, climate resilience, logistics, water/sanitation, health/education.
  • 69 members: 50 Asia-Pacific, 19 nonregional, correcting the body’s 49/20 split (its graphic gives 50/19). ADB institution.
  • Embedded comparator: AIIB, 2016, Beijing, infrastructure; African Development Bank, 1964, Abidjan. Their memberships change; source AIIB110/AfDB81 counts are dated, not permanent totals.

5 June: Pakistan financing dispute

  • Clipping reports India strongly opposed funding and sought safeguards/monitoring against resource diversion, citing fiscal/military-spending concerns. These are India’s reported objections, not proven findings that this package financed terrorism.
  • ADB approved an US$800 million package, comprising US$300 million policy-based loan + US$500 million guarantee, for resource mobilisation/utilisation reforms. Guarantee ≠ an US$800 million immediate cash loan or grant. ADB programme account.
  • Source cites Pakistan tax/GDP 13% (2017–18) → 9.2% (2022–23) and regional~19% average, requiring matching definitions/years. Requested FATF review is separate from ADB’s lending decision; it does not prove automatic relisting.

September 2025 growth outlook: tariff exposure

  • 30 September ADO: India 6.5% GDP growth in ADB FY2025 (ends 31 March 2026) and 6.5% FY2026. Current-year forecast unchanged from July, but below April 6.7%; following year reduced from July 6.7% (April 6.8%). Do not describe every comparison as a fresh cut from the latest forecast.
  • Tariffs weaken merchandise exports/investment; domestic consumption and service exports cushion effects. These are projections/risks, not final realised growth. Source comparative regional forecasts: developing Asia 4.8% in calendar 2025, 4.5% in 2026, with fiscal/calendar conventions distinct from India. ADB India release.
  • Institutional basics and corrected 69 members: 50 regional/19 nonregional are already covered; the repeated body’s 49/20 split remains incorrect.
