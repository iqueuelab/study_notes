# Diplomatic capacity, MEA funding and development partnerships

## Resources behind foreign policy

- Aspirations in the neighbourhood, Global South, Quad, ASEAN engagement and **International Solar Alliance** require missions, trained diplomats, project delivery and policy research. India engages ASEAN; it is not an ASEAN member.
- **FY 2024–25 MEA Budget Estimates: ₹22,154.67 crore**, including missions, cooperation, passport services and other heads; allocation ≠ expenditure. [MEA annual report, finance chapter](https://www.mea.gov.in/Images/CPV/140725MEAAnnualReport2024English.pdf).
- January editorial: roughly **0.4% of Union budget**, committee recommendation **1%**, suggested staged **0.6–0.8%**. These are dated shares/recommendations; compare like-for-like BE/RE/actuals, including reserve transfers.
- Source comparisons: average **4% annual rise, 2017–2023**, later **23% spike**; training **+30%**, missions/training/cultural heads **+7%**, Nalanda/South Asian University **20–22% cuts**. Different heads and comparison bases; not a single uniform change.

## Grants, loans and implementation

- **Grant** need not be repaid; **loan/Line of Credit** creates repayment/project terms. Aid can support hydropower, connectivity and crisis response; repayment risk and delivery delays matter.
- Source snapshots: aid **−10%**, foreign-government loans **+29%**; Bangladesh allocation **₹200 → ₹120 crore**, Sri Lanka **+63%**; Bhutan largest aid recipient. Bangladesh cumulative LoCs **$7.86 billion**, neighbourhood share **45%**: stocks differ from annual budget flows/disbursements.
- Connectivity through **BBIN/BIMSTEC**, energy interdependence and trusted partnerships; competition with China's BRI does not make every project a zero-sum contest.

## Reform priorities

- Expand recruitment/training and regional expertise; coordinate ministries, monitor costs/time/outcomes, assess loans and local needs, protect fiscal priorities.
- **Track 1** official diplomacy; **Track 2** academic/nonofficial dialogue. Fund research, archive digitisation and lawful declassification to improve institutional memory, with appropriate confidentiality safeguards.
- Mains chain: ambitions → capacity gap → prioritised resources → delivery/accountability → durable influence. Source prompts are coaching questions.
