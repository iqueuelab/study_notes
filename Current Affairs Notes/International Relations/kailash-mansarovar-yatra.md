# Kailash Mansarovar Yatra

## April 2025 diplomatic context
- India and China agreed in **January 2025** to resume the pilgrimage in **summer 2025**, after suspension since 2020 amid the pandemic and strained relations. At this issue date, resumption was a planned confidence-building measure; diplomatic cooperation does not settle the boundary dispute. [Official January outcome](https://www.eoibeijing.gov.in/news_detail.php?newsid=1528).
- MEA organises the annual pilgrimage with China, Uttarakhand/Sikkim/Delhi administrations and ITBP. Eligible Indian citizens require passports and medical fitness; pilgrims bear costs, with no MEA subsidy.
| Route | Recall |
| --- | --- |
| Lipulekh, Uttarakhand | Traditional route resumed in 1981; demanding high-altitude trekking; sensitive India–Nepal–China border region |
| Nathu La, Sikkim | Introduced 2015; substantially motorable, though high-altitude health risks remain |
## Geography and faith
- **Mount Kailash (~6,638 m)** and freshwater **Lake Mansarovar** lie in Tibet Autonomous Region, China, in the Trans-Himalayan landscape. The wider region contains headwaters of **Indus, Sutlej, Brahmaputra/Yarlung Tsangpo and Karnali**; these do not all flow directly out of Mansarovar.
- Hindu traditions associate Kailash with Shiva; Jain tradition associates Ashtapada with first Tirthankara Rishabhanatha; Tibetan Buddhism with Chakrasamvara/Demchok; Bon regards the landscape as sacred.
- Cultural diplomacy, people-to-people contact and route coordination can rebuild trust; border sensitivity, environmental limits and pilgrim health require cooperation.
[MEA pilgrimage information](https://www.mea.gov.in/faq-kmy.htm).

## June 21 revision
- Source reports June 2025 resumption after suspension since 2020; describe the gap by dates rather than its inaccurate “four years”. Route coordination is a confidence-building measure, not settlement of border disputes.
- **Lipulekh recall:** Dharchula → Tawaghat → Lipulekh → Tibet; Niti, Lampiya Dhura and Barahoti are distractors in the source's pass question.
- Source's MPPSC explanation incorrectly admits OCI/foreign nationals to the **MEA-organised** scheme: eligibility is Indian citizenship and a valid Indian passport. Separate private pilgrimage arrangements from this scheme. [MEA eligibility](https://www.mea.gov.in/eligibility-kmy.htm).
