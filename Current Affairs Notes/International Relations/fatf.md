# Financial Action Task Force and compliance lists

## Mandate and membership
- **FATF**, intergovernmental standard setter established at **1989 G7 Paris summit**; Secretariat in **Paris**, hosted by OECD. Combats **money laundering, terrorist financing and proliferation financing** through standards, mutual evaluations and implementation monitoring; does not investigate/prosecute individual criminal cases.
- India became a full member in **2010**. Source's **39 members /37 jurisdictions +2 organisations** is outdated after Indonesia's **October 2023** accession: FATF counts **40 members**, including **European Commission and Gulf Cooperation Council**; Russia's membership was suspended **24 February 2023**. Membership total and active participation differ. [FATF network](https://www.fatf-gafi.org/en/countries.html), [Indonesia accession](https://www.fatf-gafi.org/en/countries/detail/Indonesia.html).
## Lists: status relevant to May 2025
| List | Meaning / consequence |
| --- | --- |
| **Jurisdictions under increased monitoring**, grey list | Strategic deficiencies with agreed corrective action; greater monitoring, possible reputational/financing costs; not an automatic FATF sanctions order or instruction to sever every relationship |
| **High-risk jurisdictions subject to a call for action**, black list | Significant deficiencies; enhanced due diligence and, for the most serious cases, countermeasures; measures vary by jurisdiction |
- **21 February 2025 high-risk list: DPRK, Iran and Myanmar**. Source omits Myanmar. DPRK/Iran attracted countermeasure calls; Myanmar required proportionate enhanced due diligence, with humanitarian flows/remittances protected. The old **NCCT** label is not the current official list title, and listing does not prove every government deliberately funds terrorism. [Dated FATF statement](https://www.fatf-gafi.org/en/publications/High-risk-and-other-monitored-jurisdictions/Call-for-action-february-2025.html).
- Pakistan was removed from increased monitoring in **October 2022**; May clipping reports India considering/pushing for renewed scrutiny, not proof Pakistan had been re-listed. Review national AML/CFT effectiveness and evidence, not nationality alone. Financial restrictions derive from competent jurisdictions/UN rules; FATF recommendations and sanctions are different instruments.
