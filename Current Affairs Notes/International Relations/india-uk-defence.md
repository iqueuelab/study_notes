# India–UK defence partnership: missiles and electric propulsion

## Aero India agreements, February 2025
- **Defence Partnership–India (DP-I)**: dedicated UK Ministry of Defence programme office, coordinating bilateral industrial collaboration.
- **Thales–BDL** laser-beam-riding MANPADS contract; official release planned initial **STARStreak missiles/launchers in 2025**, correcting source “already supplied in 2024”. Later **LMM** co-production collaboration is a distinct step.
- **MBDA–BDL**, **ASRAAM assembly/test facility, Hyderabad**; air-to-air missiles differ from MANPADS. Source Jaguar/Tejas integration should be read as platform-specific programmes, not universal deployment.
- **IFEP Statement of Intent**, next-generation Navy **Landing Platform Docks**; **GE Vernova–BHEL** land-based testing work. Full electric propulsion converts supplied energy into ship propulsion; **not fuel-free operation**.
- [UK official announcement](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/uk-india-defence-agreements-boost-atmanirbhar-bharat-ambition).
## Assess outcomes
- Potential: co-production, jobs, interoperability, supplier capacity and exports. Constraints: IP/critical technology, local content, certification, costs and reliable delivery.
- Agreement/SoI, factory readiness and delivered operational systems are different stages. India–UK cooperation does not imply the UK joined **Quad** or every transfer makes India wholly self-reliant.

## Exercise recall in the June issue
- **Konkan:** Indian/Royal Navy maritime interoperability and anti-submarine cooperation; **Ajeya Warrior:** Indian/British Army counter-insurgency/terrorism training; **Indradhanush:** Indian Air Force/Royal Air Force.
- **PASSEX** is an ad hoc passage exercise, not a single exclusive India–UK annual named exercise; communication, tactical manoeuvres and interoperability can be practised with visiting naval groups. The PDF's “annual” label for every Konkan iteration should not imply no scheduling gaps.
