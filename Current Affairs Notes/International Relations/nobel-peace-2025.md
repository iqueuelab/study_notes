# Nobel Peace 2025: María Corina Machado

## Venezuela and democratic transition
- Venezuelan opposition leader María Corina Machado won the 2025 Peace Nobel for defending democratic rights and pursuing a peaceful transition. The Norwegian Nobel Committee awards this prize in Oslo; the Swedish Academy awards literature. [Nobel announcement](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/peace/2025/press-release/).
- The issue places her opposition work in the context of Nicolás Maduro's rule since 2013, Machado's exclusion from the 2024 presidential ballot, disputed elections, repression and mass outward migration. Distinguish reported accusations and political assessments from a domestic court judgment.
- Peaceful mobilisation, human rights and women's leadership are revision themes. Nobel recognition may influence diplomacy but neither changes the government nor guarantees transition.
