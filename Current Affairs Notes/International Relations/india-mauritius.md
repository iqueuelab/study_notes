# India–Mauritius: diaspora, development and maritime ties

## Relationship

- March 11–12, 2025 visit around Mauritius National Day 12 March; ~70%Indian-origin population, including descendants of indentured workers, alongside diverse communities.
- Sir Seewoosagur Ramgoolam/Indianfreedom links; Mahatma Gandhi Institute, cultural cooperation and World Hindi Secretariat; engage all communities, not only diaspora.
- Source FY2022–23 US$554 million trade is dated; DTAA/investment links and **CECPA2021** require rules/anti-abuse safeguards, not assumed automatic tax exemption.
- Mauritius is an African Union/Af CFTAmember; using it as an Africanbusiness hub does not automatically grant every Indian export duty-free access—rules of origin apply.

## Maritime cooperation

- Large EEZ, coastal radars, Agaléga infrastructure, hydrographic surveys and IFC-IORinformation links support capacity and maritime awareness.
- Colombo Security Conclave includes India, Sri Lanka, Maldives, Mauritius, Bangladesh at sourcecheckpoint; it is not simply a military alliance directed at one country.
- Respect Mauritiansovereignty, transparent projects and blue-economy/climate needs; competition with China is one dimension of a wider relationship.
