# India–Indonesia cooperation

## January 2025 context
- The PDF anticipated President **Prabowo Subianto's** Republic Day visit and cooperation proposals. Keep prospective proposals separate from concluded agreements.
- India links Indonesia with **Act East**, maritime security and wider Indo-Pacific engagement; Indonesia is an important **ASEAN** partner.
## Defence and technology
- **BrahMos**: India–Russia joint-venture **supersonic cruise missile**; variants for land, sea and air. The source discusses an Indonesian purchase proposal, not proof of a signed export contract.
- Possible cooperation in **Su-30 maintenance/upgrades**, training, technology transfer, exercises and maritime security; costs, budgets and recipient priorities shape procurement.
- Sea-lane security and a stable regional balance provide cooperation incentives; avoid describing every initiative solely as anti-China alignment.
## Public services and economy
- Interest in digital public services: **payments, e-governance and inclusion**, with access/privacy/capacity safeguards.
- School-meal cooperation links nutrition, attendance and learning; India's midday-meal experience is a comparison, not proof of identical schemes.
- Trade/investment opportunities cited: minerals, pharmaceuticals, renewable energy, infrastructure and manufacturing; ASEAN connections support market access.
## Historical and diplomatic recall
- Cultural/maritime links: **Bali Hindu traditions, Borobudur Buddhism**, Ramayana/Mahabharata performances, later Islamic links; independence-era solidarity **Nehru/Sukarno**, **Bandung 1955**. NAM formally founded later, not at Bandung itself.
- **Comprehensive Strategic Partnership, 2018**, renewed defence cooperation, Act East/ASEAN centrality and **ASEAN Outlook on the Indo-Pacific**.
- **Prabowo Subianto**, Republic Day chief guest **26 January 2025**, fourth Indonesian president invited; Sukarno was chief guest at India's **first Republic Day, 1950**. Talks/welcome **25 January**, Kartavya Path parade **26 January**.
- Source troop figures **352 total, 152 marching + 189 band** don't reconcile. Official pre-parade brief: **160 marching + 190 band**. [Defence briefing](https://www.pib.gov.in/newsite/erelcontent.aspx?lang=2&reg=48&relid=266320). Avoid unsupported “largest ever” claim.

- Source **US$450 million BrahMos deal** is an expectation/negotiation, not proof signed in January; **Dhruv/Akash interest** is not procurement. Philippines earlier BrahMos customer.
- Source refers to expanding an India–Indonesia **CECA** without evidence an existing bilateral CECA was concluded; distinguish **ASEAN–India trade framework** from bilateral proposals.
- **Malacca Strait is not Chinese sovereign territory**; geopolitical dependence differs from “Chinese-controlled chokepoints”. Trilateral Highway is a mainland India–Myanmar–Thailand project; reaching island Indonesia needs maritime links, not a straightforward road extension.
## Mains balance
- Diversify trade/close India's deficit through competitive exports; maritime awareness, blue economy, port connectivity, educational links and renewable projects.
- Constraints: tariff/market-access differences, connectivity gaps, global-power balancing and non-traditional security threats. Cooperation respects ASEAN autonomy, not an automatic anti-China military alliance.
- January planned MoUs and promises differ from final signed outcomes; source daily mains questions are coaching practice.
