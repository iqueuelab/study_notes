# India–US energy trade and diversification

## Security and trade

- India imports over 85%of crude requirements in source-era data; diversify among Russia, West Asia, US and other suppliers based on landed cost, refinery compatibility, reliability and geopolitics.
- February 2025 USvisit discussed expanded energy purchases and US$500 billion bilateral trade ambition; pledge is not a fixed executed procurement contract.
- Source Russia~40%share and US357, 000 barrels/day figure are period-specific, not constant shares. Imports do not inherently lower overall import dependency.
- Natural-gas share 15%goal and IEA2030~103 bcm forecast are targets/projections; LNGterminals, pipelines, pricing and demand determine absorption.
- Gas may have lower combustion CO₂than coal but remains fossil fuel; methane leakage affects climate benefits. Clean power, efficiency and electrification complement supply diversification.
- IEAassociation/support for membership is different from completed full membership. Maintain strategic autonomy and commercially viable sourcing.
