# India–Jordan relations: West Asian diplomacy and economic ties

## 15–16 December 2025: bilateral visit to Jordan
- PM **Narendra Modi** visited Jordan, meeting **King Abdullah II ibn Al Hussein**; Jordanian PM **Jafar Hassan** received him at the airport. First full-fledged bilateral visit by Modi and part of the **Jordan–Ethiopia–Oman** tour; a prior transit visit should not be confused with this bilateral engagement.
- **Correction: diplomatic relations began in 1950**, making **2025 the 75th anniversary**. The sheet's “1948–2023”/established-1948 statements conflict with this and are wrong. [Official December 16 backgrounder](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressNoteDetails.aspx?ModuleId=3&NoteId=156523&id=156523&lang=1&reg=6).
- Talks/business engagement focused on trade, investment, technology, bilateral cooperation and regional stability. Source identifies India as Jordan's **third-largest trading partner**; exports include pharmaceuticals, engineering products, textiles and chemicals. Country rankings are a dated official/source snapshot, not permanent status.
- Jordan's West Asian location, moderate diplomacy and cooperation against terrorism matter amid regional instability. The source's proposed **Petra** visit illustrates historical/cultural links but should not be treated as completed merely from this advance programme.
