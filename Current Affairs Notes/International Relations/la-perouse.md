# La Pérouse: multinational naval interoperability

## Exercise recall
- **French Navy-led multinational maritime exercise**, Indo-Pacific/Indian Ocean approaches; **INS Mumbai**, indigenous guided-missile destroyer, participated in January 2025.
- PIB labels 2025 **fourth edition**; French Navy release calls it **fifth**. Keep institutional discrepancy visible rather than silently settling it. [Indian Navy release](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2093998&lang=2&reg=48), [French Navy account](https://www.defense.gouv.fr/marine/actualites/clemenceau-25-gan-lance-lexercice-multinational-perouse-25-detroits-reliant-oceans-indien).
- Source participants besides India: **France, Australia, UK, USA, Indonesia, Malaysia, Singapore and Canada**; not Russia. Nine nations including India.
- Maritime situational awareness, surveillance/interdiction, information-sharing and interoperability; surface/air defence, tactical manoeuvres, cross-deck landings and **VBSS: Visit, Board, Search and Seizure**.
- Supports **SAGAR: Security and Growth for All in the Region**, safe sea lanes and rules-based maritime cooperation; a naval exercise is not a bilateral trade agreement or binding alliance.
