# India, Türkiye and Azerbaijan: diplomacy and economic links

## May 2025 backlash and relationships
- Following Türkiye/Azerbaijan support for Pakistan in the Pahalgam/India–Pakistan crisis, the issue reports **tour-operator cancellations, trader boycott resolutions and suspension of institutional MoUs including IITs/JNU**. These are organisational/public responses, **not proof of a comprehensive Indian government trade or travel ban**.
- Türkiye–Pakistan defence/diplomatic ties include Kashmir/Cyprus positions and artillery/armoured-platform transfers; Türkiye supported Azerbaijan in **2020 Nagorno-Karabakh war**. India–Armenia defence exports include surface-to-air missiles and multiple rocket launchers. SIPRI records cover **major conventional arms**, so absent records are not proof of zero trade in every defence item.
- Source data: Türkiye/Azerbaijan combined **under 1% of India's crude imports (2018–2023)**; India **third-largest Azerbaijani oil buyer in 2023**. Türkiye's machinery share around **1%** is a dated product-category claim, not import dependence across every sector.
- Indian arrivals: **under 1% of Türkiye tourists (2024)**; Azerbaijan **roughly 10% (2024), about 6% (2023)**. Combined students reportedly **777 by January 2024**, versus under 100 in 2017. Tourism, students, commodity trade and armaments have separate datasets; “limited aggregate exposure” does not guarantee no firm/person suffers boycott costs.
## Azerbaijan map and Karabakh correction
- **South Caucasus; capital Baku on western Caspian shore**; borders **Russia (Dagestan), Georgia, Armenia, Iran and Türkiye via Nakhchivan**. Nakhchivan is Azerbaijani **exclave** separated from mainland, directly bordering Türkiye/Armenia/Iran—not an indirect Turkish border.
- **Kura River → Caspian; Greater Caucasus north; Ganja, Nakhchivan City, Shusha** recall. Caspian is the largest enclosed inland water body; **Russia/Azerbaijan/Iran/Turkmenistan/Kazakhstan** are littoral States.
- Azerbaijan restored control over Karabakh **2023**; the PDF's present “mainly Armenian population” is misleading after **over 100,000 fled to Armenia**. Historical Armenian-majority/separatist administration is not the same as current demographic reality. [UNHCR](https://www.unhcr.org/am/en/refugee-response-and-coordination-0).
- **BTC oil pipeline: Baku–Tbilisi–Ceyhan**; **TANAP/TAP gas routes** connect to Europe; Azerbaijan participates in western INSTC connectivity. An interest in BRI is not proof every corridor/project is complete.
## Türkiye map recall
- Transcontinental: **Anatolia in Asia + eastern Thrace in Europe**; **Ankara capital, Istanbul largest city**. **Black Sea north, Aegean west, Mediterranean south**.
- **Bosporus: Black Sea ↔ Marmara; Dardanelles: Marmara ↔ Aegean**. Eight land neighbours: **Bulgaria, Greece, Georgia, Armenia, Azerbaijan, Iran, Iraq, Syria**.
- Turkish official language; Kurdish/Arabic spoken; majority-Muslim society. The source's **over 99%** religion share is an undated estimate, not necessary exact contemporary census fact. Diplomacy should assess security positions, business exposure and long-term engagement distinctly.
