# Katchatheevu and the Palk Bay fisheries dispute

## Island, boundary and livelihoods
- Small uninhabited island in the **Palk Strait**, between Tamil Nadu and northern Sri Lanka; source area about **285 acres/1.15 sq km**. The unverified volcanic-origin claim is omitted.
- **1974/1976 maritime boundary agreements** placed Katchatheevu on the Sri Lankan side. Indian government regards boundary/sovereignty as settled; Tamil Nadu political/legal challenges are challenges, not a court judgment reversing the agreements. Avoid treating “cession of undisputed Indian territory” as an uncontested legal conclusion. [MEA account](https://meacms.mea.gov.in/Images/pdf/India-foreign-relation-2011.pdf).
- **St Anthony's shrine** has shared religious significance; access for pilgrimage under applicable arrangements is distinct from a right to fish in another country's waters. The 1976 fishing restrictions cannot be erased by citing traditional use alone.
- Arrests, seized boats and livelihood insecurity affect Tamil Nadu fishers; destructive bottom trawling also harms seabed ecology and Sri Lankan fishers. Maritime security and humane treatment must accompany conservation.
- Way forward: sustained bilateral/fisher-community talks, prompt humane handling of detained fishers, transition from destructive trawling, alternative livelihoods and compensation. Joint/regulated access is a proposed negotiated option, not an existing unrestricted entitlement.
