# Forum for India–Pacific Islands Cooperation

## Members and summits
- India plus 14 Pacific island partners; established 2014; complements Act East/Indo-Pacific and South–South cooperation.
- **Cook Islands, Fiji, Kiribati, Marshall Islands, Federated States of Micronesia, Nauru, Niue, Samoa, Solomon Islands, Palau, Papua New Guinea, Tonga, Tuvalu, Vanuatu.** Australia/New Zealand are not members; Cook Islands/Niue are not UNmember States, so not all 14 have a UNvote.
-2014 Suva, Fiji; 2015 Jaipur; 2023 Port Moresby, PNG. Source US$200 million 2015 grant claim is wrong: annual grant-in-aid was US$200, 000 **per country**, increased from US$125, 000. [President's 2015 address](https://www.pib.gov.in/newsite/PrintRelease.aspx?lang=2&reg=48&relid=126188).
## Cooperation
- Climate/disaster resilience, renewables, health, digital skills, education, maritime resources and fisheries; Fiji diaspora supports links but countries have distinct priorities.
- Distance/connectivity, limited capacity, project delivery and geopolitical competition constrain engagement; not every partner is uniformly debt-trapped.
- Large EEZs do not mean unrestricted mining rights for outsiders; sustainability, sovereignty and relevant marine law matter. Turn pledges into reliable locally requested projects.
