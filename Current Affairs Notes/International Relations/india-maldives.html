International Relations

India–Maldives cooperation

Editable notes

January 2025 defence engagement

  • 8 January 2025, New Delhi: Rajnath Singh and Maldivian Defence Minister Mohamed Ghassan Maumoon; India's readiness for capability support aligned with Maldivian priorities, Neighbourhood First and SAGAR.
  • Defence equipment/stores handed over on request; training, platforms, maritime security and first-responder role. Defence Ministry.
  • Source discusses Ekatha Harbour, MNDF Coast Guard, Sifavaru repair facility, grant assistance and ship refit.

Geography, partnership and interests

  • Low-lying Indian Ocean coral archipelago, ~1,200 islands, near major shipping/energy routes; climate/sea-level and disaster vulnerability.
  • Operation Cactus, 1988: India intervened against attempted coup to support President Gayoom's government. Do not describe that government's democratic credentials uncritically as the source does.
  • Defence/coast-guard capacity, anti-piracy, counterterrorism/radicalisation, humanitarian/disaster response; maritime security serves both countries.
  • Food, construction materials, medicines/petroleum trade; housing, water/sanitation and infrastructure projects; tourism, education/medical travel, diaspora and cultural ties.
  • SAARC partner; India among first to recognise independence 1965, embassy/mission 1972 according to embedded historical graphic.

Political context: use correct dates

  • Yameen-era China ties; relations improved after 2018 change to Solih, rather than universally worsening after that election.
  • Muizzu elected 2023, India Out debate and Indian personnel issue; subsequent rapprochement should not be described as a new leadership change after his election.
  • Maumoon visit here is January 2025, not the text's stray 2024; Muizzu's India engagements in 2024 should not be backdated to 2023. 'China Out' phrase in source is confused.
  • Embedded Yameen-era graphic's 70% debt owed to China, 25,000 Indians, 6% tourists are undated older claims, not January 2025 statistics.
  • Policy approach: respect sovereignty, reliable project delivery, people-to-people benefits and transparent financing while managing strategic competition.
