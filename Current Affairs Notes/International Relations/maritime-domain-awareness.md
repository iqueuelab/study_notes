# Maritime domain awareness and SeaVision

## 30 April 2025: proposed US sale
- US State Department approved a **possible Foreign Military Sale**, notified by **DSCA**, estimated **US$131 million**. Congressional notification/approval is **not proof a final sale, delivery or operational deployment**. [DSCA](https://www.dsca.mil/Press-Media/Major-Arms-Sales/Article-Display/Article/4170460/india-indo-pacific-maritime-domain-awareness).
- Package: **SeaVision software enhancements/documentation, Technical Assistance Field Team training, remote software/analytic support, logistics/programme support**. Principal contractor **HawkEye 360, Herndon, Virginia**, uses space-based radio-frequency analytics; software combines maritime data rather than itself being a ship or weapons platform.
- Supports **Indo-Pacific Partnership for Maritime Domain Awareness (IPMDA)**, announced at the **May 2022 Tokyo Quad summit**; information-sharing across Indian Ocean, Southeast Asia and Pacific Islands. Source's expansion of IPMDA omits “Partnership”.
- Maritime situational awareness supports illegal-fishing, smuggling/piracy detection, safety and coastal/naval response. **Dark shipping** means non-cooperative activity such as AIS disabled; AIS silence alone does not prove illegality, and combining RF/satellite/other data improves assessment without guaranteeing identification.
- India–US capability cooperation and regional public goods support a free/open Indo-Pacific; data access, interoperability, analysis, training and responsible sharing determine actual utility.
