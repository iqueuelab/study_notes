IOS Sagar and Africa–India maritime engagement

Editable notes

Two initiatives announced in March 2025

InitiativeDistinction
IOS SagarMixed-crew deployment of INS Sunayna in south-west Indian Ocean
AIKEYMEAfrica India Key Maritime Engagement exercise, co-hosted with Tanzania at/off Dar es Salaam
  • IOS participants listed: Comoros, Kenya, Madagascar, Maldives, Mauritius, Mozambique, Seychelles, Sri Lanka and South Africa, alongside India. They are nine friendly Indian Ocean countries, not nine African countries—Maldives and Sri Lanka are Asian.
  • Planned April deployment, port calls and joint EEZ surveillance were not completed in March. Training in Kochi supports whole-ship familiarisation/interoperability. Navy announcement.
  • SLOC safety, anti-piracy, illegal-fishing/drug-trafficking response, HADR and capacity building support regional partnerships; joint surveillance respects host arrangements rather than transferring EEZ sovereignty.
  • MAHASAGAR: Mutual and Holistic Advancement for Security and Growth Across Regions; builds on SAGAR's Security and Growth for All in the Region.
  • Cooperation with Africa has its own priorities; it is not simply an extension of Act East or exclusively a response to China. Security provision depends on sustained resources, trust and local needs.

13–14 April 2025: AIKEYME begins

  • The maiden AIKEYME harbour phase was inaugurated 13 April, onboard INS Chennai at Dar es Salaam, by Tanzania's Defence Minister and India's Raksha Rajya Mantri Sanjay Seth. The exercise is co-hosted by India and Tanzania; the announced schedule was 13–18 April 2025. Inauguration, 12 April announcement.
  • Participants: Comoros, Djibouti, Kenya, Madagascar, Mauritius, Mozambique, Seychelles and South Africa, plus co-hosts India and Tanzania. This exercise list differs from the nine foreign countries supplying IOS Sagar personnel.
  • INS Chennai (destroyer), INS Kesari (landing ship tank) and IOS Sagar/INS Sunayna feature in the engagement. Harbour activities support training/planning; sea activities support combined operations, interoperability and responses to shared maritime threats.
  • INS Sunayna sailed from Karwar on 5 April with 44 naval personnel from nine friendly countries. The source's deployment window is 5 April–8 May; at the 14 April issue date, later port visits and EEZ patrols remain planned rather than completed.
  • Announced port calls: Dar es Salaam, Nacala, Port Louis, Port Victoria and Malé; planned joint EEZ surveillance concerns Tanzania, Mozambique, Mauritius and Seychelles. Malé is in Maldives, an Asian island country.
  • Tanzania map: east African Indian Ocean coast; Dodoma is the capital, Dar es Salaam a major port; neighbours shown include Kenya, Uganda, Rwanda, Burundi, DR Congo, Zambia, Malawi and Mozambique; Zanzibar/Pemba lie offshore and Lakes Victoria/Tanganyika are inland.
  • Regional priorities include piracy, trafficking, illegal fishing, maritime terrorism, safe shipping and HADR. Do not reduce African cooperation to Act East or to rivalry with China; shared capacity and regional consent matter.
