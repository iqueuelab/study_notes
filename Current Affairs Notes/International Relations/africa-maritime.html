IOS Sagar and Africa–India maritime engagement

Editable notes

Two initiatives announced in March 2025

InitiativeDistinction
IOS SagarMixed-crew deployment of INS Sunayna in south-west Indian Ocean
AIKEYMEAfrica India Key Maritime Engagement exercise, co-hosted with Tanzania at/off Dar es Salaam
  • IOS participants listed: Comoros, Kenya, Madagascar, Maldives, Mauritius, Mozambique, Seychelles, Sri Lanka and South Africa, alongside India. They are nine friendly Indian Ocean countries, not nine African countries—Maldives and Sri Lanka are Asian.
  • Planned April deployment, port calls and joint EEZ surveillance were not completed in March. Training in Kochi supports whole-ship familiarisation/interoperability. Navy announcement.
  • SLOC safety, anti-piracy, illegal-fishing/drug-trafficking response, HADR and capacity building support regional partnerships; joint surveillance respects host arrangements rather than transferring EEZ sovereignty.
  • MAHASAGAR: Mutual and Holistic Advancement for Security and Growth Across Regions; builds on SAGAR's Security and Growth for All in the Region.
  • Cooperation with Africa has its own priorities; it is not simply an extension of Act East or exclusively a response to China. Security provision depends on sustained resources, trust and local needs.
