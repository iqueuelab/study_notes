# India–Australia defence policy cooperation

## Ninth Defence Policy Talks
- Held in New Delhi on **17 March 2025**; covered maritime awareness, information-sharing, industry/science cooperation and interoperability. [Defence Ministry account](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2111839&lang=2&reg=48).
- The 2020 Mutual Logistics Support Arrangement enables agreed support/access; it does not grant unlimited ownership of each other's bases.
- Malabar/Quad participation differs from **AUKUS**, of which India is not a member.
- ECTA became operational in 2022; broader CECA negotiations are a separate process. Procurement ideas and cooperation proposals are not completed hardware contracts.
- Balance maritime security and technology opportunities with strategic autonomy, domestic capacity and regulatory safeguards.
