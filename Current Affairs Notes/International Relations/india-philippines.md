# India–Philippines: geography and strategic partnership

## 5 August 2025 strategic partnership
- During **President Ferdinand Marcos Jr.'s India visit**, both countries elevated relations to a **Strategic Partnership**, with **2025–2029 Plan of Action**. Priorities include defence/maritime security, trade, connectivity and technology; talks toward a preferential trade agreement and direct flights are initiatives, not proof a completed FTA or all flights already operating.
- Embedded clipping mentions first joint naval exercise with three visiting Indian ships and hydrographic cooperation; **freedom of navigation**, HADR and search/rescue are shared priorities. Philippines is the first overseas BrahMos customer (**2022 contract**, deliveries from 2024), distinct from new August procurement.
- [Philippine presidential announcement](https://pco.gov.ph/news_releases/philippines-india-forge-strategic-partnership-through-landmark-agreements/), [Indian economic-diplomacy account](https://indbiz.gov.in/india-philippines-advance-trade-and-strategic-ties-with-preferential-trade-framework/).
## Map recall
- Southeast Asian archipelago, western Pacific, **7,000+ islands**; **Luzon north (Manila), Visayas central, Mindanao south**. Presidential republic; unitary Philippines differs from the **federal US**, despite the source analogy.
- **Philippine Sea east, South China Sea west/northwest, Sulu Sea southwest, Celebes Sea south**. **Mount Apo, Mindanao (~2,954 m)** highest; **Cagayan, Luzon** longest river; **Agusan/Mindanao rivers** in the south. Tropical maritime/monsoonal climate varies regionally; June–November rainy/December–May dry is a broad pattern, not uniform for every island.
- US mutual defence treaty and maritime disputes explain Indo-Pacific salience. **2016 South China Sea award** was by an **UNCLOS Annex VII arbitral tribunal with PCA as registry**, not a PCA court awarding sovereignty over all disputed islands/seas; distinguish maritime entitlements from territorial sovereignty.
- [PCA 2016 award announcement](https://pca-cpa.org/en/news/pca-press-release-the-south-china-sea-arbitration-the-republic-of-the-philippines-v-the-peoples-republic-of-china/).
