# Osaka World Expo 2025 and Indian cultural diplomacy

## Expo and pavilion
- **13 April–13 October 2025**, Osaka/Kansai, Japan; theme **“Designing Future Society for Our Lives”**. World Expo organised as a six-month international exhibition, ordinarily on a five-year cycle; not every specialised Expo follows the same timing. “Living Lab”/co-creation approach links innovation and SDGs.
- India pavilion curated by **IGNCA**, autonomous Culture Ministry institution, narrates heritage/spiritual philosophy alongside contemporary scientific ambitions, inclusivity and sustainability. **Padmapani Bodhisattva from Ajanta**, lotus/Bodhi-tree imagery evoke compassion/mindfulness; the image is a cultural reference, not evidence Ajanta is in Japan. [Official pavilion](https://www.expo2025.or.jp/en/official-participant/india/).
- Source highlights space achievements and describes IGNCA as **sutradhar**, storyteller. Clipping **13,141 single-day visitors** is a dated footfall snapshot, not total Expo attendance. Curatorial responsibility differs from every construction/engineering contract.
## Soft power
- **Joseph Nye:** influence through attraction, culture/values and credible policy, distinct from coercion. Art, philosophy, cultural exchange, sustainability and scientific achievement can support international confidence.
- Exhibitions create contact/opportunity, not proof attitudes, investment or diplomacy changed. Assess accessible narration, plural heritage, contemporary relevance, visitor engagement and sustained partnerships.
