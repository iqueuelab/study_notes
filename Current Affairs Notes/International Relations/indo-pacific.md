# Indo-Pacific: geography, interests and cooperation

## Concept and map

- Connects **Indian and Pacific Ocean** strategic/economic systems; boundaries differ by country. India's broad vision extends from **African shores to the Americas**, rather than one universally fixed treaty region.
- **Hormuz**, Persian Gulf approach; **Malacca**, Indian Ocean–South China Sea route; sea lanes carry energy/trade. **South China Sea is a sea, not a strait/chokepoint**.
- Source **60% maritime trade / 60% GDP / two-thirds growth** are broad region/period-dependent estimates, not comparable exact annual shares without definitions.

## Issues

- US–China competition, Chinese maritime claims/artificial-island activity and BRI influence; diverse countries' interests cannot be reduced to identical blocs.
- South China Sea claims involve **China, Vietnam, Philippines, Malaysia, Brunei**, with **Taiwan** also relevant; East China Sea **Senkaku/Diaoyu** dispute. Maritime rights/navigation governed by applicable international law, not merely influence over trade routes.
- Piracy, illegal fishing, arms/drug trafficking; climate hazards, sea-level rise, coral loss and marine pollution; militarisation and unequal infrastructure/capacity.

## India and regional mechanisms

- **Act East, ASEAN centrality, free/open/inclusive cooperation**, strategic autonomy and international law; security alongside connectivity, blue economy and disaster resilience. [India's 2018 policy address](https://www.pib.gov.in/newsite/PrintRelease.aspx?lang=2&reg=48&relid=179711).

| Mechanism | Distinction |
| --- | --- |
| **Quad** | India, USA, Japan, Australia; partnership, not a formal defence alliance |
| **AUKUS** | Australia, UK, USA; India is not a member |
| **IPEF** | Economic cooperation framework, not identical to a free-trade agreement |
| **IONS / Malabar** | Maritime dialogue / naval exercise, respectively |

- **Andaman–Nicobar Command**, naval capacity, partner exercises; island partnerships **Sri Lanka, Maldives, Seychelles**. **Sagarmala** domestic port-led development; **Chabahar** Iran connectivity project—different functions/locations.
- Balance partnerships, protect commerce and support smaller States' choices; technology/finance/infrastructure, readiness and environmental cooperation matter as much as declarations.
