# India–Ghana relations and Volta geography

## July visit and bilateral background
- **2–3 July visit:** Modi received **Officer of the Order of the Star of Ghana** from President John Dramani Mahama and addressed Parliament. The clipping emphasises Global South cooperation, UN/global-governance reform and African Union participation in G20; historical bonds do not remove present trade/development challenges.
- Representative office **1953**, full diplomatic ties **1957**, shared Nehru–Nkrumah/NAM legacy. Ghana was a pioneer of **sub-Saharan African decolonisation in 1957**, not the first independent African country in all history.
- Source's “Kalam 2004/Vice-President 2016” visits are mistaken: **President Pranab Mukherjee made the first Indian presidential visit in June 2016**. Avoid using the source's undated~US$2 bn trade/~10,000 diaspora as 2025 totals: 11 June official brief gives **FY 2024–25 US$3.137 bn trade/15,000+ Indian community**. [High Commission brief](https://audit.hciaccra.gov.in/index.php/pages/Nzg2).
- India exports pharmaceuticals, vehicles, machinery; imports gold, cocoa, timber/cashew products. Lines of credit, water/power/agriculture, ITEC/ICCR scholarships and Pan-African e-network/e-VBAB education/health links support cooperation; defence training, peacekeeping and UN/NAM/Commonwealth engagement add strategic depth.
## Map and resources
- **Tema/Takoradi ports** serve Atlantic/Gulf of Guinea trade; gateway routes extend to Burkina Faso and other landlocked States—Ghana does not directly border Mali/Niger. **Kwahu Plateau/Gambaga Scarp** are physical features.
- **Lake Volta, Akosombo Dam; Black Volta, White Volta, Oti:** hydropower, inland transport, fisheries/irrigation. Hydropower is important, but do not assume it is the majority of Ghana's current electricity without a dated generation mix.
- **Accra coastal capital; Kumasi Ashanti cultural/trade centre; Tamale northern hub**. Gold in southern/central Ashanti/Western areas; cocoa forest belt; oil offshore western coast. Map also marks Mole, Bui, Digya, Kakum and Bia protected areas and Mt Afadjato; country/resource geography differs from the location of every mine.
