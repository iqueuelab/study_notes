## Semiconductor ecosystem: opportunities and limits

- Design → wafer fabrication → assembly/testing/packaging: each needs distinct investment/skills. Singapore experience, financing, supplier networks and training can help; Taiwan's foundry leadership must not be equated with China's position in every chip segment.
- Reliable electricity, water, clean-room quality, specialised workforce, R&D and large long-term capital; chips support autos, telecom, AI/IoT and defence. **US$5-trillion economy** is an ambition, not a guarantee delivered by one sector.
- Source eastern focus **Odisha/Assam** logistics/petrochemicals/skills is potential collaboration, not proof approved fabs in every mentioned State. Green energy/storage and efficiency reduce production footprint.
- Risks: global-cycle demand, imported equipment/IP, geopolitical disruptions, infrastructure and trade barriers. Transparent incentives, partnerships and technology capability milestones; indigenous capability does not require isolated supply chains.
