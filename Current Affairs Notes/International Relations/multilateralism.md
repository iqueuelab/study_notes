# Multilateralism, international law and unilateralism

## Institutional distinctions
- Multilateral institutions coordinate health, climate, peace, human rights and trade; unilateral withdrawals, funding cuts and sanctions can weaken collective capacity.
- In March 2025, US WHO/Paris exit processes and institutional disengagement had different legal timelines. A withdrawal instruction is not instant completion of every treaty procedure.
- **The US was not a Rome Statute party**, so ICC sanctions are not withdrawal from ICC membership. UN Human Rights Council disengagement also differs from leaving the UN.
- A proposed US DEFUND Bill is not enacted withdrawal. UN headquarters, contributions and peacekeeping effects are scenarios conditional on actual legal action.
- WTO Appellate Body vacancies impede appeals; this does not mean all WTO rules or every dispute procedure vanished.
- Smoot–Hawley tariffs (1930) illustrate escalation risks, but cannot alone explain the Great Depression or World War II.
- India can advocate institutional reform, peaceful dispute settlement, climate/health cooperation and representative global governance; US retreat does not automatically secure India a permanent UNSC seat.
