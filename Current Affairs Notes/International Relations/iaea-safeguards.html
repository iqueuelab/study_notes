International Relations

IAEA: safeguards, nuclear cooperation and India

Editable notes

Institution and limits

  • 1957, Vienna; independent international organisation related to UN, “Atoms for Peace and Development”; 2005 Nobel Peace Prize jointly with Mohamed El Baradei. Peaceful nuclear science supports energy, health, agriculture and water.
  • Safeguards verify non-diversion of nuclear material under applicable agreements; safety protects against accidents/radiation, security against malicious acts. These are related but distinct functions.
  • Reports to UNGA; safeguards non-compliance can be reported to UNSC. Membership alone does not allow inspection of every military/civil facility; mandate depends on agreements and authority.
  • Source clipping reports India’s call to put Pakistan’s nuclear arsenal under IAEA oversight following conflict. This is a diplomatic proposal, not evidence Pakistan’s weapons became subject to inspection or a nuclear accident was established.
  • India: IAEA member, not NPT party; civilian separation and India-specific safeguards enable international civil-nuclear cooperation. Additional Protocol increases agreed information/access duties; not automatic NSG membership or inspection of military installations.
  • DPRK: IAEA has had no inspectors there since 2009 ; remote monitoring/assessment must not be described as regular current onsite inspection. IAEA 2009 safeguards statement.

PYQ revision

  • 2020 : answer(b), imported versus domestic uranium, in the question’s intended civil-cooperation context. Actual safeguards status follows designated facilities/material agreements; domestic fuel does not automatically exempt an already safeguarded reactor.
  • 2018 : answer(a), civilian-reactor safeguards. India’s specific Additional Protocol obligations must not be generalised from the broad model protocol for NPT comprehensive-safeguards States.

UN status: practice distinction

  • IAEA is an autonomous organisation related to the UN, not a UN specialised agency. Its verification/reporting differs from Security Council enforcement; the undated worksheet’s correct statements are 2 and 3, answer (c). Nobel and reporting facts are recorded above. IAEA/UN relationship.

Institutional recall

  • IAEA Statute approved 23 October 1956 and entered into force 29 July 1957. Its annual General Conference sets policy/budget; the 35-member Board of Governors handles major decisions and safeguards; the Secretariat runs implementation.
  • 2020 PYQ: (b) remains the imported/domestic uranium distinction intended by the question. Do not infer all domestically fuelled civil facilities are exempt, or that NPCIL operates every Indian research or breeder installation.
