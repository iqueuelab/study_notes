International Relations

WHO, US withdrawal and global health governance

Editable notes

January 2025 development

  • 20 January 2025, newly inaugurated President Donald Trump initiated US withdrawal; he was not a former president acting outside office. WHO's 21 January statement sought reconsideration. Announcement of intent ≠ completed withdrawal that day. WHO statement.
  • Source ~18% US funding is a dated donor-share comparison, not a permanent fraction of every biennial budget. Assessed contributions and voluntary, often earmarked funds differ.

Why it matters

  • Financing/service disruptions for HIV, TB, malaria and lower-income health systems; surveillance, outbreak data and coordinated response weaken when major partners disengage.
  • Pathogens cross borders; domestic health security depends on international cooperation. Withdrawal reduces influence over reform and may increase fragmentation.
  • Alleged political bias/inefficiency should be assessed with evidence. Editorial advocates reform: transparent decisions, faster operations and predictable less-earmarked finance, reducing donor dependence.
  • Mains balance: national sovereignty/accountability concerns versus global public goods, equitable access and collective health security.
