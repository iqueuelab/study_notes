# International Organization for Migration

## Institution

- Founded **1951**, headquarters **Geneva, Switzerland**; intergovernmental migration organisation, **UN related organisation**. Not interchangeable with UNHCR's refugee-protection mandate.
- Humane/orderly migration, cooperation, practical migration management and humanitarian assistance to migrants, including displaced/refugee populations where relevant.
- Coordinates **UN Network on Migration**; source says 'coordinator for Secretary', which should not be memorised as the formal role.
- Labour mobility/human development work: policy and operations, diaspora-development links and integration. Supported **Global Compact for Safe, Orderly and Regular Migration, 2018**; the compact is not a binding refugee convention.
- Source membership **175 members, 8 observers** is an undated snapshot; membership changes. Publications: **World Migration Report**, **Migration Health Annual Report**.

## January 2025 Syria appeal

- Expanded earlier **$30 million** appeal to **$73.2 million**, response period **December 2024–May 2025**; humanitarian funding requested, not money already received. [IOM flash appeal](https://mena.iom.int/sites/g/files/tmzbdl686/files/documents/2025-01/iom-flash-appeal-for-syria-dec-2024-may-2025.pdf).
- Mains: reconcile dignity/protection, host capacity, safe mobility, integration and international burden sharing.
