# Kartarpur Sahib Corridor: faith and diplomacy

## Corridor and agreement
- Links **Dera Baba Nanak, Gurdaspur (Punjab)** with **Gurdwara Darbar Sahib, Kartarpur, Narowal (Pakistan)**, near the Ravi and about **4.5 km from the border**. Kartarpur is on the Pakistani/western side of Ravi; distinguish shrine distance from the full corridor route.
- Agreement signed **24 October 2019**, initially five years; **renewed October 2024 for another five years**. The source's initial duration is incomplete for May 2025. [Pakistan Foreign Ministry renewal](https://mofa.gov.pk/press-releases/renewal-of-the-kartarpur-corridor-agreement).
- Visa-free same-day pilgrimage for **Indian citizens and OCI cardholders**, regardless of faith, under documentation/security requirements; **visa-free does not mean passport/document-free**. Source fee **US$20 per visit**; Pakistani pilgrims do not obtain reciprocal visa-free entry to Dera Baba Nanak through this arrangement.
## Heritage and diplomacy
- Guru Nanak spent approximately his final **18 years** at Kartarpur; shrine marks his final resting place. Hymn composition, community life and **langar** express Sikh values: **Kirat Karo** honest work; **Vand Chhako** sharing; **Naam Japo** remembrance of the divine.
- Rare India–Pakistan confidence-building measure: religious tourism, people-to-people contact, cultural diplomacy and interfaith participation. Security screening, fee burden, lack of reciprocity and bilateral crises constrain access; treaty renewal does not guarantee continuous physical operation.
- Pilgrimage map: **Nankana Sahib** (Pakistan), Guru Nanak's birthplace; **Ber Sahib, Sultanpur Lodhi**; **Akal Takht/Harimandir Sahib, Amritsar**; **Keshgarh Sahib, Anandpur Sahib**; **Damdama Sahib, Talwandi Sabo**; **Patna Sahib, Bihar**; **Hazur Sahib, Nanded, Maharashtra**. Distinguish the five Takhts from other important gurdwaras.
