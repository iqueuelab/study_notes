India–Trinidad and Tobago: Girmitiyas and diaspora diplomacy

Editable notes

July 2025 diaspora engagement

  • PM's 3–4 July 2025 official visit was an in-person bilateral visit, not merely the PDF's virtual outreach. 30 May2025 marked 180 years since first Indian arrivals in1845; conferred Order of the Republic of Trinidad and Tobago, its highest national honour. Joint statement.
  • Girmitiya, from “agreement”: Indian indentured plantation labourers taken to colonies after abolition of slavery, principally from Bihar/eastern UP in this Caribbean context. Destinations included Trinidad, Guyana, Mauritius, Fiji and South Africa; indenture involved exploitative conditions, not ordinary free migration.
  • Diaspora framed as Rashtradoots, cultural ambassadors: language, yoga, festivals, heritage, business and people-to-people ties add strategic value alongside remittances. PBD, Know India Programme, OCI and scholarships illustrate engagement.
  • Announced OCI eligibility up to sixth-generation Indian-origin persons in Trinidad and Tobago, extending the earlier fourth-generation limit for this community. OCI is a foreign-national status with specified privileges, not dual citizenship or unrestricted property/political rights; eligibility/registration conditions still apply.
  • Mapping the Past initiative described in the speech: document ancestral villages, settlements and contributions of Girmitiya communities, with regular World Girmitiya Conferences proposed. Archives and genealogy preserve historical memory; proposals are not proof an exhaustive database already exists.
  • Joint statement welcomes Trinidad and Tobago as the first Caribbean country to adopt UPI; implementation/merchant access differs from a fully operational universal cross-border remittance system. Supports digital public infrastructure diplomacy and diaspora/business links.
