# UNHCR and refugee protection

## Institution and mandate
- **United Nations High Commissioner for Refugees**, established by UNGA **1950**, began work **1951** following European WWII displacement; headquarters **Geneva, Switzerland**. Nobel Peace Prize **1954 and 1981**.
- Protection/legal assistance, shelter and other emergency aid, government capacity and durable solutions: **voluntary repatriation, local integration, third-country resettlement**. Works with refugees, stateless people and displaced populations according to relevant mandates; not every internally displaced person automatically has refugee status.
- **1951 Refugee Convention/1967 Protocol:** international refugee framework. India is party to **neither**, but cooperates with UNHCR. Refugee recognition/documentation by UNHCR and domestic immigration status are distinct.
- Funding is **almost entirely voluntary**, mainly governments plus individuals/private donors; a small UN regular-budget contribution makes the source's “entirely” incorrect. [UNHCR funding](https://www.unhcr.org/us/about-unhcr/planning-funding-and-results), [regular-budget evidence](https://reporting.unhcr.org/global-report-2023/income).
- Syria, Afghanistan, Rohingya and Ukraine illustrate different crises. Funding shortfalls, host-country political resistance, uncertain legal identity and protracted displacement complicate protection. Distinguish statelessness from merely lacking an identity document.
