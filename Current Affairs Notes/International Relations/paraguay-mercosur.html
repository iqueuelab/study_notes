Paraguay, MERCOSUR and India’s Latin American engagement

Editable notes

June 2025 visit and trade

  • Santiago Peña Palacios, Paraguay President, began first State Visit to India 2–4 June 2025. Diplomatic ties 1961; resident Indian Embassy in Asunción opened 2022. Discussed digital technology, critical minerals, energy, agriculture, health, defence, railways and space; proposed collaboration is not proof signed extraction projects or completed technology transfers. MEA arrival.
  • MERCOSUR = Mercado Común del Sur, founded Treaty of Asunción, 1991; founders Argentina, Brazil, Paraguay, Uruguay. Bolivia became a State Party in 2024, with a transition to incorporate bloc rules; the source's pending-ratification label is stale. Venezuela suspended, not an active participating member. Secretariat Montevideo, Common Market Council includes foreign/economy ministers. Official membership.
  • India–MERCOSUR framework 17 June 2003; PTA signed 25 January 2004; entered into force 1 June 2009, with negotiated annexes finalised later. PTA means selected preferences, not comprehensive FTA/free movement with India. WTO treaty record.
  • Diversify exports/markets, South–South cooperation and supply partnerships; tariff/non-tariff barriers, shipping/logistics and internal bloc differences matter. The source's GDP/population totals are dated and membership-dependent; Paraguay's supposed universally neutral foreign policy or readily available critical-mineral capacity is not established by a visit.

Paraguay map

  • Landlocked South America; neighbours Brazil east/northeast, Argentina south/southwest, Bolivia northwest, not Chile. Asunción on east bank of Paraguay River; river divides populous Oriental east from more sparsely populated Gran Chaco west.
  • Paraná River: borders with Brazil and Argentina in respective stretches; Itaipu is Paraguay–Brazil hydropower project, Yacyretá Paraguay–Argentina. Do not place Itaipu on an Argentina-owned boundary merely because another Paraná stretch borders Argentina.
  • River navigation links landlocked trade to neighbours/Atlantic; hydropower, soybean, beef and forestry feature in economy. Lake Ypacaraí near Asunción. Geographic partnership does not grant India automatic tariff-free access to the whole bloc.

Map PYQ revision

  • 2025 Andes: (c), four among listed—Bolivia, Colombia, Ecuador, Venezuela; not Brazil or Paraguay. Andes extend through western South America, also Peru, Chile and Argentina.
