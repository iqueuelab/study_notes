November–December 2025: regional security architecture

  • Seventh NSA-level meeting, New Delhi, 20 November 2025: India, Sri Lanka, Maldives, Mauritius and Bangladesh participated as member States; Seychelles attended as an observer and its accession as a full member was welcomed; Malaysia was a guest, not automatically a member. Distinguish attendance status from the accession outcome. Government account of the meeting.
  • Roots in 2011 India–Sri Lanka–Maldives maritime-security dialogue, revived/expanded under CSC from 2020; Mauritius joined 2022, Bangladesh 2024. Cooperating littoral/island States have different threat perceptions and economic relationships; CSC is not a treaty alliance against China.
  • Five pillars: maritime safety/security; terrorism/radicalisation; trafficking and transnational organised crime; cybersecurity/critical-infrastructure protection; humanitarian assistance/disaster relief. The PDF's four-item shorthand omits HADR.
  • Correct the claim ‘no permanent secretariat’: Charter and MoU establishing the CSC Secretariat were signed in Colombo on 30 August 2024. Institutional continuity and implementation can still be strengthened, but recommendations should acknowledge existing founding documents. Indian High Commission, Colombo.
  • Fishing, ports, tourism and sea-borne trade link security with livelihoods and blue-economy development. Shared information, training, exercises and incident cooperation address piracy, narcotics, trafficking, cyber threats and disasters. Diverse priorities, domestic instability and overlapping IORA/IONS/Quad/BIMSTEC agendas require consent and practical coordination. The unyearred 250-word/15-mark ‘possible Mains’ prompt is practice, not an UPSC PYQ.
