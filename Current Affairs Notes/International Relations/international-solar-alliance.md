# International Solar Alliance: solar deployment and global cooperation

## Research-capacity plans in August 2025
- Embedded report: ISA planned **17 centres of excellence in as many countries by year-end**, with a proposed **Global Capability Centre in India** to support testing, laboratories, training and start-up ecosystems. A later expansion toward **50 centres** was an ambition, not an already operational network; source cites July 2025 Indian solar capacity of **~119 GW** as a dated background figure.
- **India–France initiative launched at COP21, Paris, in 2015**; intergovernmental, member-driven solar cooperation platform, headquarters at **NISE campus, Gurugram**. Deployment, finance, affordable technology, skills and equitable energy access are linked goals.
- Membership initially covered countries wholly/partly between the Tropics. Amendment **adopted 3 October 2018**, **effective 15 July 2020**, opened eligibility to **all UN members**; eligibility does not mean every UN member joined. Source's conflicting 110+/90 counts are stale/undated and not a verified August tally. [Official amendment account](https://www.indembassysweden.gov.in/section/press-releases/universalization-of-the-membership-of-the-international-solar-alliance-isa/).
- **Towards 1000 by 2030**: mobilise **US$1,000 billion**, solar access for **1,000 million people**, **1,000 GW** solar capacity and **1,000 million tonnes/year** CO2 mitigation as strategic ambitions, not achieved outputs.
- Assembly is the apex decision-making body; first Assembly **2018, Greater Noida**. Secretariat/Director General implement decisions. Source's “Steering Committee” label should not replace the Framework Agreement's **Standing Committee**. Policy analytics/advocacy, enabling activities, capacity building and project support connect governance with delivery.
- **STAR-C** technology/application resource centres, **ITEC training**, solar fellowships and **One Sun, One World, One Grid** are relevant initiatives. OSOWOG envisions interconnected grids for renewable transfer across time zones, not continuous sunshine everywhere or a completed global grid.
- **2016 ISA PYQ: A (1 only)**; launch at the 2015 climate conference is correct, universal UN membership false. Source four-statement practice **A (1, 2 and 3)**.
