# India–Qatar: strategic partnership and energy ties

## Background and February 2025 agreements

- Qatar independent **1971**; diplomatic relations with India **1973**. Emir **Sheikh Tamim bin Hamad Al-Thani** visited India **17–18 February 2025**; ties elevated to a **strategic partnership**.
- Aim to **double trade to US$28 billion over five years**; revised **DTAA** signed; prospective trade talks do not mean an FTA was concluded. [Joint communiqué](https://mofa.gov.qa/en/latest-articles/statements/qatar--india-issue-joint-communique-on-occasion-of-hh-the-amir%27s-visit).
- Source approximately US$15 billion trade differs from official FY2023–24 **about US$14 billion**; doubling target uses the latter scale, not exactly 15→28.

## Why Qatar matters

- Major **LNG supplier**, long-term Petronet arrangements, crude/petrochemical/fertiliser imports; Indian machinery, textiles, food, jewellery and services exports.
- Qatari investment in infrastructure/finance; Indian construction/IT firms; energy supply diversification must accompany long-term dependence.
- Large Indian diaspora: source **~7.5 lakh /over 20 schools /~600 prisoners** are dated figures; labour protection, consular access and recruitment safeguards matter more than memorising an undated total.
- Security: naval exchanges, maritime cooperation, counter-terrorism/cybersecurity; Qatar's mediation role and GCC membership connect to India's West Asia diplomacy.
- Ex-naval officer **Purnendu Tiwary's return issue** remained a diplomatic concern in the source; distinguish release from custody and permission to leave Qatar rather than assuming continued imprisonment.
- Balance energy/investment gains with expatriate welfare, geopolitical volatility and due process; **GCC comprises Bahrain, Kuwait, Oman, Qatar, Saudi Arabia and UAE**.
