# Colombia: geography and Belt and Road cooperation

## May 2025 cooperation and map
- Colombia signed a **Belt and Road cooperation plan with China during President Gustavo Petro’s May visit**. Infrastructure/finance opportunity and US–China influence competition form the context; signing does not mean all promised projects are financed/completed. [Colombian Foreign Ministry plan](https://www.cancilleria.gov.co/en/node/30761).
- **Northwest South America**, capital **Bogotá**, Spanish, Colombian peso; borders **Panama, Venezuela, Brazil, Peru, Ecuador**. Caribbean north and Pacific west: the only South American State with both these coasts; Caribbean is part of Atlantic.
- Andes split into three cordilleras; Amazon region; **Magdalena River**, **Putumayo River** along part of Peru border. Bogotá eastern Andes; Medellín industrial hub; Cali salsa; Cartagena/Barranquilla Caribbean ports/culture.
- Pacific Alliance participation and anti-narcotics cooperation matter. Source’s UNASUR membership is date-sensitive and should not be assumed continuously operative. Pan-American route has the **Darién Gap** between Panama/Colombia rather than an uninterrupted motor highway.
- BRI partnerships should be assessed for viability, debt/transparency, environmental effects and strategic autonomy; geography does not by itself establish alignment against the US.
