# India–Sri Lanka relations

## Links and cooperation
- Maritime neighbours across Palk Strait; cultural/Buddhist/diaspora ties, trade, tourism, investment and development cooperation.
- India provided~US$4 billion support during 2022 economic crisis; financial assistance, financing assurances and debt restructuring have different roles.
- India–Sri Lanka FTA operational 2000; ETCA discussions are not proof agreement is already implemented. UPI-enabled tourist payments differ from replacing Sri Lanka's currency/payment system.
- BIMSTEC/SAARC and maritime cooperation; strengthen infrastructure, training, education and reliable project delivery.
## Frictions
- Fisher arrests, destructive bottom trawling and livelihoods; 1974/1976 maritime agreements/Katchatheevu questions require precise rights distinctions, not assumed general fishing permission.
- Hambantota/Chinese engagement, smuggling and regional competition; Sri Lanka retains sovereign choices, rather than being an Indian “point of control”.
- Dated tourism/export shares should not be treated as current permanent rankings. Work on sustainable fishing, legal channels and broader people-to-people ties.
## Fisheries dispute
- **1974 Palk Strait and 1976 Gulf of Mannar/Bay of Bengal agreements** are maritime-boundary anchors. Cross-border fishing is not an unrestricted traditional entitlement.
- Bottom trawling damages seabed habitats and Northern Sri Lankan fishers' livelihoods; Sri Lanka banned it in 2017. Tamil Nadu's nearshore restriction concerns mechanised vessels, not a blanket ban on all fishers within three nautical miles.
- Combine bilateral Joint Working Group/ministers' dialogue, humane treatment/release processes, enforceable sustainable fishing and economically viable alternatives.
- Deep-sea transition needs vessel finance, training, fuel/logistics and market access; it is not automatically affordable or ecologically sustainable.

## Katchatheevu: geography and rights
- Small, uninhabited island in the **Palk Strait**, between Tamil Nadu and northern Sri Lanka; St Anthony's shrine sustains shared pilgrimage/fisher-community ties. Avoid the unsupported claim that it was formed by volcanic activity.
- **1974 maritime delimitation** placed Katchatheevu on the Sri Lankan side; **1976 agreements** further defined boundaries and cross-border fishing restrictions.
- India's official position treats sovereignty/boundary as settled. Political/legal challenges to the agreements are distinct from that executive position; do not describe every constitutional question as already finally adjudicated.
- Government statements distinguish traditional access for **resting, drying nets and pilgrimage** from a right to fish in Sri Lankan waters. Access to the island is not an unrestricted fishing licence. [Official parliamentary answer](https://rsdebate.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/235467/1/PQ_162_23031992_U3277_p84_p85.pdf), [MEA account](https://meacms.mea.gov.in/Images/pdf/India-foreign-relation-2011.pdf).
- Solutions require sustainable fishing and protection of livelihoods on both shores: bilateral dialogue, humane handling of detainees, viable vessel/skill support and alternatives to destructive bottom trawling. Proposed joint-fishing arrangements require agreement; they are not existing blanket permission.

## Cyclone Ditwah: Operation Sagar Bandhu, November 2025
- **Operation Sagar Bandhu began 28 November 2025**, after Cyclone Ditwah caused flooding, landslides and infrastructure damage. India redirected **INS Vikrant** and **INS Udaygiri/Udayagiri**, already in Sri Lanka for an International Fleet Review, for humanitarian assistance; the source also reports a request for shipborne aviation support.
- The initial High Commission account records **4.5 tonnes of dry rations + 2 tonnes of fresh rations**, with other survival essentials. These are an initial tranche, not final totals for the whole mission. Vikrant is India's first indigenous aircraft carrier; Udaygiri is a stealth frigate. [High Commission, 28 November](https://www.hcicolombo.gov.in/section-detail.php?id=1operation-sagar-bandhu-india-extends-immediate-humanitarian-relief-to-sri-lanka-amid-cyclone-ditwah&pageid=press-releases).
- **Neighbourhood First** and regional first-response capacity connect relief to diplomacy. **MAHASAGAR**—Mutual and Holistic Advancement for Security and Growth Across Regions—was announced in Mauritius in **March 2025**, not the PDF's March 2024; it broadens the **2015 SAGAR** vision. [March 2025 announcement](https://www.hcimauritius.gov.in/speeches_detail/?id=17).
- Keep similarly named operations distinct: SAGAR relief missions supplied Indian Ocean partners; **Dost** earthquake aid to Türkiye/Syria; **Vanilla** Madagascar disaster relief; **Neer** Maldives water-crisis relief; **Samudra Setu** COVID-era evacuation. Humanitarian assistance requires host-country coordination; naval capability is not proof of unilateral intervention rights.
