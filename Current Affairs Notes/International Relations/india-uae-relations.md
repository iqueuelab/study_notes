# India–UAE relations

## Partnership trajectory
- Diplomatic relations established **1972**. The PM’s **2015** visit set the decision to elevate ties; the Comprehensive Strategic Partnership agreement was signed **January 2017**. [2017 agreement list](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=1514426&lang=2&reg=48).
- **CEPA:** signed February 2022, effective May 2022; tariff reductions, market access, services and investment cooperation. USD 100 billion was a trade-expansion target, not proof of achievement in every reporting year.
- April source describes UAE as India’s third-largest trading partner and reports roughly USD 20–21 billion investment, including USD 15.5 billion FDI. These are source-period estimates; cumulative FDI and total investment are different measures and rankings vary by financial year.
## Cooperation map
| Area | Institutions, initiatives and significance |
| --- | --- |
| Trade | CEPA supports textiles, gems/jewellery, pharmaceuticals and diversified commerce |
| Payments | UPI–AANI instant-payment linkage and RuPay–JAYWAN cooperation; local INR–AED settlement reduces transaction friction |
| Energy | Crude, LNG and LPG supply; upstream investment, strategic reserves and renewables diversify energy security |
| Defence | Joint Defence Cooperation Committee; Desert Flag air and Desert Cyclone land exercises; IDEX/NAVDEX participation |
| Education | Indian curriculum schools; IIT Delhi–Abu Dhabi campus supports higher-education internationalisation |
| Space | ISRO–UAE Space Agency cooperation for peaceful space use; research and technology links |
| People | Large Indian diaspora, worker welfare, remittances and cultural links; BAPS temple in Abu Dhabi inaugurated in 2024 |
- **Payments caution:** agreements to interlink systems do not establish universal operational availability. RBI’s July 2023 MoU covered UPI–UAE Instant Payment Platform, RuPay–UAESWITCH, and exploration of messaging-system linkage; subsequent February 2024 agreements named AANI and JAYWAN. [RBI](https://rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_PressReleaseDisplay.aspx?prid=56054), [Parliament implementation-status reply](https://sansad.in/getFile/loksabhaquestions/annex/183/AU901_eqt2zW.pdf?source=pqals).
## Regional and multilateral links
- **I2U2:** India, Israel, UAE, USA; joint investment in water, energy, transportation, space, health and food security.
- **India–France–UAE trilateral:** Indo-Pacific cooperation, maritime security, clean energy and wider strategic coordination. **BRICS:** South–South engagement; UAE joined in 2024.
- **IMEC:** proposed India–Middle East–Europe connectivity through Gulf and European partners; announced 2023. Potential alternative trade routes and transparent infrastructure are strategic motivations, but the corridor is not a completed transport network.
- Source estimates diaspora above 3.5 million and around 35% of population; treat population shares as dated estimates, not permanent ratios.
- Strategic value: energy and export diversification, secure sea lanes, diaspora protection and partnerships in a multipolar order. Constraints: regional conflict, corridor implementation, commercial viability and coordination among multiple partners.

Retain **2017** as the formal Comprehensive Strategic Partnership agreement date, despite the PDF's 2015 shorthand.
