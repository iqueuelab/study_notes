India–Morocco defence cooperation and map recall

Editable notes

Defence cooperation MoU: 22 September 2025

  • Rajnath Singh and Abdeltif Loudiyi met in Rabat and signed a defence-cooperation MoU; India announced a Defence Wing in its Rabat embassy. Defence Ministry record.
  • Framework: defence industry, training, exercises and capacity building; counter-terrorism, maritime/cyber security, peacekeeping, military medicine and expert exchanges. Co-development/production and drones/counter-drone opportunities are cooperation intentions, not proof concluded export orders or a mutual-defence alliance.
  • Links India's defence manufacturing and Africa engagement with Moroccan capabilities and Atlantic/Mediterranean access. Measure implementation through training, contracts, deliveries and interoperability rather than MoU rhetoric alone.

Morocco map recall

  • Maghreb, northwest Africa; capital Rabat, largest city Casablanca; Algeria east, disputed Western Sahara south; Atlantic west, Mediterranean north. Morocco's Western Sahara claim/control must not imply universally recognised sovereignty.
  • Gibraltar connects Atlantic–Mediterranean, between Spain and Morocco; Spanish-administered Ceuta/Melilla lie on the North African coast. Toubkal, highest Atlas peak; Moulouya flows from Atlas to Mediterranean. Mediterranean north/coast differs from arid interior/south.
  • Constitutional monarchy, bicameral legislature; Arabic and Tamazight official, French widely used. Phosphates, agriculture, manufacturing and tourism matter; “raw-material-dependent” alone understates the economy.
  • 2019 sea-border PYQ already banked: Adriatic–Albania, Caspian–Kazakhstan, Mediterranean–Morocco correct (b); Croatia is Adriatic, Syria Mediterranean. Source's Red Sea littoral list omits Djibouti. RPSC straits exercise is not a UPSC CSE PYQ; Malacca links Andaman Sea/Indian Ocean with South China Sea/Pacific approaches, Bering Arctic–Pacific and Hormuz Persian Gulf–Gulf of Oman.
