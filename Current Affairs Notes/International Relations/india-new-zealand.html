India–New Zealand relations

Editable notes

March 2025 engagement

  • Diplomatic ties date to 1952; Commonwealth links, education, migration, tourism and diaspora connect the countries.
  • PM Christopher Luxon's visit and relaunch of FTA negotiations occurred in March 2025, not 2024. Earlier negotiations began in 2010. Negotiations in this update are not a signed FTA. Joint statement.
  • Trade opportunities: Indian pharmaceuticals, engineering, textiles and services; New Zealand horticulture, wool, wood and other products. Dairy sensitivities, farmer interests and standards require negotiation.
  • Defence cooperation, maritime security and counter-terrorism featured alongside mobility. Distinguish peaceful expression from violence/incitement; political allegations do not prove criminality.
  • New Zealand is an IORA dialogue partner, not a member; it belongs to APEC, while India does not. Common participation in other forums must be checked individually.
  • President Pranab Mukherjee visited in 2016; the source's Kovind/2018 attribution is incorrect. Diaspora/student totals require year and definition rather than merging incompatible estimates.

22 December 2025: FTA negotiations concluded

  • Negotiations launched 16 March 2025 concluded after roughly nine months; at this issue date, legal scrubbing/signature and entry into force remained future steps. The source expects signature in early/first-half 2026; negotiated concessions were not already applied duties.
  • New Zealand offered zero duty on all Indian goods exports; India offered liberalisation on 70.03% of tariff lines covering 95% of bilateral trade, with exclusions and staging. Benefits include textiles, apparel, leather/footwear, engineering, pharmaceuticals and electronics; duty-free inputs include logs, coking coal and metal scrap. Commerce Ministry, 22 December.
  • Sensitive Indian exclusions include specified dairy, onions, sugar, spices, edible oils, rubber and other agricultural/industrial lines; do not read the source's broad “all agriculture closed” shorthand as a complete tariff schedule. It lists wine, kiwifruit, cherries, avocados, persimmons, seafood and Manuka honey among concessions, with product-specific safeguards/staging. Apples/kiwifruit/honey productivity partnerships and quotas/minimum-import-price conditions accompany access.
  • US$20 billion investment commitment over 15 years and target of doubling bilateral trade are ambitions, not realised FDI. 5,000 temporary-employment entry places, stays up to three years, plus 1,000 Work and Holiday visas; eligibility/professional recognition apply. Do not automatically describe the 5,000 ceiling as 5,000 new grants every year.
  • Diversification amid US tariff pressure, Oceania engagement and services mobility balance export opportunity against farmer protection. An FTA removes/reduces agreed barriers; it need not cover every sector or create a customs union.

30 December 2025: repeated FTA overview

  • Negotiations concluded after about nine months; source expected signing in first half 2026. This repeats the 23 December development and is not a second conclusion, signed treaty or entry into force.
  • $5 bn trade/five years, $20 bn investment/15 years, tariff-free Indian exports, services access and temporary skilled mobility are intended commitments/targets. The 5,000 temporary employment pathway is a quota/capacity, not proof 5,000 new visas are issued afresh every year regardless of conditions.
  • Agriculture concessions are product-specific: dairy/rice/wheat/sugar and other sensitive exclusions coexist with concessions on some fruit/wine/forestry products. Milk-albumin/infant-formula arrangements do not imply blanket opening of all dairy. Avoid learning the worksheet's repeated 'vegetables:almonds' taxonomy or assuming every listed exclusion applies identically to every product line.
  • Mains: export diversification and labour-intensive manufacturing/services opportunities balanced with sensitive farm livelihoods, rules of origin, safeguards and adjustment capacity. Existing 23 December details remain the primary development record.
