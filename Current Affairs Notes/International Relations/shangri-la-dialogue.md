# Shangri-La Dialogue and Indo-Pacific defence diplomacy

## Singapore, 30 May–1 June 2025
- **22nd Shangri-La Dialogue**, organised by the **International Institute for Strategic Studies (IISS)** in Singapore; annual ministerial/military/strategic dialogue begun in **2002**. It is an IISS-convened forum involving governments, not a treaty organisation or binding regional alliance. [IISS 2025 event](https://www.iiss.org/events/shangri-la-dialogue/shangri-la-dialogue-2025/).
- The 30 May issue reports **CDS General Anil Chauhan's planned participation** and bilateral engagement with military leaders from Australia, EU, France, Germany, Indonesia, Japan, Netherlands, New Zealand, Philippines, Singapore, UK and US. This pre-event itinerary is not proof every proposed meeting concluded. [Defence Ministry, 29 May](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2132230&lang=2&reg=48).
- Planned engagement with academia/think tanks included **future wars/warfare** and **defence innovation for future challenges**. Maritime security, cyber risks, terrorism, emerging technology and Indo-Pacific competition provide common discussion ground.
- Dialogue can clarify positions, build trust, sustain military contacts and identify cooperation; attendance does not itself settle territorial disputes or prove participants share identical strategies. India's maritime/Indo-Pacific partnerships can be presented without assuming a formal anti-China alliance.
