# UN Security Council ISIL/Al-Qaida sanctions regime

## Regime and national-list distinction
- Security Council's **1267 regime began in 1999**; later **1989 (2011)** and **2253 (2015)** resolutions underpin the ISIL (Da’esh)/Al-Qaida regime. Committee comprises all **15 Council members** and oversees listing/delisting and implementation.
- Listed individuals/entities face **asset freeze, travel ban and arms embargo**, subject to applicable exemptions. Committee decisions follow its procedures, generally consensus; individual states implement obligations.
- July clipping concerns **US designation of The Resistance Front**: a US national designation is not automatic inclusion on the UN list. Do not treat every terrorist organisation as already listed under this particular regime. [UN committee](https://main.un.org/securitycouncil/en/sanctions/1267).
