# India–Norway green maritime cooperation

## India Maritime Week 2025
- Green shipping, shipbuilding/recycling, maritime safety, training and women's participation connect India's scale with Norway's maritime technology. The **2019 Ocean Dialogue/Blue Economy Task Force** covers sustainable ocean management and marine pollution; the issue reports a tenth maritime Joint Working Group meeting in 2025.
- **India–EFTA TEPA entered into force 1 October 2025**, correcting the PDF's 2024. EFTA comprises Norway, Switzerland, Iceland and Liechtenstein. Norway confirms the maritime partnership and TEPA context. [Norwegian government](https://www.regjeringen.no/en/whats-new/norway-and-india-strengthen-maritime-cooperation/id3132055/).
- Electric/autonomous vessels (Yara Birkeland), battery propulsion, hydrogen/ammonia fuels, cleaner port operations and environmentally sound recycling illustrate cooperation. Lifecycle fuel emissions, safety and infrastructure matter; a fuel label alone does not prove zero emissions.
- Issue examples: Wilson ASA's **14-vessel Cochin Shipyard orders**, Indian seafarers as second-largest nationality on Norwegian-controlled ships, and **Maritime SheEO** gender inclusion. The source's approximately 10% concerns Norwegian shipowners' orders built in India, not 10% of Norway's entire existing fleet.
- Maritime India Vision 2030/Amrit Kaal Maritime Vision 2047 supply Indian policy context; common standards, joint R&D, skills and gender inclusion support delivery. Support for the IMO Net-Zero Framework must be read with the October 2025 adoption-session postponement, not as proof an operative global levy already exists.
