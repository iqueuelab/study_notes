# Kurds, Madhesis and Rohingya: regional map recall

## Community–region comparison
- **Kurds:** West Asia, especially Turkey, Iraq, Iran and Syria; not Bangladesh. **Madhesis:** diverse communities of Nepal's southern Terai with representation/citizenship debates; do not label all simply foreign “Indian-origin” residents. **Rohingya:** predominantly Muslim minority associated with Myanmar's Rakhine State, with statelessness/persecution and displacement to Bangladesh and elsewhere.
- **2016 PYQ answer: 2 and 3 only (c)** for Kurd–Bangladesh, Madhesi–Nepal, Rohingya–Myanmar. Community location is distinct from citizenship status of every individual or support for a particular political movement.
