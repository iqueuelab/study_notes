# India–Greece strategic cooperation

## 9 February 2026: defence-industrial cooperation
- **Rajnath Singh–Nikos Dendias, New Delhi:** **Joint Declaration of Intent** on defence-industrial cooperation is a **starting point for developing a five-year roadmap**, not proof a completed five-year programme was already implemented. Links **Atmanirbhar Bharat** with Greece's **Agenda 2030** defence reforms. [Defence Ministry](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2225523).
- **Bilateral Military Cooperation Plan 2026** exchanged; Greece announced an **International Liaison Officer at IFC-IOR, Gurugram**, supporting maritime information cooperation. Industry visits/interactions seek partnerships, technology and capacity; these are not completed fighter/submarine procurement contracts.
- Embedded trade clipping repeats the ambition to **double bilateral trade by 2030**, agreed during Modi's **2023 Athens visit**, rather than a new binding 2026 trade outcome. Shipping, connectivity/IMEC, investment and defence provide complementary links. [Greek Foreign Minister's February 2025 explanation](https://www.mfa.gr/en/minister-of-foreign-affairs-george-gerapetritis-interview-with-the-hindu-newspaper-06-02-2025/).
## Greece: map recall
- Southeastern Europe, southern Balkans; land borders **Albania, North Macedonia, Bulgaria and Türkiye**. **Aegean east, Ionian west, Mediterranean south**; capital **Athens**.
- Mountainous terrain: **Pindus**, **Mount Olympus ~2,918 m**, **Vikos Gorge**; Mediterranean climate with highland variation. **Evros/Maritsa** forms much of the Türkiye boundary; **Axios/Vardar** and **Strymon/Struma** are cross-border rivers. Do not treat all Greek rivers as seasonal. Bauxite, lignite and marble are useful resource recall.
