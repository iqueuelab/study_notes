# El Salvador: geography and presidential re-election

## July 2025 constitutional change
- **31 July 2025**, Legislative Assembly approved/ratified changes allowing **indefinite presidential re-election**, extending terms **five→six years**, eliminating presidential runoffs and aligning election cycles. Source raises democratic-backsliding concerns: assess term limits, checks/balances and electoral competition. [Assembly announcement](https://asamblea.gob.sv/node/13645).
## Map recall
- **Central America**, capital **San Salvador**; Guatemala northwest, Honduras northeast, Pacific south; **only Central American country without Caribbean frontage**.
- Mountain/volcanic terrain; **Cerro El Pital (~2,730 m)** at Honduras border is highest. **Lempa River**; lakes **Ilopango, Coatepeque, Güija**; volcanoes **San Salvador, Ilopango, San Vicente**. Tropical climate varies with altitude; Pacific coastal lowlands can be hot despite cooler uplands.
