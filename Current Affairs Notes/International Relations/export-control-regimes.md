# Dual-use technology and export-control regimes

## Dual-use controls
- **Dual-use** goods/technology have civilian and military applications. Industrial **autoclaves** may also be used in missile-motor manufacture; the source recalls the **Da Cui Yun** shipment detained at **Kandla, Gujarat, February 2020**, and alleged links to Pakistan's National Development Complex. An allegation or controlled capability is distinct from proof of an individual financing offence.
- **MTCR**, established **1987**, is an informal voluntary political arrangement with **35 partners** and no permanent secretariat; India joined **27 June 2016**. Category I covers complete rocket/UAV delivery systems capable of **at least 500 kg payload to at least 300 km**, plus specified major subsystems/production facilities; Category II covers other specified missiles, components and technology. The source's simple 'all components = Category II' is incomplete.
- Export licensing, end-use/end-user checks and national implementation are central; membership does not grant automatic access to technology or permission for every missile export.
| Regime | Focus | India's June2025 position |
|---|---|---|
| MTCR | Missile/UAV proliferation | Member, 2016 |
| Nuclear Suppliers Group | Nuclear exports | Not a member |
| Wassenaar Arrangement | Conventional arms and dual-use items | Member, 2017 |
| Australia Group | Chemical/biological proliferation-related exports | Member, 2018 |
- **Proliferation financing** means financing WMD proliferation-related activities; FATF assesses national controls, while customs/export-control agencies enforce shipment rules.
