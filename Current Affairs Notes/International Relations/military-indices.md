## Spending and risks: dated facts
- **2023 world spending US$2,443 bn**, **+6.8% real**; India **US$83.6 bn, fourth spender, +4.2%**. [SIPRI 2023 release](https://www.sipri.org/media/press-release/2024/global-military-spending-surges-amid-war-rising-tensions-and-insecurity).
- Source **over 60 conflicts** and **civilian deaths +30%** lacks a consistent underlying dataset/year definition; distinguish number of conflicts, state-based conflicts and civilian fatalities. “Highest in history” is not established by the generic claim.
- Global Risks Report treats armed conflict as a major near-term concern; perceived risk differs from a military-capability rank.
- Mains: spending, equipment readiness, training/jointness, logistics, geography, technology and partnerships all matter; numerical manpower or spending alone cannot settle strategic outcomes.
