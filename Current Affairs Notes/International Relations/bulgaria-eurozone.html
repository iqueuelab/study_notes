International Relations

Bulgaria and euro-area accession

Editable notes

July 2025 decision

  • 8 July 2025: EU Council adopted final legal acts for Bulgaria to introduce the euro on 1 January 2026, becoming the 21st euro-area member. In this July source it is a future change, not already operating euro circulation. Croatia joined in 2023; euro-area membership stood at 20 before Bulgaria's changeover. Council decision.
  • Euro introduced in 1999 by 11 countries; ECB, Frankfurt manages monetary policy. National budgets/taxes remain national, subject to EU coordination/rules. Andorra, Monaco, San Marino and Vatican City use euro by agreement; Kosovo/Montenegro use it unilaterally and are not euro-area EU members.
  • Maastricht convergence: price stability, sound public finances, long-term interest-rate convergence, and at least two years in ERM II without severe tensions. The fiscal reference values are 3% deficit/GDP and 60% debt/GDP; legal assessment has qualifications, so do not treat the sheet's strict less-than shorthand as the entire rule.

Map recall

  • Sofia capital; southeastern Europe/eastern Balkans, Black Sea coast. Neighbours: Romania north (Danube for much of border), Serbia west, North Macedonia southwest, Greece/Turkey south.
  • Balkan Mountains centrally; Rhodopes near Greece; Rila's Musala 2,925 m highest Bulgarian peak. Rivers include Danube, Iskur, Maritsa, Struma, Tundzha and Yantra. Mostly continental climate with southern Mediterranean influence.
