International Relations

India–France defence and strategic partnership

Editable notes

Milestones and exercises

  • Diplomatic relations 1948; strategic partnership 1998. Cooperation spans defence, space, counterterrorism and the Indian Ocean/Indo-Pacific.
  • Shakti — Army; Varuna — Navy; Garuda — Air Force.
  • 2016 agreement for 36 Rafale aircraft for the IAF; distinguish it from the later Navy acquisition.
  • Scorpene/Kalvari-class conventional submarines: Mazagon Dock, India + Naval Group, France. They are not nuclear submarines.

January 2025 proposals and verified later milestones

  • Source anticipated 26 naval Rafale and three additional Scorpene submarines, with a combined reported value above $10 billion, subject to approval/contracting; a proposal is not a completed acquisition.
  • Rafale agreement signed 28 April 2025: 22 single-seat + four twin-seat aircraft, training, support and weapons; do not describe all 26 as identical carrier-capable variants. Defence Ministry release.
  • INS Vaghsheer commissioned 15 January 2025, not January 2024 as printed; see the common naval-platforms topic.
  • Paris AI Action Summit: 10–11 February 2025, not 2024. French Foreign Ministry.
  • Source graphic oversimplifies the return of French settlements: distinguish 1954 de facto transfer and 1962 de jure transfer rather than repeat 'all five returned in 1962'.

Strategic significance and evaluation

  • Carrier aviation, underwater capability, maritime interoperability and France's regional presence, including Réunion.
  • Technology transfer, domestic manufacture and maintenance support can build capacity; judge actual local content/skills rather than assume imports equal self-reliance.
  • Evaluate cost, delivery schedules, support dependence, interoperability and indigenous alternatives; maintain a multipolar approach and strategic autonomy.
  • Do not memorise the PDF's generic 'Towed Artillery System' claim as a verified named bilateral procurement deal.

Naval procurement: March 2025 status

  • Proposed 26 Rafale aircraft comprised 22 carrier-capable single-seaters and four land-based trainers; not all 26 were carrier-compatible.
  • Approval, signature, production and delivery are distinct. The source's pending deal should not be described as a completed March 2025 delivery.
  • Proposed additional Scorpene submarines involve Mazagon Dock and Naval Group; Project 75 is distinct from Project 75I.
  • TEDBF is a development programme, not an inducted naval fighter. CCS approval and estimated combined values do not establish every component's final contract terms.

Naval acquisitions: April 2025 issue

  • The issue discusses 26 naval Rafale aircraft and three additional Scorpene submarines, with a source-reported combined estimate of $11 billion. The page-one newspaper clipping reports CCS approval for Rafale, whereas the accompanying prose still says approval is awaited: the source mixes procurement stages.
  • CCS: Prime Minister chairs; Defence, Home, Finance and External Affairs Ministers are members. Approval → agreement → production → delivery → induction are distinct stages.
  • Additional submarines would expand the conventional Project-75 fleet through Mazagon Dock, Mumbai, and Naval Group. Do not apply the Rafale delivery estimate automatically to submarine construction.
  • Cost, annual budget availability, delivery delays and dependence on imported high-end technology remain evaluation points. Carrier aviation complements indigenous TEDBF development; an interim import does not establish TEDBF's operational readiness.
  • Verified later clarification, 28 April 2025: the signed Rafale agreement specified delivery completion by 2030; the issue's possible 2029 start was a forecast, not this contract milestone. Ministry of Defence.
