International Relations

India–Afghanistan engagement

Editable notes

January 2025 diplomatic step

  • 8 January 2025, Dubai: Foreign Secretary Vikram Misri met Taliban administration's acting Foreign Minister Amir Khan Muttaqi; an upgrade to Foreign Secretary-level contact after the 2021 Taliban takeover, not the first India–Afghanistan meeting ever.
  • Humanitarian assistance, Afghan needs, trade via Chabahar, Iran, connectivity and regional security. Engagement did not constitute formal Indian recognition of the Taliban government in this January context.
  • 1950 Treaty of Friendship; development links include Salma/Afghan–India Friendship Dam, Herat; 218-km Zaranj–Delaram road; Afghan Parliament; Indira Gandhi Institute of Child Health and Habibia School, Kabul.
  • Source graphic also cites Pul-e-Khumri–Kabul 220-kV transmission line and Chimtala substation, plus historical aid commitment ~$3.1 billion since November 2001; not an annual aid figure.

Interests → constraints → response

  • Central Asian access and trade; Chabahar bypasses Pakistan-dependent overland access. Afghanistan is landlocked, not an Arabian Sea port state.
  • Prevent terrorist safe havens; humanitarian goodwill and people-to-people links; avoid abandoning Afghan citizens amid political change.
  • Constraints: Taliban governance/rights and security concerns, Pakistan transit/security factor and growing Chinese engagement.
  • Practical response: conditional project delivery, aid with accountability, diplomatic channels and connectivity, without conflating contact with endorsement of domestic practices.
