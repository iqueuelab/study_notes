Editable notes

December 2025: civilisational and strategic partnership argument

  • Shared Indo-Iranian linguistic/cultural roots, similarities between Avesta and Rigveda, Persian court/administrative/literary influence, Sabk-e-Hindi and Mirza Abdul-Qadir Bedil enrich cultural diplomacy. The PDF's generic 'common Indo-Aryan' label is too narrow for the broader Indo-Iranian relationship; similarities do not prove identical religions or uninterrupted political unity.
  • Editorial advocates energy diversification, connectivity, counter-terrorism and a knowledge partnership: Indian IT/digital services and Iranian scientific/medical/nanotechnology capabilities. These are strategic opportunities/recommendations, not a newly signed comprehensive treaty or evidence oil imports resumed.
  • Chabahar bypasses Pakistan toward Afghanistan/Central Asia; INSTC multimodal India–Iran–Russia–Europe links require functioning infrastructure, finance and customs interoperability. Source 40% shorter/30% cheaper estimates are route/trial dependent, not guaranteed for every shipment. Existing Chabahar notes preserve the 2024 operating contract and 2025 sanctions/temporary-waiver chronology.
  • Sanctions, banking/insurance and third-party alignments constrain implementation. Proposed local-currency settlement, alternative payment channels and innovation cooperation are policy options; civilisational goodwill alone does not remove commercial/legal constraints.
