# Shanghai Cooperation Organisation: membership and India

## Origins and institutions
- Shanghai Five (1996): China, Russia, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan and Tajikistan; SCO founded **15 June 2001 in Shanghai**, adding Uzbekistan. Origins include border confidence-building and regional security after the Soviet breakup.
- **June 2025 membership: ten**—the six founders plus India and Pakistan (2017), Iran (2023), Belarus (2024). Afghanistan and Mongolia were observers. Do not revise from the source's pre-Belarus nine-member snapshot. [SCO 2024 accession statement](https://eng.sectsco.org/20240704/1460010.html).
- Council of Heads of State is the highest decision-making body; Council of Heads of Government handles cooperation including economic priorities. Permanent bodies: **Secretariat, Beijing**, and **Regional Anti-Terrorist Structure (RATS), Tashkent**; exercises, intelligence and cooperation against terrorism, extremism and trafficking.
## India's interests and constraints
- Access to Central Asian partners and dialogue on security, Afghanistan, connectivity, trade and energy; participation can support strategic autonomy in a multipolar setting.
- China–Pakistan tensions, sovereignty concerns over BRI, uneven connectivity and balancing Russia/China constrain benefits. India did not endorse BRI in the 2024 declaration. [Astana declaration](https://eng.sectsco.org/20240709/1438929.html).
- Source's roughly 40% world population and US$23-trillion GDP are approximate dated estimates; membership and nominal/PPP/year definitions affect such totals. SCO is not a collective-defence alliance.

## Membership PYQ
- **2022 PYQ: AIIB, MTCR and SCO—all three (d)**. India: AIIB founding membership 2016, MTCR 2016, SCO 2017. Background repeats June 20 notes; Belarus was already the tenth member in June 2025.

## June 27 development
- Source reports **India declined to sign the SCO Defence Ministers' joint statement** because the Pahalgam attack was omitted; failure to reach consensus prevented adoption. This demonstrates the limits of regional counter-terrorism cooperation when members differ on terrorism narratives; it does not mean India left SCO.
- Existing ten-member list, RATS/Secretariat and India interests apply. **2022 membership PYQ: (d), all three**—India belongs to AIIB, MTCR and SCO. The complete question was already captured from the June24 issue, so it is not duplicated in the bank.
