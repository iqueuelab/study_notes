# Nord Stream pipelines and European energy security

## Baltic Sea route and chronology
- Nord Stream connects Russia directly to Germany under the **Baltic Sea**, bypassing transit routes through Ukraine. Nord Stream 1: **Portovaya near Vyborg → Lubmin**, about **1,224 km**, first line operational November **2011**, second in 2012.
- Nord Stream 2: **Ust-Luga → German Baltic coast**, designed **55 bcm/year**, construction completed **September 2021**, not the source's 2019. Completion did not establish commercial operation. [Developer chronology](https://www.gazprom.com/projects/nord-stream2/).
- Direct pipelines reduce transit dependence but concentrate supplier/infrastructure risks. Diversification, storage, LNG and demand measures influence energy security; source's language of uninterrupted present supply ignores subsequent disruptions. The map marks September 2022 leak areas near **Bornholm**, not active new July 2025 leaks.
- Source reserve/budget charts lack common year/definitions; do not use their 40% oil/gas budget claim as a timeless figure.
