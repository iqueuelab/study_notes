# India’s nuclear submarines: SSBN deterrence and SSN roles

## SSBN and SSN
| Type | Main role |
|---|---|
| **SSBN** | Nuclear-powered ballistic-missile submarine: survivable sea-based nuclear deterrence and second-strike capability |
| **SSN** | Nuclear-powered attack submarine: anti-submarine/anti-ship operations, intelligence, escort and sea control |
- Nuclear propulsion is not synonymous with carrying nuclear weapons. High endurance is constrained by crew, food, maintenance and operational conditions; it is not literally unlimited or proof of naval dominance.
## Fleet and development: August source snapshot
- **INS Arihant** is the first Indian SSBN; **INS Arighaat**, the **second Arihant-class submarine**, commissioned **29 August 2024** at Visakhapatnam. This corrects the issue's August 2023 date and its description of Aridhaman as second. [Defence Ministry commissioning release](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleaseIframePage.aspx?PRID=2049870&lang=2&reg=3).
- Source describes **Aridhaman/S4 under sea trials** with commissioning expected, and **S4*** launched/testing. These are reported programme stages, not proof either was commissioned by 24 August 2025. Its **~7,000 tonnes, 112–125 m, 95 crew**, K-4 integration, USHUS/Panchendriya and countermeasure inventory are reported specifications, not independently confirmed for this specific hull.
- The **ATV programme/Ship Building Centre, Visakhapatnam** supports indigenous SSBN capability. SSNs can protect SSBN patrol areas and support a wider fleet; secure communications, quieting, sonar and trained crews are essential.
- Source forecasts first indigenous SSN around **2036–37**, with design/build stages and a Russian lease around **2027–28**; these are forecasts, not guaranteed delivery. Its **October 2023 approval** is inconsistent with December 2024 Navy reporting locating clearance in **October 2024**. Source cost **₹35,000 crore for two** is a reported programme estimate. [DRDO-hosted December 2024 news compilation](https://drdo.gov.in/drdo/sites/default/files/drdo-news/NPC03to04Dec2024.pdf).
- **Chakra I was a Charlie-class leased submarine; Chakra II an Akula-class**—the source incorrectly labels both Akula. Launch, sea trials, lease negotiations and induction are distinct milestones.
- **2016 PYQ: INS Astradharini is a torpedo launch and recovery vessel (c)**. K-6/MIRV claims and Agni-IV revision are retained in the separate K-6 topic.
