# P-8I maritime patrol and anti-submarine warfare

## Platform
- **Boeing P-8I**, Indian derivative of **P-8A Poseidon /737**; long-range maritime reconnaissance, anti-submarine and anti-surface warfare.
- India-specific subsystems alongside radar, electro-optical/infrared sensors, electronic surveillance, magnetic-anomaly detection and deployable **sonobuoys**. Radar detects appropriate electromagnetic returns; submerged-submarine acoustic sensing is a different task. [Boeing design announcement](https://boeing.mediaroom.com/2010-07-26-Boeing-Completes-Final-Design-Review-for-P-8I).
- Source's **AN/APS-137** designation should not be learnt as the P-8I's standard radar; the platform is associated with the **AN/APY-10** family.
- **Mk 54 torpedoes / Harpoon** illustrate weapons roles; sensor detection, classification and prosecution require an integrated mission system.
- Dimensions reported **39.5 m length /37.6 m span /12.4 m height**, CFM56 engines, about nine mission crew; range/radius/on-station time must not be conflated.
## Fleet and strategy
- **Eight ordered 2009; four additional 2016 →12** in the February 2025 fleet. Additional **six** were a procurement discussion, not delivered aircraft.
- Supports Indian Ocean sea-lane security, surveillance, search/rescue and interoperability with partner navies; costs, maintenance, data security and domestic capability remain important.
- A bilateral technology dialogue does not mean every purchase is a completed co-production/technology-transfer project.
