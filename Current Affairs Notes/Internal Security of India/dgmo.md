# DGMO: military operations and crisis communication

## Role and dated officeholders
- **Director General of Military Operations**, a senior Indian Army appointment normally held by a **Lieutenant General**, in Army Headquarters, New Delhi; military operations planning, operational readiness, intelligence coordination and advice within the Army/COAS command system.
- May 2025 briefing team: **Lt Gen Rajiv Ghai (Army DGMO), Air Marshal A.K. Bharti (Director General Air Operations), Vice Admiral A.N. Pramod (Director General Naval Operations)**. These are distinct service appointments, not three officers holding the Army DGMO post. Pakistan's counterpart in the source is **Maj Gen Kashif Abdullah**. [Official briefing collection](https://www.pib.gov.in/EventDetail.aspx?ID=1206&lang=1&reg=3).
- Source dates Ghai's appointment **25 October 2024**; officeholder recall is tied to May 2025 and must not be presented as permanently current.
## Operations, coordination and hotline
- Links planning, readiness, border/LoC operations, intelligence inputs, military policy advice and inter-service liaison. Other commanders and service headquarters retain their responsibilities; DGMO is not the independent commander of every tri-service action.
- Hotline communication conveys violations, clarifies intentions and supports de-escalation/confidence-building. Regular calls and crisis calls are distinct; the PDF's **1990 origin / weekly schedule** needs institutional-history corroboration.
- **10 May 2025** understanding to halt military actions followed DGMO communication; **12 May** talks addressed continuation and avoiding firing/aggressive action and reducing border troop concentrations. A cessation understanding is not a peace treaty or settlement of underlying disputes.
- Historical recall: **Kargil 1999, surgical strikes 2016, Pulwama–Balakot 2019** illustrate operations/crisis-coordination contexts; Balakot was an Air Force action, not simply an Army DGMO operation.
- **Pahalgam attack: 22 April 2025**, correcting the source's isolated April 2024 typo. Source's detailed branches/agency liaison list is illustrative, not a verified complete organisational chart.
