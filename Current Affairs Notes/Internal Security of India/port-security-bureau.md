# Bureau of Port Security: ship, port and cyber security

## December 2025: Bureau of Port Security announced
- **19 December announcement**: proposed statutory **BoPS under section 13, Merchant Shipping Act 2025**, within **Ministry of Ports, Shipping and Waterways**, modelled on **BCAS**. This announcement does not establish that the new institution was already fully operational. [Home Ministry](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2206443&lang=1&reg=6).
- DG to be an **IPS officer, Pay Level 15**; **DGS/DGMA** to act as DG during the **one-year transition**. Regulator oversees ship/port-facility security, information exchange and a dedicated **cybersecurity division** for port IT infrastructure.
- **Graded, risk-based security** considers vulnerability, location and trade potential; cyberattacks, GPS spoofing, data theft and logistics disruption connect economic and national security.
- **CISF remains a Recognised Security Organisation**, conducting assessments/plans and training private port-security agencies; BoPS does not imply CISF’s functions simply end. Regulation, physical security and operational policing are distinct functions.
- Source repeats the old **7,516.6-km coastline**. Official revised measure is **11,098.81 km**, promulgated **29 April 2025**, reflecting finer GIS/high-water-line measurement; it does not itself alter CRZ jurisdiction. [Earth Sciences Ministry, 4 December](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2198800&lang=1&reg=3).
- Revision MCQ: Ministry is **MoPSW**, not MHA; source answer **(b), statements 1/3/4**. It is an undated practice question, not a PYQ.
