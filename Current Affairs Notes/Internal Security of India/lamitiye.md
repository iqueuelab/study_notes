# Lamitiye: India–Seychelles military exercise

## Lamitiye 2026
- **11th edition**, Seychelles Defence Academy; **Lamitiye means friendship in Seychellois Creole**. Biennial exercise conducted in Seychelles since **2001**, strengthening cooperation/interoperability with Seychelles Defence Forces.
- Correct dates to the **9 March Defence Ministry announcement: 9–20 March 2026**, rather than the compilation's 10–22 March. This edition includes personnel from **all three Indian services**, including **Assam Regiment, INS Trikand and a C-130 aircraft**. [Official exercise announcement](https://www.pib.gov.in/Pressreleaseshare.aspx?PRID=2236791&lang=2&reg=48).
- Training: sub-conventional operations in **semi-urban environments**, joint planning/tactical drills, equipment familiarity and validation exercises relevant to peacekeeping. Exercise participation is not an actual UN operational deployment or a new mutual-defence treaty; it builds practical jointness and Indian Ocean defence ties.
