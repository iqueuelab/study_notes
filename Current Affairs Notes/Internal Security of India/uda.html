Underwater domain awareness and autonomous systems

Concept and February 2025 cooperation

  • UDA: integrated understanding of underwater activity/environment—submarines, acoustic conditions, seabed infrastructure and relevant maritime traffic.
  • ASIA =Autonomous Systems Industry Alliance, announced 13 February 2025 to scale industrial partnerships/production in the Indo-Pacific. Joint statement.

Integrated surveillance

  • Sensors/platforms →data fusion/classification →command assessment →appropriate maritime response. Passive sonar listens; active sonar emits sound and analyses returns; environmental noise/temperature affect detection.
  • February references: 12 P-8I aircraft, 24 MH-60R helicopters ordered, 15 naval MQ-9B within 31 overall acquisition, possible additional P-8Is. Ordered totals ≠delivered fleet.
  • Benefits: anti-submarine capability, sea-lane/seabed-infrastructure protection, search/rescue and partner interoperability. Constraints: endurance, acoustic ambiguity, secure communications, cost, IP and autonomy safeguards.
  • Quad cooperation and shared disaster-response mobility do not establish a formal military alliance or unlimited access to Indian maritime data.
