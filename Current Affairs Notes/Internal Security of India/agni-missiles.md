# Agni missiles: ballistic technology and strategic deterrence

## 20 August 2025 test
- **Agni 5** successfully test-fired from **Integrated Test Range, Chandipur, Odisha**, on **20 August 2025** under Strategic Forces Command; official release calls it an **intermediate-range ballistic missile** and reports validation of operational/technical parameters. Source graphics alternatively use ICBM terminology; use the official designation and stated **over-5,000 km class** without inventing a longer classified range. [Defence Ministry](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2158574&lang=1&reg=3).
- DRDO's Agni grew from **IGMDP (1983)** and a **1989 technology demonstrator** drawing on an SLV-3-derived booster and Prithvi-related technology. IGMDP also included Prithvi, Akash, Trishul and Nag. The table mixes first-test, development and induction dates; these are not interchangeable milestones.
- Revision ranges/stages: **Agni I ~700–1,200 km, one-stage solid; II ~2,000–3,000 km, two-stage solid; III ~3,000–3,500 km, two-stage solid; IV ~4,000 km, two-stage solid; V over 5,000 km, three-stage solid; Agni Prime ~1,000–2,000 km, two-stage canisterised solid**. Ranges depend on variant/payload; precise CEP/payload/mobility claims in the issue are not independently established specifications.
- Solid propellant supports readiness and storage; canisterisation aids handling/launch preparedness. **MIRV** means multiple independently targetable re-entry vehicles; it does not establish that every Agni test or variant carries MIRVs. Source's **Agni VI 6,000–10,000 km**, submarine-launch and Agni Prime moving-ship claims are speculative/unsupported, so not operational facts.
- **Ballistic:** rocket boost followed largely by ballistic flight/re-entry; **cruise:** sustained atmospheric powered/guided flight, commonly air-breathing engines. BrahMos is a supersonic cruise missile, not an ICBM. Cruise missiles are not necessarily all subsonic; flight speed alone does not define the category.
- **2014 Agni IV PYQ: A (1 only)**: surface-to-surface, solid fuel, not 7,500 km. **2023 ballistic/cruise PYQ: D (neither)**. The source's Agni V practice answer A requires a classification caveat; its unqualified ICBM label conflicts with this test's official wording.
- Strategic role: survivable retaliatory deterrence and the land leg of India's nuclear triad. **No First Use** policy includes the stated caveat on major chemical/biological attacks; do not reduce the doctrine to an exception-free slogan.
