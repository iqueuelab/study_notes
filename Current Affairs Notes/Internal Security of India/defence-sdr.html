Internal Security of India

Software-defined radios and defence logistics procurement

Editable notes

Contracts signed 20 February 2025

  • Total ₹1,917.47 crore: BEL, 149 Coast Guard SDRs, ₹1,220.12 crore; ACE/JCB India, 1,868 rough-terrain forklifts, ₹697.35 crore. Defence Ministry.
  • SDR: software-configurable communication functions/waveforms support multi-band/mode operation, secure voice/data and network interoperability; not automatically immune to interception/jamming/cyberattack.
  • Forklift: material-handling/logistics vehicle; improves loading/warehousing efficiency, not a combat tank or weapon.

Operational and industry assessment

  • Coast Guard maritime law enforcement, search/rescue, fisheries/environment protection and Navy coordination need reliable communications; field conditions, spectrum and interoperability require testing.
  • Domestic manufacture and MSME supply chains support jobs/strategic autonomy; an Indian-based company label is not proof every component or design is domestically owned.
  • Contracts ≠completed delivery. Train users, secure software updates, maintain spares/lifecycle support and assess operational reliability.
  • Source 7,516 km coastline /2.4 million km² EEZ are dated/conventional measures; survey methodology matters. DAP 2020 /iDEX /positive indigenisation lists are distinct tools; DPEPP2020 was a draft policy, not automatically a fully notified final framework.
