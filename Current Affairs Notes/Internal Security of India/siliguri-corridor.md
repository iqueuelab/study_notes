# Siliguri Corridor: geography and eastern defence posture

## November 2025: three reported Army garrisons
- Source reports **Lachit Borphukan Military Station at Dhubri, Assam**, and forward bases at **Kishanganj, Bihar**, and **Chopra, West Bengal**. Dhubri's foundation stone was laid by Eastern Army Commander **Lt Gen R. C. Tiwari**; foundation/establishment and full operational readiness are different stages.
- **Siliguri Corridor / Chicken's Neck**, northern West Bengal, is roughly **20–22 km wide at its narrowest** and carries critical road/rail links between mainland India and the Northeast. Nepal lies west, Bangladesh south/east, Bhutan north; China is farther north through the Sikkim–Tibet sector, not a direct border of the narrow strip. **Bihar has no international boundary with Bangladesh**: Kishanganj is near the wider border region through West Bengal.
- Bases support surveillance, logistics, faster reinforcement, Army–BSF coordination and protection of North Bengal/Sikkim approaches. Source links **Trishakti Corps**, Hashimara's Rafale aircraft and air-defence/missile assets to the wider security architecture; these references do not disclose a verified deployment order for each new base.
- Context: Bangladesh's political transition, Chinese infrastructure near the LAC, and the **June 2025 China–Pakistan–Bangladesh consultation in Kunming**. A possible strategic axis and migration/security concerns are assessments, not proof of a formal military alliance or attribution of threats to every migrant.
- Protect transport redundancy and civilian resilience alongside military preparedness. The source's claim of a Pakistan Navy visit being the first since 1971 is not established here and is excluded; avoid treating corridor disruption as eliminating every possible air/sea or negotiated transit connection.
