# BHARATPOL and INTERPOL cooperation

## Platform
- **CBI-developed**, launched **7 January 2025** by Union Home Minister; **CBI is NCB-New Delhi**, India's National Central Bureau for INTERPOL.
- Single digital channel connects Indian law-enforcement agencies with CBI/INTERPOL cooperation: standard requests, faster intelligence sharing, notices, documents and training.
- Crimes: cybercrime, trafficking in persons/drugs/arms, financial fraud, terrorism/online radicalisation; cross-border data requests need lawful safeguards.
| Module | Recall |
| --- | --- |
| Connect | CBI/NCB and Indian agencies on one platform |
| INTERPOL Notices | Notice requests |
| Broadcast | Disseminate foreign assistance/intelligence requests |
| References | International investigative assistance |
| Resources | Documents and capacity building |
- The PDF misassigns Notice functionality to **Connect**; keep the modules distinct. [MHA/PIB](https://www.pib.gov.in/Pressreleaseshare.aspx?PRID=2099539&lang=2&reg=48).
## INTERPOL and notices
- **International Criminal Police Organization; 1923; Lyon, France**. Source states **196 members**; '195 other countries' is consistent with India being one of 196, but '195 total members' is outdated.
| Notice | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Red | Locate and provisionally arrest a wanted person for extradition/surrender or similar lawful action |
| Blue | Obtain identity/location/activity information |
| Green | Warning about persons posing a criminal threat |
| Yellow | Missing persons, often minors; identify persons unable to identify themselves |
| Black | Identify bodies |
| Orange | Warn of dangerous events/objects/materials/methods |
| Purple | Criminal methods, devices or concealment techniques |
| INTERPOL–UNSC special | Individuals/entities subject to UNSC sanctions |
- **Red Notice is not an international arrest warrant**; national law controls legal effect. [INTERPOL notices](https://www.interpol.int/How-we-work/Notices/), [official safeguards FAQ](https://www.interpol.int/content/download/15314/file/INTERPOL%20FAQ%20on%20Personal%20Data%20Safeguards.pdf).
- Institutional priorities in graphic: counter-terrorism, organised/emerging crime and cybercrime.
