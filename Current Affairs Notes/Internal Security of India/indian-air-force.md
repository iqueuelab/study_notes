# Indian Air Force: anniversary, capability and aircraft roles

## 93rd anniversary, October 2025
- IAF Day on 8 October commemorates establishment in 1932; the 2025 ceremonial parade was at Hindon, Ghaziabad. The source's November Guwahati flypast was prospective at issue time. Modernisation, indigenous platforms, realistic training and inter-service coordination were highlighted.
- Historical correction: it began as the Indian Air Force; “Royal” was conferred in 1945 and dropped in 1950, not the founding name in 1932. Operation Sindoor was a May 2025 military counter-terror operation, not the source's speculative humanitarian mission.
- Roles: Rafale/Su-30MKI/MiG-29 fighters; Apache attack helicopters; Mi-17 transport helicopters; C-17 and C-130J transport aircraft; Akash surface-to-air defence. Tejas is indigenous light combat aviation; AMCA was a development programme, not an already operational fighter.
- Aircraft PYQ: Dornier-228 maritime patrol and C-17 military transport are correctly matched; IL-76 is a transport aircraft, not supersonic combat. Answer: only two. The question already exists in the Science and Technology bank.
