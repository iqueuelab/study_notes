# Operation Sankalp: maritime security and humanitarian readiness

## Origin and March 2026 readiness
- **19 June 2019:** Indian Navy launched maritime-security operations in the Gulf after attacks on merchant shipping, to ensure safe passage of **Indian-flagged vessels through Hormuz**. Presence, surveillance and stakeholder coordination protect trade; the original operation was not solely an anti-piracy deployment. [Defence Ministry record](https://www.pib.gov.in/Pressreleaseshare.aspx?PRID=1835382&lang=2&reg=48).
- The March issue reports a frigate and destroyer in the Gulf of Aden/Gulf of Oman available for **HADR**, and **INS Surat** at Bahrain for regional engagement. These are issue-date reported dispositions, not permanent locations or proof that evacuation had begun.
- Naval platforms can support medical care, logistics and evacuation when directed. **Standby/preparedness ≠ launched rescue/evacuation ≠ completed evacuation**. This maritime mission is distinct from the World Bank-supported **SANKALP skilling programme**.

## 17 March 2026: merchant-shipping protection
- The issue reports naval protection/escort of Indian-flagged merchant shipping in the **Gulf of Oman after Hormuz transit**, including LPG carriers **Shivalik/Nanda Devi** and crude tanker **Jag Laadki**. Merchant vessel Shivalik must not be confused with the warship INS Shivalik.
- Correct the sheet's inconsistent cargo arithmetic: the **92,700 tonnes of LPG aboard the two LPG carriers together**, already recorded under Strait of Hormuz on 14 March, must not be mixed with crude cargo. Jag Laadki's reported **over 80,000 tonnes crude** is separate; 92,172 tonnes is not a valid combined total for all three. [Government briefing carried by Akashvani](https://newsonair.gov.in/two-indian-vessels-carrying-lpg-cross-strait-of-hormuz-safely-govt-says-adequate-crude-oil-inventories-in-country/).
- Protecting **sea lanes of communication** links maritime surveillance, naval presence, merchant coordination and energy security. Hormuz/Gulf tensions and Red Sea/Houthi attacks involve different locations and actors; do not attribute every Gulf incident to the Houthis.
- Officials' assurances on household supply and a reported **36% refinery LPG-output increase** are dated administrative claims; they do not establish that all commercial shortages had ended. Origin of Operation Sankalp in June 2019 and HADR readiness are covered above.
