# Operation Sankalp: maritime security and humanitarian readiness

## Origin and March 2026 readiness
- **19 June 2019:** Indian Navy launched maritime-security operations in the Gulf after attacks on merchant shipping, to ensure safe passage of **Indian-flagged vessels through Hormuz**. Presence, surveillance and stakeholder coordination protect trade; the original operation was not solely an anti-piracy deployment. [Defence Ministry record](https://www.pib.gov.in/Pressreleaseshare.aspx?PRID=1835382&lang=2&reg=48).
- The March issue reports a frigate and destroyer in the Gulf of Aden/Gulf of Oman available for **HADR**, and **INS Surat** at Bahrain for regional engagement. These are issue-date reported dispositions, not permanent locations or proof that evacuation had begun.
- Naval platforms can support medical care, logistics and evacuation when directed. **Standby/preparedness ≠ launched rescue/evacuation ≠ completed evacuation**. This maritime mission is distinct from the World Bank-supported **SANKALP skilling programme**.
