Internal Security of India

Adamya Fast Patrol Vessel

Editable notes

Induction and capability

  • 26 June 2025, Goa: ICG inducted Adamya, first of eight FPVs built by Goa Shipyard Limited. First ship of its class in the ICG fleet with controllable-pitch propellers and indigenous gearboxes, improving manoeuvrability and operational flexibility.
  • Equipment: 30-mm CRN-91 gun; two 12.7-mm stabilised remote-control guns with fire control; integrated bridge/platform-management systems and automated power management.
  • Roles: maritime law enforcement, coastal surveillance, search and rescue, EEZ protection; illustrates domestic shipbuilding capacity. Defence Ministry.
  • INS Astradharini PYQ 2016: torpedo launch and recovery vessel; distinguish it from a submarine, aircraft carrier or amphibious warfare ship.

September 2025 commissioning

  • 19 September 2025, Paradip Port, Odisha: ICGS Adamya commissioned, first of eight Adamya-class FPVs, built by Goa Shipyard Limited, with over 60% indigenous content. This is distinct from the June induction/delivery milestone. Defence Ministry commissioning record.
  • Based at Paradip, under ICG Region (North East), through District HQ No. 7, Odisha. Source specifications: 51 m; approximately 320 tonnes; two 3,000-kW diesel engines; 28 knots; 1,500 nautical miles endurance at economical speed.
  • Indigenous controllable-pitch propellers and gearboxes improve manoeuvrability/flexibility; blade pitch can change without simply changing shaft rotation. Maritime surveillance, anti-smuggling/anti-poaching, search and rescue and pollution response support secure east-coast seas.
  • Coast Guard Act 1978 duties include marine-environment protection, safety of artificial islands/offshore installations and assistance against smuggling. CAPF 2014 example answers all three; separate from CSE PYQ banks. Graphic nautical-mile zones concern legal baselines, not kilometres measured from an arbitrary beach.
