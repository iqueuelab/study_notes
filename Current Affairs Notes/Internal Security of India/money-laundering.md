# Money laundering: PMLA, evidence and international cooperation

## Process and prevention
- Concealing criminal origins to make proceeds appear legitimate: **placement → layering → integration**. Banks, shell entities, property or businesses may be abused; these stages need not always occur as a neat sequence. 'Laundromat' is a metaphor for a laundering network, not proof the term's popular mafia origin story.
- **PMLA 2002**, effective **1 July 2005**, targets laundering and attachment/confiscation of proceeds of crime. Money laundering must connect to criminal activity relating to a **scheduled offence**; it is not every unexplained asset or tax irregularity automatically.
## Correct the worksheet's blanket legal claims
- **Section 24**: specified presumptions apply in proceedings involving proceeds of crime; prosecution must establish foundational facts. This is not a universal obligation on every person to prove innocence in every respect. [PMLA text](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/2036/5/A2003-15.pdf).
- **ECIR** is ED's internal record, not the same as a police FIR. The civil provisional-attachment/inquiry route and criminal prosecution/arrest requirements must be distinguished; the text's 'no FIR ever needed for PMLA' is too broad. **Vijay Madanlal Choudhary (2022)** addresses this distinction; do not rely on the PDF's unverified attribution of a 2017 Vir Bhadra Singh ruling to the Supreme Court.
- **DTAAs** avoid double taxation and may provide information-exchange cooperation; not immunity for illicit transfers. Treaty scope/conditions, FATF standards, beneficial-ownership transparency and cross-border cooperation support enforcement.
- Rising case registrations/low convictions alone do not establish guilt, misuse or the final effectiveness of pending cases; analyse timelines, completed trials and evidence quality. Strengthen trained investigation, reasoned decisions, judicial oversight and due process alongside recovery.
