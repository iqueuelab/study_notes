# E-4B Nightwatch and airborne command continuity

## Aircraft and mission
- **E-4B Nightwatch** is the US **National Airborne Operations Center (NAOC)**, part of the National Military Command System supporting the President, Secretary of Defense and Joint Chiefs of Staff. The issue's Los Angeles appearance report does not itself establish an imminent emergency.
- A militarised **Boeing 747-200**, providing survivable command, control and communications if ground centres fail: directing forces, transmitting emergency war orders and coordinating civil authorities. It supports **NC3—nuclear command, control and communications**—and can assist FEMA disaster response.
- **EMP protection**, nuclear/thermal shielding and satellite communications improve survivability; **in-flight refuelling** extends endurance. USAF lists **12 hours unrefuelled**; claims of remaining aloft for many days depend on refuelling, crew and logistical support and are not ordinary unrefuelled endurance. [US Air Force fact sheet](https://www.af.mil/About-Us/Fact-Sheets/Display/article/104503/e-4b/).
- Strategic concepts: continuity of command/government, redundancy, secure communication and credible deterrence. A hardened command platform reduces vulnerability; it is not invulnerable or a separate authority to launch nuclear weapons.
