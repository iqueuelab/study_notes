# Operation Sindoor and precision-strike capabilities

## 7–8 May 2025: operation and claims
- **Operation Sindoor launched 7 May 2025** after Pahalgam's **22 April** attack killed **25 Indians and one Nepali citizen**. India announced strikes on **nine terrorist-infrastructure sites in Pakistan and Pakistan-occupied Jammu and Kashmir**, linked to groups including Jaish-e-Mohammed and Lashkar-e-Taiba. The government described the initial selection as focused/measured and said no Pakistani military facilities were targeted. Attribute those assessments to the official account. [Initial MoD announcement](https://www.pib.gov.in/Pressreleaseshare.aspx?PRID=2127370&lang=2&reg=48).
- The issue discusses Bahawalpur and Rafale-delivered precision weapons; it does not establish the full platform/munition allocation for every site. The initial operation involved the **Indian Armed Forces**, so “IAF only” is too narrow. Standoff launch does not require invading enemy airspace; **Rafale is not a stealth fighter**.
## Weapon distinctions
| System | Revision facts |
| --- | --- |
| **SCALP /Storm Shadow** | MBDA air-launched deep-strike cruise missile; source approximate **1,300 kg**, public range **over 250 km**; exact configuration-dependent operational range is not established here; hardened-target warhead/precision navigation |
| **AASM HAMMER** | Safran modular precision **air-to-ground guided bomb/weapon with guidance and range-extension kits**, not the same type as a long-range cruise missile; **Highly Agile Modular Munition Extended Range**; INS/GPS, IR or laser variants; manufacturer advertises **70 km+** range under suitable conditions |
| **Loitering munition** | Uncrewed system that searches/loiters then performs a terminal strike; ISR/target identification can inform employment; autonomy varies and should not imply every weapon independently selects targets |
- SCALP offers distant deep strike; HAMMER's modular guidance supports different target/terrain requirements. Claimed drone use in the issue requires corroboration before assigning it to a specific initial strike. [Safran specifications](https://www.safran-group.com/fr/produits-services/aasm-hammertm-armement-air-sol-modulaire-haute-precision).
## Strategic assessment
- Intended aims: disrupt terrorist infrastructure, deter repeat attacks, use intelligence/precision to limit unintended harm and manage escalation. Stand-off weapons reduce crew exposure but do not eliminate risk, casualties or escalation.
- The issue's “objectives achieved without military escalation” is **premature**; India–Pakistan exchanges subsequently continued. Precision/minimal collateral harm are claims requiring evidence, not automatic proof of compliance with international law.
- “Pre-emptive self-defence” is not a universally sufficient legal justification: assess applicable UN Charter rules, necessity/proportionality and humanitarian protection. Diplomatic communication, verified battle-damage assessment, joint coordination and indigenous capabilities complement foreign-origin munitions.
## 10–13 May 2025: escalation and evidence boundaries
- The source discusses later strikes on Pakistani military airbases, including **Nur Khan near Rawalpindi**, following the initial May 7 terrorist-infrastructure operation. Do not apply the initial “no military facilities targeted” description to every subsequent exchange.
- The source's Egypt-flight/nuclear-site narratives, alleged National Command Authority meeting and US warning accounts are **claims, not verified proof of nuclear damage or the cause of cessation**. Suspected sites near Sargodha/Kirana Hills should not be labelled confirmed damaged warhead stores on social-media evidence.
- India's **12 May briefing denied targeting Kirana Hills**. Keep the denial and evidentiary limits alongside any discussion of escalation. [Official Operation Sindoor briefing collection](https://www.pib.gov.in/EventDetail.aspx?ID=1206&lang=1&reg=3).
