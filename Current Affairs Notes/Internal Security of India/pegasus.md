# Pegasus spyware and surveillance safeguards

## Surveillance technology
- **Pegasus**, developed by Israel's **NSO Group**, covertly compromises smartphones; marketed to government agencies for terrorism/organised-crime investigations. It can access messages, calls, email, contacts, calendars, location, photographs and applications, and activate microphones/cameras.
- **Zero-click** means no user action is needed; **zero-day** means a previously unknown/unpatched vulnerability. They are distinct: a zero-click exploit need not be a zero-day. Documented exploit chains targeted messaging software; Android and iOS can be affected. A missed-call attack describes a particular historical exploit, not every missed call today. [Google Project Zero technical analysis](https://projectzero.google/2021/12/a-deep-dive-into-nso-zero-click.html).
- Source graphic describes manual/wireless installation, command-and-control communication and self-deletion; these are reported capabilities, not universal behaviour in every version. Updating software and using official stores reduce risk but cannot guarantee protection against sophisticated exploits.
## Rights and accountability
- Investigations concern journalists, rights defenders, lawyers, opposition figures and public officials. The issue lists alleged operations involving India, Saudi Arabia, Mexico, Morocco, Hungary, Azerbaijan and Rwanda; country allegations do not prove every named government infected every listed target. Forensic evidence must distinguish targeting from successful infection. [Amnesty methodology](https://www.amnesty.org/en/latest/research/2021/07/forensic-methodology-report-how-to-catch-nso-groups-pegasus/).
- Consequences: privacy violations, chilled dissent/press freedom, exposure of privileged communications and national-security risks from foreign surveillance. Covert operation, changing infrastructure and client secrecy make detection and attribution difficult.
- Mains approach: lawful necessity/proportionality, independent authorisation and oversight, audit trails, remedies, responsible vulnerability disclosure and international export/accountability controls; balance legitimate security investigations with constitutional liberties.
