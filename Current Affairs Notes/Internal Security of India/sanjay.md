# SANJAY battlefield surveillance system

## January 2025 flag-off

- **24 January**, **Indian Army + Bharat Electronics Limited**, indigenous automated **Battlefield Surveillance System (BSS)**, **Buy (Indian)**; **₹2,402 crore**.
- Planned phased induction into **brigades, divisions, corps**, **March–October 2025** in three phases. Flag-off and a planned induction schedule do not prove all units were deployed in January. [Defence Ministry](https://www.pib.gov.in/Pressreleaseshare.aspx?PRID=2095712&lang=2&reg=48).

## Data to decisions

- Ground/aerial sensor inputs → integration and removal of duplicate observations → **Common Surveillance Picture** → secure Army data/satellite networks → commander assessment.
- Improved border/intrusion awareness, network-centric coordination and decision speed in conventional/sub-conventional operations; technology absorption/domestic industry benefits.
- Surveillance system **does not itself guarantee correct identification or replace command responsibility**. Sensor quality, interoperability, resilient communications, cybersecurity and operator training determine performance.
