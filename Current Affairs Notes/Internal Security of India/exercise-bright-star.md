# Exercise Bright Star: tri-service interoperability

## 2025 participation
- **28 August–10 September 2025**: over **700 Indian Armed Forces/HQ Integrated Defence Staff personnel** participating in Egypt-hosted multilateral **Bright Star**, conducted with the US since **1980**, normally biennial. India participated in **2023**; the release does not establish that as India's first-ever association in any capacity. [Defence Ministry](https://www.pib.gov.in/Pressreleaseshare.aspx?PRID=2161242&lang=2&reg=48).
- Activities: **three-service live firing → command-post planning → short tactical training → subject-matter exchanges**. Intended gains are jointness, interoperable procedures and cooperation with friendly forces.
- Mains link: defence diplomacy in the Middle East/North Africa complements interests in energy, diaspora and Suez-linked maritime routes. Exercise participation alone does not prove a new alliance or an achieved regional security-provider role.
