# Bright Star: multilateral tri-service exercise

## INS Trikand at Alexandria, September 2025
- **INS Trikand** called at **Alexandria on 1 September**, during Mediterranean deployment, to join **Bright Star 2025**, hosted by **Egypt, 1–10 September** in the naval release. Indian Army and Air Force contingents also participated. [Defence release](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2163382&lang=2&reg=48).
- Multilateral air/land/maritime interoperability, regional cooperation and hybrid-threat scenarios; professional exchanges, cross-deck visits and sports/cultural contact alongside training. Originates in US–Egypt defence cooperation; not a Quad exercise.
- **Trikand**: Russian-built Talwar-class multi-role stealth frigate, commissioned **2013**; anti-air, surface and submarine warfare roles. Egypt's Mediterranean–Red Sea connection through **Suez Canal** underlines maritime significance. Speculation about countering a particular country is analysis, not the formal exercise mandate.
