# Narcotics Control Bureau and darknet trafficking

## Mandate and Operation Melon
- **NCB constituted 17 March 1986 under NDPS Act 1985 section 4(3)**; HQ New Delhi, under **MHA**, headed by a Director General. Source's 14 November 1985 date is the Act's commencement, not NCB's formation. [Constitution order](https://www.mha.gov.in/en/commoncontent/office-order).
- Coordinates State/Central enforcement under NDPS and related Customs/drug laws; intelligence on routes/seizures/networks, direct investigations, international cooperation and drug-abuse coordination. Cooperation with UNODC/INTERPOL and foreign authorities differs from those bodies being Indian enforcement agencies.
- Source clipping reports **Operation Melon, NCB Cochin Zonal Unit**, against darknet vendor **Ketamelon**: alleged 600 shipments in 14 months; seized **1,127 LSD blots,131.66 g ketamine and₹70-lakh digital assets**. These are reported seizure/allegation figures, not convictions.
- Geographic recall: **Golden Crescent=Afghanistan–Pakistan–Iran; Golden Triangle=Myanmar–Laos–Thailand**. These names identify regions, not uniform guilt of populations.
- **2025 PYQ:** ED enforces Fugitive Economic Offenders Act 2018 but belongs to **Revenue/Finance**, not MHA; DRI and DG Systems/Data Management rows belong to Revenue/Finance—**only two rows correct**. Demand reduction/rehabilitation requires health/social-justice institutions as well as interdiction.
