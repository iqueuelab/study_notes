# Bodo identity and the 2020 peace accord

## Identity and agreement
- Bodos/Boros are a major tribal ethnolinguistic community of Assam, within the Bodo-Kachari group; not Assam's largest ethnolinguistic group overall.
- Bodo belongs to the Tibeto-Burman branch; included in the Eighth Schedule by the **92nd Amendment, 2003**.
- Third Bodo accord signed **27 January 2020** among Union/Assam governments and Bodo representatives, including NDFB groups and ABSU. Earlier accords: 1993 and 2003.
- BTAD nomenclature changed to **Bodoland Territorial Region**, within Assam; **no new State** was created. BTC autonomy derives from the Sixth Schedule; ST recognition is distinct from Fifth/Sixth Schedule area administration.
- Provisions: stronger council powers, boundary review through the prescribed mechanism, ₹1,500 crore development package over three years, cultural/educational support and rehabilitation of surrendered cadres. [MHA implementation account](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=1913900&lang=2&reg=48).
- The original four district names were Kokrajhar, Baksa, Chirang and Udalguri; subsequent district/boundary changes should not be hidden by treating that list as permanent.
- Reported “over 80% implemented” is the Home Minister's dated assessment, not proof every grievance ended. Non-Bodo representation, land rights, livelihood outcomes and monitoring remain important.

## September 2025: culture, GI products and regional livelihoods
- Ahead of BTR elections, the issue highlights youth-led GI documentation begun in **2021**, reporting **21 registrations during November 2023–May 2024** from over 50 identified products; an outreach drive seeks inclusion of **26 communities**. Treat these as the issue's dated programme figures.
- Examples: **Aronai, Dokhona, Zwmgra** textiles; **Kham, Serza, Siphung** instruments; traditional foods and beverages such as **Napham, Gwkha Gwkhwi and Maibra Zwu Bidwi**. GI villages, skills, documentation, infrastructure and direct market links are proposed to convert cultural recognition into producer income; registration alone does not assure higher prices.
- Bodos belong to the Bodo-Kachari grouping; other BTR communities include Koch-Rajbongshis, Hajongs, Gurkhas and Adivasi groups. Inclusive benefits and non-Bodo representation matter alongside identity protection.
## Sixth Schedule correction
- **Article 244(2)** and the Sixth Schedule govern relevant tribal areas of **Assam, Meghalaya, Tripura and Mizoram**; **Article 275(1)** concerns grants. **Article 244A** permits Parliament to form an autonomous State within Assam. The PDF's **Articles 244B and 244C do not exist**.
- ADC powers over specified land/non-reserved forest, cultivation, water-use and customs, local justice and taxation derive from Schedule provisions with conditions, assent and jurisdictional limits; these are not unlimited sovereign powers. [Constitution](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/19632/1/the_constitution_of_india.pdf).
