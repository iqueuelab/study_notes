# Warfare deception and electronic decoys

## Deception across domains
- Decoys redirect surveillance/weapon targeting by reproducing visual, radar, thermal or acoustic signatures, buying time and forcing expenditure of costly weapons. Physical camouflage/dummy positions and electronic countermeasures complement each other.
- **Rafael X-GUARD**: reusable fibre-optic towed active RF decoy, integrated with aircraft electronic warfare systems, deployable/retractable in flight against radar-guided air/ground-launched missiles. Manufacturer brochure gives **65 kg for pod and decoy**; the PDF's unqualified 30 kg is not a comparable full-system weight. [Rafael brochure](https://www.rafael.co.il/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/X-Guard-Brochure-English-03-2024.pdf).
- The source alleges Rafale/SPECTRA use in Operation Sindoor, PL-15E diversion and urgent procurement; these are reported claims, not verified engagement outcomes or proof an aircraft loss report was false. Do not infer platform integration from a generic manufacturer capability description.
- Comparators: **Leonardo BriteCloud**, **AN/ALE-50/55** aircraft decoys; **Nulka**, Australian–US active naval decoy, mimics ship radar returns. Chaff counters radar; flares chiefly infrared seekers; acoustic decoys target torpedoes/submarine detection.
- Ground dummies can reproduce heat/sound as well as shape; source reports an **April 2025 Indian T-90 decoy requirement**. Not every inflatable is effective against every sensor. Source labels “Inflatech” Russian; do not memorise that attribution without verification.
