# MH-60R Seahawk: naval aviation and INAS 335

## December 2025: second Seahawk squadron announced
- **INAS 335 (Ospreys)**, India's second **MH-60R** helicopter squadron, was **scheduled for commissioning on 17 December 2025 at INS Hansa, Goa**, with Navy Chief **Admiral Dinesh K. Tripathi**. The December 15 issue describes a forthcoming event; avoid its past-tense “commissioned” wording. [14 December Defence release](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2203716&lang=1&reg=3).
- Multi-role maritime helicopter: **anti-submarine warfare, anti-surface warfare, maritime surveillance and search-and-rescue**; integrated weapons, sensors and avionics support fleet operations against conventional and asymmetric threats.
- Shipborne integral aviation extends surveillance, response and operational reach, relevant to Indian Ocean trade routes, energy security and maritime presence. Blue-water capability means sustained operations at distance; acquiring helicopters alone does not establish unlimited global reach.
- First MH-60R squadron **INAS 334**, commissioned **6 March 2024 at INS Garuda, Kochi** ([Defence Ministry](https://www.pib.gov.in/Pressreleaseshare.aspx?PRID=2012589&lang=2&reg=48)); expansion combines imported platforms with domestic capability-building, rather than calling the MH-60R itself indigenous.
- Existing PYQs: **INS Astradharini (2016)—torpedo launch and recovery vessel (c)**; **Dhruv (2008)—advanced light helicopter (c)**. These full questions are already in existing banks.
