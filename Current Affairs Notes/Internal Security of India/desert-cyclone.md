# Exercise Desert Cyclone: India–UAE land forces

## December 2025: Desert Cyclone II
- **Second India–UAE joint army exercise**, scheduled **18–30 December 2025, Abu Dhabi**. Indian contingent **45**, mainly **Mechanised Infantry Regiment**; UAE contingent of similar size from **53 Mechanised Infantry Battalion**. **53 is the battalion number, not UAE personnel strength**. [Defence Ministry, 17 December](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2205132&lang=2&noshow=1&reg=3).
- Mechanised infantry combines dismounted troops and armoured mobility; joint training builds compatible tactics, procedures/communications and operational readiness in desert/urban settings. Exercise diplomacy supports India–UAE defence ties alongside maritime/counter-terrorism cooperation; participation does not establish a treaty alliance or unilateral Indian security-provider status.
- **India–UAE Comprehensive Strategic Partnership formalised 2017**, already covered. The source's supplementary payments/food-corridor/civil-nuclear graphic repeats older cooperation; an agreement does not mean India operates the **Barakah plant**.
