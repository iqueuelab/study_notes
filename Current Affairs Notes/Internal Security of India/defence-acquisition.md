# Defence Acquisition Council: approvals and procurement stages

## 12 February 2026: capital-acquisition approval
- **DAC chaired by Rajnath Singh** accorded **Acceptance of Necessity (AoN)** for proposals worth about **₹3.60 lakh crore**. AoN under **DAP 2020** establishes acquisition need; it is not a signed procurement contract, final price agreement or delivered fleet. [Defence Ministry](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2227018&lang=2&reg=3).
- IAF: **MRFA/Rafale**, combat missiles for stand-off deep strikes, and **Air-Ship Based High Altitude Pseudo Satellite (AS-HAPS)** for persistent ISR/electronic-intelligence/communications functions. Source reports **114 fighters**, with a majority to be manufactured in India; manufacturing intention differs from achieved technology transfer.
- Navy: source reports **six P-8I** long-range maritime-reconnaissance/anti-submarine aircraft and **4 MW marine gas-turbine electric generator, Make-I**. Army: **Vibhav anti-tank mines**, overhaul of **T-72, BMP-II and armoured recovery vehicles**. Coast Guard: **electro-optical/infrared systems for Dornier aircraft**.
- Capability logic: air dominance/deep strike, maritime situational awareness/ASW, armoured readiness and coastal surveillance; indigenous development aims to reduce external dependence without eliminating procurement, testing and lifecycle-support requirements.
- PYQ recall: **2024(d), none** of Rafale, MiG-29, Tejas Mk-1 is fifth-generation; **2025(b), two correct pairs**—Dornier 228 maritime patrol and C-17 transport; **IL-76 is transport**, not supersonic combat. Both already banked.

## 3 March 2026: signed maritime contracts
- **₹5,083 crore total**, two contracts: **six ALH Mk-III (Maritime Role)** for the Coast Guard, and **Surface-to-Air Vertical Launch Shtil missiles** for the Navy. These are signed contracts, beyond the AoN stage discussed earlier; delivery is a subsequent step. [Defence Ministry release](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2234987).
- **HAL, ₹2,901 crore:** helicopters with role equipment, engineering support and performance-based logistics; **Buy (Indian-IDDM)** category. Twin-engine maritime helicopters support surveillance, search-and-rescue, offshore-asset/fisher protection and operations from ships/shore. Project estimates **200+ MSMEs and ~65 lakh person-hours** describe expected industrial work, not completed jobs.
- **JSC Rosoboronexport, ₹2,182 crore:** VL-Shtil missiles and associated holding frames for frontline warships; rapid-reaction/all-weather engagement reinforces layered air defence. The imported missile contract should not be labelled Indian-IDDM merely because the helicopter contract uses that category.
