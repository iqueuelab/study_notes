# K-4 SLBM: sea-based nuclear deterrence

## December 2025: reported submarine launch
- Issue reports **DRDO-developed K-4**, a **nuclear-capable submarine-launched ballistic missile**, tested from **INS Arighaat in the Bay of Bengal**, with reported range around **3,500 km**. The supporting press account dates the test **23 December 2025**; a news compilation hosted by DRDO preserves that report but is not itself a fresh official test certification. [DRDO-hosted press compilation](https://www.drdo.gov.in/drdo/sites/default/files/drdo_news/NPC25to26Dec2025.pdf).
- **Arihant-class SSBN** delivery system; do not confuse nuclear propulsion with automatically carrying a nuclear warhead on every patrol. A missile test is distinct from completed operational validation/induction. Existing submarine notes identify Arighaat as second Arihant-class boat commissioned **29 August 2024**; that date does not commission K-4 itself.
- Source describes **solid propulsion**, **underwater cold launch** (ejection before rocket ignition clear of the platform) and inertial guidance. Exact stage count, GPS/NavIC correction, manoeuvre performance, payload and four-missile load are reported technical claims not independently established here; avoid treating the infographic as a verified specification sheet.
- **Nuclear triad:** land missiles, air-delivered weapons and sea-based deterrent. Quiet, survivable submarines support **second-strike capability** and credible minimum deterrence; survivability still depends on acoustic protection, secure communication, trained crews and patrol support, not absolute undetectability.
