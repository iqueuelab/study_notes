Immigration and Foreigners Act 2025: administration and safeguards

Editable notes

Consolidated immigration framework: September 2025

  • Immigration and Foreigners Act 2025 (Act 13) received assent 4 April 2025 and commenced 1 September 2025. It replaces the Passport (Entry into India) Act 1920, Registration of Foreigners Act 1939, Foreigners Act 1946 and Immigration (Carriers' Liability) Act 2000. Assent, passage and commencement differ. Official Act, commencement notification.
  • September rules/orders consolidate passport/visa/registration, immigration databases and Bureau of Immigration functions; institutional reporting on foreign students, biometrics and enforcement involve specified authorities/conditions. The clipping's blanket “all foreigners” formulation should not override statutory exemptions.
  • Source discusses Assam Foreigners Tribunals, up to three members, review of ex parte orders within 30 days, and additional process/enforcement powers. A tribunal finding, NRC exclusion, detention and deportation are different stages; exclusion of roughly 19 lakh in the 2019 Assam NRC does not itself establish foreign nationality. Powers must be applied through the relevant law and fair procedure. MHA rules/orders collection.
  • Exemption Order 2025: specified Hindu, Sikh, Buddhist, Jain, Parsi and Christian persons from Afghanistan/Bangladesh/Pakistan who sought shelter because of religious persecution/fear and entered on or before 31 December 2024 without valid or with expired documents receive specified passport/visa exemptions. This does not change CAA's 31 December 2014 citizenship cut-off or confer automatic citizenship.
  • Registered Sri Lankan Tamil nationals sheltered up to 9 January 2015 receive specified stay/exit exemptions; do not infer a permanent ban on all citizenship pathways. Nepalese/Bhutanese and registered Tibetans have category/route/document conditions, rather than a blanket exemption for every traveller. Official exemption order.
  • Policy balance: secure borders and reliable identity data with reasoned nationality determination, appeal/review, humane treatment, privacy and clear institutional accountability.
