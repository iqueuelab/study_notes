# Arnala: shallow-water anti-submarine warfare craft

## 8 May 2025: delivery
- **Arnala**, first of **eight GRSE ASW shallow-water craft**, delivered to Navy at **L&T Kattupalli**; GRSE designed/built under PPP collaboration, to **Indian Register of Shipping classification rules**. Delivery is distinct from commissioning/operational induction; May 10 issue should not imply commissioning already occurred.
- Named for **Arnala Fort off Vasai, Maharashtra**; length **77 m**; largest Indian naval warship powered by **diesel engine–waterjet combination**, not largest Navy warship overall. **Over 80% indigenous content**, not necessarily 80% counted components under a specified denominator.
- Roles: shallow/coastal underwater surveillance and ASW; search/rescue, **Low Intensity Maritime Operations**, advanced mine-laying. Shallow-water platforms complement other fleet assets and improve littoral security; announced roles are not evidence of a completed combat deployment.
- Indigenous design/supply chains and public/private shipbuilding cooperation support self-reliance and maritime readiness. Eight refers to this GRSE contract, not necessarily the total national ASW-SWC programme. [MoD delivery announcement](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2127729&lang=2&reg=48).

## Androth delivery versus commissioning
- **Androth**, second of the **eight GRSE ASW-SWC** vessels, **delivered 13 September 2025 at Kolkata**; named for the Lakshadweep island. Correct the coaching text's premature operational “INS/inducted” description: delivery precedes commissioning. [Official delivery](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2166488&lang=1&reg=37).
- Existing Arnala notes cover 77-m craft, over-80% indigenous content, diesel-waterjet propulsion, littoral surveillance/ASW, SAR, LIMO and mine-laying. Source mentions lightweight torpedoes, ASW rockets and sonar; these complement wider fleet/submarine capabilities rather than establish a permanent Lakshadweep deployment.
- **Arnala was commissioned 18 June 2025**, distinct from the 8 May delivery already recorded; the embedded June article is historical background, not a new September commissioning.
- National ASW-SWC programme comprises **16 vessels**; the eight mentioned above are the GRSE order, not the whole programme. [Arnala commissioning record](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2137263&lang=1&reg=37).

## October 2025: Androth commissioning
- INS Androth commissioned **6 October 2025 at Visakhapatnam**, following September delivery; second GRSE ASW shallow-water craft. At 77 m/about 1,500 tonnes and over 80% indigenous content, it strengthens coastal submarine detection/tracking and neutralisation. [MoD commissioning](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2175480&lang=1&reg=6).
- Correct source errors: Arnala commissioned **18 June 2025**, not June 2024; the national programme is sixteen craft, eight each under two shipyard contracts, not four GRSE plus four L&T. Androth takes its name from Lakshadweep; Arnala is named after Maharashtra's fort, so the entire fleet is not named exclusively after Lakshadweep islands. Existing notes cover programme roles, delivery and diesel-waterjet design.
