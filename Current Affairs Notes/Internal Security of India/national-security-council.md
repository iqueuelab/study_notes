# National Security Council and advisory architecture

## Architecture and April 2025 reconstitution
- India's **NSC was constituted in 1998**, not 1999 as the source says; the Prime Minister chairs the apex security-policy body. Defence, Home, Finance and External Affairs ministers bring cross-sector perspectives.
- **Strategic Policy Group (SPG):** inter-ministerial coordination, chaired by the **NSA** under the reconstituted arrangement; includes senior executive, service, intelligence and economic officials. Cabinet Secretary coordinates implementation by ministries and States.
- **NSAB:** outside-government experts provide long-term analysis and policy options on referred security matters. It was constituted in **December 1998**; its chair is distinct from the NSA. **Joint Intelligence Committee:** synthesises agency inputs, including IB/RAW, into assessments. **NSCS:** secretariat, research, coordination and follow-up support.
- The 1 May issue reports former RAW chief **Alok Joshi** appointed NSAB chairman, and seven members added to the **16-member** board: **D. B. Venkatesh Varma, Lt. Gen. A. K. Singh, Air Marshal P. M. Sinha, Rear Admiral Monty Khanna, Rajiv Ranjan Verma, Manmohan Singh and A. B. Mathur**. This balances continuity with defence, diplomacy and intelligence expertise.
- Reconstitution followed Pahalgam and security consultations. Reported operational freedom for armed forces concerned response planning; advisory appointments themselves are not operational command decisions. Keep **NSC, NSAB, NSA, NSCS and CCS** distinct.
