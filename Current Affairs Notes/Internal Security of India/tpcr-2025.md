# TPCR 2025: capability planning, drones and deterrence

## Fifteen-year capability roadmap
- **Technology Perspective and Capability Roadmap 2025** sets out prospective military technology needs, giving public/private industry a planning signal. A roadmap is not a procurement contract or proof systems have entered service. [Defence ministry retrospective](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2210154&lang=1&reg=1).
- Source priorities: credible minimum nuclear deterrence through survivability, secure nuclear command/control, delivery platforms and **CBRN** detection, reconnaissance and decontamination; connect conventional forces, unmanned systems and electronic warfare.
- Desired drone capabilities include long-range/stealth platforms, surveillance and artillery targeting, AI-assisted loitering munitions and EW/CBRN payloads. The reported **1,500 km /60,000 ft** are desired specifications, not verified fielded performance. A loitering munition waits/searches before striking; the issue's “reusable warhead” wording needs specification-level verification.
- Counter-drone priorities include adaptive jamming and electronic denial; a reported **15 km denial bubble** is an aspiration, not guaranteed protection against every autonomous or hardened drone.
- China/Pakistan security pressures and lessons of drone-heavy warfare motivate jointness, indigenous R&D, resilient communications, testing and industry participation. Deterrence requires survivable command chains and reliable capability, beyond equipment counts.
