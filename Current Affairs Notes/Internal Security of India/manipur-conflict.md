# Manipur conflict, hill governance and reconciliation

## Background and legal distinctions
- Imphal-valley Meitei and hill Kuki-Zo/Naga communities face intertwined land, identity, political representation, livelihoods and security tensions. Source **52% Meitei/10% valley geography** and **~40% hill tribes/90% hill geography** are rounded descriptive figures, not proof collective “ownership/control” of every parcel. **40 valley/20 hill constituencies** is not necessarily 40 Meitei MLAs.
- **Article 371 C** provides hill-area legislative-committee/Governor safeguards. Manipur hill areas are **not automatically Fifth-Schedule Scheduled Areas**, nor Sixth-Schedule autonomous areas. Land restrictions derive from applicable statutes/customary systems; ST status under **Article 342** is a distinct constitutional process. [Tribal Affairs constitutional distinctions](https://www.pib.gov.in/newsite/erelcontent.aspx?lang=2&reg=48&relid=75098).
- Armed insurgency, cross-border trafficking/refugee displacement after Myanmar's crisis, Golden Triangle proximity and uneven hill infrastructure complicate governance. Do not conflate refugees, an ethnic community and narcotics networks; source ~30 outfits/40,000 crossers require dated evidence.
## 2023 triggers and dated consequences
- Source sequence: ST-demand litigation; April 2023 High Court direction to consider recommendations; February evictions of **16 households**; State's **10 March** withdrawal decision concerning particular SoO groups; **28 April** mobilisation and **3 May 2023** outbreak. It does not prove the entire tripartite SoO framework terminated or that every missing weapon came from SoO camps.
- Polarisation, looted armouries, divided trust in state/central agencies and weak coordinated response undermined law/order; source ~4,000 looted arms is a dated estimate. Sexual violence, segregation, disrupted schooling/health/trade and displacement require survivor-centred protection and justice.
- **Source snapshot as of 3 May 2024: ** **221 deaths, 60,000 displaced, 4,786 homes burnt, 386 religious sites damaged**; not June 2025 totals. “Exports down 80%” lacks a clear period/series and is not a statewide GDP measurement.
- **Correction for June 2025: ** N. Biren Singh was no longer serving CM; **President's Rule imposed 13 February 2025**. Historical criticism of his government should be time-labelled. [Home Ministry/Parliament](https://www.pib.gov.in/Pressreleaseshare.aspx?PRID=2118913&lang=2&reg=48).
- Reconciliation: impartial investigations, weapons recovery, coordinated accountable security, relief/rehabilitation, dialogue, lawful land processes and equitable development. Naga participation/positions vary; smaller groups such as Hmar/Kom/Aimol/Chothe are not uniformly interchangeable with one ethnic umbrella.
## Cultural and tribe PYQs
- **2018: Chapchar Kut–Mizoram; Khongjom Parba–Manipur correct; Thang-Ta–Sikkim wrong → (b), 1 and 2.** Thang-Ta is Manipur's martial tradition.
- **2009 Shompen: Nicobar Islands (b)**, chiefly Great Nicobar; **2004 Onge: Andaman and Nicobar Islands (a)**, chiefly Little Andaman. Historical racial labels in source explanations are outdated classification shortcuts, not necessary exam facts.
