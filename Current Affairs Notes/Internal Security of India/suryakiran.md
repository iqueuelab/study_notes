# Exercise Suryakiran: India–Nepal army cooperation

## November 2025: Suryakiran XIX
- **19th India–Nepal joint army exercise**, **Pithoragarh, Uttarakhand**, **25 November–8 December 2025**. Indian contingent **334**, mainly **ASSAM Regiment**; Nepal contingent **334**, mainly **DEVI DATTA Regiment**. Annual exercise with alternating hosts; actual issue coverage occurs on 28 November. [Defence Ministry release](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2194129&lang=2&noshow=1&reg=48).
- Rehearses **sub-conventional operations under a UN Chapter VII scenario** and battalion-level synergy: jungle warfare, mountain counter-terrorism, **HADR**, medical response, environmental conservation and integrated ground–aviation operations.
- Source highlights **UAS, drone ISR (intelligence, surveillance and reconnaissance), AI-supported decisions, unmanned logistics and armoured protection**. Training improves tactics/techniques/procedures, interoperability, mountain survival, intelligence coordination and rescue readiness.
- **Chapter VII of the UN Charter** addresses threats to peace, breaches of peace and aggression; exercising in such scenarios does not itself create a UN mandate for real operations or mean every peacekeeping mission operates identically under Chapter VII. Military diplomacy complements India–Nepal trust and professional exchanges; avoid reducing Nepal's own interests to the label “buffer”.
