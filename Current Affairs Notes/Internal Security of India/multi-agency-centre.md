# Multi Agency Centre and intelligence coordination

## Revamp inaugurated: 16 May 2025
- Home Minister inaugurated upgraded **MAC, North Block, New Delhi**: IB-led counter-terrorism **intelligence fusion/sharing mechanism**, in existence since **2001**, providing continuous coordination between central and State agencies.
- Official account: revamp **over ₹500 crore**, **28 organisations**, connectivity expanded to **all police districts**. A connected district is not proof every threat is detected instantly; source's “₹500 crore” is rounded. [MHA announcement](https://www.pib.gov.in/Pressreleaseshare.aspx?PRID=2129141&lang=2&reg=48).
- Participants include intelligence agencies, armed forces, State police and CAPFs; **BSF is itself a CAPF**, so do not count it as a wholly separate institutional category when describing the total.
- Fusion combines information across agencies, identifies links and disseminates actionable intelligence; benefits for counter-terrorism, borders, insurgency and organised-crime networks depend on analysis and operational follow-through.
- Speech cited **Operation Sindoor** and anti-Naxal action in **Karreguttalu hills, Chhattisgarh–Telangana border**, as coordination examples. These do not prove the newly inaugurated network alone caused earlier operations' outcomes.
- Mains priorities: timely verified information, common standards, secure access controls, accountable intelligence use, district-level capacity and feedback from operations; avoid silos, duplication, cyber compromise and indiscriminate data accumulation.
