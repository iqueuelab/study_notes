# Abdali / Hatf-II missile

## Prelims distinctions

- **Pakistan; short-range ballistic missile**, road-mobile, solid propellant; source associates development with **SUPARCO**.
- January 2025 source specifications: **single stage**, **180–200 km** range, **250–450 kg** payload, inertial guidance, reported **150 m CEP**, launch mass **1,750 kg**, length **6.5 m**, diameter **0.56 m**.
- These are source/version-specific figures, not a guarantee that every test/variant has the same range or payload; **CEP is a statistical accuracy measure**.
- Carried by **Transporter-Erector-Launcher (TEL)**; ballistic differs from BrahMos cruise-missile flight.
- Alleged Bangladeshi purchase consideration is a **reported proposal**, not an independently verified completed procurement.
