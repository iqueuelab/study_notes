# Exercise Konkan: India–UK maritime interoperability

## October 2025: carrier cooperation
- Bilateral Indian Navy–Royal Navy exercise, begun in 2004, brought INS Vikrant and the HMS Prince of Wales-led British Carrier Strike Group together in the western Indian Ocean. The issue describes a four-day opening phase and the first exercise bringing both countries' carrier groups together; this should not be confused with the entire deployment's duration.
- Anti-submarine warfare, air defence, cross-deck aviation and surface/underwater coordination test interoperability. Carrier strike groups combine a carrier's aircraft with escorts, submarines and logistics; integration requires communications, procedures and replenishment as well as platforms.
- Konkan formed part of Britain's Operation Highmast deployment. Port visits and further air exercises were planned; these were prospective in the 6 October issue. Strategic context: Indo-Pacific maritime security, freedom of navigation, India–UK defence cooperation and India's SAGAR/IPOI approach. Distinguish policy alignment from an alliance commitment.
