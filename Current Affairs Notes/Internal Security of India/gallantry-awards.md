# Gallantry awards: order and eligibility

## Two principal orders
| In the face of the enemy: conventional wartime label | Not in the face of the enemy: conventional peacetime label |
| --- | --- |
| **Param Vir Chakra** — highest | **Ashoka Chakra** — highest |
| **Maha Vir Chakra** — second | **Kirti Chakra** — second |
| **Vir Chakra** — third | **Shaurya Chakra** — third |
- President confers awards for gallantry/courage/self-sacrifice under each award's conditions. “Wartime” does **not require a formally declared war**; the criterion is action in the face/presence of the enemy. “Peacetime” is not a ban on recognising dangerous counter-terrorism, counter-insurgency or rescue acts.
- PVC: most conspicuous bravery or daring/pre-eminent valour/self-sacrifice in the face of enemy; MVC/VC recognise respective degrees of gallantry in enemy presence, on land, sea or air. Ashoka Chakra recognises exceptional courage/self-sacrifice other than in enemy presence; Kirti/Shaurya follow in order.
## Dates and eligible persons
- **26 January 1950**: PVC, MVC and VC instituted, effective retrospectively from **15 August 1947**. **1952**: Ashoka Chakra Classes I, II and III instituted, likewise retrospective; **January 1967** renamed Ashoka, Kirti and Shaurya Chakra.
- Eligibility includes Army/Navy/Air Force, territorial/reserve and other lawfully constituted forces under award rules. **Civilians, police and paramilitary personnel** can receive the not-in-enemy-presence awards; civilian eligibility should not be assumed identical for every decoration.
- Service medals **Sena Medal, Nao Sena Medal, Vayu Sena Medal** recognise service-specific gallantry or distinguished devotion to duty, depending on the award category; awards can arise in wartime or peacetime. They are distinct from the six Chakra awards.

## 16 December 2025: Param Vir Dirgha
- President **Droupadi Murmu** inaugurated **Param Vir Dirgha at Rashtrapati Bhavan on Vijay Diwas, 16 December**, displaying portraits of all **21 Param Vir Chakra recipients**. Corridors previously displayed British ADC portraits; the issue reports **96 portraits** replaced. [President's Secretariat](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2204485&lang=1&reg=3).
- Gallery combines military remembrance and public education with the government's decolonisation narrative. **Vijay Diwas commemorates the 1971 victory and Bangladesh's liberation**; gallery timing is distinct from the institution of PVC in 1950, already covered.
- Example: **Captain Vikram Batra, Kargil 1999**. Existing notes explain enemy-presence criterion, posthumous awards and wartime/peacetime orders; photographs of the medals do not create additional awards.
- Source places the change alongside **Rajpath → Kartavya Path; Ross Island → Netaji Subhas Chandra Bose Dweep; Neil → Shaheed Dweep; Havelock → Swaraj Dweep; Port Blair → Sri Vijaya Puram; Mughal Gardens → Amrit Udyan**, and the Navy ensign redesign. These are background examples, not all new December 2025 renamings.
- Constitutional link: **Article 51A(b)** cherishing freedom-struggle ideals, **(c)** sovereignty/unity/integrity and **(e)** harmony. The source's ideological “Sanatan values” framing is governmental/editorial interpretation, not constitutional wording or a legal condition for citizenship.

## January 2026: Shubhanshu Shukla
- Republic Day-eve approval included **one Ashok Chakra, three Kirti Chakras and twelve Shaurya Chakras**, including a posthumous Shaurya Chakra, in the source's Chakra-series announcement.
- **Group Captain Shubhanshu Shukla** was honoured with **Ashok Chakra** following his **June 2025 ISS mission**; the President conferred it at **Kartavya Path on 26 January 2026**. The **highest gallantry award other than in the face of the enemy** is distinct from the Padma Awards. [Defence Ministry ceremony record](https://www.pib.gov.in/ShowAlbum.aspx?albumid=215444&lang=1&reg=3).
- Ax-4 made him the **first Indian aboard ISS**, not the first Indian ever in space; the earlier Rakesh Sharma milestone remains distinct. Existing notes explain Ashok/Kirti/Shaurya hierarchy and service-specific gallantry medals.
