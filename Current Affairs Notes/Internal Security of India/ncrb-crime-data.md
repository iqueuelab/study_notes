# NCRB: crime statistics and digital policing

## Crime in India 2023: released in September 2025
- **Reference year 2023**, publication in 2025. Issue reports **6.24 million cognisable crimes, +7.2%** against 2022; murder **−2.8%**, rape **−5.9%**, children **+9.2%**, women **+0.7% (~4.48 lakh cases)**, SCs **+0.4%**, STs **+28.8%**. Registered cases are not a victimisation census: reporting, registration and classification affect comparisons.
- Cybercrime **86,420**, versus **65,893 in 2022**, **+31.2%**; **rate 6.2 per lakh population**, versus **4.8**, correcting the PDF's percent signs. Fraud motive **68.9%**, sexual exploitation **4.9%**, extortion **3.8%**. Source historical totals: 2018 **27,248**; 2019 **44,735**; 2020 **50,035**; 2021 **52,974**. These are registered-case counts, not all online harms.
- Source State counts: **Karnataka 21,889**, Telangana **18,236**, Uttar Pradesh **10,794**. Higher counts alone do not establish higher per-capita risk or prove digital development causes crime. Karnataka clipping examples: personation **18,166** and obscene-video transfer **1,007** cases.
- Economic offences: **2,04,973 (+6%)**; forgery/cheating/fraud **1,81,553**, criminal breach of trust **22,759**, counterfeiting **661**. Source also reports IPC283 public obstruction **+62%** and Motor Vehicles Act violations **+103%**; categories and laws differ, so these cannot all be labelled violent offences.
- BNS took effect **1 July 2024**: 2023 data predates it. The issue's “last report under IPC” is unjustified because 2024 includes six pre-BNS months and legacy cases continue; publication year does not determine applicable substantive law.
## NCRB and response
- **NCRB, established 1986, MHA, New Delhi**: national repository/analysis of crime/criminal information; origins include Tandon Committee, National Police Commission **1977–81** and MHA task force **1985**. Implements/monitors **CCTNS**, connecting police data; Digital Police portal supports police searches/citizen services, **NDSO** and **CyTrain** training. Central Finger Print Bureau supports fingerprint identification. [Official institutional account](https://digitalpolice.gov.in/DigitalPolice/AboutUs).
- Publications: **Crime in India; Accidental Deaths & Suicides in India; Prison Statistics India**. NCRB technical/operational role for the women/children cybercrime portal should be distinguished from I4C's wider coordination and State police investigation.
- Priorities: digital literacy, prompt fraud reporting, cyber forensics/evidence capacity, CERT-In/State cyber-cell coordination, police/judicial training and accountable cooperation with banks/fintech/platforms. Embedded farmer-suicide clipping identifies Maharashtra/Karnataka concentration; the report is an ADSI context, not evidence that crime tables establish agricultural causes.
