# Sonobuoys and undersea domain awareness

## January 2025 cooperation

- India–USA **co-production of sonobuoys** for Indian Navy **Undersea Domain Awareness (UDA)**; complements submarine tracking and anti-submarine warfare.
- **Expendable electro-mechanical acoustic sensors**; aircraft/helicopters deploy canisters in patterns. Surface float/radio antenna communicates while **hydrophone/sensors descend to a selected depth**.
- **Deploy → listen/emit → transmit → analyse contacts → locate/track**. Multiple measurements improve localisation; detection is not automatically a perfect position fix.

| Type | Function |
| --- | --- |
| Passive | Listen to target/environment sounds; no active sonar pulse |
| Active | Emit acoustic energy and analyse returning echoes |
| Special purpose | Measure temperature, ambient noise and other environmental conditions |

- Components: buoyant housing, sensors/hydrophone, battery/power and radio/data link to aircraft/ship.
- Applications: detect/classify and support engagement of adversarial submarines/ships; oceanographic/environmental research and marine-mammal acoustic monitoring.
- Prelims trap: **sonar uses sound**, not radar radio waves to travel through deep water; radio link is generally above-water communication.
