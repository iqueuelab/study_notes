# Samudra Pradakshina: tri-service women sailing expedition

## September 2025 launch and planned route
- Defence Minister Rajnath Singh **virtually flagged off on 11 September 2025** from Mumbai's Gateway of India the first tri-service all-women circumnavigation expedition; **10 officers** of Army, Navy and Air Force aboard indigenous **IASV Triveni**, a 50-foot yacht. [Defence Ministry launch record](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2165584&lang=2&reg=3).
- Planned easterly voyage: approximately **26,000 nautical miles /nine months**, return envisaged May 2026; Equator twice and Capes **Leeuwin, Horn and Good Hope**. These were launch plans, not completed milestones at issue date.
- Builds seamanship, endurance and joint service cooperation; illustrates women's participation and indigenous maritime capability. **Navika Sagar Parikrama, INSV Tarini, 2017–18** was an earlier Navy all-women circumnavigation; the 2025 distinction is tri-service participation, not the first all-women voyage of any kind.
