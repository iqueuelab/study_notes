# Central Industrial Security Force

## Institution and August expansion reporting
- **CISF**, Ministry of **Home Affairs**, headquarters **New Delhi**, headed by a Director-General; established under **CISF Act 1968**, operationally raised **10 March 1969**; **1983 amendment** made it an armed force of the Union. The Act title year differs from raising year. [India Code](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/indiacode/handle/123456789/1599?view_type=browse).
- Embedded clipping: Centre approved expansion with about **58,000 additional recruits over five years**; source reports roughly **1.88 lakh existing personnel** and deployment at **359 establishments**. These are issue-period staffing/deployment figures and recruitment plans.
- Industrial/critical infrastructure security: airports, nuclear/space/power installations, ports, Delhi Metro, Parliament and selected monuments; specialised **fire wing**, VIP security and security consultancy, including approved private-sector deployment. Airport-security expansion followed the IC-814 hijacking; private-security mandate expanded through later legal changes.
- Source's seven-sector list is an organisational snapshot. Its claim CISF is the **only CAPF with daily public interaction** is overbroad; retain distinctive infrastructure/public-facing mandate, not exclusivity. Practice answer is **(a) 1 only**, not nonsensical printed '1 and 3 only' for two statements.
