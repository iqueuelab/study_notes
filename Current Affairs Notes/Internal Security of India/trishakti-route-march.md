# High-altitude readiness: Trishakti Corps route march

## Sikkim endurance exercise
- Source reports **Trishakti Corps** troops completed a **six-day route march up to 17,000 ft in Sikkim**, carrying full battle load through steep/rugged tracks, extreme cold and icy winds. Corps headquarters: **Sukna, West Bengal**.
- Tests endurance, mental resilience, teamwork, logistics and adaptability in high-altitude operations. Technology, drones and communications complement trained troops; weather or enemy disruption can degrade them. Eastern Himalayan readiness connects to the China frontier; the **2017 Doklam standoff** is relevant background, not the date of this march.
