# 26/11 and India’s counter-terrorism architecture

## Attack and April 2025 development
- **26–29 November 2008:** ten Lashkar-e-Taiba terrorists attacked Mumbai after maritime infiltration; targets included CST, Taj/Oberoi hotels and Nariman House. **166 victims died**, not “over 170 victims”; counting slain attackers separately changes totals.
- **10 April 2025:** NIA secured **Tahawwur Hussain Rana's extradition from the US** under the bilateral treaty following judicial proceedings. Extradition permits prosecution; it is not itself an Indian conviction. [NIA release](https://nia.gov.in/sites/default/files/Document/enc_088ae6bc4fa4733e429dbcaafe47c780.pdf).
## Security reforms
| Layer | Reform and function |
|---|---|
| Coast | Navy overall maritime-security responsibility; Coast Guard coastal coordination with marine/state police; patrols, radar/sensor networks and vessel identification |
| Intelligence | Strengthen IB's **Multi-Agency Centre (MAC)**, State links and continuous actionable intelligence-sharing; MAC existed before 26/11 |
| Investigation/law | **NIA Act 2008** and UAPA amendments; NIA investigates scheduled offences through statutory processes, not a general replacement for State policing |
| Rapid response | NSG hubs, trained State quick-response units, better weapons, communications and deployment logistics |
| Cooperation | Cross-border evidence, extradition, terror-financing action and diplomatic pressure |
- Four early **NSG hubs: Mumbai, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kolkata**, operationalised **30 June–1 July 2009**; not a timeless claim that NSG has only four hubs. **Operation Black Tornado** relates to the Mumbai response. [Government account](https://www.pib.gov.in/newsite/erelcontent.aspx?lang=2&reg=48&relid=51795).
- MHA's **2018–19 report** says vessels above **20 metres length** were mandated to carry AIS. That is a length-based source-era rule, not 20 tonnes, nor proof every small fishing boat is covered/tracked. AIS/radar/data fusion complement patrols; transmission gaps remain. [MHA report](https://www.mha.gov.in/web/sites/default/files/AnnualReport_18_19.pdf).
- Coastal Surveillance Network combines radar, AIS and day/night cameras; State police and fishing communities remain essential. [Coast Guard](https://www.indiancoastguard.gov.in/coastal-security).
## Gaps and strategic assessment
- Local police capacity, intelligence-to-action coordination, small-vessel identification, interoperable communications, cyber intelligence and accountability need continued investment.
- Proposed **NCTC** encountered federalism/State-policing objections and did not become the established institution described by its proposal.
- Pakistan entered FATF increased monitoring in **June 2018**, and was removed in **October 2022** following action-plan progress. This multilateral AML/CFT assessment was not an automatic punishment solely for 26/11. [FATF](https://www.fatf-gafi.org/en/countries/detail/Pakistan.html).
- Strategic restraint after 26/11 and later **Uri 2016/Balakot 2019** responses are separate decisions. The earlier restraint does not automatically establish legal justification for every later use of force; assess evidence, proportionality, deterrence and escalation.
