# AK-203 rifle and Indo-Russian small-arms production

## AK-203 and localisation
- **7.62×39 mm Kalashnikov-family assault rifle**, intended to replace INSAS rifles using 5.56×45 mm ammunition; gas-operated rotating-bolt action, automatic/semi-automatic fire and 30-round magazine. Source approximate weight **3.8 kg without magazine**, cyclic rate **700 rounds/minute**; its **400–800 m** range figure depends on target/sighting conditions, not guaranteed precision at 800 m.
- Made at **Korwa, Amethi, Uttar Pradesh**, by **Indo-Russian Rifles Private Limited (IRRPL)**, incorporated in 2019. Indian partners are **AWEIL 42.5% and Munitions India 8%**; Russian partners **Kalashnikov 42%, Rosoboronexport 7.5%**. OFB was corporatised into several companies; it did not simply become AWEIL alone. [Defence Production company profile](https://www.ddpmod.gov.in/sites/default/files/9c3f5b880209268b9f396531b32801dd9bd35cc3f66f48932b502c43112d919e/96af4231d657538353efbf754977f9048b1dca9bd0211e9919edd14d2d2f1ed2.pdf).
- July source reports a **~₹5,200-crore, over-six-lakh rifle programme**, **48,000 completed** and delivery target **December 2030**; these are the issue's dated progress/forecast, not all rifles delivered.
- Reliability in difficult terrain, modern optics/accessories and local production support readiness, technology absorption, jobs/MSMEs and reduced import dependence. Calibre alone does not establish battlefield effectiveness; training, maintenance, accuracy and logistics matter.
