# Indigenous dogs in Assam Rifles: Tangkhul Hui and Kombai

## February 2026: pilot breeding and training
- Source describes **Assam Rifles'** pilot to induct **Tangkhul Hui (Ukhrul, Manipur)** and **Kombai (Tamil Nadu)** into its dog squad, following a 2025 MHA direction promoting indigenous breeds. Prospective full induction **March 2027** and a **15-year breeding plan** are schedules, not completed deployment of every planned dog.
- **Tangkhul Hui**: traditional hunting dog, selected for endurance and local terrain adaptation; **Kombai**: traditional hunting/guard breed, noted for strength and guarding. Local adaptation and lower dependence on imported stock are selection rationales; disease resistance and operational performance require veterinary/training evidence for each line.
- **Assam Rifles**, originating 1835, guards the India–Myanmar border and conducts counter-insurgency/anti-smuggling tasks; **MHA administrative /Army operational control**. Dog roles include explosives/narcotics detection, tracking and guarding; source identifies **Assam Rifles Dog Training Centre, Jorhat** for the breeding/training work. Do not equate it with the Army RVC as one organisation.
