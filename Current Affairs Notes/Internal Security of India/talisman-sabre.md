# Talisman Sabre: multinational military interoperability

## July 2025 exercise
- Biennial Australia–US exercise begun **2005**; **11th edition opened 13 July 2025**, with an opening estimate of **35,000+ personnel from 19 countries**, including India. Major domains: sea, land, air, space and cyber; amphibious operations, live fire, logistics and command coordination build interoperability. [Australian Defence opening release](https://www.defence.gov.au/news-events/releases/2025-07-13/exercise-talisman-sabre-2025-opens).
- Activities across Queensland, Northern Territory, Western Australia, New South Wales and Christmas Island; **Papua New Guinea hosts elements for the first time outside Australia**. Opening estimates should not be silently replaced with later final totals.
- India gains joint operational experience and stronger Australian/Indo-Pacific defence ties. Participation does not itself establish a mutual-defence alliance; Quad is a separate grouping.
