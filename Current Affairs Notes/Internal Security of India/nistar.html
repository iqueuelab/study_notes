Internal Security of India

Nistar: indigenous diving support vessel

Editable notes

Delivery and capability

  • Hindustan Shipyard Limited, Visakhapatnam delivered Nistar to the Indian Navy on July 8, 2025, announced by PIB July 9. The source wrongly calls July 9 commissioning; delivery and commissioning are separate events. Navy delivery release.
  • First indigenously designed/built diving support vessel of this project: approximately 118 m, 10,000 tonnes, 75% indigenous content, built to Indian Register of Shipping classification rules.
  • Saturation diving to 300 m; side diving to 75 m; ROV operations to 1,000 m. These are different capabilities, not interchangeable diver depths. Acts as mothership for a Deep Submergence Rescue Vehicle, supporting submarine rescue, deep-sea diving, salvage and underwater repair.
  • Sanskrit name conveys liberation/rescue/salvation. Capability supports maritime safety, shipbuilding self-reliance and sustained operations away from shore. PYQ distinction: INS Astradharini is a torpedo launch/recovery vessel; Dhruv is an advanced light helicopter.

Commissioning: 18 July 2025

  • The July 19 clipping reports INS Nistar commissioned, following delivery on July 8. Commissioning, delivery and earlier launch are distinct milestones; existing diving-depth/ROV/DSRV capabilities remain unchanged.
  • Official commissioning release reports over 80% indigenous content and 120 MSMEs, compared with the PDF/earlier delivery summary's roughly 75%. Treat these as differently reported event-stage figures; the commissioning announcement is the later official account. Navy commissioning release.

Pacific Reach 2025

  • INS Nistar arrived at Changi, Singapore, 14 September 2025, its maiden foreign port call, for Pacific Reach 2025 (XPR 25) beginning 15 September. Biennial multinational submarine-rescue exercise: harbour expert exchanges/cross-deck visits, then practical rescue/intervention at sea in the South China Sea. Navy release.
  • Existing notes explain indigenous diving-support/mothership capability. This deployment develops rescue coordination and interoperability; port arrival, exercise commencement and commissioning are separate milestones.
