# Exercise Trishul 2025 and joint operations

## November 2025: tri-service preparedness
- Issue announces **Trishul** beginning **3 November**, described as a 12-day exercise; preserve this as the announced schedule. **Navy leads**, with **Western Naval Command**, Army **Southern Command** and IAF **South Western Air Command** principal formations; Coast Guard, BSF and other central agencies support integrated operations.
- Geography: **creek/desert sectors of Gujarat and Rajasthan** and **North Arabian Sea**, including amphibious operations. Objectives: joint procedures, platform/infrastructure interoperability, network integration, joint **ISR**, electronic/cyber warfare and coordinated land–sea–air action.
- Later MoD confirmation corroborates these formations, geography and objectives; it is verification, not a claim that culmination was known on 3 November. [MoD release](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2189935&lang=2&noshow=1&reg=48).
- Issue lists **20,000+ personnel**, T-90/Arjun tanks, Rafale/Su-30MKI, warships, **INS Jalashwa** and landing craft; retain as reported deployment details rather than a final audited strength. Joint exercises test indigenous systems, shared logistics/communications and readiness; they do not themselves establish that proposed integrated theatre commands are already operational.
