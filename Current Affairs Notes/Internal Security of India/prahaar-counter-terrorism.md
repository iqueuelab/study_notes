# PRAHAAR: national counter-terrorism policy

## February 2026: integrated counter-terrorism strategy
- MHA released **PRAHAAR** on **23 February 2026**. Its seven pillars are **Prevention; Response; Aggregating internal capacities; Human rights and rule of law; Attenuating radicalisation; Aligning international efforts; Recovery and resilience**. These form its acronym; “National Counter-Terrorism Policy and Strategy” is the document title.
- Threats span land, maritime and aerial routes, cross-border sponsorship, sleeper cells, organised-crime funding/logistics, encrypted communications, online recruitment, cyberattacks and drones. Intelligence-led prevention needs Centre–State and multi-agency cooperation, investigation/prosecution capacity and financial disruption.
- Protect power, transport, ports, aviation, defence, space and nuclear infrastructure; preparedness, rapid response and post-attack recovery complement prevention. The policy rejects association of terrorism with any religion, ethnicity, nationality or civilisation.
- Implementation needs lawful surveillance, trained State police, accountable coordination, community trust and safeguards for human rights; a strategy document does not replace criminal statutes. [MHA policy](https://www.mea.gov.in/Images/CPV/PRAHAAREnglish_23022026.pdf).
