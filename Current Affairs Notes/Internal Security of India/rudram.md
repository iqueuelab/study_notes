# Rudram: anti-radiation and air-to-surface missiles

## June 2025: verified capability and source caution
- DRDO's indigenous Rudram family supports the IAF's suppression of enemy air defences: anti-radiation weapons home on emissions from hostile radar, reducing exposure of strike aircraft. Standoff launch distance improves aircraft survivability; it does not guarantee interception is impossible.
- **RudraM-II** was successfully flight-tested from **Su-30 MK-I off Odisha on 29 May 2024**. Official release describes a **solid-propelled air-launched air-to-surface system** and validation of propulsion/control/guidance. [Defence Ministry](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2022076).
- The June 11 PDF gives inconsistent Rudram-4 ranges (300+, 550–600 and 600+ km), an unsupported hypersonic classification and claims Mirage/Rafale integration. These are **unverified specifications**, not established operational capabilities; likewise do not memorise its Rudram-II/III speed/range table as official data. GPS support alone cannot establish GPS-denied guidance capability.
- Indigenisation reduces supplier dependence but testing, aircraft integration, production and service induction are separate milestones.
- **2014 PYQ recall:** Agni-IV is a two-stage **solid-fuelled surface-to-surface ballistic missile**, about **4,000 km** range; the liquid-only and 7,500-km statements are false → **(a) 1 only**.
