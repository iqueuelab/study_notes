# Operation Secure and infostealer disruption

## January–April operation; June 2025 announcement
- INTERPOL coordinated **Operation Secure** with **26 participating jurisdictions including India**: over **20,000 malicious IP addresses/domains** disrupted, **41 servers** and **100+ GB** seized, **32 suspects** arrested. Figures describe the combined operation, not India's individual totals. [INTERPOL, 11 June](https://www.interpol.int/News-and-Events/News/2025/20-000-malicious-IPs-and-domains-taken-down-in-INTERPOL-infostealer-crackdown).
- **Infostealers** extract passwords, browser credentials/cookies, payment and wallet data; stolen logs facilitate further fraud, ransomware or breaches. Locating servers, mapping physical networks and cross-border takedowns combine intelligence sharing with national law-enforcement powers.
- CERT-In (incident response) and NCIIPC (critical information infrastructure protection) are relevant Indian institutions; their general relevance does not prove each executed the raids or individual operational roles assigned in the source.
