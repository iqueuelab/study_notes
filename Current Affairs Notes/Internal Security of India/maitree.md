# Maitree: India–Thailand military cooperation

## MAITREE-XIV, September 2025
- Fourteenth bilateral India–Thailand exercise at **Joint Training Node, Umroi, Meghalaya**, scheduled **1–14 September 2025**; **120 Indian personnel, Madras Regiment**, and **53 Thai personnel, 1st Infantry Battalion, 14th Infantry Brigade**. Previous edition: Fort Vachiraprakan, Tak Province, Thailand. [Defence release](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleaseIframePage.aspx?PRID=2163075&lang=2&reg=48).
- Company-level counter-terrorist operations in semi-urban terrain under a **UN Charter Chapter VII** scenario; learn tactics, coordination and interoperability. Bilateral army exercise, not a trilateral exercise with Singapore.
- Begun **2006**, alternates hosts; supports Act East engagement and India–Thailand defence ties. Chapter VII concerns threats to peace/breaches/aggression; an exercise scenario is not itself a UN operational deployment.
