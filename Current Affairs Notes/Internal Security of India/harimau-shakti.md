# Exercise Harimau Shakti: India–Malaysia army cooperation

## December 2025: fifth Harimau Shakti
- **5–18 December 2025**, **Mahajan Field Firing Range, Rajasthan**; bilateral **Indian Army–Royal Malaysian Army** exercise, **fifth edition**. India mainly **DOGRA Regiment**; Malaysia **25th Battalion**. [Ministry of Defence](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2199345&lang=1&reg=1).
- Rehearses sub-conventional/counter-terrorist operations in a **UN Chapter VII scenario**: cordon/search-and-destroy, heliborne movement, helipad security, casualty evacuation; also martial arts, combat reflex shooting and yoga. Shared tactics/techniques/procedures improve interoperability and defence cooperation, relevant to Act East.
- Chapter VII addresses threats to peace, breaches of peace and aggression; exercise scenarios are not themselves authorisation for real UN deployments.
- Source mislabels its **Mitra Shakti-2023** question as a **2023 PYQ**; exam attribution requires correction/review rather than equating exercise year with exam year. Recall **Mitra Shakti = India–Sri Lanka**, Aundh/Pune, joint counter-terrorism and air-force participation; source key **2,3,4 (d)**.
