# Vibrant Villages Programme: border development and livelihoods

## November 2025: J&K roadmap and the two programmes
- Embedded news reports a roadmap for **124 strategic villages in 43 border blocks across eight J&K districts under VVP-II**. These are reported planning figures, not completed villages. The explanatory pages mostly describe older **VVP-I** and should not be mistaken for the geographical scope of VVP-II.
- **VVP-I**: announced in **Budget 2022**, approved in **2023**, launched at **Kibithoo, Arunachal Pradesh in April 2023**. Northern-border coverage in Arunachal Pradesh, Sikkim, Uttarakhand, Himachal Pradesh and Ladakh: source **2,963 villages, 46 blocks, 19 districts; 663 priority villages**. Announcement, approval and launch are separate milestones.
- **VVP-II**, announced by Cabinet on **4 April 2025**, is a **100% Centre-funded Central Sector Scheme**, outlay **₹6,839 crore**, through **FY2028–29**, for selected strategic villages adjoining international land borders **other than the northern border already under VVP-I**, including J&K. [Cabinet decision](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2118730&lang=1&reg=3).
- Development logic: roads, reliable power, water, telecom, health/education, skills and financial inclusion → crafts, farming, homestays/tourism and local enterprises → sustainable livelihoods and reduced distress outmigration → stronger border communities and security. Residents are rights-bearing partners, not merely strategic assets.
- Gram Panchayats and district administration prepare locally grounded action plans and converge existing schemes. Scheme saturation is an objective, not evidence that every household already receives every service. Tourism/outreach including Seema Darshan should complement local livelihoods and ecological safeguards.
- **BADP** is an older MHA border-development programme; avoid overlapping work/funding. The PDF’s 16 States/2 UTs, 460 blocks/117 districts are background counts without a clear reference year, not newly verified November coverage. Nor should VVP-I’s northern focus be presented as a restriction on VVP-II.
