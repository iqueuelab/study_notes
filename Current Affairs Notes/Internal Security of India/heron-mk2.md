# Heron Mk II: surveillance UAVs and emergency procurement

## December 2025: reported emergency purchases
- Embedded report says **Army and IAF placed additional Heron Mk II orders**, while Navy was acquiring the type for the first time, moving beyond its older Searcher fleet. This is source-period procurement reporting, not confirmation that every ordered airframe was delivered or operational.
- **Israel Aerospace Industries (IAI), medium-altitude long-endurance UAV**; satellite communications support beyond-line-of-sight control and intelligence collection. EO/IR, synthetic-aperture radar and electronic-intelligence payloads have different functions/configurations; ‘all weather’ does not imply immunity to every weather condition, jamming or air defence.
- Manufacturer specification: **up to 45-hour endurance, above 35,000 ft ceiling**, configuration/mission dependent; the source's ‘30+ hours’ is a broad lower description, not a contradictory guaranteed Indian mission profile. [IAI product specification](https://www.iai.co.il/product/heron-mk-ii/).
- Operational rationale after **Operation Sindoor**: persistent LAC/LoC and maritime surveillance, timely targeting information, networked situational awareness. Imports can bridge capability gaps while indigenous **TAPAS-BH, Archer and Ghatak** develop; they are distinct programmes, not interchangeable operational equivalents.
- The issue describes emergency procurement of complete systems **up to ₹300 crore**, and local production, maintenance, training/integration partnerships with Indian PSUs/private firms. This is a reported procurement route, not an unlimited waiver of all controls or proof full technology transfer already occurred.
