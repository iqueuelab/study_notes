# Samudra Pratap: Coast Guard pollution response

## 5 January 2026: commissioning in Goa
- **Rajnath Singh commissioned ICGS Samudra Pratap in Goa**, first of two GSL pollution-control vessels. This is India's first **indigenously designed** PCV, not the Coast Guard's first pollution-control ship of any kind. Over **60% indigenous content**; official release describes it as the ICG fleet's largest at commissioning. [Defence Ministry](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2211397&lang=2&reg=3).
- Length **114.5 m**; source rounds displacement to **4,200 tonnes** while the induction release specifies **4,170 tonnes**. **23 December 2025 induction/delivery** and January commissioning are separate milestones. [Induction record](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2208089&lang=1&reg=3).
- Source specifications: speed **22+ knots**, endurance **6,000 nautical miles**; oil-spill detection, viscous-oil recovery, contaminant analysis, oil–water separation and offshore firefighting. Supports pollution response, surveillance and maritime safety in the EEZ and beyond; pollution response also protects fisheries/coastal livelihoods.
- **EEZ extends up to 200 nautical miles from baselines**; sovereign rights over resources differ from territorial sovereignty over the **12-nautical-mile territorial sea**. Contiguous zone reaches **24 nautical miles**, not an additional 24 miles beyond territorial sea. A vessel's operating reach beyond the EEZ does not expand India's jurisdiction.
