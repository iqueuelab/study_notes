# Exercise Sanjha Shakti: civil–military emergency coordination

## January 2026 exercise
- **8–9 January 2026**, **Diggi Range, Khadki Military Station, Pune**, under **Maharashtra, Gujarat and Goa Area, Southern Command**. **Sanjha Shakti** was a joint **Military Civil Fusion** exercise aimed at coordinated emergency/security response. [Defence Ministry, 9 January](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2213006&lang=1&reg=1).
- Indian Army worked with **16 civilian agencies**, including **Maharashtra Police, Force One and fire-fighting departments**; **over 350 civilian personnel**, excluding Army personnel, participated.
- Tested interoperability, communications, decision-making and rapid coordinated response under realistic security/public-safety/disaster scenarios. **Force One** is Maharashtra's specialist counterterror force.
- Mains use: whole-of-government preparedness needs shared procedures, rehearsed responsibilities, information exchange and resource coordination; conducting a drill is distinct from proof of readiness in every actual crisis.
