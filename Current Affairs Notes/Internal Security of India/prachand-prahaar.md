# Prachand Prahaar: integrated tri-service firepower

## March 2025 exercise
- **25–27 March, Arunachal Pradesh**, Eastern Command: tri-service integration of surveillance, decision-making and precision engagement in a high-altitude setting.
- Sensors/UAVs/airborne and other surveillance inputs → shared picture → target identification → coordinated fires → assessment.
- Naval aviation can contribute over land; participation does not imply naval ships operating in mountains. Integration improves awareness, not perfect knowledge of every target.
- **Poorvi Prahar (2024)** was a distinct aviation-focused exercise. Prachand Prahaar is also distinct from the **LCH Prachand helicopter**.
- Importance: interoperability, rapid coordination, logistics and deterrence along sensitive frontiers; test communications, weather limitations and deconfliction.
