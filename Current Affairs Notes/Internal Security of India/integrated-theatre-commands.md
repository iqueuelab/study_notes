# Integrated theatre commands and jointness

## Purpose and existing structures
- Theatreisation integrates Army/Navy/Air Force assets under a theatre commander for operational planning/application in a defined area; joint intelligence, logistics, communications and training aim to reduce duplication and improve response.
- Source baseline **17 single-service commands: Army 7, Navy 3, Air Force 7** includes operational/support commands, not 17 identical geographical theatres. **Andaman and Nicobar Command (2001)** is joint; **Strategic Forces Command (2003)** manages strategic nuclear forces under the nuclear-command framework, distinct from conventional geographical theatreisation.
- Kargil **1999** exposed coordination gaps; subsequent Kargil-review/Group of Ministers, Naresh Chandra and Shekatkar reform discussions support jointness with differing recommendations. Do not attribute every CDS/theatre proposal identically to all committees.
- US/Chinese unified commands offer comparators; **China's Western Theatre Command** covers the India-facing region. Other countries' joint arrangements are not identical structures simply labelled theatre commands.
## May 2025 proposal: not three operational new commands
- Issue reports proposals for **Pakistan-facing Western (Jaipur)**, **China-facing Northern (Lucknow)** and **Maritime (Coimbatore)** commands. Maritime locations, previously Karwar and other southern sites, have varied in reporting; source's Coimbatore is a **reported proposal, not a verified final notified headquarters**.
- Proposed **four-star Vice CDS**, **three-star Deputy CDS**, theatre operational control and service chiefs' **Raise, Train and Sustain** roles remain organisational ideas subject to final decisions. Source's prospective defence-minister-led **National Defence Committee** is likewise not an established operational body.
- Cross-posting, common training/logistics and headquarters restructuring are steps towards integration. **Joint Logistics Nodes at Mumbai, Guwahati and Port Blair operational since 2021**; source's claim Mumbai was already designated the first common defence station in 2024 conflates reported plans with completion. [MoD jointness account](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressNoteDetails.aspx?ModuleId=3&NoteId=154490&lang=2&reg=3).
## Law and implementation
- **Inter-Services Organisations (Command, Control and Discipline) Act 2023**, effective **10 May 2024**, empowers specified joint commanders to exercise disciplinary/administrative control over personnel from different services while respective service laws remain applicable. It supports future joint structures; it does **not itself create all proposed theatres**.
- Allocation of scarce air assets, clear theatre/service-chief authority, interoperable intelligence/communications, logistics, training/career culture and manpower-neutral restructuring are central challenges. Resource savings and faster decisions are aims to test, not assured merely by renaming headquarters.
- Jointness strengthens deterrence and coordinated two-front/sea responses; indigenous technology complements organisation but theatreisation does not automatically make imported weapons indigenous.
