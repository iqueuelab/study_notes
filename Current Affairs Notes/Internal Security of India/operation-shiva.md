# Operation Shiva and Amarnath Yatra security

## July 2025 operation
- Indian Army's **Operation Shiva** secures Amarnath Yatra with CAPFs, J&K Police and civil authorities; combines counter-terrorism, convoy/route surveillance, crowd/disaster response and high-altitude medical support.
- Source deployment snapshot: **8,500+ troops**, **50+ counter-UAS units and 50 electronic-warfare systems**, UAV/PTZ-camera surveillance and bomb-detection/disposal teams. These are dated reported figures, not permanent establishment strengths.
- **150+ medical personnel**, two Advanced Dressing Stations, nine Medical Aid Posts, 100-bed hospital and 26 oxygen booths; source reports 200,000 litres oxygen support. Engineers widen tracks, build/maintain crossings and clear landslides; EME repairs equipment and signals teams maintain communication.
- Live convoy tracking helps coordinate response to terror incidents, accidents and weather hazards. Preventive deployment must remain adaptable to terrain and emerging drone threats.
## Shrine and route recall
- Amarnath cave in J&K, source identifies Pahalgam/Anantnag region, **about 3,888 m** high; ice stalagmite revered as Shiva Lingam. Religious explanations of lunar waxing/waning should not be presented as an established physical law.
- Traditional **Pahalgam–Chandanwari–Sheshnag–Panchtarni** approach is longer; **Baltal–Domail** route is shorter, steep, approximately 14 km. Routes converge near the shrine. Do not confuse Amarnath cave with the distinct **Amarnath Peak** (source elevation ~5,186 m).
- High altitude, rain, landslides, snow and crowd pressure require medical screening, oxygen, warning/evacuation capacity and coordinated civilian support.
