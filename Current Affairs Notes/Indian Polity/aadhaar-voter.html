Bihar SIR and identity documents

  • Supreme Court's 8 September 2025 order directed Aadhaar be accepted as the twelfth document for identity in inclusion/exclusion from Bihar's revised voter list, referring to RPA 1950 section 23(4). It expressly does not prove citizenship; authorities may verify authenticity and request further evidence. Source's “August 8/2024” dates are wrong. Supreme Court order.
  • Aadhaar is a 12-digit resident identity number issued by UIDAI; residents can include eligible non-citizens. Identity authentication does not establish every eligibility requirement for voting, domicile or age; citizenship and the electoral registration law still apply.
  • The clipping records a separate challenge to Aadhaar's use and concerns about exclusion during SIR. Notice/listing for a future hearing is not a final decision invalidating SIR. Keep enrolment access, verification, privacy and correction/appeal safeguards together.
  • 2018 PYQ: (d), neither statement. Aadhaar does not prove citizenship/domicile and can be deactivated/omitted on prescribed grounds; issuance does not make it legally immutable. The source's generic non-use claim is not necessary to answer the question.
