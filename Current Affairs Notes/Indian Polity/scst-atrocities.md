# SC/ST atrocities law: anticipatory bail and victim protection

## Kiran v. Rajkumar Jivraj Jain: 1 September 2025
- **2025 INSC 1067**, not a 2024 Supreme Court ruling: Court set aside Bombay HC anticipatory bail. FIR **26 November 2024** alleged assault, caste abuse, attacks on women and retaliation concerning voting; allegations and bail assessment are distinct from conviction. [Supreme Court judgment](https://api.sci.gov.in/supremecourt/2025/26602/26602_2025_16_1501_63923_Judgement_01-Sep-2025.pdf).
- **Section 18**, SC/ST (Prevention of Atrocities) Act 1989 bars anticipatory bail where a **prima facie statutory offence** is disclosed. Courts test applicability without a mini-trial; where no prima facie offence is made out, the bar does not automatically apply merely because the FIR names the Act.
- Judgment discusses caste-targeted insult/intimidation **within public view**, alleged voting-related retaliation and the Act's protective purpose. Public view differs from simply any insult anywhere; insulting a person who belongs to an SC/ST is not by that fact alone the defined offence. The FIR's Section **3(1)(o)** concerns post-poll retaliatory violence/social-economic boycott; do not use it as a label for every form of electoral coercion.
- Act provides specified atrocity offences, Special/Exclusive Special Courts, Special Public Prosecutors, victim/witness rights and relief under rules; investigation requires a suitably qualified **DSP-level or higher** officer. State implementation and central assistance support protection against intimidation and access to justice.
- **Section 4** punishment for qualifying public servants' wilful neglect is **six months to one year**, not the source's “up to six months”. Offence-specific penalties differ; the PDF's broad minimum/maximum chart and undated 30% conviction statistic should not be treated as universal/current law. [Official Act](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/17492/1/a1989-33.pdf).
- **2015 amendment, effective 2016**, expanded offences and safeguards; **2018 amendment/section 18A** also matters to the bail/investigation framework. Implementation priorities: timely investigation, fair prima facie scrutiny, witness security, relief and accountable public duties.
