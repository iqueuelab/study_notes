Indian Polity

National Commission for Protection of Child Rights (NCPCR)

Editable notes

Institution, composition and appointment

  • Statutory body, established 2007, under the Commissions for Protection of Child Rights Act, 2005; child means below 18. India's CRC accession was in 1992. Central Government appoints chairperson and six members, including at least two women, with expertise in education, health, welfare/development, juvenile justice, psychology/sociology or child law.
  • Chairperson: eminent person with outstanding work for children's welfare. Terms three years, maximum two terms; age ceilings 65 chairperson /60 member. Chair selection committee has three members, chaired by Minister in charge of Women and Child Development, correcting the PDF's Education-Minister claim. Official Act.

Powers and safeguards

  • Reviews safeguards/laws and policy implementation; investigates complaints and acts suo motu; research, awareness, institutional inspections and recommendations to government. Monitors RTE 2009 Section 31, POCSO 2012 and relevant JJ 2015 implementation; it does not replace criminal courts or itself create POCSO Special Courts.
  • Inquiry powers resemble a civil court: summon witnesses, require records/documents and receive evidence. Can recommend prosecution/action or interim relief and approach SC/HC for appropriate directions; recommendations are distinct from a court conviction.
  • Central removal grounds include insolvency, impermissible paid work, incapacity/refusal, abuse of position and conviction involving moral turpitude; relevant statutory grounds require a reasonable opportunity of hearing.
  • POCSO e-Box: online sexual-abuse reporting; MASI, Monitoring App for Seamless Inspection, for child-care institutions; GHAR, GO Home and Re-Unite, tracks restoration/repatriation; source describes Samvardhan vulnerability mapping against child trafficking.
  • Embedded clipping links NCPCR/child protection with the Centre blocking 25 OTT platforms for obscene/vulgar content. Blocking under IT-law procedures is a government action; NCPCR is not itself the blocking authority.
