# Armed Forces Tribunal and judicial review

## Jurisdiction and composition
- **Armed Forces Tribunal Act, 2007**; Ministry of Defence; specialised adjudication for personnel governed by **Army Act 1950, Navy Act 1957, Air Force Act 1950**.
- **Original jurisdiction** over service matters; **appellate jurisdiction** over court-martial orders, findings and sentences; remedies include setting aside unsustainable convictions and modifying sentences as authorised.
- Principal Bench **New Delhi**, regional benches; source lists Chandigarh, Lucknow, Kolkata, Guwahati, Chennai, Kochi, Mumbai, Jabalpur, Jaipur and Srinagar. Bench locations/territorial arrangements change; official list uses **Jammu**, so do not memorise source Srinagar as definitive. [AFT jurisdiction](https://aftdelhi.nic.in/index.php/home/jurisdiction).
- Benches have **judicial and administrative members**; source describes retired HC judges and senior armed-forces officers/JAG. Recruitment qualifications must be read under applicable Act/rules, not reduced to 'any retired officer'.
- **Procedure Rules, 2008**, proceedings in English; natural justice and statutory procedure.
## Appeal ≠ constitutional judicial review
- Act provides a statutory appeal route to the **Supreme Court subject to statutory conditions**.
- **High Court judicial review under Articles 226/227 remains**; **Balkrishna Ram (9 January 2020)** expressly affirms this. PDF's blanket 'only Supreme Court' excludes this distinction incorrectly. [Supreme Court judgment](https://api.sci.gov.in/supremecourt/2017/4913/4913_2017_15_1501_19348_Judgement_09-Jan-2020.pdf).
- Source January 2025 discussion: additional benches for J&K/Himachal to reduce pendency; treat proposed expansion separately from actual constitution of a bench.
## Embedded tribunal recall
- **42nd Amendment Act, 1976**, **Part XIV-A**: **Article 323A**, public-service administrative tribunals, Parliament; **323B**, tribunals for specified other matters, Parliament or state legislatures within competence.
- **Administrative Tribunals Act, 1985**: CAT/SAT framework; do not assume every specialised tribunal, including AFT, is automatically created under 323A.
- Examples in graphic: CAT, NGT, ITAT, CESTAT, SAT (securities), water-disputes tribunals; old **COMPAT** label is historical because competition appeals moved to NCLAT.
- Expertise and access must accompany institutional independence, vacancies filled and constitutional review.
