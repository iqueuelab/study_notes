National Commission for Scheduled Tribes: mandate and implementation

Editable notes

Constitutional basis and added functions

  • Article 338A, inserted by the 89th Amendment Act, 2003; separate NCST began 19 February 2004. It monitors safeguards, investigates complaints, advises development planning and reports to the President; civil-court inquiry powers do not make it a criminal court.
  • The 2005 Rules prescribe eight additional fields: minor forest produce ownership; lawful water/mineral rights; viable livelihoods; relief/rehabilitation of development-displaced people; prevention/remedy of land alienation; community forest protection/afforestation; full PESA implementation; and measures concerning harmful shifting cultivation. These are oversight/recommendatory functions, not automatic conveyance of ownership of every mineral. Tribal Affairs official account.

September 2025 implementation review

  • Source reports an 11-member internal committee and three subcommittees to prepare an implementation report: Jatothu Hussain—livelihoods/MFP/water/minerals; Asha Lakra—land alienation/displacement/rehabilitation; Nirupam Chakma—PESA/forests/shifting cultivation.
  • Staff/funding shortages and weak follow-up explain the implementation concern. Coordination with Tribal Affairs, MoEFCC, States and Gram Sabhas, public reporting and attention to FRA/PESA rights matter. The source's first-in-20-years description is a reporting claim; the 2005 mandate itself is not newly created in 2025.
  • Shifting cultivation varies by fallow cycle, ecology and customary rights; do not infer every traditional jhum practice necessarily degrades land or that this mandate authorises coercive displacement.

Commission timeline: source corrections

  • Special Officer under original Article 338 (1950); Kalelkar Commission 1953, Mandal Commission 1979 under Article 340; 65th Amendment Act 1990 gave the combined SC/ST commission constitutional footing; 89th 2003 split it; 102nd 2018 constitutionalised NCBC under 338B.
  • 105th Amendment Act is 2021, not 2016: restored States' identification of SEBCs for their own purposes. Legislative Department record. Statutory NCBC in 1993 arose under its Act, not simply under Article 340.
  • Rohini Commission constituted 2 October 2017, not 2018. Official constitution date.
  • 2023 PYQ panel: only NCBC is constitutional; NHRC/NCDRC are statutory and Law Commission executive. The source prints only answer (a), omitting other options; retain explanation but hold question for review. The three commissions shown are examples, not an exhaustive count of every constitutional commission.
