# CAG: independence, appointment and public audit

## Constitutional framework
| Article | Provision |
|---|---|
| 148 | Appointment by President; independence and removal safeguards |
| 149 | Duties/powers prescribed by Parliamentary law |
| 150 | Form of Union/State accounts prescribed by President on CAG's advice |
| 151 | Reports to President/Governor for laying before respective legislature |
- Appointment is by warrant under the President's hand and seal; no statutory selection collegium was established in the March 2025 source period. A PIL/notice does not enact the proposed reform.
- **Six years or age 65, whichever earlier**, comes from the CAG's 1971 service law, not an express tenure clause in Article 148. Further office under Union/State governments is prohibited after leaving office. [CAG framework](https://cag.gov.in/en/page-constitutional-provisions).
- Removal follows the same manner/grounds as a Supreme Court judge: proved misbehaviour/incapacity and special-majority parliamentary process. Salary and specified service rights cannot be varied to disadvantage after appointment; office expenses are charged expenditure.
## Scope and reform
- Audits receipts/expenditure and bodies according to law, ownership, financing and entrustment provisions. It does **not automatically audit every private firm**, nor are all PPPs/NGOs automatically exempt.
- CAG reports support PAC scrutiny; audit findings are not themselves criminal convictions or orders to spend money.
- Reforms proposed: transparent merit-based selection, protection from conflicts, skilled timely audits, accessible reports and stronger legislative follow-up. Safeguard independence while avoiding interference in ordinary executive policy choices.

## September 2025: AI and remote public audit
- CAG is developing an **LLM trained on past inspection reports**, intended to retrieve institutional knowledge, analyse documents, identify risks/anomalies and assist drafting. A first version was expected by **November 2025**, a forecast at issue date.
- Digitised records support risk-based remote/hybrid audit; Telangana's Stamps and Registration compliance-audit pilot is the example. Receipts audits advanced faster because of standardised digital data; PM GatiShakti geospatial evidence can support infrastructure scrutiny.
- **Connect Portal** was scheduled for **19 September 2025**, providing a unified interface for about **10 lakh auditee entities**; this issue announces a plan, not completed rollout or independently measured benefits.
- Human auditors must validate evidence, materiality and findings, protect confidential records and guard against hallucinated conclusions or inherited bias. Faster drafting does not itself prove better audit quality.
- **2012 PYQ: (c), statements 2 and 3**. PAC discusses CAG reports and investigators may use findings; CAG has neither emergency exchequer control nor judicial prosecution powers. Its constitutional independence remains essential when adopting AI.
