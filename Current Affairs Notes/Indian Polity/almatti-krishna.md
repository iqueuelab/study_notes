# Almatti dam and Krishna water sharing

## Project and dispute
- **Almatti dam, Krishna River, north Karnataka**, part of **Upper Krishna Project**; irrigation/storage and hydropower. It is not on Tungabhadra, which is a Krishna tributary.
- Upstream Karnataka’s height/storage increase raises downstream Andhra Pradesh water-availability concerns; reservoir operation, seasonal timing and allocations matter alongside physical height.
- Krishna basin spans **Maharashtra, Karnataka, Telangana and Andhra Pradesh**; distinguish basin membership from each stretch of main river. Do not extrapolate the source’s two-State Almatti focus into exclusion of Maharashtra/Telangana from the broader tribunal/legal dispute.
- **Article 262; Inter-State River Water Disputes Act 1956** support adjudication. An award on allocations/storage does not automatically supply all construction/environmental permissions; assess legal stage and implementation conditions.
- Comparators: Cauvery (including Karnataka/Tamil Nadu), Mahanadi (Chhattisgarh/Odisha), Ravi–Beas and Mullaperiyar (Kerala/Tamil Nadu, including dam safety). Shared rivers require dependable data, equitable use, drought coordination and transparent releases; every listed dispute has a different legal history.
