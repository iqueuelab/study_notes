# Central Vigilance Commission: powers and accountability

## October 2025: Vigilance Awareness Week
- Observed **27 October–2 November 2025**, theme **Vigilance: Our Shared Responsibility**. Preventive vigilance links complaint/case disposal, capacity, asset management and digital initiatives with administrative integrity. [Official campaign](https://www.pib.gov.in/Pressreleaseshare.aspx?PRID=2183039&lang=2&reg=48).
- Established by executive resolution **1964** following Santhanam Committee recommendations; statutory status under **CVC Act 2003**, not a constitutional body. Composition: Central Vigilance Commissioner plus **up to two Vigilance Commissioners**. President appoints on recommendation of **PM, Home Minister and Lok Sabha Leader of Opposition** committee; tenure **four years or age 65**, whichever earlier.
- Supervises vigilance administration and specified **CBI/DSPE Prevention of Corruption Act investigations**, reviews investigation/sanction progress, advises authorities and can cause inquiries within statutory jurisdiction. It is not a general police force/prosecuting court and cannot dictate the disposal of a particular CBI case. Annual report goes to President for laying before Parliament. [CVC Act](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/2067/1/A2003-45.pdf).
- Removal for proved misbehaviour/incapacity involves President and Supreme Court inquiry; specified statutory grounds have a separate route. Advisories, civil-court inquiry powers and reporting should not be confused with power to impose criminal sentences or enforce all recommendations.
- **Whistle Blowers Protection Act 2014 was not brought into force** in the government's February 2025 reply. CVC receives public-interest disclosures under **PIDPI Resolution 2004** arrangements; do not attribute an operating nationwide statutory protection regime to the uncommenced Act. [Government clarification](https://www.pib.gov.in/Pressreleaseshare.aspx?PRID=2100256&lang=2&reg=48).
- The text's blanket 'government permission for Joint Secretary and above' is outdated: distinguish judicial invalidation of the former rank-based DSPE protection, prior approval for specified official decisions under amended PCA section 17A, and prosecution sanction. Jurisdiction/conditions matter. [Supreme Court quarterly record, 2014](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s3ec0490f1f4972d133619a60c30f3559e/documents/misc/year_-_2014_vol.ix_issue_no._2_april_-_june_2014.pdf).
- Transparent appointments, reasoned follow-up on advice, whistleblower confidentiality, sound procurement controls, coordination with CBI/Lokpal and staffing support accountability. Making every recommendation binding or adding independent police powers are reform proposals.
