# Tamil rupee logo and linguistic federalism

## Identity and constitutional context
- Tamil Nadu's 2025–26 Budget logo used **ரூ**, associated with the Tamil word for rupee. It did not replace RBI-issued currency or the national currency symbol.
- **₹**, adopted in 2010 and designed by D. Udaya Kumar, combines Devanagari र and the Latin R.
- Tamil is one of the 22 Eighth Schedule languages. The banknote language panel has 15 languages, with Hindi and English elsewhere—not only 15 languages on the whole note.
- Article 345 concerns State official-language adoption. Article 347 concerns a Presidential direction recognising a language where a substantial proportion demands it; these are distinct powers.
- Evaluate regional cultural representation, communication and federal accommodation. Political claims about legality are not automatically judicial findings.
