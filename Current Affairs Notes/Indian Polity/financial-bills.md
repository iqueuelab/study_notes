# Money Bills and Financial Bills

## Three categories
| Category | Introduction and recommendation | Rajya Sabha / joint sitting |
|---|---|---|
| Money Bill, **110** | Only listed money matters and incidental provisions; LS only, President's recommendation | RS recommends within **14 days**; LS may reject recommendations; no joint sitting |
| Financial Bill I, **117(1)** | Money matters plus other substantive provisions; LS only, President's recommendation for introduction | Ordinary bicameral passage; joint sitting possible on deadlock |
| Financial Bill II, **117(3)** | Involves Consolidated Fund expenditure without being 110/117(1); either House | President's recommendation needed before consideration/passage; normal bicameral procedure |
- Speaker certifies under **110(3)**; “final” does not mean wholly immune from constitutional judicial review.
- Taxation, borrowing, Consolidated/Contingency Funds, appropriation and specified account/audit matters are Article 110 anchors. Fines, fees or local taxation alone do not make a Money Bill.
- Finance Bill title is not itself conclusive; examine contents. Demands for grants, Appropriation Bill and taxation legislation perform different budget functions.
- Mains: efficient fiscal legislation must retain bicameral scrutiny and federal checks; classification disputes concern substance, not labels.
