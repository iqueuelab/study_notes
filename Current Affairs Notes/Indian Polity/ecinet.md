# ECINET: integrated electoral digital services

## Development-stage platform
- **ECI announced ECINET on 4 May 2025**, envisaged after the March CEO conference: unify **40+ web/mobile applications** with simpler UI/UX and fewer logins, serving electors, officials, parties and civil society. This June issue describes **development/planned rollout**, not proof every service was already migrated. [ECI announcement](https://www.pib.gov.in/Pressreleaseshare.aspx?PRID=2126682&lang=2&reg=48).
- Includes **Voter Helpline, Voter Turnout, cVIGIL, Suvidha 2.0, Saksham, KYC, ESMS**; desktop/smartphone access envisaged. Officially authorised entry and primary legal records prevailing in a conflict aim to protect data integrity; integration alone cannot guarantee error-free data.
- Governing framework: **RPA 1950/1951, Registration of Electors Rules 1960, Conduct of Elections Rules 1961** and ECI instructions.
- Source estimated reach: about **100 crore electors**, **10.5 lakh BLOs**, **15 lakh BLAs**, **45 lakh polling officials**, **15,597 AEROs**, **4,123 EROs**, **767 DEOs**. These are announcement-era estimates, not proven active platform users; BLAs are party agents, not all government staff.
- Unified services should preserve accessibility, privacy, secure authentication, auditability and assisted/offline routes. Platform integration differs from conducting exit polls or itself altering substantive election law.
## 5 June 2025: proposed turnout reporting
- New clipping describes **presiding officers entering turnout every two hours directly into ECINET** on polling day, automatically aggregating constituency totals; planned use **before Bihar Assembly polls**. This is a rollout proposal, not proof it already operated in every election.
- Post-poll entry before officers leave aims to reduce previous four-to-five-hour reporting delays, subject to connectivity; offline entries should sync when service returns. Approximate turnout trends remain distinct from legally authoritative final records/counts.
- The June issue repeats other platform objectives/services/user estimates already covered in the preceding update. Timelier data needs clear revision rules, audit trails and secure authorised entry; technology does not change statutory record precedence.
