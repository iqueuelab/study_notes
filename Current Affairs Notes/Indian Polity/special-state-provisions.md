# Articles 371–371J: differentiated federal safeguards

## Constitutional framework
- **Part XXI**, special State provisions; the heading “temporary, transitional and special” does **not** make every Article 371 safeguard temporary. Constitutional text controls scope.
| Article | State and principal safeguard |
| --- | --- |
| 371 | Maharashtra/Gujarat: regional development boards, equitable funds and education/employment opportunities; Governor's special responsibility |
| 371A | Nagaland: Parliamentary laws on specified Naga practices/customary justice and land/resources need Assembly resolution; special law/order responsibility under conditions |
| 371B | Assam: Assembly committee for specified tribal areas |
| 371C | Manipur: Hill Areas committee; Governor reporting and Union directions |
| 371D | Andhra Pradesh **and Telangana**: equitable education/public-employment opportunity arrangements |
| 371E | **Andhra Pradesh**: Parliament may establish a university; do not automatically extend it to Telangana with 371D |
| 371F | Sikkim: integration safeguards, Assembly **at least 30**, Governor's special responsibilities |
| 371G | Mizoram: specified religious/social practice, customary justice and land safeguards; Assembly **at least 40** |
| 371H | Arunachal Pradesh: Governor's law/order responsibility; Assembly **at least 30** |
| 371I | Goa: Assembly **at least 30** |
| 371J | Karnataka's Hyderabad–Karnataka / Kalyana Karnataka: development board, equitable resources and local education/employment provisions |
- **371B inserted 1969; 371C 1972**. Article 371's original 1950 form is not identical to today's Maharashtra/Gujarat wording; later additions/changes came by constitutional amendment.
- Recall **371D ≠371E**, **Nagaland ≠Mizoram land wording**; safeguards are specific, not blanket exemptions from every parliamentary law.
