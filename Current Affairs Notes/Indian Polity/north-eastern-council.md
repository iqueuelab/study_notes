# North Eastern Council: statutory composition and regional planning

## Body and role
- **NEC Act, 1971**; Council inaugurated **7 November 1972**, headquartered **Shillong**, administratively under **Ministry of DoNER**. Statutory regional planning/advisory institution; Article263 does not create NEC.
- **2002 amendment** added Sikkim and strengthened regional planning, project formulation, monitoring/evaluation. Members enumerated in section 3: **Governors and Chief Ministers of constituent States +three Presidential nominees**; President nominates Chairman from members.
- **2018 repositioning:** Union Home Minister nominated **ex-officio Chairman**, DoNER Minister Vice-Chairman. Thus the PDF's absolute “Home Minister is not a member of the Council” is misleading: the **2024 PYQ asks the statutory composition after 2002**, not the later chairmanship arrangement. [2018 Cabinet decision](https://www.pib.gov.in/newsite/PrintRelease.aspx?lang=2&reg=48&relid=179941).
- Supports coordinated transport, power, health, education, skills and entrepreneurship; inter-State consultation complements State/Centre planning. Delayed implementation, limited resources, conflict, terrain and coordination gaps impede delivery. Do not categorically equate every NEC role with either an executing department or a body barred from programme implementation.
- Embedded August clipping reports **₹11.31 crore released in July 2025**, including **₹2.09 crore for kiwi orchards**; funds released are not completed project outcomes.
- **2024 PYQ:(a),1,2,3 only**; retain the question's legal/time qualification alongside the 2018 distinction.
