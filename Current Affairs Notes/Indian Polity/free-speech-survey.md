# Free speech: public attitudes and constitutional protection

## Survey interpretation
- *Who in the World Supports Free Speech?*, Future of Free Speech: October 2024 survey, published in 2025; India ranked **24th of 33**, score **62.6**.
- In this sample, 37% of Indian respondents supported government prevention of criticism of government policies; abstract support for speech can differ from support for controversial speech. [Report](https://futurefreespeech.org/who-supports-free-speech-findings-from-a-global-survey/).
- It measures **public attitudes**, not a global ranking of legal protection or actual press freedom. Country/sample coverage, question wording and index construction limit generalisation.
- Article 19(1)(a) protects citizens' expression. Article 19(2) permits reasonable restrictions on specified grounds: sovereignty/integrity, State security, friendly foreign relations, public order, decency/morality, contempt, defamation and incitement to an offence.
- Criticism alone is not a constitutional ground for censorship. Assess legality, necessity, proportionality and procedural safeguards; media literacy and civic education support informed disagreement.
