Indian Polity

Vice-President of India: election, functions and vacancy

Editable notes

Constitutional office

  • Article 63 establishes the office; 64 makes the Vice-President ex-officio Rajya Sabha Chairman; 65 provides presidential substitution. The source clipping reports Jagdeep Dhankhar's resignation on 21 July 2025, addressed to the President.
  • Chairman maintains order, administers committee arrangements under rules and has only a casting vote. While acting as President, does not perform Chairman's duties; Deputy Chairman/other authorised presiding officer handles the House.

Election and eligibility

  • Article 66: electoral college comprises all members of both Houses, elected and nominated; excludes state MLAs. Proportional representation by single transferable vote, secret ballot; Supreme Court finally decides election disputes under Article 71.
  • Indian citizen, 35+, qualified for Rajya Sabha membership; office-of-profit disqualification has constitutional exceptions. Cannot simultaneously retain a parliamentary/state-legislative seat or another office of profit.
  • Article 69 oath administered by President/authorised person: allegiance to Constitution and faithful discharge.

Term, resignation and removal

  • Article 67: five years, eligible for re-election and ordinarily continues until successor enters office; may resign to President.
  • Removal: Rajya Sabha majority of all then members (effective majority) plus Lok Sabha agreement (ordinary majority), with 14 days' notice. No presidential-style impeachment; the summary's generic special-majority label is misleading.
  • Article 68: ordinary election before term expiry; casual-vacancy election as soon as possible, successor receives full five-year term from entry. Article 70 covers presidential contingencies beyond the standard arrangements.
  • When President's office becomes vacant, presidential election must take place within six months; temporary inability is a different Article 65 contingency.
  • Official former Vice-Presidents record confirms Dhankhar’s tenure ended 21 July 2025.

July 24: election preparations

  • Following the resignation, ECI began preparing the electoral-college list, appointing Returning/Assistant Returning Officers and background materials; schedule was still to be announced in this issue.
  • Presidential and Vice-Presidential Elections Act 1952 and Rules 1974 govern conduct under ECI's Article324 authority. By convention a Lok Sabha/Rajya Sabha Secretary-General serves as RO by rotation, assisted by Parliament Secretariat officers.
  • Nomination requires at least 20 proposers and 20 seconders from the electoral college. Party whip does not govern the secret-ballot election. ECI election resources.

September 2025 election result

  • The embedded clipping reports C. P. Radhakrishnan elected Vice-President on 9 September 2025, following Jagdeep Dhankhar's July resignation. Election and assumption of office are distinct; do not describe the September 10 result report as the swearing-in date. ECI election resources.
  • The issue's election, eligibility, oath, term and Article 63–71 material repeats the existing revision. Removal requires a majority of all then Rajya Sabha members and Lok Sabha agreement; its table's generic “special majority” wording does not mean two-thirds.
