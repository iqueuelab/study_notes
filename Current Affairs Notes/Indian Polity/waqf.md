# Waqf administration and the 2025 reform debate

## Basic distinctions
- Waqf: permanent dedication for religious, pious or charitable purposes recognised by Muslim law; includes public and eligible family-benefit forms. **Waqif creates; mutawalli manages; statutory Board supervises**—the manager is not the personal owner.
- Major national statutory anchors: **1954 Act**, **1995 Act**, **2013 amendments**; Central Waqf Council established **1964**. “1955 Act” in the heading is erroneous. [Historical overview](https://www.pib.gov.in/Pressreleaseshare.aspx?PRID=2118415&lang=2&reg=48).
- Registration, surveys, accounts, adjudication and legal challenge operate under law; not every disputed ownership claim is finally established waqf property.
- The source incorrectly suggests a two-thirds Board vote permits sale/gift/mortgage: **2013 section 104A prohibits sale, gift, exchange, mortgage or transfer**; leases have separate rules. [2013 Amendment](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/repealedfileopen?rfilename=A2013-27.pdf).
## Legislative timeline and debate
- **1 April 2025 issue** discusses the pending amendment process originating in 2024; draft provisions are not already law on that date. Passage and commencement followed later in April, so this is a historical debate snapshot.
- Proposed reforms concerned verification/registration, administrative accountability, composition and government-property disputes. Women representation was **already required in 2013**; do not say it first appeared in the 2024 proposal.
- Arguments for reform: transparent records, safeguarding beneficiaries/property and accountable management. Concerns: minority participation, excessive executive intervention, due process and religious autonomy.
- Articles **14, 25, 26 and 300A** frame equality, religious freedom/institutional rights and lawful property deprivation; freedoms operate with constitutional limits, not immunity from all regulation.
- Property-count/land-area estimates require registry date/definition; the “third-largest landowner” claim is not a reliable comparable ranking from the source alone.
- Evaluate final statutory text and court orders separately from the old draft. The undated 15-mark essay is practice, not a verified UPSC PYQ.
