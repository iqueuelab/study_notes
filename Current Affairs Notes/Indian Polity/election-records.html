Indian Polity

Election records, transparency and public trust

Editable notes

What changed in December 2024?

  • Conduct of Elections (Second Amendment) Rules, 2024, S.O. 5517(E), 20 December 2024; made under Representation of the People Act, 1951, section 169, after consultation with ECI.
  • Rule 93(2)(a): inserted “as specified in these rules” after “papers”, narrowing the category open to inspection under that provision. This is an ordinary statutory-rule amendment, not a constitutional amendment. Gazette text.
  • Debate concerns non-prescribed electronic records such as CCTV/webcasting. Do not equate it with abolition of Form 17C, which is prescribed, or cancellation of copies supplied to polling agents under the applicable rules.
  • Form 17C records votes at a polling station; polling agents receive copies. Public online access, candidate-agent access and judicial access are distinct questions. ECI explanation.

Competing considerations

  • Transparency/accountability and voter confidence versus voter privacy, ballot secrecy, security and risk of decontextualised disclosure.
  • Source raises turnout-revision concerns in Odisha, Haryana, Maharashtra and invokes Chandigarh mayor election, litigation and 2024 electoral-bonds judgment; allegations/editorial conclusions are not findings that manipulation occurred in each named election.
  • Article 324: ECI's supervision/direction/control; neutral administration and reasoned disclosure bolster trust. A challenge being filed does not itself invalidate the rule or establish a final court outcome.

Mains response

  • Publish timely, reconciled aggregate data and explain provisional/final differences; preserve auditable records and candidate safeguards.
  • Clearly define access categories/retention, protect identities/ballot secrecy, provide secure redacted access where lawful, independent review and grievance remedies.
  • Evaluate amendment through proportionality, statutory authority and constitutional values; distinguish reform recommendations from existing enforceable disclosure duties.
