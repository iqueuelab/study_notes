# Assam polygamy prohibition: Bill and constitutional questions

## November 2025: Assam prohibition Bill
- The issue reports introduction of the **Assam Prohibition of Polygamy Bill, 2025**. Treat its provisions as the **Bill at the issue date**, not proof of assent or commencement. Polygamy means simultaneous spouses; its existing legality varies with the applicable marriage/personal/customary law.
- Bill excludes **Sixth Schedule areas and Scheduled Tribes** under Article 366(25) read with Article 342. It proposes **up to seven years and fine** for a prohibited subsequent marriage, **ten years and fine** where the former marriage is concealed, and doubled punishment for a subsequent offence after conviction.
- **Correct the coaching penalties:** clause 6(4) provides **up to two years and up to ₹1 lakh** for specified village heads, Qazis, parents/guardians who dishonestly conceal and intentionally participate. Clause 6(5) provides a priest/Qazi knowingly solemnising the marriage **up to two years OR fine up to ₹1.5 lakh**. They do not automatically receive the main offender's seven/ten-year sentence; separate abetment/attempt liability also exists.
- Reporting duties in clause 7 attach to specified village heads/Qazis/parents/guardians; the source's universal “anyone failing to report → two years” is inaccurate. Police preventive intervention, cognizable offences and search/arrest powers are proposed.
- Broad “ordinarily resident” definition includes State-linked documents, government employment/benefits and property. Clause 8 also reaches ordinarily resident persons entering such marriages outside Assam and outsiders conducting them within Assam.
- Conviction restrictions concern State-funded public employment/benefits and the Bill's election clause naming **Panchayati Raj institutions and urban local bodies**. Do not present the coaching addition of Assembly elections as an independently established disqualification: legislative-election disqualifications require the applicable constitutional/Parliamentary framework.
- Balance **Articles 14, 15(3), 21 and 25**, gender justice, religious freedom and tribal autonomy. **Article 371B concerns an Assembly committee for specified tribal areas**, not a general blanket constitutional licence for polygamy. Preserve distinctions between lawful pre-commencement marriages and prospective criminal liability. [Assembly Bill text](https://assambidhansabha.org/assets/uploads/bills/en/En_UGPBK1764419953.pdf).
