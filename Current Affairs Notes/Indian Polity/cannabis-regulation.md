# Cannabis: regulated cultivation and Himachal Pradesh proposal

## January 2026: Himachal policy proposal
- Source reports Himachal Pradesh working on a **licensed medicinal/industrial hemp cultivation policy**, with Cabinet approval still required, and a projected **₹1,000–2,000 crore annual revenue**. A proposal/revenue estimate is not an enacted unrestricted legalisation or realised receipts. Wild cannabis is reported in **Kullu, Mandi and Chamba**; cultivation pockets include **Chhota/Bada Bhangal (Kangra), Karsog (Mandi)**.
- **NDPS Act 1985** regulates cultivation, narcotic preparations and permitted uses. **Section 14** permits government-authorised cultivation for industrial **fibre/seed or horticulture**, subject to conditions; medical/scientific exceptions and licensing must meet the Act/rules. State policy cannot disapply central narcotics controls. [Department of Revenue national policy](https://www.dor.gov.in/sites/default/files/inline-documents/National_Policy_on_NDPS_published.pdf).
- Distinguish plant, fibre, hemp-seed oil, resin and flowering/fruiting tops. **Seeds/leaves when not accompanied by tops are excluded from the Act's cannabis definition**; bhang can still be controlled under State excise law. This is not a blanket exemption for growing any cannabis plant. Industrial hemp and high-THC drug preparations require distinct controls.
- Fibres serve textiles/ropes; seed oil serves varnish/soap and other industrial uses. Source lists traditional **sting poultices, animal haematoma treatment, paddy-germination treatment and threadworm control in Rajouri**; retain as ethnobotanical practices, not proven safe clinical/veterinary advice. Medicinal claims require evidence, product approval and supervised use. Himachal's mountains are not simply part of the Indo-Gangetic plain despite the PDF's list.
- Governance: licence acreage/seed varieties, test composition, trace supply chains, prevent diversion, assure farmer markets, monitor health and enforcement costs before accepting revenue forecasts.
