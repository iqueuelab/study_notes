# Mahadayi river dispute and Kalasa–Banduri diversion

## Basin and proposal
- **Mahadayi/Mhadei/Mandovi:** Western Ghats, Karnataka/Bhimgad region → Goa → Arabian Sea near Panaji; basin includes Maharashtra. Source 111 km length/~76 km Goa is an approximate river-length account; basin membership does not prove the main channel traverses every State identically.
- **Chorao Island/Salim Ali Bird Sanctuary**, estuarine habitats and Western Ghats biodiversity. Source tributaries Rogaro, Kushavati, Nanorem, Nanuz, Valvota, Mapusa require local naming/reach distinctions rather than assuming every listed watercourse is identical in hydrology.
- **Kalasa–Banduri:** divert tributary water toward **Malaprabha basin**, drinking-water needs in Karnataka’s Belagavi, Dharwad, Bagalkot, Gadag. Canals/barrages require hydrological and ecological appraisal.
## Tribunal and federal dispute
- **MWDT constituted 2010 ; award 2018 :** source allocations **Karnataka 13.42 TMC, Goa 24 TMC, Maharashtra 1.33 TMC**. Allocation rights differ from diversion components/conditions; award ≠ all construction clearances. [Tribunal report](https://www.jalshakti-dowr.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/05/MAHADAYI-WATER-DISPUTES-TRIBUNAL-Report-Vol.-VII.pdf).
- Goa challenged the award and argues downstream ecological-flow harm. Source’s **2020 “in-principle environmental approval”** is too vague to prove every forest/wildlife/environmental approval is final/operative.
- Embedded clipping reports Goa controversy over a study of diversion impacts; one claimed projection does not establish realised river damage. **Article 262/Inter-State River Water Disputes Act 1956** provide the adjudication framework; interstate cooperation, transparent flow data and enforceable environmental conditions remain essential.
## Practice wording: main river versus basin
- The undated worksheet asks which States the river “passes through”, but its Karnataka/Maharashtra/Goa answer identifies **basin/dispute States**. The main channel flows through Karnataka and Goa; the basin also includes Maharashtra.
- As literally worded, the worksheet lacks a Karnataka+Goa-only option. Review its wording rather than memorise basin membership as a false main-channel route.
