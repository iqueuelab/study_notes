# Aadhaar and electoral rolls: identity versus citizenship

## Bihar SIR and identity documents
- Supreme Court's **8 September 2025** order directed Aadhaar be accepted as the **twelfth document for identity** in inclusion/exclusion from Bihar's revised voter list, referring to **RPA 1950 section 23(4)**. It expressly does **not prove citizenship**; authorities may verify authenticity and request further evidence. Source's “August 8/2024” dates are wrong. [Supreme Court order](https://api.sci.gov.in/supremecourt/2025/35785/35785_2025_2_26_63969_Order_08-Sep-2025.pdf).
- Aadhaar is a **12-digit resident identity number issued by UIDAI**; residents can include eligible non-citizens. Identity authentication does not establish every eligibility requirement for voting, domicile or age; citizenship and the electoral registration law still apply.
- The clipping records a separate challenge to Aadhaar's use and concerns about exclusion during SIR. Notice/listing for a future hearing is not a final decision invalidating SIR. Keep enrolment access, verification, privacy and correction/appeal safeguards together.
- **2018 PYQ: (d), neither statement.** Aadhaar does not prove citizenship/domicile and can be deactivated/omitted on prescribed grounds; issuance does not make it legally immutable. The source's generic non-use claim is not necessary to answer the question.

## December 2025: access expansion and record integrity
- UIDAI CEO's source-era plan: expand **full-service centres 88→473 bySeptember 2026**, aiming for about **one per two districts**. This is a planned access network, not 473 centres already operating. Proposed **Google Maps** discovery can reduce search/travel friction; online functions do not replace physical biometric capture.
- Source reports district **field verification for adult enrolment**, with NRI/OCI/eligible foreign-resident cases among applicants. Eligibility/document/residency conditions differ by category; OCI is not citizenship, and Aadhaar issuance does not regularise immigration status. Existing notes establish Aadhaar is **not proof of citizenship/domicile**.
- **24 December 2025 UIDAI DoB SOP** distinguishes correcting declared/approximate dates, operator/holder errors and false documents, with documentary/affidavit/verification procedures. Once a birth certificate was used, further changes are ordinarily barred; specific correction/exception conditions still matter. The sheet's unqualified “second correction never permitted” is too broad, and an affidavit alone does not replace required proof. [UIDAI SOP](https://www.uidai.gov.in/images/SOP_for_DOB_update.pdf).
- False documents may trigger prescribed deactivation/prosecution, but a reported individual High Court FIR direction does not establish guilt or automatic punishment for every correction request. Balance authentic records with accessible correction, transparent decisions and remedies.
- **2018 PYQ(d), neither** already banked: Aadhaar does not establish citizenship/domicile and may be deactivated/omitted on prescribed grounds. The sheet's blanket “three years non-use” claim is not necessary to answer and should not be memorised as a universal automatic-deactivation rule.
