Indian Polity

National anthem, state anthem and constitutional respect

Editable notes

National anthem

  • Jana Gana Mana, Rabindranath Tagore; first sung at INC Calcutta session 27 December 1911; adopted by Constituent Assembly 24 January 1950.
  • Full version about 52 seconds; authorised short version about 20 seconds. Source recalls the original poem's first stanza becoming the anthem.
  • Article 51A(a): respect the Constitution, its institutions, National Flag and National Anthem; constitutional duty, statutory offence and ceremonial protocol are distinct.

Statute and protocol trap

  • Prevention of Insults to National Honour Act, 1971, section 3: intentionally preventing anthem singing or disturbing an assembly engaged in singing; up to three years, fine or both. The PDF's blanket 'skipping is punishable' is inaccurate. India Code.
  • MHA's historical orders cover national salutes, civil/military investitures and designated formal functions; normally stand at attention, with relevant disability/exhibition exceptions. Use the applicable official order for present ceremonial requirements. MHA orders.

Tamil Nadu dispute: January 2025

  • Tamil Thai Vazhthu, Sundaram Pillai's 1891 Manonmaniam, designated Tamil Nadu state anthem in 2021.
  • Source assembly convention: state anthem at the start of the Governor's address; national anthem at the end. Raj Bhavan alleged disrespect; an allegation is not a judicial finding of a statutory offence.
  • State symbolism and national respect can coexist; examine the exact act, intent and protocol before declaring illegality.
