# Hate speech regulation: equality and freedom of expression

## December 2025: Karnataka Bill debate
- Source discusses the proposed **Karnataka Hate Speech and Hate Crimes (Prevention) Bill, 2025**: public expression targeting protected characteristics, including caste/religion/gender/race/sexual orientation. At this issue date, describe a **proposal/Bill**, not a fully commenced law or judicially invalidated statute.
- Stated goal: protect vulnerable groups against hate campaigns and violence. Editorial criticism: broad terms such as hatred, disharmony, ill will or enmity may invite subjective/selective enforcement and chill journalism, satire, research and dissent. These are arguments about drafting/implementation, not proof every future prosecution will be abusive.
- **Article 19(1)(a)** protects speech; **Article 19(2)** permits reasonable restrictions on specified grounds including sovereignty/integrity, security, friendly foreign relations, public order, decency/morality, contempt, defamation and incitement to an offence. The PDF's ‘only imminent violence can ever justify a restriction’ is not a complete statement of Indian constitutional law.
- **Brandenburg** is a **US Supreme Court** test, not itself an Indian Supreme Court case. **Shreya Singhal 2015** distinguishes discussion/advocacy from incitement and highlights vagueness/chilling effects; applying it requires attention to the actual provision and constitutional ground.
- The source's IPC **153A/295A/505** are historical references after **BNS commenced 1 July 2024**, subject to transitional savings; do not call every IPC section the unchanged current code. Existing criminal/IT provisions and statutory overlap need precise examination rather than assuming no law addresses hate.
- Reform test: clear intent/harm thresholds, narrow definitions, proportional penalties, safeguards for legitimate speech, independent review and equal enforcement, alongside effective victim protection. The unyearred **250-word/15-mark ‘Daily Mains’** prompt is practice, not a CSE PYQ.
