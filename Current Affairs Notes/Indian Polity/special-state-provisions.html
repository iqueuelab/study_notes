Indian Polity

Articles 371–371J: differentiated federal safeguards

Editable notes

Constitutional framework

  • Part XXI, special State provisions; the heading “temporary, transitional and special” does not make every Article 371 safeguard temporary. Constitutional text controls scope.
ArticleState and principal safeguard
371Maharashtra/Gujarat: regional development boards, equitable funds and education/employment opportunities; Governor's special responsibility
371ANagaland: Parliamentary laws on specified Naga practices/customary justice and land/resources need Assembly resolution; special law/order responsibility under conditions
371BAssam: Assembly committee for specified tribal areas
371CManipur: Hill Areas committee; Governor reporting and Union directions
371DAndhra Pradesh and Telangana: equitable education/public-employment opportunity arrangements
371EAndhra Pradesh: Parliament may establish a university; do not automatically extend it to Telangana with 371D
371FSikkim: integration safeguards, Assembly at least 30, Governor's special responsibilities
371GMizoram: specified religious/social practice, customary justice and land safeguards; Assembly at least 40
371HArunachal Pradesh: Governor's law/order responsibility; Assembly at least 30
371IGoa: Assembly at least 30
371JKarnataka's Hyderabad–Karnataka / Kalyana Karnataka: development board, equitable resources and local education/employment provisions
  • 371B inserted 1969; 371C 1972. Article 371's original 1950 form is not identical to today's Maharashtra/Gujarat wording; later additions/changes came by constitutional amendment.
  • Recall 371D ≠371E, Nagaland ≠Mizoram land wording; safeguards are specific, not blanket exemptions from every parliamentary law.
  • Constitution of India: official text.
