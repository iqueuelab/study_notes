# Controller General of Accounts and government accounting

## Institution and functions
- **Principal accounting adviser**, **Department of Expenditure, Finance Ministry**; administers Union civil departmental accounting, distinct from constitutional **CAG external audit**.
- Official history locates departmentalisation/audit-account separation in **1976**, with the 1976 Act assented **8 April**; source “October 1975 established” is inaccurate. [CGA history](https://cga.nic.in/Page/History-of-Indian-Civil-Accounts-Organisation.aspx).
- Consolidates monthly/annual Union accounts; advises accounting principles/forms/manuals, receipt/payment administration, treasury/cash reconciliation with RBI and management accounting/fiscal analysis. Internal audit supports ministry financial control and scheme compliance; CAG/PAC scrutiny is a separate accountability channel.
- Audit-para/action-taken monitoring, **PFMS** financial information and civil-pension accounting/payment authorisation through relevant offices; not every central/defence/rail pension is directly disbursed personally by CGA.
- **Article 150:** President prescribes Union/State account form on CAG advice; Article 151 concerns audit-report submission. Do not turn CGA's administrative duties into an independent constitutional power overriding CAG.
