# Repealing and Amending Bill: statutory cleanup

## December 2025: repeal of obsolete enactments
- Source reports both Houses passed **Repealing and Amending Bill, 2025**, covering **71 Acts**, including Indian Tramways Act **1886**, Levy Sugar Price Equalisation Fund Act **1976** and the **Bharat Petroleum Corporation Limited (Determination of Conditions of Service of Employees) Act, 1988**. The last is an employee-conditions law, not repeal of the company itself.
- Many repealed enactments are **amending Acts whose changes have already entered principal Acts**; repeal does not automatically reverse those amendments. Distinguish repeal of spent enactments from amendment of substantive current provisions.
- Changes to **General Clauses Act 1897, CPC 1908 and Indian Succession Act 1925** are amendments, not wholesale repeal of these principal codes. Source frames cleanup as easier administration/living/business and removal of outdated language. Passage and subsequent assent/commencement are distinct; do not infer every listed law ceased on the newspaper date.
