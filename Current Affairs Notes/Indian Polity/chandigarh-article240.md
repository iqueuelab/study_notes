# Chandigarh and Article 240: proposed law-making change

## November 2025: proposal and clarification
- A **21 November Lok Sabha bulletin** listed a proposed **Constitution (131st Amendment) Bill, 2025** to include Chandigarh in **Article 240**. On **23 November**, the Home Ministry clarified that the law-making proposal remained under consideration, no final decision had been taken and no Bill to this effect was intended for the coming Winter Session; stakeholder consultation would precede a decision. This is **not an enacted constitutional amendment**.
- **Chandigarh** is a Union Territory and joint capital of Punjab and Haryana; the Punjab Governor serves as its Administrator. The **Punjab Reorganisation Act, 1966** is central historical context. Punjab’s claim and regional opposition explain the sensitivity, but political claims do not themselves settle legal title.
- **Article 239** governs UT administration through a Presidential Administrator. **Article 240** separately permits Presidential regulations for the **peace, progress and good government** of specified UTs; it is not an automatic power over every UT lacking a legislature. The text covers Andaman and Nicobar Islands, Lakshadweep, Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu, and conditional Puducherry arrangements.
- For **Puducherry**, regulations cannot ordinarily be made while its legislature functions; the constitutional provisos address **dissolution or suspension**. The PDF’s suspension-only shorthand is incomplete. Regulations may amend/repeal applicable Parliamentary Acts and have the same force/effect as an Act of Parliament.
- Inclusion in Article 240 would concern the Central law-making route; it does **not by itself require replacing the Punjab Governor with a full-time Lieutenant Governor**. The Ministry specifically denied changing Chandigarh’s governance/administrative structure or its traditional arrangements with Punjab/Haryana. Separate speculative consequences from the stated proposal. [Official Constitution](https://www.legislative.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/08/7af1daa22d65f9d04c00ae9b9aa5a799.pdf).
