# Delhi election 2025: mandate and divided governance

## Result and careful interpretation

- **February 2025 Delhi Assembly election:** BJP **48**, AAP **22** of **70** seats; Congress none. AAP's uninterrupted majority rule was **2015–2025**; its first government in 2013 was brief, followed by President's Rule. [ECI results portal](https://results.eci.gov.in/).
- Source suggests unfulfilled promises, middle-class discontent, municipal service problems, corruption allegations and no Congress alliance. These are **explanations/hypotheses**, not causal findings established solely by vote totals; allegations are not convictions.
- A hypothetical combined AAP–Congress vote is not proof votes would transfer under an alliance.

## Mains link

- Separate **Union/Lieutenant-Governor, elected Delhi government and municipal** functions before assigning service accountability; Article **239AA** gives a special constitutional arrangement.
- Assess public-service outcomes, credible funded manifestos, administrative coordination, transparent procurement and citizen grievance mechanisms. Electoral defeat is not evidence that all welfare spending is ineffective.
