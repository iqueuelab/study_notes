# Vice-Chancellor appointments and federalism

## January 2025 draft
- **Draft UGC teacher/academic-staff qualification and higher-education standards Regulations, 2025**, released **6 January 2025**. These notes describe the **proposal**, not assume it is today's operative rule.
- Proposed eligibility: **ten years** as professor, senior research/academic administrator, or senior industry/public administration/public policy/PSU professional with significant academic/scholarly contributions.
| Proposed search committee | Nominating authority |
| --- | --- |
| Chairperson | **Visitor / Chancellor** |
| Expert | **UGC Chairman** |
| Expert | **University apex body**, e.g. Senate/Syndicate/Executive Council |
- Visitor/Chancellor constitutes committee and appoints from its **3–5-name panel**. Independence, conflicts/recusal and transparent selection matter. [Official draft, section 10](https://www.ugc.gov.in/pdfnews/3045759_Draft-Regulation-Minimum-Qualifications-for-Appointment-and-Promotion-of-Teachers-and-Academic-Staff-in-Universities-and-Colleges-and-Measures-for-the-Maintenance-of-Standards-in-HE-Regulations-2025.pdf).
## Existing framework versus proposal
- Earlier UGC norms call for eminent higher-education experts and a **panel of 3–5 candidates**; this panel size is not automatically the number of committee members.
- University Acts/statutes differ: President is Visitor of many central universities; Governor commonly Chancellor of state universities by **statute**, not an automatic constitutional power over every university. Source central/State/private examples are not a uniform all-India committee formula.
- Source conflicts: **Kerala** reappointment/Chancellor replacement; **West Bengal** interim VCs and Supreme Court-linked search process; **Tamil Nadu** search nominees and appointment Bills; **Karnataka** proposed changes for six universities; **Maharashtra** reversal of earlier restrictions. Legislative/court positions are dated January 2025, not current outcomes. Kerala's earlier controversy involved **Arif Mohammed Khan**; avoid calling him its January 2025 Governor.
## Federal balance: mains frame
- Union **List I Entry 66**: coordination/determination of higher-education standards; **Concurrent List Entry 25**: education, subject to relevant Union entries. Education shifted to Concurrent List by **42 nd Amendment, 1976**.
- National quality/merit and wider talent pool versus State autonomy, local accountability and financing; centrally nominated members do not automatically prove political misconduct.
- Consult States; clarify statutory authority; independent expert selection, recorded reasons, disclosure of conflicts and timely appointments; assess NEP 2020 goals alongside federal safeguards.
## Statutory power and precedent: January 2025 debate
- **UGC Act, 1956, section 26** authorises regulations within the Act's purposes. Whether specific VC rules adequately relate to educational standards is a legal question; an editorial claim of overreach is not itself a judgment invalidating the draft.
- **Suresh Patilkhede**, Bombay High Court (source labels **2011**): cited for doubts about applying UGC VC criteria to State statutory appointments; distinguish High Court reasoning from the full Supreme Court line of authority.
- **Kalyani Mathivanan v. K.V. Jeyaraj, 2015**: source incorrectly describes blanket mandatory application to all State universities. The ruling distinguished the 2010 framework's mandatory central/institutional operation from its directory character for relevant State institutions pending State adoption; Tamil Nadu had not adopted the scheme. Do not extrapolate this mechanically to every later regulation. [Supreme Court's subsequent discussion, paragraph 35 onward](https://api.sci.gov.in/supremecourt/2024/45256/45256_2024_8_1501_62293_Judgement_14-Jul-2025.pdf).
- **Articles 246/254** and legislative competence/repugnancy must be read alongside **Entry 66, List I** and **Entry 25, List III**. A State law versus delegated central rule dispute is not settled simply by saying either government always wins.
- UGC prescribes standards/selection norms; **final appointment authority arises from applicable university law**, commonly Chancellor/Visitor. A UGC search nominee is not direct UGC appointment of every VC.

##2025 draft and federalism
- Draft UGC2025 regulations proposed a three-membersearch committee:Chancellor/Visitor nominee, UGCChair nominee, universityapex-body nominee; broader eligibility included senior industry/public policy/administration roles.
- **Draft is not already operative law**. UGC sets applicable standards; it does not personally appoint every university VC. Constituting committees and appointing VCs follow relevant Acts/rules.
- Education Concurrent List Entry 25 coexists with Union Entry 66 coordination/standards; fiscal funding alone does not override lawful national standards.
- Gambhirdan Gadhvi judgment is 2022, not source 2019; other Sreejith/Kannur/West Bengalcases turn on theirstatutes, facts andimproperinterference, not a blanket rule that every State academic nominee is prohibited.
- Governor's Chancellor role comes fromuniversity law, not automatically fromconstitutional Governor status. State Bills changingit need assent/commencement; disputes andpending Bills are source-era snapshots.
- Balance transparent merit, independent academic selection, State voice andinstitutionalautonomy; concern overpoliticisation is an argument, not proofevery proposedappointee is biased.
