# Political-party registration and RUPP delisting

## June 2025 scrutiny
- ECI initiated verification/show-cause proceedings concerning **345 RUPPs** for non-contestation since 2019 and absence at registered addresses; this was **not a completed June cancellation of all 345**.
- Article **19(1)(c)** protects association; **RPA 1951, section 29A** governs registration. Apply within **30 days of formation**; party constitution must affirm constitutional allegiance, socialism, secularism, democracy and India's sovereignty, unity and integrity; changes in material particulars must be reported.
- **Registration ≠ recognition:** RUPPs are registered but have not met national/state-party recognition criteria. Benefits can include conditional income-tax exemption under **section 13A**, common-symbol concessions and up to **20 star campaigners**. Only qualifying campaigners' travel expenditure receives the statutory exclusion, not all their campaign spending.
- Reports of **2,800+ RUPPs/~750 participating in 2024** are dated context. Registration alone does not guarantee tax exemption: accounts, audit and contribution disclosure requirements apply, including contributions above ₹20,000.
- **Indian National Congress (I) v Institute of Social Welfare, 2002:** ECI's quasi-judicial registration power does not confer a general power of deregistration for mere inactivity; narrow exceptions include fraud, abandonment of constitutional allegiance and unlawful-association circumstances. Delisting/compliance action must be distinguished from a general statutory deregistration power.
- Reform options: express legal powers with due process, internal democracy, regular audited returns, transparent donations, verification and appropriate election-participation criteria; Law Commission recommendations are not already enacted law.
