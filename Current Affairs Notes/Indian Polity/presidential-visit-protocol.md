# Presidential visits: reception and administrative protocol

## Reception during visits to States
- March controversy concerned representation of the State government during President **Droupadi Murmu's West Bengal visit**. The issue explains the normal practice: **Governor and Chief Minister receive the President; if unavailable, the CM nominates a minister**.
- Visits of the President, Vice-President and Prime Minister use the Home Ministry's **Blue Book** and approved programmes/reception lists. State/district administrations coordinate reception, movement and security; Collector/District Magistrate and district police have key operational roles. Transit halts and full official visits can have different reception scales. [Government training material on protocol](https://www.mcrhrdi.gov.in/images/publications/Reading%20Material/Revenue%20Administration.pdf).
- Distinguish **administrative protocol/convention from an express constitutional command requiring the CM's personal attendance**. The issue's blanket “not legally mandatory” should not imply security directions or all official administrative duties are optional. Its political examples illustrate disputed compliance, not an adjudicated constitutional offence.
- Protocol supports dignity of constitutional office, coordination and security. Advance approval of airport/rail/road/port reception arrangements helps avoid confusion; political disagreement does not change the constitutional office-holder's status.
