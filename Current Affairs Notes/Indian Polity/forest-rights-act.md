# Forest Rights Act: recognition and implementation

## Statutory framework
- **Scheduled Tribes and Other Traditional Forest Dwellers (Recognition of Forest Rights) Act, 2006** addresses historical injustice and livelihood/tenure security. **Ministry of Tribal Affairs** is the nodal ministry; States implement it.
- Individual cultivation/residence rights recognise eligible pre-existing occupation, with **4 hectares as a ceiling**, not a fresh uniform grant. Community rights include minor forest produce, grazing, fishing and water use; **community forest resource rights** support protection, regeneration and management.
- Gram Sabha initiates claims/verification; Sub-Divisional and District Level Committees process/decide through the statutory framework, with State monitoring. No eviction before recognition/verification is completed under section 4(5).
- Delays, evidence burdens, wrongful rejection/eviction and pressure from conservation/infrastructure/mining projects require fair hearings, assistance and grievance redress.
## June 2025: dedicated FRA cells
- Source reports **324 district cells across 18 States/UTs**, plus State support, under **Dharti Aba Janjatiya Gram Utkarsh Abhiyaan**. These assist documentation, claimants/Gram Sabhas and data management; they do not replace statutory decision bodies.
- Reported sanctioned district counts: MP **55**, Chhattisgarh **30**, Telangana **29**, Maharashtra **26**, Assam **25**, Jharkhand **24**. MoTA grants: **₹8.67 lakh/district cell**, **₹25.85 lakh/State cell**.
- March 2025 source snapshot: **51.11 lakh claims**, **14.45% pending**; roughly **42% rejected among disposed claims**. These percentages have different denominators.
- Concern: parallel administrative structures could weaken Gram Sabha autonomy; preserve a clear assistance/decision boundary. The accompanying MoTA directive emphasises protection from unlawful eviction and grievance mechanisms.
- 2021 PYQ answer: **Ministry of Tribal Affairs (d)**.
