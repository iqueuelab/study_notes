# Political leadership: age, accountability and renewal

## July 2025 debate
- A proposal for political retirement at 75 reopened questions of generational representation, fitness and party succession. **India has no constitutional upper age limit for MPs/MLAs or a general two-term PM/CM limit**; a party convention is not a legal disqualification.
- **Articles 84(b)/173(b)**: minimum 25 for Lok Sabha/Legislative Assembly; 30 for Rajya Sabha/Legislative Council. Article 326 concerns adult suffrage, not by itself every qualification to stand for election. Assess any proposed cap against the full constitutional/legal framework, rather than assert automatic invalidity under Article 326.
- For a cap: renewal, opportunities for younger members, planned succession and concerns about capability. Against: voters' choice, experience, age discrimination and wide variation in fitness. Chronological age alone does not establish incapacity.
- Alternatives: transparent candidate selection/internal party democracy, mentoring, meaningful performance scrutiny and proportionate health transparency balanced with privacy. Fixed term limits, lower contesting age and compulsory health certification are reform proposals, not enacted rules.
