# Sansad Ratna Awards and parliamentary performance

## Award and May 2025 selection
- Instituted **2010**, inspired by **A.P.J. Abdul Kalam**; organised by **Prime Point Foundation**, a civil-society initiative, **not a Government of India or Parliament award**. Recognises parliamentary performance and encourages accountability. [Organiser](https://www.sansadratna.in/p/about-sansad-ratna-award.html).
- May clipping reports selection of **17 MPs and two Parliamentary Standing Committees** for 2025; selection precedes presentation. Named examples: **Bhartruhari Mahtab, Ravi Kishan, Supriya Sule, N.K. Premachandran and Shrirang Appa Barne**; jury chaired by **Hansraj Ahir**.
- Core reported quantitative measures: participation in debates, questions and **Private Members' Bills**, using parliamentary/PRS data and an expert jury. Do not assume the PDF's attendance, MPLADS use, debate duration/quality and generic retired-judge membership are each confirmed components of the organiser's scoring method.
- Participation counts aid scrutiny but cannot alone measure policy quality, constituency work, inclusiveness or outcomes. Distinguish a private-member Bill being introduced from its passage into law.
