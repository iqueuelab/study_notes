# Rushikonda Vishnu sculpture and archaeological inference

## Find and interpretation
- Broken granite Vishnu sculpture found at **Pedda Rushikonda Beach, Visakhapatnam**, recovered by Andhra Pradesh archaeology authorities for conservation/display at Visakha Museum.
- Tentatively attributed to the **13th–14th centuries**, and described as a Janardana form. This is a stylistic assessment, not an independently established exact date or inscription-based donor identification.
- Shankha/conch and other iconographic attributes assist identification; missing limbs constrain certainty.
- Granite rather than local khondalite raises provenance questions but does not prove import from Tamil Nadu. Immersion of a damaged temple image is a hypothesis, not evidence of a shipwreck or specific maritime trade route.
- Document find context, material/style and condition before conservation. Regional dynastic history does not justify assigning the object to a dynasty without evidence.
