Kathputli and Indian puppetry traditions

Editable notes

Rajasthan string puppetry

  • Kathputli: kath (wood) + putli (doll); traditional string-puppet theatre of Rajasthan, associated with Bhat/Nat travelling performers. The issue reports about 250 Jaipur families sustaining the tradition, a local livelihood account rather than a national artist census.
  • Wooden carved heads/upper bodies, cotton and fabric costumes; stylised large eyes, expressive faces and colourful court-inspired dress. Most lack legs: long skirts conceal the lower body and enable fluid movement. Finger-controlled strings animate neck, arms and body; precise string numbers vary by character/design.
  • Folk songs, narration and dholak accompany stories of rulers, warriors and heroes, including Amar Singh Rathore, humour, satire and social messages. Oral performance combines craft, music, theatre and inherited skill. Tourism Ministry account, official handicrafts compendium.
