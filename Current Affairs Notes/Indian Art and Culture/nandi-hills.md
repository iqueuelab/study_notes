# Nandi Hills: heritage and tourism

## Place and heritage
- Hill fortress in **Chikkaballapur district, Karnataka**, about 60 km from Bengaluru. **Yoga Nandeeshwara Temple**, dedicated to Shiva at the summit, exhibits Dravidian architecture and Chola-period associations; the source cites Rajendra/Kulottunga Chola inscriptions and earlier Ganga associations.
- Heritage, elevated fortification and scenic tourism require conservation and carrying-capacity planning.
- **SAARC correction:** India's first hosted summit was the organisation's **second summit, Bangalore, 1986**; the first SAARC summit was Dhaka, 1985. Nandi Hills is associated with the Bangalore summit retreat, not the inaugural SAARC summit. [SAARC declarations](https://saarc-sec.org/resources/summit-declarations).
