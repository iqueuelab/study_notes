# Nobel Literature 2025: László Krasznahorkai

## Hungarian literature and translation
- Hungarian novelist/screenwriter László Krasznahorkai (born 1954, Gyula) won the 2025 Literature Nobel. The Swedish Academy recognised work that finds artistic force amid catastrophic and unsettling worlds. Long sentences, philosophical/absurdist narration, social decay, endurance and hope distinguish his writing. [Nobel biobibliography](https://www.nobelprize.org/uploads/2025/10/Enbio25.pdf).
- Key works: *Satantango* (1985), *The Melancholy of Resistance* (1989), *War & War* (1999), *Baron Wenckheim's Homecoming* (Hungarian 2016; English 2019). Béla Tarr adapted the first two into *Sátántangó* and *Werckmeister Harmonies*, linking Hungarian literature with slow cinema.
- Won the 2015 Man Booker International Prize for his body of work; translators George Szirtes and Ottilie Mulzet received its translator award, rather than simply sharing the author prize. The 2019 US translated-literature National Book Award honoured *Baron Wenckheim's Homecoming*. Translation enables wider circulation of non-English literature. [Booker profile](https://thebookerprizes.com/the-booker-library/authors/laszlo-krasznahorkai).
- Related 2008 science PYQ: James D. Watson's field is genetics; this is separate from literature.
