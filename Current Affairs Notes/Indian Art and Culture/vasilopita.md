# Vasilopita

## Identification
- **Greek New Year cake/bread**, linked to **Saint Basil the Great**, a fourth-century bishop associated with charity.
- A hidden coin (**flouri**) signifies good fortune for the recipient; family/community sharing expresses faith, charity and togetherness.
- Source describes symbolic first slices for Christ, Virgin Mary and Saint Basil; practices can vary by household.
## UPSC link
- Match tradition with Greece/Greek Orthodox culture; use as an example of living heritage and community solidarity, not as a universal ritual followed identically everywhere.
