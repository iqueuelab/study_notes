# Sahitya Akademi and its literary awards

## March 2026: awards for 2025
- **2025 Sahitya Akademi Awards announced in March 2026** in 24 recognised languages. Keep the award year separate from announcement/presentation year. Categories reported: **8 poetry, 4 novels, 6 short-story collections, 2 essays, 1 criticism, 1 autobiography and 2 memoirs** (24 works).
- Notable works: **Navtej Sarna—Crimson Spring** (English novel); **Mamta Kalia—Jeete Jee Allahabad** (Hindi memoir); **Sa. Tamilselvan—Thamizh Sirukathaiyin Thadam** (Tamil criticism). [Akademi winner register](https://www.sahitya-akademi.gov.in/awards/akademi%20samman_suchi.jsp).
- Sahitya Akademi is the **National Academy of Letters**, established **1954**, an autonomous organisation under the Ministry of Culture. It recognises the **22 Eighth Schedule languages plus English and Rajasthani**; English/Rajasthani do not thereby become Eighth Schedule languages.
- Award: engraved copper plaque, shawl and **₹1 lakh**; the issue scheduled presentation for **31 March**. Translation, publications and multilingual literary exchange support cultural diversity. The announcement followed a reported temporary halt/review of the selection process.
