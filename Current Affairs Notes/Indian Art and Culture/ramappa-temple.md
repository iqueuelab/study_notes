# Ramappa Temple: Kakatiya engineering and conservation

## December 2025: conservation review
- India's UNESCO representative **Vishal V. Sharma** visited the **Kakatiya Rudreshwara (Ramappa) Temple, Palampet, Mulugu district, Telangana**, to review ASI/State conservation. Post-inscription management requires structural/scientific conservation, community participation, tourism/infrastructure control and protection of the landscape around Ramappa Cheruvu.
- Living **Shiva temple**, Kakatiya tradition; construction began **1213 CE**, associated with commander **Recharla Rudra** under **Ganapati Deva**. The source's “Rudra Samani” is a faulty name; its absolute “only temple named after its architect” is not established here.
- Sandstone temple, carved granite/dolerite beams and pillars, sculptural depictions of dance, and stepped pyramidal **vimana** built with **lightweight porous/floating bricks** reduce roof load. **Sandbox foundations** and engineering ingenuity are UNESCO-recognised features. Detailed brick ingredients and universal earthquake-proof claims should not be inferred from this summary.
- World Heritage inscription **2021**, criteria **(i) and (iii)**. ASI management works with State, religious and local authorities; conservation must preserve ritual use and wider landscape. [UNESCO property record](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1570).
- PYQ revision: **Virupaksha Temple—Hampi (2009, c)**; **Nagara, Dravida, Vesara—temple architecture (2012, c)**. The sheet conflates Hampi with the separate **Pattadakal Virupaksha**, associated with Queen Lokamahadevi/Vikramaditya II and the eighth century; that history should not be assigned to Hampi. Both questions already occur in the Art and Culture bank.
