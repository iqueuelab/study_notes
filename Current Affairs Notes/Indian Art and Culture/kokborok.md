# Kokborok language and script debate

## Tripura

- Language of **Borok/Tripuri communities**, **Sino-Tibetan family**; **kok = speech/language, borok = people**. Recognised as an official language of Tripura **19 January 1979**, observed as Kokborok Day.
- **2011 Census** source: **880,537 speakers**, about **23.97%** of Tripura population; not a 2025 census count.
- Debate over **Roman versus Bengali script**; student organisations including TSF/NESO seek script choices for education/examinations. Script preference differs from linguistic family or official-language status.
- Source mentions early grammars by **Daulat Ahmed (Kokboroma, 1897/98)** and **Thakur Radhamohan Debbarma (1900)**. Its alleged **first-century Raj Ratnakar** is not a verified surviving first-century linguistic attestation.
- Language preservation: teaching material, trained teachers, consistent exam access, community participation and digital resources.
