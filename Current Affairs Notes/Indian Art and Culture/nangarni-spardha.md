# Nangarni Spardha and agrarian racing traditions

## Konkan monsoon tradition
- **Nangarni Spardha**, traditional oxen race at **Dervan, Ratnagiri, Maharashtra**, organised during the monsoon by village communities. Barefoot drivers guide decorated oxen along muddy tracks; an expression of farmer–animal ties, skill and agrarian/community identity.
- Compare **Kambala—Karnataka, buffalo racing** and **Maramadi—Kerala, cattle/ox racing**. Worksheet explanation wrongly numbers an extra statement; its two-statement key remains **1 only**.
