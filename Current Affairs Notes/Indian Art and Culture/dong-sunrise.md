# Dong: Sunrise Festival and eastern Himalayan cultural heritage

## 1 January 2026: Alo Prabhat at Dong
- **Dong, Anjaw district, Arunachal Pradesh**, welcomed the New Year sunrise through **“Alo Prabhat: Dance of the Dawn”**, drawing on **Meyor and Mishmi** chants, songs and indigenous rhythms. The State Tourism Department's Sunrise Festival began **29 December 2025**. The festival ran **29 December 2025–2 January 2026**, confirmed by its organiser FAQ; the explainer's **3 January** end date is wrong. [Festival organiser](https://sunrisefestival.in/faq/).
- Dong is celebrated as a first-sunrise destination near India's eastern frontier, in the **Lohit valley**. Longitude influences local solar time, but terrain/latitude/season affect the observed sunrise; avoid asserting the village receives India's first rays every day under every definition. **Kibithu** is also widely described as India's easternmost inhabited settlement, so the PDF's absolute Dong label needs qualification.
- Tourism can sustain oral heritage, local livelihoods and border settlements while requiring ecological limits and community participation. **Article 29** protects distinct language/script/culture; do not imply the **Fifth or Sixth Schedule applies automatically to Arunachal Pradesh**. Its specific constitutional provision is **Article 371H**, with a different purpose and scope.
