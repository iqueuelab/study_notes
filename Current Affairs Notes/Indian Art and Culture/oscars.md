# Academy Awards and Indian cinema

## Awards recall

- **97th Oscars, 2March 2025**, Dolby Theatre, Los Angeles; Anora won five awards, Sean Baker four.
- Academy of Motion Picture Arts and Sciences founded 1927; first awards 1929. Cedric Gibbons design/George Stanley sculpture; statue's five spokes represent original branches.

| Indian recognition | Year |
| --- | --- |
| Bhanu Athaiya, Gandhi costume design; first Indian winner | 1983 |
| Satyajit Ray, honorary award | 1992 |
| AR Rahman, score/song; Gulzar, song; Resul Pookutty, sound, Slumdog Millionaire | 2009 |
| Naatu Naatu: MM Keeravani/Chandrabose; The Elephant Whisperers: Kartiki Gonsalves/Guneet Monga | 2023 |

- Mother India was a 1957 film nominated at the 1958 ceremony; film year and award year differ.
- Statue materials changed historically, including wartime plaster and later production methods; recognition is not an objective ranking of every national cinema.
