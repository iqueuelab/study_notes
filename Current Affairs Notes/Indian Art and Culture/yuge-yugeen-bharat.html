October 2025: museum development plans

  • Proposed national museum in North and South Blocks, Raisina Hill, New Delhi, under Culture Ministry/Central Vista redevelopment. Adaptive reuse repurposes historic buildings while retaining heritage character; exhibits aim to connect civilisational history with modern India, education and public access.
  • Source reports about 1.55 lakh sq m, eight thematic sections/around thirty galleries, over 20,000 artefacts, ARCOP-led design collaboration and first-gallery/full-completion ambitions in 2025/2026. These are reported design/planning claims, not completed galleries or guaranteed deadlines. Its title says first gallery by end-2026 while the table says end-2025; preserve the uncertainty rather than select a convenient date.
  • Herbert Baker designed North/South Blocks, correcting the text's attribution solely to Edwin Lutyens. Lutyens and Baker both shaped New Delhi. MEA building history.
  • 19 December 2024 agreement between National Museum and France Museums Development supports technical cooperation. Government's March 2025 reply states budget/timeline depend on feasibility and subsequent formalities. Culture Ministry.
  • 'World's largest' and 30–50 lakh annual visitors are ambitions/projections; museum complexes and gallery areas are not directly comparable without definitions. Conservation, provenance, accessibility, interpretation, safe relocation and diverse community narratives matter alongside tourist numbers.
