# Golden Globe Awards and the 83rd edition

## January 2026: principal film winners
- **83rd Golden Globes, 11 January 2026**, **Beverly Hilton, Beverly Hills, California**; annual film/television awards established **1944**, with separate drama and musical/comedy film categories. [Official ceremony account](https://goldenglobes.com/articles/one-battle-after-another-and-hamnet-take-best-picture-prizes-at-golden-globes/).
| Award | Winner |
|---|---|
| Film, musical/comedy | **One Battle After Another** |
| Film, drama | **Hamnet** |
| Director /screenplay | **Paul Thomas Anderson /One Battle After Another** |
| Actress, drama | **Jessie Buckley, Hamnet** |
| Actress, musical/comedy | **Rose Byrne, If I Had Legs I'd Kick You** |
| Actor, drama | **Wagner Moura, The Secret Agent** |
| Supporting actor /actress | **Stellan Skarsgård, Sentimental Value /Teyana Taylor, One Battle After Another** |
| Non-English-language film | **The Secret Agent, Brazil** |
| Original score /cinematic and box-office achievement | **Sinners** |
- The source's HFPA-organiser statement is outdated: **DCP/Eldridge acquired the assets/rights in June 2023**, with the HFPA winding down and a separate charitable **Golden Globe Foundation**. [Official transaction announcement](https://goldenglobes.com/articles/dick-clark-productions-and-eldridge-acquire-golden-globes/).
- International films and literary adaptations illustrate cultural circulation/soft power; **Hamnet** draws on Maggie O'Farrell's novel. Globes visibility can shape award-season attention, but it does not determine Oscar outcomes or constitute a government cultural honour.
