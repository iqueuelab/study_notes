# Dwarka and underwater archaeology

## Geography and sacred tradition
- **Dwarka, Gujarat:** Arabian Sea coast near the Gulf of Kutch; Gomati Creek is important to present archaeological exploration. **Bet/Beyt Dwarka is a separate island location**, not another name for the mainland excavation site.
- Major Vaishnava/Krishna pilgrimage centre and one of the **Sapta Puris**: Ayodhya, Mathura, Haridwar, Kashi, Kanchipuram, Ujjain and Dwarka. Dwarkadhish/Jagat Mandir is its principal Krishna temple.
- Mahabharata, Harivamsa, Vishnu Purana and Skanda Purana connect it with Krishna's kingdom and inundation traditions. These are literary/religious accounts; they do not establish an exact archaeological date for Krishna or literal proof of every epic event.
## Scientific exploration: 2025
- ASI's revived **Underwater Archaeology Wing** began work near Gomati Creek in February 2025, within renewed investigation of Dwarka/Bet Dwarka. [Culture Ministry release](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleaseIframePage.aspx?PRID=2104411&lang=2&reg=48)
- Stone anchors, pottery and submerged remains can illuminate maritime activity, settlement and environmental change; interpretation requires stratigraphy, material context and dating. Anchors alone do not prove the submerged city was precisely the epic capital.
- The source's categorical “temple built in the 13th century” should be treated cautiously: the present complex has multiple historical phases and traditional foundation narratives.
- Dwarka was among **HRIDAY** heritage cities: urban amenities and conservation-sensitive planning support pilgrimage/tourism. Preserve coastal heritage through documentation, scientific conservation, responsible visitor management and community participation.
