# Apatani culture and the Ziro landscape

## Living culture and changing practices
- **Apatani**, Ziro Valley, Lower Subansiri, Arunachal Pradesh; Tani/Tibeto-Burman language tradition. The **Apatani Cultural Landscape is on UNESCO's Tentative List**, not an inscribed World Heritage Site. [UNESCO nomination](https://whc.unesco.org/en/tentativelists/5893/).
- Elder women's facial tattoos (**Tippei**) and wooden nose plugs (**Yaping Hullo**) preserve an identity marker now declining. The issue describes discontinuation in the early 1970s; its abduction-prevention explanation is an oral origin account, not an independently established universal history.
- Dree concerns agricultural prosperity; Myoko social bonds/friendship; Murung health/prosperity; Yapung harvest/community life. Sun/Moon worship features in the source; its practice-question river-goddess distractor should not be memorised as fact.
- Wet-rice cultivation integrated with fish rearing, water management, community labour and resource stewardship link livelihoods to ecology. Rice variety names in the issue include **Emeo, Pyape and Mypia**. The source's “since 1960s” should not be treated as the origin date of the whole centuries-old farming landscape.
- Preserve community agency, intergenerational knowledge and women's own accounts while documenting cultural change; safeguarding heritage does not require compulsory continuation of bodily practices.
