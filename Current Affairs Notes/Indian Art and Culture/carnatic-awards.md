# Carnatic music and Music Academy awards

## Music and institution
- Carnatic music emphasises **raga, tala, compositions and manodharma improvisation**; composition-centred does not mean it lacks improvisation.
- Southern tradition with devotional/secular repertoire in Telugu, Sanskrit, Tamil, Kannada and other languages; vocal and instruments such as veena, violin, mridangam and flute are important.
- Trinity: **Tyagaraja, Muthuswami Dikshitar and Syama Sastri**. *Sangita Sampradaya Pradarsini* was authored by **Subbarama Dikshitar**, not Muthuswami Dikshitar.
- Music Academy originated in the 1927 Madras music/congress setting and was inaugurated **18 August 1928**. [Academy history](https://musicacademymadras.in/history/).
- **Sangita Kalanidhi** is the Academy's distinguished music honour; title instituted in 1942, recipient presides over annual academic sessions. It is not a government national decoration.
## 2025 honours
- Sangita Kalanidhi: violinist **R. K. Shriramkumar**, known for accompaniment and scholarship; 2025 marked Dikshitar's 250th birth anniversary.
- Nritya Kalanidhi: **Urmila Sathyanarayanan**, Bharatanatyam.
- Sangita Kala Acharya: **Shyamala Venkateswaran** and **T. R. Govindarajan**; TTK: **Madambi Subramania Namboothiri**, **J. T. Jeyaraaj Krishnan and Jayasri Jeyaraaj Krishnan**.
- Selection in 2025 differs from presentation: music honours at the **1 January 2026 Sadas**, dance honour on **3 January 2026**. [Academy announcement](https://musicacademymadras.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Awardee-2025.pdf).
