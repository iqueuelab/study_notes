# Carnatic music and Sangita Kalanidhi

## Music fundamentals

- South Indian classical tradition: **raga** (melodic framework), **tala** (rhythmic cycle), composed repertoire and substantial improvisation.
- Common languages: Telugu, Sanskrit, Tamil, Kannada; instruments: veena, violin, mridangam, flute. Bhakti traditions strongly influenced devotional repertoire.
- Examples: M.S. Subbulakshmi, Semmangudi Srinivasa Iyer; Chennai's December/Margazhi season supports performance and transmission.
- **Do not read composition-centred as absence of improvisation.** Avoid rigid religious/cultural binaries with Hindustani music.

## Institution and award

- Music Academy arose from the **1927 Madras Congress/music conference** and was inaugurated **18 August 1928**; annual conferences began 1929. [Academy history](https://musicacademymadras.in/history/).
- **Sangita Kalanidhi title instituted 1942**, awarded by the Music Academy, Madras; gold medal and **birudu patra** (citation). Earlier conference presidents appear retrospectively in the recipient list. [Award history](https://musicacademymadras.in/sangita-kalanidhi/).
