# Sree Padmanabhaswamy Temple and Travancore heritage

## Temple and June 2025 context
- **Thiruvananthapuram, Kerala**; Vishnu in **Anantha Shayanam** posture on Adishesha. Kerala and Dravidian architectural elements, **seven-tier gopuram**, about **80-foot gold-plated copper flagstaff**, stone/bronze work and murals including Vishnu, Ganapati, Gajalakshmi and Narasimha.
- Marthanda Varma reconstructed/patronised the shrine; **1750 Thrippadidanam** dedicated Travancore to the deity, underpinning the royal family's role as servants/trustees. Historical mentions do not establish an exact construction date.
- **Mathilakam palm-leaf records** document temple-town/Travancore administration and finance over centuries. Source cites roughly three million leaves/records; do not equate leaves with three million separate complete manuscripts. [Kerala Tourism](https://www.keralatourism.org/temples/thiruvananthapuram/sree-padmanabhaswamy).
- June issue reports **Maha Kumbhabhishekam after about 270 years**, following renovation; ritual consecration/purification is the cultural significance. **2011 vault inventory** revealed major treasures; monetary valuations remain estimates. The decorative graphic's supernatural vault claims are folklore, not evidence.
- PYQ distinctions: **Nagara/Dravida/Vesara = temple architecture (2012, c)**; famous **Virupaksha Temple = Hampi (2009, c)** on the Tungabhadra. The source conflates it with **Pattadakal's Virupaksha**, built by Lokamahadevi to commemorate Vikramaditya II's Pallava victory; keep the two sites separate.
