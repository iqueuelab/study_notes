# Jalantheeswarar Temple at Thakkolam

## Heritage anchors
- Thakkolam, Ranipet district, Tamil Nadu; associated with the historical name Thiruvooral and Shiva worship.
- Pallava origins, subsequent Chola expansion and later Nayak contributions illustrate layered temple patronage; not every visible structure belongs to the founding period.
- The sand lingam is associated with **Theenda Thirumeni**, worship without direct touching. A tiered gopuram and granite enclosure are prominent architectural features.
- One of the **Paadal Petra Sthalams** celebrated in Tevaram hymns; associated with Sambandar's seventh-century devotional tradition.
- Inscriptions, architecture and ritual practice are complementary historical evidence. Conservation status should not be assumed to be ASI protection without a verified notification.
