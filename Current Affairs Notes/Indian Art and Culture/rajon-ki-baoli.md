# Rajon ki Baoli: stepwell conservation

## Conservation completed: 16 May 2025
- **Rajon ki Baoli**, sixteenth-century **Lodhi-era** stepwell, **Mehrauli Archaeological Park, Delhi**; Lodhi dynasty **1451–1526**. Stepped access supported traditional water storage and groundwater use; baolis combine hydraulic, architectural and social functions, not simply one generic rainwater tank design.
- **ASI + World Monuments Fund India Association + TCS Foundation** announced completed conservation/rejuvenation. Part of WMF's **Climate Heritage Initiative launched 2024**, connecting water heritage/local knowledge and adaptation. Completion of works is not a guarantee of indefinite water quality or maintenance. [Project partners' announcement](https://www.wmf.org/press-releases/restoration-rajon-ki-baoli).
- **ASI:** established **1861**, associated with **Alexander Cunningham**; Ministry of Culture, HQ New Delhi. Archaeological research, excavation, documentation and centrally protected monument/site conservation under **AMASR Act 1958** and associated law. Source “over 3,600” is an approximate administrative count, not all India's heritage sites.
- **WMF**, founded **1965**, New York-based conservation organisation; Indian arm supports expertise, fundraising, advocacy, skills and community participation. It is **not UNESCO** and does not confer World Heritage status. Donations/grants/CSR help, but legal custodianship and public accountability remain distinct.
- Mains: compatible traditional materials plus scientific assessment, restored drainage/water function, community access and maintenance; evaluate structural stability, water quality and sustainable stewardship alongside visual restoration.
