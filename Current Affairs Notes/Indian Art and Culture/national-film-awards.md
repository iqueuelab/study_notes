# National Film Awards

## Institution
- **1954 institution**, first recognising 1953 films, initially State Awards; **feature, non-feature, best writing on cinema** sections. National juries reward artistic/technical excellence, cultural diversity and socially relevant cinema.
- **NFDC under Information and Broadcasting Ministry** now manages awards; DFF historically administered them from 1973. Source's unqualified 2025 DFF claim is outdated. **CBFC certifies films**, rather than choosing winners. [NFDC document](https://nfdcindia.com/nfdcapi/api/gallery/1747660305018_5874827.pdf).
