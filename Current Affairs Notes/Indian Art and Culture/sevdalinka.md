# Sevdalinka and intangible cultural heritage

## Prelims identification
- **Bosnia and Herzegovina**; traditional urban folk singing, sometimes called Balkan blues.
- Blends South-Slavic oral poetry and Ottoman musical influences; storytelling, often love/longing; performed a cappella or with instruments.
- Inscribed in **2024 on UNESCO's Representative List of the Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity**. The source's phrase 'UNESCO National Inventory' conflates a national inventory with the international list. [UNESCO inscription](https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/sevdalinka-traditional-urban-folk-song-01872).
## Heritage framework
- UNESCO **2003 Convention**: safeguarding living practices, expressions and transmitted knowledge; distinct from tangible monuments.
- Transmission: families, imitation, lessons, workshops and institutions. Supports identity, inclusion and intercommunity ties; safeguarding should keep practice alive rather than freeze it.
