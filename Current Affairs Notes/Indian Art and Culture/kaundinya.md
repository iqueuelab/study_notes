# INSV Kaundinya: stitched shipbuilding and maritime heritage

## 29 December 2025: maiden overseas voyage
- Indian Navy's traditional stitched sailing vessel **INSV Kaundinya** departed **Porbandar, Gujarat→Muscat, Oman** on 29 December; the 30 December issue reports the departure, not arrival/completion.
- Natural materials and stitched wooden planks revive historic Indian shipbuilding/seafaring knowledge; Indian Ocean trade supported cultural exchange with Oman/Arabian Peninsula. This is living maritime heritage and cultural diplomacy, rather than a combat-vessel acquisition.
- **VAdm Krishna Swaminathan**, Western Naval Command, flagged off in presence of Oman's ambassador. **Cdr Vikas Sheoran** skipper; **Cdr Y. Hemant Kumar** expedition officer-in-charge; crew **four officers+13 naval sailors**. [Indian Navy release](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2209572&lang=1&reg=3).
- Heritage conservation, traditional artisans' knowledge, seamanship and India–Oman ties are useful mains links. Different infographic crew totals/dimensions are not substituted for the official crew count.
