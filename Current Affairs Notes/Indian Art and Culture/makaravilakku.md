# Makaravilakku at Sabarimala

## Ritual recall

- Annual **Sabarimala, Kerala**, **Lord Ayyappa**, **Makara Sankranti**, culmination of main pilgrimage season.
- **Thiruvabharanam**, sacred royal ornaments, brought from **Pandalam Palace**; source roughly **5,000 police** deployment in January 2025 is event-specific.
- **Ponnambalamedu** hill: ceremonial camphor flame/aarti, seen three times; source connects earlier practice to **Malayaraya** community and later management to **Travancore Devaswom Board**.
- Distinguish the **ritually lit Makaravilakku flame** from the **Makara star/Makarajyothi** in stricter usage; popular accounts sometimes interchange names. Faith interpretation is not an astronomical explanation of the human-lit flame. [Kerala Tourism festival account](https://www.keralatourism.org/1000festivals/assets/uploads/pdf/1515493142-0.pdf).
- Source describes **seven-day festival** and **Guruthi**, offering associated with wilderness deities, concluding rites; exact temple-access/closing schedules should be checked for the relevant year.
- Cultural continuity, tribal ritual heritage, crowd safety and environmental management are useful mains links.
