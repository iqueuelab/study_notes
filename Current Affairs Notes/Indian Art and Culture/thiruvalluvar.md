# Thiruvalluvar, Thirukkural and Kanyakumari

## Thirukkural
- Tamil poet-philosopher **Thiruvalluvar**; dates remain debated, so no unsupported birth/reign year.
- **1,330 couplets = 133 chapters × 10 couplets**.
- Three divisions: **Aram (virtue), Porul (wealth/statecraft), Inbam (love)**; ethical themes include honesty, compassion, moderation, justice and public welfare.
- Sites: Thiruvalluvar statue at Kanyakumari; **Valluvar Kottam, Chennai**.
## December 2024 news
- Source reports glass bridge inaugurated **31 December 2024**, joining **Vivekananda Rock Memorial and Thiruvalluvar statue**, length 77 m, width 10 m, cost about Rs 37 crore; Thirukkural Week proposed for the last week of December.
- **Do not memorise the source's 133-foot bridge height**: it appears to confuse the statue's height with the bridge. Global 'longest/highest' rankings are also unverified.
## Mains use
- Heritage-linked tourism can support crafts/jobs; plan for marine ecology, coastal weather, maintenance and accessible/safe visitor movement. Ethics examples: wealth for social welfare and responsible governance.
