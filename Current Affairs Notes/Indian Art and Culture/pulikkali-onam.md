# Pulikkali, Onam and Kerala folk traditions

## Festival and performance
- **Pulikkali/Pulikali**, “tiger dance”, is a Kerala folk performance associated especially with **Thrissur's Swaraj Round/Ground** during Onam, usually its **fourth day**. Performers paint yellow/red/black tiger patterns, sometimes wear masks, and enact tiger/hunter themes with percussion such as **chenda, thakil and udukku**. Tradition links its patronage to **Sakthan Thampuran of Cochin**. [Kerala Tourism](https://www.keralatourism.org/onam/onam-games/pulikali).
- **Onam** is Kerala's harvest festival in **Chingam**, the Malayalam calendar's first month; **Atham to Thiruvonam** marks the principal ten-day sequence. Mahabali's annual return belongs to festival legend/cultural memory, not verified political chronology.
- **Vallamkali = boat racing**, with notable Pampa-region traditions; **Kaikottikali/Thiruvathirakali and Thumbi Thullal** are women's dance/performance traditions. Do not call Vallamkali a women's dance or imply all Kerala boat races occur on one river.
