# Bonalu festival and Telangana folk traditions

## Festival and rituals
- Telangana folk festival, especially **Hyderabad, Secunderabad and surrounding areas**, during **Ashada Masam**; thanksgiving and vow fulfilment to **Mahakali** and local forms **Yellamma, Pochamma, Maisamma, Mateshwari**.
- **Bonalu derives from Bonam (meal)**: women carry decorated brass/earthen pots of rice cooked with milk/jaggery, neem/turmeric/vermilion decorations and a lamp to temples; women are central to community participation.
- Celebrations begin at **Golconda Fort**, then different temple days over several weeks, including **Balkampet Yellamma, Ujjaini Mahakali (Secunderabad), Katta Maisamma, Pochamma (Chilkalguda), Lal Darwaza**. Do not impose an identical week schedule on every year/temple.
- **Rangam:** ritual oracle, traditionally a woman in trance; **Pothuraju**, regarded as the goddess's brother, dances/leads the procession, turmeric-smeared. Folk music, dance and devotion combine.
- Tradition associates its origin with a **19 th-century plague**; this is an origin belief, not evidence of biomedical causation. Telangana designated Bonalu a **State festival in 2014**.
