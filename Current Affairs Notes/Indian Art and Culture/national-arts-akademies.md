# National arts Akademies: literature, performance and visual arts

## Sahitya Akademi
- National Academy of Letters, **1954**, autonomous Ministry of Culture institution; promotes Indian multilingual literature through original-work and translation awards. Recognises **24 languages: 22 Eighth Schedule languages + English + Rajasthani**.
- Sahitya Akademi Fellowship is its highest honour; yearly literary awards are distinct. **Bal Sahitya Puraskar** recognises children's literature; **Yuva Puraskar** encourages young authors (up to 35 under its rules). **Gramalok** supports rural literary engagement and **Dalit Chetna** highlights marginalised voices.
- June 2025 clipping: **23 Yuva and 24 Bal Sahitya recipients** announced; examples include Yuva winners **Advait Kottary (English), Parvati Tirkey (Hindi), Lakshmihar (Tamil), Prasad Suri (Telugu)**; Bal winners **Nitin Kushalappa M.P. (English), Sushil Shukla (Hindi), Tridib Kumar Chattopadhyay (Bengali), K. Shivalingappa Handihal (Kannada), Vishnupuram Saravanan (Tamil)**. Award includes copper plaque and **₹50,000** as reported.
## Sangeet Natak Akademi
- Ministry of Education resolution **31 May 1952**; inaugurated/functional **1953**; autonomous national performing-arts academy now under Culture. Covers music, dance, theatre, folk/tribal arts, puppetry and scholarship.
- Highest honour: **Akademi Ratna Fellowship**; source cites ₹3 lakh, copper plaque and angavastram. Annual **Akademi Puraskar**: ₹1 lakh plus plaque/angavastram. Fellowship rules and monetary values are dated, not constitutional provisions. [Official institutional history](https://sangeetnatak.gov.in/sections/drama).
- Established **NSD, New Delhi (1959)**; **JN Manipur Dance Academy, Imphal (1954)**; **Kathak Kendra, New Delhi (1964)**. NSD later became autonomous; founding affiliation does not imply it remains a department of SNA.
## Lalit Kala Akademi
- National Academy of Art, New Delhi, inaugurated **5 August 1954** by Maulana Abul Kalam Azad; registered as a **society on 11 March 1957**. Society registration does not confer “statutory-body” status. Visual arts include painting, sculpture, prints and photography. [Official history](https://lalitkala.gov.in/).
- Printed **2009 PYQ**: NSD founded by SNA in 1959 and Sahitya Akademi Fellowship highest honour — both true; source omits alternative answer options, so do not invent them for JSON.
