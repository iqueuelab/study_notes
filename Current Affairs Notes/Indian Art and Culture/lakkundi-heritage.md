# Lakkundi: Chalukyan heritage and community conservation

## Place and architectural significance
- **Lakkundi, Gadag district, Karnataka**, known as **Lokkigundi** in inscriptions: major centre of **Kalyani/Western Chalukyan** architecture with Hindu temples, Jain basadis and stepped wells (**kalyanis/pushkaranis**). **Kashi Vishveshvara, Nanneshvara, Brahma Jinalaya** and **Musukina Bavi** are key examples.
- **Attimabbe**, early eleventh-century Jain patron, is associated with the prominent Jain basti; she was a patron/philanthropist, not adequately described merely as a “queen”. Fine carving, temple-water architecture and Jain–Shaiva coexistence are core revision themes. [ASI Dharwad](https://www.asidharwadcircle.in/temples-sculpture-gallery-shed/).
## Discovery, excavation and public stewardship
- Source describes a **466-g gold hoard found on January 10** by a young villager and transferred to Karnataka's archaeology authorities. It inconsistently labels its year as 2024 elsewhere; retain the **year unresolved** rather than creating a dated historical event. Its automatic “finder gets half under treasure laws” claim is not a general entitlement established here.
- Earlier surveys/excavations, inscriptions, pottery, pillars and temple foundations suggest a larger settlement beneath the present village. Source references **1953,1968,1976,2003** work and proposed further excavation; claimed **17 basadi bases/~10 buried temples/6-foot burial depth/Neolithic 12,000–4,000 years** require site-specific archaeological confirmation, not a settled uniform chronology.
- The **November 2024 heritage-awareness campaign** reportedly collected roughly **1,000 relics** door to door after ancient stones had been reused in buildings, washing and field boundaries. Interpretation displays/open-air museum proposals can turn local awareness into preservation and tourism livelihoods.
- Source reports **16 state-protected structures and eight proposed additions**; distinguish proposed protection from a notified monument. Its Gazetteer number “101 temples/basadis” is not a verified count of surviving monuments. Tentative-list proposals are distinct from UNESCO World Heritage inscription: **Lakkundi is not established here as an inscribed World Heritage Site**.
