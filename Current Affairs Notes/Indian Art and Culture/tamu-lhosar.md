# Tamu Lhosar

## Identification
- **Gurung community**, predominantly Nepal: New Year celebration; **Tamu** refers to Gurung people and **Lhosar** to New Year.
- Observed on **15 Poush** (late December); festivities include feasts, dances, music and ethnic dress.
- Associated districts: Lamjung, Gorkha, Tanahun, Syangja, Manang, Kaski and Parbat; Kathmandu's Tundikhel hosts celebrations, but the festival is not exclusive to the capital.
- Source dress terms: women - Makhamali blouse/Ghalek shawl; men - Bhoto/Bhangra/Kachhad.
## Significance and exam trap
- Cultural continuity, community identity, intergenerational transmission and tourism; Nepal recognises the festival as a holiday.
- **Do not equate 15 Poush with the astronomical winter solstice.** The supplied practice question incorrectly treats the solstice claim as true.
