# Maratha Military Landscapes of India

## July 2025 inscription
- UNESCO inscribed the **Maratha Military Landscapes of India** as India's **44th World Heritage property** in July 2025. The serial property combines **12 forts: 11 in Maharashtra and Gingee in Tamil Nadu**, representing a landscape-based defence network developed during the seventeenth to early nineteenth centuries. [UNESCO property](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1739).
- **Hill forts:** Salher, Shivneri, Lohgad, Raigad, Rajgad, Gingee; **hill-forest:** Pratapgad; **hill-plateau:** Panhala; **coastal:** Vijaydurg; **island:** Khanderi, Suvarnadurg, Sindhudurg. Forts relate to the Sahyadris, Deccan Plateau, Konkan coast and Eastern Ghats.
- **ASI protects eight:** Shivneri, Lohgad, Raigad, Panhala, Suvarnadurg, Vijaydurg, Sindhudurg, Gingee. Maharashtra protects Salher, Rajgad, Pratapgad and Khanderi.
- Local terrain, walls, watchtowers, secret routes, granaries and water storage supported command, supplies, surveillance and **Ganimi Kava** guerrilla tactics. Networked forts embody **Swarajya** and resistance, with use continued under Sambhaji/Peshwas until the 1818 defeat of Maratha power.
