# Kashi Tamil Sangamam and Agasthyar

## Edition 3.0, 2025
- **15–24 February 2025**, **Namo Ghat, Varanasi**, during the wider Maha Kumbh period; Kumbh's main venue **Prayagraj**, not Varanasi.
- Theme: **Sage Agasthyar's legacy**—Tamil language/literature, philosophy, knowledge and health traditions; strengthen cultural/intellectual ties between Tamil Nadu and Kashi.

Separate **200 Tamil-origin Central-university students**, youth participation encouraged. [Education Ministry programme](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleaseIframePage.aspx?PRID=2102893&lang=2&reg=48).
## Links: history versus tradition
- Agasthyar's foundational role in Tamil knowledge is a **traditional attribution**, not a precisely dated proof of sole authorship of a language.
- **Brahmanda Purana** narrative of Dasharatha seeking Kamakshi's blessings at Kanchipuram: religious tradition, not independently established chronology.
- Source **15th-century Parakrama Pandya** and Sivakasi temple story; **Kasi Viswanathar Temple, Tenkasi** recalls north–south religious links. Distinguish temple history from journey legends.
- **Varanasi–Kanchipuram silk trade** and pilgrimage support people-to-people exchange. Evaluate inclusion, multilingual learning and sustained exchanges rather than only delegate numbers.
