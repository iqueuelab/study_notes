# Deepavali: UNESCO living heritage inscription

## 10 December 2025: Representative List inscription
- **Deepavali** inscribed on UNESCO's **Representative List of the Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity**, at the **20th committee session in New Delhi**. This safeguards a **living practice**, distinct from inscription of a monument/property on the World Heritage List. [UNESCO announcement](https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/deepavali-inscribed-unescos-representative-list-intangible-cultural-heritage-humanity).
- Lamps, rangoli, prayers, food, charity, craft knowledge and family/community participation transmit traditions. Regional associations include Rama's return, Naraka Chaturdashi, Lakshmi/Kali worship and related Jain/Sikh observances; practices and meanings differ among communities and are not one uniform ritual.
- Value: continuity, plural identities, intergenerational learning, artisan livelihoods and cultural diplomacy. Inscription is not an automatic cash grant, guarantee of tourism income or endorsement of every commercial/environmental practice associated with celebrations.
- Correct the source's Indian example: UNESCO's **Thatheras brass/copper utensil craft is Jandiala Guru, Punjab**, not Telangana. **Garba 2023** is latest among the four options in the **2024 PYQ** (Chhau 2010, Durga Puja 2021, Kumbh Mela 2017). In the separate **2024 properties PYQ**, **Santiniketan and Hoysala ensembles** entered World Heritage in **2023**; Rani-ki-Vav **2014**, Mahabodhi **2002**. Both questions already exist in the common bank.
