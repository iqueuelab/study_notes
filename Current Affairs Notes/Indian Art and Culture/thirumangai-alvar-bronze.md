# Thirumangai Alvar bronze: provenance and restitution

## March 2026: return of the temple bronze
- **3 March 2026:** Oxford's **Ashmolean Museum** handed the Indian government a **16th-century bronze of Thirumangai/Tirumankai Alvar** at India's High Commission, London. It is linked to **Soundararaja Perumal Temple, Tamil Nadu**. Alvars are Tamil **Vaishnava poet-saints**; Nayanars belong to the Shaiva tradition.
- Museum acquisition **1967** via the J.R. Belmont collection; how it entered that collection was undocumented. **November 2019** research identified a **1957 temple photograph** in IFP–EFEO archives. A **11 February 2020** police report described a replica replacing the original; India's formal claim followed **3 March 2020**.
- Provenance research, archival comparison, temple/ASI coordination and metal analysis supported restitution. Oxford Council approved the claim **11 March 2024**; Charity Commission approval followed **December 2024**, before the physical handover. [Museum's account](https://www.ashmolean.org/press/return-of-a-16th-century-bronze-to-india-press-release).
- Restitution reconnects a sacred object with worship and community memory. Distinguish the saint's earlier life from the date of this bronze, and undocumented provenance/the police complaint from a judicial finding against every later collector.
