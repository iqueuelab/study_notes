## Policy and trade-offs
- EVs, hydrogen and biofuels aim to reduce oil dependence/emissions; **National Electric Mobility Mission**, **National Green Hydrogen Mission**, manufacturing **PLI**, charging infrastructure and scrappage policy.
- **PM Gati Shakti**, highways and multimodal logistics can lower transport costs. Demand, domestic components, skills, R&D and export competitiveness support industry.
- EVs eliminate tailpipe emissions, but total benefit depends on electricity mix, battery sourcing/recycling and vehicle use. Public transport and walkability also reduce congestion; replacing every car with an EV alone does not.
