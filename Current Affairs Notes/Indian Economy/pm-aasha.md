# PM-AASHA: farmer price support and consumer stability

## Integrated scheme
- **Pradhan Mantri Annadata Aay SanraksHan Abhiyan**, launched 2018; September 2024 continuation integrated farmer/consumer price interventions, with **₹35,000 crore through 2025–26**. [Cabinet decision](https://www.pib.gov.in/Pressreleaseshare.aspx?PRID=2055990&lang=2&reg=48).
| Component | Function |
|---|---|
| PSS: Price Support Scheme | Physical procurement of notified pulses/oilseeds/copra at MSP under conditions |
| PSF: Price Stabilisation Fund | Buffers and releases to moderate essential-commodity price volatility |
| PDPS: Price Deficiency Payment Scheme | Compensates an eligible price gap for notified oilseeds; no physical government purchase |
| MIS: Market Intervention Scheme | Market intervention for qualifying perishable horticultural produce |
- PSS normal ceiling: **25% of national production** from 2024–25; **tur, urad, masur exempted for 2024–25**, with 100% procurement provision. This is a dated, conditional procurement policy, not automatic purchase of every farmer's entire crop.
- NAFED **eSamridhi** and NCCF **eSamyukti** facilitate pre-registration. State requests, crop quality, operations and storage affect access.
- PDPS coverage was enhanced to **40% of State oilseed production**, implementation period to four months, in the September decision.
- MSP support to growers and affordable consumer prices require different tools. Improve registration access, prompt payment, transparent stocks and diversification without unlimited fiscal/storage burdens.
