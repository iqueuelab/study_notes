# Aviation MRO: Safran’s Hyderabad LEAP-engine facility

## 26 November 2025: Hyderabad inauguration
- PM virtually inaugurated **Safran Aircraft Engine Services India's LEAP-engine MRO centre**, GMR Aerospace and Industrial Park, Hyderabad. **MRO = maintenance, repair and overhaul**: inspections, repair, parts replacement and engine shop work support airworthiness and reduce overseas service dependence.
- Safran describes its largest LEAP centre: **45,000 m²**, **€200 million total investment**, **operations planned for 2026**, ramping up to **300 engine shop visits/year**. Source's **₹1,300 crore initial investment** is a separately reported initial figure, not an exact currency conversion or proof of full operational capacity in November. [Safran announcement](https://www.safran-group.com/pressroom/safran-steps-expansion-india-civil-and-military-aviation-and-deepens-commitment-make-india-2025-11-26).
- **LEAP = Leading Edge Aviation Propulsion**, made by **CFM International**, a **50:50 Safran Aircraft Engines–GE Aerospace JV**; variants power A320neo-family aircraft and Boeing 737 MAX. Advanced materials/efficient design reduce fuel/noise relative to predecessor engines; performance depends on variant and comparison baseline.
- Value: skilled jobs/training, local suppliers/MSMEs, foreign-exchange savings, faster turnaround, regional service business and India–France industrial links. PM's **Design in India** call concerns R&D/design as well as assembly; inauguration alone transfers no blanket intellectual-property rights.
- Differentiate civil LEAP centre from the separate **M88/Rafale MRO groundbreaking**. FDI references are sector/route-specific: the source's 74% defence automatic-route shorthand is not a universal 74% cap or evidence every aerospace activity has identical FDI rules.
