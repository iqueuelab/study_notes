# Pamban railway bridge and independent safety clearance

## Engineering and map
- New bridge links **Mandapam–Rameswaram/Pamban Island, Tamil Nadu**; RVNL project, about **2.08 km**, India's first vertical-lift railway sea bridge.
- **72.5 m navigational span** rises vertically by up to 17 m; deck is about 3 m above the old bridge. The source's 63 m figure is not the new lift-span length. [Railways](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleseDetailm.aspx?PRID=2118895&lang=2&reg=48).
- Old bridge opened in **1914**; Scherzer rolling-bascule leaves rotate upwards, unlike the new vertical lift. Future double-track provision differs from initial commissioned track capacity.
- March 2025: safety trials/clearance context; **inauguration followed on 6 April 2025**, not already on March 27.
## CRS
- **Commission of Railway Safety**, under Ministry of Civil Aviation; headquarters Lucknow. Inspects and authorises railway openings under the Railways Act framework and investigates specified accidents.
- Separate oversight from the railway operator helps independent assessment of structural strength, track/signalling, navigation clearance and operating procedures.
## 6 April 2025: inauguration
- The new Pamban railway bridge was inaugurated on **6 April 2025**. Its lift span is **72.5 m**, not the inconsistent 63 m figure repeated in this daily PDF. Sensors, electromechanical operation and train-control interlocking coordinate lifting with railway traffic; trains must stop while the span opens for shipping. [Railways](https://www.pib.gov.in/Pressreleaseshare.aspx?PRID=2119836&lang=2&reg=48).
