# Oil palm and NMEO-OP

## Crop and trade
- African oil palm **Elaeis guineensis**, native to West/Central Africa, has high oil yield per hectare. Indonesia/Malaysia dominate production; India relies heavily on imports from them and Thailand.
- **Mesocarp/fruit pulp → palm oil; kernel → palm-kernel oil**. Uses include food, cosmetics, soaps, pharmaceuticals and biodiesel; kernel oil is not exclusively non-edible.
- Warm humid tropical conditions, ample rainfall and well-drained fertile soils favour the crop. Andhra Pradesh, Telangana and Kerala are major Indian producers; Northeast/Andaman expansion needs water, biodiversity and land-use safeguards. The source's area/shares are dated estimates rather than current totals.
## NMEO–Oil Palm
- **Centrally sponsored scheme launched 2021** to reduce edible-oil import dependence; 2025–26 target: **additional 6.5 lakh hectares**, reaching about **10 lakh hectares**; CPO targets **11.2 lakh tonnes in 2025–26**, **28 lakh tonnes by 2029–30** are planned outcomes.
- **Viability Price** assures fresh-fruit-bunch remuneration, with viability-gap payments through DBT. Source support norms: planting **₹12,000→₹29,000/ha**, old-garden rejuvenation **₹250/plant**; special Northeast/Andaman support includes an additional **2% of CPO price**, terraces, fencing and associated works.
- Trade-off: high yields/import substitution versus plantation water demand, monocultures, habitat loss and chemical inputs; use suitable already-cleared land and transparent local consultation.
- PYQ recall: oil palm is African-origin, used in lipstick/perfume and biodiesel (**2021: statements 2,3**). Edible-oil imports exceeded domestic production in the 2018 question's reference period; blanket customs-duty exemption claim is false (**statement 1 only**). The supplied PDF omits answer-option sets for these two questions; review against the original papers before adding complete PYQs.

## August 2025 supply outlook
- Indonesia/Malaysia, roughly **85% of global supply** in source, face ageing plantations and farmers, slow subsidised replanting and higher domestic biodiesel demand. Report forecasts exports could fall **up to 20% by 2030**; a scenario, not an already-realised fall.
- Source peak yield **8–20 years** and **3–5-year income gap during replanting** explain smallholders' reluctance; alternatives include bridging finance, phased replacement and reliable planting material. Indonesian **2.5-million-ha replanting target/10% completion as of October 2024** and Malaysian **37% official versus over-50% industry ageing estimates** use different measures/periods.
- India risks import-price/food-inflation pressure; diversify suppliers/oils, improve domestic productivity and assess expansion's land/water/biodiversity costs. Source **9.2 million tonnes imports (2023–24), palm about 60% of edible-oil imports**, are dated estimates, not fixed shares.
- Repeated NMEO-OP targets/support are already in core notes. Source's starting CPO production **0.27 lakh tonnes** needs original-series/unit verification; do not treat this small baseline as established. [Original Cabinet targets](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=1746943&lang=2&reg=48). Palm-kernel oil has edible as well as industrial uses; palm oil does not supply over half of all global vegetable oils under every measure.
- **2021 PYQ:** African origin makes statement 1 false; lipstick/perfume and biodiesel uses make **2,3 correct**. PDF again omits the full option set; preserve as revision and flag bank completion against original paper.

## December 2025: NMEO–Oilseeds alongside oil palm
- **NMEO–Oilseeds, launched 2024**, complements **NMEO–Oil Palm (2021)**, with DA&FW implementation across States/UTs. Improve seed quality/productivity, processing and market linkages; expand suitable cultivation including rice/potato fallows to reduce import vulnerability.
- Primary food-oil crops include **rapeseed–mustard, groundnut, soybean, sunflower, sesame, safflower, niger and linseed**; secondary sources include **cottonseed, coconut, rice bran and suitable tree-borne oils**. Source also lists **castor, neem, karanj and jatropha**; do not infer all listed oilseed products are edible oils—several primarily have industrial/biofuel uses.
- Source funding description: **60:40 general States/legislature UTs, 90:10 NE/Himalayan States, 100% other UTs**. Oil-palm support includes nurseries/seed gardens, planting, maintenance, intercropping and price assurance; existing NMEO-OP notes contain detailed targets and safeguards.
- Source's **~60% import dependence** and **US$18–20 billion annual import bill** lack a common specified reference year and are contextual estimates, not December 2025 audited totals. Supplier concentration creates price/foreign-exchange risks; productivity gains must be assessed with water, biodiversity and farmer-income effects.
