UDAN: regional aviation and expansion proposals

Editable notes

November 2025: proposed modified UDAN

  • UDAN — Ude Desh Ka Aam Nagrik, regional connectivity scheme launched 2016 under NCAP; first service Shimla–Delhi, April 2017. Supports unserved/underserved links through viability-gap funding, concessionary charges/taxes and Centre–State participation; airlines bid/propose routes based on demand and scheme rules.
  • Source reports proposed ₹30,000 crore expansion: ₹18,000 crore airport infrastructure +₹12,000 crore VGF. It envisages continuation beyond April 2027, 120 additional destinations, and 4 crore passengers over a decade. EFC appraisal/Cabinet approval were still required in this account; proposal/outlay is not a sanctioned expenditure or achieved passenger number.
  • Targets include Northeast, hills, aspirational/tribal areas, small airports, heliports and water aerodromes. Leasing/maintenance support, reliable small aircraft, land/clearance coordination and viable demand are necessary; bidding alone cannot ensure every selected route remains commercially sustainable.
  • Source-era totals: 649 routes operationalised of 915 awarded; 93 aerodromes including 15 heliports and two water aerodromes; 1.56 crore passengers; 3.23 lakh flights; around ₹4,300 crore VGF; ₹4,638 crore infrastructure investment. The 93 already includes heliports/water aerodromes—do not add them again. These are cumulative snapshots, not proof all 649 routes still operate simultaneously.
  • 915−649 =266, so the PDF's “around 300+ never took off” does not follow directly from those counts; non-operation, temporary suspension and never-started routes are different measures.
  • Development gains: tourism/trade, healthcare/education access, emergency mobility and regional integration. Risks: underused airports, airline finances, scarce turboprops, terrain/weather constraints, operating approvals, weak surface connections and dependence after VGF expires. Judge success by durable affordable service, load factors, safety and public cost per useful connection.
