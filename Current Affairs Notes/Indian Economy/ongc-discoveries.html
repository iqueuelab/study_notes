May 2025: Mumbai Offshore discoveries

  • ONGC announced Suryamani, OALP-VI block MB-OSHP-2020/2: initial tested oil 2,235 barrels/day, gas 45,181 m³/day; Vajramani, OALP-III block MB-OSHP-2018/1: oil 2,122 barrels/day, gas 83,120 m³/day. These are discovery/well-test rates, not assured sustained commercial production or reserve volumes. Suryamani zones reported as Basal Clastics/Mukta Formation. Parliamentary confirmation of discovery tests.
  • Source additionally reports Yandapalli-1, Malleswaram petroleum mining lease, onshore Krishna–Godavari basin, depth 3,958 m. Appraisal and development economics still determine recoverable reserves and production timing.
  • Mumbai Offshore includes Mumbai High, Bassein, Heera, Neelam, Panna–Mukta; map places these in the Arabian Sea west of Maharashtra. Source shares 35% oil/18% gas lack a reference year; do not treat as current fixed national contributions.

Policy and implications

  • HELP/OALP permits companies to identify acreage and submit expressions of interest; identified blocks are then offered through competitive bid rounds. “Bid anytime” wrongly conflates EOI submission with unrestricted continuous auctioning. DGH process.
  • Potential benefits: domestic supply, foreign-exchange savings, investment/employment and infrastructure. Source's ~85% crude/~50% gas import dependence are indicative dated ratios, not all-energy dependence.
  • Discovery → appraisal → development approval/investment → infrastructure → production. No clear source development timetable; slower ramp-up is an analyst assessment, not a demonstrated failure. Financial-results/dividend announcement does not establish project commercial readiness.
