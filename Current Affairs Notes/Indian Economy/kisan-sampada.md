# Pradhan Mantri Kisan SAMPADA Yojana: food processing

## Food processing and July 2025 allocation
- **Pradhan Mantri Kisan SAMPADA Yojana**, MoFPI **Central Sector** scheme approved May 2017; distinct from **Pradhan Mantri Krishi Sinchayee Yojana**, which shares PMKSY acronym. Farm-to-market infrastructure reduces post-harvest loss, adds value, supports farmer returns/rural jobs and processed-food exports.
- **31 July 2025 Cabinet:** total **₹6,520 crore**, including **₹1,920 crore additional**, for **15th FC cycle 2021–22 to 2025–26**, rather than all spending in FY 2025.
- Additional **₹1,000 crore** supports **50 multi-product irradiation units** under Integrated Cold Chain/Value Addition and **100 NABL-accredited food-testing labs** under Food Safety/Quality Assurance; **₹920 crore** for other component projects. Approved investments, not completed facilities. Irradiation controls microbes/pests and extends shelf life; testing checks safety/quality/export compliance. [Cabinet](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2150646&lang=2&reg=48).
- Current components: cold chain, processing/preservation capacity, agro-processing clusters, food safety, R&D and Operation Greens. **Mega Food Parks, separate Backward/Forward Linkages and specified promotional/skill/HACCP components discontinued for new support**, committed liabilities continue; PDF's seven-component list is historical. [MoFPI](https://www.mofpi.gov.in//Schemes/about-pmksy-scheme).
- Practice key prints “2 and 3 only” for two statements: intended **statement 2 only**, since PMKSY is Central Sector.
