# Machilipatnam port: revival and coastal logistics

## Historical and geographical recall
- **Machilipatnam/Masulipatnam**, coastal Andhra Pradesh on the Bay of Bengal near the Krishna delta; source associates ancient **Masula** with early maritime trade. Avoid locating the town exactly at the present Krishna river mouth.
- Flourished as a textile and maritime trading centre, especially **16th–18th centuries**, with Arab/European commercial connections; colonial trade increasingly favoured Madras/Chennai. Historical names/trade claims should not imply uninterrupted modern port operation.
## July 2025 construction snapshot
- New greenfield harbour under construction near Machilipatnam; source reports **~48% complete**, ~1,250 workers in shifts and an **end-2026 operations target**. Target is not completion.
- Source engineering figures: **56 million m³ dredging**, **2.5-km breakwater**, ~**2.1 million tonnes rock**; dredging creates navigable depth and breakwater reduces wave exposure. The simplified “Manginapudi” site description should not replace detailed port-project boundaries.
- Potential Krishna/Guntur industrial/export support, coastal shipping and links toward Southeast Asia; warehousing, cold chains and road/rail connectivity determine real benefits. Port-led/Sagarmala and Blue Economy opportunities require environmental safeguards and commercial viability; decongestion of other ports is an expected benefit.
