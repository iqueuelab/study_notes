# BHAVYA: plug-and-play industrial parks

## 18 March 2026 Cabinet approval
- Cabinet approved **Bharat Audyogik Vikas Yojna (BHAVYA)**: **₹33,660 crore for 100 plug-and-play industrial parks**. The issue reports a six-year **FY2026–27–2031–32** horizon and a first phase of 50; these are planned rollout figures, not completed parks.
- Cabinet announcement specifies **100–1,000 acre** parks, **up to ₹1 crore per acre** support for core, value-added and social infrastructure; external-connectivity support up to **25% of project cost**. Partnerships with **States/private players**, challenge-based project selection and state-led single-window/reform commitments are central. [Cabinet decision](https://www.pib.gov.in/Pressreleaseshare.aspx?PRID=2241785&lang=2&reg=48).
- Ready land/utilities/services and factory/testing/warehousing facilities can reduce time to production. Alignment with **PM GatiShakti**, underground utility corridors and green/resource-efficient design supports logistics and maintenance.
- Expected manufacturing, MSME, supplier-cluster and job benefits depend on investor demand, credible clearances, land/water availability, worker amenities and execution. Source's **25-acre special-area threshold** is not stated in the 18 March Cabinet release; retain it as an issue-reported detail pending contemporaneous scheme rules, not a universal minimum.
