Editable notes

Institution and Davos

  • 1971, Klaus Schwab: not-for-profit European Management Forum; renamed World Economic Forum, 1987. Headquarters Cologny/Geneva, Switzerland; offices New York, San Francisco, Beijing and Tokyo.
  • Swiss recognition as an international organisation in 2015 does not make it a UN agency or a treaty-based global government. Dialogue among business, government, academia and civil society; stakeholder approach to economic/social challenges.
  • Annual meeting 20–24 January 2025, Davos; source about 3,000 participants, members and selected invitees. Partner corporations largely fund activities; source describes roughly 1,000 leading member companies, typically large-turnover enterprises, not a minimum turnover required of every attendee. WEF history.

Report recall

  • Global Gender Gap Report/Index; Global Risks Report; Global Competitiveness Report; travel/tourism competitiveness/development reports.
  • Source also lists Global Information Technology Report and Global Enabling Trade Report: historical report families; do not assume every title continues as an annual WEF publication with unchanged co-publishers.
  • PYQ answers: 2017 Gender Gap ranking → WEF; 2009 founder → Klaus Schwab; 2019 Global Competitiveness Report → WEF. Full questions in common prelims JSON.
  • Report trap: Human Development Report → UNDP, not WEF. The weekly coaching test repeats WEF facts but is not a UPSC PYQ.
