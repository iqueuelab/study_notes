# Morbi ceramics: energy dependence and industrial disruption

## March 2026: fuel disruption and shutdown risk
- **Morbi, Gujarat**, is a major ceramic tiles/sanitaryware cluster. Propane and piped natural gas fuel kilns and drying; Gulf shipping disruption transmits geopolitical risk into manufacturing, employment and export orders.
- Industry representatives reported about **600 units**, **2–3 lakh direct workers/~4 lakh including indirect livelihoods**, and **80% propane dependence**. These are association estimates, not a census of the whole Indian ceramics industry.
- Reported available stocks: **propane 2–4 days**, pipeline gas roughly **ten days**, CNG roughly **one week**. They describe different fuels/supply channels at the report date, not guaranteed national reserves. A Saudi-port disruption on **23 February** reportedly preceded the wider conflict.
- Predicted **30–45-day closure** after one/two weeks of disruption and up to two months' restart delay were **conditional industry warnings**, not completed shutdown outcomes. Avoid translating threats to shipping through Hormuz into proof every vessel stopped or a legally valid permanent closure of the strait.
- Policy links: diversified energy sourcing, efficient kilns, working-capital/worker support, reliable logistics and lower emissions. Substituting fuels requires technical, cost and pollution assessment, not automatic conversion.
