Indian Economy

Index of Industrial Production and industrial growth

Editable notes

July 2025 quick estimate, released 28 August

  • IIP +3.5% year-on-year; manufacturing +5.4%, mining −7.2%, electricity +0.6%. General index 155.0, versus 149.8 in July 2024; quick estimates are subject to revision. MoSPI release.
  • NSO/MoSPI's monthly volume/quantum index, base 2011–12 = 100 in this release; manufacturing, mining and electricity. Weighted production relatives aggregate output, not nominal sales or prices.
  • Use-based groups: primary, capital, intermediate, infrastructure/construction, consumer durables and non-durables. The index excludes services and is not the whole economy/GDP.
  • An index of 116 means 16% above the base-period level; it does not by itself imply 16% year-on-year growth. Growth must compare like periods; a positive index level alone says nothing about current expansion/contraction.
  • Capacity, investment, demand and supply constraints shape output; IIP assists policy/investment assessment but cannot independently identify inflation causes or replace broader national accounts.

August 2025 quick estimate, released 29 September

  • IIP +4.0% year-on-year, against July's then quick estimate +3.5%; mining +6.0%, manufacturing +3.8%, electricity +4.1%. Index 151.7, versus August 2024 145.8, on 2011–12 = 100. Subject to revision; this release reports August output, not September output.
  • Consumer non-durables −6.3%, infrastructure/construction goods +10.6%: overall growth can coexist with weakness in consumption segments. The clipping's “slows to 4%” should not be read as a fall from July's 3.5%. MoSPI release.

September 2025 output: October release context

  • Embedded clipping reports IIP growth at 4% in September. This is a year-on-year rate for September output, not a 4% index level or October production. Retain as the issue's reported quick estimate, subject to revision.
  • The remaining two-page explainer repeats existing volume-index, NSO/MoSPI, mining/manufacturing/electricity and 2011–12 source base notes. Index 116 compares with base-year output; positive index value does not prove current growth, and IIP is not a direct price/inflation index.
