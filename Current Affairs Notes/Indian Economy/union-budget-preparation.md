# Union Budget preparation and the Halwa ceremony

## Budget 2026–27 preparation
- **27 January 2026:** Finance Minister Nirmala Sitharaman and Minister of State Pankaj Chaudhary attended the customary **Halwa ceremony at the Budget Press, North Block**. It marks the final preparation stage and precedes officials' **lock-in**, intended to protect budget confidentiality. Budget presentation was scheduled for **1 February**, still prospective on this issue date. [Finance Ministry release](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleaseIframePage.aspx?PRID=2219158&lang=2&reg=48).
- Halwa is an administrative tradition, not a constitutional requirement. **Article 112** provides for the Annual Financial Statement; Demands for Grants and the Finance Bill are related, distinct budget documents. Bilingual digital documents were to become available after the Budget speech through the Union Budget app/website.
- Practice MCQ: ceremony → lock-in of budget officials, **(b)**; no authentic CSE year is supplied.
