# Kandla–Gorakhpur LPG pipeline

## Project

-~2, 800 km LPG pipeline from Kandla through Gujarat/Madhya Pradesh/Uttar Pradesh to Gorakhpur; associated distribution can serve wider regions without every State lying on the main route.

- **IHB:** IndianOil 50%, BPCL25%, HPCL25%; reported capacity~8.25 million tonnes/year and project cost~₹10, 000 crore are source-era estimates.
- March 2025 commissioning schedules were plans, not proof the whole pipeline was already operational.
- LPG is principally propane/butane; LNG is mainly methane. Pipeline transport can reduce road-tanker exposure and logistics costs but retains leak/fire/maintenance risks.
- PNGRB standards, monitoring, emergency plans and maintenance matter. It does not automatically cut consumer prices or end import dependence; cited Jaipur tanker disaster occurredDecember 2024, not 2023.
