# Pradhan Mantri Krishi Sinchayee Yojana

## Objectives and implementation
- Launched **2015**: converge irrigation investment at field level, expand assured irrigation (**Har Khet Ko Pani**), reduce water losses, encourage precision irrigation and coordinate water management at watershed/local scales.
- States, districts and Panchayati Raj Institutions participate in decentralised planning; convergence of funding and infrastructure with farm-level delivery is essential.

## Significance, constraints and answer framework
- Reliable irrigation + efficient delivery → greater crop productivity and resilience → diversification into horticulture and improved livelihoods. Watershed works also conserve soil and recharge groundwater.
- Drip/sprinklers, fertigation and water budgeting reduce avoidable losses; benefits depend on maintenance, suitable crops and sound abstraction limits.
- Challenges: fragmented ministerial responsibilities, slow disbursement/project completion, uneven micro-irrigation adoption, farmer awareness and uncertain rainfall/water supply. Improve coordination, extension, maintenance and local water-user participation.
- The issue claims increased micro-irrigation, restored water bodies and higher productivity without a consistent dated quantitative series; treat these as reported outcomes, not verified numerical achievements.
- Practice answer: introduce irrigation and efficiency goals → explain programme roles → assess productivity, income, water and rainfed-area benefits → constraints → conclude with aquifer-aware local planning.
