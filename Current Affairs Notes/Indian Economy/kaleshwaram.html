Indian Economy

Kaleshwaram lift irrigation and project accountability

Editable notes

River and engineering

  • Telangana's Godavari lift-irrigation scheme draws downstream of the Pranahita–Godavari confluence near Kaleshwaram/Medigadda; it is not a Krishna–Godavari confluence. Pumping lifts low-level water in stages to irrigation/drinking/industrial uses; gravity canals distribute water where levels permit.
  • Three main Godavari barrages: Medigadda (Lakshmi), Annaram (Saraswati), Sundilla (Parvati). The source's “four barrages including Ramadugu” conflates different infrastructure. CAG performance audit, 2024.
  • Source design figures: 1,800+ km canals, 13-district reach; allocations 169 TMC irrigation; 30 Hyderabad drinking;16 industry/miscellaneous;10 village drinking. Designed reach/new ayacut/stabilisation differ from achieved irrigated area; source's 16-lakh/45-lakh-acre references have different scopes and should not be combined.

Feasibility, damage and inquiry

  • Redesigned earlier Pranahita–Chevella proposal from Tummidihatti to Medigadda. Source reports earlier works ~₹11,000 crore, initial project estimate ~₹71,000 crore in 2015, later costs exceeding ₹1 lakh crore; estimates, spending and final costs are distinct.
  • Inquiry controversy concerns water availability, location/design choices, permeable foundations, operating storage, Cabinet approval and financial accountability. Source attributes unilateral decisions to critics/inquiry and records BRS's denial/statutory-clearance defence; allegations are not convictions.
  • Do not memorise the PDF's claim that Sundilla was the barrage whose piers sank: Medigadda suffered the major October 2023 subsidence, while Annaram/Sundilla also faced safety scrutiny. Site-specific investigations, foundation/seepage analysis and operating limits matter; construction on alluvial foundations is not inherently proof of negligence.
  • Lessons: independently test hydrology and lifecycle energy/maintenance costs, scrutinise redesign and sunk costs, ensure collective approvals and transparent procurement, monitor dam safety and verify actual water delivery. Practice B, statement2 only. Krishna and Godavari are distinct river systems; the explanation's supposed confluence in Andhra Pradesh is also misleading.
