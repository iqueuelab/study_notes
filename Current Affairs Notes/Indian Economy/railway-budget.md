# Railways Budget 2025–26: capex, safety and implementation

## Budget and promises
- Railway Budget merged with Union Budget from **2017**. FY2025–26 source capex **~₹2.62 lakh crore**, safety **₹1,16,514 crore**; distinguish total capital investment, gross budget support and actual spending.
- **200 Vande Bharat, 100 Amrit Bharat, 50 Namo Bharat rapid rail and 17,500 non-AC general coaches** planned over **two to three years**; source's “no timeline” omits the announced window. [Railway Ministry, 1 February 2025](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2098714&lang=2&reg=48).
- **100% electrification by end FY2025–26** was a target. Source **95%, ₹13 lakh crore decade investment, ~1.6 billion tonnes freight** are dated estimates; electrification does not alone mean zero-carbon electricity.
## Execution and data traps
- **Kavach** automatic train protection is one safety layer alongside track, signalling, maintenance, staff and operating discipline. **1,465 route-km** is historical coverage, not proof no further installation/upgrade work exists.
- Source “new track 113 →150 km/year” lacks category/period and is implausible as all-India aggregate; do not memorise without the original series. “5,000 underused diesels / ₹30,000 crore” is an unverified utilisation claim.
- DFCs, Mumbai–Ahmedabad high-speed rail and station redevelopment need project-wise progress; conversion of all ICF coaches into Vande Bharat is not an interchangeable technical programme.
- Freight share, speed, reliability, passenger affordability and financial sustainability should be assessed from matching data periods, not a global ranking slogan.
## Mains framework
- **Capex → bottleneck removal → dependable capacity → competitive freight / safe passengers → audited outcomes**.
- Prioritise maintenance/Kavach delivery, general-class capacity, intermodal terminals, realistic procurement and transparent costs; evaluate diesel assets' residual uses and electricity mix before declaring all stranded.
