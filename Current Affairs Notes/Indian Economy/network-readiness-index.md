# Network Readiness Index: digital capacity and India

## NRI 2025 results released February 2026
- **Portulans Institute**, Washington DC, prepared **NRI 2025**, released **4 February 2026**: **127 economies, 53 indicators**, four pillars **Technology, People, Governance, Impact**. India ranked **45 th**, up four places, scoring **54.43/100**, versus **53.63 in 2024**. The source's **43/100** is a typo, not evidence of methodological collapse. [Communications Ministry, 9 February](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2225406&lang=1&reg=1).
- India ranked first in **annual telecom investment, AI scientific publications, ICT services exports and e-commerce legislation**; second in **FTTH/building subscriptions, mobile broadband traffic and international bandwidth**; third in **domestic market scale/income-inequality indicator**. Indicator ranks do not establish universal best AI quality or absence of inequality. India ranked second among lower-middle-income economies.
- Digital readiness combines access, skills, regulation/cybersecurity and social/economic outcomes. Aggregate progress can coexist with rural access, affordability, digital literacy, privacy and cyber-risk gaps; rank movement alone does not prove a particular scheme caused the gain.
- Publisher mapping: **Global Gender Gap Index—WEF (2017 PYQ, a)**; **Global Competitiveness Report—WEF (2019 PYQ, c)**; **Rule of Law Index—World Justice Project (2018 PYQ, d)**. NRI's historical WEF association does not make WEF its current publisher.
