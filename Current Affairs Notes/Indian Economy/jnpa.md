# JNPA, container capacity and port logistics

## Port and unit
- **Jawaharlal Nehru Port Authority**, **Nhava Sheva / Navi Mumbai, Maharashtra**, **west coast**; major container gateway. **TEU = twenty-foot equivalent unit**; a 40-foot container counts as two TEUs.
- **2024 throughput 7.05 million TEUs**, source **~11% growth**; target **10 million throughput by 2027**. Throughput is cargo actually handled; installed capacity is potential annual handling.
- Source capacity **7.6 → 10.4 million TEUs** is an expansion comparison; official **21 January 2025** release already describes **10+ million capacity**. Do not push that completed capacity milestone to 2027. [Ports Ministry](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleaseIframePage.aspx?PRID=2094901&lang=2&reg=48).
- “Largest” and **60% share** require denominator: **major ports** versus **all Indian ports**, and year. Do not assume JNPA exceeds every privately operated container port or handles 60% of all national cargo.
## Expansion and efficiency
- **Bharat Mumbai Container Terminal phase 2**, source **2.4 million TEU addition**; **Nhava Sheva Freeport Terminal upgrade**, nearly **₹2,000 crore projects** source aggregate.
- **Vadhavan**, separate deep-draft port development, not simply another berth inside Nhava Sheva.
- Solar-powered boat, indigenous **70-tonne bollard-pull tugs**, renewable power/electrification; tug rating is pulling force, not cargo capacity.
- Smart systems/automation, better turnaround, rail/road hinterland links and multimodal logistics can improve trade competitiveness and jobs.
- Constraints: project/clearance delays, coastal ecology/community concerns, financing and logistics bottlenecks. More terminal capacity alone does not guarantee lower transshipment dependence or actual cargo demand.
