Himachal Pradesh: natural farming and market support

Editable notes

October 2025: linking training, certification and procurement

  • Himachal Pradesh's Prakritik Kheti Khushhal Kisan Yojana began in 2018. Source snapshot: about 3.06 lakh farmers trained, 2.22 lakh practising partly/fully, and 38,437 ha; these are dated reported measures with different denominators, not proof every trained farmer fully converted.
  • Natural farming avoids synthetic fertilisers/pesticides and uses locally prepared inputs such as Jeevamrit/Beejamrit, mulching, crop rotation and intercropping. Soil-health/biodiversity and lower purchased-input costs are intended benefits; yields, labour and transition results depend on local practice and conditions.
  • Farmer Field Schools/master trainers and CETARA–NF self-assessment certification support uptake. State procurement prices reported: naturally grown wheat ₹60/kg, maize ₹40/kg and raw turmeric ₹90/kg. These state-specific eligible-produce prices are not the all-India MSP for every farmer or all turmeric products. HP Agriculture scheme.
  • Certification credibility, yield-transition risk, farmer awareness, buyer networks and recognised market premiums determine scalability. Self-assessment does not automatically satisfy every export certification rule; higher announced prices need actual procurement/access. BPKP/PKVY are background national links, not proof this State scheme is identical to the national mission.
