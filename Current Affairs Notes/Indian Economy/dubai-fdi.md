# Dubai greenfield FDI and Indian investment

## Read the ranking correctly
- Dubai ranked first in **greenfield project count** for a fourth consecutive year in 2024, and fourth in greenfield capital attracted—not first in every measure of FDI.
- India supplied 21.5% of estimated greenfield FDI capital in the cited ranking; US 13.7%, France 11%, UK 10%, Switzerland 6.9%. [Dubai's official results](https://www.dubaidet.gov.ae/en/newsroom/press-releases/dubai-top-destination-for-greenfield-fdi-for-fourth-year).
- Greenfield investment establishes new operations; brownfield investment uses/acquires existing operations.
- Connectivity between Asia, Europe and Africa, business services, talent mobility and India–UAE CEPA support investment. A favourable tax environment does not mean every business pays zero corporate tax.
- Lawful outward FDI is not automatically panic-driven capital flight. Evaluate market access, diversification and networks alongside domestic investment needs and financial flows.
