# Fenugreek: cultivation, uses and nitrogen fixation

## Crop revision
- **Trigonella foenum-graecum**, family **Fabaceae**; methi/Vendhayam. Seeds are a spice, fresh/dried leaves a vegetable/flavouring; also fodder and traditional herbal/nutraceutical uses.
- Mainly cool-season **rabi** crop: well-drained loam/clay loam, roughly **10–25°C**; typical October–November sowing and March–April harvest vary by location. Rajasthan, Gujarat, Madhya Pradesh, Uttar Pradesh, Maharashtra, Punjab and Tamil Nadu are listed. Varieties include **RMt-305, RMt-1, Hisar Sonali**; the source's **10–12 quintals/ha** is indicative, not assured yield.
- Rhizobium-related root-nodule symbiosis supports nitrogen fixation; performance depends on inoculum, soil and crop conditions. Pests/diseases include aphids, pod borers, root rot and powdery mildew; avoid waterlogging. Research/extension through ICAR/KVKs and relevant horticulture schemes can help, without implying automatic scheme eligibility.
- Contains protein, fibre, minerals and compounds such as **trigonelline, saponins and diosgenin**. Traditional diabetes/lactation/digestive claims are not established treatment recommendations; high-quality clinical evidence is limited. [NIH evidence note](https://content.govdelivery.com/accounts/USNIHNCCIH/bulletins/2fac823?reqfrom=share).
## Nitrogen-fixer PYQ revision
- **Alfalfa, chickpea and clover** form nitrogen-fixing symbioses; amaranth, purslane and spinach are not corresponding nodulated nitrogen-fixing legumes.
- **Frankia** supports actinorhizal plants such as alder, Casuarina and sea buckthorn. **Nostoc/Anabaena** are cyanobacteria, while **Azolla is a fern with a cyanobacterial symbiont**, not itself a cyanobacterium.
