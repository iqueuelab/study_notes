# Renewable energy and grid integration

## Target and January 2025 snapshot
- India's announced 2030 target is **500 GW installed electricity capacity from non-fossil sources**. Non-fossil includes nuclear; capacity is not electricity actually generated.
- Source **November 2024** snapshot: **158.55 GW renewable capacity excluding large hydro**; solar **94.17**, wind **47.95**, small hydro **5.08**, biomass **10.72**, waste-to-energy **0.61 GW**. Components differ slightly from the rounded total.
- Solar subdivisions reported: rooftop **15.16 GW**, off-grid **4.10 GW**. These are dated source figures, not current totals.
## Resources and sites
| Resource | Location / limitation |
| --- | --- |
| Solar | Strong potential in Rajasthan, Gujarat and other high-insolation regions; land and grid access matter |
| Wind | Gujarat, Tamil Nadu, Maharashtra, Andhra Pradesh, Karnataka; potential depends on hub height and methodology |
| Offshore wind | Gujarat and Tamil Nadu coasts; high capital and marine infrastructure needs |
| Hydro | Himalayan and other suitable basins; social/ecological impacts and seasonal flows |
| Geothermal | Ladakh, Himachal Pradesh, Jharkhand examples in source |
| Ocean energy | Gulf of Kutch and Sundarbans cited for tidal prospects; wave/tidal technologies remain distinct |
- Source indicative potentials: solar **748 GW**, wind **over 300 GW**, hydro **148 GW**, geothermal **10 GW**, ocean **40 GW**; assumptions/dates differ. Do not compare them as equally deployable capacity.
- Source '46 GW hydro remains untapped' conflicts with its potential/installed-capacity discussion; do not memorise that classification without the original assessment.
## Deployment barriers → response
- Land fragmentation/community conflict → transparent land records, consent, compensation, agrivoltaics and other dual use.
- High upfront costs/uncertain revenues → predictable tariffs/contracts, affordable finance and research.
- Variable output/grid congestion/curtailment → stronger transmission, forecasting, flexible demand, storage and wind-solar hybrids.
- Saturation of good wind sites → suitable repowering/site assessment; offshore pilots where viable.
- Clearance/policy uncertainty → coordinated predictable rules without bypassing environmental assessment.
- Component waste/skills gaps → recycling, training and community engagement.
## Schemes and grid
- **PM-KUSUM**: decentralised solar generation, stand-alone pumps and solarisation of grid-connected pumps; farmer energy/income support.
- Solar-PV **PLI**, Solar Parks, National Bioenergy Programme and foreign investment support production/deployment.
- **Green Energy Corridor**: transmission links from renewable-rich regions to demand centres, plus grid integration. Storage and smart grids complement transmission; they are not substitutes for it.
- **Pradhan Mantri Suryodaya** concerned **rooftop solar**, not the PDF's claimed large-scale solar parks. **PM-Surya Ghar** was approved on **29 February 2024**, with **₹75,021 crore** and a one-crore-household aim. [Cabinet decision](https://www.pib.gov.in/Pressreleaseshare.aspx?PRID=2010133&lang=2&reg=3).
- National Green Hydrogen Mission's 2030 expectations in the graphic: **5 MMT/year hydrogen**, about **125 GW associated renewable addition**, **₹8 lakh crore investment**, **6 lakh jobs**, **50 MMT/year CO₂ avoided**. These are mission projections, not achieved outcomes.
## Mains value
- Cleaner electricity can reduce import dependence, create jobs and improve access; benefits require reliable grids and affordable supply.
- Carbon-intensity reduction, non-fossil capacity share and renewable generation share are different indicators.
## Green hydrogen and Pudimadaka hub
- Renewable electricity → **water electrolysis** → hydrogen + oxygen; hydrogen may be stored/transported or converted into derivatives; a **fuel cell** converts hydrogen/oxygen into electricity and water. Electricity origin and lifecycle emissions distinguish green production from fossil-based hydrogen.
- **January 2025 foundation-stone stage**, **Pudimadaka near Visakhapatnam, Andhra Pradesh**, **NTPC Green Energy Ltd**; first green-hydrogen hub under **National Green Hydrogen Mission**, planned **₹1.85 lakh crore** investment.
- Source focuses on **green methanol, green urea and sustainable aviation fuel**, exports and energy transition; construction announcements are not achieved output. [PMO project release](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleaseIframePage.aspx?PRID=2090664&lang=2&reg=48).

## GEAPP, ISA and grid digitalisation: January 2025
- **Global Energy Alliance for People and Planet**: philanthropic, government, technology, policy and financing partners; clean-energy access, growth and climate goals.
- Source ambitions: avoid **4 gigatonnes future carbon emissions**, energy access for **1 billion people**, enable **150 million jobs**; targets, not achieved totals.
- **ISA Multi-Donor Trust Fund**, mobilisation goal **$100 million** for solar projects; GEAPP project-management support includes governance, fundraising, financing and implementation.
- **DUET = Digitalization of Utilities for Energy Transition**: digital grid-asset inventory with coordinates/specifications + live smart sensors → load-flow analysis and voltage/current/phase tracking → reduced transmission/distribution losses.
- **ENTICE = Energy Transitions Innovation Challenge**, first launched **2023**; **ENTICE 2.0** planned January 2025, scaling investable solutions through funding, markets and mentorship.
- Distinguish philanthropic partnership/trust fund from a treaty-created global climate-finance mechanism; neither pledges nor digitised assets alone prove delivered energy access.
