# Cowpea: crop characteristics and nitrogen fixation

## Prelims recall
- **Vigna unguiculata**, family **Fabaceae/Leguminosae**; drought-tolerant legume of tropical/subtropical regions.
- Names: **lobia** (Hindi), **thattapayaru** (Tamil); bush and climbing forms.
- Seeds vary in colour (white, cream, brown, black); source reports **20–25% protein** for the cited seed context, with fibre, minerals and low fat. Composition depends on variety and basis.
- Root-associated symbiotic nitrogen fixation supports soil fertility; useful for intercropping and food security in water-stressed settings.
- **CROPS space germination** uses cowpea; experiment details are consolidated in the space-sector topic rather than repeated here.
