# ACC PLI: domestic battery cells and implementation

## Scheme and January 2026 implementation review
- **Advanced Chemistry Cells** store energy electrochemically/chemically and return it as electricity; used in EVs and stationary storage. Cell manufacture differs from assembling imported cells into battery packs. Higher energy density/life/charging performance depends on chemistry/design.
- **Ministry of Heavy Industries**, Cabinet approval **12 May 2021**, tender launched **22 October 2021**; **₹18,100 crore** programme for **50 GWh ACC** plus **5 GWh niche ACC**. Sales-linked support follows installation/eligibility; approval, installed capacity and actual incentive disbursement are different milestones.
- **Investment ₹225 crore/GWh**, **25% domestic value addition within two years, 60% within five**. The initial minimum **5-GWh bid implies ₹1,125 crore**, not a universal fixed ₹1,100-crore investment rule. [Cabinet scheme terms](https://www.pib.gov.in/Pressreleaseshare.aspx?PRID=1717938&lang=2&reg=48).
- **IEEFA–JMK January report, October 2025 snapshot:** **1.4 GWh commissioned on time (2.8% of 50 GWh)**; **8.6 GWh delayed development**, **20 GWh no development**, second-round **10 GWh development on time**, **10 GWh unallocated**. Beneficiaries: Ola, Reliance New Energy and Rajesh Exports after Hyundai Global withdrew; award totals differ from completed factories.
- Report finds **zero disbursed incentives against ₹2,900-crore target by October 2025**, with installed capacity yet to meet DVA eligibility. Visa delays for Chinese specialists, cell/component/refining gaps, tight timelines and high localisation hurdles constrain commissioning. Proposals include realistic phasing, mineral/component support, technology partnerships and skills/R&D; these are recommendations. [Original report](https://ieefa.org/sites/default/files/2026-01/Assessing%20India%27s%20ACC%20PLI%20scheme.pdf).
- The compilation's “50 GWh by 2026” is an oversimplified common deadline: first-round gestation ran **January 2023–December 2024**, with performance incentives thereafter; later awards have their own schedules. Report's subsidy table caps support at **20% of sale price /₹2,000 per kWh**, subject to bid, DVA and actual sales, not an unconditional consumer subsidy.
