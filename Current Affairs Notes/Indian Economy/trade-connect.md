# Trade Connect ePlatform and MSME export access

## Platform and July 2025 outreach
- **DGFT, Ministry of Commerce and Industry**, provides a one-stop trade-information/service portal for exporters and MSMEs, linking Indian Missions, Export Promotion Councils, Commodity Boards and government agencies.
- July outreach at the **71st India International Garment Fair** and **16th Toy Biz International B 2 B Expo** aimed to improve adoption. The source describes collaboration with MSME Ministry, EXIM Bank, DFS and MEA.
- Tools cover tariffs, certification, FTAs, buyers, trade events and eCommerce; multilingual interactive courses explain export procedures. Electronic preferential/non-preferential **Certificates of Origin** can be issued/validated through authorised agencies.
- Lower information/intermediary costs and better overseas coordination can widen MSME participation; adoption still depends on digital access and usable support. [DGFT July 17 announcement](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2145471&lang=2&reg=48).
## MSME graphic
- Revised investment/turnover ceilings (₹ crore): **micro 2.5/10; small 25/100; medium 125/500**, against earlier **1/5; 10/50; 50/250**. Both criteria matter; these are classification limits, not grants.
- Image flags credit gaps, delayed payments, low digital access and compliance burdens; links include Udyam, ECLGS, RAMP, ODOP and TReDS. Its undated GDP/export/employment/₹20-lakh-crore credit-gap figures should not be treated as a harmonised July 2025 statistical series.
- Revised classification took effect **1 April 2025**; [official MSME comparison table](https://www.msmedikarnal.gov.in/pdf/msme_defination.pdf).
