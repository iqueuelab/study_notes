# Economic Survey 2024–25: indicators and policy signals

## Purpose

- Annual economic assessment prepared by **Department of Economic Affairs, Finance Ministry**, led by **Chief Economic Adviser**; usually presented before Union Budget. Policy recommendations are advisory, not spending authorisation.
- First Survey **1950–51**, separated from Budget **1964**. Chapter structure/statistical appendix can vary; “Part A/Part B” is not a permanent constitutional rule.

## Read each indicator with its period

| Indicator | Survey snapshot |
| --- | --- |
| Real GDP | **6.4% FY2024–25 first advance estimate**; **6.3–6.8% FY2025–26 projection** |
| Retail inflation | **5.4% FY2023–24 → 4.9% April–December 2024** |
| Bank gross NPAs | **2.6%, September 2024** |
| Gross FDI inflows | **US$55.6 billion, April–November 2024**, +17.9%; not net FDI or full-year total |
| Total exports | Merchandise + services **+6%, April–December 2024** |
| Services share of GVA | **55.3% FY2024–25 estimate**, versus 50.6% FY2013–14 |

- Infrastructure Union capital expenditure **38.8% trend growth rate, FY2020–FY2024**; not a 38.8% cumulative increase. [Survey overview](https://www.indiabudget.gov.in/budget2025-26/economicsurvey/doc/eschapter/echap01.pdf), [external-sector chapter](https://www.indiabudget.gov.in/budget2025-26/economicsurvey/doc/eschapter/echap03.pdf).
- Source industry **6.2% FY2024–25 estimated growth**; electronics **17.5% CAGR FY2015–FY2024**; steel **4.6%**, automobile **12.5% FY2023–24**, and agriculture **~16% GDP FY2023–24** are period-specific figures. GDP share ≠ GVA share; forecasts ≠ eventual outcomes.
- Insurance source **₹11.2 lakh crore / 7.7%** has insufficient segment/period clarity; do not treat it as a verified common total.

## Mains connections

- Private investment and infrastructure → productivity; skilling and employment → inclusive demand; prudent fiscal/financial policy → resilience.
- Disinflation helps purchasing power but aggregate growth need not imply equal household gains. Assess jobs, regional distribution, social outcomes and environmental costs alongside GDP.
