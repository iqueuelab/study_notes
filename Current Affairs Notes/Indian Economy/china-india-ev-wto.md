# China–India WTO dispute over EV and battery incentives

## October 2025: consultation stage, DS642
- China requested WTO consultations on **15 October**, circulated **20 October 2025**, over India's **PLI ACC battery storage**, **PLI automobile/auto components**, and **Scheme to Promote Manufacturing of Electric Passenger Cars in India**. Allegations concern domestic-input conditions/discrimination under **SCM, TRIMs and GATT 1994**; this is a complaint, not a finding of violation. [WTO announcement](https://www.wto.org/english/news_e/news25_e/ds642rfc_20oct25_177_e.htm).
- Source scheme outlays: **ACC ₹18,100 crore; auto ₹25,938 crore**. Industrial goals include technology, manufacturing scale, jobs, clean transport and lower import dependence. Eligibility for foreign-owned firms manufacturing in India does not itself answer whether conditions discriminate against imported inputs.
- The source's “WTO non-actionable subsidies for R&D, regions and environment” defence is outdated: **SCM Article 8 expired at end-1999**. Developmental or green purpose and similar policies elsewhere do not automatically establish WTO compatibility. [WTO explanation](https://www.wto.org/english/tratop_e/envir_e/issu3_e.htm).
- Consultations seek an agreed settlement; after **60 days** without resolution, a panel may be requested. The Appellate Body's dysfunction complicates appeals; do not present a completed panel ruling or guaranteed timetable in October.
- Policy design must balance domestic capability and public support with nondiscrimination commitments, transparent conditions and fiscal value. The PDF's reduced EV-duty illustration is simplified; it must not be read as an unconditional 15–20% tariff available to every investor.
