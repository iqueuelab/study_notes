# Phosphoric acid: fertilisers and battery supply chains

## May 2025: proposed plant
- Embedded clipping: **Caitlyn India Private Ltd**, not Cairn, proposes **₹400 crore,50,000 tonnes/year integrated phosphoric-acid plant** in a port-accessible southern Indian industrial zone. Site under evaluation; **30–50 acres**, proposed **FY 2027 commissioning**, hemihydrate–dihydrate process and captive sulphuric-acid unit; initially fertiliser supply, possible future own NPK facility. Rock imports envisaged from **Morocco, Jordan, Egypt**. This is an announced investment/commissioning plan, not an operating plant or achieved output.
## Chemistry and uses
- **H₃PO₄**, colourless inorganic acid, non-combustible but corrosive/irritating at relevant concentration. Phosphorus **atomic number 15**; allotropes have different structures, so inset's single body-centred-cubic descriptor should not be generalised to all elemental phosphorus.
- Usually produced from phosphate rock by wet acid treatment; purification/thermal routes yield different grades. Plant phosphorus is essential for roots, energy transfer and reproduction.
- Uses: phosphate fertilisers (including downstream DAP/NPK), feed supplements, phosphate/polyphosphate industrial chemicals, detergents/cleaning/metal-treatment and food-grade acidulant/dental applications. Industrial acid is not automatically food/battery grade.
- **LFP—lithium iron phosphate (LiFePO₄)** cathodes need phosphorus feedstock; battery-grade purification demand may compete for supply, but acid is not itself the entire battery/cathode and not every EV uses LFP.
## Resources and policy
- Major phosphate resources are **sedimentary marine phosphorites**, with significant **igneous deposits** too; source's all-organic-seafloor definition is incomplete. **Morocco** holds the largest reported reserves; reserve, resource and annual production ranks differ. [USGS phosphate summary](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2023/mcs2023-phosphate.pdf).
- Source cites Indian production mainly **Rajasthan/Madhya Pradesh**, limited supply and acid imports from **Jordan, Morocco, Senegal, Tunisia**; exact shares require year/product definitions.
- Manage fertiliser affordability/food security alongside battery supply diversification, domestic processing/purification, resource efficiency/recycling and safe handling. Growing battery demand creates a potential allocation/price pressure, not evidence an actual fertiliser shortage caused by this proposed plant.
