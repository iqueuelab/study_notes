# Alternative investment funds and venture-capital migration

## Framework
- **SEBI (AIF) Regulations 2012**, privately pooled funds with a defined investment policy. **CategoryI:** venture capital, SME, infrastructure/socially beneficial funds; **II:** private equity/debt and other qualifying funds, generally no leverage except permitted operational purposes; **III:** complex/diverse strategies, potentially leverage/derivatives. **Category**, not “Type 1”, is the formal classification.
- AIFs may hold stocks/bonds; those securities are **not themselves AIFs**. Mutual funds and specified trusts/vehicles have separate frameworks. Industry body **IVCA** advocates for private-capital investors; it is not the regulator.
## Legacy VCF migration:May 2025 report
- **19 August 2024 SEBI circular** sets migration modalities for VCFs under **1996 rules** into the 2012 framework as **Migrated Venture Capital Funds**, a CategoryI sub-category. It is an **option under specified conditions**, not proof every existing fund was legally forced to migrate. [SEBI circular](https://www.sebi.gov.in/legal/circulars/aug-2024/modalities-for-migration-of-venture-capital-funds-registered-under-erstwhile-sebi-venture-capital-funds-regulations-1996-to-sebi-alternative-investment-funds-regulations-2012_85914.html).
- Clipping reports **19 July 2025** deadline, simplified application/fee relief and low uptake; IVCA urges timely action to avoid last-minute liquidation/regularisation problems. Deadline is the then-reported window, not a completed migration count; benefits/requirements depend on applicable circular and scheme status.
