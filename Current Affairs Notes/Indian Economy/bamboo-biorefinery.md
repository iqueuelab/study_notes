# Bamboo cultivation and the Numaligarh biorefinery

## Numaligarh inauguration: 14 September 2025
- Prime Minister inaugurated the bamboo-based **Assam Bioethanol Plant, Numaligarh, Golaghat**, and **laid the foundation stone** for the polypropylene unit. The source's roughly **₹5,000 crore bioethanol /₹7,230 crore polypropylene** refer to different projects; a foundation stone is not commissioning. [PMO event account](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2166529&lang=2&reg=3).
- **ABEPL**, involving Numaligarh Refinery and Finnish partners Fortum/Chempolis, uses bamboo lignocellulose for **second-generation ethanol**, with acetic acid/furfural co-products. Source design figures: **48,900 tonnes ethanol, 11,000 tonnes acetic acid, 19,000 tonnes furfural yearly**; capacities are not achieved annual production.
- Feedstock figures need moisture basis: the PDF cites **5 lakh tonnes green bamboo**; technology provider reports **300,000 tonnes bamboo/year** and about **49,000 tonnes ethanol**. Do not treat dry/processed and green feedstock quantities as interchangeable. [Chempolis project account](https://chempolis.com/indias-first-bamboo-based-biorefinery-has-been-inaugurated-by-prime-minister-modi/).
- Bamboo is botanically a **grass**. The **2017 forest-law change** removed felling/transit restrictions for bamboo grown in **non-forest areas**; it did not freely deregulate forest bamboo. National Bamboo Mission links nurseries/plantations, processing and markets; Northeast supply chains connect farmer incomes to industrial demand. [2017 official clarification](https://www.pib.gov.in/newsite/erelcontent.aspx?lang=2&reg=48&relid=173782).
- Genera include **Bambusa, Dendrocalamus, Ochlandra**; uses include construction, paper, furniture, handicrafts, agarbatti and biofuel. Sustainable harvesting, aggregation, remote transport, processing, viable prices and farmer contracts determine benefits.
- The source's bamboo area/stock/species/export totals mix unspecified or older years; **13.96 million ha /170 Mt** must not be labelled a matched 2025 inventory. Its **12% current ethanol blending** is stale, and **12 tonnes CO₂/ha/year** is not a universal sequestration rate. Evaluate lifecycle energy/emissions, feedstock regeneration and waste streams; “zero-waste” is a project claim, not proof of zero environmental impact.
