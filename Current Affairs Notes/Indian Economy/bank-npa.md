# Non-performing assets (NPAs)

## Definition and ratios
- Broadly, a bank loan is NPA when interest/principal is overdue **more than 90 days**; RBI rules contain category-specific criteria, including crop-season treatment for eligible agricultural advances.
- **Gross NPA**: all assets classified as non-performing before relevant deductions; **GNPA ratio** relates gross NPAs to gross advances.
- December 2024 Financial Stability Report: source reports scheduled-commercial-bank GNPA ratio **2.6%, a 12-year low**. Keep this historical observation separate from later reports/projections.
## Interpretation
- Lower ratio may reflect recoveries, lower fresh slippages, write-offs and loan growth. A write-off is not the same as recovery or automatic debt forgiveness.
- Mains: underwriting/monitoring, resolution/recovery, capital adequacy and macroeconomic risks; don't apply the 90-day shorthand without recognising special RBI classifications.

## March2025 comparison
- JuneFSR's **SCB GNPA2.3% inMarch2025** follows the December2024 report's2.6% earlier observation; dates must remain separate.
- A lower aggregate ratio can reflect write-offs, recoveries and denominator growth as well as careful new lending. Neither the aggregate ratio nor positive stress tests prove every bank or borrower is sound.
