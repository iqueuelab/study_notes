# Mango cultivation, varieties and exports

## Crop and regional varieties
- **Mangifera indica**, Anacardiaceae; India's national fruit, associated with the Indo–Southeast Asian region. Tropical/subtropical crop suited to well-drained alluvial, lateritic/loamy soils; source pH range **5.5–7.5** is indicative, not a rigid universal requirement.
- India is the largest producer. Major producing States include **UP, Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, Bihar, Karnataka**; source's exact 2022–23 ranking/shares (22%,15%,9%,7%,6%) and ~2.3 million ha/~21 million tonnes/~9 t/ha lack a clearly matched official table. Treat them as source estimates pending dataset verification, not current figures.
- Varieties: **Alphonso—Konkan/Maharashtra; Dasheri—UP/Malihabad; Langra—north India including UP/Bihar; Banganapalli—AP; Gir Kesar—Gujarat; Totapuri—south India**. Other graphic examples: Chausa, Himsagar, Safeda, Mankurad, Mallika, Neelum, Amrapali and regional types. Langra is not exclusive to Bihar.
- GI examples: **Alphonso, Malihabadi Dussehri, Banganapalle, Gir Kesar**; GI identifies geographic reputation/origin rather than every cultivar grown elsewhere being GI-certified.
## Value chain
- Fresh fruit and processed pulp/juice/pickles; destinations listed include **UAE, Bangladesh, UK, Saudi Arabia, Nepal and USA**. APEDA supports export promotion/traceability. Alphonso is prominent, but “top exported variety” is not established without period/product data. [APEDA mango overview](https://apeda.gov.in/Mango).
- **MIDH, RKVY and National Horticulture Board** support horticulture development; cold chains, storage, grading, processing and sanitary/phytosanitary compliance reduce losses and support exports. Genome research can assist breeding; source's unqualified “ICAR sequenced mango genome” needs an identified study before attribution.
- Mango supplies vitamins A/C and other nutrients; grafting preserves varietal traits, exemplified by Malihabad orchards. Cultural/ritual significance and references in older texts supplement economic importance. Source calls **22 July National Mango Day**; this is a popular observance, not proof of a statutory national holiday.
