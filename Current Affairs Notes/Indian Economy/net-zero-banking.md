# Net-zero banking and financed emissions

## January 2025 framework

- **Net-Zero Banking Alliance (NZBA)**: bank-led, **UNEP Finance Initiative-convened**, launched **2021**; voluntary alignment of lending/investment/capital-market portfolios with **net-zero by 2050 or sooner**, Paris goals.
- Targets for **2030**, intermediate milestones; priority to emissions-intensive energy, transport, agriculture and other sectors; annual **absolute emissions and intensity** reporting.
- CEO-signed commitment, member-bank Steering Group and UNEP FI secretariat support; source describes intermediary sector targets within **36 months**. Historical framework requirements changed later.
- Source January 2025 context: major US banks withdrawing; **no Indian bank member** reported at that time. Do not present this as current membership.
- **Financed emissions ≠ only office/branch emissions**; portfolio reductions should support real-economy transition. Offsets require credible quality and disclosure, not substitution for every reduction.

## Mains evaluation and later status

- Benefits: comparable targets, accountability and transition finance; limitations: voluntary enforcement, exits, inconsistent scope, greenwashing and undue offset reliance.
- **August 2025**: activities paused while members voted on transition to a framework. Today's official page presents **Net-Zero Banking Resources**, not a current member roster; the January alliance description is historical. [UNEP FI August update](https://www.unepfi.org/net-zero-banking/media/nzba-update-august-2025/), [resources](https://www.unepfi.org/net-zero-banking/).
