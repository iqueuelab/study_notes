# World Gold Council and official gold reserves

## Institution and functions

- **World Gold Council (WGC)**: gold-industry organisation, **1987**, headquartered **London**; represents major gold producers, promotes sustainable demand and use. It does **not regulate global gold prices or manage countries' official reserves**.
- Market development/standards; research (including **Gold Demand Trends**, quarterly reports); policy advocacy, new technological uses and investment-product development, including gold ETFs.
- Source January 2025 snapshot: **33 members**, markets representing about **75% of annual gold consumption**; membership/market coverage are dated figures.

## November 2024 reserve purchases

- Reported global central-bank **net purchases: 53 tonnes; RBI: 8 tonnes**. Monthly additions differ from total reserves and unreported purchases. [WGC January 2025 report](https://www.gold.org/goldhub/gold-focus/2025/01/central-bank-gold-statistics-november-2024).
- Embedded reserve ranking: **USA → Germany → Italy → France → Russia → China → Switzerland → India → Japan → Netherlands**. Graphic puts India at **846.76 tonnes**; undated snapshot, not a current ranking/stock.
- Mains use: reserve diversification and exposure to uncertainty; distinguish physical gold, gold ETFs, reserve holdings and annual demand.
