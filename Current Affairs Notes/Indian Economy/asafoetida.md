# Asafoetida cultivation and import substitution

## Plant and cultivation milestones
- **Heeng/asafoetida**, oleo-gum-resin obtained by incising **Ferula** taproots/rhizomes; source **F. assa-foetida**, pungent culinary spice sold in lumps/powder. A herbaceous plant, not the infographic's “tree”. Traditional medical use does not validate graphic claims to cure epilepsy, viruses or depression.
- Cold/arid Iran/Afghanistan/Central Asian origins; well-drained sandy soils, low rainfall and suitable cool climate matter. Source **<300 mm,10–20°C,−4 to 40°C tolerance** are indicative, not guarantees every stage survives extremes. Long juvenile period (~five years) and seed dormancy/low germination obstruct establishment.
- **CSIR-IHBT, Palampur**, with **ICAR-NBPGR** legal seed import/quarantine; research/import work **2018–20**, first farm planting **15 October 2020, Kwaring, Lahaul, Himachal Pradesh**. Source further trial locations include Lahaul-Spiti (Keylong/Salgran/Madgran), Mandi (Janjheli/Karsog), Kinnaur (Kalpa/Reckong Peo), Kullu (Banjar/Sainj) and Chamba (Bharmour/Pangi).
- **May 2025 first flowering reported at IHBT Palampur (~1,300 m)**; source attributes flowering/seed-set milestone to **28 May**, but exact-date and viable-seed claims need institute records. Completion of flowering supports adaptability; does not establish nationwide commercial resin output or end to import dependence. [CSIR institutional account](https://csirnews.niscair.res.in/home/article/845).
- Source **2022 germplasm resource centre**, tissue-culture propagation/niche mapping, seed supply/training support expansion; preserve conservation, planting-material quality and crop economics. Potential farmer income/import substitution are prospective benefits requiring mature harvests and viable markets.
- Undated UPPSC/HSSC questions are held separately: root extraction, and first cultivation in **Himachal Pradesh cold desert**. Do not invent exam years.
