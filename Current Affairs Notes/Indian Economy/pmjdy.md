# Pradhan Mantri Jan Dhan Yojana and financial inclusion

## Eleven years of PMJDY: August 2025
- Launched **28 August 2014**, the National Mission for Financial Inclusion links basic bank accounts, savings, remittances, credit, insurance and pensions; Jan Dhan–Aadhaar–Mobile supports DBT. Account access is a foundation, not proof of regular use or poverty elimination.
- Government's mid-August 2025 snapshot: **56.16 crore accounts, ₹2.67 lakh crore deposits**; issue rounds these to 56.2 crore/₹2.68 lakh crore and reports about **56% women, 37.5 crore rural/semi-urban accounts and 38.7 crore RuPay cards**. Do not combine differing reporting dates into a precise single-day total. [PIB anniversary overview](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressNoteDetails.aspx?ModuleId=3&NoteId=155102&lang=2&reg=3).
- Banking correspondents extend last-mile access; DBT enabled rapid COVID relief and reduced intermediaries. Insurance, pensions, micro-savings and suitable credit support informal workers, women and rural households.
- **Correction:** PMSBY provides accident insurance; PMJJBY is life insurance, not accident cover. PMJDY RuPay accident cover of ₹2 lakh applies to eligible accounts/cards under applicable conditions, not an unconditional payment to every account.
- Source's account-ownership percentages from Findex and NSS use different populations/surveys; do not treat households, adults and accounts as interchangeable denominators.
## Turning access into use
- Dormancy, low financial literacy, fraud, connectivity and grievance barriers limit inclusion. Strengthen correspondent viability, digital safety, local-language support, accessible complaints and appropriate micro-credit/insurance/pensions.
- **2015 Prelim:** purpose is financial inclusion (c). **2015 Mains:** access → DBT/security/women's agency → usage, literacy and infrastructure limitations → sustained inclusion.
