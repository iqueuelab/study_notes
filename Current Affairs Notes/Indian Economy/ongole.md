# Ongole–Nellore cattle and livestock genetics

## Breed and record correction
- **Ongole**, indigenous zebu cattle from **Andhra Pradesh**; Brazilian **Nellore/Nelore** stocks derive from Indian cattle. Heat tolerance, hardiness and muscular build make them important in tropical beef production; not immunity to all diseases.
- **Viatina-19 FIV Mara:** elite Brazilian Nelore breeding cow; source weight **1,101 kg**, embryo/genetic value and show awards explain exceptional valuation, not ordinary cattle prices.
- Guinness record: **21 June 2023**, one-third ownership auctioned; implied whole-animal value **R$21 million / about US$4.38 million**. Thus February 2025 “₹40 crore sale” is a rounded reported valuation, **not proof a new whole cow sale occurred then**. [Guinness record](https://www.guinnessworldrecords.com/news/2023/8/worlds-most-expensive-cow-sold-for-an-astronomical-price-in-brazil-756554).
## India implications
- Conserve indigenous genetic resources, use scientifically evaluated breeding/embryo technologies and maintain disease surveillance, animal welfare and traceability.
- Productivity, feed, climate adaptation and farmer access matter alongside genetics; export opportunity is conditional on veterinary/biosecurity requirements. Beef-breed value does not automatically establish superior dairy yield for every Indian system.
