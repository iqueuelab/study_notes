# Jackfruit and horticultural value addition

## Species and uses
- **Artocarpus heterophyllus, Moraceae**, associated with the Western Ghats; tropical humid conditions and well-drained soils support cultivation.
- Kerala designated jackfruit its State fruit in **2018**. Important producing areas include southern India and the North-East.
- Unripe fruit used as vegetable/texture-based meat substitute; ripe flesh eaten fresh/processed; seeds used cooked or as flour.
- Provides carbohydrate, fibre and micronutrients. **Texture-based meat substitution is not nutritional equivalence to protein-rich foods**; medicinal/degenerative-disease claims need clinical evidence.
- Perishability, bulky fruit, weak aggregation and market access constrain incomes. Processing into chips, flour, preserves and suitable plant-based products reduces losses.
- ODOP/horticulture support and startups can help where actually designated/eligible; no automatic subsidy for every grower. Quality, storage, farmer organisation and buyer links determine value addition.
