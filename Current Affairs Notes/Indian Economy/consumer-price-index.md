# Consumer Price Index and inflation measurement

## What CPI measures
- CPI tracks retail price changes in a representative, weighted household consumption basket of **goods and services**. It helps assess inflation, purchasing power and living costs; a population average need not match every household's expenses.
- For this **April 2025** coverage, NSO/MoSPI publishes monthly **CPI-Rural, CPI-Urban and CPI-Combined**, base **2012=100**; weights derive from the NSS 68th-round **2011–12** consumption survey. [MoSPI CPI portal](https://www.cpi.mospi.gov.in/Default1.aspx).
- Fixed-basket/Laspeyres intuition: **CPI = current cost of base-period basket ÷ base-period cost × 100**. Official compilation aggregates weighted price relatives; inflation is the percentage change in the index, not the index itself.
## Series and uses
| Series | Compiler / principal use |
| --- | --- |
| CPI Rural, Urban, Combined | NSO; household inflation; Combined is monetary-policy benchmark |
| CPI-IW | Labour Bureau; industrial workers; wage and DA indexation |
| CPI-AL | Labour Bureau; agricultural labourers; minimum-wage/cost-of-living analysis |
| CPI-RL | Labour Bureau; rural labourers including agricultural and other rural occupations |
| CPI-UNME | Historical CSO series; discontinued, with linked indices used temporarily pending broad CPI-Urban |
- DA for government employees/pensioners generally uses **CPI-IW**, rather than mechanically applying CPI-Combined. Appropriate series also inform wage negotiations and contractual indexation; price data support socioeconomic and poverty analysis. [MoSPI UNME discontinuation decision](https://www.mospi.gov.in/sites/default/files/press_release/t4_20jan11.pdf).

## Monetary policy and PYQ logic
- RBI's flexible inflation-targeting agreement dates to **February 2015**; the **2016 RBI Act amendment** gave statutory backing. Target: headline all-India CPI inflation, **4% with ±2 percentage-point tolerance** for the period relevant to these notes. Earlier policy monitored multiple indicators, so avoid treating the transition as a simple statutory WPI-to-CPI substitution. [RBI framework](https://rbi.org.in/scripts/PublicationsView.aspx?Id=18086).
- **2020 Prelims: answer (a), 1 and 2 only.** CPI has greater food weight and includes services; WPI measures goods and is not RBI's inflation-target benchmark.
- For the WPI **2011–12 series**, **24.38%** is the broader **Food Index** (food articles plus manufactured food products), not food articles alone. Do not confuse this with CPI's food-and-beverages group. [Office of Economic Adviser release](https://eaindustry.nic.in/pdf_files/cmonthly.pdf).
