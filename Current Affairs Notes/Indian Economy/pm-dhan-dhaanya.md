# PM Dhan-Dhaanya Krishi Yojana

## Cabinet approval: 16 July 2025
- Six-year programme from **2025–26**, covering **100 districts** selected for low productivity, low cropping intensity and low credit disbursement; states' shares reflect net cropped area and operational holdings, with at least one district per state.
- Converges **36 existing schemes across 11 departments**, state schemes and local private partnerships. Convergence does not itself mean repeal or legal abolition of PM-KISAN/PMFBY. Budget announcement envisaged about **1.7 crore farmers**; July briefing reported **₹24,000 crore annually**, not wholly new expenditure additional to every converged scheme.
- Raise productivity, diversify crops/sustainable practices, improve irrigation, panchayat/block storage and short-/long-term credit. Inspired by Aspirational Districts Programme.
- District Agriculture and Allied Activities Plans, prepared by district samitis including progressive farmers; district/state/national committees, NITI guidance and central nodal officers. **117 indicators**, monthly dashboard monitoring. [Cabinet decision](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleseDetailm.aspx?PRID=2145146&lang=2&reg=3).

## Delivery and public-investment assessment
- Convergence must complement adequate public spending, irrigation/research/storage investment and locally tailored district plans. A common umbrella can reduce duplication but may obscure declining resources or create uniform targets unsuited to local agro-climatic conditions.
- Include states, Panchayats, primary agricultural cooperatives, universities, FPOs and farmers; assess private partnerships against food security, transparency and smallholder benefits.
- PDF gives agriculture allocation shares declining **3.53% (2021–22) → 3.14% → 2.57% → 2.54% → 2.51% (2025–26)**; denominator is insufficiently explained and “Central Plan outlay” is dated terminology. Do not equate an allocation share with measured real public investment or spending delivered.
- Source's October/Rabi launch date is a July implementation expectation; six-year approval begins FY2025–26. Coaching questions are practice, not past UPSC questions.

## October 2025: launch and convergence safeguards
- PM launched PM Dhan-Dhaanya Krishi Yojana and the pulses self-reliance mission on **11 October 2025**. The reported combined ₹35,440 crore comprises ₹24,000 crore PMDDKY and ₹11,440 crore pulses mission; do not assign the full sum to PMDDKY alone or conflate an annual/convergence allocation with the other mission's multi-year outlay. [PM launch announcement](https://www.pmindia.gov.in/en/news_updates/pm-to-participate-in-a-special-krishi-programme-at-indian-agricultural-research-institute-in-new-delhi-on-11-october/).
- Existing July notes retain six years from 2025–26, 100 districts, expected 1.7 crore farmers, and convergence of 36 schemes across 11 departments. Low productivity, cropping intensity and agricultural credit are selection criteria; the PDF's universal <155% threshold, water-stress requirement and restriction of every beneficiary to <2 ha are not established by the Cabinet framework.
- The PDF's tables of 50–80% subsidies, fixed KCC loan ranges, 500 overseas trainees, 10,000 women groups, mandatory app, doubled income by 2030 and yield/loss/water targets are unsupported as universal PMDDKY entitlements or notified national targets. Retain core productivity, diversification, storage, irrigation and credit objectives; evaluate district-specific plans through accountable convergence, farmer participation and monitoring.
