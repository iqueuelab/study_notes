Financial Stability and Development Council

Editable notes

Structure and mandate

  • Non-statutory council established December2010, chaired by Union Finance Minister; not a NITI Aayog organ. Coordinates regulators and financial-sector development without replacing their statutory powers.
  • Members include RBI Governor, heads of SEBI,IRDAI,PFRDA, finance/economic-affairs/financial-services leadership and CEA; 2018 expansion added Minister of State responsible for DEA, Revenue/MeitY secretaries and IBBI chair. The PDF's abbreviated 'after2018' list is not exhaustive; FMC merged into SEBI in2015 and is not a separate current regulator.
  • Functions: financial stability, macroprudential supervision of the whole economy/system, inter-regulatory coordination, sector development, financial inclusion/literacy, and addressing gaps/risks from fintech and interconnected markets. Macroprudential concerns system-wide spillovers, distinct from supervision of a single institution.
  • RBI Governor chairs its Sub-Committee; RBI's FSR reports that collective risk assessment. International shock transmission after2008 explains the need for coordination; a council's existence does not guarantee crisis prevention.
  • 2016PYQ: statements2 and3 correct; chair is Finance Minister and mandate includes macroprudential supervision. The complete official question is in the shared bank: (c), 2 and 3 only.
