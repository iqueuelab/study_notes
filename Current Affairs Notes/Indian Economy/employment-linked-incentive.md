# Employment-linked incentives, skilling and internships

## July 2025 Cabinet approval versus earlier Budget proposal
- **1 July approval:** ₹99,446-crore ELI scheme, two parts, for eligible jobs created **1 August 2025–31 July 2027**; target **over 3.5 crore jobs**, including **1.92 crore first-time workers**. These are targets, not jobs already delivered.
- **Part A:** first-time EPFO employees, salary up to ₹1 lakh/month; one month's EPF wage up to **₹15,000 in two instalments**, after six and twelve months, second linked to financial literacy; a portion is retained in savings. **Part B:** employers receive up to **₹3,000/month per eligible additional employee**, generally two years, manufacturing four; at least six months' retention. EPFO establishments must add **two employees if under 50 staff/five if 50+**. [Cabinet terms](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2141129&lang=2&reg=3).
- The PDF's three-scheme/three-instalment narrative with targets **210 lakh/30 lakh/50 lakh** is the older **2024 Budget design**; do not mix it with the July approval. Registration/formalisation and net additional employment differ; guard against substitution, temporary hiring and subsidy capture.
- Separate 2024 package components: **20 lakh youth trained in five years through 1,000 upgraded ITIs** in hub-and-spoke arrangements; internship ambition **1 crore in five years**, twelve-month exposure, **₹5,000/month plus₹6,000 one-time** support, company training cost/10% CSR contribution. An internship is not a guaranteed permanent job.

## PM Viksit Bharat Rozgar Yojana: September editorial
- The ELI programme is now presented as **Pradhan Mantri Viksit Bharat Rozgar Yojana (PMVBRY)**, highlighted in the PM's **15 August 2025** address, not the PDF's **15 August 2024** date. Existing July notes retain approved **₹99,446 crore**, eligible job window **1 August 2025–31 July 2027**, first-timer and employer conditions; rounded ₹1-lakh-crore outlay/3.5-crore jobs are scale/target, not a fresh scheme or achieved jobs. [Independence Day factsheet](https://www.pib.gov.in/FactsheetDetails.aspx?Id=149234&lang=2&reg=48).
- Editorial links formal employment to skills, financial literacy, social protection, dignity and manufacturing demand. EPFO additions measure formal coverage/registration and cannot alone prove net economy-wide job creation.
- Source compares **19% social-protection coverage in 2015 with 64.3%/about 94 crore in 2025**; definitions, reporting coverage and reference years matter. Its **2.9 crore versus 17-plus crore jobs** comparison and “fourth-largest economy in 2025” are editorial/source claims needing matched datasets or projected-versus-realised GDP distinctions, not a harmonised new employment series.
