# GST, GST Council and appellate tribunal

## GST and appellate resolution
- **GST began 1 July 2017**, enabled by **101st Amendment Act, 2016**; destination-based indirect tax with input-tax credit, subsuming many earlier Union/State levies. The supplier generally collects/remits tax; the economic burden usually passes to consumption.
- **CGST + SGST/UTGST** generally apply to intra-State supplies; **IGST** to inter-State supplies and imports. Exports are **zero-rated**, so “IGST on exports/imports” without qualification is misleading. June source recalls nil/5/12/18/28% principal bands; exemptions/special rates exist and bands are date-dependent.
- **Article 279A GST Council:** Union Finance Minister chairs; Union Minister of State in charge of Revenue/Finance and State finance/taxation or nominated ministers participate. Quorum **half the total membership**; decisions need **three-fourths weighted votes of members present and voting**, Centre weight **one-third**, States together **two-thirds**. The graphic's simple head-count interpretation is incorrect.
- **GSTAT**, constituted under **CGST Act section 109**, hears appeals from first appellate/revisional authorities, providing a common Centre–State forum and consistent interpretation. It is the second appellate level, not a substitute for the first appeal. [GSTAT](https://gstat.gov.in/).
- Source's “tribunal may be headed by a former SC judge/HC Chief Justice” repeats an old proposal: former Jharkhand Chief Justice **Sanjaya Kumar Mishra had already become the first President in May 2024**. Appointment/constitution does not establish every bench was operational by June 2025. [President biography](https://gstat.gov.in/president).
