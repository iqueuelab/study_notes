# Sugar industry: cane geography, pricing and ethanol

## October 2025: ethanol sourcing and mill finances
- Embedded reporting warns that reduced sugar-based ethanol procurement can leave surplus sugar and pressure mill cash flow/cane payments. Cane juice/molasses diversion lowers sugar output while adding fuel revenue; the allocation among food, feed and fuel depends on actual stocks and procurement, not installed distillery capacity alone.
- **FRP** sets sugarcane procurement terms; sugar's **minimum selling price** is a distinct mill-output floor, not crop MSP. Correct the source: **₹355/quintal is 2025–26 FRP** at basic **10.25% recovery**, not 2024–25 (₹340). [Food Department](https://dfpd.gov.in/sugar-policy/en). The article's ₹31/kg sugar floor is dated policy context; suggested price increases are recommendations.
- UP/Bihar/Haryana/Punjab form the subtropical belt; Maharashtra/Karnataka/Tamil Nadu/Andhra Pradesh are important tropical producers. Temperature, rainfall/irrigation, soil drainage, cane variety, harvest timing and recovery affect location/productivity. Do not freeze State rankings or treat all southern yields as invariably higher.
- Warm humid growth, sunny maturation, fertile well-drained soils and irrigation in dry months support cane. Water stress, drought/erratic monsoons, cyclic surplus and price uncertainty threaten returns. Drip irrigation, suitable varieties, locally appropriate crop diversification, ethanol/bio-CNG and bagasse cogeneration can help; assess full water and lifecycle costs.
- Source tables mix “sugar year 2024”, March-2024 blending and 2024–25/2025–26 policy. Its **34.0/32.3 million tonnes**, **9.4 million tonnes stocks** and **11.96% blending** are older source estimates, not October 2025 outcomes. The blanket statement exports have been banned continuously since 2023–24 is unsafe: controls/permissions change by season. Do not combine these figures as a reconciled 2025 balance.
