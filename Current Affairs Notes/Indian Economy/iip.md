# Index of Industrial Production and industrial growth

## July 2025 quick estimate, released 28 August
- **IIP +3.5% year-on-year**; manufacturing **+5.4%**, mining **−7.2%**, electricity **+0.6%**. General index **155.0**, versus 149.8 in July 2024; quick estimates are subject to revision. [MoSPI release](https://www.pib.gov.in/pressreleasepage.aspx?PRID=2161516&lang=1&reg=1).
- NSO/MoSPI's monthly **volume/quantum index**, base **2011–12 = 100** in this release; manufacturing, mining and electricity. Weighted production relatives aggregate output, not nominal sales or prices.
- Use-based groups: primary, capital, intermediate, infrastructure/construction, consumer durables and non-durables. The index excludes services and is not the whole economy/GDP.
- An index of 116 means 16% above the base-period level; it does **not** by itself imply 16% year-on-year growth. Growth must compare like periods; a positive index level alone says nothing about current expansion/contraction.
- Capacity, investment, demand and supply constraints shape output; IIP assists policy/investment assessment but cannot independently identify inflation causes or replace broader national accounts.
