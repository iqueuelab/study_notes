# PM-KISAN: income support and eligibility

## Scheme recall
- **Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi**, launched **February 2019**, effective from **December 2018**; **Central Sector**, 100% Union funding, **Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare**.
- **₹6,000 annually in three ₹2,000 instalments**, direct transfer to eligible **landholding farmer families**; States/UTs identify eligible beneficiaries and verify land records.
- Initially small/marginal farmer scope; expanded irrespective of holding size **subject to exclusion criteria**. Source's 'all landholding farmers' without exclusions is incomplete. [Official scheme](https://pmkisan.gov.in/), [2019 expansion](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=1576233&lang=2&reg=48).
- Institutional landholders and specified higher-income categories excluded; tax-paying/professional/public-office categories assessed under scheme guidelines. Tenancy without qualifying recorded land ownership does not automatically create entitlement.
- Objective: supplement input/household liquidity and reduce distress borrowing, not reimburse the full cultivation cost or provide crop insurance.
- Issues: land-record errors, exclusion of vulnerable cultivators, timely transfer, grievance resolution and real value of assistance; DBT does not by itself resolve eligibility inequity.
