Honey Mission and rural livelihoods

  • KVIC, Ministry of MSME, 2017: beekeeping training, bee colonies/boxes and value-chain support to raise rural incomes, entrepreneurship and pollination services, including women and tribal communities. CBRTI, Pune is a training/research institution.
  • Distinguish KVIC Honey Mission from the agriculture ministry's National Beekeeping and Honey Mission. Processing, quality testing, traceability, branding and market access complement hive distribution.
  • Issue reports 50,000+ trained, FY 2024–25 ~20,000 MT honey/₹325 crore income/₹25 crore exports; these programme figures are not national totals. Its national production comparison 75,000 MT (2014) → 1.25 lakh MT (2025) lacks consistent crop-year documentation; retain as rounded source reporting rather than a verified 2025 annual estimate.
  • Embedded clipping: natural-honey minimum export price reduced US$2,000 → US$1,400 FOB/MT, through 31 December 2025. Ministry confirms notification dated 22 August 2025; this is a floor on exports, not a support price paid to beekeepers. Commerce annual account.
  • 2023 PYQ: honey bees perform the waggle dance; direction relative to the sun and waggle-run duration communicate food direction/distance. Karl von Frisch's work explains this social communication.
