# Swavalambini: women entrepreneurship programme

## Pilot design and implementation
- **MSDE**, with **NITI Aayog's Women Entrepreneurship Platform** as knowledge partner, launched the pilot in **February 2025**. A **9 February 2026 parliamentary reply** covers HEIs in **Assam, Meghalaya, Mizoram, Uttar Pradesh and Telangana**—the programme is not restricted to the North-East. Implementers: **NIESBUD, Noida /IIE, Guwahati**. [Official reply](https://www.pib.gov.in/newsite/erelcontent.aspx?lang=2&reg=48&relid=283806).
- Training pathway: **1,200 women students targeted for Entrepreneurship Awareness Programme →600 selected for Entrepreneurship Development Programme →21 weeks' mentorship/handholding**. EDP covers business skills, finance, market linkages, legal compliance and networks; targets are not completed enterprises.
- MSDE supervises/monitors; NITI provides workshops, mentoring, seed-funding facilitation and **Award To Reward** recognition. The programme seeks to turn business ideas into sustainable enterprises and widen women's access to skills, networks and economic independence. Funding facilitation does not mean every trainee receives an unconditional grant.
- The source's **UPPSC 2025** ministry-mapping question answers **MSDE**; excluded from CSE PYQ files. Its dated women-enterprise rankings/2016 MSME estimates are background, not newly measured 2026 outcomes.
