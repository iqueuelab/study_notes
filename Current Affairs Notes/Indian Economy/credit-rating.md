# Credit ratings and rating agencies

## Meaning and regulator

- Assessment of a borrower's/debt instrument's creditworthiness and ability to pay principal/interest; applies to corporates, governments and specified debt instruments.
- India's securities credit-rating agencies are regulated by **SEBI under the CRA Regulations, 1999**, not RBI. [SEBI regulations listing](https://sebi.gov.in/sebiweb/home/HomeAction.do?cid=21&doListingAll=yes).
- Global examples: **S&P, Moody's, Fitch**. Source's 95% market-share claim needs a dated market definition.
- Source list includes CRISIL, ICRA, CARE, SMERA/Acuité, Fitch India/India Ratings and Brickwork; avoid a fixed 'six agencies' assertion or outdated names without checking the applicable registry/date.

## PYQ distinction

- **ICRA is a public limited company; Brickwork Ratings is an Indian agency.** UPSC Prelims 2022 answer **(b), 2 and 3 only**; preserve the exam's historical statements rather than infer today's licence status.
- Ratings express credit-risk opinions; they are not guarantees against default and are different from a borrower's personal credit score.
