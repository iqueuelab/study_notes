# ALMM: solar manufacturing and upstream sourcing

## 21 March 2026: ingots and wafers
- Solar silicon chain: **polysilicon → ingots → wafers → cells → modules**. Capital/technology-intensive upstream stages remain a weakness despite growing module capacity; supply resilience requires more than assembling imported cells.
- MNRE's **17 March amendment**, announced **18 March**, extends **ALMM (Approved List of Models and Manufacturers)** upstream: **List I modules, List II cells, List III ingots/wafers**, with the new requirement effective **1 June 2028**. It is not a requirement already operative in March 2026.
- Initial List III needs **three independent units, 15 GW combined**; listed wafer makers need matching ingot capacity. Bid cutoff is **seven days after first List III publication**; grandfathering protects eligible existing projects. From the effective date, relevant List I modules must use listed cells/wafers; existing scheme **DCR** conditions continue.
- MNRE reports about **172 GW listed module capacity and 27 GW listed cells**, not installed electricity-generation capacity. The source's **~2 GW ingot/wafer** estimate illustrates upstream concentration; its PLI achievement percentages and trade values lack a clear common reference window. [MNRE announcement](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2241551&lang=1).
- PLI support, affordable capital, reliable power, scale and skilled workers can deepen value addition. Balance localisation with project cost, capacity availability, quality/traceability and transition certainty.
