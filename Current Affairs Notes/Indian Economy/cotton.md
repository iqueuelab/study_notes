# Cotton productivity, biotechnology and import policy

## August 2025: temporary duty exemption
- **11% import duty** suspended on cotton **HS 5201 from 19 August through 30 September 2025**, under notification **35/2025-Customs, 18 August**. Source-date position is temporary relief, not permanent abolition. [Parliamentary notification record](https://sansadtv.nic.in/loksabha/ls-papers-laid-on-the-table-02-00-pm-to-0201-pm-19-august-2025).
- Rationale: reported **294 lakh bales output versus 318 lakh consumption** and raw-material price pressure on textile exporters; source imports **$579 million FY2023–24 → $1.2 billion FY2024–25**, major suppliers Australia/US/Brazil/Egypt. Estimates depend on crop-year/unit definitions; one Indian bale convention is **170 kg**.
- Trade-off: cheaper inputs/export competitiveness versus growers' price/incentive concerns. Predictable trade rules, credible **CCI/MSP procurement**, efficient markets and productivity support matter. Removal is not proof farmers receive no other government support.
## Crop and biotechnology
- **Gossypium** species: **arboreum, herbaceum, hirsutum and barbadense**; India grows all four, hirsutum dominates hybrids; barbadense associated with extra-long staple. North: Punjab/Haryana/Rajasthan; central: Gujarat/Maharashtra/MP; south: Telangana/AP/Karnataka. State rankings require crop-year verification.
- Large acreage but low yields; source **435–447 kg/ha and roughly two-thirds rainfed**, pest/whitefly/pink-bollworm pressure, water stress and price volatility constrain farmers. Source global shares and production ranks mix **2022–23/2023** data and are not timeless.
- **Bt cotton approved 2002**; genes from **Bacillus thuringiensis** confer specified insect resistance. **Bollgard I/II = GM crop technologies (2022 PYQ b)**. Pest resistance evolves; refuge/integrated pest management and suitable seeds matter. Source NBRI pink-bollworm-resistant research is not evidence of commercial regulatory approval or universal resistance to all pests.
## Cotton productivity mission
- **Budget 2025–26 five-year Mission for Cotton Productivity**: science/technology support, sustainable yields and extra-long-staple cotton; serves the **Farm → Fibre → Factory → Fashion → Foreign** value chain. [July 2025 official mission account](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2146756&lang=2&reg=48). Ministry responsibilities need the notified implementation framework; the practice question's simple ministry-based key is not sufficient proof.
- Improve irrigation efficiency, drought/pest resilience, research-extension, risk cover, quality grading, market access and mill/MSME finance. Cotton supports roughly six million growers in source estimates, with a much larger processing/textile workforce; do not equate growers with all value-chain workers.
