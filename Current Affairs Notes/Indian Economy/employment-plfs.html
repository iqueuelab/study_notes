Indian Economy

Employment, unemployment and PLFS measures

Editable notes

Measures and classification

  • Labour force =employed +unemployed; unemployed means seeking/available for work under the applicable survey definition, not everyone without a salary.
  • UR =unemployed ÷labour force ×100; LFPR =labour force ÷population ×100; WPR =employed ÷population ×100. Always specify age group, rural/urban and reference period.
  • PLFS, NSO/MoSPI: source-era annual rural/urban estimates under usual/current weekly status, quarterly urban CWS estimates. Usual status reflects a longer reference period; CWS uses the preceding seven days.
  • Sampling and student/individual employment classification matter; missing formal contracts does not make informal workers statistically unemployed.

Types and traps

TypeMeaning
DisguisedExtra workers add little/no marginal output; may still count as employed
SeasonalWork available only in specified seasons
StructuralSkill/location/sector mismatch
FrictionalJob-search or transition interval
TechnologicalDisplacement following technology change; new jobs may also emerge
CyclicalDemand downturn reduces employment
  • Vulnerable employment describes insecure categories such as own-account/contributing-family work; not an unemployment category. Source “69% year-on-year automation loss” misrepresents a risk estimate, not observed annual job destruction.

Causes and response

  • Weak labour demand, skill mismatch, seasonal agriculture, unequal access/discrimination and mobility barriers; population size alone does not mechanically determine unemployment.
  • MGNREGA rural work guarantee, PMKVY /PM-DAKSH skills, SMILE targeted rehabilitation/livelihood support, Startup India; programme coverage and quality need assessment.
  • Improve labour-intensive growth, apprenticeships, portable benefits, women's safe access/childcare, market-linked training and credible placement outcomes.
  • Falling UR alone can reflect lower participation; assess job quality, earnings and underemployment alongside the headline rate.

15 May 2025 : survey redesign

  • From January 2025, PLFS rotational-panel design covers rural and urban areas, with monthly CWS indicators and rural/urban quarterly coverage; first monthly bulletin covers April 2025. Source body repeats the older urban-only quarterly objective and misses this key clipping development. Annual usual-status/CWS estimates continue with the revised design. Mo SPI redesign.
  • NSO/Mo SPI survey measures employment, participation, unemployment and work intensity; hours worked aid underemployment analysis. A survey redesign does not establish that hours had never been asked in any earlier employment survey.
  • Assess sampling/reference-period changes before comparisons; more frequent estimates do not automatically mean every month-to-month change is statistically meaningful.
