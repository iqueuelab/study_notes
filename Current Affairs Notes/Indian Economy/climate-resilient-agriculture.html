Climate-resilient agriculture: technology, adaptation and inclusion

Editable notes

January 2026: coordinated adaptation roadmap

  • CRA combines stable productivity, adaptation to heat/drought/flood/rainfall variability and resource conservation. Source estimates 51% net sown area rainfed, producing ~40% food underline vulnerability; these are dated structural estimates, not precise shares for every season. Soil degradation/water stress interact with climate and food demand.
  • ICAR's NICRA (National Innovations in Climate Resilient Agriculture), 2011: location-specific demonstration of SRI/aerobic or direct-seeded rice, zero-till wheat, tolerant varieties and residue management. 448 climate-resilient villages is the 2 December 2025 official programme snapshot, not proof uniform adoption. Agriculture Ministry. NMSA under NAPCC emphasises rainfed/integrated farming, soil and water; BioE3 includes climate-resilient agriculture as a biotechnology priority.
  • Tools: validated biofertilisers/biopesticides and soil microbiome research; suitable heat/drought/salinity/pest-tolerant crops; weather/soil/crop-data advisories, precision irrigation and pest alerts. Genome editing is not categorically separate from all genetic modification: outcomes and Indian regulatory treatment depend on the edit and retained foreign DNA; not every edited crop is already released or climate-resilient.
  • Adoption needs affordable tested inputs, locally appropriate seeds, local-language advice, extension, credit, insurance, transition support and accessible digital infrastructure. Technology alone cannot repair weak markets, unreliable water or exclusion of marginal farmers.
  • A coherent roadmap integrating agriculture, biotechnology, climate policy and farmer welfare is the editorial's recommendation, not an already enacted mandatory national CRA scheme under BioE3. Link to SDGs 2, 12, 13 and evaluate district outcomes/equity. The 250-word daily Mains question is practice, not an attributable PYQ.
