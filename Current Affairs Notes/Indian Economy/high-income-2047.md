# India’s high-income2047 pathway

## Conditional scenario

- World Bank February 28, 2025 report: average realGDP growth~**7.8%** over 22 years could support high-income status by 2047; this is a reform scenario, not a guaranteed forecast. [World Bank](https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/press-release/2025/02/28/india-accelerated-reforms-needed-to-speed-up-growth-and-achieve-high-income-status-by-2047).
- Suggested investment/GDP~33.5%→40% by 2035; labour-force participation 56.4%→65%, women's participation 35.6%→50%; improve productivity and human capital.
- High-income classification uses **GNI per capita**, not aggregateGDP rank. Real growth, population, exchange rates and the classification methodology matter.
- More jobs/skills, productive cities, infrastructure, competitive firms/trade, State reforms and inclusion; climate resilience must accompany investment.
