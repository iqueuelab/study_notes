# Special Economic Zones and semiconductor reforms

## Framework
- SEZ policy introduced **2000**; **SEZ Act 2005 and Rules2006** support exports, investment, employment and infrastructure. Deemed outside customs territory for authorised trade operations, while remaining Indian sovereign territory and subject to applicable laws.
- Benefits include duty-free authorised imports, single-window procedures and GST **zero-rating for authorised supplies**; zero-rating differs from a blanket exemption for every transaction. GIFT City's SEZ/IFSC illustrates specialised services.
- Source snapshot: **276 operational SEZs** and **US$163.69 billion exports in 2023–24**; retain reporting periods. DESH (**Development of Enterprise and Service Hubs**) was a proposed reform, not a replacement law already in force.
## 3 June2025 amendments
- Exclusive semiconductor/electronics-component SEZ minimum contiguous area **50→10ha** (Rule5); Board may relax encumbrance-free conditions for land mortgaged/leased to Central/State governments or their authorised agencies (Rule7), subject to conditions.
- Free-of-cost goods received/supplied can enter prescribed **NFE calculation**; Rule18 enables relevant units' domestic sales with applicable duties. Earlier DTA sales were not universally forbidden for every SEZ.
- **Micron, Sanand, Gujarat** and **Hubballi Durable Goods Cluster/Aequs, Karnataka** proposals: combined source investment **₹13,100 crore** (₹13,000cr and ₹100cr). These approvals are not proof production had begun.
- Correct source's **June2024** to **June2025**. [PIB,9June2025](https://www.pib.gov.in/Pressreleaseshare.aspx?PRID=2135116&lang=2&reg=48).
- Constraints: high infrastructure/land costs, regulatory burdens, services/manufacturing imbalance, idle approved zones and environmental impacts. **Baba Kalyani committee2018** recommended employment/economic enclaves, outcome-based incentives, differentiated sector approaches and easier domestic integration/clearances.
