# Kedarnath and Hemkund Sahib ropeways

## Approved proposals

| Route, Uttarakhand | Source approval figures |
| --- | --- |
| Sonprayag–Kedarnath, Rudraprayag | 12.9 km; ₹4, 081.23 crore; ~36 minute planned journey |
| Govindghat–Hemkund Sahib, Chamoli | 12.4 km; ₹2, 730.13 crore |

- Parvatmala framework, PPP; monocable/tricable detachable gondola technologies. Approval is not completed construction or operating safety certification.
- Better access for elderly/disabled pilgrims and tourism livelihoods; links to Valley of Flowers region, not a ropeway constructed inside every part of the UNESCO park.
- Himalayan slope stability, forest impacts, carrying capacity, waste/water, weather evacuation and construction disruption need assessment; lower journey time does not itself prove lower lifecycle emissions.
