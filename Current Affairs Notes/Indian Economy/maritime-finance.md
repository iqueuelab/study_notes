# Maritime financing and Amrit Kaal Vision2047

## Maritime Financing Summit: 24 July 2025
- **Ministry of Ports, Shipping and Waterways, New Delhi**; financing, capacity, demand and skills support **Maritime Amrit Kaal Vision 2047**. **Financial Digital Maturity Matrix (FDMM)** assesses and improves major ports' digital financial readiness.
- Proposed **₹25,000 crore Maritime Development Fund**, blended finance with government up to **49%**, remaining participation from ports, PSUs and private investors; aims to reduce capital cost and attract long-term investment. Announcement is distinct from disbursement.
- **100% automatic-route FDI** in covered shipping activities; GIFT IFSC ship finance/leasing, infrastructure recognition for qualifying large ships and greenfield/brownfield shipyards support capacity. Legislative reforms include **Bills of Lading 2025**, Merchant Shipping and Coastal Shipping measures; bill passage, assent and commencement differ.
- Official summit claims: turnaround **under one day**, versus four earlier, and container capacity up **70%**; specified periods/port metrics matter. Source's **819.22 million tonnes in FY2023–24** refers to **major-port cargo**, not all Indian ports. [Ministry release](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2147994&lang=2&reg=48).
- Source's 20% cargo-carriage ambition and **₹1.5 lakh crore investment by 2030** are targets, not India's realised share of all global cargo. Complementary initiatives: Sagarmala, Maritime India Vision 2030, Major Port Authorities Act 2021 and National Logistics Portal (Marine).
## Ocean-group PYQ recall
- **IONS:** Indian Navy initiative; inaugural meeting **2008, New Delhi**; voluntary naval cooperation among Indian Ocean littorals, confidence building, safety and disaster response. Not founded in 2015.
- **IORA**, formerly IOR-ARC: **1997**; Secretariat **Ebène, Mauritius**, correcting the PDF's Durban headquarters. Trade, investment, fisheries, blue economy, disaster response and academic exchange extend beyond maritime security; not created solely in response to piracy/oil spills. [IORA Secretariat location](https://www.iora.int/sites/default/files/2025-04/IORA%20enewsletter%20Jan-March%202025%20Final_0.pdf).
