# Crop insurance: PMFBY, RWBCIS and technology

## January 2025 Cabinet decision

- **PMFBY + Restructured Weather Based Crop Insurance Scheme (RWBCIS)** continued through **2025–26**, with **₹69,515.71 crore for 2021–22 to 2025–26**, not a new one-year allocation.
- **Fund for Innovation and Technology (FIAT): ₹824.77 crore** for technology and more transparent assessment/settlement. [Cabinet release](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2089249&lang=1&reg=3).

## Distinguish schemes

| Scheme | Core basis |
| --- | --- |
| PMFBY | Crop yield loss from covered risks |
| RWBCIS | Weather-index deviations, including rain/temperature/humidity/wind |

- Eligible farmers, including eligible tenants/sharecroppers, grow **notified crops in notified areas**; this does not automatically cover every crop everywhere.
- Crops: food crops (cereals, millets, pulses), oilseeds, annual commercial and horticultural crops.

| Farmer premium ceiling | Rate of sum insured |
| --- | --- |
| Kharif food/oilseed | 2% or actuarial rate, whichever lower |
| Rabi food/oilseed | 1.5% or actuarial rate, whichever lower |
| Annual commercial/horticultural | 5% or actuarial rate, whichever lower |

- State/government support covers the balance under scheme rules; premium rate is not the compensation percentage.
- Coverage subject to notifications: yield loss, eligible prevented sowing/localised calamities and specified post-harvest losses **up to 14 days**; war/nuclear and excluded preventable losses are not blanket-covered.

## Technology and Mains

- **YES-TECH — Yield Estimation System Based on Technology**: technology-assisted yield estimates; **WINDS — Weather Information Network Data Systems**: hyperlocal weather observations; National Crop Insurance Portal supports administration.
- Benefits: speed, assessment quality and transparency; challenges: basis risk, station quality, tenant documentation, awareness, delayed subsidy/claims and grievance redress.
- Combine reliable data with auditable assessment, timely funding, farmer access and effective appeals; technology alone does not remove basis risk.
