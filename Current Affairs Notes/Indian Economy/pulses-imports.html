Indian Economy

Pulses: cultivation, imports and farmer incentives

Editable notes

Crops and geography

  • Gram/chickpea/chana, lentil/masoor and dry peas are chiefly rabi; tur/arhar/pigeonpea, moong and urad are important kharif crops, with cultivar/region-dependent summer/rabi cultivation. Gram and chana are the same crop, not separate pulse species.
  • Arhar also fixes atmospheric nitrogen through root-nodule symbiosis: the source's exception is false. Pulse rotation can improve soil fertility, but actual fixation depends on plant–microbe/environmental conditions. ICAR biofertiliser crop list.
  • Main producing States in source map: Madhya Pradesh, Rajasthan, Maharashtra, Uttar Pradesh, Karnataka. Its uniform 20–27°C / 25–60 cm rainfall cannot describe every pulse/cultivar. India is a major producer/consumer/importer; map's 25% production/27% consumption/14% trade are dated approximations with different denominators.
  • Yellow peas = dry Pisum sativum L., legumes used as dal substitutes/protein ingredients; rabi sowing approximately Oct–Nov, harvest Mar–Apr, well-drained loam. Source 15–25°C / 20–40 cm indicative, 20–25% protein approximate. Canada/Russia are important suppliers, with Ukraine, US, Australia, France/China also cited; exact producer rankings need year and dry-pea definition.

31 May 2025 yellow-pea extension

  • Clipping announces free import without minimum import price/port restrictions, subject to online import-monitoring registration, for consignments with bill of lading on/before 31 March 2026; previous window ran to 31 May 2025. This supersedes rewritten “February 2025/reimposed in 2024” account. Import-policy “free” and customs zero duty are distinct measures. USDA report reproducing Indian notifications 31/2025 and 16/2025–26.
  • Source reports duty-free tur extended to 31 March 2026; chickpea/urad/lentil decisions differ by notification/date. Do not treat its blanket “no decision yet” as a verified current status for every crop.

Import–price trade-off and policy

  • Source import totals 3.31 Mt (2023), 6.63 Mt (2024) are stated all-pulse totals, not yellow peas alone; calendar/FY labels need reconciliation. Reported prices urad down 25% from₹100/kg, turUS$1,400→800/t, yellow peasUS$700→450/t are market/time-specific, not universal farmgate realisations.
  • Imports may contain food inflation/bridge shortages yet depress local prices and planting incentives; cereal procurement/irrigation, rainfed vulnerability and weak seed/storage/processing can discourage pulses. Ethanol-driven maize competition is an incentive concern, not proof every farmer switched.
  • NFSM pulses: demonstrations, seed production/hubs; source 28 States+2 UTs /150 hubs /343 varieties 2014–23 are programme-period claims, not new June achievements.
  • PM-AASHA/PSS MSP procurement and price-support instruments have scheme-specific scope; listing PDPS/private procurement does not mean all apply identically to every pulse. Budget 2025 six-year self-reliance mission focuses tur, urad, masoor, resilient/productive seeds and procurement via NAFED/NCCF. Announcement/budget allocation differs from full field implementation.
  • Recommended stable, consulted import policy, calibrated duties/quotas where justified, MSP access, storage/processing and location-specific R&D; weigh consumer affordability and farmer returns rather than assuming protection alone guarantees self-sufficiency.

Green gram (moong)

  • Vigna radiata, Fabaceae: short-duration pulse, commonly 60–75 days, fitting rotations/intercropping with maize, sorghum, cotton or sugarcane. Kharif, rabi or summer/zaid cultivation varies by region.
  • Warm conditions, well-drained loam/sandy loam; the source's 25–35°C, 600–750 mm rainfall and pH 6.2–7.5 are indicative agronomic ranges, not universal requirements. Waterlogging impairs growth/nodulation.
  • Rhizobial fixation and residues can improve soil nitrogen; actual fertiliser savings depend on conditions. MSP/NFSM-pulses support is relevant; irrigation/organic programmes are complementary instruments, not a guarantee of doubled income.
  • PYQ revision: urad can be both kharif and rabi; moong does not account for nearly half of Indian pulse output. The 2020 question's historical trend statement about declining rabi pulses is false; do not present its three-decade window as a newly measured July 2025 trend.
