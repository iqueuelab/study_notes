# Electronics Components Manufacturing Scheme: domestic value chains

## 2 January 2026: third tranche of ECMS approvals
- MeitY approved **22 proposals**, projected **₹41,863 crore investment**, **₹2,58,152 crore production** and **33,791 direct jobs**. These are projections, not money spent, output delivered or jobs already created. Earlier **24 approvals** represented **₹12,704 crore investment**. [MeitY](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleaseIframePage.aspx?PRID=2210864&lang=2&reg=3).
- **ECMS notified 8 April 2025, original outlay ₹22,919 crore**, aims to deepen component/subassembly/material supply chains, raise domestic value addition and reduce vulnerable import dependencies. Incentives vary by target segment—turnover, capital expenditure or hybrid approaches—with employment conditions; do not describe every component as receiving an identical universal 1–10% PLI benefit.
- The PDF appends the **27 October 2025 first seven-project tranche**: **₹5,532 crore**, Tamil Nadu **5**, MP **1**, AP **1**. Its contemporary backgrounder gives expected **₹44,406 crore output/5,195 jobs**; these are the source-era first-tranche estimates, not part of a second newly announced January seven-project package. [October PIB backgrounder](https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2025/oct/doc20251027678301.pdf).
- First-tranche examples: **Kaynes** (Chennai/Thoothukudi, PCBs/camera-module assemblies/laminates, source **₹3,280 crore**), **Ascent Circuits** (TN PCBs, **₹991 crore**), **SRF** (MP films/laminates), **Syrma** (AP subassemblies). Firm headquarters and project locations differ. Promised full domestic demand coverage concerns specified inputs, not all Indian electronic components.
- Faster commercial production supports incentive eligibility, but “first to finish” is not a blanket waiver of quality, investment or employment conditions. Distinguish finished-product PLI, semiconductor mission and component supply chains; chips/packaging/fabs have separate programme conditions.
- Local suppliers, precision skills, design/IP, testing, power/logistics and stable policy determine value addition. Source **18–20%→40–50% by 2026** is an aspiration, not an achieved result; **US$300bn production/$120bn exports** are dated policy targets with distinct baselines.
