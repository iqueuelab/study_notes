# Confederation of Indian Industry

## Institution and June 2025 leadership
- **Non-government, not-for-profit, industry-led organisation**; roots in **Engineering and Iron Trades Association, 1895**; headquarters **New Delhi**. It collaborates with ministries but is **not a statutory body under Commerce Ministry**.
- **1 June 2025:** **Rajiv Memani**, EY India Chairman/CEO, became CII President **2025–26**, succeeding **Sanjiv Puri** of ITC. **R. Mukundan** president-designate; **Suchitra K. Ella** vice-president. These are dated officeholders, not permanently current. [CII announcement](https://www.cii.in/ResourceDetails.aspx?enc=fuONeJl+koEWm16Dm8dwCWaLLn+t+JiaJHKzW9AhyA4%3D).
- Functions: policy advocacy/industry consultations, competitiveness and entrepreneurship, skill/innovation platforms and centres of excellence, B2B/trade-investment missions, sustainability/CSR and inclusive development.
- Source's **300 partner organisations/100 countries** is a network snapshot, not treaty membership. Participation in WTO-related business dialogue does not make CII the sovereign trade negotiator. Its activities can intersect agriculture/consumer supply chains despite the PDF's overly absolute exclusion.
