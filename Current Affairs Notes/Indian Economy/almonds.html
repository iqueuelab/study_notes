Indian Economy

Almonds: horticulture and cardiometabolic evidence

Editable notes

Crop and trade recall

  • Prunus dulcis, Rosaceae; deciduous tree, edible seed of a drupe, marketed as a tree nut. Native to western/central Asian regions.
  • Cold winters provide dormancy/chilling; warm dry summers aid maturation; well-drained loamy soil is useful. Indian temperate cultivation is concentrated in Jammu & Kashmir, with Himachal Pradesh and other suitable Himalayan areas.
  • India depends heavily on imports; United States/California and Australia are important suppliers. California's Mediterranean climate supports a major almond industry; exact import/production shares vary by year and whether measured in-shell or shelled.
  • Value-added food processing, storage and horticultural diversification can improve farmer returns. MIDH and food-processing infrastructure schemes are relevant broader programmes; source does not establish a separate almond-specific NMSA scheme.

April 2025 health report

  • Newspaper describes a 2025 perspective/evidence synthesis in Current Developments in Nutrition, drawing on meta-analyses and a July 2024 roundtable funded by the Almond Board of California; it is not one new trial proving universal benefits.
  • Evidence suggests small improvements in LDL cholesterol and diastolic blood pressure, no consistent weight gain, and improved glycaemic responses in some populations. Almonds provide unsaturated fats, protein, fibre and vitamin E.
  • Dose, dietary substitution, baseline health and study duration affect results; heterogeneous/underpowered studies leave research gaps. Findings support a balanced dietary pattern rather than a cure for hypertension, diabetes or guaranteed weight loss.

Original perspective.

Kashmir harvest and trade recall

  • Source describes a bumper Kashmir almond harvest and its livelihood/cultural significance. Badamwari's spring blossoms and dry-fruit gifting connect horticulture to local identity. Producer collectives, processing, irrigation, crop insurance and credible origin branding can improve returns.
  • Distinguish edible sweet almonds from bitter almonds, which are not suitable for casual raw consumption. Source's 800–3,000 m, pH 6.0–7.5, US import share 70–80% and California supply ~80% are context-dependent agronomic/trade ranges, not universal requirements or timeless shares. Retain temperate Himalayan cultivation; unsupported Kerala/Andhra growing-area claims are omitted.
  • Existing April health evidence remains qualified: the repeated clipping is not a new August clinical trial. Source's “superfood” wording does not establish a cure or guaranteed cognitive benefit.
  • 2024 apples/GM-food PYQ: D—India imports US apples; competent-authority approval is required for GM-food imports. Source calls J&K a State in practice; its August 2025 status was a Union Territory.
