# Salem Sago: GI identity and exports

## February 2026: Canada consignment
- **APEDA facilitated export of 0.5 metric tonnes of GI-tagged Salem Sago to Canada**, reported **19 February 2026**. APEDA is under Commerce and Industry; export promotion is distinct from the GI Registry's registration function. [Official release](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2230307&lang=1&reg=6).
- **Salem, Tamil Nadu; GI registration 2023; locally javvarisi.** Made from tapioca/cassava root starch: extract wet starch → form pearls → dry/polish. Wet processing into sago pearls differs from dry industrial starch supply. Food, textiles, paper and other industries use starch in differing forms.
- GI protects qualifying geographic reputation/characteristics under the **GI of Goods (Registration and Protection) Act 1999**; it does not guarantee every starch product sold in Salem is registered or exported.
- The sheet's two unyearred “UPSC Prelims PYQ” Salem questions are held for attribution review. Its answer to the genuine **2025 Portuguese-introduced fruits** question is wrong: **papaya, pineapple and guava—all three (c)**; the complete question already exists in the common bank.
