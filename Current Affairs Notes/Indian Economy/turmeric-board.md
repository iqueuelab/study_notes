# National Turmeric Board and value chains

## Institution
- Constituted **October 2023**; launched **14 January 2025**, **Nizamabad, Telangana**; **Ministry of Commerce and Industry**, chairman **Palle Ganga Reddy**.
- Representation: Commerce, AYUSH, Pharmaceuticals, Agriculture, research bodies, farmers/exporters and rotating State representatives; source launch States **Maharashtra, Telangana, Meghalaya**.
- Research, higher yield/quality, processing/value addition, logistics, export standards and markets; promote credible evidence for traditional/medicinal uses, not claims turmeric cures every disease.
## Production and trade
- India: leading producer/consumer/exporter, **30+ varieties**, source **over 70% of world production**. Leading States include **Maharashtra, Telangana, Karnataka, Tamil Nadu**; destinations **Bangladesh, UAE, USA, Malaysia**.
- Official **2023–24** cultivation **3.05 lakh ha**, production **10.74 lakh tonnes**; retain crop-year labels. [Commerce Ministry launch](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleaseIframePage.aspx?PRID=2092719&lang=2&reg=48).
- Better planting material + extension + processing/quality assurance + diversified markets can improve producer income. Volume growth alone does not guarantee remunerative farm prices.
## Board and commodity recall
- Headquarters **Nizamabad, Telangana**; central chairperson, Commerce-appointed secretary and representatives from relevant ministries, rotating producing States, research, farmers and exporters.
- Coordinates R&D, quality, logistics, value addition and markets alongside the Spices Board; proposed medicinal uses do not establish clinical efficacy.
- India is the largest producer/exporter; source shares and market rankings are dated. Maharashtra, Telangana, Karnataka and Tamil Nadu are important producing States.
- **Tea Board, Coffee Board, Spices Board and Coconut Development Board fall under different ministry/statutory arrangements**; not every commodity board belongs to Agriculture.
