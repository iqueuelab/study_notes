# IMF and World Bank: institutions and reports

## Bretton Woods institutions
- **1944 Bretton Woods conference:** IMF and IBRD institutional framework; IMF Articles entered into force **27 December 1945**, also India’s joining date. Reconstruction was central to IBRD; IMF’s distinct purpose is international monetary stability, exchange/payment cooperation and balance-of-payments support. [IMF Articles](https://www.imf.org/en/publications/articles-of-agreement/issues/2016/12/30/articles-of-agreement-of-the-international-monetary-fund-adopted-at-the-united-nations-357).
- **IMF had 191 members in April 2025**, after Liechtenstein joined on 21 October 2024; source’s 190 is outdated. [IMF announcement](https://www.imf.org/en/news/articles/2024/10/21/pr-24387-liechtenstein-principality-of-liechtenstein-becomes-imfs-191st-member).
- IMF reports: **World Economic Outlook**, **Global Financial Stability Report** (normally spring/autumn). The latter assesses financial-system risks; the source’s “Global Financial Stabilisation Report” wording is incorrect.
## World Bank versus World Bank Group
- **World Bank = IBRD + IDA**; **World Bank Group = these plus IFC, MIGA and ICSID**. IBRD lends to middle-income/creditworthy poorer countries; IDA provides concessional support; IFC serves private enterprise; **Multilateral Investment Guarantee Agency** provides political-risk guarantees; **International Centre for Settlement of Investment Disputes** handles investment dispute settlement. [World Bank distinction](https://www.worldbank.org/en/about/history/the-world-bank-group-and-the-imf).
- Source-era IBRD membership: **189**; membership differs across Group institutions. India belongs to IBRD/IDA/IFC/MIGA but not ICSID. Do not imply every Group institution has identical membership.
- Reports/products: **World Development Report**, **Human Capital Index**; **Doing Business** was discontinued. IMF focuses on macroeconomic/monetary stability; World Bank on longer-term development, finance and poverty reduction.
- US is the largest IBRD shareholder; source percentages (US 16.41%, Japan 7.87%, etc.) omit reference date and mix shares/voting power, so do not memorise them as April 2025 figures.
- PYQ recall (2016): **Global Financial Stability Report → IMF; Ease of Doing Business ranking → World Bank**.
## Additional IMF revision points
- Headquartered **Washington, DC**; financial operations began **1947**. Existing note records 1944/1945 origins and corrects source's outdated 190 members to **191** in May 2025.
- **Fiscal Monitor** complements WEO/GFSR with fiscal-policy analysis. Surveillance, policy advice, technical assistance/capacity development and balance-of-payments financing are distinct activities; crisis assistance commonly carries programme conditions.
- India borrowed during the **1991 balance-of-payments crisis** and later participated as a creditor/contributor; quota determines financial commitments and strongly influences voting power, but quota share is not identical to voting percentage. Sri Lanka/Pakistan illustrate programmes, not unconditional grants or automatic debt cancellation.

## July 29 WEO update
- IMF projected **global growth 3.0% in 2025,3.1% in 2026**, upgrades **0.2/0.1 percentage points** from April. India projected **6.4% in both FY 2025–26 and FY 2026–27**, also up0.2/0.1 points; India's figures use fiscal years, unlike the world aggregate's calendar years. [IMF July table](https://www.imf.org/-/media/Files/Publications/WEO/2025/update/july/english/text.ashx).
- Better effective tariff assumptions, trade front-loading, improved financial conditions/weaker dollar and fiscal support explain the global upgrade; uncertainty, tariffs and geopolitics remain downside risks. Forecast is not realised output. [IMF overview](https://www.imf.org/en/Publications/WEO/Issues/2025/07/29/world-economic-outlook-update-july-2025).
