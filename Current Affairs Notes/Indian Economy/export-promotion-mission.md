# Export Promotion Mission: finance and export readiness

## August 2025 proposal
- Announced in **Union Budget 2025–26**; source reports government **considering about ₹25,000 crore over six years, 2025–31**. Treat this as the issue-date proposed package, not final Cabinet-approved expenditure or disbursement.
- **Department of Commerce** leads convergence of Commerce/Industry, MSME and Finance. Exporter/MSME needs: affordable credit, alternative trade finance, standards compliance, market information, branding, overseas warehousing/logistics and global-value-chain access.
- Reported design: **Niryat Protsahan** for finance/credit/liquidity, including interest equalisation and factoring/invoice/e-commerce financing; **Niryat Disha** for quality compliance, market development, branding, warehousing and capacity. The source's **₹10,000-plus/₹14,500-plus crore** component estimates and **₹5,000-plus/₹4,000 crore** subitems are provisional, not notified entitlements.
- MSMEs' source **~45% export share** is an approximate dated structural reference. Exports may support jobs/foreign exchange; current-account improvement depends on imports and the wider balance of payments. Judge outcomes by additional competitive exports and firm access, beyond headline subsidy allocation.
