# Export Promotion Mission: finance and export readiness

## August 2025 proposal
- Announced in **Union Budget 2025–26**; source reports government **considering about ₹25,000 crore over six years, 2025–31**. Treat this as the issue-date proposed package, not final Cabinet-approved expenditure or disbursement.
- **Department of Commerce** leads convergence of Commerce/Industry, MSME and Finance. Exporter/MSME needs: affordable credit, alternative trade finance, standards compliance, market information, branding, overseas warehousing/logistics and global-value-chain access.
- Reported design: **Niryat Protsahan** for finance/credit/liquidity, including interest equalisation and factoring/invoice/e-commerce financing; **Niryat Disha** for quality compliance, market development, branding, warehousing and capacity. The source's **₹10,000-plus/₹14,500-plus crore** component estimates and **₹5,000-plus/₹4,000 crore** subitems are provisional, not notified entitlements.
- MSMEs' source **~45% export share** is an approximate dated structural reference. Exports may support jobs/foreign exchange; current-account improvement depends on imports and the wider balance of payments. Judge outcomes by additional competitive exports and firm access, beyond headline subsidy allocation.

## 31 December 2025: Market Access Support launched
- **Market Access Support (MAS)** launched under **Niryat Disha** of EPM, targeting MSMEs, first-time exporters and priority sectors. Support covers trade fairs/exhibitions, buyer–seller and reverse buyer–seller meets, trade delegations and buyer discovery in priority/emerging markets. [Commerce Ministry](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleaseIframePage.aspx?PRID=2210046&lang=2&reg=48).
- Source reports MAS allocation **₹4,531 crore across FY2025–26–2030–31**, including **₹500 crore for the current FY**. Embedded clipping: event assistance ceilings **₹5 crore BSM /₹10 crore RBSM**; up to **five delegates per firm**, at least **50 participants**, at least **35% MSME participation**, and source ceiling **three events/year per firm/four BSMs for MSMEs**. These are conditions described in this issue, not an entitlement for every exporter or the budget of the whole Mission.
- **Cabinet approval 12 November 2025** converts August's proposal into an approved **₹25,060 crore, six-year Mission**. Commerce, MSME and Finance jointly implement with DGFT, overseas missions, EPCs, commodity boards and industry. **Niryat Protsahan** addresses finance/credit; **Niryat Disha** addresses standards, branding, market access/logistics. Avoid presenting repeated November approval as a second January approval.
- Labour-intensive textiles, leather, gems/jewellery, engineering and marine products face market/credit constraints. Link district export hubs and diversification to competitive additional exports; source **~21% GDP/45 million jobs/~45% MSME exports/~2.5% world exports** are dated rounded indicators with different denominators, not measured Mission outcomes.

## 20 February 2026: seven additional interventions
- **Niryat Protsahan:** export factoring (**2.75%** subvention; **₹50 lakh/MSME/year** cap); e-commerce credit; shared-risk instruments for emerging/high-risk markets.
- E-commerce: direct credit up to **₹50 lakh /90% guarantee**; overseas inventory up to **₹5 crore /75% guarantee**. Their **2.75% subvention** has a **₹15 lakh/applicant/year** ceiling; do not apply that ceiling to every EPM component.
- **Niryat Disha:** **TRACE** testing/certification (**60%/75%**, ₹25 lakh/IEC/year); **FLOW** overseas fulfilment (**up to 30%** approved project cost, maximum three years); **LIFT** eligible freight (**up to 30%**, ₹20 lakh/IEC/FY); **INSIGHT** trade intelligence/capacity (**up to 50%**, eligible public proposals up to 100%, subject to ceilings).
- These supplement three operating measures—market access, shipment-credit interest subvention and collateral support—bringing **10 of 11 proposed interventions** into operation. [Commerce Ministry launch](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2230664&lang=2&reg=48).
- The repeated market-access clipping's future **31 January 2026** guideline target identifies older material. Existing notes already retain the ₹25,060 crore Mission, ₹4,531 crore MAS allocation and event conditions; this is not a new approval of those amounts.
