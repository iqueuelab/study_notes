# Ethane carriers and petrochemical feedstock logistics

## ONGC–MOL heads of agreement
- **3 July 2025:** ONGC and Japan's **Mitsui O.S.K. Lines (MOL)** reported heads of agreement for a partnership to build, own and operate **two Very Large Ethane Carriers (VLECs)**, subject to ONGC Board approval; this is not delivery or commencement of transport.
- Intended to carry imported ethane for **ONGC Petro additions Ltd (OPaL), Dahej SEZ, Gujarat**. Source plans for **800,000 tonnes/year from May 2028** are future targets.
- **Ethane (C₂H₆)**, obtained from natural-gas liquids, is cracked to **ethylene (C₂H₄)** for plastics and other petrochemical derivatives; distinguish methane, ethanol and LNG. Ethylene-derived chains include antifreeze/detergent materials, rather than every product being made by one direct reaction.
- OPaL's dual-feed cracker handles naphtha and gaseous feedstocks; propylene and other products form part of the wider complex. VLECs need specialised low-temperature gas logistics, not ordinary oil-tanker arrangements.
- Long-term shipping arrangements can secure feedstock and reduce spot-market dependence; supply, cost, utilisation and demand risks remain. Japan cooperation does not guarantee automatic technology transfer.
