# Cocoa cultivation and West African producers

## Commodity recall
- Cocoa requires warm, humid tropical conditions, fertile soils and adequate rainfall. **Côte d’Ivoire and Ghana**, both West African, are the two countries identified by the **2024 Prelims question: (c)**.
- Other important producers include Ecuador, Nigeria and Indonesia. Annual country rankings/volumes can shift with harvests and weather; source rounded production figures lack a common crop-year label.
- Algeria/Morocco and Botswana/Namibia are largely too arid for the role suggested; Madagascar/Mozambique are not the top pair in that question.
