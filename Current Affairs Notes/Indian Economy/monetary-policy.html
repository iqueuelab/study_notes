Indian Economy

Monetary Policy Committee and policy instruments

Editable notes

MPC: statutory role

  • Section 45ZB, RBI Act 1934, as amended in 2016: Central Government constitutes a six-member MPC; its policy-rate decision binds RBI. Price stability is the primary objective while keeping growth in mind.
  • Three RBI members: Governor (ex officio chair), Deputy Governor in charge of monetary policy, and an RBI officer nominated by the Central Board. Three external members: Central Government appointees with ability, integrity and expertise in economics/banking/finance/monetary policy.
  • Majority voting; Governor has a casting vote on a tie. Quorum four, including Governor or, in the Governor's absence, the monetary-policy Deputy Governor. External members have four-year terms without reappointment. Indian Economic Service: MPC.

Instruments: distinguish rates, reserves and operations

InstrumentFunction
RepoCollateralised RBI lending to eligible banks; policy-rate anchor
SDFAbsorbs overnight liquidity without collateral; corridor floor
MSFOvernight standing borrowing window for eligible banks; corridor ceiling
LAF / corridorFramework for liquidity injection/absorption; SDF → repo → MSF
Reverse repoRBI absorbs liquidity against securities; fixed reverse repo is no longer the corridor floor
Bank RateReference rate aligned with MSF; also relevant to specified penalties
CRRRequired cash balances with RBI as a share of relevant liabilities
SLRRequired holdings of eligible liquid assets; distinct from CRR
OMOOutright securities purchase injects durable liquidity; sale absorbs it
Main / fine-tuning operationsVariable-rate liquidity operations manage mismatches; tenor and instrument depend on RBI's operating framework

SDF replaced fixed reverse repo as the corridor floor in April 2022; the source's list alone does not explain this change. RBI annual-report chronology.

9 April 2025 decision

The clipping anticipated a 25-basis-point cut. RBI's actual decision reduced repo to 6.00%, with SDF 5.75% and MSF/Bank Rate 6.25%. These are dated rates, not timeless definitions. RBI, 9 April 2025.
