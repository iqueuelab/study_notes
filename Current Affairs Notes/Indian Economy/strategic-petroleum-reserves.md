# Strategic petroleum reserves: capacity and emergency use

## Capacity versus stocks on hand
- **SPR** buffers crude-supply disruptions; it differs from ordinary refinery/commercial inventories and from LPG/LNG storage. Installed capacity does not establish the amount actually filled or immediately deliverable.

- **ISPRL**, established 2004, manages the reserves through the petroleum ministry's institutional framework. **July 2021 approval:** additional **6.5 MMT** commercial-cum-strategic capacity, **Chandikhol, Odisha 4 MMT + Padur, Karnataka 2.5 MMT**, via PPP; approval is not commissioned capacity. [Petroleum Ministry statement, 20 March 2025](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleaseIframePage.aspx?PRID=2113233&lang=2&reg=48).
- Source's **60-year concession** concerns the proposed commercial arrangement, not expiry of India's entire SPR system. Low-price filling in **April–May 2020** reportedly produced about **₹5,000 crore notional savings**, a historic cost comparison, not recurring revenue.
- **IEA minimum 90 days of previous-year net imports** and India's **Association since 2017/full-membership talks** are already covered in the IEA topic. India was not thereby subject to the same membership stockholding obligation. Stocks can include eligible accessible industry holdings; 90 days does not necessarily mean government-owned caverns alone. [IEA stockholding definitions](https://www.iea.org/data-and-statistics/data-tools/oil-stocks-of-iea-countries).
- The source's US **714 million barrels** confuses a historic design-capacity figure with current inventory; its China/Japan ranking lacks a comparable date and stock definition. Days of cover vary with actual filling, drawdown capability and the consumption/import denominator. The clipping reports **25 days of crude plus 25 days of petroleum products, roughly 50 days cumulatively**, explicitly excluding SPR crude. This is an attributed commercial-stock assessment with its own accounting assumptions, not 50 days in SPR caverns; LPG/LNG stocks were separately reported sufficient for about **two–three weeks**.
