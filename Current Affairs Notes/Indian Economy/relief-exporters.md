# RELIEF: export risk cover during West Asia disruption

## 19–20 March 2026: targeted export support
- **RELIEF = Resilience & Logistics Intervention for Export Facilitation**, approved under the **Export Promotion Mission**, with **₹497 crore** outlay. Gulf diversions, congestion and war-risk surcharges threaten shipments and export employment. **ECGC Ltd**, wholly government-owned under Commerce and Industry, implements claims, verification, disbursement and monitoring.
| Component | Eligible period and support |
| --- | --- |
| Existing ECGC-insured consignments | **14 February–15 March 2026**: enhanced risk cover up to **100%** |
| Upcoming consignments obtaining ECGC cover | **16 March–15 June 2026**: support up to **95%** risk cover |
| Eligible uninsured MSME consignments | **14 February–15 March**: reimbursement up to **50%** of eligible logistics-cost escalation, ceiling **₹50 lakh/exporter**, subject to documentation/conditions |
- Destinations/transshipment include UAE, Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, Israel, Qatar, Oman, Bahrain, Iraq, Iran and Yemen. The Steering Committee can review the intervention; reimbursement is distinct from automatic compensation for all losses. [Commerce Ministry, 19 March](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2242410&lang=1).
- The clipping allocates **₹56/159/282 crore** across components and excludes energy shipments from the second; retain these as source-reported operational details pending scheme guidelines. A proposed Indian **Protection & Indemnity club** concerns marine liability insurance and is separate from ECGC export credit cover.
