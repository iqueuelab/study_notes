UP-AGREES: agricultural productivity and rural enterprises

Editable notes

9 May 2025: Uttar Pradesh launch

  • Uttar Pradesh Agriculture Growth and Rural Enterprise Ecosystem Strengthening (UP-AGREES) launched by CM Yogi Adityanath during World Bank President Ajay Banga's Lucknow visit; inset identifies 28 Bundelkhand/Purvanchal districts. Aim: productivity, farmer incomes, agricultural diversification, digital/climate-resilient practices, finance, market linkages and rural enterprises.
  • Source's yield increase 10 → 14–15 quintals/acre is a stated ambition without a specified crop/baseline; not an achieved statewide yield improvement. Value addition, post-harvest facilities and enterprise opportunities can improve incomes if implementation succeeds.
  • World Bank approval, 12 December 2024: US$325.10 million IBRD loan, planned benefit to one million farmers, with intended US$15 million private-finance mobilisation. Final maturity 33.5 years, including 6-year grace period; a six-year implementation/grace figure is not a six-year loan maturity. Source's ₹2,737 crore figure is a rupee-equivalent report, not verified funding separately allocated to AI Pragya. World Bank approval.
  • UP is a major wheat, rice, potato, fruit/vegetable producer; smallholders face investment/technology and post-harvest gaps. Source's 75% cultivable land, 15% vegetables, 11% fruits, 23% foodgrains, 16–17% population, and blanket first-place list (wheat,potatoes,mangoes,guavas,peas,mushrooms,watermelons,honey) lack common reference year/definitions; retain as source-reported context rather than verified contemporary rankings. Inset's US$1-trillion UP economy is an aspiration, not achieved size.
