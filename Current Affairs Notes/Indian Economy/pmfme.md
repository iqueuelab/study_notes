# PMFME: formalising micro food-processing enterprises

## December 2025: micro-enterprise formalisation
- **Pradhan Mantri Formalisation of Micro Food Processing Enterprises (PMFME)**: MoFPI **Centrally Sponsored Scheme**, launched **29 June 2020**, **₹10,000 crore**, target credit-linked support for **2 lakh micro units**. Tackles fragmented informal production, limited skills/credit, weak branding, food-safety compliance and outdated machinery; supports SHGs, FPOs and cooperatives as well as entrepreneurs.
- Assistance: **35% eligible project cost, maximum ₹10 lakh per individual micro unit**; **35%, maximum ₹3 crore common infrastructure**; branding/marketing grant up to **50%**; training, DPR preparation, technical handholding, labs, storage, packaging and incubation facilities.
- **SHG seed capital is ₹40,000 per eligible member**, with an applicable aggregate ceiling; the source's standalone **₹4 lakh per SHG** shorthand omits the member basis. [MoFPI parliamentary annexure](https://www.mofpi.gov.in/sites/default/files/_annex_266_au2158_f92ekv.pdf).
- Funding: general States/UTs with legislature **60:40**, NE/Himalayan States **90:10**, other UTs **100% Central**. ODOP promotes district clusters, shared inputs and market links; source conditions support new units on the ODOP theme. Verify eligibility under the applicable revised guidelines before treating a historical restriction as timeless.
- Source period **2020–21 to 2024–25** is the original window; official 2025 material records continuation through **2025–26**. [PIB September 2025 brief](https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2025/sep/doc202592626401.pdf). The source's **74% unorganised** figure is an undated sector estimate; its October progress section supplies no auditable counts.
- **Mega Food Parks** provide common processing/logistics infrastructure and reduce perishable wastage. The supplied **2011 PYQ** key is **1 and 2 only (b)**, but published keys conflict (**b/d**); bank answer withheld pending original official-key verification. Do not treat the PDF’s rejection of emerging/eco-friendly technology as settled. Do not memorise its unsubstantiated blanket **30–40% fruit/vegetable waste** estimate. PMFME is distinct from the Central Sector **Kisan SAMPADA** umbrella; new Mega Food Park support had been discontinued while committed liabilities continued.
