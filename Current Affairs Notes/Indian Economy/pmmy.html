Indian Economy

Pradhan Mantri MUDRA Yojana

Editable notes

Ten years of PMMY: April 2025

  • Launched 8 April 2015, PMMY brings small entrepreneurs into formal finance through collateral-free loans for non-corporate micro/small businesses. It covers non-farm activities and eligible allied agricultural activities; it is not a crop-cultivation subsidy or pension scheme.
  • MUDRA = Micro Units Development and Refinance Agency Ltd. Supports/refinances last-mile lenders; does not directly lend to individual entrepreneurs. Loans are made by commercial banks, RRBs, small finance/cooperative banks, NBFCs and MFIs.

  • Correction: the original ₹10-lakh ceiling and three-category description are historical. Tarun Plus became effective 24 October 2024, so the April 2025 scheme already had four categories; not every new borrower automatically qualifies for ₹20 lakh. PIB anniversary account, 8 April 2025.
  • Inclusion focus: women, SC/ST/OBC, minority communities and new entrepreneurs. Access to credit should be assessed alongside business survival, repayment, income and jobs.
  • Source cites Labour Ministry survey estimate of 1.12 crore net additional employment during 2015–2018, including 69 lakh women (62%). This is an estimate for that historical survey period, not April 2025 employment creation or a count of unique current borrowers.

PYQ reasoning

  • 2016 prelims: correct answer (a), bringing small entrepreneurs into the formal financial system. The alternatives concern crop-specific farm loans, pensions and funding voluntary skill-development organisations; these do not describe PMMY’s central aim.
