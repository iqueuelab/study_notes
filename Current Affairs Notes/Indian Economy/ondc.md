# ONDC: interoperable digital commerce

## October 2025 activity reported in December
- **Open Network for Digital Commerce** seeks interoperable buyer/seller participation across applications using open network protocols; it is not simply another single closed marketplace.
- DPIIT year-end review: **326 million-plus cumulative orders through October 2025**; **18.2 million in October**, average **5.9 lakh-plus daily transactions**. These are order/transaction metrics, not unique customers, sellers or proof incumbent platforms no longer dominate. [Official review](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2201280&lang=2&reg=3).
- Inclusion needs discovery, dependable fulfilment, payments, grievance handling and seller capability as well as interoperability. Network openness does not automatically eliminate market power or implementation costs.
