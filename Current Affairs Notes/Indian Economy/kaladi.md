# Kaladi: Dogra dairy heritage, GI and value addition

## Udhampur dairy heritage and technology
- **Kaladi**, traditional Dogra milk product from **Udhampur, Jammu & Kashmir**, made from cow/buffalo milk; commonly pan-fried/grilled and eaten with bread. GI registered as **Udhampur Kaladi**, application **807**, certificate **3 October 2023**. [GI Registry](https://search.ipindia.gov.in/GIRPublicSearch/Application/Details/807).
- **21 January 2026:** Jitendra Singh directed **CSIR-CFTRI Mysuru and CSIR-IIIM Jammu** to work on nutrient profiling, processing/packaging, recipes and shelf-life extension under **ODOP**, preserving flavour, nutrition and stretchable texture. Traditional raw full-fat milk/whey-water coagulation and short unrefrigerated shelf life motivate food-safety validation; the instruction is not proof new products already commercialised. [Science Ministry](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2217025&lang=1&noshow=1&reg=3).
- GI identifies origin-linked quality/reputation under the **Geographical Indications of Goods (Registration and Protection) Act, 1999**, administered through Chennai Registry. Registration lasts **10 years, renewable**, not five/nonrenewable. Legal use requires the registered specification and producer/authorised-user framework; origin alone is not an unrestricted naming licence.
- GI safeguards traditional knowledge/reputation and can improve producer branding/livelihoods; cold chain, quality, market organisation and fair producer returns determine benefits. **2015 PYQ: Banaras brocades/sarees and Tirupathi Laddu (1 and 3)**, already banked. Kaladi's three-statement question is unyearred practice.
