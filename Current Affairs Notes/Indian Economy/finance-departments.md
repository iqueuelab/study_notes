# Revenue, DIPAM and disinvestment

## Ministry of Finance: who does what?

| Department | Core role |
| --- | --- |
| Revenue | Direct/indirect tax administration/policy; **CBDT** and **CBIC**; income tax, customs, Union GST/central excise remit |
| **DIPAM** | Government equity investment and disinvestment/public-asset management within assigned remit; strategic/minority stake sales and investor/capital-market interface |
| Public Enterprises | Public-enterprise policy/performance matters; distinct from DIPAM |

- State GST administration and all government non-equity assets are not automatically controlled solely by DoR/DIPAM.
- **Minority disinvestment**: sell a stake while retaining control. **Strategic disinvestment**: transfer substantial stake/management control as applicable. **Privatisation**: control transferred to private owners; not every stake sale is privatisation.
- Restructuring, efficiency, fiscal proceeds and public-interest valuation; consider competition, worker protection and transparency.
