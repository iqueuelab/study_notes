# RoDTEP: export tax remission and extension

## Export remission and September 2025 extension
- **RoDTEP = Remission of Duties and Taxes on Exported Products**, effective **1 January 2021**. Refunds embedded central/State/local taxes and levies not otherwise rebated; examples electricity duty, fuel taxes and eligible local levies. Objective: prevent export of domestic tax costs, improving competitiveness without an indiscriminate production subsidy.
- **Notification 35/2025–26, 30 September 2025:** extends scheme beyond 30 September through **31 March 2026**, covering **DTA, Advance Authorisation, SEZ and EOU exports** under notified conditions. This is the September extension, not an assurance beyond that date. [Official Gazette notification](https://egazette.gov.in/WriteReadData/2025/266667.pdf).
- Rebate usually a notified percentage of **FOB = Free on Board**, with applicable per-unit caps; PDF's “Freight on Board” expansion is wrong. Transferable **electronic duty-credit scrips** maintained through CBIC's electronic system can discharge eligible basic customs duty; not an unrestricted cash grant.
- **Department of Commerce/DGFT** notifies policy/rates; **Department of Revenue/CBIC** handles customs administration and the rate committee mechanism. Calling the whole scheme exclusively a Revenue ministry programme misses this division. [Commerce Ministry scheme explanation](https://www.commerce.gov.in/ministryofcommerce/node/856).
- **MEIS** faced adverse WTO panel findings as an export-contingent subsidy; India's appeal and the panel's status should be distinguished from a final unappealable WTO verdict. Remission design seeks WTO consistency; that is not a guarantee no specific rate can ever be disputed.
- Source **₹15,070 crore FY2023–24 allocation** is a historical budget figure, not FY2025–26 funding. Analyse fiscal cost, embedded-tax measurement, coverage/access, timely credits and export competitiveness alongside trade headwinds.
