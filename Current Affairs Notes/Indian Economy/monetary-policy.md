# Monetary Policy Committee and policy instruments

## MPC: statutory role
- **Section 45ZB, RBI Act 1934**, as amended in **2016**: Central Government constitutes a six-member MPC; its policy-rate decision binds RBI. Price stability is the primary objective while keeping growth in mind.
- **Three RBI members:** Governor (ex officio chair), Deputy Governor in charge of monetary policy, and an RBI officer nominated by the Central Board. **Three external members:** Central Government appointees with ability, integrity and expertise in economics/banking/finance/monetary policy.
- Majority voting; Governor has a casting vote on a tie. Quorum **four**, including Governor or, in the Governor's absence, the monetary-policy Deputy Governor. External members have **four-year terms without reappointment**. [Indian Economic Service: MPC](https://ies.gov.in/arthapedia/concept/monetary-policy-committee-mpc).

## Instruments: distinguish rates, reserves and operations
| Instrument | Function |
|---|---|
| Repo | Collateralised RBI lending to eligible banks; policy-rate anchor |
| SDF | Absorbs overnight liquidity **without collateral**; corridor floor |
| MSF | Overnight standing borrowing window for eligible banks; corridor ceiling |
| LAF / corridor | Framework for liquidity injection/absorption; SDF → repo → MSF |
| Reverse repo | RBI absorbs liquidity against securities; fixed reverse repo is no longer the corridor floor |
| Bank Rate | Reference rate aligned with MSF; also relevant to specified penalties |
| CRR | Required cash balances with RBI as a share of relevant liabilities |
| SLR | Required holdings of eligible liquid assets; distinct from CRR |
| OMO | Outright securities purchase injects durable liquidity; sale absorbs it |
| Main / fine-tuning operations | Variable-rate liquidity operations manage mismatches; tenor and instrument depend on RBI's operating framework |

SDF replaced fixed reverse repo as the corridor floor in **April 2022**; the source's list alone does not explain this change. [RBI annual-report chronology](https://www.rbi.org.in/scripts/AnnualReportPublications.aspx?Id=1384).

## 9 April 2025 decision
The clipping anticipated a 25-basis-point cut. RBI's actual decision reduced repo to **6.00%**, with **SDF 5.75%** and **MSF/Bank Rate 6.25%**. These are dated rates, not timeless definitions. [RBI, 9 April 2025](https://www.rbi.org.in/scripts/BS_PressReleaseDisplay.aspx?prid=60177).
