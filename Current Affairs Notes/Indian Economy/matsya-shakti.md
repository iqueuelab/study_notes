# Matsya Shakti and PM VIKAS: minority fisherfolk skills

## 28 August 2025 launch
- **Matsya Shakti**, inaugurated by Union Minister of State **George Kurian**, is jointly implemented by the **Ministry of Minority Affairs and ICAR–CMFRI**, under **Pradhan Mantri Virasat Ka Samvardhan (PM VIKAS)**. Source identifies the **Vizhinjam Regional Centre**, Kerala, and phased year-long training. [Official launch](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2161624&lang=2&reg=48).
- Focus: minority fishing-community entrepreneurship; **fish production, aquaculture and women’s leadership/life skills**. Seed production, modern technologies, processing/value addition and market skills can support income and sustainable livelihoods; training targets are not jobs/income already realised.
- **ICAR belongs to the Agriculture and Farmers Welfare framework**; fisheries-sector policy responsibility and PM VIKAS sponsorship are distinct institutional roles.
- **PM VIKAS** is a **Central Sector scheme, Ministry of Minority Affairs**, integrating **Seekho aur Kamao, USTTAD, Hamari Dharohar, Nai Roshni and Nai Manzil** for skills, heritage, leadership and education/entrepreneurship. Source's roughly **9-lakh** ambition for the 15th Finance Commission period through **2025–26** is programme-wide, not Matsya Shakti's enrolled headcount.
