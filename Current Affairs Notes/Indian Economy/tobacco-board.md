# Tobacco Board and FCV tobacco

## Institution
- **Tobacco Board Act, 1975**; Board came into existence **1 January 1976**; headquarters **Guntur, Andhra Pradesh**.
- Under **Department of Commerce, Ministry of Commerce and Industry**; regulates/helps develop production, marketing and exports, especially **Flue-Cured Virginia (FCV)** tobacco. [Board's institutional history](https://tobaccoboard.commerce.gov.in/bactivities.php).
## January 2025 source context
- Reports FCV farmers' earnings more than doubled between **2019-20 and 2023-24**; tobacco-production rankings cited (India second after China, fourth in FCV) require the reference year's dataset.
- FCV is widely used in cigarettes; link crop/export economics with public-health externalities rather than treating income growth as the only outcome.

## Commodity and observance recap
- Tobacco's smoking/smokeless use creates health externalities alongside grower/export income; source cultivation **0.45 million ha/~750 million kg** and second-place production/export rankings require their dataset years. Board functions include FCV production/marketing regulation, export promotion, quality/extension and remunerative-price support; an institutional role is not a guarantee every farmer receives a particular price.
- **World No Tobacco Day: 31 May**. WHO’s 1987 resolution led to the first global observance in 1988; do not equate resolution year with first 31 May observance. [WHO history](https://www.emro.who.int/index.php?Itemid=1517&id=495&layout=blog&option=com_content&view=category). The source's **1508** tobacco-arrival claim needs historical support; avoid attaching it to Akbar's period merely because seventeenth-century northern diffusion is discussed.
- Commodity-board headquarters PYQ recall: **Coffee—Bengaluru; Rubber—Kottayam; Tea—Kolkata; Tobacco—Guntur**. The source omits distractor answer codes, so the already complete 2008 common-bank version is retained.
