# Tobacco Board and FCV tobacco

## Institution
- **Tobacco Board Act, 1975**; Board came into existence **1 January 1976**; headquarters **Guntur, Andhra Pradesh**.
- Under **Department of Commerce, Ministry of Commerce and Industry**; regulates/helps develop production, marketing and exports, especially **Flue-Cured Virginia (FCV)** tobacco. [Board's institutional history](https://tobaccoboard.commerce.gov.in/bactivities.php).
## January 2025 source context
- Reports FCV farmers' earnings more than doubled between **2019-20 and 2023-24**; tobacco-production rankings cited (India second after China, fourth in FCV) require the reference year's dataset.
- FCV is widely used in cigarettes; link crop/export economics with public-health externalities rather than treating income growth as the only outcome.
