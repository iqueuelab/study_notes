# Byadagi chilli, colour and GI protection

## Crop and value chain
- Karnataka product associated with Byadagi/Haveri and surrounding growing/trading regions; GI certificate dated **27 January 2011**. [GI register](https://search.ipindia.gov.in/GIRPublicSearch/Application/Details/129).
- Wrinkled dried fruit, deep red colour and relatively low pungency. **Capsanthin contributes colour; capsaicinoids contribute heat**—do not interchange them.
- High extractable colour supports spice/oleoresin and food/cosmetic applications. **ASTA colour value** measures extractable colour, not spiciness.
- The source's numerical capsaicin range should not override the low-pungency character; cultivar, dry-weight basis and assay matter. Prices, area and turnover fluctuate.
- Well-drained loamy soils and suitable warmth/rainfall support cultivation; thrips, climate variability, storage and market volatility are key risks.
- GI protects the origin-linked identity; it does not guarantee every producer a higher price. Quality traceability, farmer organisation, suitable storage and processing improve realised value.
