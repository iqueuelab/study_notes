# PM-SURAJ: concessional credit for disadvantaged entrepreneurs

## FY 2024–25 credit results
- **PM-SURAJ—Pradhan Mantri Samajik Utthan evam Rozgar Adharit Jankalyan**, launched 2024 under Social Justice and Empowerment, provides a digital route to concessional livelihood/business credit for disadvantaged groups. **11 February 2026 Rajya Sabha reply**: **₹1,389.61 crore /1,39,467 beneficiary entrepreneurs in FY 2024–25**, exceeding the one-lakh beneficiary target. [Official reply](https://sansad.in/getFile/annex/270/AU1417_KaAkZu.pdf?source=pqars).
- Implementing corporations include **NSFDC, NSKFDC, NBCFDC**, with lending/channel partners; eligible **DNT/nomadic/semi-nomadic applicants** can access their schemes. Eligibility follows each corporation/product, rather than a blanket promise to every SC/ST/OBC applicant. Loans are repayable credit, not automatically grants.
- A single-window application/status route can reduce information gaps and support formal enterprise. The source's universal **₹15 lakh cap**, **only new businesses** and automatic DBT characterisation need product-specific rules; do not transplant Stand-Up India's greenfield restriction to the whole portal.
- **2016 MUDRA PYQ (a)**: bring small entrepreneurs into formal finance, already banked. The old **₹10 lakh maximum** appendix omits **Tarun Plus ₹10–20 lakh**, introduced 2024 for eligible borrowers who successfully repaid Tarun loans. Shishu ≤₹50,000, Kishore >₹50,000–5 lakh and Tarun >₹5–10 lakh remain categories.
- **2016 Stand-Up India PYQ (c), both**: SC/ST and women entrepreneurship plus SIDBI refinance. Original scheme loans **₹10 lakh–1 crore** for greenfield enterprises; Stand-Up India differs from Startup India's innovation ecosystem. Preserve the exam's historical scheme context when considering later lending-policy changes.
