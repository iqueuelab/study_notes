# Amrit Bharat Station Scheme: phased redevelopment

## Scheme and design
- **Ministry of Railways**, long-term station master plans and **phased redevelopment**. Official policy announced **27 December 2022**, correcting PDF's blanket **February 2023 launch**. Needs, passenger use, funds and priorities determine implementation. [Railways policy](https://www.pib.gov.in/Pressreleaseshare.aspx?PRID=1886884&lang=2&reg=3).
- Amenities: cleaner waiting areas/toilets, accessible facilities for persons with disabilities, food outlets, legible multilingual signage; improve circulation with entry/exit arrangements, parking, footpaths and roads.
- Intermodal links to buses/taxis/autos support last-mile travel; efficient lighting, rainwater harvesting and green spaces improve sustainability. Roof plazas/commercial/public spaces can connect stations with the city; these are long-term design elements, not identical requirements already finished at every station.
- The source links ballastless track with reduced noise/vibration; actual vibration/noise performance depends on design and mitigation, so do not treat slab track as a universal noise cure.

## West Bengal progress: May 2025 clipping
- **Kalyani Ghoshpara**, near **AIIMS Kalyani**, upgraded with passenger/safety amenities; clipping cites **over ₹3 crore** invested and redevelopment of stations in the **Sealdah Division**.
- Clipping inconsistently says Kalyani Ghoshpara “and 18 other stations” while also counting it among **18 selected Division stations**; retain the named station/region, not the contradictory subtotal.
- National **103-station inauguration on May 22** was **scheduled**, not already completed on May 18. Station works, opening ceremony and future master-plan phases are distinct milestones.
- Mains: accessibility and safety → circulation/intermodal connectivity → maintainable amenities and urban integration; test passenger outcomes, upkeep, costs and local needs rather than infer redevelopment always produces a thriving commercial city centre.
