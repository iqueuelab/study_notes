# Export Inspection Council and quality certification

## Institution
- **Statutory body under section 3, Export (Quality Control and Inspection) Act, 1963**; Department of Commerce, Ministry of Commerce and Industry; headquarters **Delhi**.
- Quality control, inspection and export certification help meet importing-country safety/quality requirements and support market access. [EIC](https://eicindia.gov.in/).
- Chairperson heads the Council; **Director of Inspection and Quality Control** is executive head.
- Regional **Export Inspection Agencies: Mumbai, Kolkata, Kochi, Delhi, Chennai**, with suboffices and accredited laboratories; distinguish agencies from the Council.
## Scope and January 2025 context
- Source reports a gap assessment of **food-testing infrastructure** for exports.
- Mandatory schemes apply to **notified products**, not all exports indiscriminately. Source examples: fish/fishery, dairy, honey, egg, meat/poultry, animal casings, gelatine, ossein/crushed bones and specified feed products.
- Voluntary certification serves other food/non-food products under applicable schemes/importer requirements.
- Mains: accredited testing, traceability, harmonised standards, capacity and faster credible certification reduce rejection and trade costs without weakening safety.
