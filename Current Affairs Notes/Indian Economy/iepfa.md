# IEPFA and recovery of unclaimed investor assets

## Authority and fund
- **Investor Education and Protection Fund Authority**, established 2016 under **Companies Act, 2013**, Ministry of Corporate Affairs; administers refunds and investor education.
- IEPF receives specified unpaid/unclaimed sums after the statutory **seven-year** period, including dividends, matured deposits/debentures and refundable application money; interest/grants are also fund receipts. Share transfer has its own conditions under section 124; do not apply “all assets idle sevenyears” indiscriminately.
- **1 August 2025:** Integrated Portal in **final testing**, planned to connect companies, depositories and **PFMS**, simplify claims/status access and improve data quality. Low-value documentation review and integrated call-centre support were announced; planned simplification is not an already universal instant-refund entitlement.
- Awareness initiatives: **Niveshak Didi**(women-led literacy), **Niveshak Panchayat**(community outreach), **Niveshak Shivir**(investor camps). [MCA announcement](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2151474&lang=2&reg=48).
