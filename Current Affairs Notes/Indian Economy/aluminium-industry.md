# Aluminium industry: production, energy and recycling

## Value chain and uses
**Bauxite → alumina refining → electrolytic smelting → primary aluminium → fabricated/alloy products; scrap → secondary aluminium.**
- India is a major primary producer, officially described as **second globally** in the source period. [Mines Ministry, August 2024](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2039988).
- **Hindalco (Aditya Birla Group), NALCO (PSU) and Vedanta** are key firms. Source's capacities **1.3/2 Mt**, **FY2022–23 ~4.1 Mt output** and **1.4 Mt exports** need matching company/product/year definitions; do not use its “Hindalco largest” statement as an unqualified present ranking.
- Products: ingots, sheets, foil, conductors and alloys; packaging, construction, aerospace/vehicles and electrical applications. EV lightweighting, enclosures and components can raise demand; aluminium is not necessarily the active battery material in every chemistry.
- Smelting is electricity-intensive; power cost/emissions, responsible bauxite mining, land/ecological safeguards and refining residues constrain growth. Scrap collection, segregation and quality control can reduce demand for primary production and energy.
- **National Mineral Policy 2019** is a general policy framework, not automatic approval for any mine. The source's dedicated **aluminium PLI** and blanket government power-subsidy claims lack verification; official 14-sector PLI list includes **specialty steel**, not a general aluminium PLI. [Official PLI review](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2114011&lang=1&reg=46).
## Bauxite: ore, landscape and dated statistics
- **Primary aluminium ore**, a mixture dominated by hydrated aluminium oxides/hydroxides; commonly residual/lateritic caps on hills/plateaux, with lower/coastal deposits including Gujarat/Goa. **Bayer process → alumina**, followed by smelting; ore is not already aluminium metal. [USGS ore account](https://www.usgs.gov/centers/national-minerals-information-center/bauxite-and-alumina-statistics-and-information).
- Demand links construction, transport, packaging and electronics. India's principal Eastern Ghats resources include **Odisha/Andhra Pradesh**; mining economics depend on grade, accessibility, permissions and environmental/community costs.
- Source's historical world-reserve total **~30 billion tonnes (2015)** and shares **Guinea 25%, Australia 20%, Vietnam 12%, Brazil 9%, Jamaica 7%, Indonesia 4%, China 3%** are an older series, not June 2025 inventory. Its production shares **Australia 29%, China 19%, Guinea 18%, Brazil 10%, India 7%** have no matching explicit year; reserve/production shares differ and should not be treated as current rankings.
- Source India share table labelled **2019**: Odisha **51%**, Andhra Pradesh **16%**, Gujarat **9%**, Jharkhand **6%**, Maharashtra **5%**, MP/Chhattisgarh **4% each**; it may conflate **resources with economically defined reserves**. Odisha production **71%** is source **2020** data. Keep reference year/metric rather than merge these with latest mine output.

## Rayagada mining dispute: June 5, 2025
- Embedded clipping reports police preventing **Medha Patkar and other activists** from entering/remaining in **Rayagada, Odisha**, for a World Environment Day public meeting about **Sijimali bauxite mining**. Officials invoked tension/order concerns; activists disputed the exclusion. These are reported positions, not a judicial finding that a project is lawful/unlawful or that protests caused violence.
- Mains: aluminium supply/value addition versus forest/water impacts, displacement, recognition of FRA rights and lawful consultation/clearances; independent impact assessment, fair compensation and transparent grievance redress support legitimate development.

## PYQ revision: Chhattisgarh
- **2008 answer (d): all four—bauxite, dolomite, iron ore, tin—occur naturally.** **Bailadila, Dantewada** is the key iron-ore recall; **Bastar** has tin occurrences. Do not infer Chhattisgarh is the only place with any natural tin occurrence from its tin-concentrate production role.
- Source's claim that dolomite is mainly in Bastar/Dantewada is an inadequately supported district ranking; natural occurrence, mine output and reserve dominance are separate tests.
