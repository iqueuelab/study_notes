# Anti-dumping, countervailing duties and safeguards

## Three distinct remedies
| Remedy | Trigger |
|---|---|
| Anti-dumping | Export below normal value, material injury and causal link |
| Countervailing | Actionable subsidy with injury and causal link |
| Safeguard | Import surge causing/threatening serious injury; unfair conduct not necessary |
- **Dumping is a relative price comparison**, not every cheap import, below-cost sale or proven predatory intention. Normal value and export price need comparable trade levels/adjustments.
- DGTR, Department of Commerce, investigates/recommends; **Finance Ministry/Department of Revenue notifies anti-dumping duty**. Recommendation is not automatic imposition. [DGTR](https://www.dgtr.gov.in/faq).
- Duty cannot exceed dumping margin; India's lesser-duty approach addresses injury where a lower rate suffices. There is no universal statutory 5–35% range.
- Normal expiry is five years unless continued through appropriate sunset review. Product, country/exporter and notification details govern coverage, not a ban on every import from the country.
- Safeguard measures can involve duties or quantitative arrangements; the practice sheet's blanket exclusion of tariff-rate quotas is unsafe given the applicable safeguard framework.
- Protect injured industry while assessing downstream input costs, consumers, competition and WTO compliance; these remedies are not a guaranteed solution to the overall trade deficit.

## March 2026: ethyl chloroformate investigation
- **DGTR initiated an anti-dumping investigation on 17 March 2026** into **ethyl chloroformate from China**, following **Paushak Limited's** application. It is an organic intermediate used in pharmaceuticals/agrochemicals. The sheet's tentative “Pusalk” name is incorrect. [Initiation notification](https://dgtr.gov.in/sites/default/files/2026-03/Intiation%20Notification%20English%20ECF.pdf).
- An allegation/initiation is **not a final finding of dumping/injury or an imposed duty**. Compare export price with normal value and establish material injury and causal link. DGTR investigates/recommends; Revenue/Finance decides notification under the applicable framework.
- Protecting injured producers must be assessed alongside downstream drug/agrochemical input costs. Cheap imports alone do not prove dumping; steel safeguard tariffs mentioned as context have a different legal test and product coverage.
