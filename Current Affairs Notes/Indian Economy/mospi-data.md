# MoSPI: statistical rebasing and new household surveys

## Plans announced in July 2025
- Issue schedules new **GDP base 2022–23** series for **27 February 2026**, replacing 2011–12; **IIP base 2022–23** planned from April 2026 and **CPI base 2024** planned earlyFY 2026–27, replacing 2012. These are **then-announced schedules**, not July implementation or guaranteed eventual launch dates.
- Rebasing updates weights, production/consumption structures and methods. It does not itself create real output or automatically lower inflation; old/new series need methodological reconciliation.
- **DTES (Domestic Tourism Expenditure Survey)** and **NHTS (National Household Travel Survey)** run **July 2025–June 2026**, NSS 80th round: tourism spending and travel purpose/modes/origin-destination patterns. NHTS is not confined to international tourism. [NSO announcement](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2137863&lang=2&reg=48).
- **Annual Survey of Service Sector Enterprises (ASSSE)** planned for January 2026 to improve formal-services evidence, analogous to industrial surveys. GST, e-Vahan, UPI, online prices and web-scraped data were being explored as administrative/digital inputs, not complete replacements for surveys.
## Institution and outputs
- **NSO, MoSPI** handles national accounts, CPI, IIP and major surveys; CSO/NSSO are predecessor organisational components, not unchanged standalone current departments. Ministry also monitors **MPLADS/Twenty Point Programme**.
- **National Statistical Commission** is an advisory body created by government resolution, not a constitutional commission. **ISI** is a statistics research/teaching institution; **eSankhyiki, EnviStats India, HCES, National Accounts Statistics** are useful portal/publication/survey links.

## December 2025: rebasing plans repeated
- Issue repeats previously announced schedules: **GDP 2022–23 base planned 27 February 2026; IIP 2022–23 base planned April 2026; CPI 2024 base planned early FY 2026–27**. Treat these as the source-period plans rather than already launched series or a statement of their eventual release dates.
- **DTES/NHTS July 2025–June 2026, ASSSE planned January 2026**, and exploratory GST/e-Vahan/UPI/online-price/web-scraping inputs repeat existing coverage. A new base does not create growth or automatically fix informal-sector measurement. Survey and administrative data need transparent methods and reconciliation.
- MoSPI/NSO national accounts, CPI, IIP; programme-monitoring, MPLADS/Twenty Point Programme; NSC advisory role, ISI research/teaching and eSankhyiki/EnviStats/HCES are retained in common notes. Do not portray predecessor CSO/NSSO as two unchanged independent current compilers.
