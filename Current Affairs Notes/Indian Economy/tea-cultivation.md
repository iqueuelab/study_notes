# Tea cultivation, Tea Board and export performance

## Crop and regions
- **Camellia sinensis**; origins in the China–northeast India–northern Myanmar region. **International Tea Day: 21 May**.
- Warm humid tropical/subtropical climate, approximately **20–30°C**, **150–300 cm well-distributed rain**, slightly acidic well-drained porous soils; frost/extreme heat are harmful. Management and cultivar affect these ranges.
- Assam: Brahmaputra/Assam Valley and Cachar; West Bengal: **Darjeeling, Dooars, Terai**; southern production: **Tamil Nadu, Kerala, Karnataka**, notably Nilgiris. India is a major producer/consumer and black-tea supplier; most output is consumed domestically.
- **Darjeeling Tea** was India's first GI-registered product; premium origin protection is distinct from a generic variety name.
## Institutions and 2024 exports
- **Tea Board**, statutory body under the **Tea Act 1953**, constituted **1954**, Commerce Ministry; HQ **Kolkata**. Functions include industry development, producer support and export promotion.
- Source/Akashvani March 2025 reporting places **2024 exports at 255 million kg**, about 10% above 231.69 million kg in 2023, and describes India as second-largest exporter after Kenya, surpassing Sri Lanka.
