# Venezuela: resource curse and physical geography

## December 2025: resource wealth without productive capacity
- **Resource curse/paradox of plenty:** dependence on one commodity can combine with weak institutions, rent-seeking, underinvestment and price shocks to undermine development. Venezuela illustrates this risk; it is not an inevitable law for every resource-rich country.
- Source **303 billion barrels proven reserves (2023)** contrasts with **0.921 million barrels/day production (2024)**. Much Orinoco oil is extra-heavy and needs diluents, specialised upgrading/refining, capital and technical skills. Reserves are an economic/technical category rather than the volume cheaply producible immediately.
- PDVSA underinvestment, loss of expertise/politicisation after the **2002 coup attempt and 2002–03 strike**, weak diversification and the **post-2014 oil-price downturn** are domestic/market factors. **US financial restrictions in 2017, PDVSA sanctions in 2019, partial easing in 2023 and subsequent tightening** are source-period background; do not attribute the whole crisis solely to sanctions.
- Source says oil-export share fell from **over 4% in the 1990s to around 0.35% in 2023**, output was 56% below its 1980s comparison, and debt/per-capita income remained strained. These are article-specific historical comparisons, not newly measured December totals; unnamed debt rankings and the absolute 'no other country' comparison are not established.
- Diversification, transparent fiscal management, institutional independence, skills/technology and resilience funds matter for India and other mineral exporters. Natural abundance creates opportunities; capability and governance determine results.
## Map recall
- Northern South America: **Caribbean and Atlantic coasts; Colombia west, Brazil south, Guyana east; capital Caracas**. Northwest Andes/Maracaibo basin, central seasonally flooded **Llanos**, southeast **Guiana Highlands/Shield**.
- **Orinoco drains to Atlantic; Rio Negro belongs to Amazon system**, linked across basins by the Casiquiare channel. **Lake Maracaibo** connects with Gulf of Venezuela; **Guri** is a hydroelectric reservoir. **Pico Bolívar** is highest peak; **Angel Falls** descends from a tepui in Guiana Highlands.
- PYQs already banked: **2024 Venezuela(d)**—large oil reserves do not establish recovery/stopped emigration; **2025 Andes(c), four listed countries: Bolivia, Colombia, Ecuador, Venezuela**, excluding Brazil/Paraguay.

- The resource-curse analysis, dated oil figures, PDVSA/sanctions background, physical map and **2024 Venezuela / 2025 Andes PYQs** repeat the December entry without a new measured series; retain that common explanation and coverage.

## January 2026: US intervention, sovereignty and the Monroe Doctrine
- Two editorials examine the **3 January US military action in Venezuela and capture of Nicolás Maduro**. The UN Secretary-General expressed concern about respect for international law and the precedent for interstate relations. Separate the operation's occurrence from disputed justifications and editorial interpretations of its motives. [UN Security Council account, 5 January](https://www.un.org/sg/en/content/sg/statements/2026-01-05/secretary-generals-remarks-the-security-council-venezuela).
- **UN Charter Article 2(4)** prohibits threat/use of force contrary to territorial integrity, political independence or UN purposes. **Article 51 self-defence** and **Security Council collective-security authorisation** are central exceptions; valid territorial-state consent is a distinct legal basis. A domestic criminal indictment does not itself authorise military action abroad. Anticipatory/pre-emptive self-defence and cross-border counterterrorism claims require careful legal scrutiny, not blanket acceptance.
- **Personal immunity (ratione personae)** protects certain incumbent senior officials before foreign national courts; immunity is procedural, not impunity. **ICJ Arrest Warrant judgment (2002)** directly concerned the DRC's **incumbent foreign minister**, while recognising the broader senior-office principle. It was not a judgment about Maduro. Recognition, office/effective control, jurisdiction, waiver and court type complicate application; the clipping's absolute prediction that US courts cannot try him should not be memorised as a case-specific ICJ ruling. [ICJ case](https://www.icj-cij.org/case/121).
- **Monroe Doctrine, 1823, President James Monroe:** opposition to renewed European colonisation/intervention in the Americas; later US interventions and sphere-of-influence policies are historical developments, not the complete original declaration. The editorials interpret renewed US primacy, China/Russia competition and oil access as possible motives; their “Donroe Doctrine” label is political commentary.
- Existing notes explain Venezuela's huge proven reserves and extra-heavy-oil constraints. The issue's **~17% reserves /~80% exports to China /24 regional BRI partners** are source-specific estimates with unclear matching years, not independently established January measurements. Alleged drug offences, resource motives and promised oil-sector liberalisation are claims/positions, not proof of every editorial conclusion.
- Mains: sovereignty, non-intervention, lawful accountability and collective diplomacy must coexist; alleged domestic authoritarianism alone does not authorise another state's unilateral use of force.
