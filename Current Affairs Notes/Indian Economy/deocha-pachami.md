# Deocha–Pachami: basalt, coal and land

## Project and trade-offs

- **Birbhum, West Bengal**; thick basalt overburden must be addressed to access coal. Basalt is volcanic igneous rock, useful in aggregates/construction.
- Reported coal 1, 240 million tonnes/basalt 675 million tonnes and 100–350 m basalt thickness are project estimates; ranking “Asia's second largest mine” confuses resource/block potential with operating output.
- Initial 326-acre work, proposed additional 50 acres, 71.5%revenue sharing and ₹5, 000 crore expected basalt revenue are source-era plans/contracts/forecasts.
- Land consent/acquisition, rehabilitation, environmental permissions, waste/air/water management and technical feasibility govern outcomes. Basalt permission does not automatically clear the entire coal project.
- State Investment Synergy Committee, chaired by Chief Secretary, coordinates approvals; political claims about jobs/elections are not measured project benefits.
