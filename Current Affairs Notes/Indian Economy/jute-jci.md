# Jute cultivation and Jute Corporation of India

## Crop and geography
- **Golden fibre**, natural bast fibre; warm humid conditions, roughly **25–35°C**, abundant rainfall **150–250 cm**, fertile well-drained alluvial soils. Ganga–Brahmaputra delta supports cultivation; retting separates fibres, requiring appropriate water/processing.
- **India** is a leading raw-jute producer; **Bangladesh** a major exporter. West Bengal dominates Indian cultivation, with **Bihar and Assam** important. Source's **99% three-state share /55% global share /75% Bangladesh versus 7% Indian exports /₹5,500-crore annual exports** lack consistent years/product coverage; do not equate raw-fibre, jute-products and government-purchase measures.
- Uses: sacking/gunny bags, yarn, rope, mats, carpets, crafts and geotextiles; value addition and diversified demand can support farmer/mill livelihoods.
## JCI and 2025–26 support
- **Jute Corporation of India, 1971**, Government enterprise under **Textiles Ministry**, headquarters **Kolkata**; nodal price-support procurement agency protecting growers from distress sales. **JCI implements MSP, it does not independently determine Cabinet MSP policy.** [JCI](https://jutecorp.in/).
- **22 January 2025 CCEA approval:** raw jute **TD-3 MSP ₹5,650/quintal for 2025–26**, **₹315** above ₹5,335 in 2024–25; corresponding grades differ. Price-support losses reimbursed by Centre. May clipping's “minimum selling price” wording should not replace **Minimum Support Price**. [Cabinet decision](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2095068).
- May article reports applicability from the crop season beginning **1 July**, asks growers to use procurement access instead of below-MSP distress sale, and describes **Jute Commissioner's ISRO collaboration/remote sensing** for soil, moisture, production and environmental data. This is not proof JCI itself has launched every listed satellite technology.
- Market support needs accessible centres, prompt payment, quality assessment and improved retting/productivity. Promotion/export/industry-modernisation roles across JCI, Jute Commissioner and other agencies should not all be assigned to JCI alone.
