Soybean: crop characteristics and international trade

Editable notes

January 2026: China–Brazil–US trade incentives

  • The report expects Brazil to dominate Chinese soybean purchases in the first half of 2026 because of lower landed prices, South American harvest availability and crusher margins. This is a forecast, not an already observed full-half-year outcome. Soybeans supply edible oil and protein meal for livestock/poultry, linking trade policy to food/feed security.
  • After the late-2025 tariff changes, US soybeans faced 13% Chinese import tariffs versus Brazil's 3%. USDA's contemporary update confirms that differential and resumed US purchases, illustrating how diplomatic purchases and private-commercial incentives may differ. USDA FAS, December 2025.
  • Source quotes Brazil about US$507/t versus US Gulf US$517/t and a US$31–108m possible premium on 12m tonnes. These are shipment/timing-dependent estimates, not a universal price spread. Seasonal Southern Hemisphere supply versus the US autumn harvest can shift relative competitiveness; private crushers, Sinograin and COFCO have different procurement roles.

Crop recall

  • Glycine max, family Fabaceae/Leguminosae; domesticated in East Asia, mainly a kharif oilseed in India, also used as green vegetable and protein food/feed. Warm, moist conditions and fertile well-drained soils favour it; excess waterlogging/frost damage crops. The supplied 600–650mm rainfall /26–30°C are broad teaching ranges, not strict boundaries for all varieties/locations.
  • Symbiotic root-nodule bacteria, especially Bradyrhizobium, fix atmospheric nitrogen; effective nodulation and crop rotation can support soil fertility. Seed contains roughly 40–42% protein /19–21% oil, varying by cultivar and conditions. Soy products are lactose/cholesterol-free plant foods, but the source's broad disease-prevention/hormonal claims should not be treated as assured medical outcomes. ICAR-hosted crop study.
  • Main Indian belt: Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra and Rajasthan, with cultivation also in Karnataka/Gujarat and other states. The infographic explicitly uses March 2024 data, so its MP-first ranking is historical, not a timeless rule. Brazil, US, Argentina, China and India are major global producers; Brazil is a major exporter, China the principal import market.
  • 2024 PYQ: all three (c)—groundnut, horse-gram and soybean are Fabaceae despite different agricultural uses. 2022 PYQ: 1,3,4 (a)—alfalfa, chickpea and clover are nitrogen fixers; amaranth, purslane and spinach are not the listed symbiotic nitrogen-fixing legumes. The 2022 question is already banked.
