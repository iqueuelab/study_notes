# Decarbonising India’s logistics sector

## Why green logistics matters
- Freight transport, storage and distribution connect producers with markets. Cleaner logistics supports competitiveness, energy security, urban air quality and India's **net-zero 2070** ambition.
- Road dependence and diesel trucks, energy-intensive warehouses and difficult-to-electrify aviation are major challenges. Rail and water transport can lower emissions per tonne-kilometre where routes, load factors and first/last-mile connections are suitable.
- The source reports logistics at **13.5% of national GHG emissions**, road transport at **88% of that**, and trucks at **38% of CO₂ emissions**; these have different or unspecified denominators. Treat them as source estimates requiring dataset/year verification rather than comparable shares. The source also reports roads handling about **70% of freight** and **90% of passenger movement**; passenger and freight shares are distinct metrics. Its 2022 modal-split image reinforces road dominance, with rail next and shipping/air much smaller.
## Measures and policy links
| Lever | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Multimodal networks | Connect rail freight, coastal shipping and inland waterways with efficient terminals and last-mile roads |
| Trucking | EV charging, cleaner fleets, route optimisation and consolidation of loads |
| Warehouses | Energy audits, efficient lighting/cooling, rooftop renewables and appropriate green-building ratings such as GRIHA |
| Shipping/aviation | Research sustainable fuels; assess lifecycle emissions, fuel availability and safety |
- **PM Gati Shakti** supports integrated infrastructure planning; **National Logistics Policy** supports efficient logistics systems and coordination. [Government logistics overview](https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2025/feb/doc202521495001.pdf).
- The source's Delhi–Jaipur overhead-electric-highway example and tripling waterborne cargo by 2030 are proposals/source-reported targets; implementation and the cargo baseline require verification.
- LNG, biofuels, hydrogen and ammonia differ in lifecycle carbon impact and hazards; LNG remains a fossil fuel. Solar assistance may reduce vessel energy demand but does not automatically power all ship operations.
## International benchmark and implementation
- **IMO 2023 strategy:** net-zero international-shipping GHG emissions **by or around 2050**; indicative reductions against 2008 of **at least 20%, striving for 30%, by 2030** and **at least 70%, striving for 80%, by 2040**. The source's 50%-by-2050 figure belongs to the older ambition. [IMO strategy](https://www.imo.org/en/OurWork/Environment/Pages/2023-IMO-Strategy-on-Reduction-of-GHG-Emissions-from-Ships.aspx).
- Rail use in China/US illustrates modal choice; the source's China 50% share needs a comparable metric before adoption.
- Constraints: capital costs, charging/fuel networks, reliability, technology maturity and inter-ministry coordination. Use PPPs, targeted incentives, green finance and R&D, while tracking actual emissions per tonne-kilometre and total emissions.
- **Efficient logistics → less empty running and energy use → lower costs and emissions**, provided growing freight demand does not outweigh efficiency gains.
