# Seed regulation, quality and farmers’ rights

## November 2025: Draft Seeds Bill
- **Draft Seeds Bill 2025** opened for pre-legislative consultation, seeking modern regulation of seeds/planting material and replacement of the **Seeds Act 1966**. The **Seeds (Control) Order 1983** is existing dealer-control background; draft proposals are not enacted obligations. [Agriculture Ministry authority's consultation notice](https://plantauthority.gov.in/news/pre-legislative-consultation-seeds-bill-2025).
- Source highlights State registration of dealers, quality parameters (**germination, genetic/physical purity, seed health and performance information**), stronger action against spurious seed, easier imports and decriminalisation of minor offences while retaining penalties for serious violations. Distinguish mandatory quality/registration from a claim that every seed must use one identical certification route.
- Industry welcomes investment, R&D, efficient supply and access to improved varieties; farmer organisations seek clarity on affordability, compensation for failure, accountability, corporate concentration, domestic biodiversity and farmer seed rights. Seed quality regulation, GM biosafety approval and plant-variety/farmers' rights legislation are separate legal layers; existing law does not leave hybrids/private seeds wholly unregulated.
- The source calls this a third attempt after 2004/2019 and says both were withdrawn after protests; legislative histories differ, so retain those as earlier reform attempts without asserting identical parliamentary status. Compensation mechanisms and details must be checked against draft text rather than assuming every reported aim is an enforceable remedy.
- **Seed replacement rate:** share of cropped area using fresh certified/quality seed rather than farm-saved seed; distinguish seed replacement from replacement of an old variety by a new variety. **National Seeds Policy 2002 exists**, and private seed companies participate, especially vegetables/hybrids; low-value, high-volume seed supply gaps constrain wider replacement.
- **Correct PYQ year: 2014, not the source's 2017**. Official paper Q94 has the three statements and answer **3 only (b)**; the official 2014 scan confirms its complete options.
