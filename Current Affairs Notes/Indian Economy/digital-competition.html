Indian Economy

Data, platform dominance and competition regulation

Editable notes

WhatsApp–Meta case

  • CCI's 18 November 2024 order imposed ₹213.14 crore for abuse of dominance connected with WhatsApp's 2021 privacy update and mandatory cross-Meta data sharing. Relevant markets included smartphone OTT messaging and online display advertising. CCI order account.
  • January 2025 appellate relief stayed the five-year advertising-data-sharing restriction and required a 50% penalty deposit. Interim relief is not a final finding that all conduct was lawful.
  • Later, 4 November 2025, NCLAT upheld the major findings/penalty while setting aside the fixed five-year ban; user-choice remedies remained significant. CCI appellate summary.

Why digital markets differ

  • Users → data → service/ad targeting → more users can reinforce network effects, switching costs and entry barriers. Zero monetary price does not mean zero consumer harm.
  • Dominance is not itself prohibited; abuse is. Market definition, conduct, effects and remedies must be established, not inferred from size alone.
  • Data is reusable but not legally usable without limit; privacy, consent and security requirements constrain collection/use. Competition and data-protection law are complementary.
  • Consider interoperability, portability, transparent choices and proportionate behavioural/structural remedies. Mandatory data sharing can itself threaten privacy; design safeguards and regulator coordination.
  • Foreign cases are jurisdiction-specific; the US AT&T breakup created seven regional holding companies, not a simplistic divestment into “22 new companies”.
