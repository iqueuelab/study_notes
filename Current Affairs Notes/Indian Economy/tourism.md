# Tourism: infrastructure, arrivals and sustainable growth

## Infrastructure programme
- FY **2024–25**: **40 projects in 23 States, ₹3,295.76 crore**, approved under **SASCI—Development of Iconic Tourist Centres to Global Scale**. Approval differs from completion. [Tourism Ministry](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleaseIframePage.aspx?PRID=2099157&lang=2&reg=3).
- Special capital assistance includes long-term interest-free State loans; tourism selection considers connectivity, carrying capacity, utilities, sustainable management and local value.
- **Swadesh Darshan 2.0**: sustainable destinations; **PRASHAD**: pilgrimage/heritage infrastructure; **RCS-UDAN**: regional connectivity. Travel for LiFE promotes responsible behaviour; skill programmes support hospitality/local enterprise.
- Adopt a Heritage amenities partnerships do not transfer monument ownership. A **draft National Tourism Policy** is not automatically a final enacted policy.
## Read statistics correctly
- **TTDI 2024: India 39th**, WEF. **2023 international tourist arrivals 18.89 million** include **9.52 million foreign tourist arrivals plus NRI arrivals**; the two totals are not synonyms.
- GDP estimates depend on direct versus total tourism contributions and methodology; avoid equating a single industry estimate with the official satellite-account measure.
- Tourism creates jobs, foreign exchange and local demand, but seasonal overcrowding, safety, scams, waste, water pressure and habitat damage can undermine benefits.
- Develop lesser-known destinations within carrying capacity; improve transport/accessibility, reliable sanitation, local skills, fair community benefits and outcome-based maintenance.
