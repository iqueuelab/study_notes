# Tourism: infrastructure, arrivals and sustainable growth

## Infrastructure programme
- FY **2024–25**: **40 projects in 23 States, ₹3,295.76 crore**, approved under **SASCI—Development of Iconic Tourist Centres to Global Scale**. Approval differs from completion. [Tourism Ministry](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleaseIframePage.aspx?PRID=2099157&lang=2&reg=3).
- Special capital assistance includes long-term interest-free State loans; tourism selection considers connectivity, carrying capacity, utilities, sustainable management and local value.
- **Swadesh Darshan 2.0**: sustainable destinations; **PRASHAD**: pilgrimage/heritage infrastructure; **RCS-UDAN**: regional connectivity. Travel for LiFE promotes responsible behaviour; skill programmes support hospitality/local enterprise.
- Adopt a Heritage amenities partnerships do not transfer monument ownership. A **draft National Tourism Policy** is not automatically a final enacted policy.
## Read statistics correctly
- **TTDI 2024: India 39th**, WEF. **2023 international tourist arrivals 18.89 million** include **9.52 million foreign tourist arrivals plus NRI arrivals**; the two totals are not synonyms.
- GDP estimates depend on direct versus total tourism contributions and methodology; avoid equating a single industry estimate with the official satellite-account measure.
- Tourism creates jobs, foreign exchange and local demand, but seasonal overcrowding, safety, scams, waste, water pressure and habitat damage can undermine benefits.
- Develop lesser-known destinations within carrying capacity; improve transport/accessibility, reliable sanitation, local skills, fair community benefits and outcome-based maintenance.

## January 2026: from attractions to visitor experience
- Editorial's **three-I framework**: **image** (safety, sanitation, scams and trust); **infrastructure** (last-mile transport, toilets, signage, connectivity, museums and maintenance); **institutional/service behaviour** (immigration, crowd management, guides and hospitality). Branding cannot compensate for a poor end-to-end trip.
- Segment promotion around spiritual/cultural circuits, adventure, luxury and regional experiences; link digital storytelling to reliable services. Proposed improvements include tourist police with women officers, multilingual guides, verified information, hospitality/homestay training and predictable e-visa/immigration procedures.
- PPP amenities and Adopt a Heritage can support maintenance; assess destination outcomes rather than only built assets. Regulate footfalls, protect ecology/culture and share benefits with local communities through responsible tourism.
- The source's Singapore/Thailand comparisons and **40% trained-staff shortfall** lack a consistent year/denominator here; use them as the author's concern, not verified current national statistics. Foreign tourist arrivals, NRI-inclusive international arrivals and tourism receipts are distinct measures.

## February 2026: PRASHAD and pilgrimage infrastructure
- **Pilgrimage Rejuvenation and Spiritual Heritage Augmentation Drive (PRASHAD)**: Tourism Ministry **Central Sector scheme, 2014–15**, providing assistance for integrated pilgrimage/spiritual heritage infrastructure through State/UT implementing arrangements. [Scheme guidelines](https://tourism.gov.in/sites/default/files/2023-08/PRASHAD%2015th%20FC%20Scheme%20Guidelines_0.pdf).
- Andhra Pradesh temple-destination plans highlight drinking water, sanitation, approach/accessibility, lighting/signage and pilgrim facilitation. Central assistance, project sanction, expenditure and completed facilities are separate milestones; 100% eligible public-infrastructure funding is not a promise to pay every tourism-related private cost.
- Mission Directorate coordination, State/UT execution and maintenance determine outcomes; PPP/CSR may supplement eligible development. Jobs, local craft sales and cultural preservation need carrying-capacity, waste management and community benefits. PRASHAD infrastructure is distinct from e-visas, tourist helplines and Dekho Apna Desh promotion; the NDA question stays outside CSE banks.
