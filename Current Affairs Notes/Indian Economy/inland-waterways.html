IWAI and inland-waterway infrastructure

Editable notes

Institution and routes

  • Inland Waterways Authority of India Act 1985; IWAI established 27 October 1986, HQ Noida, Ports, Shipping and Waterways Ministry. Develops/maintains navigation infrastructure on national waterways.
  • 23 January 2025, Varanasi sub-office upgraded to Regional Office for NW-1, Ganga and Uttar Pradesh waterways; sixth Regional Office alongside Guwahati, Patna, Kochi, Bhubaneswar, Kolkata in source snapshot. Ministry release.
  • Source jurisdiction: Betwa, Chambal, Gomti, Tons, Varuna, parts of Gandak, Ghaghra, Karamnasa, Yamuna, coordination with UP State IWT Authority. Naming a river ≠ every reach already commercially navigable.
  • Benefit depends on fairway depth, terminals, last-mile freight, seasonal flows and ecological safeguards; inland shipping is not automatically feasible on every tributary.
