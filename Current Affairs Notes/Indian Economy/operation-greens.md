# Operation Greens

## Purpose and scope

- Launched **2018** by the **Ministry of Food Processing Industries**: improve producer returns, stabilise prices for producers/consumers, reduce post-harvest losses and build processing capacity.
- Initially **TOP = Tomato, Onion, Potato**. Budget 2021-22 expanded long-term coverage to **22 perishables, including shrimp**. [MoFPI/PIB](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleaseIframePage.aspx?PRID=1744337&lang=2&reg=48).
- Supports value-chain infrastructure, farmer-producer organisations, storage, transport, processing and cluster-to-market links; short-term support and long-term projects should be distinguished.

## Parliamentary review reported in January 2025

- Source reports **34% of Rs 59.44 crore** allocated for 2024-25 spent by October 2024, alongside Maharashtra onion-price falls and potato shortages. Preserve the reporting period; do not present these as current figures.
- Bottlenecks: unused funds, inadequate cold chains, stakeholder coordination, export-policy uncertainty and weather/supply shocks.

## Mains framework

- Producer income + consumer affordability require predictable trade decisions, timely funding, market intelligence, efficient logistics and accountable implementation.
