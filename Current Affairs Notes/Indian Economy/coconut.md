# Coconut economy and circular value chains

## Crop, institutions and observance
- Coconut is a tropical plantation crop supporting coastal livelihoods; **Kerala, Karnataka and Tamil Nadu** are major producing states. The compilation's **20,535.88 million nuts** is a **2022–23 production figure**, not a current annual estimate.
- **Coconut Development Board:** statutory body established **12 January 1981**, headquartered at **Kochi**, under the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare; promotes cultivation, productivity, processing and value addition.
- **International Coconut Community:** intergovernmental organisation formed under UNESCAP's auspices in **1969**; India is a founding member. It is not itself a UN agency.
- **World Coconut Day: 2 September**, observed since **2009**. The **2024 theme** emphasised circular use and partnerships to maximise value.

## Whole-coconut value chain
| Material | Useful products |
|---|---|
| Water and flesh | Beverages, food, milk and processed products |
| Kernel | Oil and value-added foods/cosmetics |
| Husk | Coir fibre and coir-pith products |
| Shell | Handicrafts, charcoal and activated-carbon products |

- Circular processing creates employment and export opportunities while reducing discarded material. Replanting, resilient varieties, irrigation matched to local conditions, pest control, credit, producer aggregation, quality certification and processing research strengthen the sector.
- Risks: drought, erratic rainfall, rising temperatures, ageing palms and post-harvest losses. Water needs and carbon benefits depend on cultivation and processing conditions; coconut is not universally a low-water crop.
- **Health correction:** coconut water can provide fluids and electrolytes; coconut products are not proven cures for diabetes or heart disease. Coconut oil is rich in saturated fat; WHO prefers unsaturated fats to saturated fats.

## July 2025 oil-price pressure
- Source reports retail coconut oil near**₹460/kg**; a local/date-specific price, not a fixed national quotation. **Copra**, dried kernel, yields oil used in cooking, soaps, cosmetics and hair products. Source estimates **5.7 lakh tonnes/year**, **3.9 lakh tonnes edible**, without a clear reference year.
- Supply pressures: El Niño/drought in Philippines/Indonesia, ageing palms, long harvest cycles, possible raw-coconut export restrictions and diversion to **coconut methyl ester(CME) biodiesel**. Biofuel and food demand compete for feedstock.
- Source's “third-largest(2023)” and“31.45% of global output(FY 22)” mix periods/measurement; retain year/unit distinctions rather than derive a current rank from them. India/Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, Kerala are major production centres; acreage, productivity and total output are different measures.
- The Lakshadweep productivity/oil-content question is **CDS 2024**, excluded from CSE PYQ banks; the unsupported“highest productivity in India” superlative needs year-specific CDB comparison.
