# Banana cultivation and exports

## Cultivation
- Tropical crop; source growing conditions: **15-35°C**, relative humidity **75-85%**, deep fertile well-drained loam and pH **6.5-7.5**. Suitability varies by cultivar/local conditions.
- Varieties: **Dwarf Cavendish, Robusta, Monthan, Poovan, Nendran**.
- Major producing states named: **Andhra Pradesh, Maharashtra, Gujarat**.
## Export context
- Source attributes to **APEDA** a tenfold export increase over the preceding decade and **$1 billion ambition over the next five years**; this is a January 2025 reported target, not achieved exports.
- Supply-chain focus: quality, handling, cold-chain/transport, reliable markets and farmer value realisation.
