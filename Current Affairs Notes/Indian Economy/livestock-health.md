# Livestock Health and Disease Control Programme

## Revision

- Source 2024–25/2025–26 outlay ₹3, 880 crore, including ₹75 crore **Pashu Aushadhi** component for affordable generic veterinary medicines.
- Vaccination, surveillance and Mobile Veterinary Units; medicine distribution via eligible PM-Kisan Samriddhi Kendras/cooperatives.
- FMD, brucellosis, PPR and **CSF=Classical Swine Fever**, not cerebrospinal fluid; lumpy skin disease is another animal-health concern. [Animal Husbandry portal](https://pashuaushadhi.dahd.gov.in/).
- Healthy livestock protects income/food supply; One Health links animal/human/environment surveillance, but not every livestock disease is zoonotic.
