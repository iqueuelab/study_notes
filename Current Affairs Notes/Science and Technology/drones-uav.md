# Drones and Indian UAV programmes

## Uses and regulation
- UAVs support surveillance, mapping, agriculture, infrastructure inspection, deliveries and disaster response; FPV means the operator sees a camera feed, not necessarily autonomy or permission for beyond-visual-line-of-sight flight.
- **Drone Rules 2021**, amended in 2022: remote pilot certificate terminology. Exemptions cover **nano**, and **micro for non-commercial use**, not every micro flight. [2021 rules summary](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=1749154&lang=2&reg=48).
- Green-zone flights normally need no prior airspace permission within conditions; yellow/red zones require relevant permissions. Check the applicable map and operational restrictions; military systems have a distinct regulatory scope.
- **₹120 crore drone/component PLI**, three-year scheme; **Drone Shakti** announced in Budget 2022 encourages drone services. Neither announcement grants universal flight permission.
## Programme distinctions
| Name | Correct category |

| Lakshya | Pilotless aerial target; not a cruise-missile strike system |
| SWiFT | Autonomous flying-wing technology demonstrator; not an anti-tank missile |
| CATS | **Combat Air Teaming System**, manned–unmanned teaming concept |
| Rustom / TAPAS | UAV surveillance programmes; TAPAS is a MALE platform, not a light combat aircraft |
| Nishant | Tactical surveillance UAV |
| Netra UAV | Small quadrotor; distinct from manned **Netra AEW&C** on EMB-145 |

- The source classification table mislabels multiple programmes. [DRDO ABHYAS](https://drdo.gov.in/drdo/en/offerings/products/high-speed-expendable-aerial-target-abhyas).
- Benefits need indigenous components, skilled operators and viable services; manage collision, privacy, cyber security and misuse risks with proportionate rules and counter-UAS capabilities.
## 10 June 2025: evolving warfare
- Source examples: **Operation Sindoor**, Ukraine's **Operation Spider Web**, Azerbaijan's loitering-munition warfare and Myanmar resistance's improvised/3 D-printed drones. They illustrate surveillance/strike, low-cost adaptation and reach; drones do not by themselves explain victory or eliminate personnel/civilian risk.
- Low-altitude flights can exploit radar coverage gaps; multiple cheap drones can saturate defences. **Autonomy/AI navigation, electronic-warfare resistance and coordinated swarming are capabilities of selected systems**, not every commercial UAV. Jamming/spoofing, kinetic interception, weather, endurance/payload and communications remain constraints.
- India needs affordable manufacture and resilient components, training/logistics and layered detection/soft-kill/hard-kill counter-UAS for military and lawful civilian protection. 3 D printing accelerates some airframe/part manufacture but does not print every sensor/engine/control system.
- Infographic examples **Heron TP, MQ-9, Harpy, Nagastra-1** have different roles; source Rustom-II weapon carriage and **AURA 3,000-km/5–6-hour** specifications are not verified operational capability. Compare configuration and demonstrated integration, not a generic “all drones armed” list.
- Regulatory weight bands: **nano ≤250 g; micro >250 g–2 kg; small >2–25 kg; medium >25–150 kg; large >150 kg**. Source incorrectly labels small/medium; civil Drone Rules permissions are distinct from defence use. [Drone Rules 2021](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=1749154&lang=2&reg=48).
- **2020 drone applications PYQ: all three (d)**, spraying, volcano inspection and whale breath sampling. **2025 UAV PYQ: none (d)** of universal vertical-landing, hovering and battery-only claims. Fixed-wing, rotary, hybrid VTOL and fuel-powered architectures differ; hovering is not exclusive to multirotors.

## Reported MALE procurement proposal
- July 10 clipping reports a proposed **87 locally manufactured MALE UAVs**, about **₹20,000 crore**, with a **minimum 60% indigenous-content requirement**; trials had assessed proposed designs and final clearance was pending. These are procurement plans, not delivered aircraft.
- Stated requirement: **over 30 hours endurance and at least 35,000 ft**, all-weather intelligence/surveillance/reconnaissance and combat capability. Generic MALE altitude bands are descriptive, not a universal 30,000-ft ceiling.
- Examples: **TAPAS BH-201/Rustom-II (DRDO)**; **Hermes 900/Drishti 10**; armed **Bayraktar TB2**. Performance varies by payload/configuration. Remote operation removes the onboard pilot's exposure but does not eliminate civilian or ground-personnel risk.

## ULPGM-V 3 flight trial
- **25 July 2025**, DRDO tested **UAV-Launched Precision Guided Missile-V 3** at **National Open Area Range, Kurnool, Andhra Pradesh**; enhanced from V 2. This is a munition carried/launched by UAV, not a new UAV airframe.
- Official release: **dual-channel high-definition seeker**, day/night and plain/high-altitude operation; **two-way data link permits post-launch target/aim-point updates**. Modular warheads: **anti-armour (including ERA-protected targets), penetration-cum-blast/anti-bunker, pre-fragmentation**.
- Indigenous UAV integration and partners **Adani Defence, BDL, MSMEs/startups/Dc PPs** strengthen precision-strike production. Source's **4 km day/2.5 km night,12.5 kg, dual-thrust propulsion** are reported specifications, not substantiated by this official trial release; do not substitute them for verified V 3 performance. [DRDO/PIB trial](https://www.pib.gov.in/Press Release Page.aspx?PRID=2148320&lang=2&reg=48).
- Existing PYQs:2020 drone applications all three;2025 universal VTOL/hovering/battery-only claims none. **Hovering is not exclusive to multirotors**; some other rotorcraft/VTOL designs can hover.
