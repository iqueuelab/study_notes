# Electric freight locomotives and Dahod manufacturing

## 26 May 2025: Dahod milestone
- PM inaugurated Dahod locomotive manufacturing plant/flagged off the first **9,000 HP** locomotive, Siemens technology partnership. Source names **D9**, and reports~90% technology made in India and component facilities at Nashik, Aurangabad/Mumbai; technology sourcing is not automatically audited local-value content. [PM event](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2131273&lang=2&reg=48).
- Graphic programme targets: **1,200 locomotives over 11 years; 9,000 HP; 120 km/h maximum speed; 4,500 tonne haulage illustration; 35 years maintenance**. Targets/design ratings do not mean all delivered or routine operation at maximum speed; load depends on route/gradient and operating conditions.
- **“India's most powerful electric freight engine” is false unqualified:** **12,000 HP Madhepura locomotives** operated commercially from 18 May 2020. D9 is a powerful six-axle category; distinguish from double-section WAG-12B. [Railways 2020 announcement](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=1625158&lang=2&reg=48).
- Benefits: freight capacity/productivity, domestic manufacturing/employment, predictive maintenance and potential shift from road to lower-emission rail. Electrification reduces direct exhaust; total emissions depend on electricity mix. National Rail Plan freight-share ambition **45%** is a target, not achieved share; PM Gati Shakti promotes integrated logistics.
