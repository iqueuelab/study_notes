# Bharat Forecast System and local weather prediction

## Launch and resolution
- The May 26 issue previews launch “today”; MoES later confirmed **official launch 26 May 2025**. **Bharat Forecast System (BharatFS)**, developed by **IITM Pune under Ministry of Earth Sciences**, supports real-time numerical weather prediction. [MoES Parliamentary reply, 31 July](https://www.moes.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/08/d5741b4be405d5b111e04ea32d760b08.pdf).
- **6 km horizontal grid**, versus previous **GFS T1534 ~12 km**; **Triangular Cubic Octahedral (TCo)** dynamical grid improves terrain representation/filtering/conservation. Forecast cells can serve village/panchayat **clusters**, not a guarantee of an independently exact forecast for every household.
- “Highest resolution globally” concerns the announced **global operational model** comparison, not all regional/weather research models—some regional grids are finer than 6 km. More grid points do not by themselves ensure every small storm/fog event is correctly predicted.
## Infrastructure and use
- Source says **Arka installed 2023**: correction—**Arka at IITM Pune and Arunika at NCMRWF Noida were inaugurated September 2024**. IITM facility **11.77 petaflops**, NCMRWF **8.24**; HPC supports intensive modelling/data assimilation. [MoES inauguration](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2059397&lang=2&reg=48).
- Forecasting combines observations/satellite and real-time inputs, assimilation, atmospheric models and evaluation. Uses: local rainfall/temperature/wind advisories, crop/irrigation/harvest planning, reservoir management, transport/health preparedness and extreme-event warning.
- Cyclone/heavy rain/thunderstorm prediction supports flood/landslide risk assessment; a weather model is not itself a complete flood or landslide model. Last-mile alerts, local response plans, uncertainty communication and verification turn improved forecasts into safety benefits.
## May 27 clipping: forecast horizons
- The embedded report says improvements are largely visible in **short/medium-range rainfall forecasts (3–7 days)**; they do not automatically change month-ahead seasonal forecasts or ensure prediction of every sudden severe thunderstorm. Radar observations and specialised models remain important. Existing common notes cover launch, resolution and infrastructure.
