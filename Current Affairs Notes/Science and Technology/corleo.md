# CORLEO and hydrogen-powered robotic mobility

## CORLEO: concept, not an operational fleet

- **3 April 2025:** Kawasaki announced CORLEO, a **2050 concept** for Expo 2025 Osaka–Kansai, opening on 13 April. Promotional terrain footage does not establish commercial availability or independently tested field performance.
- Four-legged personal off-road mobility concept combines motorcycle and robotics design; independently moving rear legs absorb shocks, while rider weight shifts guide movement.
- Proposed energy chain: **hydrogen canister → hydrogen engine → electricity → robotic leg movement**. The manufacturer specifies an **engine**, not a hydrogen fuel cell. [Kawasaki announcement](https://global.kawasaki.com/en/corp/newsroom/news/detail/?f=20250403_9193)
- Robotic assistance and human–machine interfaces could support difficult-terrain exploration, rescue and logistics; these are potential applications, not demonstrated deployments of CORLEO.

## Revision distinctions

| Hydrogen engine | Hydrogen fuel cell |
|---|---|
| Burns hydrogen to produce mechanical power; a generator can supply electricity | Electrochemical conversion directly produces electricity |
| Combustion can produce nitrogen oxides | No combustion within the cell |

Hydrogen's climate benefit depends on its production, storage and transport. No carbon in the fuel does **not** mean zero lifecycle emissions. Safety, fuel infrastructure, reliability, affordability and environmental disturbance remain implementation questions.
