Science and Technology

Norovirus

Editable notes

Identification and spread

  • Highly contagious virus causing acute gastroenteritis: vomiting, diarrhoea, nausea and abdominal discomfort. 'Stomach flu' does not mean influenza.
  • Faecal-oral transmission through contaminated food/water, surfaces and close contact; outbreaks can spread rapidly in shared settings.
  • Supportive care/rehydration is central; no specific antiviral medicine. Antibiotics do not treat a viral infection.
  • Source reports environmental persistence, including freezing and heat exposure near 60°C; do not use that as a food-safety or disinfection instruction.

Prevention and context

  • Soap-and-water handwashing, safe food handling, effective environmental cleaning and keeping symptomatic handlers away from food preparation.
  • January source refers to a US case surge; it is a dated surveillance context, not a claim about current outbreak levels.
