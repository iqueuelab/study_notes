Science and Technology

Laser directed energy weapons

Editable notes

13 April 2025: Mk-II(A) demonstration

  • DRDO's CHESS (Centre for High Energy Systems and Sciences), Hyderabad, with other laboratories, industry and academia, demonstrated a vehicle-mounted laser DEW at the National Open Air Range, Kurnool, Andhra Pradesh. Government reporting describes a 30-kW system.
  • Demonstrated engagements included fixed-wing UAVs, multiple drones and surveillance sensors; avoid treating general missile-defence potential as proof of interception of every missile class. Government report.

Working and limits

Detect → track → focus beam → maintain dwell → heat/damage target. DEWs direct concentrated energy; laser and high-power microwave systems differ from kinetic projectiles. Particle-beam systems are another conceptual class.

AdvantageQualification
Light travels about 300,000 km/sDestruction requires tracking and sufficient energy deposition over time; it is not instantaneous
Precise targeting; potentially lower cost per engagementAcquisition, electricity, cooling and maintenance still cost money; 'a few litres of petrol' is an illustrative firing-cost comparison, not total life-cycle cost
No projectile magazinePower supply and thermal management limit sustained engagements
Potentially reduced collateral damageDamage is not automatically zero; line of sight, eye hazards and target detonation matter
Counter-drone potentialFog, rain, dust, atmospheric turbulence, target material and movement can reduce effectiveness
  • Layered air defence still needs surveillance, command systems and other interceptors. Naval/airborne integration and more powerful systems are future possibilities, not completed deployments established by this trial.
  • Indigenous development supports defence self-reliance and collaboration. Countering inexpensive drones matters economically; export potential depends on maturity, operational testing and policy.

Practice revision

The source's expected prelims questions are undated practice. A laser beam propagates at light speed, not sound speed; cost advantages are conditional. CHESS belongs to DRDO.
