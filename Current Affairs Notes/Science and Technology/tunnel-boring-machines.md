# Tunnel boring machines and urban tunnelling

## Mechanism and machine selection
**Rotating cutterhead → excavate soil/rock → remove spoil by conveyor/muck cars → advance thrust system → install appropriate support/lining.** TBMs (sometimes “moles”) excavate circular tunnels and reduce surface excavation; choice depends on ground, water pressure and project geometry.
| Type | Typical use |
|---|---|
| Earth Pressure Balance | Soft ground; conditioned excavated material balances face pressure |
| Slurry shield | Water-bearing/unstable ground; pressurised slurry supports face |
| Hard-rock TBM | Disc cutters fracture competent rock; shield/support configuration varies |
| Open/gripper design | Suitable stable rock; not interchangeable with all unsupported soil excavation |
- Benefits: mechanised precision, potential continuous operation and less urban surface disruption than open excavation. Not guaranteed faster/safer in every geology; monitor settlement/utilities/buildings, groundwater and worker safety.
- Constraints: high purchase/mobilisation cost, complex underground repair, fixed diameter, geological uncertainty and poor economics on some short drives. “Eco-sensitive” does not eliminate spoil disposal, groundwater, vibration or habitat impacts.
- Source generic **5–17 m diameter, 10–15 m/day, ₹100–150 crore, 500–2,000 m³/day, 15–30 workers/shift** are indicative, not universal specifications; rates vary with diameter/geology/support/down-time. Project examples: **GMLR Mumbai planned 14.5 m; Metro Line 3 ~6.68 m; Delhi ~6.5 m; Chennai Phase 2 ~6.6 m; Mumbai Coastal Road 12.19 m** need machine-specific comparison. **Silkyara is not a verified 9–10 m TBM project**; do not confuse main tunnel construction, rescue auger and tunnel dimensions. [MoRTH Silkyara record](https://www.pib.gov.in/Pressreleaseshare.aspx?PRID=1521024&lang=2&reg=48).
## 7 June 2025: Delhi Golden Line breakthrough
- **Maa Anandmayee Marg → Tughlakabad Railway Colony**, Phase IV **Aerocity–Tughlakabad Golden Line**: TBM completed **792 m** drive. **96 m machine length** differs from bore diameter; tunnel roughly **18 m deep**, **566 precast rings**, **5.8 m inner diameter**; **EPB** method.
- Parallel second drive was expected **July 2025**; construction milestone is not opening the whole metro line. June clipping's **mid-2026** operations estimate is a forecast. DMRC gives **19.343 km underground** for this corridor and **40.109 km** approved Phase IV underground work. [DMRC 7 June release](https://delhimetrorail.com/pages/en/pressrelease_details/dmrc-completes-one-more-tunnelling-milestone-achie).
