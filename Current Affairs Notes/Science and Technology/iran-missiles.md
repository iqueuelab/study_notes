# Iranian missiles: types, range claims and deterrence

## Arsenal and classification
- Iran's missile forces support deterrence and retaliation against regional targets; conventional missile capability does not establish possession of a nuclear warhead. Mobile launchers and underground/mountain facilities improve survivability without making the force invulnerable.
| Missile | Type and approximate source-reported range |
|---|---|
| Sejjil | Solid-fuel ballistic; ~2,000 km |
| Emad / Ghadr | Shahab-derived ballistic; ~1,700 / ~2,000 km |
| Shahab-3 / Khorramshahr | Ballistic; ~1,300 / ~2,000 km, variant dependent |
| Shahab-1 / Zolfaghar | Short-range ballistic; ~300 / ~700 km |
| Tondar-69 | Chinese-derived short-range ballistic; ~150 km |
| Soumar / Hoveyzeh | **Land-attack cruise missiles**, not ballistic; source claims ~3,000 / ~1,350 km |
- Ballistic missiles receive powered boost then largely follow a ballistic trajectory; cruise missiles sustain aerodynamic flight with propulsion. The source incorrectly groups **Soumar and Hoveyzeh** under ballistic weapons. Range figures are reported/advertised envelopes, not guaranteed combat reach. [US DIA, Iran Military Power](https://www.dia.mil/Portals/110/Images/News/Military_Powers_Publications/Iran_Military_Power_LR.pdf).
- Iran unveiled **Fattah** in June 2023 with hypersonic claims. Speed above Mach 5 alone does not distinguish a modern manoeuvring hypersonic weapon from an ordinary ballistic re-entry vehicle; unveiling is not the same as an independently verified test or demonstrated interception evasion. The source's conflicting upgraded-Shahab range should not replace variant-specific specifications.
