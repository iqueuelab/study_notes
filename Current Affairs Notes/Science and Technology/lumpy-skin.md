# Lumpy skin disease and DIVA-marker vaccination

## Disease

- **LSDV**, genus **Capripoxvirus**, family **Poxviridae**; principally cattle/buffalo disease, **not a human zoonosis**. Skin nodules, fever, swollen nodes/limbs, reduced milk/weight and sometimes death.
- Blood-feeding arthropods such as flies, mosquitoes and ticks transmit infection; animal movement/biosecurity also matter. Different from bacterial mastitis or foot-and-mouth disease virus.

## February 2025 vaccine report

- **Biolumpivaxin, Biovet (Bharat Biotech group)**, developed with **ICAR**; source reports **CDSCO licence**, testing through **ICAR-NRCE /IVRI** and **Malur/Mallur, Karnataka** capacity **500 million doses/year**.
- **DIVA = Differentiating Infected from Vaccinated Animals**: marker vaccination plus suitable diagnostic tests helps distinguish infection-derived response from vaccination; **not an automatic zero-false-positive guarantee**.
- “World's first/safest” is the producer's claim; licence, efficacy under field conditions, delivered doses and maximum manufacturing capacity are different measures. [Company announcement reported 10 February](https://www.biospectrumindia.com/news/22/25654/biovet-gets-cdsco-license-for-worlds-first-ever-diva-marker-vaccine-for-lumpy-skin-disease-.html).

## Why it matters

- Dairy livelihoods, milk supply and animal welfare; public–private research partnerships can reduce outbreak losses.
- Link vaccination with surveillance, cold chain/coverage, vector control, movement safeguards and farmer information. Export potential is conditional on domestic needs and importing-country approvals.
