# KC-135 Stratotanker and CENTCOM

## 12–13 March 2026: Iraq crash
- **KC-135 Stratotanker**, US aerial-refuelling aircraft, crashed in western Iraq on **12 March** during an incident involving two aircraft; the other landed safely. **CENTCOM confirmed all six aboard dead on 13 March**, and said hostile/friendly fire was not the cause while investigation continued. A militia claim does not supersede the official account. [CENTCOM statement](https://www.centcom.mil/MEDIA/STATEMENTS/Statements-View/Article/4434083/all-crew-members-of-us-kc-135-loss-in-iraq-confirmed-deceased/).
- Aerial refuelling extends aircraft endurance/range without landing. Normal operating crew: **pilot, co-pilot and refuelling-boom operator**, with mission-dependent additional personnel/navigation duties. Normal crew complement does not contradict six persons aboard this mission; source gives up to **37 passengers** for suitable configurations.
- **US Central Command** coordinates US operations across West/Central Asian and specified South Asian responsibilities; it is a geographic combatant command, not a UN body or the command for all Asia.
- Separate early-March incident: **three US F-15E jets** were lost to friendly Kuwaiti fire, with all six aircrew safely ejecting. **Friendly fire** means inadvertent attack on own/allied forces, often involving identification/coordination failures; do not label the KC-135 loss friendly fire by association.
