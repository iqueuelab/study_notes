# BrahMos: supersonic cruise missile

## System and distinctions
- **Brahmaputra + Moskva** gives the name; **DRDO–Russia's NPOM** joint venture. **Supersonic cruise missile**, not a ballistic missile or hypersonic BrahMos-II.
- Two stages: **solid booster → liquid-fuel ramjet** sustained flight; approximately **Mach 2.8**. Original **~290-km** range differs from extended-range tested variants; specify variant instead of treating one range as universal.
- Land/ship/air versions; air launch from modified **Su-30MKI**. Submarine version was tested from a **submerged platform on 20 March 2013**; demonstration is not proof operational fit on every Indian submarine. [BrahMos test](https://brahmos.com/press-release/166).
- All-weather/day-night and fire-and-forget guidance; no continuous operator command does **not** mean no onboard guidance. Mission planning, sensors, target profile and platform configuration affect precision; avoid absolute “fastest in world” claims.
- Tactical strike/deterrence, indigenous capability with international collaboration and defence-export diplomacy. The clipping's “missile triad” means land/sea/air launch modes, **not India's nuclear triad**.
- **Philippines first delivery, 19 April 2024**, already preceded this issue: source incorrectly groups it with merely prospective exports. Vietnam discussions do not establish a signed/delivered sale. [BrahMos delivery](https://www.brahmos.com/brahmos-in-media/114).
## 16 May 2025 : export interest
- Source reports heightened interest following Operation Sindoor. **Philippines: confirmed US$375 million contract for three coastal-defence batteries**, with deliveries beginning in 2024. Indonesia/Vietnam/Malaysia and other listed Asian, Latin American, Gulf, African and European markets are **reported discussions/interest**, not signed/delivered sales.
- DRDO–NPOM ownership **50.5%–49.5%**; first test **12 June 2001**. Source warhead 200–300 kg, speed Mach 2.8–3 and extended 500–800 km ranges are variant/configuration-dependent, not specifications of every exported missile. Submerged-platform tests do not establish operational submarine deployment.
- Brah Mos-II is a **hypersonic development ambition**, distinct from existing supersonic Brah Mos. Export opportunities depend on approvals, financing, integration, training and support; commercial interest alone is not export revenue.

## Practice wording correction: May 2025

- Statement 3's unqualified “range limited to 290 km due to MTCR” is ambiguous/stale: **original variant about 290 km; extended-range variants existed before this issue**. Source answer **(b) 2 and 3** works only if explicitly referring to the original configuration; **(a) 2 only** fits an assertion that every present variant is still capped.
