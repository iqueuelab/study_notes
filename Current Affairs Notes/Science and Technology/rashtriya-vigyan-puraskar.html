Rashtriya Vigyan Puraskar: scientific recognition and selection

Editable notes

26 October 2025: national science awards announced

  • Four categories: Vigyan Ratna (lifetime contribution), Vigyan Shri (distinguished contribution), Vigyan Yuva–Shanti Swarup Bhatnagar (scientists up to 45 years), and Vigyan Team (three or more researchers working together). Awards cover 13 scientific/technology domains; selection committee is chaired by the Principal Scientific Adviser.
  • The official list totals 24 awards: one Ratna, eight Shri, fourteen Yuva and one Team—23 individual awards plus a team, correcting the issue's 25/24-individual arithmetic. Ratna: astrophysicist Jayant Vishnu Narlikar, posthumously; Team: CSIR Aroma Mission, associated with aromatic crops and rural livelihoods. October announcement.
  • Announcement, selection and presentation are distinct. The October release says the ceremony would be notified separately; do not apply the source's generic 11 May/23 August calendar as actual 2025 dates. Scientific recognition can encourage research, role models, teamwork and scientific temper (Article 51A(h)).
  • The reproduced newspaper raises selection transparency: publish clear criteria, manage conflicts, communicate decisions and protect independent scientific assessment. The issue's OPSC question gives a contradictory explanation for its 2023 statement; retain institution/first-ceremony distinctions and do not learn its answer as an authentic CSE key.
