# RS-24 Yars and ballistic-missile terminology

## Recall

- Russian three-stage solid-fuel intercontinental ballistic missile, related to Topol-M; silo and road-mobile versions; entered service around 2010.
- Common NATO designation **SS-27 Mod 2**; source's SS-29 label is unreliable. Reported length~22.5 m, diameter~2 m, mass~49 tonnes and range~10, 500 km are approximate variant-dependent specifications.
- **MIRV:** independently targeted re-entry vehicles; differs from multiple vehicles without independent targeting. Source 300kilotons describes explosive yield, not warhead mass; warhead-count claims vary.
