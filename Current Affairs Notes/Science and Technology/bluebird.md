# BlueBird and direct-to-device satellite communications

## Planned commercial launch: August 2025
- **BlueBird Block 2**, **AST SpaceMobile (USA)**, communications satellite for **direct-to-device mobile broadband**, including ordinary phones through compatible networks; distinct from NISAR radar/Gaganyaan crew capsule.
- Issue reports **~6,500 kg**, **LVM3 (formerly GSLV Mk III)** launch from **Sriharikota**; satellite arrival expected September 2025 and launch late 2025/early 2026. Forecasts, not completed milestones in this issue.
- Heavy commercial launches support revenue, domestic skills and India–US cooperation. Coverage, spectrum authorisation, bandwidth, terrestrial integration and delivered service differ from launch success.
- **NISAR=NASA–ISRO**, not ESA; L/S radar observes Earth. LVM3 naming does not make every configuration human-rated.
