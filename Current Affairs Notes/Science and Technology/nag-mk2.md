# Nag Mk 2: fire-and-forget anti-tank missile

## Anti-armour recall

- **Third-generation, fire-and-forget anti-tank guided missile (ATGM)**; indigenous DRDO development. Lock onto the target before launch; autonomous guidance thereafter reduces continued operator exposure.
- **13 January 2025, Pokhran**: three field trials at minimum/maximum ranges; demonstrated destruction of targets, including those protected by **explosive reactive armour (ERA)**. Do not confuse ERA defeat with intercepting every active-protection system.
- **NAMICA version 2** carrier evaluated alongside the missile; trials established readiness for induction, not evidence every formation had received it. [Defence release](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2092585&lang=2&reg=48).
- Distinguish **Nag: anti-tank** from **Akash: surface-to-air** and **BrahMos: supersonic cruise** missile.
