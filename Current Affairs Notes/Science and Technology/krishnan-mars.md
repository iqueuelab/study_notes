# Krishnan crater and Indian names on Mars

## November 2025: Martian nomenclature
- Source reports **Krishnan crater**, an approximately **3.5-billion-year-old** Martian feature, named for geologist **Maharajapuram Sitaram Krishnan (1898–1970)**, born at Thanjavur and the first Indian Director of the **Geological Survey of India**. Age is a geological estimate, not the name-approval date.
- IAU-approved nomenclature is recorded in the **USGS Gazetteer of Planetary Nomenclature**. **Krishnan Palus** is a **separately named plain**, approved **24 November 2025** and named from the nearby crater; do not identify the plain and crater as one feature. [Gazetteer record](https://planetarynames.wr.usgs.gov/Feature/16439).
- Associated names reported: small craters **Valiamala, Thumba, Bekal, Varkala**, and **Periyar Vallis**. **Vallis = valley**, **palus = named plain/marsh-type feature**; the descriptor does not prove liquid water or an actual present-day Martian marsh. Crater naming does not prove Indian landing/rover exploration there.
- Researchers **Asif Iqbal Kakkassery and Rajesh V. J.** proposed names following Martian geological work; source cites publication in **Meteoritics & Planetary Science**. Nomenclature assists shared mapping/communication and recognises scientific/cultural heritage. Avoid the unsupported universal claim that these are the first Indian names anywhere on Mars.
