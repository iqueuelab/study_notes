# Meteoroids, meteors and meteorites

## Three terms
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Meteoroid | Small natural space object |
| Meteor | Luminous atmospheric-entry phenomenon |
| Meteorite | Material surviving entry and reaching the surface |
- Entry heating involves interaction/compression with atmospheric gas; a meteor is not simply the rock itself. A fireball is an unusually bright meteor.
- Beed, Maharashtra, was the reported fall location. Laboratory classification is needed before assigning its composition or claiming rare organic compounds were found.
- Meteorites preserve mineral/isotope records of Solar System formation; some come from the Moon/Mars and some contain organics. **Organic molecules do not by themselves prove extraterrestrial life**.
- NASA's all-sky camera network records fireballs; Canada's CMOR detects meteor trails by radar. Observations constrain trajectories, velocities and potential recovery areas.
