# B-2 Spirit and bunker-penetrating munitions

## Platform and June2025 use
- Northrop Grumman **B-2 Spirit**, US Air Force long-range low-observable bomber; first flight **1989**, first operational delivery **1993**. Flying-wing shape, radar-absorbent materials and management of infrared/acoustic signatures reduce detectability; **stealth is not invisibility**.
- Two-person crew; four turbofans; high-subsonic flight; unrefuelled range about **6,000 nautical miles**, extended by aerial refuelling. Carries conventional and nuclear weapons. Exact radar-cross-section/cost comparisons in the source are not reliable universal figures.
- **GBU-57 Massive Ordnance Penetrator (MOP)** is an approximately **30,000lb conventional bunker-penetrating bomb**, distinct from a nuclear weapon. The B-2 can deliver it against hardened underground targets.
- **Operation Midnight Hammer,22 June2025 Iran time:** US strikes targeted Fordow, Natanz and Isfahan; B-2s delivered MOPs at Fordow/Natanz while submarine-launched cruise missiles also participated. Damage assessments and political claims of complete destruction were not settled facts at the issue date. The June25 text misidentifies the weapon as GBU-37. [US Defence account](https://www.defense.gov/News/News-Stories/Article/Article/4227082/historically-successful-strike-on-iranian-nuclear-site-was-15-years-in-the-maki/).
