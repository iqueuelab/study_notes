# Chronic venous insufficiency

## Mechanism and effects
- Inadequate venous return from legs to heart: weakened vein walls/damaged one-way valves allow backward flow and **blood pooling**, especially while standing.
- Symptoms include aching/heavy legs, swelling, cramps, varicose veins and skin discolouration; advanced disease can cause leg ulcers.
- Risks include ageing, obesity, pregnancy, family history and previous deep-vein thrombosis; prolonged sitting/standing also matters. The source's **1-in-20 adults** prevalence lacks a specified population/date.
- Management may involve exercise, leg elevation, weight management, compression and clinician-directed medicines/procedures; severe disease may need vein ablation/removal or other intervention. These are revision categories, not a prescription for unexplained swelling. [MedlinePlus](https://www.medlineplus.gov/ency/article/000203.htm).
