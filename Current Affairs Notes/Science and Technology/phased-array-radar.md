# Phased-array radar and strategic early warning

## How it works
- Electronic phase control across antenna elements steers a beam rapidly without mechanically rotating the whole antenna. It can track multiple targets and support ballistic-missile early warning.
- Detection is not interception. Range depends on frequency, power, target characteristics, height, terrain and Earth's curvature; a claimed 5,000 km range does not imply detection of every low-flying aircraft at that distance.
- The source reports a Chinese long-range radar in Yunnan; its operational performance claims require independent verification.
- US **PAVE PAWS**: PAWS means Phased Array Warning System; PAVE is a programme code name. The source's expanded PAVE wording is incorrect.
- Advanced radar capabilities are not confined to the US, Russia and China; India and other countries also operate relevant systems.
- Strategic implications: tracking and warning, intelligence about tests, and requirements for resilient communications and survivable deterrence. APJ Abdul Kalam Island is off Odisha.
