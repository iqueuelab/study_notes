# Naval Anti-Ship Missile–Short Range

## Trial and technology
- **DRDO/Indian Navy test 25 February 2025**, Sea King helicopter off Chandipur; **NASM-SR means Short Range**, despite source long-range/hypersonic wording. [Defence release](https://www.pib.gov.in/pressreleasepage.aspx?lang=2&prid=2106482&reg=48).
- Sea-skimming flight; imaging-infrared seeker, fibre-optic-gyro inertial navigation, radio altimeter and two-way data link.
- **Man-in-the-loop** allows target selection/retargeting via live seeker imagery; demonstrated direct hit and relevant control modes.
- DRDO laboratory collaboration includes RCI/DRDL/HEMRL/TBRL. Successful trial is not proof every unit is operationally inducted or an export contract exists.
