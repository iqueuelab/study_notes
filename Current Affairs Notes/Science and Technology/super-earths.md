# Super-Earths: TOI-1846 b and HD 20794 d

## Classification and July 2025 discoveries
- **Super-Earth** denotes planets above Earth's mass/size and below Neptune's regime; it does **not** establish terrestrial composition, life or habitability. Rocky, volatile-rich and gas-envelope possibilities require observations; “mini-Neptune” is not interchangeable with every super-Earth.
- **TOI-1846 b**, TESS transit discovery around a nearby M dwarf, **~154 light-years**, radius **1.79 ±0.07 Earth radii**, period **~3.93 days**. **4.4 Earth masses is predicted from a mass–radius relation**, not a directly measured mass. Paper gives equilibrium temperature **589 ±20 K for zero Bond albedo**, rather than source's unqualified 568 K. [Discovery paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.18550).
- Host source parameters: ~0.42 solar masses, ~0.40 solar radii, ~3,568 K, estimated age ~7.2 billion years. Small stars favour transit detection, but red dwarfs are not universally quiet; flares and stellar activity matter.
- Possible volatile/water-rich interior or atmosphere remains a model, not confirmed liquid oceans. **HD 20794 d**, ~20 light-years away, reported mass scale ~6 Earth masses and an eccentric orbit affecting habitable-zone residence. A habitable zone allows possible surface liquid water under suitable atmospheric conditions; it is not evidence of inhabited or assured habitable conditions.
