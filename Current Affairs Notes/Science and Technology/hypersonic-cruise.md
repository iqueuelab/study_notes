# Hypersonic cruise missiles and scramjet development

## July 2025: separate demonstrated technology from reported weapon specifications
- Hypersonic flight exceeds Mach 5. A scramjet uses atmospheric oxygen and maintains supersonic airflow through combustion; it differs from a ramjet and from an unpowered hypersonic glide vehicle. Sustained combustion, thermal protection, control and guidance are major challenges.
- Officially demonstrated: DRDL's actively cooled subscale scramjet combustor ran for over 1,000 seconds on **25 April 2025**, following January's 120-second ground test. This validates combustor/test-facility technology; it is not an operational missile deployment. [DRDO release](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleaseIframePage.aspx?PRID=2124415&lang=2&reg=48).
- July PDF reports an **ET-LDHCM / Project Vishnu**, Mach 8, 1,500-km range, 1,000–2,000-kg payload, multiple launch platforms, 12 weapons, 2027–28 glide vehicles and 2030 deployment. These detailed specifications/timelines lack confirmation in the cited official test release; retain them as reported claims, not established capability. Speed/manoeuvrability can complicate interception but do not prove immunity to every defence system.
