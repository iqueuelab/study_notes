# HANSA trainer aircraft and technology transfer

## 4 April 2025 development
- **CSIR–National Aerospace Laboratories (NAL)** licensed **HANSA-3(NG)** know-how to **Pioneer Clean Amps Pvt. Ltd.** for manufacture and commercialisation, including production support and after-sales services.
- Public R&D → licensed private manufacturing → pilot-training capacity: the agreement supports domestic aerospace supply chains, skilled employment and potential exports. A technology licence is distinct from aircraft certification or delivery.

## Aircraft recall
- **Two-seat, all-composite light trainer**, designed and developed by CSIR-NAL for basic flying training. Composite construction offers low weight; the bubble canopy improves visibility, while dual controls support instruction.
- NG features include a **Rotax digitally controlled engine**, glass cockpit and electrically operated flaps. Indigenous design does not imply every component is Indian: Rotax is a foreign engine supplier.
- The original HANSA programme predates 1998; **HANSA-3 received DGCA certification in February 2000**. Do not label the 2025 licence India's first-ever indigenous aircraft manufacture.
- Domestic trainer availability can reduce dependence on imported training aircraft; sustained benefits require regulatory certification, production quality, maintenance networks and viable operating costs.
