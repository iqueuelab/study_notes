# Rheumatoid arthritis and responsive drug delivery

## Disease and research
- **Rheumatoid arthritis (RA)** is autoimmune inflammation, commonly damaging the synovial lining of hand, wrist and knee joints; pain, swelling, stiffness and eventual deformity may occur. It can affect lungs, heart and eyes. **DMARDs**, including methotrexate, aim to slow progression and prevent damage.
- April issue revisits **INST Mohali (DST)** research announced **4 February 2025**: inflammation-responsive methotrexate microspheres form a **polymer–lipid hybrid micro-composite**. **Soya lecithin** improves drug encapsulation; **gelatin** responds to elevated **MMP-2/MMP-9** enzymes in inflamed joints, enabling on-demand release.
- Intended advantage over frequent systemic dosing: longer local relief and less unnecessary exposure/side effects. **Animal studies** reported reduced swelling, inflammation and cartilage damage; this is preclinical research, not proof of an approved human cure or an established replacement for clinical DMARD treatment. [DST/PIB](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2099618&lang=2&reg=3).
