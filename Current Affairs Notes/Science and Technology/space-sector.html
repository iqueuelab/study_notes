Science and Technology

IN-SPACe, POEM and SpaDeX

Editable notes

Institutions and platform

  • IN-SPACe, under Department of Space: promotes, authorises and facilitates non-government space activities and access to infrastructure.
  • POEM repurposes PSLV's spent fourth stage (PS4) into an orbital experimental platform: cheaper microgravity testing and space qualification without each experiment needing a full satellite.

Mission facts

  • PSLV-C60/SpaDeX launched 30 December 2024, not 18 December 2023 as printed in the source. ISRO mission summary.
  • POEM-4 carried 24 payloads: 14 ISRO/DoS + 10 non-government, including academia/startups through IN-SPACe. SpaDeX satellites and hosted POEM payloads serve distinct experimental purposes. ISRO payload details.

Mains significance

  • Lower entry costs, private innovation, jobs, public-private collaboration, technology validation and downstream communication/navigation/science services.
  • Needs: authorisation clarity, affordable infrastructure, safety/debris management, finance and competitive technology. Private participation does not mean privatisation of ISRO.

POEM-4: plants and walking robotics

  • CROPS — Compact Research Module for Orbital Plant Studies: cowpea germination under microgravity, reported within four days; automated thermal/environment control and a two-leaf-stage objective. A germination experiment does not yet establish a complete sustainable food system. ISRO CROPS.
  • RRM-TD — Relocatable Robotic Manipulator–Technology Demonstrator, developed by IISU: seven degrees of freedom; relocates itself to defined targets using inchworm walking, rather than simply relocating objects. Potential: inspection, assembly and servicing. ISRO payload description.

SpaDeX milestone, 16 January 2025

  • SDX-01 + SDX-02, roughly 220 kg each, successfully docked in orbit after earlier postponements. Rendezvous brings craft together; docking physically joins them; combined control/power transfer are further steps. ISRO docking confirmation.
  • Enables orbital assembly/resupply, satellite servicing and future Chandrayaan-4 sample-return architecture; demonstrates enabling technology, not a fully operational space station or every interplanetary capability.
  • Source BAS “end of decade” needs distinction between initial module plans and full station ambition. POEM/cowpea germination remains a separate hosted experiment from docking.
  • Mains: sustained funding, skilled teams, safe iterative testing, international/private cooperation and clear national-use/research priorities. Source's 250-word/15-mark daily question is coaching practice, not a verified UPSC PYQ.

Third Launch Pad approval, January 2025

  • Cabinet, 16 January 2025: SDSC Sriharikota, Andhra Pradesh, proposed Third Launch Pad, ₹3,984.86 crore, 48 months target. Approval/schedule are not completion.
  • Supports Next Generation Launch Vehicle, LVM3 with semi-cryogenic stages, enlarged NGLV configurations; standby for Second Launch Pad, future human-spaceflight/greater launch frequency.
  • Share existing facilities, adaptable design and industry participation; redundancy reduces disruption but construction/qualification must be completed. Cabinet decision.
  • First Launch Pad serves PSLV/SSLV, source about 30 years old; Second serves GSLV/LVM3 and PSLV backup, about 20 years. Ages are January 2025 approximations.
  • The repeated SpaDeX section adds no separate docking event; consolidated milestone remains 16 January 2025.

SpaDeX milestones

  • PSLV-C60 launched SDX-01 and SDX-02 on 30 December 2024; each approximately 220 kg, targeting a roughly 460 km orbit.
  • First docking: 16 January 2025. Successful undocking: 13 March 2025, about 09:20 IST. ISRO.
  • Later milestone: second docking on 20 April and power transfer on 21 April 2025. Power transfer had not already been demonstrated on 13 March. ISRO milestone record.
  • Rendezvous, docking and power-sharing support future stations, sample return and servicing. They do not mean every planned mission or refuelling capability is already operational.
