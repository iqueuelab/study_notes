IN-SPACe, POEM and SpaDeX

Editable notes

Institutions and platform

  • IN-SPACe, under Department of Space: promotes, authorises and facilitates non-government space activities and access to infrastructure.
  • POEM repurposes PSLV's spent fourth stage (PS4) into an orbital experimental platform: cheaper microgravity testing and space qualification without each experiment needing a full satellite.

Mission facts

  • PSLV-C60/SpaDeX launched 30 December 2024, not 18 December 2023 as printed in the source. ISRO mission summary.
  • POEM-4 carried 24 payloads: 14 ISRO/DoS + 10 non-government, including academia/startups through IN-SPACe. SpaDeX satellites and hosted POEM payloads serve distinct experimental purposes. ISRO payload details.

Mains significance

  • Lower entry costs, private innovation, jobs, public-private collaboration, technology validation and downstream communication/navigation/science services.
  • Needs: authorisation clarity, affordable infrastructure, safety/debris management, finance and competitive technology. Private participation does not mean privatisation of ISRO.

POEM-4: plants and walking robotics

  • CROPS — Compact Research Module for Orbital Plant Studies: cowpea germination under microgravity, reported within four days; automated thermal/environment control and a two-leaf-stage objective. A germination experiment does not yet establish a complete sustainable food system. ISRO CROPS.
  • RRM-TD — Relocatable Robotic Manipulator–Technology Demonstrator, developed by IISU: seven degrees of freedom; relocates itself to defined targets using inchworm walking, rather than simply relocating objects. Potential: inspection, assembly and servicing. ISRO payload description.
