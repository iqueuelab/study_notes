# Critical and Emerging Technologies Index

## Index and interpretation
- **Harvard Belfer Center,5 June2025:** model/dashboard compares technological power across25 countries plus a Europe aggregate; adjustable indicators/weights mean rankings depend on methodology. Five sectors: **semiconductors35%, AI25%, biotechnology20%, space15%, quantum5%** under default weights.
- Source reports India **15.2**, below France and above Russia/Canada/Australia; relatively stronger **space rank7**. These are index-specific assessments, not universal measures of research quality or all innovation. US leads overall, followed by China and Europe; Europe aggregate overlaps separately listed European states.
- Assessed pillars include economic resources/funding, human capital, regulation/security/global influence and sector capabilities: chip design/manufacturing, AI data/compute/algorithms, biotech/pharma/vaccines, space launch/navigation/defence and quantum core technologies.
- India: space ecosystem, talent/startups and market size offer strengths; semiconductor infrastructure, sustained R&D/private finance, talent retention, applied AI compute and academia–industry links remain constraints. Execute National Quantum Mission and use international collaboration with domestic capacity building. [Original index/methodology](https://www.belfercenter.org/critical-emerging-tech-index).
