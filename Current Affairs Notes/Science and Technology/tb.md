# Tuberculosis: elimination and health-system gaps

## Disease and resistance
- **Mycobacterium tuberculosis**, chiefly airborne from infectious pulmonary TB; lungs commonly affected, but **extrapulmonary TB (EP-TB)** involves other organs. **Latent infection is not contagious active disease**.
- Symptoms: persistent cough/sputum, weight/appetite loss, fever/night sweats and fatigue. Risks include **undernutrition, HIV, diabetes, smoking/alcohol**, and occupational exposure/social barriers.
- **MDR-TB**: resistance to at least **isoniazid + rifampicin**. **Pre-XDR**: MDR/rifampicin-resistant TB plus any fluoroquinolone resistance. **XDR** adds resistance to **bedaquiline or linezolid**. Source graphic's “isoniazid, rifampicin, fluoroquinolone and second-line injectable” reflects the **old definition**. [WHO 2021 update](https://www.who.int/news/item/27-01-2021-who-announces-updated-definitions-of-extensively-drug-resistant-tuberculosis).
- **BPaLM = bedaquiline + pretomanid + linezolid + moxifloxacin**; shorter regimen for eligible drug-resistant cases under clinical/programme criteria. Other named drugs **isoniazid, rifampicin, pyrazinamide, ethambutol, streptomycin** are not an instruction to self-combine treatment.
## Dated targets and burden
- India **2025 elimination ambition**, global **2030 End TB/SDG horizon**; ambition is not proof disease eradicated. **NTEP**, **Pradhan Mantri TB Mukt Bharat Abhiyaan/Ni-kshay Mitras**, nutrition/community support and preventive therapy for eligible high-risk contacts; source **Project Akshaya Plus** contact-tracing/testing example.
- Source report **~26% global burden**, **EP-TB 24% notified cases**, **>95% treatment initiation**, **24% mortality reduction from 2015**: retain indicator/period labels; initiating notified cases differs from treatment coverage among all estimated incident cases.
- Graphic world deaths **1.32 million (2022) → 1.25 million (2023)**, about **3,500 daily**; low/middle-income countries **87% burden**, Indonesia/China/Philippines/Pakistan plus India account for a major share. Notifications reflect diagnosis/reporting, not automatic incidence increase.
## Gaps and response
- Drug/diagnostic supply interruptions, including source **2023 shortages**; delayed EP-TB diagnosis, cartridge/staff shortages and underused **CBNAAT/Truenat**; high costs and migrant/occupational barriers.
- Decentralised rapid molecular testing and referral, trained workers, uninterrupted procurement, adherence/nutrition support and portable migrant records.
- **Idukki–Kudumbashree SHGs**: community participation; **Vietnam active case finding** in high-risk groups: adapt rather than copy uncritically.
- Prevention → find cases early → test resistance → complete appropriate treatment → support contacts/nutrition → monitor outcomes. COVID disruption and social determinants require sustained action beyond targets.
