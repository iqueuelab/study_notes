# Nuclear transmutation: lead into gold at the LHC

## ALICE result: 8 May 2025
- CERN's **ALICE** measured electromagnetic transmutation during **ultra-peripheral lead-ion interactions**: nuclei pass close without a head-on nuclear collision; intense electromagnetic fields induce photon–nucleus excitation and nucleon emission.
- **Lead Z=82 → gold Z=79 requires emission of THREE protons**, correcting the PDF's one/two-proton claim. One proton gives thallium; two mercury. Neutron emission changes isotope/mass number; illustrated **Pb-208 minus 3p and 2n → Au-203**.
- **Zero-degree calorimeters** register forward nucleon signals to identify channels. Newly produced nuclei rapidly leave the beam and strike accelerator components; fleeting survival is not evidence they all instantaneously radioactively decay or become collectible gold metal.
- Tiny yields and accelerator energy/cost make commercial production impractical. Measurements test electromagnetic-dissociation models and help understand beam-loss behaviour. [ALICE/CERN announcement](https://alice-collaboration.web.cern.ch/2025-alice-lead-to-gold).
## Chemistry versus nuclear physics
- Element identity depends on **proton number**. Chemical reactions rearrange electrons/bonds and cannot turn lead into gold; nuclear processes can alter nuclei.
- Alchemy's historic gold-making aspiration differed from modern experimental nuclear transmutation. This is measurement of a particular high-energy mechanism, not the first-ever artificial gold or validation of ordinary alchemical chemistry.
