# LCA Mk2, AMCA and stealth fighter technology

## Indian programmes
| Programme | February 2025 source account |
| --- | --- |
| LCA Mk2 | Larger multirole Tejas development, GE-F414 engine; **11 weapon stations**; projected prototype end-2025, flight 2026, induction 2028–29 |
| AMCA | Fifth-generation stealth development; five planned prototypes, reported **~1.5-tonne internal weapons bay**; projected rollout 2026–27, flight 2028, certification 2032, induction 2034 |
- Source initial **four AMCA aircraft/year** is a production-plan claim, not then-achieved output.
- **These dates were reported development targets, not confirmed completed milestones or current schedules.** Source April-2024 sanction timing needs confirmation against official approval history; do not universalise it.
- IAF source **31 squadrons versus sanctioned 42** is a dated readiness comparison, not a theorem that exactly 42 guarantees two-front success.
## Stealth comparison
| Aircraft | Recall |
| --- | --- |
| Su-57 | Sukhoi, Russia; low-observable multirole design, thrust vectoring; capability varies with engine/version |
| F-35 | Lockheed Martin, US; **A conventional, B short take-off/vertical landing, C carrier**; sensor fusion, quoted max Mach 1.6 |
| AMCA | Indian development goals; planned capability is not operational performance |
- Stealth reduces radar/other signatures; **not invisibility**. External stores can increase signature; internal bay trades payload flexibility against observability.
- **Supercruise** = sustained supersonic flight without afterburner, not any supersonic speed. Source's categorical supercruise/data-fusion ranking lacks common testing criteria; avoid memorising it as settled superiority.
- India withdrew from the joint Russian FGFA development in **2018**; Aero India displays or F-21/MRFA marketing do not establish a fighter order. Neither “India will buy” nor “India will never buy” follows from attendance.
## Industrial constraints
- Engines/materials, stealth shaping, sensors/software, test/certification, funding and production capacity; local manufacture does not establish complete imported-component independence.
- Stage-gated development, reliable supplier/IP arrangements, private capability and realistic delivery plans balance near-term readiness with long-term autonomy.
