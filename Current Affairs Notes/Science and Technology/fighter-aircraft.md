# LCA Mk2, AMCA and stealth fighter technology

## Indian programmes
| Programme | February 2025 source account |
| --- | --- |
| LCA Mk 2 | Larger multirole Tejas development, GE-F414 engine; **11 weapon stations**; projected prototype end-2025, flight 2026, induction 2028–29 |
| AMCA | Fifth-generation stealth development; five planned prototypes, reported **~1.5-tonne internal weapons bay**; projected rollout 2026–27, flight 2028, certification 2032, induction 2034 |
- Source initial **four AMCA aircraft/year** is a production-plan claim, not then-achieved output.
- **These dates were reported development targets, not confirmed completed milestones or current schedules.** Source April-2024 sanction timing needs confirmation against official approval history; do not universalise it.
- IAF source **31 squadrons versus sanctioned 42** is a dated readiness comparison, not a theorem that exactly 42 guarantees two-front success.
## Stealth comparison
| Aircraft | Recall |
| --- | --- |
| Su-57 | Sukhoi, Russia; low-observable multirole design, thrust vectoring; capability varies with engine/version |
| F-35 | Lockheed Martin, US; **A conventional, B short take-off/vertical landing, C carrier**; sensor fusion, quoted max Mach 1.6 |
| AMCA | Indian development goals; planned capability is not operational performance |
- Stealth reduces radar/other signatures; **not invisibility**. External stores can increase signature; internal bay trades payload flexibility against observability.
- **Supercruise** = sustained supersonic flight without afterburner, not any supersonic speed. Source's categorical supercruise/data-fusion ranking lacks common testing criteria; avoid memorising it as settled superiority.
- India withdrew from the joint Russian FGFA development in **2018**; Aero India displays or F-21/MRFA marketing do not establish a fighter order. Neither “India will buy” nor “India will never buy” follows from attendance.
## Industrial constraints
- Engines/materials, stealth shaping, sensors/software, test/certification, funding and production capacity; local manufacture does not establish complete imported-component independence.
- Stage-gated development, reliable supplier/IP arrangements, private capability and realistic delivery plans balance near-term readiness with long-term autonomy.
## Su-57 offer, February 2025
- Russia offered cooperation/local manufacture of **Su-57**, with proposed radar, engine and software access; this was an **offer**, not a signed Indian procurement contract or confirmed 2025 production start.
- Evaluate stealth/sensor integration, sortie availability, engine maturity, cost, Indian manufacturing/IP access and interoperability. India's earlier FGFA withdrawal does not automatically settle a fresh offer.
- Russian assurances cannot determine whether US **CAATSA** sanctions or waivers apply. Technology-transfer promises need contractual scope and independent performance assessment.

## 2024 PYQ recall
- **Rafael (official printed spelling, referring to Rafale), Mi G-29, Tejas MK-1:** none is a fifth-generation fighter; answer **(d) None**. Capability upgrades do not automatically change the platform generation.

## Squadron capacity debate
- Source compares~31 squadrons with a sanctioned 42; delivery forecasts and retirements are not completed fleet changes.
- Aircraft numbers need engines/components, trained personnel, maintenance, sensors, AEW/refuelling and timely production. Private/DPSU collaboration cannot eliminate every supply constraint.
- LCA Mk 2/AMCA are development programmes; reported foreign purchase interest is not equivalent to aircraft delivered. Draft industrial policies must be labelled as drafts.

- Otherpartners include L&T, Tata Advanced Systems, VEMand LMW; component handover is not delivery of an entirefighter or proof every 83 aircraft-contractdeadline will be met.

## AMCA and readiness: March 2025
- CCS approved AMCA development in **March 2024**, not 2023; five prototypes and a roughly ₹15,000 crore programme are development plans, not an operational fleet.
- Initial F414-powered designs and a proposed higher-thrust future engine are different stages. Stealth and supercruise are design objectives until demonstrated.
- Proposed Pakistani J-35/KAAN acquisitions must not be recorded as completed deliveries. Hotan is in Xinjiang, not Tibet.
- India's sanctioned fighter strength is 42 squadrons; MRFA 114-aircraft proposals are not completed procurement. LCA Mk1A production and Mk2 development are distinct.
- Readiness also depends on engines, spares, weapons, training, airborne warning, logistics and delivery capacity—not aircraft counts alone.

## Tejas variants: avoid mixing specifications
- Programme launched in 1983; ADA established in 1984; Tejas achieved final operational clearance in 2019. **HF-24 Marut preceded it** as an indigenous fighter.
- Mk1/Mk1A use the **GE F404 family**; **Mk2 plans use F414**. Mk1A improvements do not mean it has already switched to F414.
- Ferry range differs from combat radius and depends on configuration. Naval LCA is a technology demonstrator, not an inducted carrier fighter fleet; TEDBF is a separate development programme.
- Engine supply, domestic subsystems, certification, production capacity and maintenance determine operational readiness, beyond an order announcement.

## July 2025: second F404 engine
- Embedded clipping reports receipt of the **second GE F404 engine**, following the first in March; engine supply disruption delayed Mk1A deliveries. Source-period **83-aircraft order**, additional **97 proposal**, near-term **10 aircraft deliveries** and **12 engines by financial-year end** describe orders/plans, not completed deliveries.
- Tejas technology-demonstrator first flight **4 January 2001**; trainer is a two-seat operational-conversion aircraft retaining combat functions. Multirole weapons, refuelling, AESA/BVR/EW and improved maintenance should be understood by variant. [HAL aircraft brochure](https://hal-india.co.in/backend/wp-content/uploads/2022/12/LCA.pdf).
- The PDF's **Mach 1.8 and 3,000-km combat range** conflict with its embedded Mk1 graphic (**Mach 1.6**) and lack mission/configuration qualifiers; do not learn ferry range as combat radius. Its **2025–26 Mk2 production** graphic is an older forecast, not proof achieved. Existing F404/F414 and naval-demonstrator distinctions correct the text's mixed variants.

## August 2025 fleet transition
- Two remaining **MiG-21 Bison squadrons (Panthers/Cobras)** were approaching retirement; India inducted **872 MiG-21s**, beginning in the 1960s with later HAL production. Source accident count **450+** is a cumulative report, not an investigation proving every loss resulted from age.
- Engine delays constrained **83 Mk1A contract (2021, roughly ₹48,000 crore)**; additional **97** remained a proposed follow-on in this issue. The source's April first-F404-delivery claim conflicts with earlier March reporting already retained; no new completed delivery is established here.
- Source describes planned upgrade of **84 Su-30MKIs**, acquisition of **12 replacements**, and longer-term **~600 fighters over two decades** spanning Mk1A/Mk2/MRFA/AMCA. Fleet strength, orders, proposals and targets are separate categories; blanket 'all Jaguars/Mirages/MiG-29s retired by 2030' is not a firm common retirement date.
- **MRFA means Multi-Role Fighter Aircraft**, not 'Medium Role'; 114-aircraft competition remains prospective. GE F404 for Mk1A and F414 for Mk2 are distinct, and proposed local engine manufacture is not proof both are already license-built at scale.
- **2024 PYQ repeated:** Rafale, MiG-29 and Tejas Mk1 are not fifth-generation—**none (d)**. Existing common bank already contains the full question; retain one copy.

## 25 September 2025: 97 Mk1A contract signed
- MoD signed with **HAL** for **97 LCA Mk1A and associated equipment**, **over ₹62,370 crore excluding taxes**: **68 fighters and 29 twin-seaters**. This advances the previously reported proposal to a signed contract; deliveries **begin FY2027–28 over six years**, not already delivered aircraft. [Defence Ministry](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2171108&lang=2&reg=48).
- **Over 64% indigenous content**, **67 additional items** compared with the January 2021 order; indigenous **UTTAM AESA radar, Swayam Raksha Kavach and control-surface actuators**. **Buy (Indian-IDDM), DAP 2020**; nearly **105 Indian suppliers** and **11,750 direct/indirect jobs yearly over six years** are the release's estimates.
- Delivery, engine supply, certification, spares and production capacity determine readiness and industrial benefits. Imported **F404** remains distinct from **F414 for Mk2**; Mk1A is not automatically a higher-thrust-engine replacement. Repeated **3,000-km combat range/Mach 1.8** and naval-variant claims remain corrected above.
- **2024 fighter-generation PYQ (d), none**: Rafale, MiG-29 and Tejas Mk1 are not fifth-generation; the question is already banked. Contract signatures and future AMCA development must be separated from aircraft in service.

## 17 October 2025: HAL's Nashik production expansion
- Rajnath Singh inaugurated HAL's **third LCA Tejas Mk1A production line** and **second HTT-40 line**, and flagged off the first Nashik-built Mk1A. Nashik adds **eight Mk1A aircraft/year**, bringing stated HAL capacity to **24/year**; capacity is not actual annual deliveries. Engine supply, certification, suppliers and timely execution remain decisive. [Defence Ministry](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2180339&lang=1&reg=3).
- **HTT-40 = Hindustan Turbo Trainer-40**, HAL's indigenous **basic turboprop trainer**; distinguish basic pilot training from fighter missions. Mk1A's AESA, EW, refuelling and maintainability upgrades are already explained above; it remains distinct from Mk2/AMCA.
- Official release attributes **around 1,000 jobs and over 40 industry partners** to the expansion; these are inauguration-period claims. It praised HAL's operational maintenance support and Su-30/BrahMos integration during Operation Sindoor; this is retrospective recognition, not a new October military operation.
- The Minister's **65% domestic manufacture** and future **100% ambition** are attributed speech figures, not a verified common-denominator series proving all imported-component dependence ended. Source's DPEPP 2020 figures belong to the **draft policy** and its earlier targets, not a newly achieved output total.
- **2024 PYQ recall:** Rafael/Rafale, MiG-29 and Tejas Mk1 are not fifth-generation fighters—**(d) None**. Already banked; no duplicate.
