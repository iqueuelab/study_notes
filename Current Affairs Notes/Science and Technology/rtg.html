Radioisotope thermoelectric generators

Editable notes

RTG revision

  • Radioisotope decay supplies heat, converted directly into electricity by thermoelectric materials; RTGs are not miniature fission reactors.
  • Pu-238 is a common heat-source isotope; applications include Voyager, New Horizons and Curiosity where durable power and weak sunlight favour radioisotope systems.
  • PYQ 2024 supplied answer: 2 and 3 only (b). Retain the question's wording about weapons-development by-products, but do not confuse Pu-238 with the main weapons isotope Pu-239 or assume all modern Pu-238 is merely recovered weapons waste.
