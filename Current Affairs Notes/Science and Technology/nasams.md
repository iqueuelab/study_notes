# NASAMS and networked air defence

## System

- **National Advanced Surface-to-Air Missile System**, **Kongsberg (Norway) + Raytheon (USA)**; ground-based short/medium-range **networked air defence**, fielded by Norway **1994**.
- Radar/sensors → **Fire Distribution Centre** → distributed launchers; **AIM-120 AMRAAM** adapted for ground launch, with configuration-dependent other missiles. Aircraft, helicopters, cruise missiles and drones are typical targets.
- Modular open architecture, active radar and passive electro-optical/IR inputs; distributed components improve survivability/interoperability, not immunity to jamming.
- Source **75 km radar / 30 km engagement**, **six missiles per launcher**, **three launchers / 72 targets** combine configuration-specific claims. **Detection range ≠ missile range; launcher inventory ≠ simultaneous target capacity**. [Manufacturer description](https://www.kongsberg.com/what-we-do/defence-and-security/integrated-air-and-missile-defence/nasams-air-defence-system/).

## January context

- Taiwan reported planned **north/Taipei deployment** of purchased systems; procurement, delivery and deployment dates differ.
- Users source lists Norway, Spain, US, Finland, Hungary, Netherlands, Australia, Indonesia, Qatar, Oman and Lithuania; **13-country count** is dated/incomplete, not a permanent membership list. US capital-region protection since **2005**.
- Practice's “Canada never uses NASAMS” distractor requires date/end-user distinction; Canada's procurement for Ukraine differs from Canadian domestic operational deployment. Do not memorise a timeless negative.
