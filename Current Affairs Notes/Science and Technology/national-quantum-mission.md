# National Quantum Mission: teaching, research and technology

## March 2026: quantum teaching infrastructure
- **16 March official review:** approvals for quantum teaching facilities/laboratories in **23 academic institutions**, with **100 more under consideration**. Approval is not proof every laboratory has begun operation. [Science Ministry](https://www.pib.gov.in/newsite/archiveContent.aspx?lang=2&reg=48&relid=286087).
- NQM: approved **2023**, **₹6,003.65 crore over FY2023–24–2030–31**; four technology areas: computing, communication, sensing/metrology, and materials/devices. Target **50–1,000 physical-qubit intermediate-scale computers** over eight years; a target is not demonstrated fault-tolerant computing.
- **Qubit superposition** represents amplitudes of basis states; it does not allow reading all possible answers simultaneously. Quantum advantage is task-specific. Secure quantum communication still needs authenticated protocols and reliable hardware; “unbreakable encryption” is too absolute.
- Teaching labs build experimental skills and a research/industry workforce. The same review discussed **IISF 2026**, with Pune identified as a proposed venue and DBT developing the framework; the final programme was still to be settled.
