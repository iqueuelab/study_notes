# NASA Dynamic Targeting and autonomous Earth observation

## July 2025 demonstration
- NASA/JPL's **Dynamic Targeting** lets a satellite analyse look-ahead imagery onboard and choose observations without awaiting ground commands. **Cogni SAT-6**, commercial Cube Sat launched March 2024, hosted July flight tests; Open Cosmos satellite/Ubotica AI payload.
- Look ahead roughly**500 km**→identify clouds→plan clear views→repoint and image: **60–90 seconds**. Saves storage/downlink and improves usable optical observations; onboard AI complements mission planning.
- First test proved **cloud avoidance**; wildfire/volcano thermal anomalies and storms were further test goals, not all established operational services. [NASA/JPL 24 July account](https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/news/how-nasa-is-testing-ai-to-make-earth-observing-satellites-smarter/).
## Linked satellite revision
- **Astro Sat**, India's first dedicated multiwavelength space observatory, launched**2015**,~**1,515 kg**,~**650 km** orbit. The 2016 PYQ's 2000 kg/1650 km and India-only-besides-US/Russia claims are false.
- **IRS**, imaging/remote sensing since**IRS-1 A 1988**, supports crop assessment, groundwater prospects, mineral exploration, forestry, water, planning and disasters. **GSAT-type communications satellites** serve telecom.2015 PYQ accepts crop, groundwater, mineral exploration; do not turn its exclusion of“traffic studies” into a universal claim that remote-sensing data can never support transport planning.
