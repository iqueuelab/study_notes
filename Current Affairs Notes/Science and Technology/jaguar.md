# SEPECAT Jaguar and fleet modernisation

## Aircraft and Indian service
- **Anglo-French SEPECAT Jaguar**, British Aircraft Corporation/Breguet lineage, is a twin-engine deep-penetration ground-attack aircraft; Indian name **Shamsher**. Distinguish strike role from strategic transport and fifth-generation fighter classifications.
- India began induction in **1979**, with imported aircraft and HAL licence manufacture. Sheet's roughly **160 inducted/~120 operational/six squadrons** are source-period estimates, not a current verified inventory.
- Variants: **IS single-seat strike; IB two-seat trainer; IM maritime strike**. Refuelling probe, navigation/attack avionics, radar altimeter, inertial navigation and upgraded cockpit support range/precision. Equipment differs by variant/upgrade; a list is not proof every airframe carries every listed system.
- Source specifications: up to **4,500 kg payload**, about **1,699 km/h**, ceiling **46,000 ft**, combat-radius figures **850 km/internal fuel, 1,400 km/drop tanks**; mission profiles/load determine actual performance. Its “introduced 1968” conflates early flight/development with service introduction; do not use it as IAF induction year.
- July clipping reports two pilots killed in a crash near **Churu, Rajasthan**, described as the third Jaguar crash that year. Ageing fleets require maintenance, accident investigation and replacement planning; no specific cause is established merely from aircraft age.
