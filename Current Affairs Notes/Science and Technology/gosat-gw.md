# GOSAT-GW and Japan’s H-IIA final flight

## Launch and climate monitoring
- **29 June 2025 JST** (28 June UTC), Tanegashima: **50th and final H-IIA mission** launched **GOSAT-GW — Global Observing SATellite for Greenhouse gases and Water cycle**. H-IIA began in 2001 and completed **49 successes in 50 launches (98%)**, its failure occurring in 2003; Hayabusa2 (2014) and SLIM (2023) are earlier missions.
- Third GOSAT-series spacecraft combines greenhouse-gas and water-cycle observations: **TANSO-3** for CO₂/CH₄ and **AMSR3** microwave observations for water-cycle/weather variables. It continues earlier GOSAT and Shizuku/AMSR observations; climate/forecasting, floods/droughts, sea temperature and atmospheric moisture applications matter. [JAXA mission](https://www.jaxa.jp/projects/pr/model/files/p_gosat-gw_2025.pdf).
- Operational data release follows commissioning/calibration; “within a year” is a forecast, not data already available on 1 July. **H3** is Japan's successor large launcher; **Epsilon** serves smaller missions. Claimed cost reductions are design goals, not a universal guaranteed price.
