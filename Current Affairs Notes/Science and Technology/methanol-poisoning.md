# Methanol poisoning and illicit liquor regulation

## Chemistry and public-health risk
- **Hooch:** illicit/spurious liquor outside reliable licensed production; toxic contamination can arise from deliberate industrial-alcohol diversion or unsafe production. Not all illicit alcohol contains the same methanol concentration.
- **Methanol CH₃OH**, wood alcohol: colourless, volatile, flammable, water-miscible industrial alcohol; solvents, formaldehyde/chemical manufacture, antifreeze and fuel uses. Modern synthesis uses **syngas—CO/H₂**, potentially from natural gas, coal or biomass; industrial production is not necessarily wood distillation.
- **Ethanol C₂H₅OH** is the principal beverage alcohol and a CNS depressant; beverage presence does not make unlimited consumption safe. Smell/appearance cannot establish liquor safety.
- **Methanol → formaldehyde → formate/formic acid**: metabolic acidosis, optic-nerve injury/blindness and neurological/organ damage; early symptoms may resemble intoxication, with deterioration after a delay. Source's **10 mL blindness /30 mL fatality** figures are illustrative toxic exposures, not dependable safe/lethal thresholds for every person.
- Suspected exposure requires **urgent emergency assessment**; antidotes such as fomepizole and other hospital interventions block toxic metabolism/treat complications. These are clinician-directed treatments, not an instruction to self-administer ethanol. [CDC/NIOSH](https://www.cdc.gov/niosh/ershdb/emergencyresponsecard_29750029.html).

## Governance: May 2025 revision
- **FSSAI Alcoholic Beverages Regulations 2018** prescribe product-specific methanol limits; no single limit applies identically to every beverage. **MSIHC Rules 1989**, State excise controls and poison/industrial-chemical rules address distinct parts of production, storage and sale. [FSSAI compendium](https://www.fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Compendium_Alcoholic_Beverages_Regulations_30_06_2022.pdf).
- **Poisons Act 1919** provides enabling powers/rules, not an identical blanket national ban on every industrial use. Gujarat/Bihar prohibition does not imply all lawful industrial methanol handling is absolutely prohibited; permissions/exemptions and applicable State law matter.
- Drivers: illegal manufacture, industrial diversion, poor traceability, enforcement corruption/gaps, limited consumer awareness and weak health–excise–police coordination.
- Response: licensed supply-chain records and inspections, chemical traceability, inter-State intelligence, rapid outbreak identification, accessible emergency treatment and targeted awareness. The source's comprehensive new central law is a **recommendation**, not an enacted May measure; evaluate existing-law enforcement as well as legislative gaps.

## June 20 PYQ revision
- Ethanol-production question (2025): **United States produces more ethanol than Brazil**; US feedstock is principally corn, Brazil's principally sugarcane. Statement I is false and II true (d); source production volumes are dated approximations.
- Source CDS explanation mistakenly calls “methanal” a typo for formaldehyde: **methanal is formaldehyde**. Methanol metabolism proceeds through formaldehyde to formate; formate chiefly drives acidosis and optic injury. Preserve that distinction from the source's oversimplified protoplasm explanation.
