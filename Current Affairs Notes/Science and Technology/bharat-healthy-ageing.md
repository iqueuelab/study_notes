# BHARAT study and healthy-ageing biomarkers

## India-specific ageing research
- **BHARAT = Biomarkers of Healthy Aging, Resilience, Adversity and Transitions**, an IISc Bengaluru population study under **Longevity India**, seeks a healthy-ageing baseline and indicators of age-related risk. [IISc study](https://www.longevity.iisc.ac.in/the-bharat-study).
- **Chronological age:** elapsed time; **biological age:** estimated physiological condition from selected markers. Models measure aspects of ageing, not a single universal “true age”.
- Integrate genomic, proteomic, metabolomic and other biomarkers with clinical, lifestyle/environmental and social information. AI/ML may identify trajectories, predictors and candidate interventions, which still need external validation.
- Population-specific reference ranges require diverse, well-characterised samples. Longitudinal follow-up, healthy-volunteer selection, urban/rural/regional representation, funding, consent and genomic/data privacy are key challenges.
- Source examples about CRP, vitamin D and cholesterol illustrate hypotheses about baselines; do not conclude that elevated inflammation/lipid abnormalities are harmless for Indians or validated clinical thresholds can be ignored.
