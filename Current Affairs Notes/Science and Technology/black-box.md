# Aircraft black boxes and accident investigation

## Flight recorders
- Flight recorders preserve evidence for accident investigation; Australian scientist **David Warren** pioneered the combined flight-data/voice-recording concept. Bright orange casings aid recovery.
- **CVR:** cockpit conversations, radio traffic, alarms and ambient sounds. **FDR:** parameters such as speed, altitude, heading, engine performance, autopilot and attitude; the number recorded depends on aircraft/equipment standards.
- Crash-survivable memory uses strong steel/titanium protection and thermal insulation; underwater locator beacons assist searches. Installation is commonly towards the rear, but survival is never guaranteed; separate and combined recorder designs exist.
- June 2025 context: recorder recovery after the Ahmedabad Air India crash; recovery itself does not establish accident cause. DGCA ordered additional checks of Air India's Boeing 787 fleet, including fuel/control systems, cabin air-compressor components and engine/electronic controls.
