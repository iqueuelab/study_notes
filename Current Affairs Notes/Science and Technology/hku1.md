# Human coronavirus HKU1

## Endemic coronavirus, not HKU5
- One of four common endemic human coronaviruses: **229E, NL63, OC43 and HKU1**. HKU1 is a betacoronavirus in **Embecovirus**; SARS-CoV and MERS-CoV belong to different subgenera.
- First scientific identification/publication was **2005 in Hong Kong**, using a patient sample collected in 2004. [CDC research](https://wwwnc.cdc.gov/eid/article/12/5/05-1316_article).
- Usually causes upper-respiratory symptoms; bronchiolitis/pneumonia can occur, especially in vulnerable patients. Endemic does not mean incapable of causing severe illness.
- Respiratory spread and close contact matter; no established specific vaccine or targeted routine antiviral for HKU1. Care is supportive and severity-dependent.
- Ventilation, hand/respiratory hygiene and avoiding exposing vulnerable people help prevention. A Kolkata report is not discovery of a novel COVID-like virus or proof of an imminent pandemic.
