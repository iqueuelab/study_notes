# Pinaka rocket artillery and ammunition procurement

## System and roles
- Indigenous **Multi-Barrel Rocket Launcher / MLRS**, developed by **DRDO–ARDE** for Indian Army; Army is user, not sole designer.
- **Two pods × six rockets**, mobile truck launcher with stabilising outriggers; loader/replenishment vehicles and command post. **214 mm** basic rocket, source payload about **100 kg**, differing warheads.
- Area targets: exposed troops, vehicles, communications, airfield facilities and ammunition/fuel dumps; pre-fragmented high explosive and area-denial rounds have different effects. Artillery rocket system ≠ surface-to-air defence.
- Basic Mk-I commonly cited around **38 km**; enhanced/guided rounds extend range/accuracy. Source's inconsistent **48/60/90 km** version table, **48-second** salvo and exact **5,757.70 km/h Mk-3** should not be learned as universal specifications. Launcher commonly cited **12 rounds in ~44 seconds**; launcher versus six-launcher battery differs.
- January source describes enhanced HE **45 km**, area-denial **37 km**, guided developments about **75 km**, future **120/300 km** ambitions. Planned variants ≠ then-operational capability, and “air attacks” is misleading.
## January approval and contract distinction
- January **CCS** approval reported near **₹10,000 crore**, source approximate splits **₹6,050 / ₹4,000 crore**, signing expected by March. Approval ≠ contract signed or ammunition delivered.
- Correct **MIL = Munitions India Limited**, not **Mishra Dhatu Nigam (MIDHANI)**. Private supplier **Economic Explosives Limited (EEL), Solar group**.
- **6 February 2025 clarification**: MoD signed rocket contracts totalling **₹10,147 crore** with EEL/MIL; **BEL SHAKTI software upgrade** also contracted. [MoD release](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleaseIframePage.aspx?PRID=2100335&lang=2&reg=3).
- Domestic ammunition capacity, logistics/replenishment, credible delivery schedules and quality trials support readiness; higher volume/range alone does not prove precision or eliminate escalation concerns.
