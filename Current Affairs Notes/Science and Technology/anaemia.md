# Anaemia and AnemiaPhone

## Mechanism and causes
- **Anaemia**: low haemoglobin/oxygen-carrying capacity for the relevant group; iron deficiency is a major cause, **not the only cause**.
- Other contributors: **B12/folate deficiency**, poor intake, infections, blood loss and inherited blood disorders; nutrition and access inequalities matter.
- Fatigue, weakness, breathlessness, impaired function; severe cases can be life-threatening. Pregnancy risks include prematurity/low birthweight; signs alone do not establish the cause.
## AnemiaPhone: point-of-care iron assessment
- **Cornell University** technology licensed to **ICMR** for public-health use; announced **December 2024**, discussed in January 2025 notes. Integrates into anaemia/women's/maternal-child-health programmes; transfer is not proof of universal rollout.
- Finger-prick blood → **test strip + portable reader** → result in minutes → phone/tablet/computer-connected clinical record; source describes Bluetooth/Wi-Fi-enabled readers.
- Enables decentralised **iron-status screening**, potentially before clinical anaemia, and appropriate referral/differential diagnosis; not symptom-only smartphone diagnosis. [Cornell](https://news.cornell.edu/stories/2024/12/new-cornell-tech-evaluate-anemia-be-used-across-india).
## NFHS-5 and programme recall
- **2019–21 survey**: pregnant women 15–49 **52.2%** anaemic; children **6–59 months 67.1%**; all women 15–49 **57%**. PDF's general '50–70% pregnant / 50% children' should not replace specified survey figures. [Health Ministry](https://www.pib.gov.in/Pressreleaseshare.aspx?PRID=1795421&lang=2&reg=48).
- **Anaemia Mukt Bharat, 2018**, life-cycle approach: supplementation, testing/treatment, diet/behaviour change and attention to non-nutritional causes; public-health support does not justify self-prescribing iron for every anaemia.
- Clinical workup may use CBC/haemoglobin, iron indicators and other cause-specific tests. Digital screening needs quality control, trained workers, reliable supply chains, follow-up and privacy.
