# GAURAV long-range glide bomb

## System and guidance
- **GAURAV:** indigenous air-launched **1,000 kg class**, air-to-surface long-range glide bomb integrated with **IAF Su-30MKI**. The class designation is not a statement that its warhead contains 1,000 kg of explosives.
- A released bomb glides towards its target under guidance; conventional warheads are intended for hardened/ground targets. **INS + GPS** hybrid navigation combines inertial guidance with satellite position updates; digital control steers the weapon.
- The 2024 maiden-test account identifies **Research Centre Imarat, Hyderabad**; the 2025 release names **RCI, Armament Research and Development Establishment and Integrated Test Range, Chandipur** as developers.

## Dates: maiden flight versus subsequent release trials
| Date | Development |
|---|---|
| **13 August 2024** | DRDO announced the maiden flight test from Su-30MKI off Odisha; target at Long Wheeler's Island struck; telemetry/electro-optical systems recorded flight data |
| **8–10 April 2025** | Release trials with multiple aircraft stations and different warhead configurations; island land target; range close to **100 km** demonstrated |
| **11 April 2025** | Official announcement of those April trials |
- Thus the 12 April source's “recent maiden test” repeats an **August 2024** event; April 2025 trials were a later development.
- April release describes progress **towards induction**, not confirmed operational induction. Industry partners include Adani Defence Systems & Technologies, Bharat Forge and MSMEs; CEMILAC and DGAQA supported certification/quality assurance.
