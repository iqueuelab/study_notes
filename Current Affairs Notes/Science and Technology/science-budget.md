# Budget 2025–26: science funding, deeptech and research balance

## Separate corpus, allocation and mission
- **₹1 lakh crore** private-sector R&D corpus announced earlier; **₹20, 000 crore FY2025–26 allocation** to implement private-sector-driven research/development/innovation, **not total DST spending**. [Budget overview](https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2025/feb/doc202521492801.pdf).
- **DBT ₹3, 446.64 crore**, source **+51.45%** must specify BE/RE comparator; separate Department of Scientific & Industrial Research and DST totals.
- Source **National Geospatial Mission ₹100 crore**, germplasm/high-yielding seeds, cotton productivity, manufacturing and deeptech initiatives; distinguish individual budget lines from total ministry resources.
- Additional **₹10, 000 crore Fund of Funds for start-ups** and proposed exploration of a **deeptech fund** are not identical to a confirmed single SIDBI deeptech-only fund.
- **Nuclear SMR R&D ₹20, 000 crore /100 GW 2047**, separate from the equal-sized private-R&D allocation; avoid adding them as one fund.
## Programmes and priorities
- **Bio-RIDE**: biotechnology R&D, innovation/entrepreneurship, biomanufacturing/biofoundry; National Manufacturing Mission supports technology/commercialisation.
- Source CSIR missions: **aroma/floriculture, millets, cotton, seaweed, packaging and indigenous manufacturing**; some predate the Budget and should not all be presented as new FY2025 announcements.
- Green hydrogen and nuclear initiatives support energy transition; agricultural seed/genetic work links climate resilience and productivity.
## Mains balance
- Private capital, translational research and domestic supply capability complement curiosity-driven public research and university capacity.
- “Basic-science funding reduced” needs scheme-wise and BE/RE comparisons; nominal ministry rise does not prove every institute received more.
- **NEP 6% GDP** public-education aspiration cannot be compared with an allocation percentage unless both denominator and period are supplied. Source **2.54%** is insufficiently labelled; do not silently treat it as either GDP share or Union-Budget share. Evaluate actual spending, research autonomy, access and measurable additional R&D.
## Private-sector R&D financing
- March 2025 discussion of ₹1 lakhcrore corpus concerned a proposed financing mechanism; concessional long-term loans/risk finance differ from university research grants and PLI output incentives.
- Final terms, eligibility, administrator and tenor require notified scheme checks; newspaper 5–7 year/nearly-zero-interest/IT-exclusion claims are not all final rules.
- Source GERD~0.65%GDP and private 30–36%share are dated estimates. ANRF's ₹50, 000 crore ambition, including non-government contributions, is a different funding envelope.
- Commercialisation, industry–academic collaboration, patient capital, transparent selection and measurable additional research matter; applied research support need not replace basic science.

## RDI scheme approved 1 July 2025
- **₹1-lakh-crore Research Development and Innovation Scheme** approved for private RDI in strategic/sunrise sectors; long-tenor **low/nil-interest finance/refinance**, growth/risk capital, critical technology acquisition and Deep-Tech Fund of Funds. This is not simply a universal grant for basic science or support restricted to exactly TRL 4.
- **ANRF Governing Board**, chaired by PM, sets strategic direction; Executive Council approves guidelines; Cabinet-Secretary-led empowered group oversees scheme changes/sectors/fund managers; **DST is nodal department**. Two-tier fund structure uses an ANRF special-purpose fund and second-level fund managers. [Approval](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2141130&lang=2&reg=3).
- Source's TRL reminder: **1–3 basic principles/proof-of-concept;4–6 validation/demonstration;7–9 advanced prototype/operational maturity**. Crossing the commercialisation “valley of death” needs patient finance and testing/market capability.
- Earlier corpus announcement and **₹20,000-crore 2025–26 allocation** remain distinct from total spending. Source private-R&D shares **India≈36%, US≈70%, China≈75%** are dated comparative estimates with different possible denominators, not identical 2025 audited shares; applied finance must complement sustained public basic research.

## September 2025: R&D survey participation
- Source reports the NSTMIS/DST national R&D survey, covering roughly 8,000 public/private bodies, launched December 2024, with the September 2025 deadline extended to November. Response rates reported: government institutions 73%, Indian industry 35%, multinational firms 9%; these are survey-response shares, not shares of national R&D expenditure.
- Weak response and differing definitions of R&D can bias estimates of GERD, personnel, patents and private participation. Clarify definitions, simplify questionnaires and improve institutional reporting. Source's “75% public funding” and “36% private contribution” use inadequately aligned periods/denominators; do not combine as one funding breakdown.
- The repeated ₹1-lakh-crore RDI approval, ₹20,000-crore FY2025–26 allocation, TRL/valley-of-death and governance discussion are already covered. It is not support confined to exactly TRL 4 or a universal basic-research grant.
- **2015 NIF PYQ: statement 1 only (a)**. NIF is an autonomous DST body promoting grassroots technological innovation and outstanding traditional knowledge; it is not a programme for elite international scientific collaboration. Honey Bee Network is a related grassroots network, not simply a NIF-owned flagship.

## ESTIC 2025 and research finance
- Correct the source repeatedly: first **Emerging Science, Technology and Innovation Conclave**, **3–5 November 2025**, Bharat Mandapam, New Delhi, not November 2024. It brings academia, industry, researchers and government together across **11 themes**, including AI, quantum, agriculture, health, energy/climate, space, advanced manufacturing and blue economy. [PMO address](https://www.pmindia.gov.in/en/news_updates/pm-addresses-the-emerging-science-technology-and-innovation-conclave-2025/).
- The **₹1 lakh crore Research, Development and Innovation (RDI) Scheme Fund** launched at ESTIC supports private-sector research; it should not be renamed the entire **ANRF budget**. Conclave participation/funding announcements are different from realised research outputs. The issue's R&D doubling, patent growth and **6,000 deeptech start-ups** are speech-era aggregates with differing bases; avoid recasting them as verified 2014–2024 series without definitions.
- Address links scientific innovation to nutrition, low-cost fertilisers/soil health, genomic diversity and precision medicine, affordable clean-energy storage, battery alternatives and recycling. **Hydrogen fuel cells are electrochemical energy-conversion devices, not simply sodium-ion-type batteries.** Commercialisation needs evidence, skills, accessible finance, academia–industry links and public-interest research support. The issue's claim that ESTIC legally replaces the Indian Science Congress is not established by the official programme.
