# Budget 2025–26: science funding, deeptech and research balance

## Separate corpus, allocation and mission
- **₹1 lakh crore** private-sector R&D corpus announced earlier; **₹20,000 crore FY2025–26 allocation** to implement private-sector-driven research/development/innovation, **not total DST spending**. [Budget overview](https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2025/feb/doc202521492801.pdf).
- **DBT ₹3,446.64 crore**, source **+51.45%** must specify BE/RE comparator; separate Department of Scientific & Industrial Research and DST totals.
- Source **National Geospatial Mission ₹100 crore**, germplasm/high-yielding seeds, cotton productivity, manufacturing and deeptech initiatives; distinguish individual budget lines from total ministry resources.
- Additional **₹10,000 crore Fund of Funds for start-ups** and proposed exploration of a **deeptech fund** are not identical to a confirmed single SIDBI deeptech-only fund.
- **Nuclear SMR R&D ₹20,000 crore /100 GW 2047**, separate from the equal-sized private-R&D allocation; avoid adding them as one fund.
## Programmes and priorities
- **Bio-RIDE**: biotechnology R&D, innovation/entrepreneurship, biomanufacturing/biofoundry; National Manufacturing Mission supports technology/commercialisation.
- Source CSIR missions: **aroma/floriculture, millets, cotton, seaweed, packaging and indigenous manufacturing**; some predate the Budget and should not all be presented as new FY2025 announcements.
- Green hydrogen and nuclear initiatives support energy transition; agricultural seed/genetic work links climate resilience and productivity.
## Mains balance
- Private capital, translational research and domestic supply capability complement curiosity-driven public research and university capacity.
- “Basic-science funding reduced” needs scheme-wise and BE/RE comparisons; nominal ministry rise does not prove every institute received more.
- **NEP 6% GDP** public-education aspiration cannot be compared with an allocation percentage unless both denominator and period are supplied. Source **2.54%** is insufficiently labelled; do not silently treat it as either GDP share or Union-Budget share. Evaluate actual spending, research autonomy, access and measurable additional R&D.
