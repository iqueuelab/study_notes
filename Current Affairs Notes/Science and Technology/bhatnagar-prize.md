# Shanti Swarup Bhatnagar Prize

## Science honour and PYQ
- **2009 Prelims answer (c), Science**. Named after CSIR's founding Director **Shanti Swarup Bhatnagar**; recognises outstanding fundamental/applied scientific research, mainly work done in India.
- First award year **1958**; CSIR-HRDG describes institution in **1957**, whereas source simply gives 1958. Distinguish establishment from first award. Historical eligibility: Indian citizens **up to 45 years** as of **31 December of the preceding year**. [CSIR prize history](https://www.csir.res.in/en/shanti-swarup-bhatnagar-prize/shanti-swarup-bhatnagar-prize-science-and-technology-1958-1998), [HRDG institutional account](https://www.csirhrdg.res.in/Home/Index/1/Default/1887/8).
- Seven historical disciplines: **biological; chemical; earth/atmosphere/ocean/planetary; engineering; mathematical; medical; physical sciences**. Preserve the historical prize question without assuming its rules automatically describe every later national science-award category.
