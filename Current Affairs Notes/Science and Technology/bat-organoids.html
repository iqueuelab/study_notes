Science and Technology

Bat organoids, cell models and pandemic preparedness

Editable notes

May 2025 experimental platform

  • Organoids: self-organising 3 D cultures from stem/progenitor cells that reproduce selected tissue structures/functions; not complete functioning organs. Cell lines: maintained cell populations; not all are single-cell clones or immortal. Primary cultures have limited growth, while immortalisation permits sustained proliferation; HeLa and Vero illustrate established lines, not universally engineered immortalisation.
  • Kim and colleagues, Science, May 2025: organoids from five insectivorous bat species, four organ types—trachea, lung, kidney, intestine—with mucus/goblet-cell and alveolar features. Species have wider Asian/European ranges, but sampled bats were captured in South Korea, not necessarily across both continents. Original study, authors' account.
  • Tested SARS-CoV-2, MERS-CoV, influenza A and Seoul orthohantavirus; observed species/tissue-specific susceptibility and strong MERS replication in relevant models. Source reports SARS-CoV-2 infection enabled by introduced human TMPRSS 2, a cell-entry-related protease, not the main ACE 2 receptor. Results from selected cultures do not establish immunity of all bats or settle pandemic origins.
  • Fecal-sample work identified mammalian orthoreovirus and paramyxovirus; detection/isolation in bats does not prove human pathogenicity or imminent pandemic spread.
  • Separate PLOS Biology study, 15 April 2025, developed kidney/brain/liver/spleen lines from Carollia perspicillata, Seba's short-tailed fruit bat; tested MERS-CoV, vesicular-stomatitis and Andes orthohantavirus-related susceptibility/immune responses. ATCC distributes research biological resources. Original study.

Applications, limits and India

  • Disease/entry mechanisms, innate immunity, antiviral/toxicity screening and tissue-specific spillover assessment; organoids offer architecture while scalable lines support high-throughput experiments. Human intestinal/brain organoids also model disease and personalised treatment research.
  • Bats support pollination, seed dispersal and insect control; some species host zoonotic viruses. Nipah reservoir fruit bats differ from the insectivorous study species; don't infer every bat carries every listed virus. COVID-19 origin and reservoir pathways are not conclusively determined by this experiment.
  • Source India 120+ species, limited virological data, Northeast Ebola/Marburg-reactive antibodies and an April 2024 inter-ministerial initiative are reported context; antibodies do not prove a local Ebola outbreak or exact infecting species/virus.
  • Models reduce some live-animal handling but infectious-virus culture still requires appropriate biosafety, permissions and ethical oversight. Build One Health surveillance, BSL capacity, diagnostics and collaborative data sharing; laboratory susceptibility cannot alone predict future human transmission.
