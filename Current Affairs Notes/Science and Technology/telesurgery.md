# Telesurgery and SSI Mantra

## Remote operation
- **Telesurgery**: surgeon controls a robotic surgical system remotely through a reliable, high-speed data link; cameras return operative images. **Tele-proctoring**: remote expert supervises/mentors, rather than directly operating.
- **January 2025 SSI Mantra 3** demonstration: Gurugram → Manipal Hospital, Jaipur, **286 km**, reported **40 ms latency**, two cardiac operations including beating-heart **Totally Endoscopic Coronary Artery Bypass (TECAB)**.
- Indigenous platform received **CDSCO approval for telesurgery/tele-proctoring**. Source's worldwide exclusivity/first claims are attributed promotional claims, not a timeless assertion about all robotic systems; a successful demonstration does not establish equal performance on every network.
- **CDSCO**, national drugs/medical-device regulator; **Drugs and Cosmetics Act, 1940**, with applicable device rules. Regulatory clearance differs from proof of universal clinical superiority.
## Wider healthcare robotics
| Use | Recall |
| --- | --- |
| Monitoring / telepresence | Remote clinical observation and suitable sensors; cameras alone do not measure every vital sign |
| Prosthetics / exoskeletons | Mobility and rehabilitation assistance |
| Disinfection | UV-C or hydrogen-peroxide systems with exposure controls |
| Logistics | Medicines, supplies and meals |
- Cost, trained teams, latency/jitter, network redundancy, cybersecurity, informed consent, liability and local emergency backup determine safe deployment.
- Policy context: **National Health Policy 2017**; **draft National Strategy on Robotics 2023**, proposed Robotics Innovation Unit; **ARTPARK at IISc Bengaluru**. A draft/proposed unit is not automatically an operational scheme.
