# WaveX BhashaSetu language-technology challenge

## Challenge launched 30 June 2025
- Ministry of Information & Broadcasting's **WaveX**, under WAVES, invited startups to develop **BhashaSetu**: real-time translation, transliteration and voice localisation across **at least 12 Indian languages**.
- Translation changes language/meaning; transliteration changes script while retaining sound; voice localisation adapts spoken delivery. Uses include regional media, entertainment, education and public communication; accessibility and emotion-aware communication are goals.
- Open to startups at any stage with **no minimum eligibility criteria**; scalable, affordable open-source/low-cost models encouraged, proprietary solutions also permitted. Winning startup receives accelerator incubation/mentoring; prototype deadline **22 July 2025**. [PIB launch](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2140887&lang=2&reg=48).
- India's Constitution lists **22 Scheduled languages**, not 22 universally applicable national official languages. The challenge was an invitation to build a solution, not proof of a fully deployed service.
