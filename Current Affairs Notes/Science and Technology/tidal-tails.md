# Tidal tails and galaxy evolution

## Mechanism

- Close gravitational encounter/merger → **tidal forces** strip/distort outer **stars and gas** → elongated tails, sometimes leading/trailing features.
- Tails can outlast the merger and reveal interaction history; their material may form stellar clumps or tidal dwarf/ultra-diffuse systems.
- Examples: **Tadpole Galaxy; Mice Galaxies**. Not every interacting galaxy must have exactly two observable tails.

## NGC 3785: January 2025 news

- In **Leo**, about **430 million light-years** away; study reports an exceptionally long tail, **projected length ~390 kpc (~1.27 million light-years)**, **84 star-forming clumps** and a candidate nascent **ultra-diffuse galaxy at its end**. [Research paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.18811).
- Interpret as observational evidence/proposed formation mechanism, not proof that all ultra-diffuse galaxies share that origin.
- Source cites about **10% of an interacting galaxy's star formation** and **~1% globally** in tails; context-dependent estimates, not universal constants.
