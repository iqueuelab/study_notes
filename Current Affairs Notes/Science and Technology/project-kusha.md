# Project Kusha and indigenous air-defence development

## May 2025 development stage
- **Project Kusha**, DRDO-led long-range surface-to-air defence development, aims to protect against aircraft, missiles and UAV threats; often compared with Russia's S-400, but analogy is not proof of identical design/performance or completed induction.
- **BEL** is a development-cum-production partner for radars/control subsystems. Its **20 May 2025 earnings call** says overall system integrator was **not yet finalised**. **₹40,000 crore** is a potential order opportunity conditional on integration/procurement allocation, **not sanctioned total project cost or an order already awarded to BEL**. [BEL official transcript](https://bel-india.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/दिनांक-20.05.2025-को-आयोजित-कॉन्फ्रेंस-कॉल-की-प्रति.pdf).
- Source's **12–18 months prototype /12–36 months trials** are expectations requiring technical trials, user evaluation and procurement; not completed milestones or a binding delivery promise. Technological performance, reliability, timely qualification, domestic supply chains and integration with **IACCS** determine utility.
## Related systems and procurement distinctions
- **QRSAM**, DRDO-led quick-reaction low-level air-defence programme, has BEL as production agency; expected **₹30,000 crore combined Army/Air Force order** discussed by BEL remained a **future expectation in May**, not contract receipt. BEL partnership does not replace DRDO's development role.
- Source **S-400 400 km** describes an advertised **missile engagement range for a particular interceptor**, not a universal radar-detection radius. India contracted five systems in **2018** for roughly **US$5.4 billion**; source **three delivered/two pending** is a dated delivery account, not proof all arrived.
- Operation Sindoor revived attention to layered defence, including S-400, Akash and Barak-8; **BrahMos is a strike cruise missile, not a SAM interceptor**. Source's “over 300 Pakistani drones neutralised” cannot be inferred merely from reported drone numbers or assigned to each named system without verified operational evidence.
- Indigenous air-defence R&D supports strategic autonomy and reduced import dependence; fielded range/accuracy, availability, interoperability, replenishment and lifecycle maintenance matter beyond comparison slogans.
