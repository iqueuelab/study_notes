# IN-SPACe, POEM and SpaDeX

## Institutions and platform
- **IN-SPACe**, under Department of Space: promotes, authorises and facilitates non-government space activities and access to infrastructure.
- **POEM** repurposes PSLV's spent fourth stage (**PS4**) into an orbital experimental platform: cheaper microgravity testing and space qualification without each experiment needing a full satellite.
## Mission facts
- **PSLV-C60/SpaDeX launched 30 December 2024**, not 18 December 2023 as printed in the source. [ISRO mission summary](https://new1.isro.gov.in/Atomospheric_Re_entry_of_POEM4.html).
- **POEM-4 carried 24 payloads: 14 ISRO/DoS + 10 non-government**, including academia/startups through IN-SPACe. SpaDeX satellites and hosted POEM payloads serve distinct experimental purposes. [ISRO payload details](https://www.isro.gov.in/POEM_4_Payloads_spadex.html).
## Mains significance
- Lower entry costs, private innovation, jobs, public-private collaboration, technology validation and downstream communication/navigation/science services.
- Needs: authorisation clarity, affordable infrastructure, safety/debris management, finance and competitive technology. Private participation does not mean privatisation of ISRO.
## POEM-4: plants and walking robotics
- **CROPS — Compact Research Module for Orbital Plant Studies**: cowpea germination under microgravity, reported within **four days**; automated thermal/environment control and a two-leaf-stage objective. A germination experiment does not yet establish a complete sustainable food system. [ISRO CROPS](https://www.isro.gov.in/CROPS.html).
- **RRM-TD — Relocatable Robotic Manipulator–Technology Demonstrator**, developed by **IISU**: **seven degrees of freedom**; relocates itself to defined targets using **inchworm walking**, rather than simply relocating objects. Potential: inspection, assembly and servicing. [ISRO payload description](https://www.isro.gov.in/ISRO_EN/POEM_4_Payloads_spadex.html).

## SpaDeX milestone, 16 January 2025
- **SDX-01 + SDX-02**, roughly **220 kg each**, successfully docked in orbit after earlier postponements. Rendezvous brings craft together; docking physically joins them; combined control/power transfer are further steps. [ISRO docking confirmation](https://www.isro.gov.in/spadex_docking.html).
- Enables orbital assembly/resupply, satellite servicing and future **Chandrayaan-4** sample-return architecture; demonstrates enabling technology, not a fully operational space station or every interplanetary capability.
- Source BAS “end of decade” needs distinction between initial module plans and full station ambition. **POEM/cowpea germination** remains a separate hosted experiment from docking.
- Mains: sustained funding, skilled teams, safe iterative testing, international/private cooperation and clear national-use/research priorities. Source's 250-word/15-mark daily question is coaching practice, not a verified UPSC PYQ.

## Third Launch Pad approval, January 2025
- **Cabinet, 16 January 2025**: **SDSC Sriharikota, Andhra Pradesh**, proposed **Third Launch Pad**, **₹3,984.86 crore**, **48 months** target. Approval/schedule are not completion.
- Supports **Next Generation Launch Vehicle**, **LVM3 with semi-cryogenic stages**, enlarged NGLV configurations; standby for **Second Launch Pad**, future human-spaceflight/greater launch frequency.
- Share existing facilities, adaptable design and industry participation; redundancy reduces disruption but construction/qualification must be completed. [Cabinet decision](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2093358&lang=2&reg=48).
- **First Launch Pad** serves **PSLV/SSLV**, source about 30 years old; **Second** serves **GSLV/LVM3** and PSLV backup, about 20 years. Ages are January 2025 approximations.
- The repeated SpaDeX section adds no separate docking event; consolidated milestone remains **16 January 2025**.

## SpaDeX milestones
- PSLV-C60 launched SDX-01 and SDX-02 on 30 December 2024; each approximately 220 kg, targeting a roughly 460 km orbit.
- First docking: **16 January 2025**. Successful undocking: **13 March 2025, about 09:20 IST**. [ISRO](https://www.isro.gov.in/spadex_undocking_successful.html).
- Later milestone: second docking on 20 April and power transfer on **21 April 2025**. Power transfer had not already been demonstrated on 13 March. [ISRO milestone record](https://www.isro.gov.in/Spadex_Successful_demonstration_of_Second_Docking_and_Power_Transfer.html).
- Rendezvous, docking and power-sharing support future stations, sample return and servicing. They do not mean every planned mission or refuelling capability is already operational.

## 12 August 2025: commercial Earth-observation PPP selection
- **IN-SPACe selected a PixxelSpace India-led consortium** with **PierSight Space, SatSure Analytics India and Dhruva Space** to design, build and operate a commercial EO constellation. Source plan: **12 satellites, investment over₹1,200 crore across five years**; selection/commitment is not launch or full deployment. [IN-SPACe announcement](https://www.inspace.gov.in/).
- Planned data/services support agriculture, flood/fire/disaster management, climate, urban infrastructure, marine surveillance and national security. Indigenous capability improves data sovereignty; it cannot promise complete elimination of specialised/global foreign data.
- **Analysis Ready Data (ARD)** is preprocessed for analysis; **Value-Added Services (VAS)** transform observations into products such as crop-health indices/flood-risk maps. Optical/infrared/radar sensors have different weather/light capabilities.
- **Oceansat-2**, launched **23 September 2009**: **OCM-2** ocean colour/chlorophyll/sediment; **Ku-band scatterometer** ocean-surface winds; Italian **ROSA** radio occultation for atmospheric profiles. These explain **2010 PYQ: all three(d)**—water-vapour estimation, monsoon-onset prediction and coastal-pollution monitoring. [ISRO mission](https://new1.isro.gov.in/Oceansat_2.html).
- EO practice intended **1,2,3 only**, which is **option2**, despite printed answer saying “option3”; fourth statement claiming complete replacement of foreign data is false.
