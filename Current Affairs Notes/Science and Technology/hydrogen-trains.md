# Hydrogen trains and fuel-cell rail mobility

## July 2025 prototype milestone
- Source reports successful testing of India's first hydrogen **Driving Power Car at Integral Coach Factory, Chennai**; a coach test is distinct from regular passenger-service commissioning. The printed **1,200 HP**, top-speed and early-2025 operational-date graphic are not reliable final specifications; retain the demonstrated milestone without treating those graphic claims as achieved performance.
- **Fuel cell:** hydrogen + atmospheric oxygen → electricity for traction + water/heat. Tailpipe CO₂ is absent, but lifecycle emissions depend on hydrogen production, electricity and supply chains. A hydrogen train still uses electric traction.
- **Hydrogen for Heritage:** 35 trains envisaged for heritage/hill routes; original estimates **₹80 crore/train + ₹70 crore ground infrastructure/route**. Separate **₹111.83 crore DEMU retrofit pilot**, including facilities, on **Jind–Sonipat, Haryana**. These are programme estimates, not an identical ₹150-crore final cost for every route. [Railways programme](https://www.pib.gov.in/Press Release Iframe Page.aspx?PRID=1896102&lang=2&reg=48).
- Benefits: lower local emissions, reduced diesel dependence, demonstration of indigenous fuel-cell integration. Constraints: initial cost, reliable low-emission hydrogen, storage/refuelling, safety and maintenance; scale may reduce cost. Source recalls Indian Oil's hydrogen-bus demonstration to Bhutan's PM in 2024 as another mobility application.
