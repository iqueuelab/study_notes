# Chandrayaan-5 and lunar polar exploration

## Missions and goals
- March 2025 reporting described approval for the India–Japan **LUPEX/Chandrayaan-5** lunar polar mission: ISRO lander and JAXA rover roles, investigating water and regolith.
- A reported approximately 250 kg rover was a design-stage figure, not final delivered hardware; Chandrayaan-3's Pragyan was approximately 26 kg.
- Chandrayaan-4 is a separate sample-return programme approved in September 2024. Proposed launch dates are targets.
| Mission | Milestone |
|---|---|
| Chandrayaan-1 | Launched 22 October 2008; water-molecule evidence |
| Chandrayaan-2 | Launched 22 July 2019; orbiter science continued despite landing failure |
| Chandrayaan-3 | Launched 14 July 2023; soft landing 23 August 2023 |
- Polar-region landing is not landing at the exact geographic pole. Larger payload capacity does not itself prove ice extraction capability.
