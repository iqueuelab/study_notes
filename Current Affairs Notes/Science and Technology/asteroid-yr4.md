# Asteroid 2024 YR4 and planetary-defence risk

## Discovery and changing estimates
- **2024 YR4**, discovered **27 December 2024**, ATLAS, Chile. Preliminary orbital observations briefly raised concern about **22 December 2032** Earth encounter.
- Source **2.1%** is a temporary probability, not a certain impact prediction. Additional observations reduced uncertainty; **24 February 2025 NASA reported no significant Earth-impact risk** for 2032 or beyond. [NASA facts](https://science.nasa.gov/solar-system/asteroids/2024-yr4-facts/).
- A provisional geographic “risk corridor” is conditional on an impact solution, not a prediction of destruction in every listed country. Risk estimates can rise before falling as the orbital uncertainty region narrows.
## Defence and communication
- Detection →repeated observation →orbit refinement →risk communication →mitigation assessment if warranted.
- **IAWN** coordinates warnings; **SMPAG** assesses space-mission options. Kinetic impactors demonstrated by **DART/Dimorphos, 2022**; other deflection concepts depend on warning time/size and are not approved missions for this asteroid.
- **Tunguska 1908** flattened roughly **2,000 km²**, not the PDF's 830 km². Size, composition, atmospheric breakup and trajectory determine consequences.
- Maintain observatory capacity and transparent updates; distinguish asteroid risk from sensational early headlines and hypothetical nuclear-deflection proposals.
## May 2025 stale-source correction
- This issue repeats the earlier **2.1% 2032 Earth-risk** narrative; it had already been superseded by late-February observations and must not be treated as an active May emergency. NASA **2 April 2025** reaffirmed **no significant Earth threat in 2032 or beyond**; Webb observations on **26 March** indicated diameter roughly **53–67 m**. [NASA dated update](https://science.nasa.gov/blogs/webb/2025/04/02/nasas-webb-finds-asteroid-2024-yr4-is-building-sized/).
- Main asteroid belt lies between **Mars/Jupiter**; **Kuiper Belt beyond Neptune** contains icy bodies. The page's general Solar-System diagram is not a map of YR4's precise orbit. Provisional country corridors and nuclear/kinetic-deflection discussions in the repeated source do not establish a selected impact site or approved mitigation mission.
