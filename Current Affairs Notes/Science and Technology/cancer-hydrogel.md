# Injectable hydrogel for targeted cancer therapy

## Research mechanism
- **IIT Guwahati + Bose Institute, Kolkata**: ultra-short peptide hydrogel for local delivery of chemotherapy, reported **2 January 2025**.
- Hydrogel: water-rich three-dimensional polymer/peptide network; a local drug reservoir that responds to elevated **glutathione (GSH)** at the tumour site.
- Experimental work used **doxorubicin** in a **murine breast-cancer model**. This is preclinical research, not an approved human cure. [IIT Guwahati](https://www.iitg.ac.in/iitg_press_details?p=158%2Fiit-guwahati-researchers-develop-innovative-injectable-hydrogel-for-targeted-breast-cancer-therapy), [Research abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39552584/).
## Why it matters
- Local/sustained drug release may reduce systemic exposure and improve delivery; the source's assertion that only tumour cells are affected is too absolute.
- Translation needs safety, biocompatibility, dose/release control, clinical trials, scalable manufacture and affordability; lower resistance/cost are potential benefits, not established clinical outcomes.
