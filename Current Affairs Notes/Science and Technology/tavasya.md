# Triput, Tavasya and frigate localisation

## Project milestones
- Additional follow-on **Project 1135.6** frigates: India–Russia arrangement for four ships, two built in Russia and two at Goa Shipyard with technology transfer.
- **Triput launched July 2024**, not July 2022; **Tavasya launched 22 March 2025**. Launch is entry into water, not completed trials, delivery or commissioning. [Defence Ministry](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleaseIframePage.aspx?PRID=2114025&lang=2&reg=48).
- Tavasya is the second Goa-built ship of this pair, not the fourth frigate ever in the wider Talwar/Krivak-derived family.
- Designed for anti-surface, anti-air and anti-submarine roles; reduced radar signature does not make a ship invisible.
- Russian design cooperation, Indian systems/shipbuilding and Ukrainian-origin gas turbines illustrate multinational supply chains. Local manufacture strengthens skills without proving every critical component is indigenous.
- Delivery schedules are targets; assess integration, trials, engine supply, maintenance and timely production.
