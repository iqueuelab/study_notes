# PUNCH: mapping the solar wind

## Mission recall
- **Polarimeter to Unify the Corona and Heliosphere**, NASA; four small spacecraft in low Earth orbit: one Narrow Field Imager coronagraph and three Wide Field Imagers.
- Polarised sunlight scattered by electrons helps reconstruct the corona–solar-wind transition in 3D; track coronal mass ejections and space-weather evolution.
- Complements Parker Solar Probe's in-situ measurements and Aditya-L1 observations; a remote map is different from directly sampling plasma.
- February launch plans changed: launched with SPHEREx on 11March 2025 California time, 12MarchUTC. [NASA](https://www.nasa.gov/news-release/nasa-launches-missions-to-study-sun-universes-beginning/).
