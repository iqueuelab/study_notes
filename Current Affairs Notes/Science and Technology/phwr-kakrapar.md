# PHWRs and Kakrapar operating licences

## July2025 regulatory milestone
- **AERB** granted **NPCIL** five-year operating licences for **Kakrapar Units3and4,Gujarat**, India's first indigenous **700MWe PHWRs**; formally handed over **3July2025**. Permission for full-power commissioning came earlier: **KAPS3August2023;KAPS4August2024**. Licence, first criticality, full-power permission and commercial operation are distinct stages.
- Review covered design, siting,construction andcommissioning over approximately15years, with multi-tier assessment and technical-support organisations; operating experience at rated power informed the final licence. [AERB announcement](https://aerb.gov.in/storage/uploads/News/newsd7RsE.pdf).
- **PHWR:** heavy water(D₂O) moderator/coolant and natural-uranium fuel; supports indigenous design/manufacture, resource use and low-carbon electricity. Relative cost/safety depend on the design/project, not a universal advantage over every LWR.
- Common700MWe design supports the **ten-unit fleet-mode programme**; licensing these two does not prove ten more are already built. Independent review, emergency readiness and safe waste management remain necessary.
