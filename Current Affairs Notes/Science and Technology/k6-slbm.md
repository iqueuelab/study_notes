# K-6 reports and submarine-launched ballistic missiles

## Reported K-6 development
- The issue reports an **8,000-km, nuclear-capable K-6 SLBM** under development at DRDO's Advanced Naval Systems Laboratory, Hyderabad, intended for future S-5 ballistic-missile submarines. Range, Mach 7.5, dimensions and payload are **reported specifications, not independently confirmed operational capabilities**.
- **SSBN:** nuclear-powered submarine carrying ballistic missiles; the sea-based leg improves survivability of nuclear deterrence. **MIRV:** a missile deploys several re-entry vehicles capable of independently targeting different locations.
- The source's 12 m × 2 m dimensions cannot describe an S-5 submarine; do not reproduce its claims of K-3/K-5 testing/induction as established facts. High speed alone does not make interception impossible.
- **Agni-IV PYQ 2014:** surface-to-surface, two-stage solid-propellant missile; approximately 4,000-km range and one-tonne payload. Answer: **(a) 1 only**; liquid-only propulsion and 7,500-km range are incorrect.
