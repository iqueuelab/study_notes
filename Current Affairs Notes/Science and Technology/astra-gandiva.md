# Astra MkIII, Gandiva and ramjet propulsion

## Concepts and status

- Astra family: beyond-visual-range air-to-air weapons; reported Mk III/Gandiva development involves **Solid Fuel Ducted Ramjet**, not source's “dual-fuel” expansion. [DRDO SFDR demonstration](https://www.pib.gov.in/newsite/PrintRelease.aspx?lang=2&reg=48&relid=179682).
- Ramjet draws atmospheric oxygen; sustained thrust can improve engagement energy. BVR engagement still needs detection, tracking, guidance and aircraft integration.
- Source 340 km/190 km envelopes, Mach ranges and manoeuvre angles are reported development specifications, not universally demonstrated combat performance or proof of global superiority.
- Ground/technology trials, captive carriage, separation, live firing and operational induction are distinct. Planned Su-30 MKI/Tejas integration is not completed fleet-wide integration.
