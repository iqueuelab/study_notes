# Astra MkIII, Gandiva and ramjet propulsion

## Concepts and status
- Astra family: beyond-visual-range air-to-air weapons; reported Mk III/Gandiva development involves **Solid Fuel Ducted Ramjet**, not source's “dual-fuel” expansion. [DRDO SFDR demonstration](https://www.pib.gov.in/newsite/PrintRelease.aspx?lang=2&reg=48&relid=179682).
- Ramjet draws atmospheric oxygen; sustained thrust can improve engagement energy. BVR engagement still needs detection, tracking, guidance and aircraft integration.
- Source 340 km/190 km envelopes, Mach ranges and manoeuvre angles are reported development specifications, not universally demonstrated combat performance or proof of global superiority.
- Ground/technology trials, captive carriage, separation, live firing and operational induction are distinct. Planned Su-30 MKI/Tejas integration is not completed fleet-wide integration.

## July 11 indigenous-seeker flight tests
- DRDO and IAF flight-tested Astra with an **indigenous RF seeker from Su-30 MKI off Odisha**, with two launches against high-speed unmanned aerial targets under different range/aspect/platform conditions. This is the development reported in the July 12 clipping. [Defence release](https://www.pib.gov.in/Pressreleaseshare.aspx?PRID=2144118&lang=2&reg=48).
- **BDL** manufactures Astra. Source Mk-I recall: approximately **3.6 m, 178 mm, 154 kg, Mach 4.5**, head-on envelope **80–110 km** versus shorter **20–40 km** tail chase; these are indicative configuration/engagement figures, not every-shot guarantees. Mid-course inertial guidance and terminal active radar homing perform different tasks.
- **LOBL** acquires a lock before launch; **LOAL** acquires it after launch. All-weather capability does not remove targeting/network limitations. Mk-II and SFDR-based Mk-III development should be separated from Mk-I induction; ramjet propulsion alone does not establish a hypersonic operational weapon. Meteor is a multinational MBDA programme, not simply a French missile.
