# LR-AShM: hypersonic glide and coastal defence

## January 2026: planned Republic Day display
- **DRDO Long Range Anti-Ship Hypersonic Missile (LR-AShM)** and launcher to feature at the **77th Republic Day parade, Kartavya Path**. Designed for Indian Navy **coastal batteries**, engaging static and moving targets with indigenous avionics/terminal sensors; display announcement is not proof of fleet-wide operational induction.
- **Two-stage solid rocket boost → unpowered manoeuvring atmospheric glide**. Official description: quasi-ballistic, multiple skips, starting around **Mach 10**, average **Mach 5**; differs from an air-breathing scramjet cruise missile. Low-altitude manoeuvres and speed compress detection/interception time; avoid treating invisibility or immunity to all defences as absolute.
- Source graphic cites **about 1,500-km range**; strategic role is maritime deterrence/sea denial and **anti-access/area denial**. Indigenous development requires testing, targeting and reliable production as well as speed.
- DRDO's separate **Bharat Parv, Red Fort, 26–31 January 2026** tableau was announced on combat-submarine technologies. [Defence Ministry, 22 January announcement](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2217280&lang=1&noshow=1&reg=3).
