## Applications, institutions and legacy

- Central idea: use advanced science for **communication, education, weather, agriculture and national development**, alongside scientific research.
- **SITE, 1975–76**: Satellite Instructional Television Experiment; NASA's ATS-6 supported rural educational broadcasting. Conceived during his leadership and implemented after his death.
- **Aryabhata**, India's first satellite, launched in **1975**, not 1980; distinguishes satellite development from launch-vehicle milestones.
- Legacy centres: **VSSC, Thiruvananthapuram**; **Space Applications Centre, Ahmedabad**; contributions to **ECIL, Hyderabad**, and India's uranium/nuclear institutions including **UCIL, Jaduguda**. Later centres/institutional mergers should not all be described as personally founded before his death.
- Source also notes international roles: Physics Section, Indian Science Congress (**1962**); IAEA General Conference (**1970**); UN peaceful uses of atomic energy conference (**1971**).
- **Sarabhai crater** on the Moon; the source attributes 'Mahatma Gandhi of Indian Science' to A.P.J. Abdul Kalam.

## Exam distinctions

- **ISRO: 1969; Aryabhata: 1975; SITE: 1975–76**.
- The PDF's claim that SITE laid the foundation for a later Krishi Darshan is chronologically doubtful: do not use that causal sequence without checking the programme's earlier history.
- **Mangalyaan = Mars Orbiter Mission**; India succeeded on its first Mars-orbit attempt, but was not the second space entity to reach Mars.
- **GL-10 = NASA's electric aircraft demonstrator**. Both related PYQs were confirmed in the saved official **2016** UPSC Prelims GS-I paper and added to the common subject JSON.

## Ethics / Mains illustration

- Institution building, curiosity, interdisciplinary teamwork and public-purpose science; link innovation to social needs rather than list awards alone.
