# CL-20, HMX and LLM-105: energetic materials

## Explosive compounds and the 2025 PYQ
- **CL-20, HMX and LLM-105** are high-energy explosive materials associated with military weapons; **2025 PYQ answer (b)**. They are not refrigerant alternatives or ordinary jet fuel; the question asks for their common characteristic.
- **CL-20** is a caged nitramine; **HMX (Octogen)** is a military nitramine explosive; **LLM-105, 2,6-diamino-3,5-dinitropyrazine-1-oxide**, is an insensitive high explosive. The PDF's description of all three as “nitramines” is incorrect: LLM-105 is a pyrazine N-oxide. [LLNL research programme](https://pls.llnl.gov/research-and-development/materials-science/exprt).
- Warhead explosive and propellant fuel are distinct functions; avoid the absolute claim that an energetic compound can never appear in a specialised propellant formulation. The exam's common-characteristic answer remains military explosives.
