# Project Silica: archival data in glass

## February 2026: laser-written archival storage
- Microsoft Research's **Project Silica** uses femtosecond lasers to write microscopic three-dimensional **voxels** inside glass; optical imaging and machine learning decode patterns. Hundreds of layers increase density; this is archival research rather than an ordinary rewritable SSD substitute.
- **18 February 2026** research extends methods from specialised fused silica to cheaper, widely available **borosilicate glass**; parallel writing and simpler reading reduce practical barriers. Source cites a **120 × 120 × 2 mm** glass plate and roughly **4.8 TB** demonstration; capacity depends on material/encoding design.
- Accelerated ageing supports an estimated **10,000-year** persistence, not observation over 10,000 years. Passive durable media could reduce archive refresh/energy needs; writing/reading equipment, error correction, retrieval costs and future readability still matter. [Microsoft research](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/blog/project-silicas-advances-in-glass-storage-technology/).
