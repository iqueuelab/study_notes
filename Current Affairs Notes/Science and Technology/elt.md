# Extremely Large Telescope: optics and astronomical goals

## Observatory and optics
- **ESO**, intergovernmental astronomy organisation founded **1962**; ELT under construction at **Cerro Armazones, Atacama Desert, Chile**; optical/near-infrared observations.
- **39-m primary mirror, 798 hexagonal segments**, each about **1.4 m** across (source rounds to 1.5); **five-mirror design**. Adaptive optics/deformable mirror correct atmospheric distortion; high, dry site helps observation. [ESO mirrors](https://elt.eso.org/mirror/).
- Large aperture increases light collection and angular resolution; “16 times sharper than Hubble” is a wavelength/operating-condition comparison, not a universal performance promise. It complements space telescopes, not automatically surpasses JWST in every band/task.
## Science and institutions
- Exoplanet imaging/atmospheric spectra, early galaxies, dark matter/energy and gravity tests; habitability indicators are **not proof of extraterrestrial life**.
- ESO operates **La Silla, Paranal, Chajnantor** facilities; source membership **16 countries** is dated. **TMT / GMT** are separate large-telescope projects; India's TMT participation is not ELT membership.
- Source cost **€1.3 billion** is a dated estimate; engineering, adaptive optics, instruments and long-term funding determine delivery.
- **Later ESO schedule:** technical telescope first light **March 2029**, first scientific observations **December 2030**; these are planned milestones, not completed observations. [ESO update](https://www.eso.org/public/unitedkingdom/announcements/ann25001/).
