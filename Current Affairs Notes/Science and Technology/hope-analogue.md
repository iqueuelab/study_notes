# HOPE: Himalayan Outpost for Planetary Exploration

## Tso Kar analogue mission
- **HOPE=Himalayan Outpost for Planetary Exploration**, correcting “Human Outer Planet Exploration”. Inaugurated **31 July 2025**, **Tso Kar Valley, Ladakh**; industry partner **Protoplanet**, with ISRO's Human Space Flight Centre leading analogue research. Not NASA-led or a Mars landing.
- **Two crew**, planned **1–10 August 2025** isolation; experiments examine physiological, psychological, genomic/epigenetic responses, health monitoring, surface operations and sampling/microbiology.
- High UV, low pressure, cold extremes, saline permafrost simulate some early-Mars conditions. Connected **8 m living habitat/5 m utility module** support operations.
- Findings support protocols, habitat design, teamwork, resources and environmental adaptation; Earth cannot reproduce all off-world conditions. Earlier **Ladakh Human Analog Mission, November 2024**, and **Anugami, July 2025**, qualify any “first analogue study” claim. [ISRO 1 August](https://www.isro.gov.in/ISRO_EN/Space_Analog_Mission.html).
- Source comparisons: **HI-SEAS, Hawaii; Mars Desert Research Station, Utah; Iceland**. Private/public collaboration complements Gaganyaan/future exploration.
