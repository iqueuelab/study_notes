# Xenobiology: alternative biological chemistry

## October 2025: life beyond familiar chemistry
- Xenobiology investigates possible biological systems differing from conventional DNA/RNA/protein chemistry; synthetic biology engineers such alternatives, while astrobiology studies life in its cosmic context. It does not establish that extraterrestrial life has been found.
- Research includes additional synthetic DNA bases beyond A/T/C/G, alternative nucleic-acid backbones, non-standard amino acids and artificial cells/metabolic systems. Expanded-alphabet experiments are real research; complete self-sustaining silicon-based organisms or ammonia/methane-based ecosystems remain hypotheses, not demonstrated products.
- Titan's methane/ethane lakes motivate solvent questions; Europa/Enceladus have subsurface-ocean interest. Do not describe every icy moon as a demonstrated non-water-life habitat or replace all planetary water with methane.
- Possible applications: novel drugs/proteins, specialised bioremediation, origin-of-life tests, biosignatures and biological containment. Dependence on non-natural building blocks can help containment but cannot guarantee engineered organisms never escape, evolve or affect ecosystems.
- Research requires case-specific biosafety, ecological assessment, misuse controls and ethical oversight. The source's institution list is illustrative context, not verified evidence every named Indian institute has a dedicated xenobiology programme.
