Science and Technology

Majorana1, qubits and evidence for quantum progress

Editable notes

Basics and announcement

  • Qubit: quantum information unit; superposition and entanglement enable suitable quantum algorithms. Entanglement does not transmit messages instantly, and quantum computers are not faster for every task.
  • 19 February 2025 Microsoft Majorana1: claimed eight topological qubits, correcting PDF's four; million-qubit scale a design ambition, not a working million-qubit machine. Microsoft announcement.
  • 1937 Ettore Majorana: theoretical self-antiparticle fermion concept; condensed-matter Majorana zero modes are quasiparticle excitations, distinct from proof of a new fundamental particle.
  • Indium arsenide/aluminium hybrid structures seek topological superconductivity; superconductors show zero resistance under suitable conditions. Intended topological protection does not guarantee no errors under all disturbances.

Evidence and policy

  • Separate demonstrated device measurements, claimed topological interpretation, qubit operations and scalable fault tolerance; peer review of one measurement paper does not validate every company roadmap claim.
  • Prior controversial/retracted evidence underscores replication and open benchmarking; do not replace hype with the equally absolute claim no relevant experimental evidence can exist.
  • Applications may include simulation/optimisation and, with sufficiently capable machines, threats to some existing public-key cryptography; not instant defeat of every encryption method today.
  • India's National Quantum Mission2023: ₹6,003.65 crore,2023–24 to2030–31, computing, communication, sensing/metrology and materials/devices. Institutions include IISc, IITs, TIFR/RRI with strategic-agency work.
  • Invest in skills, rigorous research, hardware validation and post-quantum cryptographic transition; count useful logical performance, not physical qubits alone.
  • 2022 PYQ: “qubit” →quantum computing, not cloud, visible-light or wireless communication.
  • Cabinet approval of NQM confirms outlay and2023–31 period.

September revision and 2025 PYQ

  • Existing notes' Microsoft attribution, indium-arsenide/aluminium platform and caveats about claimed topological protection remain applicable; the article revisits protection against noise, not proof of a functioning fault-tolerant million-qubit computer.
  • 2025 PYQ: Majorana 1 is intended for quantum computing; it is not AWS's chip; deep learning is a subset of machine learning → I and III only (c). 2022 qubit PYQ already banked.
