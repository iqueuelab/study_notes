# LHS 1903: rocky and gaseous exoplanets

## Four planets and gas-depleted formation
- **LHS 1903**, a red dwarf about **117 light-years** away, has four characterised planets. From inner to outer: **b rocky; c and d with extended gaseous atmospheres; e without a substantial gas envelope**. CHEOPS observations combined with transit and radial-velocity data constrain radii/masses and hence densities. [Discovery paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.11271).
- Orbital periods range **2.2–29.3 days**. The system spans the **radius valley** separating small super-Earths and sub-Neptunes; those are size/population categories rather than automatic proofs of a specific composition.
- The outer rocky planet challenges a simple expectation that cooler outer small planets retain gas. Researchers favour formation from **gas-depleted material**, consistent with later assembly after disc gas dwindled. Atmospheric loss is a general alternative mechanism for the radius valley, not an equally established explanation of this planet.
- The source's roughly **60°C** temperature estimate does not demonstrate a habitable surface, atmosphere or life. Distinguish equilibrium-temperature estimates from measured surface conditions.
