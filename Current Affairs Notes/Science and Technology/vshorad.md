# VSHORADS: portable short-range air defence

## System and trial
- **Very Short-Range Air Defence System**, indigenous **MANPADS**, developed by **DRDO Research Centre Imarat** with other laboratories and production partners; close-range defence against low-flying aerial threats.
- **1 February 2025, Chandipur, Odisha**: three trials intercepted high-speed, low-altitude targets with reduced thermal signatures simulating drones. Two field operators performed readiness, acquisition and firing in final deployment configuration. [MoD release](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2098784&lang=2&reg=48).
- Infrared homing; source approximate **6 km** range and supersonic flight. Compare **Stinger—US**, **Igla-S—Russia**, but variant-dependent ranges are not fixed universal specifications.
- Designed to meet Army/Navy/Air Force needs; successful development trials **do not establish operational induction in all three services**.
## Strategic significance
- Mobile battlefield/local asset defence, including drone threats; reduced import dependence, domestic production and possible export potential.
- Must integrate detection, identification, command and ammunition supply; not a universal counter to every UAV, swarm or high-altitude aircraft.
- Source defence question is **practice, no verified PYQ year**.
