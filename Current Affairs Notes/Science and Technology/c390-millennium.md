# C-390 Millennium: Embraer–Mahindra cooperation

## 17 October 2025: cooperation for India's transport-aircraft programme
- Brazil's **Embraer Defense & Security** and **Mahindra Group** signed a Strategic Cooperation Agreement, building on their **February 2024 MoU**, to advance the C-390 bid for the IAF's **Medium Transport Aircraft (MTA)** programme. Joint marketing, industrialisation and developing India as a manufacturing/support hub are intended cooperation: the agreement is **not an IAF aircraft purchase order or proof Indian production has started**. [Embraer announcement](https://www.embraer.com/media-center/en?detail=22551&mediatype=NEWS&page=1).
- C-390 Millennium is a **medium-lift, multi-mission military transport**: manufacturer quotes **26-tonne payload and 470-knot cruise speed** (about 870 km/h), temporary/unpaved-runway operations, troop/cargo transport, airdrop, medical evacuation, search and rescue, firefighting and humanitarian relief. Refuelling configuration supports tanker/receiver roles. [Mahindra filing](https://www.mahindra.com/sites/default/files/2025-10/SEIntimationEmbrarerandMahindrasignAgreement17thOctober2025.pdf).
- The source lists selected customer countries as though all were already operational users; selection, delivery and operational induction are different milestones. Range depends on payload/configuration; do not turn its broad 2,000–3,000-km figure into a fixed aircraft limit.
- Potential benefits: local aerospace skills/suppliers, MRO, export support, supplier diversification and India–Brazil/South–South technology cooperation. Real benefits depend on procurement selection, contractual technology/IP access, costs, certification and reliable delivery. C295 and C-390 occupy different payload classes; an existing C295 assembly line does not itself settle the MTA competition.
- **2025 PYQ recall:** Dornier-228 is used for maritime patrol; **IL-76 is a transport, not a supersonic combat aircraft**; C-17 is a military transport. **Two pairs correct, (b)**. The complete question already exists in the common bank.

## February 2026: conditional India production-hub proposal
- Embraer's proposed Indian **KC/C-390 manufacturing and Asia-Pacific support hub remains conditional on IAF selection** in its medium-transport-aircraft competition; the source's 60–80 requirement is an indicative procurement range, not confirmed orders.
- Preserve the distinction between **Embraer–Mahindra's military C-390 bid** and **Embraer–Adani's civil E175 proposal**. Cargo/troop airlift, medical evacuation, HADR and tanker configurations offer operational flexibility; procurement/technology-transfer outcomes remain contingent. Aircraft PYQ is already in the common bank.
