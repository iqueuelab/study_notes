# E175 regional jets: proposed Indian assembly

## February 2026: enhanced industrial MoU
- **21 February 2026:** Adani Defence & Aerospace and Brazil's **Embraer** exchanged an enhanced MoU proposing an **E175 final assembly line** under a regional transport aircraft ecosystem. It is a proposed industrial arrangement, not proof the line is operational or a final aircraft order. [Embraer announcement](https://status.embraer.com/media-center/en?detail=24365&mediatype=NEWS&page=46).
- E175 seats **up to 88**; intended market includes smaller Indian cities. Final assembly brings major structures/systems together, followed by testing and certification; local content, technology access and MRO/supplier development depend on contracts and capability.
- Connectivity, skilled jobs and India–Brazil cooperation are potential benefits. Demand, financing, certification, airport suitability and operating economics remain constraints; regional-jet size alone does not prove compatibility with every short runway or eligibility for a UDAN route subsidy.
