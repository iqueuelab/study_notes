# Typhoid, paratyphoid and vaccine development

## Disease
- Enteric fever: **Salmonella enterica serovar Typhi /Paratyphi**; faecal–oral transmission through contaminated water/food. Not caused by a virus or by ordinary airborne spread.
- Fever, gastrointestinal illness and systemic complications; sanitation, safe water, surveillance and antimicrobial resistance are central public-health issues. Source's **360/100,000 /4.5 million annual cases** need study/year context.
## ICMR vaccine initiative
- Source reports **ICMR–NIRBI, Kolkata** candidate using bacterial **outer-membrane vesicles**, targeting Typhi and Paratyphi A, with calls for industrial collaboration.
- Development/technical validation →safety/efficacy assessment →regulatory approval →manufacture/market; an expression of interest is **not a licence or proof of clinical efficacy**.
- ICMR calls for typhoid/paratyphoid vaccine commercialisation predate this February report; do not infer every reported candidate is a newly licensed 2025 product. [ICMR commercialisation invitation](https://www.icmr.gov.in/icmrobject/custom_data/1720273182_eoi_salmonella_re_publication.pdf).
## Vaccine distinctions
- **TCV:** conjugate vaccines, usable from six months for approved products; **Vi polysaccharide:** typically ≥two years; **Ty21a:** oral live vaccine with product-specific eligibility.
- Existing typhoid vaccines do not automatically protect equally against all paratyphoid strains. Availability differs by programme/product; “only private sector everywhere” is too broad.
- Vaccination complements WASH and surveillance; antibiotic choice requires resistance-aware clinical assessment, not memorising the source's drug list as guaranteed treatment.
