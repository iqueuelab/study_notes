# T-72 tanks and engine upgrades

## Upgrade rather than new fleet
- A March 2025 contract with Rosoboronexport, reported at US$248 million, concerns **1,000 horsepower engines** replacing existing 780 HP engines; 1,000 is the power rating, not the number ordered.
- Supply and technology transfer involve fully built and knocked-down configurations, with work at Heavy Vehicles Factory, Avadi.
- T-72 is a Soviet-origin tank family; variant-dependent features include a 125 mm smoothbore gun, autoloader and machine guns.
- More engine power can improve mobility; outcomes depend on transmission, reliability, maintenance and terrain. Local assembly and technology transfer are not identical to wholly indigenous components or intellectual property.
