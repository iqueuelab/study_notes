# CEREBO: portable traumatic brain injury triage

## Device and clinical role
- **CEREBO**, by **Bioscan Research**, uses near-infrared spectroscopy with machine learning to assess traumatic brain injury/intracranial bleeding; handheld, non-invasive point-of-care screening for rapid triage/monitoring. [BIRAC product record](https://www.birac.nic.in/product-detail.php?product=221).
- Source describes collaboration/validation involving **ICMR–MDMS, AIIMS Bhopal and NIMHANS Bengaluru**, under **mPRiDE**, and colour-coded results with a short training period. Near-infrared means **no ionising radiation**, not literally no electromagnetic radiation.
- Potential in ambulances, rural clinics, disaster/trauma settings and repeated bedside assessment, including where imaging is unavailable. Positive findings/clinical concern require appropriate CT/MRI, referral and treatment; it does **not replace neurosurgery or all diagnostic imaging**.
- “Under one minute”, training time, injury/death totals and half-of-deaths-within-two-hours are source-reported performance/burden claims, not guarantees for every patient. Two hours should not be renamed the conventional **golden hour**. Implementation needs operator training, validated indications, referral pathways, maintenance and affordable access.
