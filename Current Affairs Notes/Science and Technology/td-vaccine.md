# Td vaccine and indigenous immunisation supply

## February 2026: CRI vaccine launch
- Health Minister launched CRI Kasauli's indigenously manufactured **tetanus and adult diphtheria (Td) vaccine on 21 February 2026**; the compilation's future-tense launch description is outdated. CRI is under **DGHS, Health Ministry**. Planned UIP supply: **55 lakh doses by April 2026**, a target rather than doses already administered. [Official launch](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2231147).
- Td combines inactivated tetanus and diphtheria **toxoids**; an adjuvant improves immune response and a preservative may protect multidose formulations. Cold-chain protection, especially against freezing, matters; formulation ingredients should be checked for the actual licensed product.
- **Tetanus:** *Clostridium tetani*, toxin-mediated muscle rigidity/spasms/lockjaw; spores enter wounds, with no person-to-person transmission. **Diphtheria:** toxigenic *Corynebacterium diphtheriae*, often respiratory spread, throat membrane and possible cardiac/neurological complications. “Non-communicable” here describes tetanus transmission, not classification as a lifestyle NCD.
- Td extends diphtheria protection while maintaining tetanus immunity for adolescents/pregnancy under the immunisation schedule; indigenous supply complements coverage, cold chain and surveillance. The UPPCS question is outside the UPSC CSE bank.
