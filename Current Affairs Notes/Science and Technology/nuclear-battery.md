# Betavoltaic nuclear batteries

## Electricity from decay
- **Betavoltaic:** beta particles from a radioisotope interact with a semiconductor to generate electricity directly. **Radioisotope thermoelectric generator (RTG):** decay heat → thermoelectric conversion. Neither requires a controlled reactor chain reaction.
- **Nickel-63 → stable copper-63** through beta decay; BV100 uses a diamond semiconductor. Radioactive decay is an energy source, not rechargeable chemical storage.
- Betavolt's **50-year life**, temperature range **−60°C to +120°C** and high energy-density figures are manufacturer claims. Long life does not mean constant output forever: activity declines as the isotope decays.
- The manufacturer’s **8 January 2024** announcement reports BV100 output of **100 microwatts at 3 V**, far below ordinary smartphone/drone power requirements. A proposed one-watt model is a development target; combining cells adds material, volume and cost. [Betavolt announcement](https://www.betavolt.tech/646785-646785_2562735.html).
## Opportunities and limits
- Best conceptual fit: ultra-low-power remote sensors, microelectronics and difficult-to-service aerospace systems; medical implants require device-specific safety/clinical approval. Not every implant or AI device can run on such low power.
- Advantages: long operation without charging, compactness and potential performance where solar/chemical batteries are impractical. **High total energy density ≠ high instantaneous power.**
- Isotope supply, semiconductor cost, containment, radiation protection, licensing, damage scenarios and end-of-life management matter. A stable decay product does not remove radioactive-material obligations during service or permit ordinary disposal.
- City Labs develops **tritium-based** betavoltaics; carbon-14/diamond research is another approach. These are different isotopes/platforms, not proof every announced product is commercially ready. [City Labs technology](https://citylabs.net/technology-overview/).
- Indian R&D opportunities: sensors, space/defence applications, materials and reliable packaging, with appropriate nuclear regulatory oversight. Do not infer India has no research merely from limited coverage.
## Practice recall
- Direct conversion of **beta-particle energy**, not heat, sunlight or a chemical reaction, identifies betavoltaic technology. The supplied multiple-choice exercise has no exam year and is practice, not an authenticated UPSC PYQ.
