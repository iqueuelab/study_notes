# SHAKTI–IRIS and India’s semiconductor ecosystem

## Indigenous processor: division of work
- **IRIS-LV**, SHAKTI-based **180 nm** processor; conceived by ISRO's **IISU, Thiruvananthapuram**, designed by **IIT Madras**; software booted by IIT Madras.
- Fabricated at **SCL, Chandigarh** → packaged by **Tata Advanced Systems, Karnataka** → motherboard PCB by **PCB Power, Gujarat** → assembled by **Syrma SGS, Chennai**. [IIT Madras announcement](https://www.iitm.ac.in/hi/node/17019).
- **RISC-V:** open instruction-set architecture; no ISA royalty does not imply every processor design, fabrication tool or component is automatically open/free. SHAKTI designs support customised embedded/strategic applications.
- Mature **180 nm** technology can suit rugged industrial/space applications; it is not a leading-edge 2 nm commercial fabrication achievement. Radiation tolerance and space qualification need specific testing.
## Semiconductor chain
| Stage | Recall |
| --- | --- |
| Design | Architecture, circuits, verification; EDA tools and intellectual property |
| Fabrication | Patterning/transistors on wafers; capital-intensive cleanrooms |
| Packaging/test | Interconnections, protection, validation; ATMP/OSAT capabilities |
| Systems | Boards, software, application integration |
- Chips underpin electronics, communication, vehicles, AI and defence. India's design workforce strengths coexist with imported fabrication equipment/materials and limited domestic fabrication capacity.
- **India Semiconductor Mission / semiconductor and display programme:** support fabs, compound semiconductors, ATMP and design; PLI and design-linked incentives have different purposes. Announced facilities ≠ operational mass production.
- Benefits: supply resilience, strategic autonomy, skilled employment and domestic value addition. Constraints: capital, yields, reliable power/water, chemicals, waste control, talent and vendor ecosystems.
- Efficient cooling, renewable electricity, water reuse and hazardous-waste treatment matter. Source claim **31% of global greenhouse emissions from semiconductors** is unsupported; do not memorise it.
- Policy test: competitive sustained output and locally retained skills/IP, rather than subsidies or an “indigenous” label alone.
## Imports and ecosystem gaps
- Source FY2024 monolithic ICimports~₹1.05 lakhcrore and FY2025 April–November 2.09%total-import share are different periods; customs classifications/base effects complicate 2000–4800%growth claims.
- Semicon India 2021 supports fabrication/display/assembly-test-packaging ecosystem; Tata Dholerafab/Tata Morigaonassembly projects have different technology stages.
- Imports still cover EDAtools, core IP, wafers, manufacturingequipment and components. Indian chip-design talent does not imply all toolchains/materials are domestic.
- Source“first Indianchip 2024”is an outdated forecast and ignores earlier domestic chips; commercialfab milestones must be precisely labelled.
- Source actual spending/BE/RE mixes ₹681.11 crore and ₹1, 503.36 crore for FY2024; reconcile series before asserting utilisation. Bigcaps, long gestation, power/water, skills and supply chains require coordinated investment.
- USCHIPSAct is USlegislation, not an international alliance India joins; Quad semiconductor cooperation is a separate mechanism.

## 14 May 2025 : Jewar unit approved
- Cabinet approved **HCL–Foxconn joint venture near Jewar airport, YEIDA, Uttar Pradesh**, about **₹3700 crore**, for display-driver chips used in phones/laptops/PCs/vehicles. Sixth unit approved under the mission; approval is not commissioned production. [Cabinet decision](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2128604&lang=2&reg=3).
- Planned capacity **20000 wafers/month,36 millionchips/month** concerns different production-stage units. Source’s “first UP chip plant” is a State/project-specific claim, not India’s first-ever domestic chip.
- The clipping quotes a claim of meeting~40% domestic demand once operational; forecast depends on product mix/yields/demand. Possible later display-panel investment is an expectation, not part of an already operating panel fab.

## 12 August 2025: four additional projects
- Cabinet approvals brought ISM's total from **six to ten projects**; combined new investment source **₹4,594 crore**, rounded official figure **₹4,600 crore**. Programme outlay **₹76,000 crore** dates to December 2021; investment approval is not operational production.
| Company / location | Proposed activity |
|---|---|
| **SiCSem, Info Valley, Bhubaneswar** | Integrated silicon-carbide compound-semiconductor facility |
| **3D Glass Solutions, Bhubaneswar** | Advanced packaging and embedded glass substrates |
| **CDIL, Mohali, Punjab** | Expansion of discrete-device production, including silicon/SiC devices |
| **ASIP Technologies, Andhra Pradesh** | Semiconductor packaging/manufacturing; exact site unspecified in this issue |
- **SiC** suits high-voltage/high-temperature power electronics: EVs, chargers, rail, defence and renewable-energy conversion. Glass substrates/interposers and system-in-package integration improve interconnection and compactness; packaging is a distinct stage from wafer fabrication.
- The source's older five-facility insert predates these approvals. Its end-2025 commercial-chip expectation is a forecast, not India's first-ever chip. Existing notes retain dated import/spending qualifications and EDA/IP/material/equipment gaps. Practice **1 only (A)**: support is not restricted to silicon. [Cabinet release](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2155459&lang=1&reg=37).

## SEMICON India: September 2025
- PM's **2 September** address linked faster Centre–State approvals through the National Single Window System with predictable long-term semiconductor policy. Source investment snapshot: **US$18 billion / over ₹1.5 lakh crore across ten approved projects**; ten approvals were already recorded in August and are not ten operational fabs. [PM address](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2163000&lang=2&reg=3).
- **VIKRAM3201**, presented to the PM, is VSSC–SCL's fully domestic 32-bit processor qualified for launch-vehicle conditions; upgrades **VIKRAM1601**, flying since **2009**. Not India's first-ever processor. First production lots of VIKRAM3201 and KALPANA3201 were handed over on **5 March 2025**; September presentation is a distinct milestone. [ISRO](https://www.isro.gov.in/vikram3201.html).
- Used in launch-vehicle avionics; trusted design/fabrication supports strategic autonomy. The source showcases **31 academic prototype chips**; a prototype is not mass commercial production. Its five units under construction, one pilot line and two expected production starts describe status/expectations at this event.
