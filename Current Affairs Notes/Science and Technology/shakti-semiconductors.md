# SHAKTI–IRIS and India’s semiconductor ecosystem

## Indigenous processor: division of work
- **IRIS-LV**, SHAKTI-based **180 nm** processor; conceived by ISRO's **IISU, Thiruvananthapuram**, designed by **IIT Madras**; software booted by IIT Madras.
- Fabricated at **SCL, Chandigarh** → packaged by **Tata Advanced Systems, Karnataka** → motherboard PCB by **PCB Power, Gujarat** → assembled by **Syrma SGS, Chennai**. [IIT Madras announcement](https://www.iitm.ac.in/hi/node/17019).
- **RISC-V:** open instruction-set architecture; no ISA royalty does not imply every processor design, fabrication tool or component is automatically open/free. SHAKTI designs support customised embedded/strategic applications.
- Mature **180 nm** technology can suit rugged industrial/space applications; it is not a leading-edge 2 nm commercial fabrication achievement. Radiation tolerance and space qualification need specific testing.
## Semiconductor chain
| Stage | Recall |
| --- | --- |
| Design | Architecture, circuits, verification; EDA tools and intellectual property |
| Fabrication | Patterning/transistors on wafers; capital-intensive cleanrooms |
| Packaging/test | Interconnections, protection, validation; ATMP/OSAT capabilities |
| Systems | Boards, software, application integration |
- Chips underpin electronics, communication, vehicles, AI and defence. India's design workforce strengths coexist with imported fabrication equipment/materials and limited domestic fabrication capacity.
- **India Semiconductor Mission / semiconductor and display programme:** support fabs, compound semiconductors, ATMP and design; PLI and design-linked incentives have different purposes. Announced facilities ≠ operational mass production.
- Benefits: supply resilience, strategic autonomy, skilled employment and domestic value addition. Constraints: capital, yields, reliable power/water, chemicals, waste control, talent and vendor ecosystems.
- Efficient cooling, renewable electricity, water reuse and hazardous-waste treatment matter. Source claim **31% of global greenhouse emissions from semiconductors** is unsupported; do not memorise it.
- Policy test: competitive sustained output and locally retained skills/IP, rather than subsidies or an “indigenous” label alone.
## Imports and ecosystem gaps
- Source FY2024 monolithic ICimports~₹1.05 lakhcrore and FY2025 April–November 2.09%total-import share are different periods; customs classifications/base effects complicate 2000–4800%growth claims.
- Semicon India 2021 supports fabrication/display/assembly-test-packaging ecosystem; Tata Dholerafab/Tata Morigaonassembly projects have different technology stages.
- Imports still cover EDAtools, core IP, wafers, manufacturingequipment and components. Indian chip-design talent does not imply all toolchains/materials are domestic.
- Source“first Indianchip 2024”is an outdated forecast and ignores earlier domestic chips; commercialfab milestones must be precisely labelled.
- Source actual spending/BE/RE mixes ₹681.11 crore and ₹1, 503.36 crore for FY2024; reconcile series before asserting utilisation. Bigcaps, long gestation, power/water, skills and supply chains require coordinated investment.
- USCHIPSAct is USlegislation, not an international alliance India joins; Quad semiconductor cooperation is a separate mechanism.

## 14 May 2025 : Jewar unit approved
- Cabinet approved **HCL–Foxconn joint venture near Jewar airport, YEIDA, Uttar Pradesh**, about **₹3700 crore**, for display-driver chips used in phones/laptops/PCs/vehicles. Sixth unit approved under the mission; approval is not commissioned production. [Cabinet decision](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2128604&lang=2&reg=3).
- Planned capacity **20000 wafers/month,36 millionchips/month** concerns different production-stage units. Source’s “first UP chip plant” is a State/project-specific claim, not India’s first-ever domestic chip.
- The clipping quotes a claim of meeting~40% domestic demand once operational; forecast depends on product mix/yields/demand. Possible later display-panel investment is an expectation, not part of an already operating panel fab.

## SEMICON India: September 2025
- PM's **2 September** address linked faster Centre–State approvals through the National Single Window System with predictable long-term semiconductor policy. Source investment snapshot: **US$18 billion / over ₹1.5 lakh crore across ten approved projects**; ten approvals were already recorded in August and are not ten operational fabs. [PM address](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2163000&lang=2&reg=3).
- **VIKRAM3201**, presented to the PM, is VSSC–SCL's fully domestic 32-bit processor qualified for launch-vehicle conditions; upgrades **VIKRAM1601**, flying since **2009**. Not India's first-ever processor. First production lots of VIKRAM3201 and KALPANA3201 were handed over on **5 March 2025**; September presentation is a distinct milestone. [ISRO](https://www.isro.gov.in/vikram3201.html).
- Used in launch-vehicle avionics; trusted design/fabrication supports strategic autonomy. The source showcases **31 academic prototype chips**; a prototype is not mass commercial production. Its five units under construction, one pilot line and two expected production starts describe status/expectations at this event.

## 15 December 2025: DHRUV64 unveiled
- **C-DAC DHRUV64**, under MeitY **Microprocessor Development Programme**, **1 GHz, 64-bit, dual-core** processor; December announcement describes indigenous development and **DIR-V/RISC-V** ecosystem support. Suitable application areas include communications, automotive/industrial control, embedded/IoT and strategic systems; this is not evidence it matches leading laptop/phone CPUs. [Government backgrounder](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressNoteDetails.aspx?ModuleId=3&NoteId=156505&id=156505&lang=1&reg=6).
- Open **RISC-V instruction-set architecture** avoids ISA licence fees; it does not automatically make the core design, fabrication tools or complete chip supply chain open/free/domestic. **Indigenous design, ISA, fabrication, packaging, toolchain and IP ownership are separate questions**. Source discloses no clear fabrication node/foundry, yield, performance-per-watt or full benchmark set; do not fill these gaps by inference.
- DHRUV64 supports prototyping by startups/industry/academia. Ecosystem: **SHAKTI—IIT Madras; AJIT—IIT Bombay; VIKRAM—ISRO/SCL; THEJAS—C-DAC**. Existing notes already correct the VIKRAM timeline: earlier variants predate 2025; no claim DHRUV64 is India's first processor of every kind.
- **THEJAS32 fabricated at Silterra, Malaysia; THEJAS64 at SCL Mohali**. Government identifies DHRUV64 as a third DIR-V chip; that does not establish every stage was fabricated domestically. **Dhanush/Dhanush+** are development-roadmap processors; the source's frequency/core/node targets are plans, not released silicon specifications.
- Supporting programmes **C2S, DLI, INUP-i2i and ISM** address training, designs, laboratory access and fabrication/packaging investment. Source's **ten approved ISM projects/about ₹1.6 lakh crore across six States** is an approval snapshot, not ten operational fabs; source's **₹250-crore C2S** figure lacks verified scheme-scope definition and is not retained as settled national outlay.
- Adoption needs developer boards/software, validated operating-system/toolchain support, security audits, reliable supply and manufacturing tests. Procurement demand can reduce commercial risk, but “indigenous” alone does not prove absence of vulnerabilities.
- **2025 Majorana 1 PYQ repeats existing bank: statements I/III, (c)**. Microsoft introduced Majorana 1; deep learning is a subset of machine learning. The PDF's stronger explanation that practical topological quantum computing is already achieved overstates the announced evidence.

## December 2025: SCL modernisation and mature-node value
- Government announced **₹4,500 crore SCL Mohali modernisation on 28 November 2025**, with assurance against privatisation; proposed wafer-capacity expansion is a target, not achieved output. **28 chips from 17 academic institutions** handed over; cumulative **56 C2S student-designed chips** fabricated. [MeitY](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2196011&lang=1&reg=3).
- **SCL is under MeitY**, having moved from Department of Space; its integrated design/fabrication/packaging/test capability supports strategic ASICs, optoelectronics, MEMS, academic prototypes and indigenous processors. **180 nm CMOS** is a mature node, distinct from leading-edge 3/5 nm technologies. [SCL introduction](https://www.scl.gov.in/introduction.html).
- Newspaper recalls plant setup/inauguration **1983**, production **1984**, fire **1989**; these milestones should not be confused with the earlier institutional/company formation. Fire was an important setback, not proof SCL once led the entire world or the sole cause of subsequent industry gaps.
- Proposed role includes trusted legacy/strategic chips, student/startup access, LED/optoelectronic and high-volume products. Larger transistors can serve useful rugged/industrial functions; “legacy” does not mean no strategic value. Modernisation is not evidence a leading-edge commercial fab is already operating.
- Cleanrooms, yields, equipment/process know-how, skills, reliable utilities and suppliers matter alongside investment. **September SEMICON India** clipping—ten approved projects, US$18 billion/₹1.5 lakh crore, faster approvals and ₹76,000-crore programme—repeats the existing September record; **7.8% GDP growth** is an older quarter-specific reference, not a new December annual result.

## 25 December 2025: self-reliance claim at Gwalior summit
- At the **Abhyudaya Madhya Pradesh Growth Summit, Gwalior**, Home Minister **Amit Shah** said India would become semiconductor self-reliant and an exporter. This is a **policy expectation**, not evidence all chips/equipment are already domestically produced or a newly commissioned fab at this event.
- Existing notes distinguish **design → fabrication → packaging/testing → systems/software**, and approval/prototype/production stages. Strategic resilience still needs technology, yields, skilled staff, supplier ecosystems, competitive scale, reliable power/water and environmental safeguards; exports can coexist with imports.
- Source also reports **₹2 lakh-crore-plus industrial projects** across Madhya Pradesh and praises regional investment conclaves. Distinguish announced/foundation-stone/inauguration components and projected jobs from fully operating output. Regional clusters can widen investment beyond major cities but require actual logistics, skills and market access.

## 28 February 2026: Micron Sanand commercial production
- PM inaugurated **Micron's Sanand, Gujarat ATMP facility**; commercial assembly/test production began. It converts **DRAM/NAND wafers from Micron's global fabrication network** into finished memory/storage products. This is a major backend manufacturing milestone, **not India's first-ever wafer fab or processor**. [PMO inauguration record](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2233998&lang=1&reg=3), [Micron explanation](https://micron.gcs-web.com/news-releases/news-release-details/micron-celebrates-opening-indias-first-semiconductor-assembly).
- **ATMP = assembly, testing, marking, packaging; OSAT = outsourced semiconductor assembly and test.** Packaging/test supports yield, interconnection and reliability but differs from fabrication of transistors on wafers. The source's adjacent **Tata Dholera wafer-fab graphic** describes a different project; planned capacity/output there is not Sanand production.
- **ISM 2.0 in Budget 2026–27** emphasises equipment/materials, full-stack Indian chip IP, supply resilience, research/training and industry-led capability. Announcement/design of support differs from every component already being operational. [Budget announcement](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleaseIframePage.aspx?PRID=2221522&lang=2&reg=3).
- Existing notes retain programme outlay, DLI/C2S, skills, cleanrooms/utilities, capital/technology and environmental constraints. The source's **Taiwan 66%** chart concerns an unspecified segment/year and must not be read as its share of every semiconductor product or fabrication capacity; no automatic fall in all electronics prices follows from one facility.
