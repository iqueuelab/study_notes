# Chikungunya: vectors, disease and vaccines

## Disease and vector control
- **CHIKV: RNA Alphavirus, Togaviridae**; identified in **southern Tanzania, 1952**. Kimakonde name refers to a bent posture from severe joint pain.
- Infected female **Aedes aegypti/Aedes albopictus**, mainly daytime biters, transmit disease; these also transmit dengue/Zika. Ae. aegypti is an important yellow-fever vector, but do not equate both species' vector roles for every disease.
- Sudden fever, severe/prolonged joint pain, joint swelling, headache, muscle pain and rash; dengue/Zika overlap complicates diagnosis. Africa/Asia/Americas predominate, with outbreaks in Europe/Pacific; travel, vector suitability and climate affect spread.
- PCR early/serology later; no specific antiviral. Symptomatic treatment, rest/hydration; **paracetamol initially**, delay aspirin/NSAIDs until dengue is excluded because of bleeding risk. [WHO, 14 April 2025](https://www.who.int/en/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/chikungunya).
- Reduce container/stagnant-water breeding, improve sanitation, repellents/clothing and appropriate nets; community surveillance and early reporting complement clinical care. National vector-borne programme covers **malaria, dengue, chikungunya, lymphatic filariasis, kala-azar and Japanese encephalitis**, now coordinated through NCVBDC.
## Vaccines: distinguish dates and safety
- **Ixchiq**, Valneva, single-dose intramuscular **live-attenuated** vaccine: FDA first approval **9 November 2023**, adults **18+** at increased exposure risk. US approval does not establish Indian approval/universal routine use.
- **9 May 2025:** FDA/CDC recommended pausing Ixchiq for **60+** while reviewing serious postmarketing neurologic/cardiac reports. Reported events are not automatically proved vaccine-caused; source's “only mild symptoms” understates recognised risk. [FDA dated safety communication](https://www.fda.gov/vaccines-blood-biologics/safety-availability-biologics/fda-and-cdc-recommend-pause-use-ixchiq-chikungunya-vaccine-live-individuals-60-years-age-and-older).
- **Vimkunya**, non-live virus-like-particle vaccine, FDA approval **14 February 2025**, **12+**: by May 2025 Ixchiq was no longer the only approved product. [FDA product/approval letter](https://www.fda.gov/vaccines-blood-biologics/vimkunya). Vaccines supplement vector control; availability and recommendations remain jurisdiction-specific.
