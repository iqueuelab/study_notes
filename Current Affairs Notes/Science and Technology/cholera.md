# Cholera and waterborne-disease control

## Infection and prevention
- Acute diarrhoeal infection from **Vibrio cholerae**, transmitted by contaminated water/food. Epidemic disease chiefly **O1/O139**, with **O1 responsible for recent outbreaks**; infection ranges from asymptomatic to severe rice-water diarrhoea, vomiting, cramps and rapidly fatal dehydration.
- Timely **oral rehydration**, IV fluids for severe dehydration and appropriate antibiotics for severe cases save lives. Safe water, sewage treatment, hygiene, surveillance/rapid response and oral vaccination work together; vaccination cannot replace WASH. [WHO disease facts](https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/cholera).
## Historical trend versus the May 2025 issue
- Source refers to **2022**, not new May cases: WHO reports **472,697 cases versus 223,370 in 2021**, more than double. Countries/regions including Africa and Asia, Lebanon, Syria and Afghanistan experienced outbreaks; inconsistent reporting means notifications are not the full incidence burden. Source Nigeria/25%-share comparisons need matching regional denominators. [WHO 2022 report](https://iris.who.int/bitstream/handle/10665/372993/WER9838-431-43.pdf?isAllowed=y&sequence=1).
- **Ending Cholera: global roadmap to 2030**, launched **2017** by the **GTFCC** partnership: target **90% reduction in deaths by 2030**; targeted multisectoral control/elimination in countries is not a claim of completed worldwide eradication.
## Vaccine correction
- Source list **Dukoral, Shanchol, Euvichol-Plus** is outdated: **Euvichol-S prequalified 12 April 2024**; Shanchol production stopped in **2022**, last doses distributed **2023**. [WHO vaccine record](https://extranet.who.int/prequal/vaccines/p/euvicholr-s), [WHO 2023 annual report](https://cdn.who.int/media/docs/default-source/dco/wer_36_2024_cholera-annual-report-for-2023_bilingual-proof.pdf).
- Standard full adult protection generally uses two doses; **single-dose outbreak regimens since October 2022 during supply shortage** give shorter-term protection. Source “all require two doses” omits this policy and age/product differences. Track prequalification, production, availability and campaign schedule separately.
