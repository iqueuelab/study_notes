# Nobel Physics 2025: macroscopic quantum tunnelling

## October 2025: quantum behaviour of a circuit
- John Clarke, Michel H. Devoret and John M. Martinis received the Physics Nobel for macroscopic quantum mechanical tunnelling and energy quantisation in an electric circuit. Their 1980s experiments demonstrated collective quantum behaviour in superconducting circuits. [Nobel announcement](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/physics/2025/press-release/).
- A Josephson junction couples superconductors across a thin barrier; Cooper-pair tunnelling permits supercurrent. The prize experiment concerns the circuit's collective electrical variable escaping a metastable state by quantum tunnelling and exhibiting discrete energy levels, not merely the original discovery of current across a junction.
- Quantisation means allowed circuit energies are discrete; tunnelling allows escape across an energy barrier without classical activation over it. A macroscopic circuit can have quantum behaviour without implying a cricket ball tunnels through the ground in everyday conditions.
- Foundations support superconducting qubits, precision measurement and quantum sensors. Cryogenic operation, materials loss, noise and decoherence limit control; basic research can yield technologies decades later.
