# Melioidosis and environmental exposure

## Organism and public health
- Caused by **Burkholderia pseudomallei**, a bacterium in soil and water. Entry can occur through skin inoculation, inhalation or ingestion; it is not a mosquito-borne disease.
- Manifestations range from skin infection to pneumonia and severe sepsis. Diagnosis and prolonged treatment require clinical expertise; confusion with other bacteria can delay care.
- An Odisha study of 144 cases during 2015–23 linked case patterns with environmental/weather conditions. Association does not establish that every weather variable independently causes infection.
- Modelled global burden is distinct from laboratory-confirmed reported cases. The source's inconsistent India-versus-South-Asia percentage should not be memorised as a verified national share.
- Improve laboratory identification, exposure awareness and surveillance, particularly around monsoon conditions and occupational contact with soil/water.
