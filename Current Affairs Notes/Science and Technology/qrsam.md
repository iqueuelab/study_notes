# Quick Reaction Surface-to-Air Missile

## Design and role
- **DRDO QRSAM**, mobile Army air defence for mechanised formations; surveillance/tracking while moving and engagement after a brief halt, rather than an unqualified claim it fires in every movement state.
- Canisterised surface-to-air missiles, phased-array radars, inertial/mid-course data-link guidance and active-radar terminal seeker; high-explosive fragmentation warhead.
- Source **25–30 km range**, **6 km altitude**, **Mach1.8–2**, **30-second response** are reported/variant figures, not a universally guaranteed envelope. Target geometry, radar tracking and electronic conditions matter.
- Complements layered air defence against aircraft, helicopters and suitable unmanned/cruise-missile threats; not identical to Akash, naval Barak or S-400.
## Trials and procurement
- Multiple Chandipur trials since 2017; **six Army evaluation flight tests completed September 2022**, correcting the source's assertion all final trials ended December 2021. [Official tests](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=1857724&lang=2&reg=48).
- February forecast of contract in **four–five months /production 18 months after signing** is a conditional timetable, not delivered/operational status.
- Reliability across terrain/weather, multi-target engagement, interoperability, maintenance, crew training and secure indigenous supply determine practical capability.
