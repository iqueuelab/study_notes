# Akash Prime and high-altitude air defence

## Ladakh trial: 16 July 2025
- Upgraded **Akash Prime** destroyed two high-speed unmanned aerial targets in Ladakh during First of Production Model firing trials. Customised for altitudes above **4,500 m**, with an **indigenous radio-frequency seeker**; Army Air Defence/DRDO, BDL, BEL and industry participated. Trial success enables induction; it is not proof all planned regiments were already inducted. [Defence release](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2145517&lang=2&reg=48).
- Akash is an indigenous mobile surface-to-air weapon family; ground radar/command guidance of original Akash must not obscure Prime's seeker upgrade. Rajendra multifunction radar, electronic counter-countermeasures, mobility and integration support air defence.
- Source original-system figures about **25-km range, 18-km altitude, Mach 2.5–3.5 and 60-kg warhead** are configuration-dependent. Do not treat advertised hit percentages as battlefield guarantees or endorse the PDF's unsupported nuclear-warhead claim.
- Separate source news: **Prithvi-II and Agni-I** ballistic-missile user trials under Strategic Forces Command at Chandipur; these are not Akash interceptors.

- **Cold-weather adaptation** improves high-altitude operation; July 20 source range **about 25–30 km** is an indicative configuration figure, not a universal range for every Akash-family variant.
