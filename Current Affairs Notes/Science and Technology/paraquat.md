# Paraquat: herbicide toxicity and regulation

## Toxicity
- **Paraquat**, methyl viologen, commonly paraquat dichloride; herbicide for weeds/grass, also agricultural desiccation in permitted uses. Not a fertiliser.
- **WHO hazard Class II**, source “moderately hazardous”; classification does **not** make swallowing safe. Regulatory restriction differs from a toxicological class.
- Ingestion can cause severe mouth/GI injury and lung, kidney, liver/heart damage, seizures and respiratory failure. Longer/stronger skin exposure, especially broken skin, can poison; inhalation/eye exposure also harms. [CDC](https://www.cdc.gov/chemical-emergencies/chemical-fact-sheets/paraquat.html).
- **No proven antidote/cure**; urgent specialist treatment and supportive care. Source mentions charcoal haemoperfusion/immunosuppression as clinical approaches, not guaranteed effective cures or self-treatment instructions.
## Regulation and January context
- January 2025 Kerala poisoning-case sentencing brought attention to access and handling; trial judgment ≠ proof appeal outcomes are final.
- Source says banned in **70+ countries**; country counts/date/meaning of ban vary. EU/China restrictions and **US restricted-use licensing** illustrate different regimes; source's “banned in India” practice distractor is false in its January account.
- Safe storage, access control, training, labelling and poisoning surveillance matter; occupational and intentional exposures require distinct prevention measures.
