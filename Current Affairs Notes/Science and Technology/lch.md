# LCH Prachand: high-altitude attack helicopter

## Platform and procurement
- HAL's **Light Combat Helicopter, Prachand**: armed high-altitude operations; Kargil experience highlighted the capability need. First flight **2010**; IAF induction **3 October 2022**.
- Twin **Shakti** engines, developed through HAL–Safran cooperation; relates to indigenous rotary-wing experience but differs from Dhruv utility transport and heavy-lift helicopters.
- High-altitude performance depends on load/weather; manufacturer altitude claims do not guarantee identical capability in every configuration.
- **28 March 2025 contracts: 156 helicopters—66 IAF, 90 Army—₹62,700 crore excluding taxes**, including training/associated equipment. The source's older proposed number is outdated. [MoD](https://www.pib.gov.in/Pressreleaseshare.aspx?PRID=2116411&lang=2&reg=48).
- Order, phased production, delivery, training and operational induction are distinct; local supply chains and maintenance matter alongside indigenous design.
