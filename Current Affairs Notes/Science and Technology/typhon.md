# Typhon missile system and regional deterrence

## Ground-launched system

- **US Army Mid-Range Capability (MRC), Typhon**, **Lockheed Martin system integration**; mobile ground launchers, operations/command centre and reload/support vehicles; reported battery **four launchers**.
- Fires **SM-6 and Tomahawk**; missiles are associated with **Raytheon**, which differs from identifying the Typhon system integrator. [Manufacturer account](https://www.lockheedmartin.com/en-us/news/features/2023/keeping-the-us-army-ahead-of-ready-with-next-generation-launch-systems.html).
- **SM-6** has naval air/surface roles; **Tomahawk** cruise missile has land-attack and maritime variants. A missile's broader family roles **do not prove this deployed Army configuration provides every naval air-defence function**.
- Source **500 km SM-6 / 2,500 km Tomahawk** are reported variant-dependent estimates, not universal official ranges. **Patriot is a different system**, not a Typhon round; ground launcher is not submarine-launch infrastructure.

## January 2025 context

- Reported relocation **within Philippines** to improve mobility/survivability; Chinese/Russian objections and regional deterrence debate. Distinguish relocation from first deployment or a transfer of US missiles into Philippine ownership.
- Regional security balance versus escalation/host-country concerns; deployable strike capability differs from a standing military alliance's political commitments.
