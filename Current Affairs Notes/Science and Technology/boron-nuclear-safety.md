# Boron and nuclear criticality safety

## Boron: established science and May 2025 rumours
- **Boron-10** strongly absorbs neutrons; boron-bearing materials/solutions help control reactor reactivity and prevent inadvertent criticality in appropriate fuel-handling systems. Effectiveness depends on concentration, geometry and validated engineering, not merely delivery of raw boron. [IAEA criticality safety](https://nucleus.iaea.org/sites/nss-oui/Published%20Chunks/m_50d5ac40-0b83-46c9-9192-223620d551fe/c_bee262ad-39f1-4966-ba74-b09dd92b8558__10_0.Html).
- The May 13 PDF links a purported **11 May Egyptian aircraft visit (EGY1916)** to possible boron delivery after India–Pakistan exchanges. **No confirmed cargo, nuclear accident or remediation operation is established**. Flight-tracker interpretation and reports of Nile Delta soil boron do not demonstrate shipment or make Egypt a proven strategic supplier.
- Historical use of neutron absorbers during emergencies, including Chernobyl, does not corroborate this particular rumour. Alleged Kirana Hills tunnel area, stored weapons and radiation damage remain unverified in this material; do not memorise the supplied 68 sq km as an established nuclear inventory.
