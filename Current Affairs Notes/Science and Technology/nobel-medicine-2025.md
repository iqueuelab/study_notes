# Nobel Medicine 2025: regulatory T cells and immune tolerance

## October 2025: peripheral immune tolerance
- Mary E. Brunkow, Fred Ramsdell and Shimon Sakaguchi received the 2025 Medicine Nobel for discoveries concerning peripheral immune tolerance. Central tolerance removes many self-reactive developing T cells in the thymus; regulatory T cells suppress harmful responses outside it.
- Sakaguchi identified regulatory T cells in 1995. Brunkow/Ramsdell identified Foxp3 mutations in scurfy mice in 2001; human FOXP3 mutations cause IPEX. In 2003 Sakaguchi linked Foxp3 to regulatory-T-cell development/function. Thus the source's “around the same time” should not collapse these stages into 1995.
- Tregs balance immune defence with restraint. Expanding/stabilising them may help autoimmunity or transplant tolerance; reducing tumour-associated suppression may support cancer treatment. These are research/clinical-development directions, not universal cures; unwanted suppression or autoimmunity, cost and equitable access remain challenges. [Nobel announcement](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/medicine/2025/press-release/).
