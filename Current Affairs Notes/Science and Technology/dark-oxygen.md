# Dark oxygen and polymetallic nodules

## Deep-sea finding
- **2024 study**, dark abyssal **Clarion–Clipperton Zone**, around **4,000 m** depth: oxygen increase recorded near **polymetallic nodules** without photosynthesis; January 2025 reports follow-up research plans.
- Nodules contain principally **manganese/iron** with valuable **nickel, cobalt and copper**; source also mentions lithium, which is not their principal commercial target.
- Proposed mechanism: electrochemical potentials across nodules may support **water splitting**. Observed oxygen production and the suggested **“geobattery” explanation** are distinct; electrolysis is not demonstrated as the sole settled mechanism.
- Photosynthesis by plants/algae/cyanobacteria is a major oxygen source, but “all oxygen can only be made by photosynthesis” is incorrect.
- Reproducibility, controls and ecological significance need study; deep-sea mining could disturb poorly understood seafloor processes/habitats. [Original research](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41561-024-01480-8).
