# Igla-S portable air defence

## Platform and capabilities
- **Russian MANPADS—man-portable air defence system**, Western designation **SA-24 Grinch**; source dates Russian service to **2004**. Shoulder-fired by an individual or small crew for low-altitude defence against aircraft/helicopters and suitable drone/cruise-missile targets.
- **9M342 missile + 9P522 launch mechanism**; maintenance/test equipment cited: **9V866-2 mobile station / 9F719-2 test set**.
- Source performance envelope: **up to 6 km range / about 3.5 km target altitude**; **2.5-kg high-explosive fragmentation warhead**. Maximum envelope is not guaranteed interception at every distance/angle or against every drone; target signature, countermeasures, weather and operator conditions matter.
- Source describes improved warhead/fuzing relative to earlier variants; do not treat a fuze as a mechanism extending missile propulsion range.

## India procurement: May 2025 source account
- Issue repeats a Russian supply/licensed-production contract announcement without establishing a fresh May signature/date. **Contract, technology transfer, assembly, delivery and operational deployment are distinct milestones**; licensed assembly is not proof of complete indigenous design or manufacture.
- Short-range point/formation protection complements wider radar-linked air-defence layers; distinguish imported Igla-S from **DRDO's indigenous VSHORADS** programme. Portability improves mobility but does not replace integrated detection, identification and training.
