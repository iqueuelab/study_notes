# EOS-09 and PSLV-C61 mission

## 16 May 2025 : planned launch
- **PSLV-C61/EOS-09** planned for **18 May**, First Launch Pad, Satish Dhawan Space Centre, Sriharikota; source says 5.59 am. Earth-observation satellite with **C-band synthetic-aperture radar**, intended all-weather/day-night surface imaging and surveillance. Launch preparation/mobile-service-tower transfer is not orbit insertion. [ISRO mission plan](https://new1.isro.gov.in/ISRO_HINDI/Mission_PSLV_C61_EOS_09.html?timeline=timeline).
- **Later 18 May outcome:** mission **not accomplished**; keep this separate from the 16 May forecast. [ISRO launch register](https://www.isro.gov.in/ISRO_EN/PSLV_Launchers.html). Radar capability of intended payload does not establish delivered operational service.

## Failure review: August 2025 report
- After **18 May PSLV-C61** failure, issue reports committee analysis completed, submission to PM pending. **Third-stage anomaly**, not demonstrated EOS-09 payload malfunction; no root cause can be inferred from “small issue” or confidential report.
- Practice conflates **geostationary orbit** with **geosynchronous transfer orbit(GTO)**. PSLV can place suitable smaller payloads into GTO; this is not direct insertion into final geostationary orbit. Its explanation cites nonexistent statement 3. Failed orbit insertion delivered no operational radar service.
