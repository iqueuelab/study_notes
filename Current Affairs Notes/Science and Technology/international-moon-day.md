# International Moon Day and peaceful lunar exploration

## Observance and meaning
- **20 July** marks the **Apollo 11 lunar landing in 1969**; UN recognised International Moon Day in 2021 following COPUOS recommendation. Focus: international lunar exploration, peaceful cooperation, sustainability and responsible use of resources.
- Landing date is 20 July; Armstrong's first step was **21 July 1969,02:56 UTC**. These refer to different events/time references, not competing dates for one identical event.
- Outreach/sky-watching/education can encourage participation. The source's criticism about privileging an American achievement is a viewpoint; modern lunar activity is multinational.
- [UN observance](https://www.un.org/en/observances/moon-day); [NASA event times](https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/static/history/ap11ann/ap11events.html).
