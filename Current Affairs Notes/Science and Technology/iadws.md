# Integrated Air Defence Weapon System (IADWS)

## Maiden integrated test: 23 August 2025
- DRDO tested **IADWS off Odisha**: indigenous **QRSAM + advanced VSHORADS + high-power laser directed-energy weapon**, engaging three targets at different ranges/altitudes simultaneously: two high-speed fixed-wing UAVs and a multicopter drone. [Defence Ministry](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2160257&lang=2&reg=48).
- Radar/detection → communications → central command/control → appropriate interceptor. **DRDL** is nodal laboratory and developed command/control; **RCI** developed VSHORADS, **CHESS** developed the laser weapon.
- Layered missile/laser interception protects important facilities and supports indigenous area defence. Demonstrated target interceptions are not proof of universal interception of every aircraft/cruise missile or an operational nationwide shield.
- The source calls QRSAM medium-range; retain its name/role as **quick-reaction surface-to-air defence** rather than infer an unsupported range category. IADWS is indigenous, not a joint Israeli development; integrating drones as targets/detection threats does not mean each drone is a weapon-system component.
