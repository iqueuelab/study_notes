# Bhargavastra and counter-drone systems

## January 2025 test account

- **Economic Explosives Limited, Solar Industries**: **Bhargavastra** counter-drone system using guided micro-missiles; source detection **about 6 km**, engagement **over 2.5 km**, salvo capability **about 64**. These are reported configuration/test figures, not guaranteed in all terrain/conditions.
- Mobile deployment, radar/sensors and command/fire-control integration; protect bases/assets from small drones and coordinated attacks.

| Approach | Mechanism / limitation |
| --- | --- |
| **Soft kill** | Jam/spoof communications or navigation; resistant/autonomous drones may bypass it |
| **Hard kill** | Physical interception, guns/missiles/energy systems; debris and cost matter |

## Swarms

- Multiple drones coordinate tasks through centralised/distributed or pre-programmed control. **SWARM is not a universal expansion of “Smart War-Fighting Array…”**, and there is no universal rule that a swarm must contain at least three vehicles.
- Reconnaissance/strike, search/rescue, firefighting and mapping potential; coordination, spectrum, autonomy and identification of friendly drones matter.
- Keep January micro-missile reporting separate from later variants/test announcements.
