# Subsea cables: digital connectivity and resilience

## Network and Indian geography
- Optical fibres carry encoded **light signals**; repeaters/support conductors maintain long-distance transmission. Nearshore armour/burial can reduce anchor/fishing damage; deep-sea design differs. Satellites complement rather than replace the bulk international backbone.
- Correct source's ambiguous **90% global data**: ITU describes **over 99% international data traffic** through submarine cables; this is not all domestic data. Source **$10tn financial flows /80% world trade** needs period/definition. [ITU February 2025](https://www.itu.int/en/mediacentre/Pages/PR-2025-02-27-submarine-cables-summit-nigeria.aspx).
- Mumbai/Chennai are major Indian gateways; **CANI Chennai–Andaman/Nicobar** and **Kochi–Lakshadweep** improve island connectivity. **17 international systems** is a legacy snapshot; systems, landing points, stations and traffic/capacity are different counts. Source **Mumbai 95% /1% stations /3% systems /6-km concentration** should not be treated as equivalent universal shares.
- **2Africa Pearls/SEA-ME-WE 6 landings** support new capacity, including planned **over 100 Tbps** for Pearls; landing does not mean all end-to-end links commercially active. [Airtel contemporaneous results](https://assets.airtel.in/static-assets/cms/investor/docs/quarterly_results/2024_25/Q4/Quarterly-IR-Pack-Bharti-Airtel-Consolidated.pdf).
## Resilience and strategic agenda
- Anchors, fishing, dredging, seismic events, route concentration and security threats can cause breaks. Source's attribution of **2024 Red Sea cable cuts specifically to Houthi sabotage is unproven here**; disrupted shipping/anchors and deliberate attack require separate evidence.
- Permitting, foreign repair-vessel dependence and storage/spares can delay restoration; **51+ approvals /3–5 months** are source case claims, not a fixed statutory count/service time.
- Proposed single-window clearance, diversified gateways **Visakhapatnam/Kochi/Thiruvananthapuram/Andamans**, domestic flagged repair vessels/depots, secure monitoring and PPP investment increase redundancy. These are recommendations, not all completed capacity.
- India–US/Quad partnerships can support trusted networks, cloud/data economy and Indo-Pacific links to Africa/Europe/Southeast Asia; assess data jurisdiction, provider diversity and emergency restoration. Ownership alone does not guarantee digital sovereignty.
- **38% demand growth in 2021–28** lacks CAGR-versus-total clarification; **$5 tn digital economy** is an ambition/source framing, not a verified realised sector size.
