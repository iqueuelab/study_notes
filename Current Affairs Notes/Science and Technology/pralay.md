# Pralay missile and DRDO Republic Day display

## Tactical strike
- **DRDO Pralay**, mobile-launch **solid-propellant surface-to-surface tactical ballistic missile**, **150–500 km**; advanced navigation/guidance and manoeuvrability for battlefield targets such as command centres, radar and airbases.
- **Conventional strike role**. Source **350–700 kg** is a reported payload range; its claim of nuclear warheads/strengthening nuclear second-strike capability is unsupported. **Tactical strike ≠ nuclear second strike**.
- Maiden test **22 December 2021**, **A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Island, Odisha**. Embedded “two-stage Mach 1.6” schematic is not an authoritative specification; do not memorise it as verified missile performance. [Official test](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=1784136&lang=2&reg=48&s=09).
## Republic Day 2025 planned display
- DRDO theme **Raksha Kavach: multilayer protection against multi-domain threats**; **laser-based directed energy weapon**, Pralay and integrated protection.
- Tableau systems: **QRSAM; Arudhra medium-power radar; drone detection/deterrence/destruction; ATAGS 155 mm/52 calibre; indigenous unmanned aerial system**.
- **2024 milestones**: long-range hypersonic anti-ship missile, **Zorawar light tank, ABHED lightweight bulletproof jacket**. Other contingents: **Nag, Pinaka, BrahMos, Akash**.
- Display/test achievement differs from universal operational deployment. [DRDO announcement](https://drdo.gov.in/drdo/en/documents/press-release/theme-raksha-kavach-multilayer-protection-against-multi-domain-threats-drdo).

## User evaluation trials: 28–29 July 2025
- DRDO conducted consecutive successful tests from **Dr APJ Abdul Kalam Island, Odisha**, validating **maximum/minimum range**; Army/Air Force representatives attended. Tracking sensors and a ship near impact assessed trajectory, accuracy and subsystems. Trials do not by themselves establish universal operational induction. [Defence Ministry](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2149610&lang=1&reg=3).
- **Research Centre Imarat** led development with DRDO laboratories, **BDL/BEL**, industry and MSMEs. Quasi-ballistic guidance/manoeuvring, road mobility, solid propellant and varied conventional warheads support precision battlefield strikes.
- Practice MCQ's answer text **1 and 3 only** corresponds to **option C**, despite the printed answer letter A; it is a practice question, not a UPSC PYQ.

## 31 December 2025: two-missile salvo
- **DRDO launched two Pralay missiles in quick succession from the same launcher**, off Odisha at about **10:30**, during **user evaluation trials**. ITR Chandipur tracked both trajectories; shipborne telemetry confirmed terminal events. This validates the tested salvo, without establishing universal deployment or a guarantee of defeating every missile defence. [Defence Ministry](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2210128&lang=2&reg=48).
- Existing notes cover solid propulsion, quasi-ballistic manoeuvring, mobile launch, conventional battlefield role, **150–500 km** reported range and RCI/BDL/BEL participation. Quasi-ballistic manoeuvring can complicate interception; ballistic missiles are not all perfectly fixed parabolas.
- Do not memorise the PDF's **Mach 6.1, CEP <10 m or 500–1,000 kg payload** as verified specifications: this official salvo release does not establish them, and the embedded “two-stage/Mach 1–1.6” diagram is inconsistent. Accuracy alone cannot guarantee minimal collateral harm; conventional strikes can still escalate conflict.
