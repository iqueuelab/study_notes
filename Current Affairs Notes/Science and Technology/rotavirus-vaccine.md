# Rotavirus and Rotavac: real-world vaccine effectiveness

## October 2025: evidence from routine immunisation
- Rotavirus spreads mainly by the **faecal–oral route**, causing gastroenteritis with diarrhoea, vomiting, fever and potentially dangerous dehydration, especially in infants/young children. Vaccination prevents severe disease; it does not eliminate every cause of diarrhoea. Rehydration and appropriate supportive care remain important.
- **Rotavac**, Bharat Biotech's indigenous oral live vaccine based on **G9P[11]**, entered India's **Universal Immunization Programme in 2016**; scheduled doses are **6, 10 and 14 weeks**. Development involved Indian public research/DBT, Bharat Biotech and international research/funding partners. Distinguish development/licensing from public-programme introduction.
- The Nayana P. Nair/Samarasimha N. Reddy collaborative study was published in **Nature Medicine on 7 October 2025**, not 2024 as the PDF repeatedly claims. It studied **2016–2020** surveillance across **31 hospitals in nine states**, using a **test-negative case–control design**. Three-dose adjusted protection against severe rotavirus gastroenteritis was **54% (95% CI 45–62%)**, sustained in the first two years and comparable to earlier trial efficacy. [Primary study](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41591-025-03998-9).
- Rotavirus positivity among children hospitalised with gastroenteritis fell from roughly **40% to 20%** at impact-surveillance sites. This is a hospital-surveillance finding, not a measured 50% fall in every childhood diarrhoeal illness or national mortality. Effectiveness in routine practice differs conceptually from controlled-trial efficacy; observational estimates remain subject to selection/confounding limitations.
- Policy lesson: indigenous vaccine R&D, affordable public delivery, surveillance and high coverage reinforce child survival/SDG 3, reduced hospital burden and vaccine capability. The source's old annual-death estimates need their original year/scope; do not present them as current counts.
