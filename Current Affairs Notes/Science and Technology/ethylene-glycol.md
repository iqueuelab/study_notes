# Ethylene glycol and medicine quality control

## January 2026 medicine alert
- **Telangana DCA, 10 January 2026**, issued a stop-use notice for **Almont-Kid syrup, batch AL-24002**, after a CDSCO East Zone, Kolkata laboratory alert identified **ethylene glycol adulteration**. Manufacturer: **Tridus Remedies, Hajipur, Bihar**; manufacture **January 2025**, expiry **December 2026**. This is a specified-batch alert, not evidence that every product of this name is contaminated. [Official notice](https://dca.telangana.gov.in/openfile.php?f=407).
## Chemical and industrial recall
- **Ethylene glycol (EG), C₂H₆O₂**, HO–CH₂–CH₂–OH: a **diol** with two hydroxyl groups; colourless, nearly odourless, sweet-tasting and water-soluble. Industrially produced by hydration of **ethylene oxide**.
- Uses: antifreeze/engine coolant, polyester fibres and PET manufacture, resins, hydraulic fluids, inks and solvents. Concentrations vary across antifreeze products; the source's **95%** is not a universal composition or India-wide poisoning statistic.
- Ingestion can cause nervous-system depression, **metabolic acidosis and acute kidney injury**, potentially death, as toxic metabolites accumulate. Industrial EG is not interchangeable with pharmaceutical-grade solvents. [ATSDR toxicology](https://www.atsdr.cdc.gov/toxprofiles/tp96.pdf).
- Governance: test raw materials and finished liquid formulations, ensure supplier traceability, enforce batch recalls and coordinate Centre/State drug regulators. The image-only antifreeze question is from **Rajasthan CET**, so is excluded from UPSC CSE PYQs.
