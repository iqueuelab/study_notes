# Red ivy and an experimental wound-healing pad

## Plant knowledge and biomedical materials
- Kerala's **JNTBGRI** researchers report a wound dressing using **red ivy (Strobilanthes alternata; Acanthaceae)**, locally **Murikooti Pacha**, traditionally applied to cuts.
- Source reports isolation of **acteoside**, a plant compound with tissue-repair potential, and incorporation with **neomycin sulphate** into a multilayer dressing with electrospun nanofibres. Porosity permits gas exchange; biodegradable polymers seek to reduce waste.
- The reported **0.2% activity** and first identification in this plant are research claims, not proof of clinical efficacy or a licensed product. Use of FDA-approved constituent polymers does not establish FDA approval of the finished dressing. Standardised extraction, safety, infection control, clinical evaluation and scalable manufacture determine translation.
