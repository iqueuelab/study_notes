# ASC ARJUN: railway robotics and passenger safety

## January 2026 deployment
- **Indian Railways, 23 January**, announced **ASC ARJUN** at **Visakhapatnam station, Andhra Pradesh**, developed locally using indigenous technology to support **Railway Protection Force** personnel. [Railways release](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleaseIframePage.aspx?PRID=2217905&lang=2&reg=48).
- Features: facial-recognition-assisted intrusion detection, AI crowd monitoring, real-time RPF alerts, **English/Hindi/Telugu announcements**, semi-autonomous movement along predefined paths with obstacle avoidance, and fire/smoke detection. Assistance supplements staff rather than replacing legal authority or human judgement.
- Benefits depend on accuracy, maintenance, accessible communication and response procedures. Surveillance needs privacy, access controls, bias/error monitoring and human verification; an automated match is not proof of guilt. Deployment is distinct from independent proof of all claimed safety improvements.
- The accompanying **BPSC 70th re-exam** question about **Manav**, a different 3D-printed humanoid, is excluded from CSE PYQs; “humanoid”, “AI-enabled” and “3D-printed” describe different attributes.
