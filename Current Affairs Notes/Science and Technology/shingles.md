# Shingles: latent virus and prevention

## Mechanism
- **Varicella-zoster virus** causes chickenpox, remains latent in sensory ganglia and can reactivate as herpes zoster/shingles; age and impaired immunity increase risk, but cases are not confined to over 50 s.
- Pain/tingling followed by usually one-sided band-like blisters; **postherpetic neuralgia** can persist after rash resolution.
- VZV exposure from an affected person can cause chickenpox in a susceptible contact, not directly transmit “shingles” as the same clinical condition.
- Eye/neurological complications can be serious; rare deaths occur, so source “never life-threatening” is incorrect. [CDC](https://www.cdc.gov/vaccines/hcp/current-vis/shingles.html).
## Public health
- **Shingrix is recombinant, non-live**; vaccination helps prevent shingles/complications. US recommendations include adults 50+ and specified immunocompromised adults 19+; national programmes/eligibility differ.
- Early clinical assessment and antiviral treatment can reduce disease severity; source “no treatment/cure” must not obscure effective management.
- Awareness, affordability and older-adult access matter; 90%prior-virus exposure/56.6%unawareness are source survey claims, not inevitable disease prevalence or nationally representative facts without methodology.
- Adding vaccination to India's programme is a proposal in this source, not proof universal rollout.
