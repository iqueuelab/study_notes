# HAL–UAC SJ-100: proposed civil-aircraft production

## 27 October 2025: civil-aircraft manufacturing MoU
- **HAL and Russia's PJSC–UAC signed in Moscow**, announced **28 October**, an MoU for **SJ-100 civil commuter aircraft production**. The manufacturer's release describes a twin-engine, narrow-body regional aircraft and a plan for Indian manufacturing rights for domestic customers. **MoU and prospective production are not delivered Indian aircraft or completed certification.** [HAL release](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/corporate/HAL_28102025111802_Mediarelease_28102025.pdf).
- Roughly **100 seats**, short-haul/regional service; UDAN connectivity is a potential market, not an automatic procurement order. HAL cites demand possibilities **200 domestic + 350 Indian Ocean-region aircraft over ten years**; these are market estimates, not contracted orders.
- Source's **200+ aircraft/16 operators** concerns the wider existing Superjet lineage; do not assume the new import-substituted SJ-100 version had all those operational deliveries. Aircraft variant, engine configuration, certification and range need configuration-specific comparison.
- HAL's earlier **Avro HS-748** production provides historical context. Claiming India made no civilian aircraft since 1988 erases **Dornier-228** and other activity; the quoted revival refers to this passenger-jet-scale proposal.
- Potential: aerospace skills, suppliers, MRO, local value addition and India–Russia civil cooperation. Constraints: Indian type validation, safety, financing, component/engine supply, localisation/IP and competition. EASA/FAA approval is not automatically necessary for every domestic route; relevant regulator/market certification determines requirements.
