# Leprosy: transmission, treatment and stigma

## Disease and diagnosis
- **Hansen disease**, mainly **Mycobacterium leprae**, bacterial; affects skin, **peripheral nerves**, eyes and upper-respiratory mucosa. Pale/discoloured patches with reduced sensation, nerve weakness, ulcers and advanced deformity/eye damage.
- Spread usually prolonged close contact with untreated disease, respiratory droplets; not highly contagious, not ordinary casual contact. Incubation averages about **5 years**, may extend **20+ years**; source “5–20” is not a strict minimum/maximum.
- Clinical cardinal signs and nerve assessment guide diagnosis; slit-skin smear/biopsy where needed. **Lepromin test does not diagnose active infection**.
| Treatment category | Recall | MDT duration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Paucibacillary (PB) | Usually 1–5 lesions, no demonstrated bacilli | **6 months** |
| Multibacillary (MB) | >5 lesions, nerve involvement or smear bacilli as per criteria | **12 months** |
- **Rifampicin + clofazimine + dapsone**, curable with MDT; early treatment prevents disability. Contact screening and eligible **single-dose rifampicin post-exposure prophylaxis** add prevention. [WHO](https://www.who.int/en/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/leprosy).
## India: elimination is not eradication
- National public-health elimination **2005**, prevalence **<1/10,000**; new cases/local transmission persist. **NLEP 1983**, **SPARSH** awareness, **Nikusth 2.0** surveillance and **NSP/Roadmap 2023–27**, source ambition **zero transmission by 2027**—target, not achievement. [2023 announcement](https://www.pib.gov.in/pressreleasepage.aspx?lang=2&prid=1909081&reg=48).
- PB three-drug revision issued **17 January 2024**, Indian implementation from **1 April 2025**; source's January wording should not imply already implemented everywhere. Nikusth 2.0 launched **30 January 2023**. [Programme chronology](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2174943&lang=2&reg=3).
- January source focus **124 districts**, Bihar/Chhattisgarh/Jharkhand/Maharashtra/Odisha; broader high-burden list includes UP/West Bengal/MP. **53.6% global cases, 2021–22**, **~60%, 2023**, **~9% children** refer to different periods/denominators, not one current statistic.
- Stigma, hidden cases, delayed care, disability and remote/tribal access need trained primary care, rehabilitation, anti-discrimination and community participation.
- WHO **2021–2030** global strategy, ILEP partners; BCG offers some protection but routine BCG programme is not a dedicated guaranteed leprosy vaccine. Free MDT is distinct from general health-insurance coverage.

## July 2025 legal-discrimination development
- Supreme Court urged States to amend/remove derogatory, discriminatory legal provisions against leprosy-affected people, including through special Assembly sessions/ordinances; clipping referred to **more than 145 State laws with objectionable provisions** and a compliance report due **7 October**. This is a direction toward reform, not evidence every State had already repealed them.
- Existing clinical/MDT/NLEP notes cover the repeated disease background. Public-health elimination in 2005 did not mean eradication or absence of stigma; legal equality, rehabilitation and early detection remain priorities.
