# Osteoporosis and bone health

## Mechanism and signs
- Bone resorption exceeds formation → reduced bone strength and microstructure → **fragility fracture risk**.
- Often silent until fracture; common sites **hip, wrist and vertebrae**. Vertebral fractures may cause back pain, loss of height and stooped posture.
- Age and hormonal changes matter: lower **estrogen**, particularly after menopause, or lower **testosterone**; other risks and medicines also contribute. [NIH NIAMS](https://www.niams.nih.gov/health-topics/osteoporosis?navPos=0).
## Prevention and care: exam level
- Appropriate nutrition, calcium/vitamin D adequacy, suitable weight-bearing/resistance activity and fall prevention.
- Clinical assessment may include bone-density measurement and prescribed medicines; supplements alone are not a universal cure.
- Link to ageing, primary-care prevention and affordable disability reduction; screening should be risk-appropriate.
