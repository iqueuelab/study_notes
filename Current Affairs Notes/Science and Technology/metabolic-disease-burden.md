# Metabolic disease burden: GBD estimates and prevention

## GBD-based Asia-Pacific analysis
- The report reviews **1990–2023** estimates and projects to **2030** for **type 2 diabetes, high systolic BP, high BMI, high LDL cholesterol and MASLD**. This is a mixed set of diseases and risk factors, not five mutually exclusive patient groups.
- **DALY = years of life lost from premature death + years lived with disability**. Death counts, mortality rates, absolute DALYs and age-standardised rates answer different questions; a large population can lead absolute burden while smaller Pacific islands lead relative burden.
- Source India **2023** estimates: type 2 diabetes **~21m DALYs/0.58m deaths**; high systolic BP **~38m DALYs/1.57m deaths**. These are modelled burdens, not March 2026 newly diagnosed patients. Do not add overlapping risk-attributable deaths into a unique total.
- India overtaking China in a selected absolute-DALY comparison does not establish first rank for every metabolic condition: source keeps China ahead in high BMI/LDL/MASLD. **2.7–2.9% annual rise** requires its particular indicator/timeframe; it is not every disease's current prevalence.
- Primary paper identifies hypertension as the dominant contributor and most burdens continuing to rise; **2030 figures are forecasts**. Link integrated primary-care screening/referral with food environments, activity-friendly cities and evidence-based nutrition labelling. Existing diabetes/MASLD notes retain diagnosis and care details. [Original study](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.metabol.2026.156575).
