Nimesulide: formulation-specific restrictions and drug safety

Editable notes

December 2025: immediate-release oral restriction

  • Source reports prohibition of manufacture, sale and distribution of oral nimesulide formulations above 100 mg in immediate-release dosage form. S.O. 6091(E), 29 December 2025, invokes Section 26A, Drugs and Cosmetics Act 1940, following DTAB consultation. The Telangana Drugs Control Administration identifies the notification. Official notice.
  • Nimesulide is an NSAID, used for pain/inflammation; the article highlights potential liver toxicity and safer alternatives. This notification's scope is dose/formulation-specific; absence from this particular prohibition does not certify every lower-dose, sustained-release or topical product safe or approved, nor supersede other restrictions.
  • The vulture graphic concerns a separate issue: all nimesulide formulations for animal use were prohibited by S.O. 5633(E), 30 December 2024, reiterated by CDSCO 20 February 2025, because of animal/vulture risk. Do not merge veterinary prohibition with this human oral-dose measure. CDSCO veterinary direction.
  • Regulatory takeaway: pharmacovigilance, risk–benefit assessment, enforceable product definitions and monitoring of substitutes; this is a revision issue, not dosing advice.
