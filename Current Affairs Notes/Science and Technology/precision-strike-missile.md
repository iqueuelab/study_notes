# Precision Strike Missile: range, launchers and capability

## PrSM and long-range fires
- **Precision Strike Missile (PrSM)**: US Army surface-to-surface precision ballistic missile, developed by **Lockheed Martin**, intended to succeed ATACMS. The issue reports CENTCOM's announcement of first combat use during the Iran operation; operational claims remain attributed to the force making them.
- Compatible with **M142 HIMARS** and the **M270 MLRS family**; **two missiles per launch pod**. Open-system architecture and modular design support upgrades. The manufacturer's published specification is **60–499+ km**, more precise than treating “at least 500 km” as a fixed minimum engagement range. [Manufacturer](https://www.lockheedmartin.com/en-us/products/precision-strike-missile.html).
- Source describes solid propellant and GPS/inertial navigation. An **Insensitive Munitions energetic payload** reduces vulnerability to accidental initiation; it does not make ammunition incapable of exploding accidentally. Do not equate a high-speed ballistic trajectory with a dedicated hypersonic glide vehicle or assume every future increment's seeker/network capability is present in the baseline missile.
