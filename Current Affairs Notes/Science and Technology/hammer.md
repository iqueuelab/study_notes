# AASM HAMMER precision-guided air-to-ground weapons

## Manufacturer and architecture

- **Safran AASM HAMMER** = Highly Agile Modular Munition Extended Range; **not an MBDA product** and not simply a cruise missile.
- Guidance and propulsion kit fitted to bomb bodies; advertised configurations **125 /250 /500 /1,000 kg**. Combines inertial/GPS guidance with infrared or laser terminal options depending on version. [Safran product](https://www.safran-group.com/products-services/aasm-hammer-tm-highly-agile-modular-munition-extended-range).
- Booster permits stand-off attacks; commonly reported **~70 km** depends on release conditions/version. “Mach 1.5” is not a universal speed for every configuration.
- Precision/all-weather claims depend on guidance type, target data and countermeasures; laser designation conditions differ from GPS/INS or imaging guidance.

## Indian relevance

- Rafale-associated Indian procurement/deployment interest; the PDF's MBDA manufacturer/contract attribution is erroneous.
- Potential: accuracy, flexible bomb sizes, reduced exposure and aircraft integration. Assess integration/certification, cost, electronic warfare resilience, supply assurance and genuine domestic technology access.
- A precision weapon can reduce some collateral risks but does not eliminate targeting obligations or guarantee zero civilian harm.
