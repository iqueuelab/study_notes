# Marine and space biotechnology: biomanufacturing frontiers

## Marine resources and biology beyond Earth
| Frontier | Inputs /applications |
|---|---|
| **Marine biotechnology** | Microbes, algae, seaweeds and other marine organisms → bioactive compounds, enzymes, biostimulants, biopolymers and food ingredients |
| **Space biotechnology** | Microbes, algae, plants and human cells under **microgravity, radiation and closed environments** → food, regenerative life support, health and pharmaceutical research |
- Marine habitats can select unusual pressure/salinity/light/temperature adaptations useful for research; **not every marine organism experiences every extreme or makes a molecule absent from all land organisms**. Bioprospecting potential is not demonstrated drug efficacy or permission to exploit protected biodiversity.
- **Agar/carrageenan** are associated with relevant **red algae**, **alginates** with **brown algae**. Source estimates cultivated marine biomass around **70,000 tonnes/year** without a clear year or common product basis; India's imports/low production are an industrial opportunity, not a complete current marine-output census. Scale cultivation, extraction, purification and product testing together rather than treating raw seaweed tonnage as high-value output.
- **BioE3 (Biotechnology for Economy, Environment and Employment)**, approved **24 August 2024**, includes marine/space research among priority sectors. **DBT–ISRO MoU, 25 October 2024**, provides cooperation in space biotechnology/biomanufacturing; a policy/MoU does not prove mature commercial production. [Cabinet policy](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2048569&lang=2&reg=3), [ISRO agreement](https://www.isro.gov.in/DBT_ISRO_signs_Framework_Memorandum_of_Understanding_on_Cooperation_in_Space_Biotechnology.html).
- **ICAR–Central Marine Fisheries Research Institute**, State initiatives and firms such as **Sea6 Energy/ClimaCrew** illustrate the ecosystem. EU shared research infrastructure, US/ISS biological research and Chinese seaweed scale provide comparisons; long R&D cycles, capital needs, biological variability and fragmented coordination limit scale. Align science, industry and policy through realistic roadmaps, shared facilities and responsible value chains.
- **Coastline correction:** supplementary prose's **~7,500 km** is outdated; original clipping uses over 11,000 km. Official 2025 reassessment gives **11,098.81 km**, reflecting measurement resolution/method, not sudden territorial expansion. **EEZ over 2 million km²** is a separate area measure. [Earth Sciences Ministry, December 2025](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2198800&lang=1&reg=6).
- Marine processes may reduce pressure on land/freshwater in suitable systems, but energy, nutrients, waste, ecological effects and supply chains need assessment. Space-biological findings must be validated for terrestrial applications; experimental protein crystals or cell studies are not automatically market-ready medicines.
