# Eurodrone and India’s observer status

## Programme identity

- **Medium Altitude Long Endurance Remotely Piloted Aircraft System (MALE RPAS)**; **Germany, France, Italy, Spain**, managed by **OCCAR—Organisation for Joint Armament Cooperation**, Germany lead nation.
- Industrial prime **Airbus Defence and Space, Germany**; partners **Dassault Aviation, France; Leonardo, Italy; Airbus Spain**. **UK is not one of the four programme States**.
- **21 January 2025, India observer status**, joining Japan as an observer; **observer ≠ full programme partner, procurement order or operational deployment**. [OCCAR programme announcements](https://occar.int/news?cat=13).

## Planned capability

- **Twin-turboprop**, maximum mission payload **2.3 tonnes excluding fuel**, endurance **up to 40 hours** under relevant configuration/conditions. Maximum payload and maximum endurance need not occur simultaneously.
- **ISTAR = intelligence, surveillance, target acquisition, reconnaissance**, possible maritime missions; source ASW/early-warning roles require appropriate mission sensors/configuration, not automatic capability of any airframe.
- Designed with **civil-airspace integration/certification** in mind; manufacturer's superiority/first claims are promotional comparisons, not universal proof. [Airbus specification](https://www.airbus.com/en/products-services/defence/uas/eurodrone).
- European capability/industrial autonomy and reduced reliance on imported Reaper/Heron platforms; India's observation supports familiarity and cooperation without implying a signed purchase.
