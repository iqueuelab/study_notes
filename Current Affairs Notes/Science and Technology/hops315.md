# HOPS-315 and the earliest planet-forming solids

## July 2025 observation
- **HOPS-315**, a young protostar in **Orion**, roughly **1,300 light-years** away: **JWST infrared spectroscopy** and **ALMA millimetre observations in Chile** identified **silicon monoxide gas and crystalline silicates** in the inner protoplanetary disc.
- Hot dust vaporises; cooling gas condenses into refractory crystalline grains, potential building blocks that can aggregate into planetesimals and planets. Similar mineral records survive in ancient Solar-System meteorites.
- Crucially the signals originate **in the disc atmosphere, within about 2.2 AU**, distinct from the outward jet; ALMA helped locate the source. This is evidence of an early condensation stage, not observation of completed Earth-like planets. [ESO discovery release](https://www.eso.org/public/news/eso2512/).
- Source lists forsterite, enstatite/silica and approximate **1,300 K hot /470 K cooler components**; these should not be read as one universal crystallisation temperature for every mineral. Observations test whether Solar-System formation processes recur elsewhere.
