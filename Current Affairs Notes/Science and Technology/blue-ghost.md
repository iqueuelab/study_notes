# Blue Ghost lunar landing

## Mission
- Firefly Aerospace **Blue Ghost landed 2 March 2025 in Mare Crisium**; launched 15 January on Falcon 9. NASA CLPS delivery carried 10 NASA instruments.
- Payload themes: heat flow/drilling, regolith/plume interactions, lunar navigation signals, magnetic fields, dust mitigation, retroreflection and Earth/space observations.
- Commercial delivery still involves government funding/payloads. Odysseus 2024 preceded it as a commercial lunar landing but tipped; specify the basis of any “first” claim.
## India connections
- India joined **Artemis AccordsJune 2023**, not still awaiting a first invitation inMarch 2025.
- Chandrayaan 3 landed near 69°south, not exactly at the geographic south pole. Chandrayaan 4/LUPEX concern different exploration objectives; Gaganyaan is a human-spaceflight programme, not itself an announced lunar landing.
