# Atomic Energy Commission and DAE

## Institutional timeline
- **AEC first constituted August 1948** under Department of Scientific Research; **DAE established 3 August 1954**, directly under **Prime Minister**.
- Commission reconstituted **1 March 1958** within DAE; **1958 is not its original creation**. DAE Secretary is ex-officio AEC chairman; appointments follow the government resolution. Headquarters **Mumbai**. [DAE institutional history](https://dae.gov.in/atomic-energy-commission/).
## Functions
- Atomic-energy policy and coordination; nuclear-power programmes, fuel-cycle capability and research.
- Uranium/fuel fabrication, heavy water, reactors, reprocessing and radioactive-waste management; resource development and research into fast reactors/fusion.
- Accelerators, lasers, materials and biological research; radioisotopes/radiation for **medicine, agriculture, food preservation and industry**.
- **AEC policy body ≠ AERB safety regulator**; DAE non-power applications should not be confused with only electricity generation.
