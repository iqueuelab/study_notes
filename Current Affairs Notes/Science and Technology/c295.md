# C-295: tactical transport and Indian aircraft production

## January 2026: Indian production milestone planned
- Source reports the first Indian-made **C-295** expected **by/before September 2026** from the **Airbus–Tata Final Assembly Line, Vadodara, Gujarat**. It is a scheduled rollout, not a delivered Indian-made aircraft in January.
- **Twin-engine turboprop tactical transport**, short take-off/landing and rough/unprepared-strip operation; roles include troops/cargo, medical evacuation and humanitarian/disaster response. Source endurance **up to about 11 hours**, payload **up to about 9 tonnes /71 personnel**, mission-dependent; not a supersonic fighter.
- **56-aircraft IAF programme to replace Avro-748**: **16 fly-away from Spain, 40 manufactured/assembled by TASL in India**. First Spanish-built delivery was September 2023; Indian production is a separate milestone. Private-sector assembly, supplier/MSME skills, testing and lifecycle support strengthen industrial capability without implying every component/design is Indian. [Airbus programme](https://www.airbus.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2024-02-india-airbus-c295-programme-receives-approval-from-indian-regulator).
- Revision: **Dornier-228—maritime patrol; IL-76—heavy transport; C-17—military transport**; 2025 PYQ has **two correct pairs (b)** and already exists in the common bank.
