# Common chargers and USB-C

## Core facts
- A common charging interface improves interoperability and reduces charger purchases and e-waste; USB-C identifies the connector, not a guarantee of identical charging speed.
- EU rules apply to specified rechargeable radio equipment from **28 December 2024**; laptops follow from **28 April 2026**. Covered categories include phones, tablets, cameras, headphones, handheld consoles, speakers, e-readers, keyboards, mice, navigation devices and earbuds. [EU Council](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/infographics/2022-06-07-common-charger-directive/).
## India and implementation
- The supplied January 2025 notes describe consultations beginning in 2022 and a proposed June 2025 phone/tablet deadline, followed by laptops in 2026. **Treat these Indian dates as source-reported proposals pending notification verification.**
- Benefits: reusable chargers, lower consumer costs, simpler inventories, less waste and easier international compatibility.
- Challenges: redesign costs, industry resistance, legacy devices, clear power-rating information and consumer awareness. Complement standardisation with repair, refurbishment and formal recycling.
