# R-37M and long-range air-to-air missiles

## System and use

- Russian **R-37M**, long-range beyond-visual-range air-to-air missile; evolution of the R-37 family associated with the earlier R-33 interception role.
- Source specifications: **over 4 m, about 510 kg, 60 kg warhead, Mach 6, 300–400 km**. These are reported variant/envelope figures; maximum range varies with launch altitude, target motion and missile version, not guaranteed combat reach.
- Inertial/mid-course updating followed by radar terminal homing; do not casually substitute semi-active homing for the advertised active-radar seeker.
- Targets can include high-value **AWACS, tankers and surveillance aircraft**, as well as combat aircraft. Disrupting support assets affects an opponent's wider air operations.
- Export **RVV-BD** and domestic R-37M specifications are not interchangeable. Aircraft integration, sensors, targeting data and rules of engagement limit operational effectiveness.
- February source links the missile to a Su-57 offer; this does **not prove an Indian acquisition**, assured integration or unrestricted technology transfer.
