Science and Technology

Battery energy storage and clean-grid integration

Editable notes

BESS functions and units

  • BESS combines rechargeable batteries, power-conversion/inverters, management/control and thermal/safety equipment; it is not simply an inverter. It stores electricity from grid/renewable sources and discharges for balancing, peak supply, backup and ancillary services.
  • MW = power; MWh = stored energy. A 20 MW/40 MWh system has about two hours at rated power before losses/reserves. Source “four hours daily” assumes separate charging/discharging cycles; this is not four uninterrupted hours at 20 MW from one charge.
  • Lithium-ion is common; lead-acid/flow technologies have different cost/life/energy characteristics. Charging losses, degradation/cycle limits, capital cost, critical minerals, fire/thermal controls and recycling constrain deployment. Emissions benefits depend on charging mix; batteries do not create energy or automatically ensure cheap 24/7 electricity.

Delhi project and policy chronology

  • Kilokri, South Delhi, BRPL project: 20 MW/40 MWh, developed with IndiGrid/AmpereHour and Global Energy Alliance support. Source forecast “operational by March 2025” is old: project partner announced commercial operation/launch 8 May 2025. The narrow claim is first utility-scale standalone commercial/distribution project, not India’s first battery installation of any kind. Partner announcement.
  • Source 12,000 low-income consumers is a project-beneficiary estimate, not a guarantee each receives continuous backup from this battery alone. Renewable PPAs can provide charging energy; dispatch/settlement matters.
  • September 2023 VGF: 4,000 MWh by 2030–31, up to 40% capital support; initial scheme outlay ₹9,400 crore includes ₹3,760 crore budget support. Source conflates total outlay with grant support and gives conflicting 2026/2030–31 deadlines. Cabinet decision.
  • ₹18,100 crore advanced-chemistry-cell PLI supports manufacturing, distinct from deployment VGF. Source FY2023 37 MWh and 1.3 million tonnes annual avoided emissions require scope/charging assumptions; neither proves current installed capacity or realised savings.
