National Council of Science Museums

Editable notes

Institution and outreach

  • NCSM, autonomous society under Ministry of Culture, registered 4 April 1978; headquarters Kolkata. Promotes public science awareness and scientific temper through national/regional/district science centres, interactive exhibits, training, demonstrations, publications and workshops. NCSM FAQ.
  • Central Research and Training Laboratory, Kolkata supports exhibit development, research and professional science communication. Mobile Science Exhibitions extend outreach to rural/remote communities.
  • International work cited: Rajiv Gandhi Science Centre, Mauritius, and India's Buddhism gallery at the International Buddhist Museum, Sri Lanka; travelling exhibitions in US/Russia/China/France/Bangladesh/Bhutan/Trinidad and Tobago/Guyana/Bulgaria. Examples do not imply a science centre already exists in every district.

May 2025: Saroj Ghose

  • Embedded obituary: Saroj Ghose died 17 May 2025 in Seattle, aged 89; NCSM announcement 18 May. Architect of India's decentralised, interactive science-centre movement; led NCSM 1979–1997, later museum adviser and former ICOM president. Padma Shri 1989; Padma Bhushan 2007. NCSM/PIB tribute.
  • Science museums link accessible informal education with curiosity, evidence and public understanding; measure reach and engagement rather than buildings alone.
