# Trigeminal neuralgia

## Core facts
- **Tic douloureux:** chronic facial pain involving the trigeminal nerve, often experienced as brief, severe electric-shock-like attacks on one side. Touch, chewing, toothbrushing or smiling can trigger attacks.
- Compression by a blood vessel is a common cause; the disorder is more common in women and older adults. Episodes may become more frequent/prolonged. Treatment can involve medicines or surgery; the colloquial “suicide disease” is not a clinical classification.
- Diagram recall: **ophthalmic V1 → upper face; maxillary V2 → midface; mandibular V3 → lower face/jaw**.
- **ACE2 PYQ 2021:** answer **spread of viral diseases**; ACE2 is a host receptor used by SARS-CoV-2 for cell entry.
- Clinical reference: [NIH/NIDCR](https://www.nidcr.nih.gov/health-info/trigeminal-neuralgia).
