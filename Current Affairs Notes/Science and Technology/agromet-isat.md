# iSAT and AI-based agrometeorological advisories

## Initiative and delivery
- ICRISAT-led **AI-powered Context-Specific Agromet Advisory Services for Climate-Resilient Agriculture at Scale**, inception workshop **29–30 July 2025, Hyderabad**, supported by **Monsoon MissionIII**.
- Builds on **Intelligent Systems Advisory Tool (iSAT)** piloted under Monsoon MissionII; combines weather forecasts, historical climate, soil/crop calendars, crop models and AI/ML to tailor sowing, irrigation and pest-management advice.
- Maharashtra initial implementation through **ICAR Agro-Meteorological Field Units (AMFUs)**; WhatsApp bots/digital channels aim to reach smallholders. Nationwide/Global South expansion is planned, not completed adoption.
- Consortium: **ICRISAT, ICAR-CRIDA, ILRI**; technical support **IITM, IMD, IIT Ropar, CSIR-CSIO, IISc**. Forecast uncertainty, local-language delivery, connectivity, agronomic validation and farmer feedback determine usefulness. [ICRISAT initiative account](https://isscanews.icrisat.org/copy-icrisat-and-partners-launch-ai-powered-climate-advisory-initiative-to-boost-farmer-resilience).
