# HKU5-CoV lineage 2: cell entry and zoonotic uncertainty

## What the study shows

- Researchers reported a bat **merbecovirus HKU5-CoV lineage2**, capable of using **human ACE2** as an entry receptor in laboratory systems. MERS-CoV also a merbecovirus but normally uses **DPP4**, not ACE2; SARS-CoV-2 is a sarbecovirus.
- **Receptor recognition ≠efficient human transmission ≠a human outbreak.** Laboratory cell-entry/organ-model results do not establish person-to-person spread, clinical severity or pandemic inevitability. [Primary Cell study](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0092867425001448).
- **Furin cleavage** concerns viral-protein processing; it is not a receptor that substitutes for ACE2. Source incorrectly compresses these mechanisms into one claim.
- No human infection established by the supplied February account; its fever/cough/diarrhoea list is extrapolated from other diseases, **not observed HKU5-CoV-2 patient symptoms**.

## Surveillance and governance

- Monitor animal–human interfaces and viral evolution, coordinate human/animal/environment data through **One Health**, secure laboratory practice and transparent evidence sharing.
- INSACOG specifically supports SARS-CoV-2 genomic work; emerging-virus preparedness needs broader systems too, not automatic assumption every bat virus falls in its existing mandate.
- Discovery by a Wuhan laboratory does not demonstrate artificial creation, a laboratory leak or COVID-19's origin. Vaccine-company share movements are financial reactions, not clinical-risk evidence.
- Communicate uncertainty accurately; hypothetical mutation possibilities do not justify claims a new pandemic is underway or that an existing vaccine is proven to prevent this virus.
