# Sky Sting: beyond-visual-range air-to-air missile

## Reported Indian interest
- **Sky Sting**, Israeli **Rafael** long-range **beyond-visual-range air-to-air missile (BVRAAM)**, is discussed in the context of the PM's Israel visit. A potential offer/assessment is not a signed Indian purchase, certified Tejas/Su-30 integration or entry into service. [Manufacturer's product page](https://www.rafael.co.il/system/sky-sting-6th-gen/).
- Source/manufacturer descriptions include a **three-pulse solid-propellant motor**, radio-frequency seeker, two-way data link and electronic counter-countermeasures. Separate propulsion pulses help preserve energy for terminal manoeuvres; a data link updates targeting during flight.
- **~250 km, terminal speed above Mach 5, AI discrimination and “sixth generation”** are reported/marketing specifications. Launch/target geometry, sensors, countermeasures and integration affect practical performance; a generation label is not a universal standard or proof of reliable destruction of stealth aircraft.
- The **2008 Barak CSE PYQ** is already banked: purchase country **Israel (a)**. Original Barak point defence differs from later jointly developed India–Israel Barak-8; the source conflates them.
