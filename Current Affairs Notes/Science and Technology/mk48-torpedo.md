# Mk48 torpedo: acoustic homing and underwater blast

## Heavyweight submarine torpedo
- **US Navy Mk 48**: heavyweight **acoustic-homing** weapon against submarines and surface ships, with sonar/digital guidance and control; approximate weight **1,700 kg**, diameter **21 in**, liquid-propellant propulsion.
- Official Navy fact file lists a **650 lb high-explosive warhead**. This differs from the infographic's **500 lb TNT-equivalent** claim; explosive mass and TNT-equivalent energy are different measures. Acoustic homing and wire-guidance/control features should not be reduced to a guaranteed single attack path. [Navy specification](https://www.navy.mil/Resources/Fact-Files/Display-FactFiles/Article/2167907/mk-48-heavyweight-torpedo/).
- An under-keel detonation creates shock and a pulsating gas bubble that can cause catastrophic hull-girder damage; **“metal fatigue”** is an imprecise description of rapid explosive failure. It does not guarantee every ship splits identically.
- Historical torpedo attacks include **INS Khukri (PNS Hangor, 1971)**, **ARA General Belgrano (HMS Conqueror, 1982)** and **ROKS Cheonan (2010)**. The source's 176 Khukri total omits **18 officers: 194 deaths in all**. [Indian Navy tribute](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=1898632&lang=2&reg=48). These examples do not establish the legal circumstances of the 2026 incident.
