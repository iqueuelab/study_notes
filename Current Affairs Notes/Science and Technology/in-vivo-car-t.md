# In vivo CAR T-cell engineering

## Conventional and experimental therapy
- **CAR: chimeric antigen receptor**; engineered T cells recognise target antigen. Conventional autologous workflow: collect T cells → laboratory engineering/expansion → conditioning chemotherapy → reinfusion. Time, facilities, cost and immune toxicity limit access; source's ₹60–70lakh is not a fixed nationwide price.
- **June2025 preclinical study:** antibody-targeted lipid nanoparticles deliver **mRNA to CD8+ T cells in vivo**, inducing temporary CAR expression without external cell manufacturing; differs from permanent viral integration. [Original study](https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.ads8473).
- Humanised mice: tumour control. **Cynomolgus monkeys:** B-cell depletion, up to85% CAR expression in CD8+ T cells under study conditions; returning B cells largely naive. This is not proof of durable human cure or harmlessness of every returning cell.
- Potential cancer/autoimmune applications could reduce complexity/cost, but clinical efficacy, safety, durability and repeat dosing remained research questions. Severe illness after repeated doses in one animal highlights toxicity risk.
- Animal results are not routine IV cancer treatment; avoid universal claims of no chemotherapy/hospitalisation. **2022 B/T-cell PYQ:** protection from pathogens, already captured June11; B cells and T-cell subsets have distinct functions.
