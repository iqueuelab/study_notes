# Selenium: essential nutrient and excess exposure

## Essential versus excessive

- **Se, atomic number 34**; essential trace element. Selenoproteins support antioxidant defence, thyroid-hormone metabolism and reproduction; examples glutathione peroxidases, thioredoxin reductases and selenoproteinP.
- USNIH reference intake: adults 55, pregnancy 60, lactation 70 **micrograms/day**, not milligrams. Food content depends on soil; sources include seafood, meat and Brazil nuts. [NIH](https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/Selenium-HealthProfessional/).
- Excess can cause **selenosis**: hair loss, brittle/lost nails and gastrointestinal symptoms; deficiency and toxicity are different problems.
- Buldhana hair-loss reports linked suspect wheat/selenium exposure: an investigated hypothesis is not proof all ration wheat or every Punjab/Haryana crop is unsafe. Establish exposure through food/soil testing and clinical/epidemiological assessment.
