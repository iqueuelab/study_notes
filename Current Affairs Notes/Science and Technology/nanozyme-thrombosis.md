# Nanozymes and experimental thrombosis prevention

## 3–4 June 2025: IISc research
- **G. Mugesh’s IISc team** investigated **vanadium pentoxide (V₂O₅) nanozymes**, enzyme-like nanomaterials modulating reactive oxygen species. Physiological clotting limits bleeding; excess platelet activation/aggregation can contribute to thrombosis and pulmonary embolism.
- **Oxidative stress → excess ROS → platelet signalling/activation → aggregation**; antioxidant-enzyme mimic reduces triggers. This is **not direct thrombolysis of an existing clot**. Shapes/sizes were compared using isolated human platelets and agonists such as thrombin; spherical particles performed best in the source account.
- The published work also investigated **pulmonary embolism in mice**; human platelets in laboratory experiments are **not a human clinical trial**. [IISc announcement](https://www.iisc.ac.in/events/novel-nanozyme-prevents-excess-clotting/).
- Proposed next work on **ischaemic stroke** remains research. PTE, COVID-related clotting and stroke are potential disease contexts, not evidence of an approved drug curing each condition. Safety, biodistribution/dose and controlled efficacy studies precede clinical adoption; “non-invasive therapy” is not established merely by nanozyme chemistry.
