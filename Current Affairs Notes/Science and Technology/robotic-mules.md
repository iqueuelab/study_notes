# Robotic MULES and military autonomy

## Platform and purpose
- **MULE: Multi-Utility Legged Equipment**, quadrupedal unmanned ground robot associated with **AeroArc/ARCV**, Delhi; displayed at **77th Army Day, Pune, 15 January 2025**. Theme **Samarth Bharat, Saksham Sena**. Army Day recalls **K. M. Cariappa's command takeover, 1949**.
- Legs traverse stairs, slopes and debris; sensors/thermal cameras support reconnaissance/perimeter security, light supply carriage, hazardous-material handling and possible bomb-disposal support. Payload options are not proof every deployed unit has every attachment or weapon.
| Technical recall | Qualification |
| --- | --- |
| Weight / payload / top speed | **51 kg / 12 kg / 3 m/s** |
| Computing / protection | **NVIDIA Xavier / IP67** |
| Endurance | **About 3 hours walking**, **20–21 hours standby**, not 20 hours continuous movement |
| Temperature | Product page gives **−40°C to +55°C** in narrative, **−45°C** lower limit in feature list; tested specification/version needs checking |
- Manufacturer lists modular cameras, robotic arm, chemical/radiation detection and weapon-system options. [AeroArc product specifications](https://aeroarc.in/products/). The **100-km command range** in coaching text lacks supporting product specification here.
## Dated procurement claims and evaluation
- Source places delivery of **100 units in June 2023** before its stated **September 2023 order**; inconsistent sequence, not a verified procurement timeline. It cites emergency procurement ceiling **₹300 crore**, which is not evidence of actual contract value.
- Pune was a first Pune parade, not the first ever outside Delhi; source merges parade and Southern Command investiture contexts. [Official Army Day report](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleaseIframePage.aspx?PRID=2093060&lang=2&reg=3).
- Benefits: reduce human/canine exposure, carry small loads, improve situational awareness; potential disaster-response/search-and-rescue uses.
- Limits: endurance, payload, communications loss, terrain reliability, cyber security and maintenance. An armed autonomous-capable robot requires human accountability, lawful targeting and appropriate control; autonomy does not establish independent lethal deployment.
- Indian assembly/vendor presence alone does not establish wholly indigenous hardware, software or IP.
