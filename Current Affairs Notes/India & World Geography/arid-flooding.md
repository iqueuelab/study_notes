# Saudi floods and arid-region urban risk

## January 2025 case
- Source reports heavy rain, thunderstorms and flooding in **Mecca/Makkah, Madinah and Jeddah**, Saudi Arabia; roads/buildings inundated, vehicles displaced, travel disrupted.
- Source rainfall snapshot: **Al-Shafiyah, Madinah 49.2 mm; Al-Basateen, Jeddah 38 mm**; accumulation period unspecified, so do not compare with annual rainfall.
- **National Center of Meteorology (NCM)** alerts; **Saudi Red Crescent Authority** preparedness. 'Unprecedented' is the article's description, not a verified historical record.
## Mechanism and mains application
- Arid climate does **not exclude flash flooding**: concentrated rainfall + rapid runoff/impervious surfaces + insufficient drainage → short-warning floods.
- **Mecca**: Kaaba/Hajj pilgrimage; **Jeddah**, Red Sea gateway/transport hub; pilgrimage demand raises exposure and evacuation complexity.
- Impacts: disrupted roads/tourism/business, damaged infrastructure, contaminated water/waterborne-disease risk and rescue demands.
- Actions: rainfall nowcasting, drainage/wadi-flow protection, resilient roads, public warnings, rescue/health coordination and safe drinking water.
- Climate change can increase intense-rain risk, but attributing this specific event requires evidence; the PDF alone does not establish causation or a region-wide trend.
