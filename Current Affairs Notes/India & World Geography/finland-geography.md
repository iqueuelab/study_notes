# Finland: Baltic geography, lakes and strategic setting

## Finland: map and physical geography
- **Nordic, northern Europe; Helsinki** on the southern coast. Land neighbours **Sweden, Norway, Russia**; no German land border. **Gulf of Bothnia** lies west, **Gulf of Finland** south, both part of the Baltic system.
- Extensive forests, lakes, rivers and mires; northern Lapland experiences polar night/midnight sun, not every Finnish location. **Lake Saimaa** is the largest lake system; lake counts depend on minimum area and whether connected basins are grouped.
- **Statistics Finland 2024** gives roughly **168,000 lakes ≥500 m²**, about **57,000 >one hectare**. The source's 56,000 is not a valid unqualified total of all lakes. **Halti highest Finnish point 1,324 m**, not 1,328 m; it lies on the Norway-border slope, distinct from the massif's higher Norwegian summit. [Official geographical data](https://otos.stat.fi/bitstreams/0b74f055-709b-415b-802b-8b683615602a/download).
- **EU member; NATO joined 4 April 2023**. [Accession record](https://www.nato.int/en/news-and-events/articles/news/2023/04/04/finland-joins-nato-as-31st-ally). Copper, nickel, cobalt and chromium support mineral/industrial activity; listed resources do not mean Finland leads global production of each. Forest/lake landscapes reflect glaciation and high-latitude environments, while southern/northern climate conditions differ.
