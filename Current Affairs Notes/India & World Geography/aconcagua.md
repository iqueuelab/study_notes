# Aconcagua and the Andes

## February 2026: summit and geography recall
- Embedded clipping reports **Tripura mountaineer Aritra Roy's solo Aconcagua summit**. The separate prose about a Defence Minister flag-off concerns another expedition; do not merge the two accounts or turn a flag-off into a completed summit.
- **Mendoza, western Argentina**, near Chile, in the **Principal Cordillera of the Andes**; roughly **6,961 m**, highest in **South America, the Americas and outside Asia**. It is near the international boundary, not a binational summit on the boundary. [Mendoza park authority](https://informacionoficial.mendoza.gob.ar/energiayambiente/aconcagua/).
- Andes formed through plate convergence/subduction and uplift; Aconcagua is not an active volcanic cone. Cold, dry, windy high-altitude conditions and rain-shadow effects coexist with glaciers; altitude sickness, weather and exposure matter even on routes without major technical climbing.
- Seven Summits recall: **Everest—Asia; Aconcagua—South America; Denali—North America; Kilimanjaro—Africa; Elbrus—Europe; Vinson—Antarctica**. **Kosciuszko** represents continental Australia in one convention; **Puncak Jaya** is used for broader Oceania in another. UKPSC RO/ARO question is not a UPSC CSE PYQ.
