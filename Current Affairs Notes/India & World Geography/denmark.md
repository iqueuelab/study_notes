# Denmark: geography and digital child protection

## November 2025: map recall and social-media age proposal
- **7 November political agreement** proposes a **15-year minimum for specified social-media platforms**, with parental permission potentially allowing **13–14-year-olds**; it is not proof an unconditional blanket ban was already enforced. **160 million Danish kroner/14 initiatives** support digital child protection, safer design, enforcement and action on illegal influencer marketing. [Danish Ministry](https://www.digmin.dk/digitalisering/nyheder/nyhedsarkiv/2025/nov/ny-politisk-aftale).
- **Scandinavia: Denmark, Norway, Sweden** in usual usage; **Nordic** additionally includes Finland and Iceland and associated territories. Do not equate the two lists. **Kingdom of Denmark** comprises Denmark, Greenland and the Faroe Islands, with self-government arrangements for the latter two.
- European Denmark lies on **Jutland** and islands including **Zealand, Funen, Lolland, Bornholm**; capital **Copenhagen** principally on Zealand (also Amager). **Germany** is European Denmark's land neighbour; the wider Kingdom also shares a land boundary with **Canada on Hans Island/Tartupaluk following the 2022 agreement**. [Danish foreign ministry](https://canada.um.dk/en/the-arctic).
- Seas/straits: **North Sea west, Baltic east, Skagerrak/Kattegat north/northeast**, Danish straits linking the basins. Mostly low-lying glacial terrain, **Møllehøj ~171 m**; **Limfjord** crosses northern Jutland. Not every Danish inlet is a steep glacial Norwegian-style fjord.
- **Temperate maritime climate**, Atlantic influence; coastal flooding exposure. **Gudenå ~160 km**, Odense and Skjern rivers; Skjern drains to **Ringkøbing Fjord**. Source resources: North Sea hydrocarbons, fisheries, limestone/chalk/salt and wind-energy expertise; resource presence is different from an export-share ranking.
