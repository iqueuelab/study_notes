# Landslides and disaster relief

## Wayanad context
- January 2025 source discusses the 2024 Wayanad landslide and the Centre's assessment as a disaster of **severe nature**. It reports 254 deaths and 128 missing; these are reporting-time figures, not final totals.
- Impacts: displacement/trauma, infrastructure and crop losses, livelihood disruption, erosion and ecosystem damage; vulnerable groups need targeted assistance.
## Relief framework
- **Disaster Management Act, 2005**; NDMA for national disaster-policy coordination; SDRF provides state-level immediate relief.
- NDRF may supplement SDRF after assessment and prescribed procedure for severe disasters. **NCCF is the predecessor of NDRF**, not a separate contemporary additional fund as the source suggests. [Official fund explanation](https://ies.gov.in/arthapedia/concept/national-disaster-response-fund-ndrf).
- **NDRF fund ≠ National Disaster Response Force**; use full names to avoid confusion. Relief assistance is not full compensation for every loss.
## Risk reduction
- Hazard mapping, slope-sensitive land use and drainage, resilient roads/buildings, local rainfall monitoring and evacuation protocols; livelihood recovery and psychosocial support.
- Link to **Sendai Framework** and climate adaptation. A tsunami-warning system is a different hazard-specific example, not a landslide detector.
## Himalayan landslide dams
- Alaknanda study discussed on 9 January 2025: landslide blockage → temporary lake → possible dam breach → downstream flood. Risk combines slope failure with flood exposure; monitoring must follow both hazards.

## January 2026: Himalayan roads and cumulative ecological risk
- Editorial criticises **Char Dham road widening** in fragile Himalayan terrain: source reports proposed/approved removal of **about 7,000 deodar trees, 43 ha forest diversion and roughly 12-m DL–PS road width**. These are the editorial's project figures; the clipping alone does not establish the precise current clearance/court status for every section.
- **Young fold mountains, active faults/Main Central Thrust, steep slopes, weathered rock and seismicity** combine with heavy rain/glacier hazards. Wider cuts, poorly drained slopes and river/valley muck dumping can amplify landslide and flood losses. Angle of repose is a useful concept, but safe road design requires site-specific rock structure, groundwater and slope analysis.
- **Deodar (Cedrus deodara)** forests help bind soil, moderate microclimate and protect catchments; removing mature trees loses site-specific functions that sapling planting/translocation cannot immediately replace. Do not turn the source's antimicrobial-compound explanation into proof deodar alone purifies the Ganga.
- **Bhagirathi ESZ** notified **18 December 2012**, Gaumukh–Uttarkashi watershed, approximately **100-km river stretch /4,179.59 km²**; Zonal Master Plan approved **16 July 2020**. ESZ regulation permits specified development subject to safeguards, not a blanket ban on every activity north of the MCT. [MoEFCC](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=1639387&lang=2&reg=48).
- **National Mission for Sustaining the Himalayan Ecosystem** links glacier/biodiversity monitoring, ecosystem protection and livelihoods. Use cumulative watershed-level assessments, carrying capacity/tourism limits, drainage/muck management, slope-specific engineering, monitoring and evacuation planning; bolts/mesh cannot substitute for sound initial design.
- Climate change multiplies risk; glacier basins may experience an initial rise in meltwater followed by decline after **peak water**, with timing differing by basin. “Floods now, drought later” is a schematic mechanism rather than a uniform forecast.
- Source's **331 disaster days /over 4,000 deaths**, **50%-faster warming** and **800 landslide zones along 700 km** need their period/geographic denominator. CSE's verified January–September 2025 figures are **270/273 days and 4,064 deaths across India**, not deaths mainly in Himachal/Uttarakhand. [CSE assessment](https://www.cseindia.org/content/downloadreports/12927).
