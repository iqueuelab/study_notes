# Barbados: Atlantic geography and coral limestone

## Map and relief
- Independent island state, capital **Bridgetown**, in the **Atlantic east of the main Caribbean Sea**, northeast of Venezuela; part of the wider Caribbean/West Indies.
- **Easternmost Lesser Antilles island** in conventional regional geography, lying east of the volcanic island arc. The PDF incorrectly turns its position outside that volcanic arc into exclusion from the Lesser Antilles. [Regional climate centre country profile](https://rcc.cimh.edu.bb/files/2018/06/Country-Profile-Barbados.pdf).
- Predominantly uplifted **coral limestone**, rather than a volcanic cone; gullies and terraced relief. **Mount Hillaby**, Scotland District, about **340 m**, highest point. Coral cover does not make every part of the island a reef-built atoll; Scotland District exposes older sedimentary material. [Barbados national biodiversity report](https://biodiversity.gov.bb/wp-content/uploads/2018/12/1st-National-Report.pdf).
