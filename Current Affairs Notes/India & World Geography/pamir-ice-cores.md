# Pamir ice cores and the Karakoram glacier anomaly

## December 2025: Pamir climate archives
- International **PAMIR Project** extracted **104.7 m and 105 m ice cores** from **Kon Chukurbashi ice cap, Tajikistan**, about **5,814 m altitude** in autumn 2025. One supports scientific analysis; one was designated for **Ice Memory Antarctic preservation**, not proof it was already deposited there by this clipping. [Project team](https://pamir-project.ch/pamir-team-secures-two-ice-cores-over-100-metres-long-from-the-kon-chukurbashi-ice-cap-in-tajikistan/).
- Ice layers preserve accumulation/refreezing histories; water isotopes help infer climate; sulphate peaks can mark volcanic episodes. Layer interpretation needs dating and local calibration rather than a one-to-one temperature label for every clear layer. Source hopes for **10,000+ years** of records and mentions a **~6,000-year-old warm phase**; these are research expectations/background, not a completed dated core chronology.
- **Pamir–Karakoram anomaly** describes regional/period-specific relative stability or mass gain in some glaciers while global losses accelerate. Not every Pamir glacier grows, and this is not evidence that warming spares the region permanently. Test snowfall, atmospheric circulation and irrigation-moisture hypotheses rather than present any as proven.
- Glacier mass balance connects accumulation and ablation; water-security, ecosystem and downstream-flow implications matter for the Himalaya/Indus region. Understanding an anomaly can improve projections; it does not provide a transferable technique guaranteed to stop global melting.
- **Pamir Knot** in Central Asia, mainly Tajikistan: geographical junction associated with **Tian Shan, Kunlun, Karakoram and Hindu Kush**. Nanga Parbat is a peak; Dhauladhar belongs to the Lesser Himalaya. Source's complete **Haryana Civil Services 2014** question is a State PSC question, excluded from CSE PYQ banks.
