# Iron ore: types and distribution

## Ore and industrial use
- **Ore:** a mineral/rock from which a valuable constituent can be economically extracted; price, grade, technology and extraction costs influence viability. Iron ore chiefly supplies iron/steel for construction, transport, machinery, tools and pipes.
| Mineral | Formula / character | Pure-mineral iron content versus mined grade |
|---|---|---|
| Magnetite | Fe3O4; black, magnetic | About 72.4% theoretical Fe; ore grades vary |
| Haematite | Fe2O3; reddish | About 69.9% theoretical Fe; principal Indian commercial ore |
| Limonite | Variable hydrous iron oxides/oxyhydroxides; yellow-brown | Mixture, not a single fixed formula or iron percentage |
| Siderite | FeCO3; iron carbonate | About 48.2% theoretical Fe; mined ore can be lower grade |
- **Lumps** can feed suitable furnaces directly; **fines** generally require agglomeration; **pellets** are formed from beneficiated fine material. Actual ore contains gangue, so a mineral's theoretical Fe percentage is not its deposit's guaranteed grade.

| Belt | Revision locations |
|---|---|
| Odisha–Jharkhand | Badampahar (Mayurbhanj), Keonjhar deposits; Gua/Noamundi in Singhbhum |
| Durg–Bastar–Chandrapur | Chhattisgarh–Maharashtra; Bailadila's 14 deposits; Dalli–Rajhara |

- Bailadila ore has historically served export routes through Visakhapatnam, including Japan/South Korea; market destinations can change.
- **Kudremukh mining stopped at end-2005 (effective 1 January 2006)** following the Supreme Court decision. Its slurry pipeline to Mangaluru describes the historical mine operation; KIOCL's pellet business does not prove the mine remains operational. ## Statistics: correct scope and date

- **22,487 million tonnes as at 1 April 2015** is India's haematite **reserves plus remaining resources**, not total iron-ore reserves; magnetite resources were separately about **10,789 million tonnes**. Resources and economically recoverable reserves differ. [IBM statistical profile](https://ibm.gov.in/writereaddata/files/12092021164614Statistical_Profile_2019-20.pdf).

The source's exact state shares, 7% global share, fourth-place ranking and 79% value claim require a stated statistical year and denominator; do not memorise them as timeless/current shares. Its “second-largest haematite producer after Russia” assertion is not retained as verified.
- The reported target to auction **500 mineral blocks by 2025–26** covers minerals broadly, including rare earths and precious metals, not 500 iron-ore mines.

## World distribution
- Major regions: Western Australia; Brazil; China's Manchuria/Chang Jiang areas and Hainan; USA/Canada's Great Lakes region; Russia's Kursk and Urals; Sweden; historic Lorraine/Normandy/Pyrenees regions of France and the Rhine region; Liberia, South Africa and Algeria.
- **Kerch Peninsula is in Crimea, Ukraine, under Russian administration**; the source's placement simply under Russia obscures the territorial qualification. Reserve rankings depend on year, ore versus contained iron and reserve/resource definitions. [IBM mineral statistics](https://ibm.gov.in/IBMPortal/pages/Indian_Minerals_Yearbook).
