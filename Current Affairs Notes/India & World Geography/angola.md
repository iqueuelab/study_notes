# Angola: geography and India relations

## Presidential visit and map revision
- **Droupadi Murmu's 8–11 November 2025 State visit** was the first by an Indian President to Angola; she addressed its National Assembly at **Luanda on 10 November**, around the **50th independence anniversary**. Angola became independent from Portugal in **1975**. Correct the source: formal India–Angola diplomatic relations began in **1985**, not 1975. [Presidential address](https://presidentofindia.nic.in/press_releases/president-india-addresses-national-assembly-angola), [MEA bilateral brief](https://www.mea.gov.in/Portal/ForeignRelation/Public_Bilateral_Brief_as_on_April_2023.pdf).
- **Southwestern Africa, Atlantic coast; capital Luanda**. Neighbours: **DR Congo, Republic of Congo, Zambia, Namibia**. **Cabinda is an exclave**, separated from the main territory by DR Congo; the Republic of Congo border is with Cabinda. Do not place Angola on the Indian Ocean.
- Map anchors: **Cuanza/Kwanza River—Capanda hydropower; Cuango—Congo basin and diamond areas; Kalandula/Calandula Falls—Lucala River; Mount Moco—2,620 m, Huambo**. The **cold Benguela Current** helps shape western coastal aridity; climate varies from tropical north to drier southwest.
- Resources: petroleum and northeastern **Lunda diamonds**, with other mineral/agricultural potential; coffee, sisal, cotton and sugarcane are source examples. “Second-largest African oil producer” and “top-five diamond producer” require year/metric and should not be timeless ranks.
- India links: crude-oil imports/energy diversification; exports of pharmaceuticals, machinery and vehicles; **ITEC training, scholarships and South–South cooperation**. The issue's **US$4–5 billion trade** has no matching financial year; retain as a source estimate, not a current audited total. Its ONGC Videsh “investment interests” is not proof of an operating asset.
