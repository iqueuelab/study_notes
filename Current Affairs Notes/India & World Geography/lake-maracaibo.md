# Lake Maracaibo and Catatumbo lightning

## Location and lightning hotspot
- **Northwestern Venezuela**; Catatumbo River enters Maracaibo, a large lake/estuarine basin connected to the Caribbean via the Gulf of Venezuela.
- **Catatumbo lightning / Beacon of Maracaibo / Everlasting Storm**: frequent nocturnal thunderstorms, especially in the wetter part of the year. Warm moist lowland air and surrounding mountain circulation favour convergence and convection.
- Source cites roughly **260 storm days annually**, with strong October activity; these are descriptive averages, not guaranteed daily conditions. Lightning density, not simply total storm count, underlies its hotspot designation; sailors historically used the glow for navigation.
- Lake–country revision: **Maracaibo—Venezuela; Onega—Russia; Michigan—USA; Baikal—Russia**. Michigan lies entirely in the USA; Baikal is the deepest freshwater lake. The UPPSC 2023 Onega–Canada pairing is false.
