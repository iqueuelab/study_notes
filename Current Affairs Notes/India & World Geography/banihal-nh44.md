# Banihal Bypass and NH-44 connectivity

## January 2025 project facts
- **Banihal, Jammu and Kashmir**, on the **Ramban–Banihal stretch of NH-44**; source reports completion of a **four-lane, 2.35-km bypass**, **₹224.44 crore**.
- **Four viaducts, total 1,513 metres; three culverts**; bypasses town-market bottlenecks, aiding civilian/tourist and defence movement towards Kashmir.
## Route recall
- **NH-44: Srinagar → Kanyakumari**; an amalgamation of previous highway stretches, including old NH-7, rather than every section being old NH-7.
- Source length **3,745 km** is a dated/route-definition figure; avoid unverified world-rank '22nd'.
- Listed states/UTs: **Jammu and Kashmir → Punjab → Haryana → Delhi → Uttar Pradesh → Madhya Pradesh → Maharashtra → Telangana → Andhra Pradesh → Karnataka → Tamil Nadu**. Do not call all 11 'states'.
- Major cities along the corridor include Delhi, Agra, Hyderabad and Bengaluru.
- Geography link: transport corridors need slope/drainage safety, maintenance and disaster resilience, not only travel-time savings.
