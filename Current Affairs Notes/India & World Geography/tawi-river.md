# Tawi River and Jammu geography

## Location recall
- The issue flags **Tawi River** under Places in News without a detailed article. It flows through **Jammu**, part of the **Chenab/Indus drainage**; distinguish Jammu Tawi from other similarly named streams in the region. [MoEF regional river account](https://moef.gov.in/uploads/2017/08/Jammu-Kashmir.pdf); [Jammu district](https://jammu.nic.in/about-district/).
