Sonamarg / Z-Morh tunnel

Editable notes

Location and purpose

  • Gagangir–Sonamarg, Ganderbal, Jammu and Kashmir, on Srinagar–Leh NH-1. Name Z-Morh refers to a Z-shaped road stretch; terrain above 8,500 feet, prone to snow/avalanches.
  • Main tunnel 6.4 km in official release; source rounds to 6.5 km. Project includes an egress tunnel and approach roads, approximately 12 km overall.
  • Source specifications: two-lane road, designed around 1,000 vehicles/hour, 80 km/h; cites 4.26-km escape and 0.6-km ventilation components. Escape passage is for emergencies, not ordinary parallel traffic.
  • 11 January 2025 announcement: inauguration scheduled 13 January; subsequently inaugurated that day. PMO announcement, inauguration.

Corridor recall

  • Srinagar → Gagangir → Sonamarg → Zoji La → Drass/Kargil → Leh. Distinct from Banihal–Jammu NH-44.
  • Sonamarg tunnel enables all-weather access to Sonamarg; alone it does not complete year-round access across every Ladakh bottleneck.
  • Separate Zojila tunnel, source 14.15 km, planned eastward connection across the pass; do not confuse its length/completion with Sonamarg.
  • Benefits: winter tourism, local livelihoods, rescue and defence logistics near sensitive northern/eastern frontiers; monitor ecological disturbance, avalanche risk and emergency ventilation/egress.

Practice-test trap

  • The 19 January key overstates year-round access to all of Ladakh. Sonamarg access ≠ completion of the separate Zoji La tunnel; check the specific section of the route.
