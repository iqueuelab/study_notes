# Levant: eastern Mediterranean geography

## Map recall
- The **Levant** roughly denotes the **eastern Mediterranean shores**: Syria, Lebanon, Israel, Palestine and Jordan, with boundaries sometimes extending into adjoining Türkiye.
- A historical crossroads between Africa, Asia and Europe; relevant to ancient civilisations, trade and contemporary Syrian/Israel–Palestine conflicts.
- French **lever**, to rise, evokes the eastern rising sun; Arabic **al-Mashriq** is related in meaning but geographically broader.
- **2022 Prelims: (a)** eastern Mediterranean shores; neither the entire Mediterranean nor the Persian Gulf/Horn of Africa.
