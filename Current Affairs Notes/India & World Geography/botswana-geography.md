# Botswana: geography and inland ecosystems

## Map and terrain
- **Landlocked Southern African country; capital Gaborone**. Neighbours: **Namibia** west/north, **Zambia** across the short Kazungula boundary, **Zimbabwe** northeast, **South Africa** south/southeast.
- **Sandveld:** extensive Kalahari sands; **Hardveld:** eastern rocky terrain, supporting denser settlement and relatively better vegetation. The Kalahari occupies much of the country; the PDF's 70–80% figure is approximate. Its unnamed “highest point north of Lobatse, 1,490 m” is not sufficiently established for exact-height recall.
## Rivers, wetlands and resources
- **Okavango River → Okavango Delta** in northern Botswana: an inland wetland without an ocean outlet; seasonal flooding sustains elephants, hippos, lions and other wildlife. UNESCO World Heritage inscription **2014**. Water-system cooperation involves upstream Angola and Namibia as well as Botswana. [UNESCO](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1432/).
- **Chobe River:** northern wildlife landscape; **Limpopo:** forms part of the South African boundary.
- **Makgadikgadi Pans:** large salt flats on former lake beds; wet-season water supports migratory birds and flamingos.
- Resources include **diamonds, copper, nickel, soda ash and coal**. Jwaneng is the diamond-value landmark; distinguish minerals from the biodiversity/tourism economy and producer value from producer volume.
