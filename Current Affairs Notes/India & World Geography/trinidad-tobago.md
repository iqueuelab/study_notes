# Trinidad and Tobago

## Map recall
- Capital **Port of Spain**; island country in the **south-eastern West Indies**, near north-eastern Venezuela and north-western Guyana; member of **CARICOM**.
- **Northern Range** linked geologically to Venezuela's coastal ranges; highest point **Mount Aripo**.
- **Pitch Lake**, Trinidad: natural asphalt deposit; rivers **Ortoire and Caroni**.
- January 2025 source context: emergency declared amid violence; retain as a dated news trigger, not a permanent governance status.

## Diaspora link
- **January 2025**: President **Christine Carla Kangaloo** was chief guest of the 18th Pravasi Bharatiya Divas in Bhubaneswar, addressing virtually; an example of Indian Caribbean diaspora links.

## Image-based map facts
- Twin-island country at south-eastern Caribbean/West Indies, close to **Venezuela**, north-west of Guyana; capital **Port of Spain**; CARICOM member.
- Northern Range and **Cerro del Aripo**; rivers Caroni/Ortoire; **Pitch Lake**, a natural asphalt deposit.
- Violence-related state-of-emergency report is a dated development, not a permanent constitutional condition.

## July map additions
- **Caroni flows west to the Gulf of Paria; Ortoire flows east to the Atlantic**. Port of Spain is on Trinidad's north-west coast; Trinidad is the larger industrial island, Tobago more tourism-oriented. Northern Range links to Venezuela's coastal geology; **Cerro del Aripo ~940m** is the high point. Pitch Lake is at **La Brea, south-west Trinidad**.
- India–Trinidad diaspora/UPI developments are covered in the common diplomatic topic; the physical map remains the southeastern Caribbean, close to Venezuela and northwest of Guyana.
