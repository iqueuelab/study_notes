# Ethiopia: Horn of Africa geography and climate diplomacy

## December 2025: location and COP32
- **Landlocked East African/Horn of Africa country**, landlocked after **Eritrean independence in 1993**; capital **Addis Ababa**, currency **birr**. Borders **Eritrea, Djibouti, Somalia, Kenya, South Sudan and Sudan**.
- Relief: **Ethiopian Highlands**, eastern/western highlands and lowlands separated by the **East African Rift**; highest point **Ras Dejen (Ras Dashen)**. The sheet gives 4,533 m; height varies by source, so prioritise identification over this unverified exact value. Major languages include Amharic, Oromo, Afar, Somali and Tigrinya.
- **Lake Tana**, northwestern highlands, is the source of the **Blue Nile/Abay**, central to Ethiopia–Sudan–Egypt hydropolitics. **Omo** and **Awash** are other major rivers. The sheet's “50% of Ethiopia's freshwater” lacks a defined denominator and is not retained.
- Addis Ababa hosts the **African Union** and **UN Economic Commission for Africa**. Ethiopia's founding UN membership and Pan-African/decolonisation role underpin its multilateral importance.
- **Correction:** by the December issue, COP30 had accepted Ethiopia's offer to host **COP32 in Addis Ababa, 8–19 November 2027**; the sheet's “likely/confirmation expected” is outdated. African priorities include adaptation, climate finance and loss and damage. [UNFCCC adopted dates-and-venues text](https://unfccc.int/sites/default/files/resource/COP30_2g_dates_venues_auv.pdf).
