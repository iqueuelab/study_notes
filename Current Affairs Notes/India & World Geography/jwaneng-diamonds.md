# Jwaneng mine and the diamond value chain

## Jwaneng: location, geology and ownership
- **Botswana, Southern Africa**, southwest of capital Gaborone; an open-pit mine exploiting **three main kimberlite pipes**. World's richest/most valuable diamond mine refers to diamond value, not necessarily greatest output or largest excavation.
- Debswana is a **50:50 partnership of Botswana's government and De Beers**. Operator history dates discovery to **1972**, correcting the PDF's 1973; full production began **July 1982**, official opening August 1982. [Debswana history](https://www.debswana.com/our-history/).
- Kimberlite pipes are ancient volcanic conduits that transport mantle diamonds towards the surface; the PDF's “dormant volcano” wording should not imply a modern volcano at the mine. “Jwaneng” is associated with gems/stones in Setswana; “Prince of Mines” is its popular nickname.
- **10 January 2024:** Debswana approved a development phase for an underground project; this is a planned transition, not proof underground mining had begun. [De Beers announcement](https://www.debeersgroup.com/news-insights/business-market/2024/debswana-board-approves-key-development-phase-for-jwaneng-underground-project).
- Source's 2023 output **11.86 million carats** is inconsistent with the company's comparative production table (**13.329 million** in 2023; **6.779 million** in 2024). Its “over $1 billion” worth lacks a valuation basis and 2036 is a project-life estimate, not a guaranteed closure date. [Production report](https://www.debeersgroup.com/news-insights/latest-group-news/2025/production-report-for-the-fourth-quarter-of-2024).
## Diamonds: revision essentials
- Crystalline carbon; natural diamonds form under mantle pressure/temperature. **Mohs hardness 10**; hardness means scratch resistance, not immunity to fracture. Primary deposits occur in **kimberlite and lamproite**; gem-grade stones serve jewellery, industrial diamonds cutting, drilling and grinding.
- Country rankings depend on **year and metric**: Russia is a major volume producer; Botswana a major value producer. Canada, Angola and South Africa also matter. Mine recall: **Jwaneng—Botswana; Udachnaya/Mir—Russia; Argyle—Australia**. Argyle, famous for pink diamonds, closed in **November 2020**; its later operations were underground, so the PDF's simple open-pit label is incomplete. [Rio Tinto](https://www.riotinto.com/operations/anz/western-australia/argyle).
- India has a long diamond-mining heritage, especially the Golconda trade; Koh-i-Noor and Hope are famous historic stones. **Majhgawan near Panna, Madhya Pradesh**, operated by NMDC, is India's **only mechanised diamond mine**, correcting the broader “only active mine” claim. [NMDC](https://www.nmdc.co.in/about-nmdc/operating-mines/diamond-mining-project-panna).
- India imports rough diamonds for processing; **Surat, Gujarat**, is a leading cutting/polishing hub. **GJEPC = Gem and Jewellery Export Promotion Council**; De Beers is a major producer/marketer, but a “world's largest” label needs a metric/year.
- **Kimberley Process Certification Scheme (2003)** regulates the international trade in rough diamonds to keep conflict diamonds out; it is not a guarantee covering every social/environmental problem in the entire jewellery chain. [Kimberley Process](https://www.kimberleyprocess.com/about/what-is-kp).
## Resource governance: mains link
- Botswana illustrates how state participation, expertise, public revenues and reinvestment in health, education and infrastructure can support resource-led development. Transparent benefit-sharing and diversification matter: diamond dependence still exposes an economy to price/demand shocks.
- Examine mineral diplomacy, environmental safeguards and PPP accountability rather than treating resource wealth as automatically eliminating the resource curse.

## August 2025: tariff exposure in the processing chain
- Embedded clipping reports a US **25% tariff from 7 August** and a further **25% announced for 27 August**, as understood at this issue date. [6 August US announcement](https://www.whitehouse.gov/fact-sheets/2025/08/fact-sheet-president-donald-j-trump-addresses-threats-to-the-united-states-by-the-government-of-the-russian-federation/). The second component was prospective; do not report both as already effective on 16 August. Surat processors face order, employment and margin pressures despite established cutting/polishing capacity.
- The clipping's argument that US buyers have few alternatives is an industry assessment, not proof tariffs cannot alter sourcing, demand or margins. Its **over 30% US export share, over 90% global cutting/polishing and roughly 7.5 lakh workers** are source-era industry estimates with different denominators, not new audited national totals. Existing geology, mine-location and Kimberley Process qualifications apply.
