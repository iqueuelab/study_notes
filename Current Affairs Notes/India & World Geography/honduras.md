# Honduras: map recall

## Location
- Central America: borders **Guatemala, El Salvador and Nicaragua**; Caribbean coast on Gulf of Honduras, Pacific access through **Gulf of Fonseca**.
- Capital **Tegucigalpa**; highland terrain, highest peak Cerro Las Minas. Rivers Coco, Patuca and Ulúa; Lake Yojoa and Caratasca lagoon.
- Forest, mineral/agricultural resources and hurricane/flood exposure; source Indian 26-tonne aid report followed Tropical Storm Sara, a dated humanitarian event.
