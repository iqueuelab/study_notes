Kopili River and National Waterway 57

Editable notes

River and navigation

  • Inter-State river of Meghalaya/Assam, left/south-bank tributary of Brahmaputra; important drainage through the Dima Hasao/North Cachar, Karbi Anglong, Nagaon–Morigaon region. Source length 256 km (78 km border reach+178 km Assam); its origin wording conflates Meghalaya with North Cachar Hills, so do not memorise that administrative description without a basin map.
  • 2 August 2025: MV V. V. Giri trial carried 300 MT cement from Govardhan Bridge, Chandrapur (Kamrup) to Hatsingimari, using NW57+NW2; ~300 km/12–14 hours. First cargo trial on 46 kmNW57 since 2014, part of revival of intra-State freight. River length, designated waterway length and total voyage distance differ. Ministry release.
  • Assam's operational national-waterway network reported 1,168 km across BrahmaputraNW2, BarakNW16, DhansiriNW31, KopiliNW57. A cargo trial is not proof of year-round deep-draft navigation or every segment's commercial viability.
  • Inland transport can reduce road congestion/freight emissions; needs terminals, last-mile links, adequate water levels and ecological safeguards. Meghalaya coal-mining-related acidification threatens river health and infrastructure.
  • Embedded old map mislabels Barak NW6; the National Waterways Act, 2016 expanded the total to111, and Barak=NW16. Do not use the inset's old “five waterways/4,603 km” as a 2025 inventory. Declared status and navigational operationalisation are different dates.
