Sawalkote hydropower: clearance, Chenab and environmental appraisal

Editable notes

October 2025: renewed environmental appraisal

  • Issue reports fresh environmental clearance after September EAC appraisal for the proposed 1,856-MW Sawalkote/Sawalkot Chenab project, implemented by NHPC in Jammu & Kashmir. NHPC identifies Ramban/Udhampur location and design energy about 7,995 million units annually; this is projected generation, not achieved output. Source estimated cost ₹31,380 crore is a dated estimate; agency estimates vary by price basis.
  • Run-of-river does not mean no dam or negligible displacement: NHPC specifies a 192.5-metre dam. Pondage, diversion tunnels and hydropower infrastructure can alter flows, sediment, fish connectivity and habitats. Evaluate environmental flows, seismic/slope risks, rehabilitation and cumulative cascade impacts. NHPC project.
  • A 2021 NHPC–JKSPDC arrangement entrusted implementation on BOOT terms; do not convert a 40-year operating period into a guaranteed calendar end of 2061 irrespective of commissioning. Source forest-clearance dates and simple “2017 granted EC” history require caution: appraisal recommendation, formal EC, in-principle forest permission and final forest permission are different decisions.
  • The source describes this as the first major clearance after India's April treaty-abeyance announcement; retain as its dated report rather than an adjudication of treaty compliance. Clearance does not establish all investment/forest approvals or construction completion. Practice answer on agency: NHPC, not continuing sole JKSPDC implementation.
