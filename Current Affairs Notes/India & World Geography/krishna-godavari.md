# Krishna and Godavari: basins, tributaries and flood management

## August flood alerts and basin recall
- Embedded report describes high inflows and alerts along **Krishna and Godavari** in Andhra Pradesh/Telangana. Coordinate reservoir releases, forecasts, evacuation and downstream warnings; inflows/discharges in a news clipping are time-specific.
| Feature | Krishna | Godavari |
|---|---|---|
| Source | Near Mahabaleshwar, Western Ghats, Maharashtra | Trimbakeshwar near Nashik, Maharashtra |
| Basin | Maharashtra, Karnataka, Telangana, Andhra Pradesh; about 2.59 lakh km² | Largest peninsular basin; about 3.13 lakh km² |
| Tributaries | Left: Bhima, Musi; right: Ghataprabha, Malaprabha, Tungabhadra | Left: Purna, Pranahita, Indravati, Sabari; right: Pravara, Manjira, Manair |
| Mouth | Bay of Bengal near Hamsaladeevi | Bay of Bengal, Andhra Pradesh delta |
- Godavari is **Dakshin Ganga**; Nashik's Kumbh and Bhadrachalam/Rajahmundry link river and culture. Wardha and Wainganga form **Pranahita**, with Penganga part of the tributary network, not three equal main-stem sources at one confluence.
- Krishna dams include **Almatti, Srisailam, Nagarjunasagar and Dhom**; Tungabhadra dam is on its namesake tributary. Godavari projects include **Polavaram, Sriram Sagar, Kaleshwaram and Sadarmatt**; Inchampalli is a proposal, not a completed dam.
