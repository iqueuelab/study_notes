Iron ore: types and distribution

Editable notes

Ore and industrial use

  • Ore: a mineral/rock from which a valuable constituent can be economically extracted; price, grade, technology and extraction costs influence viability. Iron ore chiefly supplies iron/steel for construction, transport, machinery, tools and pipes.
MineralFormula / characterPure-mineral iron content versus mined grade
MagnetiteFe3O4; black, magneticAbout 72.4% theoretical Fe; ore grades vary
HaematiteFe2O3; reddishAbout 69.9% theoretical Fe; principal Indian commercial ore
LimoniteVariable hydrous iron oxides/oxyhydroxides; yellow-brownMixture, not a single fixed formula or iron percentage
SideriteFeCO3; iron carbonateAbout 48.2% theoretical Fe; mined ore can be lower grade
  • Lumps can feed suitable furnaces directly; fines generally require agglomeration; pellets are formed from beneficiated fine material. Actual ore contains gangue, so a mineral's theoretical Fe percentage is not its deposit's guaranteed grade.

Statistics: correct scope and date

  • The source's 2.3% GDP contribution (2022–23) concerns mining activity, not iron ore alone. Its 1,319 reporting mines is a wider mining statistic, not the count of iron-ore mines. Likewise, 87 minerals and the seven-state concentration must not be relabelled as iron-only data.
  • 22,487 million tonnes as at 1 April 2015 is India's haematite reserves plus remaining resources, not total iron-ore reserves; magnetite resources were separately about 10,789 million tonnes. Resources and economically recoverable reserves differ. IBM statistical profile.
  • Odisha is the leading producer, followed by other major producers such as Chhattisgarh, Karnataka and Jharkhand. The source's exact state shares, 7% global share, fourth-place ranking and 79% value claim require a stated statistical year and denominator; do not memorise them as timeless/current shares. Its “second-largest haematite producer after Russia” assertion is not retained as verified.
  • The reported target to auction 500 mineral blocks by 2025–26 covers minerals broadly, including rare earths and precious metals, not 500 iron-ore mines.

World distribution

  • Major regions: Western Australia; Brazil; China's Manchuria/Chang Jiang areas and Hainan; USA/Canada's Great Lakes region; Russia's Kursk and Urals; Sweden; historic Lorraine/Normandy/Pyrenees regions of France and the Rhine region; Liberia, South Africa and Algeria.
  • Kerch Peninsula is in Crimea, Ukraine, under Russian administration; the source's placement simply under Russia obscures the territorial qualification. Reserve rankings depend on year, ore versus contained iron and reserve/resource definitions. IBM mineral statistics.
