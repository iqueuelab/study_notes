# Perito Moreno Glacier and Patagonian ice

## Map recall and change
- **Perito Moreno Glacier**, Patagonian Andes, **Santa Cruz, Argentina**, near **El Calafate**, terminates in **Lake Argentino**; part of **Los Glaciares National Park**, UNESCO World Heritage **1981**. Do not confuse it with the separately named Perito Moreno National Park. [UNESCO](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/145).
- Source dimensions **~30 km long /ice front ~60 m above water** are approximate; visible cliff height differs from total ice thickness. Southern Patagonian Ice Field contributes substantial freshwater storage; glacier dynamics include accumulation, flow and calving.
- The June source reports increasing ice loss after a long reputation for stability. Individual glacier trends require measurements and period-specific evidence; calving alone does not establish identical annual net loss everywhere.
- Source's “formed 18,000 years ago” is an unsupported exact age and misrepresents the long, fluctuating last glacial period. Preserve Ice Age landscape context without dating today's glacier to one universal formation year.
- Mains links: warming, freshwater systems, tourism and ecosystem management; climate trends and local geometry both influence retreat.
