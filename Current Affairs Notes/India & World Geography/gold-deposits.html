Gold deposits, exploration and monetary reserves

Editable notes

Two meanings of reserves

  • Geological resources/reserves concern gold in the ground; RBI monetary gold is a component of official foreign-exchange reserves. They are not interchangeable.
  • Mineral occurrence → exploration/resource estimate → economic reserve assessment → permissions/lease → production. A discovery or block auction is not a producing mine.
  • Odisha reports concerned districts including Deogarh, Sundargarh, Koraput, Nabarangpur, Angul, Keonjhar and Mayurbhanj; quantity/grade and feasibility must precede claims of a “massive” commercial reserve.
  • Karnataka: Hutti, Raichur, important operating producer; Kolar Gold Fields closed in 2001. Andhra Pradesh's Ramagiri/Jonnagiri and Jharkhand's Singhbhum/Lawa/Parasi are map anchors; avoid the stale blanket claim that Hutti is India's only possible operating gold project.
  • Lode deposits occur in bedrock/veins; placer deposits concentrate transported particles in sediments. Reported occurrences do not establish equal grade or profitability.
  • Domestic production may reduce some import dependence but not necessarily meet all demand. Assess tailings/water pollution, forest impacts, rehabilitation, applicable PESA/FRA rights, consultation and fair local benefits.
