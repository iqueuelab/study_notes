# Major and minor minerals: classification and map recall

## Legal distinction

- **MMDR Act 1957, section 3(e)** defines minor minerals: building stones, gravel, ordinary clay, ordinary sand except prescribed uses, and others notified by Centre. It is a **legal classification**, not simply low output/value or a small mine. [Act](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/20096/1/a1957-67.pdf).
- State rules regulate minor-mineral concessions under **section 15**; central notification determines additional minor minerals. Source's attribution solely to **MCR 1960** and inclusion of all gemstones/asbestos as minor are unreliable; diamond is not minor just because it is a gemstone.
- **As at January 2025** mica/quartz/barytes were minor. **20 February 2025 notification** subsequently moved **barytes, felspar, mica, quartz to major**; preserve that date when revising. [Ministry announcement](https://www.pib.gov.in/Pressreleaseshare.aspx?PRID=2105215&lang=2&reg=48).
