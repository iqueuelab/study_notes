Kalpeni atoll and coral formation

Editable notes

Map and landforms

  • Lakshadweep, Arabian Sea; southeast of Kavaratti, between Andrott and Minicoy, about 287 km / 155 nautical miles from Kochi.
  • Official/source area 2.79 km²; north–south alignment; lagoon maximum width about 2.8 km, not its total area.
  • Kalpeni + Tilakkam + Pitti + northern Cheriyam form an atoll; Cheriyam described as uninhabited, not Kalpeni itself. Administration geography, atoll constituents.
  • Volcanic island/fringing reef → subsidence with upward coral growth → barrier reef/lagoon → ring-like atoll: classic schematic, not proof each island has an identical geological history.
  • Coral reefs shelter lagoons and support diverse marine life; tourism includes snorkelling/diving; Kerala-like maritime monsoon climate.

January 2025 news and correction

  • Divers reported wreckage possibly from a 17th/18th-century European warship; identity/date were provisional, not an authenticated archaeological conclusion.
  • Source's Maliku community label confuses Kalpeni with Minicoy/Maliku; administration distinguishes Malayalam-speaking islands from Minicoy's Mahl language. Lakshadweep islands.
  • Protect reefs and wreck sites: scientific investigation, visitor limits, waste control and community livelihoods; avoid souvenir removal or unverified heritage claims.
