# Vembanad and lake-location recall

## Vembanad-Kol system

- **Kerala**, across **Alappuzha, Kottayam and Ernakulam**; elongated coastal/backwater system with Arabian Sea outlets. **India's longest lake**, Kerala's largest; the PDF's unqualified 'largest lake in India' is misleading across lake types.
- Names: **Vembanad Kayal / Vembanad Kol**, **Punnamada** in Kuttanad, **Kochi Lake** in Kochi.
- Rivers include **Meenachil, Achankovil, Pampa and Manimala**; these are not the entire catchment. Ramsar description identifies **10 feeding rivers**.
- **Ramsar designation 19 August 2002**; listed **Vembanad-Kol wetland area 151,250 ha (1,512.5 km²)**. Do not equate the PDF's **2,033 km²** lake/system estimate with the Ramsar site boundary. [Convention on Wetlands](https://www.ramsar.org/news/india-designates-eleven-new-wetlands-international-importance).
- Islands: **Pathiramanal, Perumbalam, Pallippuram**; **Kumarakom Bird Sanctuary on eastern shore**.
- **Nehru Trophy Boat Race**, Punnamada; traditionally August, actual annual scheduling can change. Vallam Kali is the broader boat-racing tradition, not just this race.

## Ecosystem and livelihoods

- Fisheries/clams, rice cultivation, navigation, tourism, birds, flood buffering and groundwater recharge.
- Sewage/industrial and slaughterhouse/shrimp-processing pollution, encroachment/shrinking wetland and altered flows/salinity threaten water quality and livelihoods.
