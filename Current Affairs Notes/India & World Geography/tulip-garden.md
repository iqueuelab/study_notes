# Srinagar Tulip Garden

## Location and significance
- Indira Gandhi Memorial Tulip Garden: Zabarwan foothills overlooking Dal Lake, Srinagar; opened in 2007, formerly the Model Floriculture Centre.
- Roughly 30 hectares, with seven terraces; varieties and bloom dates vary by season. Its size recognition is not UNESCO World Heritage status.
- **Tulipa belongs to Liliaceae**; native chiefly to Central and south-central Asia. Spring flowering, cup-shaped flowers and varied colours support floriculture and tourism; the Netherlands is a major commercial centre.
- The spring Tulip Festival links horticulture, livelihoods and Kashmir tourism. Climate and flowering windows affect visitor planning.
