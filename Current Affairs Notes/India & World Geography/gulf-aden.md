# Gulf of Aden: chokepoints and maritime security

## Map recall
- Between **Yemen / Arabian Peninsula (north)** and **Somalia / Horn of Africa (south)**; **Djibouti** at western approach, **Arabian Sea** to east. **Socotra belongs to Yemen**, near the eastern approach.
- **Arabian Sea → Gulf of Aden → Bab el-Mandeb → Red Sea → Suez Canal → Mediterranean**. Gulf of Aden does not directly open into the Mediterranean.
- **Guardafui Channel**: between northeastern Somalia and Socotra; **Gulf of Tadjoura**: western inlet in Djibouti. **Hormuz** connects Persian Gulf/Gulf of Oman, not Aden/Red Sea.
- Source dimensions roughly **900 km × 500 km**, area **410,000 km²**; approximate boundary-dependent measures. **Sheba Ridge**, active spreading system; salinity generally lower than the highly evaporative Red Sea.
- Ports/cities: **Aden, Mukalla, Ahwar, Balhaf (Yemen); Berbera, Bosaso (Somali coast); Djibouti City**.
## Security and economy
- Suez route vital for Asia–Europe commerce, energy and container shipping. Source **11% of seaborne petroleum** lacks a reference year/definition; avoid treating as an unchanging flow.
- Piracy, attacks on shipping and regional conflict threaten vessels and raise insurance/freight costs; diversions around the Cape lengthen voyages.
- Indian naval patrols, maritime-domain awareness, escorts/rescue and partner information-sharing; protect commerce while following international law and avoiding escalation.
