# Pamba River: geography, pilgrimage and river health

## Basin and map recall
- Kerala's **third-longest river**, after **Periyar (244 km)** and **Bharathappuzha (209 km)**; source gives Pamba **176 km**, basin **2,235 km²** entirely within Kerala. Origin: **Pulachi Mala, Western Ghats**; flows into **Vembanad backwaters**, then Arabian Sea, rather than a simple direct river mouth.
- **Kakki, Azhutha, Kakkad and Kallar** are associated tributaries; Manimala and Achankovil are distinct rivers in the connected lower wetland/drainage system, so avoid treating every named river as an identical upper-basin tributary.
- **Dakshina Bhagirathi**: sacred association with Sabarimala; pilgrims bathe before ascending Neelimala. **Correction:** Kerala Tourism identifies the pilgrimage **Triveni as Pamba–Kallar–Azhutha**, not the source's Pamba–Achankovil–Manimala. [Official Pamba account](https://www.keralatourism.org/sabarimala/pamba).
## Uses and management
- Paddy/coconut/banana irrigation, hydropower and Western Ghats ecology; Kakki/Anathodu reservoirs and Sabarigiri tail-race water feed irrigation, with Kakkad water picked up at **Maniyar barrage**. [Kerala Irrigation Department](https://irrigation.kerala.gov.in/node/725).
- Untreated sewage, solid waste and pilgrimage pressure affect river health; the **2018 floods** illustrate basin-wide exposure. Protect riparian habitats, manage sewage/seasonal crowds and coordinate reservoir/floodplain management.
- Revision from non-CSE question panels: Periyar longest, Bharathappuzha second, Pamba third; **Tamraparni is east-flowing to the Gulf of Mannar**, while the three Kerala rivers flow west. SSC/NDA examples support revision but are excluded from CSE PYQ banks.
