# Polavaram: river linking and multipurpose water infrastructure

## Core facts
- Multipurpose national project on the **Godavari, Andhra Pradesh**, not on Krishna; envisages an **80 TMC diversion to the Krishna basin**.
- Planned **gross irrigation potential 4,36,825 ha**, **960 MW hydropower**, drinking water for **28.5 lakh people in 611 villages**. Gross/annual irrigation potential differs from physical command area. [Polavaram Project Authority](https://ppa.gov.in/WPSCore/Common/WebPages/Home/ProjectMap.aspx).
- Spillway design discharge about **50 lakh cusecs / 1,41,435 cumecs**. Source calls this 'world's highest'; treat superlative as unverified rather than a durable ranking.
- PDF simplifies dimensions to **54 m / 1.2 km / 48 radial gates**; distinguish dam, spillway and full-reservoir elevation when comparing engineering specifications. A '1-in-1000-year' flood is a probability concept, not a guarantee of no flood for 1,000 years.
## Benefits and costs
- Irrigation, drinking/industrial water, energy and basin connectivity; benefits depend on completion and operating rules.
- Forest/farm submergence, tribal displacement, backwater impacts, altered sediment/environmental flows and fisheries livelihoods.
- Mains: assess demand/alternatives, interstate effects, fair rehabilitation and compensation, ecological flows, dam safety and transparent technical monitoring.
