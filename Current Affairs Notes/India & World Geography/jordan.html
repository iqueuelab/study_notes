Jordan: borders, Aqaba and the Dead Sea

Editable notes

Map revision in the December 2025 visit context

  • West Asian country, capital Amman; borders Syria, Iraq, Saudi Arabia, Israel and the Palestinian West Bank. It has a small Red Sea outlet at the Gulf of Aqaba, so “landlocked” is incorrect.
  • Aqaba/Al-Aqabah, southwestern Jordan, is its principal seaport and maritime access point. No Mediterranean coastline; the nearby Sea of Galilee is not Jordan's sea outlet.
  • Arid plateau with western Jordan Rift Valley/Dead Sea depression, part of the wider rift system. Jordan River flows south into the Dead Sea, an inland saline terminal lake; its shore is Earth's lowest exposed land elevation.
  • Highest peak Jabal Umm ad Dami, near the southern Saudi border. Phosphate, potash near the Dead Sea and oil shale are relevant resources; oil shale reserves do not imply established cheap petroleum production.
  • PYQs already banked: 2017 Mediterranean list—Lebanon and Syria only (c); 2015 country without a Mediterranean outlet—Jordan (b). Jordan's Aqaba access does not make it Mediterranean-facing.
