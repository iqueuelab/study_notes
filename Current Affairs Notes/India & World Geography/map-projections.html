Map projections: Mercator, equal area and Africa’s representation

What each projection preserves

ProjectionUseful propertyMain limitation
Mercator, 1569Conformal: local angles; constant-bearing rhumb lines plot straightArea inflation grows towards poles; poles cannot be shown at finite distance
Equal Earth, 2018Equal area, suitable for world thematic/area comparisonsShapes/angles are not all preserved
Gall–PetersEqual areaShape distortion varies with latitude; equatorial regions are stretched vertically, high latitudes horizontally
OrthographicGlobe-like view from spaceOnly one hemisphere; substantial distortion near its rim
  • No flat world map preserves every property. Mercator exaggerates high-latitude area relative to equatorial area; it does not literally reduce the true area of countries on Earth. A straight rhumb line is a constant compass bearing, not generally the shortest great-circle route.
  • Africa ~30 million km² versus Greenland ~2.1 million km², roughly 14 times larger; Africa is about three times Europe's area under standard continental definitions. Projection choice affects perceived regional importance but does not alone prove a specific political intent.

August 2025: Correct the Map campaign

  • Source reports African Union Commission support for Correct the Map, favouring area-faithful alternatives such as Equal Earth in education and public representation. Later AU documentation explicitly dates the deputy chairperson's support interview to August 2025; its later formal Assembly endorsement must not be moved back into this issue date. AU retrospective account.
  • Replacing school/atlas/app maps involves curricula, publishing, technical design and user habits. South-up/rotated maps challenge customary orientation but are not automatically equal-area projections.
  • Practice: statement 1 is false; the issue intends 2, 3 and 4 only (c), but (b) and (c) print the same combination. Do not memorise this as a valid single-answer option set. Its blanket claim NASA, National Geographic and World Bank all use Equal Earth is not established by the issue; institutions use different projections for different tasks.
