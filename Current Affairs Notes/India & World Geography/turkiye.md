# Türkiye: borders, straits and river systems

## Map recall
- Transcontinental: **Anatolia** in Asia and **Eastern Thrace** in Europe; capital **Ankara**, major city **Istanbul**.
- Eight land neighbours: Greece, Bulgaria, Georgia, Armenia, Azerbaijan (**Nakhchivan exclave**), Iran, Iraq and Syria.
- Seas: Black north, Marmara between the straits, Aegean west, Mediterranean south.
- **Black Sea → Bosphorus → Marmara → Dardanelles → Aegean → Mediterranean**. Straits have major trade/security significance.
- Tigris and Euphrates originate in Türkiye and ultimately join as the **Shatt al-Arab**, flowing to the Persian Gulf.
- Kızılırmak: longest river wholly within the country; **Mount Ararat (~5,137 m)** in eastern Türkiye.
- Location connects Europe, Caucasus and West Asia; map proximity does not determine identical diplomatic positions on every conflict.
