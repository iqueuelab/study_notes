# Penna River and basin

## Map recall
- **Chenna Kesava hill, Nandidurg/Nandi Hills, Karnataka → Andhra Pradesh → Bay of Bengal**; an east-draining peninsular river with an initially northward course.
- Length about **597 km**; basin about **55,000 sq km**, spanning Karnataka and Andhra Pradesh. Distinguish Penna from Tamil Nadu's Ponnaiyar/South Pennar.
