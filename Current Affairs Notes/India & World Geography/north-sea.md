# North Sea geography and shipping risks

## Map anchors
- A shallow continental-shelf sea of the north-eastern Atlantic, between Great Britain and north-western continental Europe.
- Coastal countries: UK, Norway, Denmark, Germany, Netherlands, Belgium and France. It opens northward to the Atlantic and south-westward through the Dover Strait/English Channel.
- Skagerrak–Kattegat–Danish straits connect toward the Baltic. Important river systems include Rhine–Meuse–Scheldt, Elbe, Weser, Ems, Thames and Humber.
- Oil/gas, fisheries, shipping and offshore energy are major uses. Approximate area estimates vary with boundaries.
- The March 2025 Solong–Stena Immaculate collision off eastern England illustrates fuel-spill, fire, shipping-safety and coastal-ecosystem risks; early reports do not establish final pollutant fate.
