# Lake Turkana and ancient enamel proteins

## Image-only July clipping
- **Lake Turkana (Lake Rudolf/Jade Sea)** lies mainly in northern Kenya and extends into Ethiopia; **Omo** is its main perennial inflow. Africa's fourth-largest lake and world's largest permanent desert lake; saline water and arid Rift Valley setting support distinctive ecology/palaeontology.
- **Lake Turkana National Parks** World Heritage property comprises **Sibiloi, Central Island and South Island**; this does not designate the entire lake/basin as one national park. [UNESCO description](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/801).
- Source says researchers extracted 18–20-million-year-old mammal tooth-enamel proteins. Primary study reports enamel protein fragments from **Buluk (~16 Ma) and Loperot (~18 Ma), Turkana Basin**, with preservation extending to at least 18 million years; do not inflate this to a demonstrated exact 20-million-year age or imply extraction from lake water. [Nature study](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-09040-9).
- Ancient proteins can reveal evolutionary relationships beyond some DNA-preservation limits; protein recovery is distinct from recovering intact ancient DNA or resurrecting an extinct animal.
