# Diego Garcia and Chagos sovereignty

## Location and strategic use
- **Coral atoll**, largest/southernmost Chagos island, **central Indian Ocean**, south of equator; V-shaped land rim/open northern lagoon. Source roughly **44 km²**, **24 km length**, **11 km maximum width**, more than half BIOT land area: definitions of land/lagoon matter.
- Source report **15 Tamil Nadu fishermen detained**, alleged maritime violation; allegation/detention does not prove conviction.
- European navigation/contact in early **16th century**; “discovered” does not establish absence of earlier regional knowledge.
## Dated sovereignty sequence
- **1965**: Chagos detached from Mauritius into **British Indian Ocean Territory**; **1966 UK–US defence arrangements** for base, followed by forced removal of Chagossians.
- **2019**: ICJ advisory opinion and non-binding UN General Assembly resolution on completing Mauritius decolonisation; distinguish advisory/resolution status from a bilateral treaty.
- **October 2024** political agreement, **13 January 2025** renewed commitment to concluding a treaty: Mauritius sovereignty with long-term secure operation of UK–US Diego Garcia base proposed. It was **not already a completed January 2025 transfer**. [January joint statement](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/uk-mauritius-joint-statement-on-the-chagos-archipelago-13-january-2025).
- Treaty **signed 22 May 2025**, envisages **99-year base arrangements**; signing and entry into force/implementation are separate. [UK signing release](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/uk-secures-future-of-vital-diego-garcia-military-base-to-protect-national-security).
- Indian Ocean logistics/security, decolonisation, displaced people's rights and maritime jurisdiction are related but distinct issues. Dates here are historical milestones, not a claim about today's completed legal implementation.
## Chagos: legal and diplomatic stages
- UK separation of Chagos in 1965 preceded Mauritian independence in 1968. The ICJ's 25 February 2019 opinion was **advisory**, concerning incomplete lawful decolonisation—not a contentious judgment in a bilateral lawsuit. [ICJ case](https://www.icj-cij.org/case/169).
- The October 2024 announcement was a political agreement; it did not itself complete sovereignty transfer. The proposed long-term Diego Garcia arrangement concerns a UK–US base, not an India–US base.
- Keep agreement, treaty signature, ratification and implementation distinct. Chagossian displacement, return rights and strategic interests are separate issues to assess.
- In the Colombo Security Conclave, Bangladesh became a member in 2024; Seychelles was an observer in the source period.
