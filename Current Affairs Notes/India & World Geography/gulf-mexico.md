# Gulf of Mexico: map, resources and naming

## Map recall
- Atlantic marginal sea on southeastern North America; bordered by **USA, Mexico and Cuba**, world's largest gulf; sometimes called **Mediterranean of the Americas**.
| Connection | Passage |
| --- | --- |
| Gulf ↔ Atlantic | **Straits of Florida**, between Florida and Cuba |
| Gulf ↔ Caribbean | **Yucatán Channel**, between Yucatán Peninsula and Cuba |
- Rivers: **Mississippi**, **Rio Grande**. Tropical/subtropical climate, hurricanes and severe thunderstorms; substantial continental-shelf petroleum/natural gas, fisheries and maritime trade.
## Dated naming development
- January 14 source: President-elect Trump proposed **Gulf of America**.
- **20 January 2025 executive order** subsequently directed US federal geographic-name changes for the described US continental-shelf area and agency references. It does not itself redraw maritime boundaries or impose nomenclature on Mexico/Cuba. [Executive order](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2025/01/restoring-names-that-honor-american-greatness/).
- Prelims trap: **Florida → Atlantic; Yucatán → Caribbean**, not the reverse. A political naming change does not alter physical geography.
