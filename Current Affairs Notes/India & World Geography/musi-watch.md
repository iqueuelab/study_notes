# Musi River and World Monuments Watch

## River recall
- **Anantagiri Hills near Vicarabad, Telangana → Hyderabad → Krishna near Wazirabad, Nalgonda region**; Krishna tributary.
- **Osman Sagar** on Musi; **Himayat Sagar** on its tributary **Esi**. Do not place both dams on the same main channel.
- Esi/Musa headwater descriptions do not imply the whole river is only the source's **8 + 13 km**; Hyderabad's old/new city lie on opposite sides.
## 2025 Watch
- **Musi River Historic Buildings** and Gujarat's **Bhuj Historic Water Systems** included in **2025 World Monuments Watch**.
- **World Monuments Fund**, New York-based heritage organisation; Watch launched **1996**, biennial nomination/advocacy programme. **Not UNESCO World Heritage inscription**.
- Risks: climate/water stress, disasters, conflict and insensitive development/tourism. Heritage preservation should support living communities and resilient urban planning. [2025 WMF Watch](https://www.wmf.org/world-monuments-watch/2025).

## March 2026: riverfront project and residents
- Telangana proposes rejuvenation/public spaces across roughly **55 km of Hyderabad's Musi riverfront**, including parks, heritage/leisure/commercial areas and more sewage treatment. The approximately **240 km Krishna tributary** is sewage-dominated outside the monsoon; downstream irrigation and settlements are affected. [MRDCL project background](https://musi.telangana.gov.in/Media/AC.pdf).
- Issue proposes **39 additional STPs alongside 31 existing facilities** and **2.5 TMC Godavari water via Mallanna Sagar/twin reservoirs** for river flow. These are project/source-period plans, not evidence all plants operate or year-round clean flow is achieved. The State's **September2025 announcement** distinguished **17.5 TMC for drinking water +2.5 TMC for Musi rejuvenation** within a 20-TMC diversion. [State announcement](https://www.telangana.gov.in/news/news-and-press-releases/2025/09/cm-revanth-reddy-participated-in-various-development-programmes-and-addresses-the-public-at-gandipet/).
- The issue describes ADB's **in-principle** support for phase I; do not call it final loan approval/disbursement. It reports an October2024 consultancy consortium/DPR process, evictions and acquisition notices linked to **Gandhi Sarovar**, opposed by residents/civil groups under **Musi Jan Andolan**.
- Mains: publish the DPR and floodplain/ecological assessments, ensure meaningful consultation and lawful rehabilitation/livelihood support, protect heritage, and measure sewage/water-quality outcomes. Riverbank redevelopment and river restoration are not interchangeable outcomes. Existing notes correctly place **Osman Sagar on Musi and Himayat Sagar on Esi**, rather than both on the main river.
