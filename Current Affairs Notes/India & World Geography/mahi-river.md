# Mahi River and Gambhira bridge collapse

## River map
- West-flowing **Mahi/Mahisagar** originates in the **Vindhya region, Madhya Pradesh**; passes through **MP → Rajasthan (Banswara/Vagad) → Gujarat**, reaching the **Gulf of Khambhat, Arabian Sea**, through an estuary.
- **583 km; basin approximately 34,842 km²**. The embedded graphic rounds basin area to 36,000; retain the specified hydrological value. [Gujarat river data](https://guj-nwrws.gujarat.gov.in/showpage.aspx?contentid=1486&lang=English).
- Important tributaries: **Som, Anas and Panam**; the sheet also lists Jakham, Eru, Moran, Bhadar, Chap and Nori. Basin lies between Aravalli/Malwa/Vindhya regions and Gulf of Khambhat. Map identifies **Mahi Bajaj Sagar and Kadana dams**.
- Source reports **Gambhira–Mujpur bridge collapse in Padra taluka, Vadodara**, over Mahi. Bridge inspection, maintenance, flood/scour and structural assessment require engineering investigation; the river's presence alone does not establish the cause.
