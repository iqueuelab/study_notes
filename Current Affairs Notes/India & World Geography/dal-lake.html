Dal Lake: Srinagar wetland and floating gardens

Editable notes

Location, culture and ecology

  • Srinagar, Jammu and Kashmir, at the foot of the Zabarwan hills; the issue's Pir Panjal framing is not the immediate lake-side range. A major shallow urban lake, generally described as J&K's second largest after Wular, with size varying by wetland/open-water boundary and season.
  • Basins: Gagribal, Lokut Dal, Bod Dal and Nagin/Nigeen; Nigeen is sometimes treated separately. Roph Lank/Char Chinari in Lokut Dal and Sone Lank in Bod Dal are island recall points. Do not invent a name for the source's unspecified third island.
  • Raad/Radh floating gardens support vegetable cultivation; shikara markets, houseboats and Shalimar/Nishat Mughal gardens link livelihoods and tourism. Lotus blooms, aquatic plants, fish and migratory birds contribute ecological value; severe winter cold can freeze the surface.
  • Source dimensions ~18 km² open water /21.1 km² including associated wetland, ~15.5 km shore, and depths ~2.5–6 m are approximate and boundary/season dependent, not permanent surveyed constants.
  • Encroachment, sewage, solid waste, siltation, invasive/excessive aquatic growth and tourism pressure threaten water quality and area. Catchment management, sewage treatment, pollution control and livelihood-sensitive restoration are needed.
  • The first Khelo India Water Sports Festival, 21–23 August 2025, brought water-sports competition to Dal Lake; its programme is covered with Khelo India. Official venue account.
