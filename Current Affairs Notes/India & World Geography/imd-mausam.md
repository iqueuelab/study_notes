# IMD at 150 and Mission Mausam

## Institution and milestone
- **India Meteorological Department**, **1875**, **Ministry of Earth Sciences**; **150th Foundation Day, 14 January 2025**, Bharat Mandapam, New Delhi. Cyclone **Calcutta 1864** and other disasters helped motivate coordinated meteorological services.
- **Mission Mausam** and **IMD Vision 2047** launched/released at anniversary: better observation/modelling, severe-weather detection, tailored services and weather-ready/climate-smart society. [Official programme](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleaseIframePage.aspx?PRID=2092386&lang=2&reg=48).
## Source technology timeline
| Milestone | Source year |
| --- | --- |
| Indigenous radar / ISRO satellite-product collaboration | **1958 / since 1983** |
| Automatic weather stations / Doppler radar expansion | **2000 / 2010** |

- Dates describe source-selected programme milestones, not proof no radar/weather station existed earlier.
- High-resolution observations, next-generation radars/satellites and **high-performance computing**; impact-based forecast asks **what weather will do**, not only its intensity.
- **Flash Flood Guidance** and regional support to Nepal/Bhutan/Bangladesh/Sri Lanka; WMO representation claim “six decades/longest Asia-Pacific” is source context, not an indefinite leadership term.
## Targets and limitations
- Source goals **accuracy +10–15% in five years**, **three-day zero-error/five-day 90% by 2047**, customised services; ambitions, not achieved guarantees. Claimed past **50% improvement** needs hazard, metric and baseline.
- Weather forecast skill ≠ short-term earthquake prediction; source's earthquake-warning aspiration should not imply reliable exact earthquake forecasts or that IMD alone performs all seismic services.
- Link early warning to last-mile communication, evacuation, agriculture/transport decisions and resilient infrastructure; forecasting alone cannot remove disaster exposure.
## Institutional recall
- Established by the **Government of India in 1875**, consolidating meteorological work under a **central authority**, rather than founded as a provincial department. Initial headquarters **Calcutta**, later Shimla → Pune → **New Delhi**. [IMD history](https://mausam.imd.gov.in/imd_latest/contents/history.php).
- **India** is a founding WMO member since **1950**; IMD is its national meteorological service, not a separate country member. The 19 January test's 1949 membership claim is incorrect. [WMO anniversary remarks](https://wmo.int/content/opening-remarks-occasion-of-imds-150th-anniversary).
