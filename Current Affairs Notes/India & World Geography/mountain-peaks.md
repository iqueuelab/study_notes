# Indian Himalayan peaks: height and map recall

## Peaks are not ranges
| Peak | Approx. height | Map anchor |
|---|---|---|
| Kanchenjunga | 8,586 m | Sikkim–Nepal boundary; world's third highest |
| Nanda Devi | 7,816 m | **Kumaon**, Uttarakhand |
| Kamet | 7,756 m | Chamoli/Garhwal Himalaya |
| Saltoro Kangri | 7,742 m | Saltoro Range, Karakoram |
| Saser Kangri I | 7,672 m | Saser Muztagh, Karakoram |
| Mamostong Kangri | 7,516 m | Rimo Muztagh, Karakoram |
| Rimo I | 7,385 m | Rimo Muztagh, Karakoram |
| Hardeol | 7,151 m | Kumaon/Milam region |
| Chaukhamba I | 7,138 m | Garhwal/Gangotri massif |
| Trisul I | 7,120 m | Kumaon Himalaya |
- **Nanda Devi is in Kumaon**, not Garhwal. Kanchenjunga is a boundary peak, not wholly inside India.
- **K2 (8,611 m)** lies in territory claimed by India and administered by Pakistan. A list based on territory under Indian administration is different from one using India's territorial claim.
- Heights vary slightly by survey; the source list is not a definitive ranking of every subsidiary summit.
