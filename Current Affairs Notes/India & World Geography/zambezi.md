# Zambezi River: headwaters, basin and Victoria Falls

## December 2025: longest-source reassessment
- A 2025 hydrological study identifies the **Lungwebungu headwater in Angola's southern highlands** as the most distant source: **3,421 km to the Indian Ocean**, **342 km longer** than the traditional source near **Mwinilunga, Zambia**. This is a source-definition/study finding, not a claim that the river physically lengthened overnight. [Research paper](https://doi.org/10.5194/hess-29-4557-2025).
- Africa's **fourth-longest river**, conventionally after Nile, Congo and Niger, and its longest east-flowing river. Main course/border associations: **Zambia, Angola, Namibia, Botswana, Zimbabwe and Mozambique**; these should not be memorised as the PDF's oversimplified single straight sequence. Basin countries and countries touched by the main channel differ.
- **Victoria Falls / Mosi-oa-Tunya (“the smoke that thunders”)**: Zambezi on the **Zambia–Zimbabwe border**. **Chavuma Falls** near the Angola–Zambia border. **Kariba Dam/reservoir** is shared by Zambia/Zimbabwe; **Cahora Bassa** is in Mozambique. Dams affect power generation, seasonal flows, floods and sediment delivery.
- Tributaries: **Kabompo, Kafue, Luangwa, Cuando/Kwando and Shire**; **Shire drains Lake Malawi** into the Zambezi. Delta opens to the **Indian Ocean in Mozambique**. Source reports Zambia has **41.6%** of the basin, its largest national share; percentage is a source snapshot.
- PYQ mapping: **Mekong → South China Sea; Thames → North Sea; Volga → Caspian Sea; Zambezi → Indian Ocean**; supplied **2020** key **3 and 4 only**. **2003 Victoria waterfall** answer **Zambezi (d)**. Source's “UPSC Exam 2022” Victoria two-statement item has ambiguous CSE/UPPSC attribution and is retained for revision only: Zambia–Mozambique false, Zambezi true.
