Cyclone Montha: formation, naming and preparedness

Editable notes

28 October 2025: Andhra Pradesh preparedness

  • The issue forecasts Montha crossing near Kakinada, with strong winds/heavy rain across coastal Andhra Pradesh and impacts inland/into Odisha. Evacuation, shelters, fishing restrictions, school closures, rescue readiness and satellite-phone backup illustrate anticipatory disaster management. Forecast track/intensity and a warning are not observed landfall or measured rainfall.
  • IMD's early 28 October bulletin already calls Montha a cyclonic storm with forecast intensification; the coaching text's still-unnamed deep-depression wording is stale. Contemporary IMD bulletin.
  • Tropical cyclones need warm ocean water/depth, humidity, atmospheric instability, sufficient Coriolis force, a pre-existing disturbance and low vertical wind shear. Condensation releases latent heat. Northern Hemisphere circulation is anticlockwise; Southern clockwise. Eye → eyewall → spiral rainbands distinguish relatively calm centre from strongest surrounding winds/rain.
  • Extratropical cyclones draw primarily on horizontal temperature contrasts/fronts; they also involve latent heat and are not invariably weaker. October–November conditions favour Bay of Bengal storms, without proving every October system is 'very severe'.
  • RSMC New Delhi/IMD, within WMO/ESCAP regional arrangements, handles North Indian Ocean naming; naming began in 2004, now with 13 contributing countries. Names aid clear alerts, avoid offence and follow regional lists. Correct the issue's examples: Tauktae came from Myanmar, Gulab from Pakistan, Asani from Sri Lanka, not India. Montha was contributed by Thailand. WMO list.
  • Green: no warning; yellow: be updated; orange: be prepared; red: take action. Colour expresses potential impact/action, not a fixed cyclone category or an assertion that every red-warning locality is already inundated. Use IMD's operational wind categories/units; source rounded km/h boundaries are not a substitute for bulletins.
  • Mains recall: 2013 Phailin naming—regional bodies, member lists, criteria and communication; 2022 colour warnings—meaning, timely local dissemination, evacuation and coordinated response. The 1995 'Willy-willy' excerpt supplies only a stem and correct option, so remains incomplete for bank inclusion.
