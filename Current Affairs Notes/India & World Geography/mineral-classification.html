Major and minor minerals: classification and map recall

Editable notes

Legal distinction

  • MMDR Act 1957, section 3(e) defines minor minerals: building stones, gravel, ordinary clay, ordinary sand except prescribed uses, and others notified by Centre. It is a legal classification, not simply low output/value or a small mine. Act.
  • State rules regulate minor-mineral concessions under section 15; central notification determines additional minor minerals. Source's attribution solely to MCR 1960 and inclusion of all gemstones/asbestos as minor are unreliable; diamond is not minor just because it is a gemstone.
  • As at January 2025 mica/quartz/barytes were minor. 20 February 2025 notification subsequently moved barytes, felspar, mica, quartz to major; preserve that date when revising. Ministry announcement.
