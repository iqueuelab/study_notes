# Reykjanes Peninsula: rifting, eruptions and geothermal activity

## July 2025 eruption and setting
- Southwest **Iceland**, where the **Mid-Atlantic Ridge** emerges on land and the **North American/Eurasian plates diverge**; fissures, lava fields, craters, earthquakes and geothermal activity illustrate constructive plate margins.
- Eruption began **16 July 2025** at the Sundhnúkur crater row: ninth in that local series since December 2023. Do not confuse this with the total for all Reykjanes eruptions since renewed activity in 2021. [Icelandic Meteorological Office](https://en.vedur.is/about-imo/news/a-magma-instrusion-has-started-on-sundhnuksgigarod-crater-row?gsid=d4201c00-d860-4cdd-9a3c-02a31dcdc835).
- **Reykjanes UNESCO Global Geopark**, designated 2015, lies near Reykjavík; the source's claim that Reykjavík is a major settlement inside the geopark/peninsula should not be used as a precise boundary fact. The source's **829 km²** peninsula figure differs from UNESCO's **818 km² geopark** boundary. [UNESCO](https://www.unesco.org/en/iggp/reykjanes-unesco-global-geopark).
- **Gunnuhver** is a geothermal area with hot springs/steam vents. The inset oceanic-subduction cross-section illustrates a different plate setting; it does not explain Iceland's divergent ridge.
