Mud volcanoes and temporary islands

Editable notes

Mechanism

  • Pressurised gas, water and sediment escape through weak rocks → mud eruptions/cones. Often sedimentary/oil-field settings; methane/hydrocarbons, not necessarily magma.
  • Repeated eruption builds cones, erosion removes them; size/hazard varies. Geothermal mud pots involving boiling water/altered rock are a related-looking but distinct process, not a complete explanation for every sedimentary mud volcano.
  • Source “only warm mud / always less hazardous” is unsafe generalisation: large flows can bury infrastructure, and flammable gas may ignite/explode.

Kumani Bank

  • Caspian Sea, off Azerbaijan: eruption formed island early 2023, nearly eroded away by December 2024; satellite comparison reported January 2025. NASA images.
  • Other reported regions: Ukraine, Italy, Romania, Iran, Pakistan, Indonesia, China; Alaska, California, Trinidad, Venezuela, Colombia.
  • Source ~1,000 on land/shallow water is an inventory estimate, not exhaustive global/submarine total. Mud eruption ≠ lava eruption; isolated small cones do not establish every system is small.
